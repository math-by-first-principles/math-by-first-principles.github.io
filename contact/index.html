Contact

Found an error? Have a question about something on the site? Have an idea that could make a lesson better?

Send me an email. I read every message.

Email Math by First Principles math.by.first.principles@gmail.com

If your email app does not open, use Copy to grab the address and send your message from your own email instead.

What to send

Found an error?

Send the lesson name or page link and tell me which step, example, question, or answer looks wrong.

Something unclear?

Tell me what was hard to follow and where you got stuck. Student feedback about confusing explanations is especially helpful.

Have an idea?

Suggest an improvement, example, visual, practice question, or topic you would like to see.

Please don't include personal information that isn't needed for your message.

I read every message and reply when I can.
