    You invest 200020002000 dollars total in two accounts, one paying 5%5\%5% and one paying 8%8\%8% simple annual interest, earning 139139139 dollars in a year. How much is invested at 8%8\%8%?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Let xxx and yyy be the amounts at 5%5\%5% and 8%8\%8%: x+y=2000x + y = 2000x+y=2000 and 0.05x+0.08y=1390.05x + 0.08y = 1390.05x+0.08y=139. Substitute x=2000−yx = 2000 - yx=2000−y.

    0.05(2000−y)+0.08y=139  ⇒  0.03y=39  ⇒  y=13000.05(2000 - y) + 0.08y = 139 \;\Rightarrow\; 0.03y = 39 \;\Rightarrow\; y = 13000.05(2000−y)+0.08y=139⇒0.03y=39⇒y=1300

    So 130013001300 dollars is invested at 8%8\%8% (and 700700700 at 5%5\%5%).

  2. 2

    A two-digit number has digits that sum to 111111, and its tens digit is 333 more than its units digit. What is the number?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Let ttt and uuu be the tens and units digits: t+u=11t + u = 11t+u=11 and t=u+3t = u + 3t=u+3. Substitute u+3u + 3u+3 for ttt.

    (u+3)+u=11  ⇒  2u=8  ⇒  u=4(u + 3) + u = 11 \;\Rightarrow\; 2u = 8 \;\Rightarrow\; u = 4(u+3)+u=11⇒2u=8⇒u=4

    Then t=7t = 7t=7, so the number is 747474.

  3. 3

    Coffee worth 121212 dollars per pound is blended with coffee worth 777 dollars per pound to make 202020 pounds worth 999 dollars per pound. How many pounds of the 121212-dollar coffee are used?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Let xxx and yyy be the pounds of the 121212- and 777-dollar coffees: x+y=20x + y = 20x+y=20 and 12x+7y=9(20)=18012x + 7y = 9(20) = 18012x+7y=9(20)=180. Substitute y=20−xy = 20 - xy=20−x.

    12x+7(20−x)=180  ⇒  5x=40  ⇒  x=812x + 7(20 - x) = 180 \;\Rightarrow\; 5x = 40 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 812x+7(20−x)=180⇒5x=40⇒x=8

    The blend uses 888 pounds of the 121212-dollar coffee.

  4. 4

    A canoe goes 121212 miles downstream in 222 hours and 121212 miles upstream in 333 hours. What is the speed of the current?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Downstream 2(b+c)=122(b + c) = 122(b+c)=12 gives b+c=6b + c = 6b+c=6; upstream 3(b−c)=123(b - c) = 123(b−c)=12 gives b−c=4b - c = 4b−c=4. Subtract to eliminate bbb.

    (b+c)−(b−c)=6−4  ⇒  2c=2  ⇒  c=1(b + c) - (b - c) = 6 - 4 \;\Rightarrow\; 2c = 2 \;\Rightarrow\; c = 1(b+c)−(b−c)=6−4⇒2c=2⇒c=1

    The current runs at 111 mph (and the canoe at 555 mph).

  5. 5

    A rectangle's perimeter is 545454 cm, and its length is 333 cm more than twice its width. What is the length?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Let LLL and WWW be length and width: 2L+2W=542L + 2W = 542L+2W=54, so L+W=27L + W = 27L+W=27, and L=2W+3L = 2W + 3L=2W+3. Substitute.

    (2W+3)+W=27  ⇒  3W=24  ⇒  W=8(2W + 3) + W = 27 \;\Rightarrow\; 3W = 24 \;\Rightarrow\; W = 8(2W+3)+W=27⇒3W=24⇒W=8

    Then L=2(8)+3=19L = 2(8) + 3 = 19L=2(8)+3=19, so the length is 191919 cm.

  6. 6

    A jet flies 210021002100 miles with the wind in 333 hours, and the return against the wind takes 3.53.53.5 hours. What is the wind speed?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    With the wind the ground speed is 2100/3=7002100 / 3 = 7002100/3=700, so p+w=700p + w = 700p+w=700; against it 2100/3.5=6002100 / 3.5 = 6002100/3.5=600, so p−w=600p - w = 600p−w=600. Subtract to eliminate ppp.

    (p+w)−(p−w)=700−600  ⇒  2w=100  ⇒  w=50(p + w) - (p - w) = 700 - 600 \;\Rightarrow\; 2w = 100 \;\Rightarrow\; w = 50(p+w)−(p−w)=700−600⇒2w=100⇒w=50

    The wind speed is 505050 mph (and the jet is 650650650 mph in still air).

  7. 7

    How many liters of a 15%15\%15% acid solution should be mixed with a 40%40\%40% acid solution to make 101010 liters of a 25%25\%25% acid solution? Give the liters of the 15%15\%15% solution.

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Let xxx be the liters of 15%15\%15% solution and yyy the liters of 40%40\%40%: x+y=10x + y = 10x+y=10 and 0.15x+0.40y=0.25(10)=2.50.15x + 0.40y = 0.25(10) = 2.50.15x+0.40y=0.25(10)=2.5. Substitute x=10−yx = 10 - yx=10−y.

    0.15(10−y)+0.40y=2.5  ⇒  0.25y=1  ⇒  y=40.15(10 - y) + 0.40y = 2.5 \;\Rightarrow\; 0.25y = 1 \;\Rightarrow\; y = 40.15(10−y)+0.40y=2.5⇒0.25y=1⇒y=4

    Then x=10−4=6x = 10 - 4 = 6x=10−4=6, so 666 liters of the 15%15\%15% solution (and 444 of the 40%40\%40%).

  8. 8

    A collection of nickels and quarters has 222222 coins worth 3.903.903.90 dollars. How many quarters are there?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Work in cents. Let nnn and qqq be the counts: n+q=22n + q = 22n+q=22 and 5n+25q=3905n + 25q = 3905n+25q=390. Multiply the count equation by 555 and subtract.

    (5n+25q)−(5n+5q)=390−110  ⇒  20q=280  ⇒  q=14(5n + 25q) - (5n + 5q) = 390 - 110 \;\Rightarrow\; 20q = 280 \;\Rightarrow\; q = 14(5n+25q)−(5n+5q)=390−110⇒20q=280⇒q=14

    There are 141414 quarters (and 888 nickels).

  9. 9

    Maria is 333 times as old as Tom. In 444 years she will be twice as old as Tom. How old is Tom now?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Let MMM and TTT be their present ages: M=3TM = 3TM=3T and M+4=2(T+4)M + 4 = 2(T + 4)M+4=2(T+4). Substitute 3T3T3T for MMM.

    3T+4=2T+8  ⇒  T=43T + 4 = 2T + 8 \;\Rightarrow\; T = 43T+4=2T+8⇒T=4

    Tom is 444 now (and Maria is 121212).

  10. 10

    A kayaker paddles 161616 miles downstream in 222 hours and 161616 miles upstream in 444 hours. What is the kayaker's paddling speed in still water?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Let bbb be the still-water speed and ccc the current, both unknown. Downstream 2(b+c)=162(b + c) = 162(b+c)=16 gives b+c=8b + c = 8b+c=8; upstream 4(b−c)=164(b - c) = 164(b−c)=16 gives b−c=4b - c = 4b−c=4. Add to eliminate ccc.

    2b=12  ⇒  b=62b = 12 \;\Rightarrow\; b = 62b=12⇒b=6

    The still-water speed is 666 mph (and the current is 222 mph).

    A purse holds twice as many dimes as quarters, worth 4.504.504.50 dollars in all. How many quarters are in the purse?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Work in cents. Let qqq be quarters and ddd dimes: d=2qd = 2qd=2q and 25q+10d=45025q + 10d = 45025q+10d=450. Substitute 2q2q2q for ddd.

    25q+10(2q)=450  ⇒  45q=450  ⇒  q=1025q + 10(2q) = 450 \;\Rightarrow\; 45q = 450 \;\Rightarrow\; q = 1025q+10(2q)=450⇒45q=450⇒q=10

    There are 101010 quarters (and 202020 dimes).

    Which system finds the liters xxx of a 25%25\%25% juice and yyy of a 60%60\%60% juice that make 141414 liters of a 40%40\%40% juice?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    One equation adds the volumes and one adds the juice content, matching each concentration to its own variable.

    x+y=14,0.25x+0.60y=0.40(14)x + y = 14, \qquad 0.25x + 0.60y = 0.40(14)x+y=14,0.25x+0.60y=0.40(14)

    Using whole percents as 25x+60y25x + 60y25x+60y mixes up the units, and pairing 0.600.600.60 with xxx swaps the two concentrations.
