  8. Problem 8 Two complete records

    The complete pairs for fff are (0,2)(0,2)(0,2) and (1,3)(1,3)(1,3). The complete pairs for ggg are (2,0)(2,0)(2,0), (3,1)(3,1)(3,1), and (4,0)(4,0)(4,0).
