    1. Evaluating logarithms, their domain, and a claimed rule for sums . Foundational, 12 points. Question 1 of 5.

    Foundational 12 pts

    A logarithm is only ever defined where its own definition can point to an exponent, and each of its three laws combines a PRODUCT, a QUOTIENT, or a POWER, never a sum. This question checks the definition directly, checks when an expression is even defined, and checks whether a proposed rule for combining two logarithms actually belongs among the three laws.

    2. B
