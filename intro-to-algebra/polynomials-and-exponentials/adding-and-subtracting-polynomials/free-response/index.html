Problem 1 of 10

  1. Problem 1 The term cards

    Three cards describe the terms of a polynomial in xxx: coefficient −4-4−4 with degree 222, coefficient 666 with degree 000, and coefficient 111 with degree 555. Write the polynomial in standard form.

    1. Hint 1

      Each coefficient belongs with the power specified on its card.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the highest power first and the constant last.

    Answer

    x5−4x2+6x^5-4x^2+6x5−4x2+6.

    Full solution

    The cards give −4x2-4x^2−4x2, 6x06x^06x0, and x5x^5x5.

    The degree-zero term is the constant 666.

    Sorting by decreasing degree gives

    x5−4x2+6x^5-4x^2+6x5−4x2+6

    The result has exactly the three stated coefficients and powers.

    Answer

    x5−4x2+6x^5-4x^2+6x5−4x2+6.

    Key idea

    A term record determines a polynomial by pairing each coefficient with its power before ordering the terms.

  2. Problem 2 The sign slots

    Each box holds either a plus sign or a minus sign. Fill the boxes so that −(x2 □ 4x □ 7)-(x^2\,\square\,4x\,\square\,7)−(x2□4x□7) equals −x2+4x−7-x^2+4x-7−x2+4x−7 for every real xxx.

    1. Hint 1

      The outside minus reverses the sign of every term inside.

    2. Hint 2

      Work backward from the signs of the xxx term and constant in the required result.

    Answer

    First box: minus. Second box: plus.

    Full solution

    To obtain +4x+4x+4x after negation, the inside term must be −4x-4x−4x.

    To obtain −7-7−7, the inside term must be +7+7+7.

    Thus

    −(x2−4x+7)=−x2+4x−7-(x^2-4x+7)=-x^2+4x-7−(x2−4x+7)=−x2+4x−7

    Both filled signs produce the required terms.

    Answer

    First box: minus. Second box: plus.

    Key idea

    An outside minus reverses each inside sign, so signs can be recovered by reversing that action.

  3. Problem 3 The empty columns

    Write (5x3+2x2−7)−(3x3−4x+1)(5x^3+2x^2-7)-(3x^3-4x+1)(5x3+2x2−7)−(3x3−4x+1) in standard form.

    1. Hint 1

      Subtracting a polynomial subtracts each of its terms, so every sign inside the second parentheses turns over.

    2. Hint 2

      Two of the four powers appear in only one of the polynomials, so those terms have nothing to combine with.

    Answer

    2x3+2x2+4x−82x^3+2x^2+4x-82x3+2x2+4x−8.

    Full solution

    Distributing the minus sign flips all three signs of the second polynomial, so its terms become −3x3-3x^3−3x3, +4x+4x+4x, and −1-1−1.

    Combining like terms one power at a time gives

    x3:5−3=2x2:2−0=2x:0+4=4x0:−7−1=−8\begin{aligned}x^3&: 5-3=2\\x^2&: 2-0=2\\x&: 0+4=4\\x^0&: -7-1=-8\end{aligned}x3x2xx0​:5−3=2:2−0=2:0+4=4:−7−1=−8​

    Written in descending order, the difference is

    2x3+2x2+4x−82x^3+2x^2+4x-82x3+2x2+4x−8

    Checking at x=2x=2x=2, the two polynomials read 414141 and 171717, and their difference 242424 matches

    2(8)+2(4)+8−8=24.2(8)+2(4)+8-8=24.2(8)+2(4)+8−8=24.

    Answer

    2x3+2x2+4x−82x^3+2x^2+4x-82x3+2x2+4x−8.

    Key idea

    A power present in only one polynomial carries down unchanged once the subtraction's signs are distributed.

  4. Problem 4 The canceling entries

    A record lists the terms 2x42x^42x4, −3x-3x−3x, 777, −2x4-2x^4−2x4, and 5x25x^25x2. Combine the record into standard form, then state its degree and leading coefficient.

    1. Hint 1

      Terms with the same power share one coefficient in the final record.

    2. Hint 2

      Check the two degree-444 entries before choosing the leading term.

    Answer

    5x2−3x+75x^2-3x+75x2−3x+7; degree 222; leading coefficient 555.

    Full solution

    The degree-444 terms cancel:

    2x4−2x4=02x^4-2x^4=02x4−2x4=0

    The other powers have one entry each, so standard form is

    5x2−3x+75x^2-3x+75x2−3x+7

    Its surviving terms have degrees 222, 111, and 000.

    The largest is 222, and its coefficient is 555.

    Answer

    5x2−3x+75x^2-3x+75x2−3x+7; degree 222; leading coefficient 555.

    Key idea

    The degree and leading coefficient come from the terms remaining after like terms combine.

  5. Problem 5 The sensor offset

    Two sensors report A(x)=3x2−2x+6A(x)=3x^2-2x+6A(x)=3x2−2x+6 and B(x)=x2+4x−1B(x)=x^2+4x-1B(x)=x2+4x−1 for a real input xxx. A display reports the first reading minus the second, then removes a fixed offset of 555. Write the display rule D(x)D(x)D(x) in standard form and find its reading at x=−1x=-1x=−1.

    1. Hint 1

      Follow the order of the stated changes to the two readings.

    2. Hint 2

      Subtract every term of B(x)B(x)B(x), then include the separate offset.

    Answer

    D(x)=2x2−6x+2D(x)=2x^2-6x+2D(x)=2x2−6x+2; D(−1)=10D(-1)=10D(−1)=10.

    Full solution

    The rule is D=A−B−5D=A-B-5D=A−B−5.

    The coefficients combine as

    3−1=2−2−4=−66−(−1)−5=2\begin{aligned}3-1&=2\\-2-4&=-6\\6-(-1)-5&=2\end{aligned}3−1−2−46−(−1)−5​=2=−6=2​

    Hence

    D(x)=2x2−6x+2D(x)=2x^2-6x+2D(x)=2x2−6x+2

    At the requested input,

    D(−1)=2+6+2D(-1)=2+6+2D(−1)=2+6+2

    so the reading is 101010.

    Checking separately, A(−1)=11A(-1)=11A(−1)=11 and B(−1)=−4B(-1)=-4B(−1)=−4, giving

    11−(−4)−5=10.11-(-4)-5=10.11−(−4)−5=10.

    Answer

    D(x)=2x2−6x+2D(x)=2x^2-6x+2D(x)=2x2−6x+2; D(−1)=10D(-1)=10D(−1)=10.

    Key idea

    A sequence of changes to polynomial readings becomes a sum of signed contributions at each power.

  6. Problem 6 The reversed difference

    For real xxx, two quantities satisfy U(x)−V(x)=2x3−x+4U(x)-V(x)=2x^3-x+4U(x)−V(x)=2x3−x+4. A record needs V(x)−U(x)+3x−6V(x)-U(x)+3x-6V(x)−U(x)+3x−6. Write that expression in standard form and state its degree.

    1. Hint 1

      Reversing a subtraction negates the entire difference.

    2. Hint 2

      Negate the given polynomial before adding the two new terms.

    Answer

    −2x3+4x−10-2x^3+4x-10−2x3+4x−10; degree 333.

    Full solution

    The reversed difference is

    V(x)−U(x)=−2x3+x−4V(x)-U(x)=-2x^3+x-4V(x)−U(x)=−2x3+x−4

    Adding 3x−63x-63x−6 combines the linear terms and constants, giving

    −2x3+4x−10-2x^3+4x-10−2x3+4x−10

    The leading coefficient is −2-2−2, which is nonzero, so the degree is 333.

    At x=0x=0x=0, the original difference is 444 and the requested quantity is −4−6=−10-4-6=-10−4−6=−10, agreeing with the constant.

    Answer

    −2x3+4x−10-2x^3+4x-10−2x3+4x−10; degree 333.

    Key idea

    Reversing a difference changes every sign before any further terms are combined.

  7. Problem 7 The total counter

    A counter totals three contributions. Their first two together equal 4x3+x2−64x^3+x^2-64x3+x2−6, while their last two together equal x3−2x+5x^3-2x+5x3−2x+5. The middle contribution is 2x3−3x+12x^3-3x+12x3−3x+1. Find the total of all three in standard form.

    1. Hint 1

      Adding the two given pair totals counts the middle contribution twice.

    2. Hint 2

      Remove one copy of the middle contribution from the sum of the pair totals.

    Answer

    3x3+x2+x−23x^3+x^2+x-23x3+x2+x−2.

    Full solution

    Call the contributions AAA, BBB, and CCC.

    The total is (A+B)+(B+C)−B(A+B)+(B+C)-B(A+B)+(B+C)−B, and each power is counted on its own row:

    x3:4+1−2=3x2:1+0−0=1x:0−2−(−3)=1x0:−6+5−1=−2\begin{aligned}x^3&: 4+1-2=3\\x^2&: 1+0-0=1\\x&: 0-2-(-3)=1\\x^0&: -6+5-1=-2\end{aligned}x3x2xx0​:4+1−2=3:1+0−0=1:0−2−(−3)=1:−6+5−1=−2​

    Written in descending order,

    A+B+C=3x3+x2+x−2A+B+C=3x^3+x^2+x-2A+B+C=3x3+x2+x−2

    At x=0x=0x=0, the pair totals are −6-6−6 and 555, and the shared entry is 111, so the total is −6+5−1=−2-6+5-1=-2−6+5−1=−2, matching the result.

    Answer

    3x3+x2+x−23x^3+x^2+x-23x3+x2+x−2.

    Key idea

    When overlapping totals are added, remove the shared contribution once to count every part once.

  8. Problem 8 The shared coefficient

    Polynomials AAA and BBB each have degree 333 and leading coefficient 444. Nia says A−BA-BA−B must have degree 222. Is she right? If not, give polynomials AAA and BBB meeting these conditions whose nonzero difference does not have degree 222.

    1. Hint 1

      The leading terms cancel, but the remaining powers depend on the other terms.

    2. Hint 2

      Choose two cubics whose degree-222 coefficients also agree.

    Answer

    No; for example, A=4x3+xA=4x^3+xA=4x3+x and B=4x3B=4x^3B=4x3, giving degree 111.

    Full solution

    The shared leading coefficient guarantees that the cubic terms cancel, but it does not guarantee a quadratic term.

    Choose A=4x3+xA=4x^3+xA=4x3+x and B=4x3B=4x^3B=4x3.

    Then

    A−B=xA-B=xA−B=x

    Both inputs satisfy the degree and coefficient conditions, while their nonzero difference has degree 111, so the claim is false.

    Answer

    No; for example, A=4x3+xA=4x^3+xA=4x3+x and B=4x3B=4x^3B=4x3, giving degree 111.

    Key idea

    Canceling leading terms gives an upper bound on the remaining degree, not a guarantee of the next degree.

  9. Problem 9 The degree report

    A nonzero polynomial PPP has degree 555. Another polynomial QQQ has degree at most 333. Leo says the degree of P+QP+QP+Q is the same as the degree of P−QP-QP−Q. Is he right? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Check which powers QQQ can change in either operation.

    2. Hint 2

      Neither adding nor subtracting QQQ changes the degree-555 coefficient.

    Answer

    Yes; both degrees are 555.

    Full solution

    Let a≠0a\ne0a=0 be the coefficient of x5x^5x5 in PPP.

    In the sum and difference, the coefficients at that power are

    a+0=aa−0=a\begin{aligned}a+0&=a\\a-0&=a\end{aligned}a+0a−0​=a=a​

    Both coefficients stay nonzero, and no higher power appears.

    Thus both degrees are 555, so Leo is right.

    Answer

    Yes; both degrees are 555.

    Key idea

    A lower-degree polynomial cannot change a higher-degree leading coefficient through addition or subtraction.

  10. Problem 10 Two canceling quartics

    Create two different polynomials, each with exactly three nonzero terms and degree 444, whose sum is a nonzero constant. Give the polynomials and their sum, and explain your construction.

    1. Hint 1

      Adding polynomials adds coefficients power by power, so the sum is a constant exactly when every coefficient above x0x^0x0 adds to zero.

    2. Hint 2

      Use opposite coefficients on two shared positive powers and constants that do not add to zero.

    Answer

    One choice: P=x4+2x+3P=x^4+2x+3P=x4+2x+3, Q=−x4−2x+1Q=-x^4-2x+1Q=−x4−2x+1; P+Q=4P+Q=4P+Q=4.

    Full solution

    Choose the degree-444 and degree-111 terms in opposite pairs, but choose constants 333 and 111.

    Then

    x4−x4=02x−2x=03+1=4\begin{aligned}x^4-x^4&=0\\2x-2x&=0\\3+1&=4\end{aligned}x4−x42x−2x3+1​=0=0=4​

    Thus P=x4+2x+3P=x^4+2x+3P=x4+2x+3 and Q=−x4−2x+1Q=-x^4-2x+1Q=−x4−2x+1 each have three nonzero terms and degree 444, yet their sum is the nonzero constant 444.

    Many choices of opposite nonconstant terms work.

    Answer

    One choice: P=x4+2x+3P=x^4+2x+3P=x4+2x+3, Q=−x4−2x+1Q=-x^4-2x+1Q=−x4−2x+1; P+Q=4P+Q=4P+Q=4.

    Key idea

    A constant sum can result when every nonconstant coefficient cancels and the constants do not.
