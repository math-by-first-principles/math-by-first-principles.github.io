    As mmm grows without bound, the values (1+1m)m\left(1 + \frac{1}{m}\right)^{m}(1+m1​)m for m=1,2,10,100,…m = 1, 2, 10, 100, \ldotsm=1,2,10,100,… get closer and closer to:

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The values rise by smaller and smaller amounts and crowd toward a single number.

    (1+1m)m⟶e≈2.71828\left(1 + \frac{1}{m}\right)^{m} \longrightarrow e \approx 2.71828(1+m1​)m⟶e≈2.71828

    That limit is the definition of eee, the base of continuous compounding.
