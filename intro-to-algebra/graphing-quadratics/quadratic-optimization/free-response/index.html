    3. What the sign of a decides, and does not . Reasoning, 10 points. Question 3 of 5.

    Reasoning 10 pts

    A claim: the sign of a quadratic quantity's leading coefficient does not just decide between a maximum and a minimum, it also decides whether that best value itself is positive.       Explain what it means Words, not just symbols. Say what the number is telling you about the situation. 3 points

    Hints

    One at a time, each one a step further than the last. Take only as many as you need.

    The answer

    Part A

    Take y=−2x2−4x−9y=-2x^2-4x-9y=−2x2−4x−9. Here a=−2<0a=-2<0a=−2<0, so the vertex is a maximum, occurring at x=−1x=-1x=−1, but the maximum value is y=−7y=-7y=−7, which is negative. One such example is enough to refute a claim made for ALL quadratics with a<0a<0a<0.

    y=a(x−h)2+k  ⇒  {a>0:y≥ka<0:y≤ky=a(x-h)^2+k \;\Rightarrow\; \begin{cases}a>0: & y\ge k \\ a<0: & y\le k\end{cases}y=a(x−h)2+k⇒{a>0:a<0:​y≥ky≤k​

    Part C

    Worked solution

    Part A

    Both height equations share the same −16t2+64t-16t^2+64t−16t2+64t part, so a=−16a=-16a=−16 and b=64b=64b=64 for each. The peak time depends only on those two coefficients.

    t=−b2a=−642(−16)=−64−32=2.t=-\frac{b}{2a}=-\frac{64}{2(-16)}=-\frac{64}{-32}=2.t=−2ab​=−2(−16)64​=−−3264​=2.

    That computation never used either constant term, so both the 101010 foot platform and the 404040 foot platform peak at the same time, t=2t=2t=2 seconds.

    Part B

    Substitute t=2t=2t=2 into each platform's own height equation, including its own starting height.

    For the 101010 foot platform:

    h=−16(2)2+64(2)+10=−64+128+10=74.h=-16(2)^2+64(2)+10=-64+128+10=74.h=−16(2)2+64(2)+10=−64+128+10=74.

    h=−16(2)2+64(2)+40=−64+128+40=104.h=-16(2)^2+64(2)+40=-64+128+40=104.h=−16(2)2+64(2)+40=−64+128+40=104.

    Part C

    Line the two comparisons up. The peak times from part A are the same number for both platforms, because x=−b2ax=-\tfrac{b}{2a}x=−2ab​ is built only out of aaa and bbb, and those two coefficients never changed between the versions.

    The peak heights from part B are not the same: substituting the shared time back into h=−16t2+64t+ch=-16t^2+64t+ch=−16t2+64t+c adds in ccc, the starting height, which is the one number that did change between the platforms, by 40−10=3040-10=3040−10=30 feet.
