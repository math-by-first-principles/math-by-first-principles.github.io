    A cinema sells 300300300 tickets at 888 dollars each. Research shows each 111 dollar price increase sells 303030 fewer tickets. What ticket price maximizes revenue?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Let xxx be the number of 111 dollar increases. The price is 8+x8 + x8+x and the tickets sold are 300−30x300 - 30x300−30x, so revenue is R=(8+x)(300−30x)=−30x2+60x+2400R = (8 + x)(300 - 30x) = -30x^2 + 60x + 2400R=(8+x)(300−30x)=−30x2+60x+2400.

    x=−602(−30)=1  ⇒  price=8+1=9x = -\frac{60}{2(-30)} = 1 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{price} = 8 + 1 = 9x=−2(−30)60​=1⇒price=8+1=9

    A price of 999 dollars maximizes revenue.
