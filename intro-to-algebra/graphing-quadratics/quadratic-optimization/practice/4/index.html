    Two positive numbers add up to 242424. What is their greatest possible product?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    With one number xxx, the other is 24−x24 - x24−x, so the product is P=x(24−x)=−x2+24xP = x(24 - x) = -x^2 + 24xP=x(24−x)=−x2+24x, largest at the vertex.
