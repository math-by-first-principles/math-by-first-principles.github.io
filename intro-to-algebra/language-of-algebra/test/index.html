    The two sides differ, so the claim fails at x=1x = 1x=1, and a claim about every number is false once a single value breaks it.

    At x=0x = 0x=0 both sides happen to be 000, so that value would not have shown anything.

    No law licenses the classmate's step.

    The distributive law spreads a factor across the terms of a sum, and 2x2x2x is not a sum: it is the product of 222 and xxx, so the 777 multiplies it once.

    Sending the 777 to both factors multiplies by 777 twice, which makes the right side 777 times the left side at every xxx.

    Answer

    Step 1: commutative law of multiplication; step 2: identity law of multiplication, read right to left; step 3: distributive law, read right to left. The claim is false: at x=1x = 1x=1 its sides are 141414 and 989898.

    Key idea

    A factor spreads across the terms of a sum, not across the factors of a product, and one value where the two sides differ refutes a claim about every number.

  10. Problem 10 A common factor in two letters

    Jamal factors 20a3b2−30a2b3+10a2b220a^3b^2 - 30a^2b^3 + 10a^2b^220a3b2−30a2b3+10a2b2 and writes 10ab(2a2b−3ab2+ab)10ab(2a^2b - 3ab^2 + ab)10ab(2a2b−3ab2+ab).

    Decide whether Jamal's factorization is complete, and if it is not, complete it. Then explain why no factor common to all three terms can contain a3a^3a3 or b3b^3b3.

    1. Hint 1

      A factor common to all three terms has to divide each of them, and the greatest common factor is fully out when the terms left inside the bracket share no factor other than 111.

    2. Hint 2

      Look at the three terms inside Jamal's bracket, 2a2b2a^2b2a2b, −3ab2-3ab^2−3ab2 and ababab, and find what all of them still contain.

    3. Hint 3

      For the last part, compare the power of aaa in each of the three original terms, and then the power of bbb. A power of a letter can be shared only if every term contains at least that many factors of it.

    Answer

    Jamal's factorization is not complete. The complete factorization is 10a2b2(2a−3b+1)10a^2b^2(2a - 3b + 1)10a2b2(2a−3b+1), and the lowest powers of aaa and bbb in the three terms are a2a^2a2 and b2b^2b2.

    Full solution

    The greatest common divisor of 202020, 303030 and 101010 is 101010.

    The powers of aaa in the three terms are a3a^3a3, a2a^2a2 and a2a^2a2, and the powers of bbb are b2b^2b2, b3b^3b3 and b2b^2b2.

    The lowest of each is contained in every term, so the greatest common factor is 10a2b210a^2b^210a2b2.

    Jamal took out 10ab10ab10ab: the right number, but only one factor of each letter.

    The terms inside his bracket, 2a2b2a^2b2a2b, −3ab2-3ab^2−3ab2 and ababab, all still contain aaa and bbb, so they share the factor ababab, and his factorization is not complete.

    Taking that shared ababab out as well finishes the job.

    10ab(2a2b−3ab2+ab)=10ab⋅ab(2a−3b+1)=10a2b2(2a−3b+1).\begin{aligned} &10ab(2a^2b - 3ab^2 + ab) \\ &= 10ab \cdot ab(2a - 3b + 1) \\ &= 10a^2b^2(2a - 3b + 1). \end{aligned}​10ab(2a2b−3ab2+ab)=10ab⋅ab(2a−3b+1)=10a2b2(2a−3b+1).​

    Check it term by term: 10a2b2⋅2a10a^2b^2 \cdot 2a10a2b2⋅2a is 20a3b220a^3b^220a3b2, 10a2b2⋅(−3b)10a^2b^2 \cdot (-3b)10a2b2⋅(−3b) is −30a2b3-30a^2b^3−30a2b3, and 10a2b2⋅110a^2b^2 \cdot 110a2b2⋅1 is 10a2b210a^2b^210a2b2.

    The terms left inside, 2a2a2a, −3b-3b−3b and 111, share no factor other than 111, so the greatest common factor has now come out in full.

    No common factor can contain a3a^3a3, because a common factor has to divide every term, and −30a2b3-30a^2b^3−30a2b3 and 10a2b210a^2b^210a2b2 each contain only two factors of aaa.

    In the same way, 20a3b220a^3b^220a3b2 and 10a2b210a^2b^210a2b2 contain only two factors of bbb, so no common factor can contain b3b^3b3.

    That is why each letter in the greatest common factor appears at its lowest power.

    Answer

    Jamal's factorization is not complete. The complete factorization is 10a2b2(2a−3b+1)10a^2b^2(2a - 3b + 1)10a2b2(2a−3b+1), and the lowest powers of aaa and bbb in the three terms are a2a^2a2 and b2b^2b2.

    Key idea

    Each letter in the greatest common factor appears at its lowest power among the terms, and that factor is fully out only when the terms left inside share no factor other than 111.
