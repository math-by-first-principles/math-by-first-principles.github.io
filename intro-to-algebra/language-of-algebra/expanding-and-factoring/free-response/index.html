  1. Problem 1 One factor, three terms

    Expand −4x(2x2−3x+5)-4x(2x^2 - 3x + 5)−4x(2x2−3x+5), and check your expansion by evaluating both it and the original product at x=2x = 2x=2.

    1. Hint 1

      The factor outside the parentheses has to reach every term inside, all three of them, and its minus sign goes with it into each product.

    2. Hint 2

      Work out each product in two pieces: multiply the numbers, signs included, then add the exponents where xxx meets a power of xxx.

    3. Hint 3

      The middle product is (−4x)(−3x)(-4x)(-3x)(−4x)(−3x), and two negative coefficients multiply to a positive one. At x=2x = 2x=2, work out the parentheses first in the original.

    Answer

    −8x3+12x2−20x-8x^3 + 12x^2 - 20x−8x3+12x2−20x; check: at x=2x = 2x=2 both forms equal −56-56−56.

    Full solution

    The factor −4x-4x−4x has to multiply each of the three terms inside the parentheses, and its minus sign goes with it into every product.

    In each product, multiply the numbers with their signs, then add the exponents of xxx:

    (−4x)(2x2)=−8x3(−4x)(−3x)=12x2(−4x)(5)=−20x.\begin{aligned} (-4x)(2x^2) &= -8x^3 \\ (-4x)(-3x) &= 12x^2 \\ (-4x)(5) &= -20x. \end{aligned}(−4x)(2x2)(−4x)(−3x)(−4x)(5)​=−8x3=12x2=−20x.​

    The middle product is positive because its coefficients, −4-4−4 and −3-3−3, are both negative, and their product is 121212.

    Adding the three products gives the expansion

    −8x3+12x2−20x.-8x^3 + 12x^2 - 20x.−8x3+12x2−20x.

    Check at x=2x = 2x=2.

    In the original, the parentheses give 2(4)−3(2)+52(4) - 3(2) + 52(4)−3(2)+5, which is 777, and the factor outside is −4(2)-4(2)−4(2), which is −8-8−8, so the product is −56-56−56.

    In the expansion, the three terms at x=2x = 2x=2 are −64-64−64, 484848 and −40-40−40, which also add to −56-56−56.

    One agreeing value does not prove the two forms equal for every xxx, but a disagreement would have exposed a slip.

    A value such as x=2x = 2x=2 is a better test than x=1x = 1x=1, where every power of xxx equals 111 and a wrong exponent would go unnoticed.

    Answer

    −8x3+12x2−20x-8x^3 + 12x^2 - 20x−8x3+12x2−20x; check: at x=2x = 2x=2 both forms equal −56-56−56.

    Key idea

    A factor outside the parentheses multiplies every term inside, carrying its sign into each product and adding exponents where powers of xxx meet.

  2. Problem 2 Two binomials

    Multiply (3x−7)(2x+5)(3x - 7)(2x + 5)(3x−7)(2x+5) and simplify, then check your result by evaluating both it and the original product at x=3x = 3x=3.

    1. Hint 1

      Each term of the first factor must multiply each term of the second, so the product starts out as four separate pieces.

    2. Hint 2

      FOIL is one way to be sure all four are there: First, Outer, Inner, Last. Keep the minus sign with the 777 in both products it enters.

    3. Hint 3

      Two of the four products are xxx terms with opposite signs. Add their coefficients, signs included, before you write the result.

    Answer

    6x2+x−356x^2 + x - 356x2+x−35; check: at x=3x = 3x=3 both forms equal 222222.

    Full solution

    Every term of 3x−73x - 73x−7 multiplies every term of 2x+52x + 52x+5, which makes four products.

    FOIL names them First, Outer, Inner and Last:

    (3x)(2x)=6x2(3x)(5)=15x(−7)(2x)=−14x(−7)(5)=−35.\begin{aligned} (3x)(2x) &= 6x^2 \\ (3x)(5) &= 15x \\ (-7)(2x) &= -14x \\ (-7)(5) &= -35. \end{aligned}(3x)(2x)(3x)(5)(−7)(2x)(−7)(5)​=6x2=15x=−14x=−35.​

    The outer and inner products, 15x15x15x and −14x-14x−14x, are like terms.

    Their coefficients add to 111, so together they make 1x1x1x, which is written xxx.

    The product is

    6x2+x−35.6x^2 + x - 35.6x2+x−35.

    Check at x=3x = 3x=3.

    The first factor is 3(3)−73(3) - 73(3)−7, which is 222, and the second is 2(3)+52(3) + 52(3)+5, which is 111111, so the original product is 222222.

    The expansion gives 6(9)+3−356(9) + 3 - 356(9)+3−35, which is also 222222.

    Answer

    6x2+x−356x^2 + x - 356x2+x−35; check: at x=3x = 3x=3 both forms equal 222222.

    Key idea

    Multiplying two binomials makes four products, one for each pairing of terms; FOIL is a way of listing them, and the like terms among them are combined at the end.

  3. Problem 3 The largest shared factor

    Pull the greatest common factor out of 18x4−12x3+6x218x^4 - 12x^3 + 6x^218x4−12x3+6x2, and check your factoring by expanding it.

    1. Hint 1

      The greatest common factor has a number part and a variable part: the greatest common divisor of the coefficients, and the smallest power of xxx that appears.

    2. Hint 2

      The coefficients 181818, 121212 and 666 all divide by 666, and every term contains at least x2x^2x2. For each term, ask what the common factor must be multiplied by to give that term: that is what goes in the parentheses.

    3. Hint 3

      One term is exactly the factor you are pulling out, and the factor times 111 gives it back. So that term leaves 111 in the parentheses, not nothing.

    Answer

    6x2(3x2−2x+1)6x^2(3x^2 - 2x + 1)6x2(3x2−2x+1); expanding it returns 18x4−12x3+6x218x^4 - 12x^3 + 6x^218x4−12x3+6x2.

    Full solution

    The number part of the greatest common factor is the greatest common divisor of 181818, 121212 and 666, which is 666.

    The variable part is the smallest power of xxx that appears, which is x2x^2x2, and each of x4x^4x4, x3x^3x3 and x2x^2x2 has x2x^2x2 as a factor.

    So the greatest common factor is 6x26x^26x2.

    Write each term as 6x26x^26x2 times something:

    18x4=6x2⋅3x2−12x3=6x2⋅(−2x)6x2=6x2⋅1.\begin{aligned} 18x^4 &= 6x^2 \cdot 3x^2 \\ -12x^3 &= 6x^2 \cdot (-2x) \\ 6x^2 &= 6x^2 \cdot 1. \end{aligned}18x4−12x36x2​=6x2⋅3x2=6x2⋅(−2x)=6x2⋅1.​

    The distributive law read from right to left lifts the shared 6x26x^26x2 out front and leaves the other factors inside:

    6x2(3x2−2x+1).6x^2(3x^2 - 2x + 1).6x2(3x2−2x+1).

    The last term leaves 111 behind.

    Dropping it would give 6x2(3x2−2x)6x^2(3x^2 - 2x)6x2(3x2−2x), which expands to only two terms.

    Check by expanding: 6x26x^26x2 times 3x23x^23x2, −2x-2x−2x and 111 gives 18x418x^418x4, −12x3-12x^3−12x3 and 6x26x^26x2, the three terms of the original.

    The terms left inside, 3x23x^23x2, −2x-2x−2x and 111, share no number factor greater than 111 and no factor of xxx, so nothing more can be pulled out.

    Answer

    6x2(3x2−2x+1)6x^2(3x^2 - 2x + 1)6x2(3x2−2x+1); expanding it returns 18x4−12x3+6x218x^4 - 12x^3 + 6x^218x4−12x3+6x2.

    Key idea

    The greatest common factor is the greatest common divisor of the coefficients times each shared variable at its smallest power, and a term equal to that factor leaves 111 in the parentheses.

  4. Problem 4 A binomial times a trinomial

    Multiply (4x−3)(x2+2x−6)(4x - 3)(x^2 + 2x - 6)(4x−3)(x2+2x−6) and simplify, then check your result by evaluating both it and the original product at x=−1x = -1x=−1.

    1. Hint 1

      FOIL lists the four products of two two-term factors, but the second factor here has three terms. Go back to the rule FOIL names: every term of one factor times every term of the other.

    2. Hint 2

      Distribute each term of 4x−34x - 34x−3 across the whole trinomial: first 4x4x4x times all three terms, then −3-3−3 times all three terms. That makes six products.

    3. Hint 3

      Two pairs of the six products are like terms, the two x2x^2x2 terms and the two xxx terms. At x=−1x = -1x=−1, remember that (−1)2=1(-1)^2 = 1(−1)2=1 and (−1)3=−1(-1)^3 = -1(−1)3=−1.

    Answer

    4x3+5x2−30x+184x^3 + 5x^2 - 30x + 184x3+5x2−30x+18; check: at x=−1x = -1x=−1 both forms equal 494949.

    Full solution

    The second factor has three terms, so FOIL, which lists the four products of two two-term factors, cannot cover this product.

    The rule it names still applies: each of the two terms of 4x−34x - 34x−3 multiplies each of the three terms of x2+2x−6x^2 + 2x - 6x2+2x−6, which makes 2×3=62 \times 3 = 62×3=6 products.

    Distributing 4x4x4x across the trinomial gives 4x34x^34x3, 8x28x^28x2 and −24x-24x−24x.

    Distributing −3-3−3 gives −3x2-3x^2−3x2, −6x-6x−6x and 181818, where the last is positive because (−3)(−6)(-3)(-6)(−3)(−6) is a product of two negatives.

    Now collect like terms.

    The x2x^2x2 terms and the xxx terms combine:

    8x2−3x2=5x2−24x−6x=−30x.\begin{aligned} 8x^2 - 3x^2 &= 5x^2 \\ -24x - 6x &= -30x. \end{aligned}8x2−3x2−24x−6x​=5x2=−30x.​

    The x3x^3x3 term and the constant have no partners, so the product is

    4x3+5x2−30x+18.4x^3 + 5x^2 - 30x + 18.4x3+5x2−30x+18.

    Check at x=−1x = -1x=−1.

    The first factor is 4(−1)−34(-1) - 34(−1)−3, which is −7-7−7, and the second is 1−2−61 - 2 - 61−2−6, which is also −7-7−7, so the original product is 494949.

    In the expansion, (−1)3=−1(-1)^3 = -1(−1)3=−1 and (−1)2=1(-1)^2 = 1(−1)2=1, so it gives −4+5+30+18-4 + 5 + 30 + 18−4+5+30+18, which is also 494949.

    Answer

    4x3+5x2−30x+184x^3 + 5x^2 - 30x + 184x3+5x2−30x+18; check: at x=−1x = -1x=−1 both forms equal 494949.

    Key idea

    The number of products is the number of terms in one factor times the number in the other, so a two-term factor times a three-term factor gives 2×3=62 \times 3 = 62×3=6, not FOIL's four.

  5. Problem 5 Two letters, then a common factor

    Expand and simplify (x+2y)(3x−y)−x(3x+4y)(x + 2y)(3x - y) - x(3x + 4y)(x+2y)(3x−y)−x(3x+4y), then factor the result by pulling out its greatest common factor.

    1. Hint 1

      Expand each product on its own first. The minus sign in front of x(3x+4y)x(3x + 4y)x(3x+4y) belongs to that whole product, so it changes the sign of every term the product makes.

    2. Hint 2

    3. Hint 3

      After the subtraction, look for terms that cancel. Then ask which letter every remaining term contains, and to what smallest power.

    Answer

    xy−2y2xy - 2y^2xy−2y2, which factors as y(x−2y)y(x - 2y)y(x−2y).

    Full solution

    Multiplying xxx by each term of 3x−y3x - y3x−y gives 3x23x^23x2 and −xy-xy−xy, and multiplying 2y2y2y by each term gives 6xy6xy6xy and −2y2-2y^2−2y2.

    The second product expands to 3x2+4xy3x^2 + 4xy3x2+4xy.

    3x2+5xy−2y2−3x2−4xy=xy−2y2.\begin{aligned} &3x^2 + 5xy - 2y^2 - 3x^2 - 4xy \\ &= xy - 2y^2. \end{aligned}​3x2+5xy−2y2−3x2−4xy=xy−2y2.​

    The 3x23x^23x2 terms cancel, and 5xy−4xy5xy - 4xy5xy−4xy leaves xyxyxy.

  6. Problem 6 Raising the rental price

    A bike rental shop rents out 404040 bikes a day at 151515 dollars each. Each time the owner raises the price by 111 dollar, the shop rents 222 fewer bikes a day. Let nnn be the number of one-dollar price rises, a whole number from 000 to 202020.

    Write the shop's daily takings after nnn price rises as a product of two expressions in nnn, then expand and simplify it. Say what the constant term of your expansion tells the owner.

    1. Hint 1

      Takings are the price of one rental times the number of rentals. Write each of those two quantities in terms of nnn before multiplying.

    2. Hint 2

      After nnn rises the price has gone up by nnn dollars, and the number of bikes rented has gone down by 222 for each rise. Multiply the two expressions, every term by every term.

    3. Hint 3

      The constant term is what the expansion equals when n=0n = 0n=0. Ask what n=0n = 0n=0 means for the shop.

    The takings are (15+n)(40−2n)(15 + n)(40 - 2n)(15+n)(40−2n) dollars, which expands to 600+10n−2n2600 + 10n - 2n^2600+10n−2n2 (or −2n2+10n+600-2n^2 + 10n + 600−2n2+10n+600). The constant term 600600600 is the takings in dollars with no price rise.

    Takings are the price of one rental times the number of rentals.

    After nnn one-dollar rises the price is 15+n15 + n15+n dollars, and the shop rents 2n2n2n fewer bikes, so it rents 40−2n40 - 2n40−2n.

    (15+n)(40−2n).(15 + n)(40 - 2n).(15+n)(40−2n).

    15⋅40=60015⋅(−2n)=−30nn⋅40=40nn⋅(−2n)=−2n2.\begin{aligned} 15 \cdot 40 &= 600 \\ 15 \cdot (-2n) &= -30n \\ n \cdot 40 &= 40n \\ n \cdot (-2n) &= -2n^2. \end{aligned}15⋅4015⋅(−2n)n⋅40n⋅(−2n)​=600=−30n=40n=−2n2.​

    The two nnn terms are like terms, and −30n-30n−30n and 40n40n40n combine to 10n10n10n.

    So the takings are 600+10n−2n2600 + 10n - 2n^2600+10n−2n2 dollars, which can also be written −2n2+10n+600-2n^2 + 10n + 600−2n2+10n+600.

    The constant term, 600600600, is what the expansion equals when n=0n = 0n=0, that is, with no price rise.

    A spot check at n=3n = 3n=3: the product is 18⋅3418 \cdot 3418⋅34, which is 612612612, and the expansion gives 600+30−18600 + 30 - 18600+30−18, also 612612612.

    Answer

    The takings are (15+n)(40−2n)(15 + n)(40 - 2n)(15+n)(40−2n) dollars, which expands to 600+10n−2n2600 + 10n - 2n^2600+10n−2n2 (or −2n2+10n+600-2n^2 + 10n + 600−2n2+10n+600). The constant term 600600600 is the takings in dollars with no price rise.

  7. Problem 7 One bed in place of two

    A gardener has two rectangular flower beds, each 3x3x3x meters wide, where xxx is positive. One bed is 4x+54x + 54x+5 meters long and the other is x+3x + 3x+3 meters long.

    Find the total area of the two beds as a simplified expression in xxx. Then factor that expression using its greatest common factor, and use the factored form to find how long a single rectangular bed, also 3x3x3x meters wide, must be to have the same total area.

    1. Hint 1

      A rectangle's area is its width times its length, and a product in factored form can be read the same way, as a width times a length.

    2. Hint 2

      Multiply 3x3x3x into every term of each bed's length, then add the two areas and collect like terms. For the greatest common factor, take the greatest common divisor of the two coefficients and the smallest power of xxx in the two terms.

    3. Hint 3

      A bed 3x3x3x meters wide and LLL meters long has area 3x⋅L3x \cdot L3x⋅L square meters. Set your factored form beside that product and compare the factors.

    Answer

    The total area is 15x2+24x15x^2 + 24x15x2+24x square meters, which factors as 3x(5x+8)3x(5x + 8)3x(5x+8). The single bed must be 5x+85x + 85x+8 meters long.

    Full solution

    Each bed's area is its width times its length, and the factor 3x3x3x has to reach every term of each length:

    3x(4x+5)=12x2+15x3x(x+3)=3x2+9x.\begin{aligned} 3x(4x + 5) &= 12x^2 + 15x \\ 3x(x + 3) &= 3x^2 + 9x. \end{aligned}3x(4x+5)3x(x+3)​=12x2+15x=3x2+9x.​

    Add the two areas and collect like terms: 12x212x^212x2 and 3x23x^23x2 make 15x215x^215x2, and 15x15x15x and 9x9x9x make 24x24x24x.

    So the total area is 15x2+24x15x^2 + 24x15x2+24x square meters.

    The greatest common divisor of 151515 and 242424 is 333, and both terms contain xxx, whose smallest power there is xxx itself, so the greatest common factor is 3x3x3x.

    Writing each term as 3x3x3x times something, 15x215x^215x2 is 3x⋅5x3x \cdot 5x3x⋅5x and 24x24x24x is 3x⋅83x \cdot 83x⋅8, so

    15x2+24x=3x(5x+8).\begin{aligned} &15x^2 + 24x \\ &= 3x(5x + 8). \end{aligned}​15x2+24x=3x(5x+8).​

    Check by expanding: 3x⋅5x3x \cdot 5x3x⋅5x is 15x215x^215x2 and 3x⋅83x \cdot 83x⋅8 is 24x24x24x, the two terms of the total.

    Inside the parentheses, 5x5x5x and 888 share no number factor greater than 111, and 888 has no factor of xxx, so nothing more can be pulled out.

    A bed 3x3x3x meters wide and LLL meters long has area 3x⋅L3x \cdot L3x⋅L square meters.

    The factored form writes the total area as 3x3x3x times 5x+85x + 85x+8, so a bed 5x+85x + 85x+8 meters long has exactly the total area.

    No other length does, because 3x3x3x is not zero when xxx is positive.

    The same length comes straight from the distributive law, since both beds are 3x3x3x meters wide: 3x(4x+5)+3x(x+3)3x(4x + 5) + 3x(x + 3)3x(4x+5)+3x(x+3) is 3x3x3x times the sum of the two lengths, and (4x+5)+(x+3)(4x + 5) + (x + 3)(4x+5)+(x+3) is 5x+85x + 85x+8.

    Check with x=2x = 2x=2.

    The beds are 666 by 131313 and 666 by 555 meters, with areas 787878 and 303030 square meters, 108108108 in all.

    The expanded form gives 15(4)+24(2)15(4) + 24(2)15(4)+24(2), which is 108108108, and a single bed 666 meters wide and 181818 meters long also covers 108108108 square meters.

    Answer

    The total area is 15x2+24x15x^2 + 24x15x2+24x square meters, which factors as 3x(5x+8)3x(5x + 8)3x(5x+8). The single bed must be 5x+85x + 85x+8 meters long.

    Key idea

    An area in factored form reads as width times length, so pulling a known width out as a factor leaves the matching length inside the parentheses.

  8. Problem 8 A pattern on the calendar

    On a monthly calendar laid out in rows of seven days, each date sits directly above the date one week later. Choose any 2×22 \times 22×2 block of four dates from the same month. Multiply the top-right date by the bottom-left date, multiply the top-left date by the bottom-right date, and subtract the second product from the first.

    Writing nnn for the top-left date, express this difference in terms of nnn and simplify it. Then explain what your result shows about every such block.

    1. Hint 1

      Name all four dates in terms of nnn: one place to the right adds 111 to a date, and one row down adds 777.

    2. Hint 2

      The two products are (n+1)(n+7)(n + 1)(n + 7)(n+1)(n+7) and n(n+8)n(n + 8)n(n+8). Expand both, every term times every term, and subtract the second as a whole.

    3. Hint 3

      Once the difference is simplified, look at whether any nnn is left in it, and what that means for the choice of block.

    Answer

    The difference is (n+1)(n+7)−n(n+8)(n + 1)(n + 7) - n(n + 8)(n+1)(n+7)−n(n+8), which simplifies to 777: every 2×22 \times 22×2 block gives the same difference, 777.

    Full solution

    One place to the right on the calendar adds 111 to the date, and one row down adds 777.

    So with nnn top-left, the top row holds nnn and n+1n + 1n+1, and the bottom row holds n+7n + 7n+7 and n+8n + 8n+8.

    The top-right date times the bottom-left date is (n+1)(n+7)(n + 1)(n + 7)(n+1)(n+7), and the top-left date times the bottom-right date is n(n+8)n(n + 8)n(n+8).

    The difference is

    (n+1)(n+7)−n(n+8).(n + 1)(n + 7) - n(n + 8).(n+1)(n+7)−n(n+8).

    Expand the first product, every term times every term:

    (n+1)(n+7)=n2+7n+n+7=n2+8n+7.\begin{aligned} &(n + 1)(n + 7) \\ &= n^2 + 7n + n + 7 \\ &= n^2 + 8n + 7. \end{aligned}​(n+1)(n+7)=n2+7n+n+7=n2+8n+7.​

    The second product expands to n2+8nn^2 + 8nn2+8n.

    It is subtracted as a whole, so both of its terms change sign:

    n2+8n+7−n2−8n=7.\begin{aligned} &n^2 + 8n + 7 - n^2 - 8n \\ &= 7. \end{aligned}​n2+8n+7−n2−8n=7.​

    The n2n^2n2 terms cancel and the 8n8n8n terms cancel, so no nnn is left.

    The equation (n+1)(n+7)−n(n+8)=7(n + 1)(n + 7) - n(n + 8) = 7(n+1)(n+7)−n(n+8)=7 is true for every number nnn, so every 2×22 \times 22×2 block on the calendar gives the difference 777, whichever date is top-left.

    For example, the block with 888 top-left holds 888, 999, 151515 and 161616, and 9⋅15−8⋅169 \cdot 15 - 8 \cdot 169⋅15−8⋅16 is 135−128135 - 128135−128, which is 777.

    Trying blocks one at a time shows the pattern only for the blocks tried; the expansion shows it for all of them at once.

    Answer

    The difference is (n+1)(n+7)−n(n+8)(n + 1)(n + 7) - n(n + 8)(n+1)(n+7)−n(n+8), which simplifies to 777: every 2×22 \times 22×2 block gives the same difference, 777.

    Key idea

    Expanding an expression written with a letter can show that a numerical pattern holds for every case at once, not just for the examples tried.

  9. Problem 9 Jordan's four products

    Jordan multiplies (x−4)(x2+5x+2)(x - 4)(x^2 + 5x + 2)(x−4)(x2+5x+2) using FOIL. He takes the First terms x⋅x2x \cdot x^2x⋅x2, the Outer terms x⋅2x \cdot 2x⋅2, the Inner terms (−4)⋅x2(-4) \cdot x^2(−4)⋅x2 and the Last terms (−4)⋅2(-4) \cdot 2(−4)⋅2, and writes the answer x3−4x2+2x−8x^3 - 4x^2 + 2x - 8x3−4x2+2x−8.

    Test Jordan's answer against the original product at x=2x = 2x=2, and find the correct product. Then explain which products Jordan missed and why FOIL could not have found them.

    1. Hint 1

      FOIL is a name for the four products of two two-term factors. Count the terms in each of Jordan's factors before trusting it.

    2. Hint 2

      At x=2x = 2x=2, work out each factor of the original product on its own and multiply them; then work out Jordan's four terms at x=2x = 2x=2 and compare.

    3. Hint 3

      Every term of x−4x - 4x−4 must multiply every term of x2+5x+2x^2 + 5x + 2x2+5x+2, which makes six products. Look for the two that Jordan never formed: both involve the same term of the trinomial.

    Answer

    At x=2x = 2x=2 the original product is −32-32−32 but Jordan's answer gives −12-12−12. The correct product is x3+x2−18x−8x^3 + x^2 - 18x - 8x3+x2−18x−8. Jordan missed 5x25x^25x2 and −20x-20x−20x, the products with 5x5x5x: FOIL names four products, and this multiplication needs six.

    Full solution

    Test first.

    At x=2x = 2x=2 the first factor is 2−42 - 42−4, which is −2-2−2, and the second is 4+10+24 + 10 + 24+10+2, which is 161616, so the original product is −32-32−32.

    Jordan's answer gives 8−16+4−88 - 16 + 4 - 88−16+4−8, which is −12-12−12.

    The values differ, so Jordan's answer is not equal to the product.

    Now multiply correctly.

    Every term of x−4x - 4x−4 multiplies every term of x2+5x+2x^2 + 5x + 2x2+5x+2, which makes 2×3=62 \times 3 = 62×3=6 products.

    Distributing xxx gives x3x^3x3, 5x25x^25x2 and 2x2x2x, and distributing −4-4−4 gives −4x2-4x^2−4x2, −20x-20x−20x and −8-8−8.

    Collect like terms: 5x25x^25x2 and −4x2-4x^2−4x2 make x2x^2x2, and 2x2x2x and −20x-20x−20x make −18x-18x−18x.

    So the product is

    x3+x2−18x−8.x^3 + x^2 - 18x - 8.x3+x2−18x−8.

    At x=2x = 2x=2 this gives 8+4−36−88 + 4 - 36 - 88+4−36−8, which is −32-32−32, matching the original product.

    Jordan's four products are all correct, but two of the six are missing: xxx times 5x5x5x, which is 5x25x^25x2, and −4-4−4 times 5x5x5x, which is −20x-20x−20x.

    Both come from the middle term 5x5x5x of the trinomial.

    FOIL names four products, first, outer, inner and last, one for each pairing when both factors have two terms.

    With three terms in the second factor there are six pairings, and the words first and last pick out only its end terms, so the middle term 5x5x5x is never multiplied.

    Jordan's answer is in fact what FOIL gives for (x−4)(x2+2)(x - 4)(x^2 + 2)(x−4)(x2+2), the product with the 5x5x5x deleted.

    Answer

    At x=2x = 2x=2 the original product is −32-32−32 but Jordan's answer gives −12-12−12. The correct product is x3+x2−18x−8x^3 + x^2 - 18x - 8x3+x2−18x−8. Jordan missed 5x25x^25x2 and −20x-20x−20x, the products with 5x5x5x: FOIL names four products, and this multiplication needs six.

    Key idea

    FOIL is a name for the four products of two binomials; when a factor has more terms, every term times every term finds all of the products.

  10. Problem 10 Three attempts at one factoring

    Three students factor 16x3+40x216x^3 + 40x^216x3+40x2. Ana writes 4x(4x2+10x)4x(4x^2 + 10x)4x(4x2+10x), Ben writes 8x2(2x+5)8x^2(2x + 5)8x2(2x+5), and Cal writes 8x(2x2+5)8x(2x^2 + 5)8x(2x2+5).

    Expand each answer and say which ones are equal to 16x3+40x216x^3 + 40x^216x3+40x2. Then say which answer pulls out the greatest common factor, and explain why expanding alone could not settle that second question.

    1. Hint 1

      Expand each answer by multiplying the factor outside into every term inside, then compare the result with 16x3+40x216x^3 + 40x^216x3+40x2 term by term.

    2. Hint 2

      For the greatest common factor, find the greatest common divisor of 161616 and 404040, and the smallest power of xxx that appears in the two terms.

    3. Hint 3

      Two answers expand correctly. Look inside their parentheses: do the terms there still share a number factor greater than 111 or a factor of xxx, and would expanding ever reveal that?

    Answer

    Ana's and Ben's expand to 16x3+40x216x^3 + 40x^216x3+40x2; Cal's gives 16x3+40x16x^3 + 40x16x3+40x, so it is not equal. Only Ben's, 8x2(2x+5)8x^2(2x + 5)8x2(2x+5), pulls out the greatest common factor. Expanding tests only equality, which Ana's unfinished factoring also passes.

    Full solution

    Expand each answer by multiplying the factor outside into every term inside.

    Ana's gives 4x⋅4x2=16x34x \cdot 4x^2 = 16x^34x⋅4x2=16x3 and 4x⋅10x=40x24x \cdot 10x = 40x^24x⋅10x=40x2, which is the original.

    Ben's gives 8x2⋅2x=16x38x^2 \cdot 2x = 16x^38x2⋅2x=16x3 and 8x2⋅5=40x28x^2 \cdot 5 = 40x^28x2⋅5=40x2, which is the original too.

    Cal's gives 8x⋅2x2=16x38x \cdot 2x^2 = 16x^38x⋅2x2=16x3 but 8x⋅5=40x8x \cdot 5 = 40x8x⋅5=40x, not 40x240x^240x2, so Cal's answer is not equal to 16x3+40x216x^3 + 40x^216x3+40x2.

    Since 40x240x^240x2 is 8x⋅5x8x \cdot 5x8x⋅5x, the second term inside should have been 5x5x5x, and Cal dropped the xxx.

    The greatest common factor has number part 888, the greatest common divisor of 161616 and 404040, and variable part x2x^2x2, the smallest power of xxx that appears.

    So it is 8x28x^28x2, and Ben's is the answer that pulls it out.

    The terms left in Ben's parentheses, 2x2x2x and 555, share no number factor greater than 111 and no factor of xxx.

    Ana's answer is equal to the original, but it is not finished: the terms inside, 4x24x^24x2 and 10x10x10x, still share the factor 2x2x2x.

    Pulling that out as well turns 4x(4x2+10x)4x(4x^2 + 10x)4x(4x2+10x) into 4x⋅2x(2x+5)4x \cdot 2x(2x + 5)4x⋅2x(2x+5), which is Ben's 8x2(2x+5)8x^2(2x + 5)8x2(2x+5).

    Expanding answers one question only: is the product equal to the original expression?

    Ana's and Ben's are both equal to it, so both pass, and expanding cannot tell them apart.

    To see whether the factor outside is the greatest, look inside the parentheses instead: if the terms there still share a number factor greater than 111 or a factor of xxx, the factor outside was not the greatest.

    Answer

    Ana's and Ben's expand to 16x3+40x216x^3 + 40x^216x3+40x2; Cal's gives 16x3+40x16x^3 + 40x16x3+40x, so it is not equal. Only Ben's, 8x2(2x+5)8x^2(2x + 5)8x2(2x+5), pulls out the greatest common factor. Expanding tests only equality, which Ana's unfinished factoring also passes.

    Re-expanding checks that a factoring is correct; to check that the factor pulled out is the greatest, see whether the terms left inside still share a number factor greater than 111 or a factor of xxx.
