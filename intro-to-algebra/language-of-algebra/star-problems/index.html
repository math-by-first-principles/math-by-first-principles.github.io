  1. Problem 1 Three nearby products

    ★ Stretch Difficulty: 1 of 3 stars, Stretch

    Without calculating any of the four-digit products separately, evaluate

    2027⋅2032−2029⋅20302028⋅2031−2029⋅2030.\frac{2027\cdot2032-2029\cdot2030}{2028\cdot2031-2029\cdot2030}.2028⋅2031−2029⋅20302027⋅2032−2029⋅2030​.

    Explain why the answer is unchanged if every number appearing in the expression is increased by the same real number.

    1. Hint 1

      All three products have factors with the same sum. Give the smallest number a short name.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the three products as n(n+5)n(n+5)n(n+5), (n+1)(n+4)(n+1)(n+4)(n+1)(n+4), and (n+2)(n+3)(n+2)(n+3)(n+2)(n+3). Compare their expanded forms.

    Answer

    333, unchanged under every common real shift.

    Full solution

    Let nnn be the smallest number.

    The three products are n(n+5)=n2+5nn(n+5)=n^2+5nn(n+5)=n2+5n, (n+1)(n+4)=n2+5n+4(n+1)(n+4)=n^2+5n+4(n+1)(n+4)=n2+5n+4, and

    (n+2)(n+3)=n2+5n+6.(n+2)(n+3)=n^2+5n+6.(n+2)(n+3)=n2+5n+6.

    They share the entire variable part; only the constants differ.

    The numerator is therefore −6-6−6, and the denominator is −2-2−2.

    Their quotient is 333.

    Increasing every displayed number by a real number ttt merely replaces nnn by n+tn+tn+t.

    The same cancellations still occur, so the quotient remains 333.

    The denominator stays −2-2−2 even if some individual factors become zero or negative, so no common shift creates an undefined expression.

    Answer

    333, unchanged under every common real shift.

    Key idea

    Products with the same sum of factors often share a common variable part after expansion.

  2. Problem 2 Information hidden in a ratio

    ★ Stretch Difficulty: 1 of 3 stars, Stretch

    Positive real numbers aaa and bbb satisfy ab+ba=7\frac ab+\frac ba=7ba​+ab​=7. Find the exact values of

    (a+b)2a2+b2and(a−b)2a2+b2.\frac{(a+b)^2}{a^2+b^2}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\frac{(a-b)^2}{a^2+b^2}.a2+b2(a+b)2​anda2+b2(a−b)2​.

    Explain why there is no need to determine aaa or bbb separately.

    Builds on Algebraic Fractions

    1. Hint 1

      Combine the two fractions in the given information.

    2. Hint 2

      The condition gives a2+b2=7aba^2+b^2=7aba2+b2=7ab. Expand each requested numerator.

    Answer

    97\frac9779​ and 57\frac5775​, respectively.

    Full solution

    Combining the given fractions gives (a2+b2)/(ab)=7(a^2+b^2)/(ab)=7(a2+b2)/(ab)=7, so

    a2+b2=7ab.a^2+b^2=7ab.a2+b2=7ab.

    The product ababab is positive and may safely be canceled.

    Expand

    (a+b)2=a2+2ab+b2=9ab.(a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2=9ab.(a+b)2=a2+2ab+b2=9ab.

    Dividing by a2+b2=7aba^2+b^2=7aba2+b2=7ab gives 9/79/79/7.

    Similarly, (a−b)2=a2−2ab+b2=5ab(a-b)^2=a^2-2ab+b^2=5ab(a−b)2=a2−2ab+b2=5ab, which gives 5/75/75/7.

    The condition determines the ratio between a2+b2a^2+b^2a2+b2 and ababab, and that ratio is all either answer uses.

    Individual values of aaa and bbb would contain unnecessary information.

    In fact, multiplying both numbers by any positive factor preserves both the hypothesis and the requested values.

    Answer

    97\frac9779​ and 57\frac5775​, respectively.

    Key idea

    Determine the combination of quantities a question needs before trying to determine the quantities themselves.

  3. Problem 3 When roots preserve addition

    ★ Stretch Difficulty: 1 of 3 stars, Stretch

    Let nnn be a positive integer. For odd nnn, un\sqrt[n]{u}nu​ means the unique real nnnth root of uuu; for even nnn, it means the nonnegative real nnnth root, defined when u≥0u\ge0u≥0.

    For each nnn, find all pairs of real numbers (a,b)(a,b)(a,b) for which

    ann+bnn=(a+b)nn.\sqrt[n]{a^n}+\sqrt[n]{b^n}=\sqrt[n]{(a+b)^n}.nan​+nbn​=n(a+b)n​.

    Explain why every displayed root is defined, and prove your classification.

    Builds on Fractional Exponents and Radicals

    1. Hint 1

      The answer depends on whether the power retains or loses the sign of its input.

    2. Hint 2

      For even nnn, compare the cases in which a,ba,ba,b have the same sign or opposite signs. In the opposite-sign case, write the two nonzero magnitudes as positive numbers.

    Answer

    For odd nnn, every real pair works. For even nnn, exactly the pairs with ab≥0ab\ge0ab≥0 work, including pairs with a zero entry.

    Full solution

    If nnn is odd, taking an nnnth power and its unique real nnnth root returns the original number, including its sign.

    Thus the equation becomes a+b=a+ba+b=a+ba+b=a+b, and every real pair works.

    All odd roots are defined.

    If nnn is even, every quantity inside a root is an even power and is therefore nonnegative, so again all displayed roots exist.

    The root of a powered number equals that number when it is nonnegative and its negative when it is negative.

    If a,b≥0a,b\ge0a,b≥0, the equation becomes a+b=a+ba+b=a+ba+b=a+b.

    If a,b≤0a,b\le0a,b≤0, it becomes

    −a−b=−(a+b).-a-b=-(a+b).−a−b=−(a+b).

    These cases include zero entries.

    If a,ba,ba,b have opposite nonzero signs, let their positive magnitudes be u,vu,vu,v.

    The left side is u+vu+vu+v, but the right side is u−vu-vu−v when u≥vu\ge vu≥v and v−uv-uv−u when v≥uv\ge uv≥u.

    In either case it is strictly smaller, because both u,vu,vu,v are positive.

    Therefore, for even nnn, the equation holds exactly when the two numbers have the same weak sign, equivalently ab≥0ab\ge0ab≥0.

    Answer

    For odd nnn, every real pair works. For even nnn, exactly the pairs with ab≥0ab\ge0ab≥0 work, including pairs with a zero entry.

    Key idea

    An even power loses sign information; track that loss before moving a root across addition.

  4. Problem 4 Three fractions that cooperate

    ★★ Challenge Difficulty: 2 of 3 stars, Challenge

    Positive real numbers x,y,zx,y,zx,y,z satisfy xyz=1xyz=1xyz=1. Prove that

    11+x+xy+11+y+yz+11+z+zx=1.\frac1{1+x+xy}+\frac1{1+y+yz}+\frac1{1+z+zx}=1.1+x+xy1​+1+y+yz1​+1+z+zx1​=1.

    Your proof should explain why three apparently different denominators can be coordinated.

    Builds on Algebraic Fractions

    1. Hint 1

      Try multiplying one of the denominators by one of the variables.

    2. Hint 2

      If D=1+x+xyD=1+x+xyD=1+x+xy, then x(1+y+yz)=Dx(1+y+yz)=Dx(1+y+yz)=D. Find a similar relation for the third denominator.

    Answer

    The sum is always 111.

    Full solution

    Let D=1+x+xyD=1+x+xyD=1+x+xy.

    The condition xyz=1xyz=1xyz=1 gives

    x(1+y+yz)=x+xy+xyz=D.x(1+y+yz)=x+xy+xyz=D.x(1+y+yz)=x+xy+xyz=D.

    It also gives

    xy(1+z+zx)=xy+xyz+x2yz=xy+1+x=D.xy(1+z+zx)=xy+xyz+x^2yz=xy+1+x=D.xy(1+z+zx)=xy+xyz+x2yz=xy+1+x=D.

    Since all variables are positive, DDD and all three original denominators are positive.

    We may therefore rewrite the three fractions as 1/D1/D1/D, x/Dx/Dx/D, and xy/Dxy/Dxy/D, respectively.

    Their sum is

    (1+x+xy)/D=1.(1+x+xy)/D=1.(1+x+xy)/D=1.

    The useful common denominator did not come from multiplying three large expressions together.

    Instead, the product condition supplied exactly the scaling factors needed to turn each denominator into DDD.

    Keeping the given relation available during simplification prevents unnecessary expansion.

    Answer

    The sum is always 111.

    Key idea

    A constraint linking variables may reveal a much smaller common denominator than a mechanical calculation would produce.

  5. Problem 5 Four number cards and a constant

    ★★ Challenge Difficulty: 2 of 3 stars, Challenge

    Choose four distinct cards from 1,2,3,4,5,61,2,3,4,5,61,2,3,4,5,6 and place their numbers in the positions a,b,c,da,b,c,da,b,c,d, with a<ba<ba<b and c<dc<dc<d. You want

    Full solution

    This is constant exactly when a+b=c+da+b=c+da+b=c+d; otherwise changing xxx changes the value.

    At sum 888, (2,6),(3,5)(2,6),(3,5)(2,6),(3,5) give difference 333; at sum 999, (3,6),(4,5)(3,6),(4,5)(3,6),(4,5) give difference 222.

    Substitution confirms that its expression is always 666.

    Answer

    The maximum is 666, attained only by (a,b,c,d)=(3,4,1,6)(a,b,c,d)=(3,4,1,6)(a,b,c,d)=(3,4,1,6).

    Key idea

    First translate a condition that holds for every input into a restriction on coefficients; then optimize within that smaller set.

  6. Problem 6 Zeros that disappear with the denominator

    ★★ Challenge Difficulty: 2 of 3 stars, Challenge

    Consider the expression

    R=x2−4x−2−x2−9x−31x−2−1x−3.R=\frac{\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-2}-\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}}{\dfrac1{x-2}-\dfrac1{x-3}}.R=x−21​−x−31​x−2x2−4​−x−3x2−9​​.

  7. Problem 7 Build an identity from three tests

    ★★ Challenge Difficulty: 2 of 3 stars, Challenge

    Find real constants A,B,CA,B,CA,B,C such that

    A(x−1)(x−2)+B(x−2)(x−3)+C(x−1)(x−3)=1A(x-1)(x-2)+B(x-2)(x-3)+C(x-1)(x-3)=1A(x−1)(x−2)+B(x−2)(x−3)+C(x−1)(x−3)=1

    for every real xxx. Prove both that your constants work for every xxx and that there is no other choice.

    1. Hint 1

      Choose inputs that make two of the three terms disappear.

    2. Hint 2

      Testing x=1,2,3x=1,2,3x=1,2,3 determines the three constants separately. Afterward, expand to verify the full identity.

    Answer

    A=B=12A=B=\frac12A=B=21​ and C=−1C=-1C=−1.

    Full solution

    Answer

    Key idea

    Choose substitutions that eliminate terms to discover a candidate, then use an identity to prove it works everywhere.
