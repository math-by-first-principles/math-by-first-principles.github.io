  1. Problem 1 A difference over a single term

    Simplify 10x2−35x15x\dfrac{10x^2 - 35x}{15x}15x10x2−35x​ completely, and state every value of xxx that must be excluded.

    1. Hint 1

      A fraction simplifies only by canceling a factor of the whole top and the whole bottom, so first write each of them as a product.

    2. Hint 2

      Pull the greatest common factor out of 10x2−35x10x^2 - 35x10x2−35x, then look for that same factor inside 15x15x15x.

    3. Hint 3

      The excluded values come from the original denominator, not from the simplified one. Ask where 15x15x15x is zero.

    Answer

    2x−73\tfrac{2x - 7}{3}32x−7​ (or 2x3−73\tfrac{2x}{3} - \tfrac{7}{3}32x​−37​), for x≠0x \neq 0x=0.

    Full solution

    The numerator is a difference of two terms, so it has to be factored before anything can cancel.

    The coefficients 101010 and 353535 share the factor 555, and both terms contain xxx, so the greatest common factor is 5x5x5x:

    10x2−35x=5x(2x−7).10x^2 - 35x = 5x(2x - 7).10x2−35x=5x(2x−7).

    The denominator contains the same factor, since

    15x=5x⋅3.15x = 5x \cdot 3.15x=5x⋅3.

    So the fraction is

    5x(2x−7)5x⋅3.\frac{5x(2x - 7)}{5x \cdot 3}.5x⋅35x(2x−7)​.

    The factor 5x5x5x multiplies the whole top and the whole bottom, and 5x5x=1\tfrac{5x}{5x} = 15x5x​=1 whenever x≠0x \neq 0x=0, so it cancels:

    2x−73.\frac{2x - 7}{3}.32x−7​.

    Nothing else cancels.

    The 777 is a term of 2x−72x - 72x−7, not a factor of it, and 2x−72x - 72x−7 shares no factor with 333.

    The original denominator 15x15x15x is zero only at x=0x = 0x=0, so x=0x = 0x=0 is excluded.

    The simplified form has a value at 000 but the original fraction does not, so the condition x≠0x \neq 0x=0 stays with the answer.

    Check at x=2x = 2x=2: the original gives 40−7030\tfrac{40 - 70}{30}3040−70​, which is −1-1−1, and the simplified form gives 4−73\tfrac{4 - 7}{3}34−7​, which is also −1-1−1.

    Answer

    2x−73\tfrac{2x - 7}{3}32x−7​ (or 2x3−73\tfrac{2x}{3} - \tfrac{7}{3}32x​−37​), for x≠0x \neq 0x=0.

    Factor the top and the bottom first: a factor of the whole numerator and the whole denominator cancels, and the excluded values come from the original denominator.

  2. Problem 2 A product of two fractions

    Multiply x−810x⋅4x23x−24\dfrac{x - 8}{10x} \cdot \dfrac{4x^2}{3x - 24}10xx−8​⋅3x−244x2​, simplify the result completely, and state every value of xxx that must be excluded.

    1. Hint 1

      A product of fractions is the product of the tops over the product of the bottoms, so a factor on either top can cancel against the same factor on either bottom.

    2. Hint 2

      Factor 3x−243x - 243x−24 first. A binomial then appears on one top and one bottom, and the numbers and powers of xxx share factors too.

    3. Hint 3

    2x15\tfrac{2x}{15}152x​, for x≠0x \neq 0x=0 and x≠8x \neq 8x=8.

    Full solution

  3. Problem 3 Two fractions over x+7x + 7x+7

    Simplify 7x+20x+7−3x−8x+7\dfrac{7x + 20}{x + 7} - \dfrac{3x - 8}{x + 7}x+77x+20​−x+73x−8​ completely, and state every value of xxx that must be excluded.

    1. Hint 1

      The denominators already match, so the work happens in the numerators. The minus sign between the fractions applies to the whole second numerator.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the new numerator as (7x+20)−(3x−8)(7x + 20) - (3x - 8)(7x+20)−(3x−8), and distribute the minus to both terms in the second group before collecting like terms.

    3. Hint 3

      Once the numerator is collected, factor it and compare it with the denominator: the fraction may simplify further.

    444, for x≠−7x \neq -7x=−7.

    Both fractions have the denominator x+7x + 7x+7, so keep it and subtract the numerators.

    (7x+20)−(3x−8)x+7.\frac{(7x + 20) - (3x - 8)}{x + 7}.x+7(7x+20)−(3x−8)​.

    The numerator is then 7x+20−3x+87x + 20 - 3x + 87x+20−3x+8, which collects to 4x+284x + 284x+28.

    The numerator factors as 4(x+7)4(x + 7)4(x+7), which shares the factor x+7x + 7x+7 with the denominator.

    That factor is not zero because x≠−7x \neq -7x=−7, so it cancels:

    4(x+7)x+7=4.\frac{4(x + 7)}{x + 7} = 4.x+74(x+7)​=4.

    Dropping the parentheses without flipping the −8-8−8 would give the numerator 4x+124x + 124x+12 instead, and the fraction would not reduce to a number.

    Check at x=1x = 1x=1: the first fraction is 278\tfrac{27}{8}827​ and the second is −58\tfrac{-5}{8}8−5​.

    Their difference is 27+58\tfrac{27 + 5}{8}827+5​, which is 328\tfrac{32}{8}832​, or 444.

  4. Problem 4 A quotient of two fractions

  6. Problem 6 Two rectangles with equal areas

    A rectangle has sides of length 5x3x+27\dfrac{5x}{3x + 27}3x+275x​ and x+910\dfrac{x + 9}{10}10x+9​, where xxx is a positive number and every length is in meters. Find its area as a single fraction in simplest form.

    A second rectangle has the same area, and one of its sides has length 2x9\dfrac{2x}{9}92x​ meters. Find the length of its other side.

    1. Hint 1

      The area of a rectangle is the product of its side lengths, and a missing side is the area divided by the side you know.

    2. Hint 2

      Factor 3x+273x + 273x+27 before multiplying: it shares a binomial with the other side, and the numbers 555 and 101010 share a factor too.

    3. Hint 3

      For the second rectangle, divide the area by 2x9\tfrac{2x}{9}92x​ by multiplying by its reciprocal, and cancel before you multiply.

    Answer

    The first rectangle's area is x6\tfrac{x}{6}6x​ square meters. The second rectangle's other side is 34\tfrac{3}{4}43​ meter, for every positive xxx.

    Full solution

    Because xxx is positive, every numerator and denominator here is positive, so every length is a positive number and no value of xxx in the problem is excluded.

    The area is the product of the two sides.

    Factor 3x+27=3(x+9)3x + 27 = 3(x + 9)3x+27=3(x+9) first, so the product is

    5x3(x+9)⋅x+910.\frac{5x}{3(x + 9)} \cdot \frac{x + 9}{10}.3(x+9)5x​⋅10x+9​.

    Cancel before multiplying.

    The binomial x+9x + 9x+9 is on a top and a bottom, and 555 and 101010 share the factor 555, leaving 111 and 222.

    What remains multiplies to

    x3⋅2=x6.\frac{x}{3 \cdot 2} = \frac{x}{6}.3⋅2x​=6x​.

    So the area is x6\tfrac{x}{6}6x​ square meters.

    The second rectangle's other side is its area divided by the known side.

    Dividing by 2x9\tfrac{2x}{9}92x​ means multiplying by its reciprocal 92x\tfrac{9}{2x}2x9​, which exists because xxx is not zero:

    x6⋅92x.\frac{x}{6} \cdot \frac{9}{2x}.6x​⋅2x9​.

    The xxx cancels, and 999 and 666 share the factor 333, leaving 333 on top and 2⋅22 \cdot 22⋅2 on the bottom.

    So the other side is 34\tfrac{3}{4}43​ meter.

    No xxx is left in that length, so it is 34\tfrac{3}{4}43​ meter whatever positive number xxx is.

    The area x6\tfrac{x}{6}6x​ and the known side 2x9\tfrac{2x}{9}92x​ are each a fixed number times xxx, so their quotient does not depend on xxx.

    Check with x=9x = 9x=9: the first rectangle's sides are 4554\tfrac{45}{54}5445​, or 56\tfrac{5}{6}65​, and 1810\tfrac{18}{10}1018​, or 95\tfrac{9}{5}59​, so its area is 4530\tfrac{45}{30}3045​, or 32\tfrac{3}{2}23​, which is 96\tfrac{9}{6}69​.

    Answer

    The first rectangle's area is x6\tfrac{x}{6}6x​ square meters. The second rectangle's other side is 34\tfrac{3}{4}43​ meter, for every positive xxx.

    Key idea

    An area is a product of lengths and a missing side is a quotient, so multiplying and dividing algebraic fractions answers both questions.

  7. Problem 7 The third stretch of a trail

    A hiking trail is made of three stretches with a total length of 5x6\dfrac{5x}{6}65x​ kilometers, where xxx is a number greater than 666. The first stretch is x4\dfrac{x}{4}4x​ kilometers long and the second is x−63\dfrac{x - 6}{3}3x−6​ kilometers long.

    Find the length of the third stretch as a single fraction in simplest form, and check your answer by taking x=12x = 12x=12.

    1. Hint 1

      The third stretch is what is left of the total once both known stretches are taken away, so write it as one expression with two subtractions.

    2. Hint 2

      The least common denominator of 666, 444 and 333 is 121212. Rewrite all three fractions over 121212, keeping x−6x - 6x−6 in parentheses.

    3. Hint 3

      The minus sign in front of x−63\tfrac{x - 6}{3}3x−6​ reaches both of its terms, so its −6-6−6 turns positive. After collecting, look for a factor the numerator shares with 121212.

    Answer

    x+84\tfrac{x + 8}{4}4x+8​ kilometers; at x=12x = 12x=12 both the formula and the stretches give 555 km.

    Full solution

    The three stretches add up to the total, so the third is the total minus the other two:

    5x6−x4−x−63.\frac{5x}{6} - \frac{x}{4} - \frac{x - 6}{3}.65x​−4x​−3x−6​.

    10x12−3x12−4(x−6)12.\frac{10x}{12} - \frac{3x}{12} - \frac{4(x - 6)}{12}.1210x​−123x​−124(x−6)​.

    Combine over the common denominator.

    The last minus sign reaches both terms of 4(x−6)4(x - 6)4(x−6), which is 4x−244x - 244x−24, so it contributes −4x+24-4x + 24−4x+24:

    10x−3x−4x+2412.\frac{10x - 3x - 4x + 24}{12}.1210x−3x−4x+24​.

    Collecting like terms, 10x−3x−4x10x - 3x - 4x10x−3x−4x is 3x3x3x, so the numerator is 3x+243x + 243x+24.

    It factors as 3(x+8)3(x + 8)3(x+8), and the 333 cancels with the 333 in 12=3⋅412 = 3 \cdot 412=3⋅4:

    3(x+8)12=x+84.\frac{3(x + 8)}{12} = \frac{x + 8}{4}.123(x+8)​=4x+8​.

    So the third stretch is x+84\tfrac{x + 8}{4}4x+8​ kilometers.

    Answer

    Taking two fractions away from a total is one subtraction over a common denominator, and each minus sign reaches every term of the numerator after it.

  8. Problem 8 Jordan's simplification

    Jordan simplifies 6x+142x\dfrac{6x + 14}{2x}2x6x+14​. He crosses out the xxx in 6x6x6x and the xxx in 2x2x2x, which leaves 6+142\dfrac{6 + 14}{2}26+14​, and he writes his answer as 101010.     Explain why crossing out the two copies of xxx is not a legal cancellation, and why his check at x=1x = 1x=1 could not catch the mistake. Then simplify the fraction correctly, and state the value of xxx that must be excluded.

    1. Hint 1

      Canceling divides the whole numerator and the whole denominator by the same factor. Ask whether xxx multiplies all of 6x+146x + 146x+14.

    2. Hint 2

      Try another value, such as x=2x = 2x=2, in the original fraction and compare it with 101010. Then compare Jordan's fraction 6+142\tfrac{6 + 14}{2}26+14​ with the original at x=1x = 1x=1.

    3. Hint 3

      To simplify correctly, take the greatest common factor out of 6x+146x + 146x+14 and look for the same factor in 2x2x2x.

    The xxx multiplies only the term 6x6x6x, not the whole numerator. His 6+142\tfrac{6 + 14}{2}26+14​ is the original with x=1x = 1x=1 put in, so a check at x=1x = 1x=1 had to agree. Correct: 3x+7x\tfrac{3x + 7}{x}x3x+7​ (or 3+7x3 + \tfrac{7}{x}3+x7​), for x≠0x \neq 0x=0.

    Full solution

    A cancellation divides the whole numerator and the whole denominator by the same factor, and it is legal because that factor over itself is 111.

    The xxx that Jordan crossed out on top multiplies only the term 6x6x6x.

    The 141414 has no factor of xxx, so xxx is not a factor of the whole numerator 6x+146x + 146x+14, and it cannot be canceled.

    Crossing it out changes the value.

    At x=2x = 2x=2 the original fraction is 12+144\tfrac{12 + 14}{4}412+14​, which is 264\tfrac{26}{4}426​, or 132\tfrac{13}{2}213​, but Jordan's answer is still 101010.

    A single value at which the two disagree proves they are not equal.

    His check could not catch this, because his fraction 6+142\tfrac{6 + 14}{2}26+14​ is exactly the original with 111 put in place of each xxx.

    So at x=1x = 1x=1 his answer and the original are the same number, 101010, and the check only compared that number with itself.

    An agreement at one value is evidence, not proof, and x=1x = 1x=1 is a poor test for a lost factor of xxx, since multiplying by 111 changes nothing.

  9. Problem 9 Two claims to test

  10. Problem 10 A fraction with one excluded value

    Write an algebraic fraction that is undefined at x=6x = 6x=6 and at no other value of xxx, and that simplifies to 2x−12x - 12x−1.

    Full solution

    Simplifying cancels a factor that the whole numerator and the whole denominator share.

    To build a fraction that simplifies to 2x−12x - 12x−1, start from 2x−12x - 12x−1 and put a shared factor in on purpose.

    The denominator must be zero at x=6x = 6x=6 and at no other value.

    Put x−6x - 6x−6 on the bottom, and 2x−12x - 12x−1 times x−6x - 6x−6 on the top:

    (2x−1)(x−6)x−6.\frac{(2x - 1)(x - 6)}{x - 6}.x−6(2x−1)(x−6)​.

    For x≠6x \neq 6x=6 the factor x−6x - 6x−6 is shared by the whole top and the whole bottom, so it cancels and leaves 2x−12x - 12x−1.
