  1. Problem 1 Collecting like terms

    Simplify 9k−4+2k2−11k+7−5k2+k9k - 4 + 2k^2 - 11k + 7 - 5k^2 + k9k−4+2k2−11k+7−5k2+k, and check your result by substituting k=2k = 2k=2 into both the original expression and your simplified one.

    1. Hint 1

      Only like terms can be merged, so sort the terms into families by their variable part, keeping each sign with the term that follows it.

    2. Hint 2

      There are three families: the k2k^2k2 terms, the kkk terms and the constants. The lone kkk at the end has coefficient 111.

    3. Hint 3

      Add the coefficients within each family, so the kkk terms give (9−11+1)k(9 - 11 + 1)k(9−11+1)k. At k=2k = 2k=2, square before multiplying: 2k22k^22k2 is 2⋅42 \cdot 42⋅4.

    Answer

    −3k2−k+3-3k^2 - k + 3−3k2−k+3; at k=2k = 2k=2 both expressions equal −11-11−11.

    Full solution

    Read off the terms with their signs attached: 9k9k9k, −4-4−4, 2k22k^22k2, −11k-11k−11k, 777, −5k2-5k^2−5k2 and kkk.

    Sort them by variable part into three families: the k2k^2k2 terms, the kkk terms and the constants.

    The k2k^2k2 terms are 2k22k^22k2 and −5k2-5k^2−5k2.

    Adding their coefficients gives

    2k2−5k2=(2−5)k2=−3k2.\begin{aligned} 2k^2 - 5k^2 &= (2 - 5)k^2 \\ &= -3k^2. \end{aligned}2k2−5k2​=(2−5)k2=−3k2.​

    The kkk terms are 9k9k9k, −11k-11k−11k and kkk, and the lone kkk has coefficient 111:

    9k−11k+k=(9−11+1)k=−k.\begin{aligned} 9k - 11k + k &= (9 - 11 + 1)k \\ &= -k. \end{aligned}9k−11k+k​=(9−11+1)k=−k.​

    A coefficient of −1-1−1 is written as the minus sign alone.

    The constants −4-4−4 and 777 add to 333.

    So the simplified expression is

    −3k2−k+3.-3k^2 - k + 3.−3k2−k+3.

    Its three terms have different variable parts, so nothing more combines.

    Check at k=2k = 2k=2.

    The terms of the original become 181818, −4-4−4, 888, −22-22−22, 777, −20-20−20 and 222, and these add to −11-11−11.

    The simplified form gives −3(4)−2+3-3(4) - 2 + 3−3(4)−2+3, which is also −11-11−11.

    The two agree, which is good evidence that no term was lost.

    Answer

    −3k2−k+3-3k^2 - k + 3−3k2−k+3; at k=2k = 2k=2 both expressions equal −11-11−11.

    Key idea

    Combine each family of like terms by adding its coefficients, counting a lone variable as having coefficient 111.

  2. Problem 2 Subtracting a whole expression

    Subtract (5y2−3y−6)(5y^2 - 3y - 6)(5y2−3y−6) from (2y2−7y+4)(2y^2 - 7y + 4)(2y2−7y+4) and simplify the result. Check it by substituting y=−1y = -1y=−1 into both the original difference and your answer.

    1. Hint 1

      Subtracting a whole expression means adding its opposite, so the minus sign reaches every term of the expression being subtracted. Take care over which expression comes first.

    2. Hint 2

      Subtracting AAA from BBB means B−AB - AB−A, so start from (2y2−7y+4)−(5y2−3y−6)(2y^2 - 7y + 4) - (5y^2 - 3y - 6)(2y2−7y+4)−(5y2−3y−6) and drop the second parentheses by flipping the sign of each of its three terms.

    3. Hint 3

      After the flips, the second group contributes −5y2-5y^2−5y2, +3y+3y+3y and +6+6+6. Collect the y2y^2y2 terms, the yyy terms and the constants. At y=−1y = -1y=−1, y2y^2y2 is 111.

    Answer

    −3y2−4y+10-3y^2 - 4y + 10−3y2−4y+10; at y=−1y = -1y=−1 the original difference and the result both equal 111111.

    Full solution

    Subtracting (5y2−3y−6)(5y^2 - 3y - 6)(5y2−3y−6) from (2y2−7y+4)(2y^2 - 7y + 4)(2y2−7y+4) means starting with 2y2−7y+42y^2 - 7y + 42y2−7y+4 and taking the other expression away:

    (2y2−7y+4)−(5y2−3y−6).(2y^2 - 7y + 4) - (5y^2 - 3y - 6).(2y2−7y+4)−(5y2−3y−6).

    The minus sign in front of the second group is a factor of −1-1−1, so it flips the sign of every term inside: 5y25y^25y2 becomes −5y2-5y^2−5y2, −3y-3y−3y becomes +3y+3y+3y, and −6-6−6 becomes +6+6+6.

    The difference is now a plain sum:

    2y2−7y+4−5y2+3y+6.2y^2 - 7y + 4 - 5y^2 + 3y + 6.2y2−7y+4−5y2+3y+6.

    Collect each family.

    The y2y^2y2 terms give 2y2−5y2=−3y22y^2 - 5y^2 = -3y^22y2−5y2=−3y2, the yyy terms give −7y+3y=−4y-7y + 3y = -4y−7y+3y=−4y, and the constants 444 and 666 give 101010.

    So the result is

    −3y2−4y+10.-3y^2 - 4y + 10.−3y2−4y+10.

    Check at y=−1y = -1y=−1, where y2=1y^2 = 1y2=1.

    The first expression is 2+7+42 + 7 + 42+7+4, which is 131313, and the second is 5+3−65 + 3 - 65+3−6, which is 222, so the original difference is 111111.

    The result gives −3(1)−4(−1)+10-3(1) - 4(-1) + 10−3(1)−4(−1)+10, which is also 111111.

    The two agree, which is good evidence that no sign was dropped.

    Answer

    −3y2−4y+10-3y^2 - 4y + 10−3y2−4y+10; at y=−1y = -1y=−1 the original difference and the result both equal 111111.

    Key idea

    Subtracting AAA from BBB means B−AB - AB−A, and the minus sign in front of AAA flips the sign of every one of its terms.

  3. Problem 3 Two products, then collect

    Simplify 5(2r−3)−3(4r−7)5(2r - 3) - 3(4r - 7)5(2r−3)−3(4r−7), and check your result by substituting r=−1r = -1r=−1 into both the original expression and your simplified one.

    1. Hint 1

      The number in front of each pair of parentheses multiplies every term inside, and its sign travels with it to each term.

    2. Hint 2

      The second product is multiplied by −3-3−3, not by 333, so both of its terms change sign. The −7-7−7 inside contributes +21+21+21.

    3. Hint 3

      Once both products are expanded, the expression is a plain sum: collect the rrr terms and the constants. At r=−1r = -1r=−1, work out each pair of parentheses first.

    Answer

    −2r+6-2r + 6−2r+6 (or 6−2r6 - 2r6−2r); at r=−1r = -1r=−1 both expressions equal 888.

    Full solution

    Distribute each factor across every term of its parentheses, keeping the signs.

    The first product gives

    5(2r−3)=10r−15.5(2r - 3) = 10r - 15.5(2r−3)=10r−15.

    The second factor is −3-3−3, so it multiplies both 4r4r4r and −7-7−7 by −3-3−3, and (−3)(−7)=21(-3)(-7) = 21(−3)(−7)=21:

    −3(4r−7)=−12r+21.-3(4r - 7) = -12r + 21.−3(4r−7)=−12r+21.

    The expression is now 10r−15−12r+2110r - 15 - 12r + 2110r−15−12r+21.

    The rrr terms give 10r−12r=−2r10r - 12r = -2r10r−12r=−2r, and the constants give −15+21=6-15 + 21 = 6−15+21=6, so the result is

    −2r+6.-2r + 6.−2r+6.

    Check at r=−1r = -1r=−1.

    The original is 5(−5)−3(−11)5(-5) - 3(-11)5(−5)−3(−11), which is −25+33-25 + 33−25+33, or 888.

    The result gives −2(−1)+6-2(-1) + 6−2(−1)+6, which is also 888.

    The two agree, which is good evidence that both products were distributed correctly.

    Answer

    −2r+6-2r + 6−2r+6 (or 6−2r6 - 2r6−2r); at r=−1r = -1r=−1 both expressions equal 888.

    Key idea

    A factor in front of parentheses multiplies every term inside, and a negative factor changes the sign of each one.

  4. Problem 4 Reading the variable parts

    Give the coefficient and the variable part of each term of 7ab2−a2b+3−ab2+4ba2−87ab^2 - a^2b + 3 - ab^2 + 4ba^2 - 87ab2−a2b+3−ab2+4ba2−8, and use them to simplify the expression.

    1. Hint 1

      Like terms need exactly the same letters raised to exactly the same powers, while the order the letters are written in does not matter. A term keeps the sign in front of it.

    2. Hint 2

      A term that shows no number has coefficient 111 or −1-1−1. Write each variable part with its letters in alphabetical order, so the part of 5yx25yx^25yx2 is x2yx^2yx2y.

    3. Hint 3

      Compare ab2ab^2ab2 and a2ba^2ba2b letter by letter: which letter is squared in each? Then add the coefficients within each family of like terms.

    Answer

    Coefficients, in order: 777, −1-1−1, 333, −1-1−1, 444, −8-8−8. Variable parts: ab2ab^2ab2, a2ba^2ba2b, none, ab2ab^2ab2, a2ba^2ba2b, none. Simplified: 6ab2+3a2b−56ab^2 + 3a^2b - 56ab2+3a2b−5.

    Full solution

    Read the terms with their signs attached: 7ab27ab^27ab2, −a2b-a^2b−a2b, 333, −ab2-ab^2−ab2, 4ba24ba^24ba2 and −8-8−8.

    The coefficient of each is its numerical factor, sign included.

    So 7ab27ab^27ab2 has coefficient 777 and variable part ab2ab^2ab2.

    The terms −a2b-a^2b−a2b and −ab2-ab^2−ab2 show no number, so each has coefficient −1-1−1; their variable parts are a2ba^2ba2b and ab2ab^2ab2.

    In 4ba24ba^24ba2 the coefficient is 444.

    Multiplication can be done in any order, so ba2ba^2ba2 is the same product as a2ba^2ba2b, and the variable part is a2ba^2ba2b.

    The numbers 333 and −8-8−8 are constant terms: each is its own coefficient, and neither has a variable part.

    The parts ab2ab^2ab2 and a2ba^2ba2b are different.

    The first is a⋅b⋅ba \cdot b \cdot ba⋅b⋅b, one aaa and two bbb's, while the second is a⋅a⋅ba \cdot a \cdot ba⋅a⋅b, two aaa's and one bbb.

    So the terms fall into three families: the ab2ab^2ab2 terms, the a2ba^2ba2b terms and the constants.

    Add the coefficients within each family.

    The ab2ab^2ab2 terms give 7−17 - 17−1, which is 666; the a2ba^2ba2b terms give −1+4-1 + 4−1+4, which is 333; and the constants 333 and −8-8−8 give −5-5−5.

    So the simplified expression is

    6ab2+3a2b−5.6ab^2 + 3a^2b - 5.6ab2+3a2b−5.

    Check at a=−1a = -1a=−1 and b=2b = 2b=2, not at a=1a = 1a=1, where a2a^2a2 and aaa are equal and a slip in a power of aaa would not show.

    The terms of the original become −28-28−28, −2-2−2, 333, 444, 888 and −8-8−8, which add to −23-23−23, and the simplified form gives −24+6−5-24 + 6 - 5−24+6−5, also −23-23−23.

    Answer

    Coefficients, in order: 777, −1-1−1, 333, −1-1−1, 444, −8-8−8. Variable parts: ab2ab^2ab2, a2ba^2ba2b, none, ab2ab^2ab2, a2ba^2ba2b, none. Simplified: 6ab2+3a2b−56ab^2 + 3a^2b - 56ab2+3a2b−5.

    Key idea

    Like terms need the same letters to the same powers; the order the letters are written in does not matter, but the powers do.

  5. Problem 5 Brackets inside brackets

    Simplify 3t−4[2t−3(t−2)]3t - 4[2t - 3(t - 2)]3t−4[2t−3(t−2)].

    1. Hint 1

      Nested grouping symbols are cleared from the inside out: finish the innermost parentheses completely before touching the brackets around them.

    2. Hint 2

    It turns −t-t−t into 4t4t4t and 666 into −24-24−24:

    −4(−t+6)=4t−24.-4(-t + 6) = 4t - 24.−4(−t+6)=4t−24.

    7t−24.7t - 24.7t−24.

    Inside the brackets, 2(3)−3(3−2)2(3) - 3(3 - 2)2(3)−3(3−2) is 6−36 - 36−3, or 333, so the original is 9−4(3)9 - 4(3)9−4(3), which is −3-3−3.

  6. Problem 6 What was taken away?

    Find the expression that must be subtracted from 3x2−4x+13x^2 - 4x + 13x2−4x+1 to leave x2+2x−6x^2 + 2x - 6x2+2x−6, and check your answer by carrying out the subtraction.

    1. Hint 1

      Think of the same question with numbers first: what must be subtracted from 101010 to leave 444? The relationship you use there holds for expressions too.

    2. Hint 2

      If subtracting EEE from AAA leaves BBB, then E=A−BE = A - BE=A−B. Here AAA is 3x2−4x+13x^2 - 4x + 13x2−4x+1 and BBB is x2+2x−6x^2 + 2x - 6x2+2x−6; put BBB in parentheses before you subtract it.

    3. Hint 3

      Subtracting BBB flips the sign of each of its terms. For the check, subtract your expression from 3x2−4x+13x^2 - 4x + 13x2−4x+1 in the same way and compare with x2+2x−6x^2 + 2x - 6x2+2x−6.

    Answer

    2x2−6x+72x^2 - 6x + 72x2−6x+7; the check: (3x2−4x+1)−(2x2−6x+7)(3x^2 - 4x + 1) - (2x^2 - 6x + 7)(3x2−4x+1)−(2x2−6x+7) is x2+2x−6x^2 + 2x - 6x2+2x−6.

    Full solution

    With numbers, the amount subtracted from 101010 to leave 444 is 666, found as 10−410 - 410−4.

    In the same way, if subtracting an expression EEE from 3x2−4x+13x^2 - 4x + 13x2−4x+1 leaves x2+2x−6x^2 + 2x - 6x2+2x−6, then EEE is the first expression minus the second, so EEE equals

    (3x2−4x+1)−(x2+2x−6).(3x^2 - 4x + 1) - (x^2 + 2x - 6).(3x2−4x+1)−(x2+2x−6).

    Drop the second parentheses, flipping the sign of each of its terms:

    E=3x2−4x+1−x2−2x+6.E = 3x^2 - 4x + 1 - x^2 - 2x + 6.E=3x2−4x+1−x2−2x+6.

    Collect each family.

    The x2x^2x2 terms give 3x2−x2=2x23x^2 - x^2 = 2x^23x2−x2=2x2, the xxx terms give −4x−2x=−6x-4x - 2x = -6x−4x−2x=−6x, and the constants 111 and 666 give 777.

    So

    E=2x2−6x+7.E = 2x^2 - 6x + 7.E=2x2−6x+7.

    Check by carrying out the subtraction, flipping each sign of EEE:

    (3x2−4x+1)−(2x2−6x+7)=3x2−4x+1−2x2+6x−7=x2+2x−6.\begin{aligned} &(3x^2 - 4x + 1) - (2x^2 - 6x + 7) \\ &= 3x^2 - 4x + 1 - 2x^2 + 6x - 7 \\ &= x^2 + 2x - 6. \end{aligned}​(3x2−4x+1)−(2x2−6x+7)=3x2−4x+1−2x2+6x−7=x2+2x−6.​

    That is exactly what was to be left, so the answer checks.

    Answer

    2x2−6x+72x^2 - 6x + 72x2−6x+7; the check: (3x2−4x+1)−(2x2−6x+7)(3x^2 - 4x + 1) - (2x^2 - 6x + 7)(3x2−4x+1)−(2x2−6x+7) is x2+2x−6x^2 + 2x - 6x2+2x−6.

    Key idea

    The expression that must be subtracted from AAA to leave BBB is A−BA - BA−B, and subtracting BBB flips the sign of each of its terms.

  7. Problem 7 Trimming a poster

    A rectangular poster is www cm wide and (3w+4)(3w + 4)(3w+4) cm tall, where www is at least 111. A strip 555 cm tall is cut off along its bottom edge, across the full width, so the poster keeps its width and becomes 555 cm shorter.

    Write the area of the trimmed poster as a simplified expression in square centimeters. Then find that area when w=12w = 12w=12, and check it by multiplying the trimmed poster's width by its height.

    1. Hint 1

      The area of a rectangle is its width times its height, and that stays true when a length is written with a letter. The strip that is cut off is a rectangle too.

    2. Hint 2

      The whole poster has area w(3w+4)w(3w + 4)w(3w+4) and the strip has area 5w5w5w. Distribute the www across both terms, remembering that w⋅3ww \cdot 3ww⋅3w is 3w23w^23w2.

    3. Hint 3

      Subtract the strip and collect the two www terms. For the check, the trimmed poster is 121212 cm wide, and its height is 3w+43w + 43w+4 with 555 taken off.

    Answer

    3w2−w3w^2 - w3w2−w square centimeters. At w=12w = 12w=12 the area is 420420420 square centimeters, matching the trimmed poster's 121212 cm by 353535 cm.

    Full solution

    The whole poster is a rectangle www cm wide and (3w+4)(3w + 4)(3w+4) cm tall, so its area is w(3w+4)w(3w + 4)w(3w+4) square centimeters.

    Distribute the www across both terms.

    Since w⋅3ww \cdot 3ww⋅3w is 3w23w^23w2, this gives

    w(3w+4)=3w2+4w.w(3w + 4) = 3w^2 + 4w.w(3w+4)=3w2+4w.

    The strip cut off is a rectangle www cm wide and 555 cm tall, so its area is 5w5w5w square centimeters.

    The trimmed poster is what remains, so subtract the strip and collect the www terms:

    3w2+4w−5w=3w2+(4−5)w=3w2−w.\begin{aligned} 3w^2 + 4w - 5w &= 3w^2 + (4 - 5)w \\ &= 3w^2 - w. \end{aligned}3w2+4w−5w​=3w2+(4−5)w=3w2−w.​

    The same area can be found directly.

    The trimmed poster is (3w+4)−5(3w + 4) - 5(3w+4)−5 cm tall, which is 3w−13w - 13w−1 cm, and w(3w−1)w(3w - 1)w(3w−1) is also 3w2−w3w^2 - w3w2−w.

    At w=12w = 12w=12 the area is 3(144)−123(144) - 123(144)−12, which is 420420420 square centimeters.

    Check: the trimmed poster is 121212 cm wide and 3(12)−1=353(12) - 1 = 353(12)−1=35 cm tall, and 12⋅3512 \cdot 3512⋅35 is 420420420.

    The two results agree.

    Answer

    3w2−w3w^2 - w3w2−w square centimeters. At w=12w = 12w=12 the area is 420420420 square centimeters, matching the trimmed poster's 121212 cm by 353535 cm.

    Key idea

    An area can be found as the whole minus the piece removed, or as the new width times the new height, and both routes simplify to the same expression.

  8. Problem 8 Two days of ticket sales

    A cinema charges 111111 dollars for an adult ticket and 666 dollars for a child ticket. On Saturday it sold aaa adult tickets and ccc child tickets, where ccc is at least 444. On Sunday it sold twice as many adult tickets as on Saturday, and 444 fewer child tickets than on Saturday.

    Write a simplified expression for Sunday's takings minus Saturday's takings, in dollars. Then say which of aaa and ccc your expression depends on, and explain why.

    1. Hint 1

      Each day's takings are the number of adult tickets times their price plus the number of child tickets times theirs. Write Sunday's ticket numbers in terms of aaa and ccc first.

    2. Hint 2

      Sunday sold 2a2a2a adult tickets and c−4c - 4c−4 child tickets, so its takings are 11(2a)+6(c−4)11(2a) + 6(c - 4)11(2a)+6(c−4). Subtract Saturday's takings as a whole, in parentheses.

    3. Hint 3

      Once the products are distributed and Saturday's signs are flipped, look at the two terms that contain ccc. What does each day's 6c6c6c stand for?

    Answer

    11a−2411a - 2411a−24 dollars. It depends on aaa only: the child-ticket terms 6c6c6c and −6c-6c−6c cancel, and Sunday's child tickets bring in 242424 dollars less than Saturday's, whatever ccc is.

    Full solution

    Saturday's takings are 11a11a11a dollars from adult tickets and 6c6c6c dollars from child tickets, so 11a+6c11a + 6c11a+6c dollars in all.

    On Sunday the cinema sold 2a2a2a adult tickets and c−4c - 4c−4 child tickets.

    Its takings are 11(2a)+6(c−4)11(2a) + 6(c - 4)11(2a)+6(c−4) dollars, and distributing gives 22a+6c−2422a + 6c - 2422a+6c−24 dollars.

    Subtract Saturday's takings as a whole, flipping the sign of each of its terms:

    (22a+6c−24)−(11a+6c)=22a+6c−24−11a−6c.\begin{aligned} &(22a + 6c - 24) - (11a + 6c) \\ &= 22a + 6c - 24 - 11a - 6c. \end{aligned}​(22a+6c−24)−(11a+6c)=22a+6c−24−11a−6c.​

    Collect like terms.

    The aaa terms give 22a−11a=11a22a - 11a = 11a22a−11a=11a, and the ccc terms give 6c−6c=06c - 6c = 06c−6c=0, which leaves

    11a−24.11a - 24.11a−24.

    So the expression depends on aaa only.

    The ccc terms cancel because both days' takings contain 6c6c6c.

    Sunday's child tickets are Saturday's with 444 taken away, and those 444 tickets are worth 6⋅4=246 \cdot 4 = 246⋅4=24 dollars however large ccc is.

    Each term of 11a−2411a - 2411a−24 has a meaning.

    Sunday sold aaa more adult tickets than Saturday, worth 11a11a11a dollars, and 444 fewer child tickets, worth 242424 dollars.

    Neither depends on ccc.

    Check with a=5a = 5a=5 and c=10c = 10c=10: Saturday takes 55+60=11555 + 60 = 11555+60=115 dollars and Sunday takes 110+36=146110 + 36 = 146110+36=146 dollars.

    The difference is 313131 dollars, and 11(5)−2411(5) - 2411(5)−24 is 313131 too.

    Answer

    11a−2411a - 2411a−24 dollars. It depends on aaa only: the child-ticket terms 6c6c6c and −6c-6c−6c cancel, and Sunday's child tickets bring in 242424 dollars less than Saturday's, whatever ccc is.

    Key idea

    When a whole expression is subtracted, terms can cancel, and a letter that cancels is a quantity the result does not depend on.

  9. Problem 9 A check that passed

    Priya simplifies 6−2(x−3)−(5−x)6 - 2(x - 3) - (5 - x)6−2(x−3)−(5−x). Her work reads

    6−2(x−3)−(5−x)=6−2x+6−5−x=−3x+7.\begin{aligned} &6 - 2(x - 3) - (5 - x) \\ &= 6 - 2x + 6 - 5 - x \\ &= -3x + 7. \end{aligned}​6−2(x−3)−(5−x)=6−2x+6−5−x=−3x+7.​

  10. Problem 10 Four claimed simplifications

    Each claim below says that its two sides are equal for every value of the letters in it.

    Claim A: 4m+3m2=7m34m + 3m^2 = 7m^34m+3m2=7m3

    Claim B: 6ab−2ba=4ab6ab - 2ba = 4ab6ab−2ba=4ab

    Claim C: 7−(k−3)=4−k7 - (k - 3) = 4 - k7−(k−3)=4−k

    Claim D: p(p+6)−6p=p2p(p + 6) - 6p = p^2p(p+6)−6p=p2

    Decide whether each claim is true. Show why each true claim holds for every value, using the laws of arithmetic, and disprove each false claim with a value that makes its two sides differ.

    1. Hint 1

      A claim about every value needs a reason that works for every value at once. A claim that fails needs only one value at which its two sides come out different.

    2. Hint 2

      Simplify the left side of each claim with the tools of this lesson, reading each variable part carefully, and compare the result with the right side.

    3. Hint 3

      When you test a value, do not stop at 000 or 111: they can make different powers of a letter look alike. Try 222 or −1-1−1 as well.

    Answer

    A: false (for example, at m=2m = 2m=2 the sides are 202020 and 565656). B: true. C: false (for example, at k=0k = 0k=0 the sides are 101010 and 444; any kkk works). D: true.

    Full solution

    A claim about every value is proved by rewriting one side into the other with laws that hold for every number.

    It is disproved by a single value at which the two sides differ.

    Claim A is false.

    The terms 4m4m4m and 3m23m^23m2 have different variable parts, mmm and m2m^2m2, so they cannot be combined by adding coefficients.

    At m=2m = 2m=2 the left side is 8+12=208 + 12 = 208+12=20 and the right side is 7⋅87 \cdot 87⋅8, which is 565656.

    The values m=0m = 0m=0 and m=1m = 1m=1 happen to make the two sides of claim A agree, at 000 and at 777.

    Those matches are coincidences, and the one mismatch at m=2m = 2m=2 settles the claim.

    Claim B is true.

    Multiplication can be done in either order, so ba=abba = abba=ab, and the two terms are like terms with variable part ababab:

    6ab−2ba=6ab−2ab=(6−2)ab=4ab.\begin{aligned} 6ab - 2ba &= 6ab - 2ab \\ &= (6 - 2)ab \\ &= 4ab. \end{aligned}6ab−2ba​=6ab−2ab=(6−2)ab=4ab.​

    Claim C is false.

    The minus sign flips both terms of (k−3)(k - 3)(k−3), so the left side is 7−k+37 - k + 37−k+3, which is 10−k10 - k10−k, not 4−k4 - k4−k.

    The claim forgot to flip the −3-3−3.

    At k=0k = 0k=0 the left side is 7−(−3)7 - (-3)7−(−3), which is 101010, and the right side is 444.

    In fact the two sides differ by 666 at every value of kkk.

    Claim D is true.

    Distributing the ppp across both terms gives

    p(p+6)=p2+6p.p(p + 6) = p^2 + 6p.p(p+6)=p2+6p.

    Then the 6p6p6p and the −6p-6p−6p cancel:

    p2+6p−6p=p2.p^2 + 6p - 6p = p^2.p2+6p−6p=p2.

    Answer

    A: false (for example, at m=2m = 2m=2 the sides are 202020 and 565656). B: true. C: false (for example, at k=0k = 0k=0 the sides are 101010 and 444; any kkk works). D: true.

    Key idea

    A claimed simplification is proved by laws that hold for every value and disproved by one value where its sides differ; matching values alone do not prove it.
