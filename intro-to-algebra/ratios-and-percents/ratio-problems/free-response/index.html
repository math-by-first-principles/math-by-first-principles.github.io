  1. Problem 1 The three teams

    A club's 120120120 members are split into teams A, B, and C in the ratio 4:7:94:7:94:7:9. Find the number of members on each team, and check that your counts reduce to the given ratio.

    1. Hint 1

      The ratio fixes how the teams compare, and the total fixes the size of one part.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the team sizes as 4s4s4s, 7s7s7s, and 9s9s9s, and set their sum equal to 120120120.

    3. Hint 3

      Solve for sss, then multiply it back through each of the three terms.

    Answer

    Team A has 242424 members, team B has 424242, and team C has 545454.

    Full solution

    Write the team sizes as 4s4s4s, 7s7s7s, and 9s9s9s, where sss is the number of members in one part.

    Every member is on a team, so the three sizes add to the total.

    4s+7s+9s=1204s+7s+9s=1204s+7s+9s=120

    Combine the like terms.

    20s=12020s=12020s=120

    Divide both sides by 202020.

    s=6s=6s=6

    Multiply back through each term: team A has 4⋅6=244\cdot 6=244⋅6=24 members, team B has 7⋅6=427\cdot 6=427⋅6=42, and team C has 9⋅6=549\cdot 6=549⋅6=54 members.

    Check: the counts add to 24+42+54=12024+42+54=12024+42+54=120 as stated, and dividing each count by their common factor 666 gives 4:7:94:7:94:7:9.

    Answer

    Team A has 242424 members, team B has 424242, and team C has 545454.

    Key idea

    Dividing a total by the number of parts gives the common multiplier, and each amount is its ratio number times that multiplier.

  2. Problem 2 The two ropes

    Two ropes have lengths in the ratio 11:611:611:6, and the longer rope is 353535 meters longer than the shorter one. Find the length of each rope, and check that your lengths reduce to the given ratio.

    1. Hint 1

      A difference between two amounts fixes the size of one part, just as a total does.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the lengths as 11s11s11s and 6s6s6s meters, and ask how many parts longer the longer rope is.

    3. Hint 3

      Set 11s−6s11s-6s11s−6s equal to 353535 and solve for sss.

    Answer

    The longer rope is 777777 meters long and the shorter rope is 424242 meters long.

    Full solution

    Write the lengths as 11s11s11s and 6s6s6s meters, where s>0s>0s>0 is the length of one part.

    The longer rope is 353535 meters longer, so the difference of the two lengths is 353535.

    11s−6s=3511s-6s=3511s−6s=35

    Combine the like terms.

    5s=355s=355s=35

    Divide both sides by 555.

    s=7s=7s=7

    Multiply back: the longer rope is 11⋅7=7711\cdot 7=7711⋅7=77 meters and the shorter rope is 6⋅7=426\cdot 7=426⋅7=42 meters.

    Check: the difference is 77−42=3577-42=3577−42=35 meters as stated, and dividing both lengths by their common factor 777 gives 11:611:611:6.

    Answer

    The longer rope is 777777 meters long and the shorter rope is 424242 meters long.

    Key idea

    A known difference is the difference of the ratio numbers times one part, so it fixes the common multiplier as a total does.

  3. Problem 3 The trail mix batch

    A trail mix uses peanuts, raisins, and sunflower seeds in the ratio 5:2:35:2:35:2:3, measured in cups. One batch uses 141414 cups of raisins. Find the number of cups of peanuts and of sunflower seeds in the batch, and check that the three amounts reduce to the given ratio.

    1. Hint 1

      One known amount is enough to fix the size of one part, with no total needed.

    2. Hint 2

      Raisins are the 222 term of the ratio, so set 2s2s2s equal to 141414.

    3. Hint 3

      Multiply the part size by each of the other two ratio numbers.

    Answer

    353535 cups of peanuts and 212121 cups of sunflower seeds.

    Full solution

    Write the amounts as 5s5s5s cups of peanuts, 2s2s2s cups of raisins, and 3s3s3s cups of sunflower seeds.

    The batch uses 141414 cups of raisins, and raisins are the 222 term.

    2s=142s=142s=14

    Divide both sides by 222.

    s=7s=7s=7

    Multiply back: the batch uses 5⋅7=355\cdot 7=355⋅7=35 cups of peanuts and 3⋅7=213\cdot 7=213⋅7=21 cups of sunflower seeds.

    Check: dividing 353535, 141414, and 212121 by their common factor 777 gives 5:2:35:2:35:2:3.

    The whole batch is 35+14+21=7035+14+21=7035+14+21=70 cups, which also equals

    (5+2+3)s=10⋅7.(5+2+3)s=10\cdot 7.(5+2+3)s=10⋅7.

    Answer

    353535 cups of peanuts and 212121 cups of sunflower seeds.

    Key idea

    One known amount fixes the common multiplier, so every other amount follows without a total.

  4. Problem 4 The counted trays

    Cards are divided among three trays A, B, and C in the ratio 2:3:52:3:52:3:5. A count of A and B gives 303030 cards, and then four cards move from C to A. Find the original counts and the new ratio in simplest whole numbers.

    1. Hint 1

      Use the partial total to find the common part size.

    2. Hint 2

      The transfer increases A and decreases C by the same count.

    Answer

    Original: A has 121212 cards, B has 181818 cards, and C has 303030 cards. New ratio: A:B:C=8:9:13A:B:C=8:9:13A:B:C=8:9:13.

    Full solution

    Write the counts as 2s2s2s, 3s3s3s, and 5s5s5s.

    The counted trays give

    2s+3s=302s+3s=302s+3s=30 s=6s=6s=6

    Thus the original counts are 121212, 181818, and 303030.

    After the transfer they are 161616, 181818, and 262626.

    Their common factor 222 gives the new ratio 8:9:138:9:138:9:13.

    The total remains 606060, and the original counts reduce to 2:3:52:3:52:3:5.

    Answer

    Original: A has 121212 cards, B has 181818 cards, and C has 303030 cards. New ratio: A:B:C=8:9:13A:B:C=8:9:13A:B:C=8:9:13.

    Key idea

    A partial total fixes the common part size, and so every amount, before a transfer changes them.

  5. Problem 5 The shared collection

    A collection holds three kinds of folder, A, B, and C, with A:B=4:3A:B=4:3A:B=4:3 and A:C=2:5A:C=2:5A:C=2:5. There are 212121 more C folders than B folders. Find all three counts and check both given ratios.

    1. Hint 1

      The shared term is A, even though it comes first in both ratios.

    2. Hint 2

      Match the A terms, then express the difference between C and B in shared parts.

    Answer

    A: 121212 folders. B: 999 folders. C: 303030 folders.

    Full solution

    Scale A:C=2:5A:C=2:5A:C=2:5 by 222 to get A:C=4:10A:C=4:10A:C=4:10.

    This matches A in the other ratio, so

    A:B:C=4:3:10A:B:C=4:3:10A:B:C=4:3:10

    Let the counts be 4s4s4s, 3s3s3s, and 10s10s10s.

    The difference gives

    10s−3s=2110s-3s=2110s−3s=21 s=3s=3s=3

    The counts are 121212, 999, and 303030.

    Their difference is 30−9=2130-9=2130−9=21, while 12:912:912:9 reduces to 4:34:34:3 and 12:3012:3012:30 reduces to 2:52:52:5, checking both given ratios.

    Answer

    A: 121212 folders. B: 999 folders. C: 303030 folders.

    Key idea

    Match the quantity shared by two ratios before applying a difference or total.

  6. Problem 6 The exchanged counters

    A bag has red and blue counters in the ratio 5:35:35:3. Four red counters are taken out and replaced by four blue counters. The new ratio is 3:53:53:5. Find the original counts and verify both ratios.

    1. Hint 1

      One color loses exactly what the other gains.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the changed amounts as 5s−45s-45s−4 and 3s+43s+43s+4 before using the new ratio.

    Answer

    101010 red counters and 666 blue counters.

    Full solution

    The original counts are 5s5s5s and 3s3s3s, with s>0s>0s>0.

    Since 3s+4>03s+4>03s+4>0, the new ratio gives

    5s−43s+4=35\frac{5s-4}{3s+4}=\frac353s+45s−4​=53​

    Cross-multiplication yields

    5(5s−4)=3(3s+4)5(5s-4)=3(3s+4)5(5s−4)=3(3s+4)

    Expand both sides.

    25s−20=9s+1225s-20=9s+1225s−20=9s+12

    Collect the sss terms on the left and the numbers on the right.

    16s=3216s=3216s=32 s=2s=2s=2

    The original counts are 101010 and 666, reducing to 5:35:35:3.

    After the exchange they are 666 and 101010, reducing to 3:53:53:5.

    All counts are whole and positive.

    Answer

    101010 red counters and 666 blue counters.

    Key idea

    An exchange changes both actual amounts, so both terms must be adjusted before the new ratio is imposed.

  7. Problem 7 The two batches

    Batch A contains red and white beads in the ratio 2:32:32:3 and has 252525 beads. Batch B has the same color ratio and contains 999 white beads. The batches are combined. Find the combined red and white counts and the ratio of red to white in the combined batch, in simplest form.

    1. Hint 1

      Each batch has its own part size, although the two color comparisons match.

    2. Hint 2

      Find each part size before adding matching colors.

    Answer

    161616 red beads and 242424 white beads; ratio 2:32:32:3.

    Full solution

    For batch A, five parts make 252525, so each part is 555 beads.

    It has 101010 red and 151515 white.

    For batch B, three white parts make 999, so each part is 333 beads.

    It has 666 red and 999 white.

    The combined counts are

    10+6=1610+6=1610+6=16 15+9=2415+9=2415+9=24

    Since 16:2416:2416:24 reduces to 2:32:32:3, the combined batch keeps the common ratio.

    Answer

    161616 red beads and 242424 white beads; ratio 2:32:32:3.

    Key idea

    Batches with the same ratio can have different part sizes and still preserve that ratio when combined.

  8. Problem 8 The doubled amounts

    Two positive amounts are in the ratio 4:74:74:7. Inez doubles both amounts and says the difference between them stays unchanged because the ratio stays unchanged. Is she correct? Explain using a common multiplier.

    1. Hint 1

      A ratio and a difference measure different features of a pair.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the original amounts as 4s4s4s and 7s7s7s, then compare their differences before and after doubling.

    Answer

    No; the difference doubles.

    Full solution

    With s>0s>0s>0, the original difference is

    7s−4s=3s7s-4s=3s7s−4s=3s

    After doubling, the difference is

    14s−8s=6s14s-8s=6s14s−8s=6s

    This is twice the original difference.

    The ratio is still 4:74:74:7, but the actual size of each part has doubled.

    Since the original difference is positive, it is not unchanged.

    Answer

    No; the difference doubles.

    Key idea

    Scaling preserves a ratio while scaling the difference between the actual amounts.

  9. Problem 9 The reported excess

    Three positive amounts have ratio 2:5:62:5:62:5:6. A report says the largest exceeds the sum of the other two by 444. Can this report be correct?

    1. Hint 1

      Compare the ratio parts before looking for their common size.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the three amounts as 2s2s2s, 5s5s5s, 6s6s6s and turn the report into an equation in sss.

    Answer

    No.

    Full solution

    Write the amounts as 2s2s2s, 5s5s5s, and 6s6s6s, where s>0s>0s>0.

    The reported difference would be

    6s−(2s+5s)=46s-(2s+5s)=46s−(2s+5s)=4 −s=4-s=4−s=4

    This would require a negative part size.

    In fact the largest amount is one positive part smaller than the sum of the other two, so it cannot exceed that sum.

    Answer

    No.

    Key idea

    Comparing the ratio parts can reveal an impossible condition before actual amounts are found.

  10. Problem 10 The equal additions

    Two positive amounts have ratio a:ba:ba:b, where aaa and bbb are positive. Each amount is increased by the same positive amount hhh. A learner claims the ratio must stay a:ba:ba:b. Decide exactly when the claim is true and justify your answer.

    1. Hint 1

      Write the original amounts with one positive multiplier.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare the adjusted ratio with a:ba:ba:b by cross-multiplying.

    Answer

    The ratio stays a:ba:ba:b exactly when a=ba=ba=b.

    Full solution

    Write the original amounts as asasas and bsbsbs, where s>0s>0s>0.

    After the increase the amounts are as+has+has+h and bs+hbs+hbs+h.

    For the ratio to stay a:ba:ba:b,

    as+hbs+h=ab\frac{as+h}{bs+h}=\frac{a}{b}bs+has+h​=ba​

    Both denominators are positive, so cross-multiply.

    b(as+h)=a(bs+h)b(as+h)=a(bs+h)b(as+h)=a(bs+h)

    Expand both sides.

    abs+bh=abs+ahabs+bh=abs+ahabs+bh=abs+ah

    Subtract absabsabs from both sides.

    bh=ahbh=ahbh=ah

    Since h>0h>0h>0, division by hhh gives a=ba=ba=b.

    Conversely, when a=ba=ba=b, the original amounts are equal and adding the same amount keeps them equal.

    Both ratios are then 1:11:11:1.

    Answer

    The ratio stays a:ba:ba:b exactly when a=ba=ba=b.

    Key idea

    Adding the same positive amount preserves a positive two-term ratio precisely when its two amounts are equal.
