  1. 1

    The equation y=18xy = \dfrac{18}{x}y=x18​ describes which kind of relationship?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The variable xxx sits in the denominator, matching the form y=kxy = \dfrac{k}{x}y=xk​ with k=18k = 18k=18.

    xy=18xy = 18xy=18

    The product is constant, which is inverse variation.

    In a direct variation, if xxx is doubled, what happens to yyy?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Direct variation is y=kxy = kxy=kx. Replacing xxx with 2x2x2x gives k(2x)=2(kx)k(2x) = 2(kx)k(2x)=2(kx).

    ynew=2kx=2yy_{\text{new}} = 2kx = 2yynew​=2kx=2y

    So yyy doubles.

  4. 4

    Which equation shows that yyy is directly proportional to xxx?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Direct proportion means yyy is a constant multiple of xxx.

    y=kxy = kxy=kx

    Adding or dividing would not keep the ratio y/xy/xy/x constant.

  5. 5

    Which equation shows that yyy is inversely proportional to xxx?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Inverse proportion puts xxx in the denominator so the product stays constant.

    y=kx⇒xy=ky = \frac{k}{x} \quad\Rightarrow\quad xy = ky=xk​⇒xy=k

  10. 10

    Which real situation is an example of inverse variation?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    In inverse variation, one quantity rises as the other falls, with a constant product. More workers on a fixed job means less time, and (workers) times (time) is the fixed amount of work.

    (workers)×(time)=constant(\text{workers}) \times (\text{time}) = \text{constant}(workers)×(time)=constant

    The other three rise together, which is direct variation.

    Then y=18x=183=6y = \dfrac{18}{x} = \dfrac{18}{3} = 6y=x18​=318​=6.

    A relationship keeps the ratio y/xy/xy/x constant. This tells you the relationship is:

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution
