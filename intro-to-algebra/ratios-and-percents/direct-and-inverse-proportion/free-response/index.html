  1. Problem 1 The recorded equation

    Positive readings satisfy 3y=7x3y=7x3y=7x. Write the relationship as y=kxy=kxy=kx and identify kkk.

    1. Hint 1

      Direct variation expresses one reading as a fixed multiple of the other.

    2. Hint 2

      Divide both sides by the coefficient of yyy.

    Answer

    y=73xy=\frac73xy=37​x; k=73k=\frac73k=37​.

    Full solution

    Divide both sides by the nonzero number 333.

    y=73xy=\frac73xy=37​x

    Thus the constant of proportionality is 7/37/37/3.

    For every positive xxx, the ratio y/xy/xy/x is 7/37/37/3, which checks that the relationship is direct variation.

    Answer

    y=73xy=\frac73xy=37​x; k=73k=\frac73k=37​.

    Key idea

    A constant ratio can be present even when an equation is not initially solved for its output.

  2. Problem 2 The product record

    Positive readings satisfy xy5=9\frac{xy}{5}=95xy​=9. Write yyy in terms of xxx in the form y=kxy=\frac{k}{x}y=xk​ and identify kkk.

    1. Hint 1

      Inverse variation keeps the product of the readings fixed.

    2. Hint 2

      Clear the constant denominator before dividing by positive xxx.

    Answer

    y=45xy=\frac{45}{x}y=x45​; k=45k=45k=45.

    Full solution

    Multiply both sides by 555.

    xy=45xy=45xy=45

    Since x>0x>0x>0, division by xxx is allowed.

    y=45xy=\frac{45}{x}y=x45​

    The product of xxx and its matching yyy is 454545, so k=45k=45k=45 is the inverse-variation constant.

    Answer

    y=45xy=\frac{45}{x}y=x45​; k=45k=45k=45.

    Key idea

    Clearing a numerical denominator can expose the fixed product in an inverse relationship.

  3. Problem 3 The juice bottles

    A fixed volume of juice exactly fills 444444 bottles that each hold 0.750.750.75 liters. The number of bottles it fills varies inversely with the size of each bottle. Find the constant of proportionality, with its unit, and how many 0.60.60.6 liter bottles the same juice exactly fills.

    1. Hint 1

      In inverse variation the product of the two quantities stays fixed.

    2. Hint 2

      Multiply the known number of bottles by the known size to get the constant, then use it for the new size.

    Answer

    k=33k=33k=33 liters; 555555 bottles.

    Full solution

    Let nnn be the number of bottles and sss the size of each bottle in liters.

    Inverse variation means ns=kns=kns=k.

    The known pair gives

    k=44(0.75)k=44(0.75)k=44(0.75) k=33k=33k=33

    The constant is the total volume of juice, 333333 liters.

    For bottles of 0.60.60.6 liters,

    n=330.6n=\frac{33}{0.6}n=0.633​ n=55n=55n=55

    The check is 55(0.6)=3355(0.6)=3355(0.6)=33, the same volume.

    Smaller bottles need more of them, as inverse variation predicts.

    Answer

    k=33k=33k=33 liters; 555555 bottles.

    Key idea

    In inverse variation the constant is the fixed product, here the total volume, and it predicts every other matching value.

  4. Problem 4 The incomplete table

    The figure shows a table for a relationship known to be either direct or inverse variation for positive inputs. Complete the two blank entries, identify the kind of variation, and state its equation.

    Text description of this figure

    A table with two columns, headed x and y, and four rows of equal height, every cell outlined. Row one: x is 3 and y is 30. Row two: x is 5 and y is 18. Row three: x is 9 and the y cell is empty. Row four: the x cell is empty and y is 6. No other columns, labels or equations are shown.

    1. Hint 1

      Use the complete rows to test a repeated ratio and a repeated product.

    2. Hint 2

      Once the constant is known, each incomplete row must give that same ratio or product.

    Answer

    Blank y=10y=10y=10; blank x=15x=15x=15; inverse variation, y=90xy=\frac{90}{x}y=x90​.

    Full solution

    The complete rows are (3,30)(3,30)(3,30) and (5,18)(5,18)(5,18).

    Their ratios are 101010 and 3.63.63.6, which differ.

    Their products are both 909090, so the relationship is inverse with

    y=90xy=\frac{90}{x}y=x90​

    At x=9x=9x=9,

    y=909y=\frac{90}{9}y=990​ y=10y=10y=10

    When y=6y=6y=6,

    x=906x=\frac{90}{6}x=690​ x=15x=15x=15

    Both completed rows have product 909090.

    Answer

    Blank y=10y=10y=10; blank x=15x=15x=15; inverse variation, y=90xy=\frac{90}{x}y=x90​.

    Key idea

    Complete rows identify a variation constant that then fills incomplete rows.

  5. Problem 5 The added material

    The mass of a piece of uniform steel rod varies directly with its length. Adding 555 meters of the same rod to one piece increases its mass by 444 kg. Find the proportionality constant in kg per meter and the mass of a 121212 meter piece.

    1. Hint 1

      The added length has the same mass per meter as the original piece.

    2. Hint 2

      Call the original piece's length xxx meters, and write the difference between the masses of lengths x+5x+5x+5 and xxx using one constant.

    Answer

    0.80.80.8 kg per meter, or 45\frac4554​ kg per meter; 9.69.69.6 kg.

    Full solution

    Let m=kxm=kxm=kx describe mass in kg for length xxx in meters.

    With the original piece xxx meters long, the increase gives

    k(x+5)−kx=4k(x+5)-kx=4k(x+5)−kx=4 5k=45k=45k=4 k=0.8k=0.8k=0.8

    For twelve meters,

    m=0.8(12)m=0.8(12)m=0.8(12) m=9.6m=9.6m=9.6

    The added five meters have mass 0.8(5)=40.8(5)=40.8(5)=4 kg, checking the reported change.

    Answer

    0.80.80.8 kg per meter, or 45\frac4554​ kg per meter; 9.69.69.6 kg.

    Key idea

    A difference in direct-variation measurements can reveal the same constant as a complete pair.

  6. Problem 6 The production setting

    Output qqq varies jointly with positive settings aaa and bbb. When a=2a=2a=2 and b=5b=5b=5, the output is 303030. Find the value of bbb that gives output 363636 when a=4a=4a=4.

    1. Hint 1

      Joint variation uses the product of the two inputs.

    2. Hint 2

      Find the constant from the complete record before solving for the new setting.

    Answer

    b=3b=3b=3.

    Full solution

    Write q=kabq=kabq=kab.

    The complete record gives

    30=k(2)(5)30=k(2)(5)30=k(2)(5) k=3k=3k=3

    For the required output,

    36=3(4)b36=3(4)b36=3(4)b 36=12b36=12b36=12b b=3b=3b=3

    The check is

    3(4)(3)=36.3(4)(3)=36.3(4)(3)=36.

    All settings are positive as required.

    Answer

    b=3b=3b=3.

    Key idea

    A joint-variation rule can be solved backward for a missing input after its constant is found.

  7. Problem 7 The unchanged output

    Positive output yyy varies directly with xxx and inversely with zzz. A record with x=4x=4x=4 and z=3z=3z=3 has y=20y=20y=20. A new record must keep y=20y=20y=20 while increasing xxx to 101010. Find the required zzz and write the variation equation with its constant filled in.

    1. Hint 1

      In combined variation, the ratio of the direct input to the inverse input controls the output.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the complete record in y=kx/zy=kx/zy=kx/z, then solve for the new denominator.

    Answer

    z=7.5z=7.5z=7.5, or 152\frac{15}{2}215​; y=15xzy=\frac{15x}{z}y=z15x​.

    Full solution

    The record gives

    20=4k320=\frac{4k}{3}20=34k​ k=15k=15k=15

    Thus y=15x/zy=15x/zy=15x/z, with z>0z>0z>0.

    The new condition gives

    20=150z20=\frac{150}{z}20=z150​ 20z=15020z=15020z=150 z=7.5z=7.5z=7.5

    Both input ratios are 4/34/34/3 and 10/7.5=4/310/7.5=4/310/7.5=4/3, so the output is unchanged.

    Substituting gives 15(10)/7.5=2015(10)/7.5=2015(10)/7.5=20, as required.

    Answer

    z=7.5z=7.5z=7.5, or 152\frac{15}{2}215​; y=15xzy=\frac{15x}{z}y=z15x​.

    Key idea

    To hold a combined-variation output fixed, the direct and inverse inputs must be scaled by the same factor, so their ratio stays the same.

  8. Problem 8 The rising entries

    The table in the figure has positive xxx and yyy values that both increase down the rows. Ana claims this alone shows direct variation. Decide whether the displayed data fit direct variation, inverse variation, or neither, and explain.

    Text description of this figure

    A table with two columns, headed x and y, and three rows of equal height, every cell outlined. Row one: x is 2 and y is 7. Row two: x is 4 and y is 14. Row three: x is 6 and y is 24. No other columns, labels or highlighting are shown.

    1. Hint 1

      Increasing together is not the definition of direct variation.

    2. Hint 2

      Test the ratios and products across all three rows.

    Answer

    Neither; the claim is false.

    Full solution

    The ratios y/xy/xy/x in the table are 3.53.53.5, 3.53.53.5, and 444.

    They are not all equal, so the data do not fit direct variation.

    The products are 141414, 565656, and 144144144, also not equal.

    Thus the data do not fit inverse variation either.

    Both quantities increasing is not enough to establish a fixed ratio.

    Answer

    Neither; the claim is false.

    Key idea

    The ratio or product must repeat across the data, not merely follow a general increasing or decreasing trend.

  9. Problem 9 The shared product

    Two positive pairs (a,b)(a,b)(a,b) and (c,d)(c,d)(c,d) have both the same ratio of second coordinate to first coordinate and the same product of their coordinates. Lee claims the two pairs must be identical. Is this true? Justify the decision.

    1. Hint 1

      Use the common ratio to write each second coordinate as the same multiple of its first.

    2. Hint 2

      The common products then compare the squares of the two positive first coordinates.

    Answer

    Yes; a=ca=ca=c and b=db=db=d.

    Full solution

    Let the shared positive ratio be kkk.

    Then b=kab=kab=ka and d=kcd=kcd=kc.

    Equal products give

    ka2=kc2ka^2=kc^2ka2=kc2

    Since k>0k>0k>0, divide by kkk to get

    a2=c2a^2=c^2a2=c2

    Both aaa and ccc are positive, so they are the same positive square root and a=ca=ca=c.

    Then b=ka=kc=db=ka=kc=db=ka=kc=d.

    Thus Lee is correct.

    Answer

    Yes; a=ca=ca=c and b=db=db=d.

    Key idea

    For positive coordinates, a shared ratio and a shared product determine the pair uniquely.

  10. Problem 10 The unit labels

    A direct relationship is written y=2xy=2xy=2x when xxx measures length in meters. Let uuu be the numerical measure of the same length in centimeters, with 111 meter equal to 100100100 centimeters. Bo claims the same equation becomes y=2uy=2uy=2u. Decide whether this is correct and give the equation in uuu.

    1. Hint 1

      The numerical measure changes when its unit changes.

    2. Hint 2

      Express the meter measure xxx in terms of the centimeter measure uuu.

    Answer

    No; y=0.02uy=0.02uy=0.02u, or y=u50y=\frac{u}{50}y=50u​.

    Full solution

    Since there are one hundred centimeters per meter,

    x=u100x=\frac{u}{100}x=100u​

    Substituting into the original relationship gives

    y=2(u100)y=2\left(\frac{u}{100}\right)y=2(100u​) y=0.02uy=0.02uy=0.02u

    For example, one meter corresponds to u=100u=100u=100 and gives y=2y=2y=2 under both correct forms.

    The claimed form would give 200200200, so it is incorrect.

    Answer

    No; y=0.02uy=0.02uy=0.02u, or y=u50y=\frac{u}{50}y=50u​.

    Key idea

    A proportionality constant depends on the units used for its numerical input.
