    How many centimeters are in 555 meters? Use 1 m=100 cm1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm}1 m=100 cm.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Meters should cancel, so put meters in the denominator of the factor.

    5 m×100 cm1 m=5×100=500 cm5 \text{ m} \times \dfrac{100 \text{ cm}}{1 \text{ m}} = 5 \times 100 = 500 \text{ cm}5 m×1 m100 cm​=5×100=500 cm

    So 555 meters is 500500500 centimeters.
