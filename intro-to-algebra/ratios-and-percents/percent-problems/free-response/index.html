    1. The one equation, three unknowns . Foundational, 9 points. Question 1 of 5.

    Foundational 9 pts

    Every percent question is the same relationship, part equals rate times whole, p=rwp = rwp=rw, just solved for a different letter. This question runs that equation two ways, once for the part and once for the whole, and then asks what decides which operation you use.

    1. A
      Part A.

      Find the part: 24%24\%24% of 175175175.

      Solve and show your work Write each step out, and end with the value and its units. 3 points

    2. B
      Part B.

      A part of 515151 is 12%12\%12% of an unknown whole. Find the whole.

      Solve and show your work Write each step out, and end with the value and its units. 3 points

    3. C
      Part C.

      Parts A and B both start from p=rwp = rwp=rw, yet one multiplies by the rate and the other divides by it. Explain the rule that decides which operation to use, stated in terms of which of the three quantities is missing.

      Explain why it works A sentence or two. Reasons, not steps. 3 points

    Hints

    One at a time, each one a step further than the last. Take only as many as you need.

    1. Hint 1 of 4

      Every percent question boils down to one equation, part equals rate times whole. Before doing any arithmetic, decide which of the three quantities you are given and which one you are missing.

    2. Hint 2 of 4 · Part A

      Convert the percent to a decimal first. Once that is done, this part is a single multiplication with nothing left to isolate.

    3. Hint 3 of 4 · Part B

      Write the sentence part equals rate times whole with the rate and the part filled in, and notice that the whole is now multiplied by a known decimal.

    4. Hint 4 of 4 · Part C

      Look at whether the missing letter in each part sat alone already, or was multiplied by something else, and let that decide the operation rather than the words of the problem.

    That is every hint for this question.

    Answer and solution

    Check your answer first. If it is wrong, go back to your paper: the worked solution will still be here.

    The answer

    Part A

    424242.

    Part B

    425425425.

    Part C

    Multiply by the rate when the whole is known and the part is missing; divide by the rate when the part is known and the whole is missing. Whichever letter sits alone gets solved for, and whichever letter is multiplied by a known number gets divided out.

    Not what you got? Look for the slip on your own paper before you open the solution. Finding it yourself is worth more than reading it.

    Worked solution

    Part A

    Convert the percent to a decimal rate, 24%=0.2424\% = 0.2424%=0.24, and substitute into p=rwp = rwp=rw with w=175w = 175w=175:

    p=0.24×175=42.p = 0.24 \times 175 = 42.p=0.24×175=42.

    So 24%24\%24% of 175175175 is 424242.

    Part B

    The rate and the part are known, and the whole is unknown. Substitute into p=rwp = rwp=rw:

    51=0.12 w.51 = 0.12\,w.51=0.12w.

    Divide both sides by the rate:

    w=510.12=425.w = \frac{51}{0.12} = 425.w=0.1251​=425.

    Check it forward: 12%12\%12% of 425425425 is 0.12×425=510.12 \times 425 = 510.12×425=51, the part given.

    Part C

    In p=rwp = rwp=rw, whichever letter is missing is isolated by ordinary algebra, and that choice is what decides the operation.

    p=rw  ⟹  p=rw (direct),w=pr (divide).p = rw \;\Longrightarrow\; p = rw \text{ (direct)}, \qquad w = \frac{p}{r} \text{ (divide)}.p=rw⟹p=rw (direct),w=rp​ (divide).

    In part A, www and rrr were both known and ppp was missing, so the equation was already solved for ppp: just multiply. In part B, ppp and rrr were known and www was missing, so www sat multiplied by rrr inside rw=prw = prw=p, and isolating www meant dividing both sides by rrr. The rule is not about the story in the problem, it is about which letter the algebra has left alone: whenever the missing letter is multiplied by a known one, undo that multiplication with a division.

    In one line

    24%24\%24% of 175175175 is 424242; a part of 515151 that is 12%12\%12% of a whole makes the whole 425425425; and whether you multiply or divide by the rate depends only on which of the three quantities, part, rate, or whole, is the one that is missing.

    2. From wholesale to retail, and back . Application, 10 points. Question 2 of 5.

    Application 10 pts

    A furniture wholesaler sells items to a retailer at a wholesale cost, and the retailer marks each one up 40%40\%40% to set the price a customer sees.

    1. A
      Part A.

      A marked-up bookshelf sells for 294294294 dollars. Write an equation for the wholesale cost ccc using the markup factor, and solve for ccc.

    Hints

    The answer

    Part A

    1.40 c=2941.40\,c = 2941.40c=294, so c=210c = 210c=210 dollars.

    The markup multiplied the cost by 1.401.401.40 to produce the shelf price, so undoing it takes the matching division by that factor. Subtracting 40%40\%40% of the shelf price treats the shelf price as the base, but the markup was always a percent of the wholesale cost, not of the shelf price.

    3. An estimate that used the right numbers and the wrong base . Application, 9 points. Question 3 of 5.

    Application 9 pts

    During a sale, a jacket marked "30%30\%30% off" is bought by a shopper named Priya for 565656 dollars. Wanting to know the original price, she reasons: "The discount was 30%30\%30%, so the original price must be 30%30\%30% more than what I paid." She computes 30%30\%30% of 565656 dollars, gets 16.8016.8016.80 dollars, adds it to the 565656 dollars she paid, and announces, "The original price was 72.8072.8072.80 dollars."

    1. A
      Part A.

    2. B
      Part B.

      Find the jacket's actual original price, and confirm it produces the 565656 dollar sale price under a genuine 30%30\%30% discount.

      Solve and show your work Write each step out, and end with the value and its units. 3 points

    3. C
      Part C.

      By how much did Priya's method miss the true original price, and is her number too high or too low? Explain why her method is guaranteed to land on that side, not just the wrong number.

      Carry your own answer forward Use whichever original price you found in part B, even if it is not the one intended: the point here is comparing your own number to Priya's 72.8072.8072.80 dollars and explaining the direction of the gap, not reproducing one particular figure.

      Explain what it means Words, not just symbols. Say what the number is telling you about the situation. 3 points

    Hints

    One at a time, each one a step further than the last. Take only as many as you need.

    1. Hint 1 of 4

      Every number Priya wrote down is arithmetically correct. The mistake is not a computation, it is a choice about which quantity the 30%30\%30% should have been taken of.

    2. Hint 2 of 4 · Part A

      Ask what the word "off" is measuring 30%30\%30% of: the price before the discount, or the price after it.

    3. Hint 3 of 4 · Part B

    4. Hint 4 of 4 · Part C

      Compare the two bases, the 565656 dollar sale price and the true original, and ask which one gives the bigger number when you take 30%30\%30% of it. That comparison alone tells you which direction the error has to run.

    That is every hint for this question.

    Answer and solution

    Check your answer first. If it is wrong, go back to your paper: the worked solution will still be here.

    The answer

    Part A

    The 30%30\%30% discount is a percent of the original price, not of the 565656 dollars she paid. Taking 30%30\%30% of the sale price uses the wrong base, because the sale price is what is left after the discount, not the amount the discount was measured against.

    Part B

    808080 dollars.

    Part C

    Priya's 72.8072.8072.80 dollars is 7.207.207.20 dollars too low. Her method is guaranteed to undershoot, because 30%30\%30% of the smaller sale price is always a smaller dollar amount than 30%30\%30% of the larger original price, so adding back too little can never reach the true original.

    Not what you got? Look for the slip on your own paper before you open the solution. Finding it yourself is worth more than reading it.

    Worked solution

    Part A

    A percent statement always names its base, the number the percent is a fraction of, and "30%30\%30% off" means 30%30\%30% of the original price is removed, so

    sale price=(1−0.30)×original,\text{sale price} = (1 - 0.30) \times \text{original},sale price=(1−0.30)×original,

    not the other way around. Priya took 30%30\%30% of 565656, the amount she paid after the discount, so her 16.8016.8016.80 is 30%30\%30% of the wrong number entirely. The original price is the base the 30%30\%30% was always measured against, and it is exactly the quantity she does not yet know, so it cannot also be the number she takes 30%30\%30% of.

    Part B

    A 30%30\%30% discount multiplies the original price by the factor 1−0.30=0.701 - 0.30 = 0.701−0.30=0.70, so with sale price 565656 and original PPP:

    0.70 P=56.0.70\,P = 56.0.70P=56.

    Divide by the factor:

    P=560.70=80.P = \frac{56}{0.70} = 80.P=0.7056​=80.

    Check it forward: 30%30\%30% of 808080 is 242424, and 80−24=5680 - 24 = 5680−24=56, the price Priya actually paid.

    Part C

    Compare your original price from part B to Priya's number: the gap is

    80−72.80=7.2080 - 72.80 = 7.2080−72.80=7.20

    dollars, so Priya's answer is too low. This is not a coincidence of these particular numbers. A percent is always a fraction of its base, so a bigger base gives a bigger dollar amount for the same rate. Since the 565656 dollar sale price is smaller than the true 808080 dollar original, 30%30\%30% of the sale price is necessarily smaller than 30%30\%30% of the original would have been. Adding back a too-small amount to 565656 can only land short of 808080, never past it, so the direction of Priya's error is forced by which of the two bases is larger, not by any arithmetic slip.

    In one line

    Priya's 72.8072.8072.80 dollars used the wrong base: her 30%30\%30% was taken of the 565656 dollars she paid, not of the original price. The true original price is 808080 dollars, found from 0.70 P=560.70\,P = 560.70P=56. Priya's answer is 7.207.207.20 dollars too low, and it has to be low, because 30%30\%30% of the smaller sale price is always less than 30%30\%30% of the true, larger original.
