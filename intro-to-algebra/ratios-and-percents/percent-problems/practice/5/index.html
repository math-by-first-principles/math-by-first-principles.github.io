    After a 20%20\%20% discount, a lamp costs 484848 dollars. What was the original price, in dollars?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A 20%20\%20% discount multiplies the original by 1−0.20=0.801 - 0.20 = 0.801−0.20=0.80, so 484848 is 0.800.800.80 of the original. Divide by the factor to undo it.

    0.80 w=48  ⟹  w=480.80=600.80\,w = 48 \implies w = \frac{48}{0.80} = 600.80w=48⟹w=0.8048​=60

    Adding 20%20\%20% of 484848 back gives 57.6057.6057.60, which is wrong.

    A store's cost is marked up 60%60\%60% to a selling price of 969696 dollars. What was the cost, in dollars?

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A price including 15%15\%15% tax is 929292 dollars. What was the price before tax, in dollars?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Tax multiplies the pre-tax price by 1+0.15=1.151 + 0.15 = 1.151+0.15=1.15, so divide the total by that factor.

    1.15 w=92  ⟹  w=921.15=801.15\,w = 92 \implies w = \frac{92}{1.15} = 801.15w=92⟹w=1.1592​=80

    A population grows 10%10\%10% in year one and 20%20\%20% in year two. What is the overall percent increase?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Multiply the growth factors 1.101.101.10 and 1.201.201.20.

    1.10×1.20=1.321.10 \times 1.20 = 1.321.10×1.20=1.32

    That is a 32%32\%32% increase, not the 30%30\%30% from adding the rates.

  5. 5

    A 200200200 dollar price rises 20%20\%20%, then that new price falls 20%20\%20%. What is the final price, in dollars?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Multiply the two factors, 1.201.201.20 for the rise and 0.800.800.80 for the fall.

    200×1.20×0.80=200×0.96=192200 \times 1.20 \times 0.80 = 200 \times 0.96 = 192200×1.20×0.80=200×0.96=192

    The equal rise and fall do not cancel, because the fall is taken of the larger raised price.

    A quantity is increased by 20%20\%20% and then by 30%30\%30%. By what single percent does it increase overall?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    That is a 56%56\%56% increase, not the 50%50\%50% you would get by adding the rates.
