  1. Problem 1 A new equation from old roots

    ★ Stretch Difficulty: 1 of 3 stars, Stretch

    The real numbers rrr and sss are the roots of 2x2−7x+3=02x^2-7x+3=02x2−7x+3=0. Define u=1/(r−1)u=1/(r-1)u=1/(r−1) and v=1/(s−1)v=1/(s-1)v=1/(s−1).
