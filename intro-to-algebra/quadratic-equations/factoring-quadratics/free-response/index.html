  1. Problem 1 A missing entry

    Complete the identity (x+□)(x+6)=x2+10x+24(x+\square)(x+6)=x^2+10x+24(x+□)(x+6)=x2+10x+24 with an integer.

    1. Hint 1

      The constant term comes from multiplying the two constant entries.

    2. Hint 2

      The missing entry must also make the two constants add to the coefficient of xxx.

    Answer

    444.

    Full solution

    Let the missing entry be ppp.

    The constant terms require

    6p=24,6p=24,6p=24,

    so p=4p=4p=4.

    The middle coefficient checks because 4+6=104+6=104+6=10.

    Thus (x+4)(x+6)(x+4)(x+6)(x+4)(x+6) expands to the required trinomial.

    Answer

    444.

    Key idea

    Both the constant term and the middle coefficient constrain a missing factor entry.

  2. Problem 2 Factor and check

    Factor x2−4x−32x^2-4x-32x2−4x−32, then check your factors by expanding them.

    1. Hint 1

      The sign of the constant term tells you whether the two entries share a sign.

    2. Hint 2

      List the pairs of whole numbers whose product is 323232, then attach signs so that the sum comes to −4-4−4.

    Answer

    (x−8)(x+4)(x-8)(x+4)(x−8)(x+4).

    Full solution

    The constant −32-32−32 is negative, so the two entries have opposite signs.

    Their sum is −4-4−4, which is negative, so the entry of greater size is the negative one.

    The size pairs with product 323232 are 111 and 323232, then 222 and 161616, then 444 and 888, and placing the minus sign on the larger member gives the sums −31-31−31, −14-14−14 and −4-4−4.

    Only the last pair fits, so the entries are −8-8−8 and 444.

    x2−4x−32=(x−8)(x+4)x^2-4x-32=(x-8)(x+4)x2−4x−32=(x−8)(x+4)

    Expanding rebuilds the original, since

    x2+4x−8x−32=x2−4x−32.x^2+4x-8x-32=x^2-4x-32.x2+4x−8x−32=x2−4x−32.

    Answer

    (x−8)(x+4)(x-8)(x+4)(x−8)(x+4).

    Key idea

    Read the sign of the constant term first, then let the middle coefficient decide where the minus sign goes.

  3. Problem 3 Solve and check

    Solve x2+9x+14=0x^2+9x+14=0x2+9x+14=0 by factoring, then check one of your roots by substitution.

    1. Hint 1

      Both the constant term and the middle coefficient are positive here, which fixes the sign of each entry.

    2. Hint 2

      Once the left side is written as a product, use the fact that a product is zero only when one of its factors is zero.

    Answer

    x=−2x=-2x=−2 or x=−7x=-7x=−7.

    Full solution

    The constant 141414 is positive, so the two entries share a sign, and the sum 999 is positive, so both are positive.

    The positive pairs with product 141414 are 111 and 141414, with sum 151515, and 222 and 777, with sum 999.

    The second pair fits.

    x2+9x+14=(x+2)(x+7)x^2+9x+14=(x+2)(x+7)x2+9x+14=(x+2)(x+7)

    A product is zero only when one factor is zero, so x+2=0x+2=0x+2=0 or x+7=0x+7=0x+7=0.

    The roots are x=−2x=-2x=−2 and x=−7x=-7x=−7, each the opposite sign of the number inside its factor.

    Substituting x=−2x=-2x=−2 confirms the first root, since 4−18+14=04-18+14=04−18+14=0.

    Answer

    x=−2x=-2x=−2 or x=−7x=-7x=−7.

    Key idea

    Once one side is a product and the other is zero, each factor set to zero contributes one root.

  4. Problem 4 Two ages from a product

    Mara is 333 years older than Kip, and the product of their ages in years is 888888. Find both ages, and say why the other root of your equation is not used.

    1. Hint 1

      Name Kip's age with a letter, then write Mara's age using that same letter.

    2. Hint 2

      Multiply the two ages, move every term to one side, and factor the trinomial that results.

    Answer

    Kip is 888 years old and Mara is 111111 years old; the negative root is rejected: an age is positive.

    Full solution

    Let Kip's age be kkk years, so Mara's age is k+3k+3k+3 years.

    Their product is 888888.

    k(k+3)=88k(k+3)=88k(k+3)=88

    Expanding and moving 888888 across gives a trinomial equal to zero.

    k2+3k−88=0k^2+3k-88=0k2+3k−88=0

    Two entries with product −88-88−88 and sum 333 are 111111 and −8-8−8.

    (k+11)(k−8)=0(k+11)(k-8)=0(k+11)(k−8)=0

    The roots are k=−11k=-11k=−11 and k=8k=8k=8.

    An age is positive, so the root −11-11−11 is not used, which leaves Kip at 888 and Mara at 111111.

    Their product is 8⋅118 \cdot 118⋅11, which is 888888 as required.

    Answer

    Kip is 888 years old and Mara is 111111 years old; the negative root is rejected: an age is positive.

    Key idea

    The context can rule out one algebraic root, so test every root against the conditions of the problem.

  5. Problem 5 A printing total

    A printer makes nnn identical sheets, where nnn is a whole number. The total charge is 3n2+12n3n^2+12n3n2+12n dollars. The total is 180180180 dollars. Find the number of sheets.

    1. Hint 1

      Put the charge equation in a form with zero on one side.

    2. Hint 2

      Remove the common numerical factor before looking for two integer factor entries.

    Answer

    666 sheets.

    Full solution

    The charge equation gives

    3n2+12n−180=0.3n^2+12n-180=0.3n2+12n−180=0.

    Dividing by 333 yields

    n2+4n−60=0.n^2+4n-60=0.n2+4n−60=0.

    The entries 101010 and −6-6−6 give

    (n+10)(n−6)=0.(n+10)(n-6)=0.(n+10)(n−6)=0.

    Thus n=−10n=-10n=−10 or n=6n=6n=6.

    Only n=6n=6n=6 satisfies n≥0n \ge 0n≥0, and

    3(6)2+12(6)=180.3(6)^2+12(6)=180.3(6)2+12(6)=180.

    Answer

    666 sheets.

    Key idea

    Removing a common numerical factor makes a quadratic easier to factor without changing its roots.

  6. Problem 6 Two payment rules

    For a whole number of units uuu, one plan charges u2+2uu^2+2uu2+2u dollars and another charges 9u9u9u dollars. Find every value of uuu for which the charges agree.

    1. Hint 1

      Two charges agree exactly at the values of uuu that satisfy one equation, so work with that equation rather than testing values one at a time.

    2. Hint 2

      Move every term to one side, then factor the left side completely rather than dividing through by anything.

    Answer

    u=0u=0u=0 or u=7u=7u=7.

    Full solution

    Equality of the charges gives

    u2+2u=9u.u^2+2u=9u.u2+2u=9u.

    Moving terms and factoring produces

    u2−7u=0,u^2-7u=0,u2−7u=0, u(u−7)=0.u(u-7)=0.u(u−7)=0.

    The values are u=0u=0u=0 and u=7u=7u=7.

    At zero both charges are zero; at seven both are 636363 dollars.

    Answer

    u=0u=0u=0 or u=7u=7u=7.

    Key idea

    Factoring out a shared variable preserves the zero root that division by the variable would lose.

  7. Problem 7 A one-entry repair

    A worksheet shows x2−5x−50=(x−10)(x+2)x^2-5x-50=(x-10)(x+2)x2−5x−50=(x−10)(x+2). Change exactly one constant entry in the two factors so that the identity becomes correct, and verify your repair by expansion.

    1. Hint 1

      The two entries must satisfy the required product and the required sum at the same time.

    2. Hint 2

      Keep one entry, work out what the other would have to be, then test that pair against both conditions.

    Answer

    Replace 222 with 555: (x−10)(x+5)(x-10)(x+5)(x−10)(x+5).

    Full solution

    The two entries need product −50-50−50 and sum −5-5−5, and the printed factorization fails both, since (x−10)(x+2)(x-10)(x+2)(x−10)(x+2) expands to x2−8x−20x^2-8x-20x2−8x−20.

    Keeping −10-10−10 forces the other entry to be 555, since −10-10−10 times 555 is −50-50−50, and that pair also has the sum −5-5−5.

    Expansion checks the repair.

    (x−10)(x+5)=x2−5x−50(x-10)(x+5)=x^2-5x-50(x−10)(x+5)=x2−5x−50

    Keeping 222 instead forces the other entry to be −25-25−25, whose sum with 222 is −23-23−23 rather than −5-5−5, so changing that entry alone cannot work.

    Answer

    Replace 222 with 555: (x−10)(x+5)(x-10)(x+5)(x−10)(x+5).

    Key idea

    A repair to a factorization must restore both the product and the sum of the two entries.

  8. Problem 8 An irreducibility claim

    Pat claims that for every even integer bbb, the trinomial x2+bx+33x^2+bx+33x2+bx+33 is irreducible over the integers. Decide whether the claim is correct and justify your answer.

    1. Hint 1

      A positive constant term means the two integer entries share a sign.

    2. Hint 2

      List the integer pairs whose product is 333333, find each pair's sum, then test those sums against the claim about every even bbb.

    Answer

    No; for example, x2+14x+33=(x+3)(x+11)x^2+14x+33=(x+3)(x+11)x2+14x+33=(x+3)(x+11).

    Full solution

    The positive integer pairs with product 333333 are 111 and 333333, with sum 343434, and 333 and 111111, with sum 141414.

    Their negative versions have sums −34-34−34 and −14-14−14.

    Each of those four sums is a value of bbb for which the trinomial does factor.

    Taking the pair 333 and 111111 gives a factorization.

    x2+14x+33=(x+3)(x+11)x^2+14x+33=(x+3)(x+11)x2+14x+33=(x+3)(x+11)

    Since 141414 is even, the claim fails at b=14b=14b=14, and one counterexample is enough to refute a claim made about every even bbb.

    Answer

    No; for example, x2+14x+33=(x+3)(x+11)x^2+14x+33=(x+3)(x+11)x2+14x+33=(x+3)(x+11).

    Key idea

    A claim about a whole family of coefficients is refuted by a single integer pair that fits.

  9. Problem 9 A coefficient family

    An integer bbb makes x2+bx+17x^2+bx+17x2+bx+17 factorable as (x+p)(x+q)(x+p)(x+q)(x+p)(x+q) with integers p,qp,qp,q. Find every possible value of bbb and explain why no other value works.

    1. Hint 1

      The constant term limits the possible integer factor entries.

    2. Hint 2

      List both the positive and negative pairs with product 171717, then find their sums.

    Answer

    The two values are b=18b=18b=18 and b=−18b=-18b=−18; (1,17)(1,17)(1,17) and (−1,−17)(-1,-17)(−1,−17) are the only integer pairs with product 171717.

    Full solution

    The integer pairs with product 171717 are (1,17)(1,17)(1,17) and (−1,−17)(-1,-17)(−1,−17), apart from reversing their order.

    Their sums are 181818 and −18-18−18.

    Thus the possibilities are

    x2+18x+17=(x+1)(x+17)x^2+18x+17=(x+1)(x+17)x2+18x+17=(x+1)(x+17)

    and

    x2−18x+17=(x−1)(x−17).x^2-18x+17=(x-1)(x-17).x2−18x+17=(x−1)(x−17).

    Every other integer bbb gives an irreducible trinomial over the integers because the complete pair list has no matching sum.

    Answer

    The two values are b=18b=18b=18 and b=−18b=-18b=−18; (1,17)(1,17)(1,17) and (−1,−17)(-1,-17)(−1,−17) are the only integer pairs with product 171717.

    Key idea

    Listing every integer pair for the constant term determines exactly which middle coefficients permit integer factors.

  10. Problem 10 A student's report

    A student factors x2−11x+30x^2-11x+30x2−11x+30 as (x−6)(x−5)(x-6)(x-5)(x−6)(x−5), then reports that the roots of x2−11x+30=0x^2-11x+30=0x2−11x+30=0 are −6-6−6 and −5-5−5. Decide whether the reported roots are correct, give the correct roots, and check one by substitution.

    1. Hint 1

      A root is a value that makes the trinomial equal to zero, so any reported root can be tested by substitution.

    2. Hint 2

      Set each factor equal to zero and solve it, rather than copying the numbers printed inside the factors.

    Answer

    The reported roots are not correct. The roots are x=6x=6x=6 and x=5x=5x=5.

    Full solution

    Expanding the proposed factors rebuilds the trinomial, so the factoring itself is correct.

    (x−6)(x−5)=x2−11x+30(x-6)(x-5)=x^2-11x+30(x−6)(x−5)=x2−11x+30

    A root is a value that makes one factor zero, so solve x−6=0x-6=0x−6=0 and x−5=0x-5=0x−5=0.

    That gives x=6x=6x=6 and x=5x=5x=5, each the opposite sign of the number printed inside its factor.

    Substituting x=6x=6x=6 gives 36−66+30=036-66+30=036−66+30=0, and substituting x=5x=5x=5 gives 25−55+30=025-55+30=025−55+30=0, so both are roots.

    The reported value −6-6−6 fails the same test, since 36+66+30=13236+66+30=13236+66+30=132, so the student copied the entries instead of solving each factor.

    Answer

    The reported roots are not correct. The roots are x=6x=6x=6 and x=5x=5x=5.

    Key idea

    The factor x−rx-rx−r is zero at x=rx=rx=r, so a root has the opposite sign of the entry written in its factor.
