  11. 11

    For what value of bbb do the roots of x2+bx+10x^2 + bx + 10x2+bx+10 sum to −7-7−7?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The sum of the roots is −b-b−b. Set it equal to −7-7−7.

    −b=−7 ⇒ b=7-b = -7 \ \Rightarrow \ b = 7−b=−7 ⇒ b=7

    So b=7b = 7b=7. The sum being negative does not make bbb negative here; the minus signs cancel.
