  1. Problem 1 The equation label

    The line y=2x+c−5y=2x+c-5y=2x+c−5 has y-intercept (0,−1)(0,-1)(0,−1). Find ccc.

    1. Hint 1

      The y-intercept is the output when the input is zero.

    2. Hint 2

      Match the whole constant term to the given intercept height.

    Answer

    c=4c=4c=4.

    Full solution

    At x=0x=0x=0, the equation gives

    y=c−5y=c-5y=c−5

    Thus the intercept information requires

    c−5=−1c-5=-1c−5=−1 c=4c=4c=4

    The resulting line is y=2x−1y=2x-1y=2x−1, whose output at zero is indeed −1-1−1.

    Answer

    c=4c=4c=4.

    Key idea

    The entire constant term in slope-intercept form gives the intercept height.

  2. Problem 2 A divided record

    Find the slope and y-intercept of the line 4(y+1)=12−5x4(y+1)=12-5x4(y+1)=12−5x.

    1. Hint 1

      Both pieces of information can be read once yyy stands alone.

    2. Hint 2

      Divide both sides by 4, then subtract the amount needed to isolate yyy.

    Answer

    Slope −54-\frac54−45​, or −1.25-1.25−1.25; y-intercept (0,2)(0,2)(0,2).

    Full solution

    Dividing both sides by 4 gives

    y+1=3−54xy+1=3-\frac54xy+1=3−45​x

    Subtracting 1 and ordering the terms gives

    y=−54x+2y=-\frac54x+2y=−45​x+2

    The slope is −5/4-5/4−5/4 and the intercept is (0,2)(0,2)(0,2).

    In the original equation, x=0x=0x=0 gives 4(y+1)=124(y+1)=124(y+1)=12, confirming y=2y=2y=2.

    Answer

    Slope −54-\frac54−45​, or −1.25-1.25−1.25; y-intercept (0,2)(0,2)(0,2).

    Key idea

    Read the slope and constant after all operations needed to isolate the output.

  3. Problem 3 One parameter

    For which values of bbb does y=bx−by=bx-by=bx−b pass through the origin?

    1. Hint 1

      A line through the origin must give output zero at input zero.

    2. Hint 2

      At x=0x=0x=0, inspect the constant term rather than the coefficient of xxx.

    Answer

    b=0b=0b=0 only.

    Full solution

    At the origin the equation becomes

    0=−b0=-b0=−b

    Thus b=0b=0b=0 is required.

    For b=0b=0b=0, the equation is y=0y=0y=0, which contains the origin.

    Answer

    b=0b=0b=0 only.

    Key idea

    Passing through the origin forces the constant term to be zero.

    Dividing by 2 gives

    y=32x−12y=\frac32x-\frac12y=23​x−21​

    The intercept is (0,−1/2)(0,-1/2)(0,−1/2).

    A move of 2 right and 3 up reaches (2,5/2)(2,5/2)(2,5/2); extend the line through those points.

    At the x-intercept,

    0=3x−10=3x-10=3x−1 x=13x=\frac13x=31​

    Thus the crossing is (1/3,0)(1/3,0)(1/3,0).

    The point (2,5/2)(2,5/2)(2,5/2) checks in the original equation since both sides are 5.

    Answer

    The y-intercept and a slope step locate the line, while the other crossing requires solving.

  5. Problem 5 A burning candle

    The height hhh cm of a candle ttt hours after it is lit satisfies 3t+2h=183t+2h=183t+2h=18 until it burns out. With ttt on the horizontal axis and hhh on the vertical axis, find the slope of this line, and both of its intercepts as ordered pairs (t,h)(t,h)(t,h). Say what each intercept means for the candle.

    1. Hint 1

      Treat ttt as the input and hhh as the output; solving for hhh exposes the slope and the vertical intercept.

    2. Hint 2

      The vertical intercept comes from t=0t=0t=0; the horizontal intercept comes from setting the height to zero.

    Answer

    Slope −1.5-1.5−1.5, or −32-\frac32−23​, cm per hour; vertical intercept (0,9)(0,9)(0,9): the candle is 9 cm tall when lit; horizontal intercept (6,0)(6,0)(6,0): it burns out 6 hours after it is lit.

    Full solution

    Subtracting 3t3t3t from both sides gives

    2h=18−3t2h=18-3t2h=18−3t

    Dividing both sides by 2 and ordering the terms gives

    h=−1.5t+9h=-1.5t+9h=−1.5t+9

    So the slope is −1.5-1.5−1.5 cm per hour: the candle gets 1.5 cm shorter each hour.

    At t=0t=0t=0 the height is 9, so the vertical intercept is (0,9)(0,9)(0,9).

    The candle is 9 cm tall at the moment it is lit.

    Setting h=0h=0h=0 in the original equation gives 3t=183t=183t=18, so t=6t=6t=6 and the horizontal intercept is (6,0)(6,0)(6,0).

    The height reaches zero after 6 hours, which is when the candle burns out.

    Both points check in the original equation, since 3(0)+2(9)3(0)+2(9)3(0)+2(9) and 3(6)+2(0)3(6)+2(0)3(6)+2(0) both equal 18.

    Answer

    Slope −1.5-1.5−1.5, or −32-\frac32−23​, cm per hour; vertical intercept (0,9)(0,9)(0,9): the candle is 9 cm tall when lit; horizontal intercept (6,0)(6,0)(6,0): it burns out 6 hours after it is lit.

    Key idea

    In a context, the vertical intercept is the output when the input is zero, and the horizontal intercept is the input at which the output reaches zero.

  6. Problem 6 A family record

    A family of lines has slope a−1a-1a−1, and each member passes through (1,2a+1)(1,2a+1)(1,2a+1), where aaa is real. Starting from point-slope form at that point, write the equation with yyy alone and read off its y-intercept. Then find the member whose x-intercept is (2,0)(2,0)(2,0).

    1. Hint 1

      Point-slope form needs one point and the slope, and here both are written in terms of aaa.

    2. Hint 2

      Distribute a−1a-1a−1, then move the constant across so yyy stands alone; an x-intercept at (2,0)(2,0)(2,0) means y=0y=0y=0 when x=2x=2x=2.

    Answer

    y=(a−1)x+a+2y=(a-1)x+a+2y=(a−1)x+a+2, with y-intercept (0,a+2)(0,a+2)(0,a+2); the member with x-intercept (2,0)(2,0)(2,0) has a=0a=0a=0, which is y=−x+2y=-x+2y=−x+2.

    Full solution

    Point-slope form at (1,2a+1)(1,2a+1)(1,2a+1) with slope a−1a-1a−1 reads

    y−(2a+1)=(a−1)(x−1)y-(2a+1)=(a-1)(x-1)y−(2a+1)=(a−1)(x−1)

    Distributing a−1a-1a−1 on the right gives

    y−(2a+1)=(a−1)x−a+1y-(2a+1)=(a-1)x-a+1y−(2a+1)=(a−1)x−a+1

    Adding 2a+12a+12a+1 to both sides gives

    y=(a−1)x+a+2y=(a-1)x+a+2y=(a−1)x+a+2

    Setting x=0x=0x=0 leaves y=a+2y=a+2y=a+2, so the y-intercept is (0,a+2)(0,a+2)(0,a+2).

    The x-intercept is (2,0)(2,0)(2,0) exactly when y=0y=0y=0 at x=2x=2x=2.

    Substituting gives

    2(a−1)+a+2=02(a-1)+a+2=02(a−1)+a+2=0

    The left side simplifies to 3a3a3a, so 3a=03a=03a=0 and a=0a=0a=0.

    With a=0a=0a=0 the equation is y=−x+2y=-x+2y=−x+2.

    It passes through (1,1)(1,1)(1,1), which is the point (1,2a+1)(1,2a+1)(1,2a+1) when a=0a=0a=0, and through (2,0)(2,0)(2,0), as required.

    Answer

    y=(a−1)x+a+2y=(a-1)x+a+2y=(a−1)x+a+2, with y-intercept (0,a+2)(0,a+2)(0,a+2); the member with x-intercept (2,0)(2,0)(2,0) has a=0a=0a=0, which is y=−x+2y=-x+2y=−x+2.

    Key idea

    Solving a point-slope equation for yyy exposes the y-intercept, and setting y=0y=0y=0 then locates the x-intercept.

  7. Problem 7 Three special lines

    On the blank grid in the figure, draw the lines A: y=−3y=-3y=−3, B: x=4x=4x=4 and C: y=−2xy=-2xy=−2x. For each line, give its y-intercept and its x-intercept, or state that it has none.

    Text description of this figure

    A blank rectangular coordinate grid. The horizontal x-axis runs from negative 4 to 5 and the vertical y-axis from negative 5 to 5, and each axis has an arrowhead at both ends. One unit has the same length on both axes, and gridlines are drawn every unit. Each whole-number tick is labeled: negative 4 through 5 on the x-axis, negative 5 through 5 on the y-axis, and the origin is labeled 0. No points, lines or other markings are drawn in the plotting area.

    1. Hint 1

      A y-intercept needs a point with x=0x=0x=0 and an x-intercept needs a point with y=0y=0y=0; ask whether each line has one.

    2. Hint 2

      A keeps the same height and B keeps the same input everywhere; for C, find a second point by stepping the slope from (0,0)(0,0)(0,0).

    Answer

    A: y-intercept (0,−3)(0,-3)(0,−3), no x-intercept; B: no y-intercept, x-intercept (4,0)(4,0)(4,0); C: both intercepts are (0,0)(0,0)(0,0). Drawn: A horizontal through (0,−3)(0,-3)(0,−3), B vertical through (4,0)(4,0)(4,0), C through (0,0)(0,0)(0,0) and (1,−2)(1,-2)(1,−2).

    Full solution

    A is y=−3y=-3y=−3, a horizontal line.

    Setting x=0x=0x=0 leaves y=−3y=-3y=−3, so its y-intercept is (0,−3)(0,-3)(0,−3).

    Setting y=0y=0y=0 would require 0=−30=-30=−3, which is false, so A has no x-intercept.

    Draw A as the horizontal line through (0,−3)(0,-3)(0,−3).

    B is x=4x=4x=4, a vertical line.

    Setting y=0y=0y=0 leaves x=4x=4x=4, so its x-intercept is (4,0)(4,0)(4,0).

    Setting x=0x=0x=0 would require 0=40=40=4, which is false, so B has no y-intercept.

    Draw B as the vertical line through (4,0)(4,0)(4,0).

    C is y=−2xy=-2xy=−2x.

    Setting x=0x=0x=0 gives y=0y=0y=0, and setting y=0y=0y=0 gives x=0x=0x=0, so both intercepts are the origin.

    That single point does not fix the line, so step by the slope −2-2−2: right 1 and down 2 reaches (1,−2)(1,-2)(1,−2).

    Draw C through (0,0)(0,0)(0,0) and (1,−2)(1,-2)(1,−2), and label the three lines A, B and C.

    Answer

    A: y-intercept (0,−3)(0,-3)(0,−3), no x-intercept; B: no y-intercept, x-intercept (4,0)(4,0)(4,0); C: both intercepts are (0,0)(0,0)(0,0). Drawn: A horizontal through (0,−3)(0,-3)(0,−3), B vertical through (4,0)(4,0)(4,0), C through (0,0)(0,0)(0,0) and (1,−2)(1,-2)(1,−2).

    Key idea

    A horizontal line off the x-axis has no x-intercept, a vertical line off the y-axis has no y-intercept, and a line through the origin with nonzero slope has both intercepts at (0,0)(0,0)(0,0).

  8. Problem 8 Two constant settings

    A student says that increasing bbb by 4 in y=2x+by=2x+by=2x+b also increases the x-intercept coordinate by 4. Is this correct? Justify your answer by finding the x-intercepts of the lines with b=6b=6b=6 and b=10b=10b=10.

    1. Hint 1

      The x-intercept coordinate must be found from output zero.

    2. Hint 2

      For each value of bbb, set 2x+b=02x+b=02x+b=0 and solve for xxx, then compare the two results.

    Answer

    No; the crossings are (−3,0)(-3,0)(−3,0) and (−5,0)(-5,0)(−5,0), so the x-intercept coordinate decreases by 2.

    Full solution

    For b=6b=6b=6, the crossing satisfies 0=2x+60=2x+60=2x+6, giving x=−3x=-3x=−3.

    For b=10b=10b=10, it satisfies 0=2x+100=2x+100=2x+10, giving x=−5x=-5x=−5.

    The change is

    −5−(−3)=−2-5-(-3)=-2−5−(−3)=−2

    Thus it decreases by 2, not increases by 4.

    In general here x=−b/2x=-b/2x=−b/2, which agrees with the computed change.

    Answer

    No; the crossings are (−3,0)(-3,0)(−3,0) and (−5,0)(-5,0)(−5,0), so the x-intercept coordinate decreases by 2.

    Key idea

    For y=mx+by=mx+by=mx+b with m≠0m\ne0m=0, the x-intercept is at x=−b/mx=-b/mx=−b/m, so a change in bbb moves it by that change divided by −m-m−m.

  9. Problem 9 Opposite axis signs

    A nonvertical line crosses the y-axis above the origin and crosses the x-axis to the left of the origin. A student claims its slope must be positive. Is the claim correct? Explain using the two crossings.

    1. Hint 1

      Imagine traveling from the left crossing to the upper crossing.

    2. Hint 2

      Track the signs of the rise and run in that direction.

    Answer

    Yes; its slope is positive.

    Full solution

    Write the crossings as (a,0)(a,0)(a,0) and (0,b)(0,b)(0,b), where aaa is negative and bbb is positive.

    Traveling from the first to the second gives

    m=b−am=\frac{b}{-a}m=−ab​

    The numerator is positive and the denominator is also positive, so the ratio is positive.

    The nonzero run makes this division valid.

    The line rises as it moves right from one crossing to the other, checking the sign.

    Answer

    Yes; its slope is positive.

    Key idea

    The positions of two axis crossings can determine the sign of a slope.

  10. Problem 10 A total of heights

    Two points on a line have coordinates (0,b)(0,b)(0,b) and (1,c)(1,c)(1,c). You are told only that b+c=6b+c=6b+c=6. Is that enough to determine the slope? Justify your answer.

    1. Hint 1

      Write the slope between (0,b)(0,b)(0,b) and (1,c)(1,c)(1,c) in terms of bbb and ccc.

    2. Hint 2

      Try two different pairs of heights with the same sum and calculate their slopes.

    Answer

    No; for example, y=4x+1y=4x+1y=4x+1 and y=2x+2y=2x+2y=2x+2 have slopes 444 and 222.

    Full solution

    The run between the points is 1, so their slope is

    m=c−bm=c-bm=c−b

    Choose b=1b=1b=1 and c=5c=5c=5.

    Their sum is 6 and the line is y=4x+1y=4x+1y=4x+1.

    Choose instead b=2b=2b=2 and c=4c=4c=4.

    Their sum is also 6 and the line is y=2x+2y=2x+2y=2x+2.

    The slopes differ, so the given sum does not determine a slope.

    Each proposed line gives its chosen heights at inputs 0 and 1.

    Answer

    No; for example, y=4x+1y=4x+1y=4x+1 and y=2x+2y=2x+2y=2x+2 have slopes 444 and 222.

    Key idea

    A fixed sum of heights does not fix the difference that determines slope.
