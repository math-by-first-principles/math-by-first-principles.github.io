  1. 1

    For the sequence 4,12,36,108,…4, 12, 36, 108, \ldots4,12,36,108,…, what is the first term a1a_1a1​?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The first term is the number written at the start of the list, the term in position 111.

    a1=4a_1 = 4a1​=4

    The value 333 is the common ratio and 121212 is the second term, not the first.

  2. 2

    What is the common ratio rrr of 4,12,36,108,…4, 12, 36, 108, \ldots4,12,36,108,…?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The common ratio is any term divided by the term before it.

    r=124=3r = \frac{12}{4} = 3r=412​=3

    Every ratio in the list is the same 333, which is what makes the sequence geometric.

  3. 3

    What term comes next after 808080 in 5,10,20,40,80,…5, 10, 20, 40, 80, \ldots5,10,20,40,80,…?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Each term is the one before it times the common ratio r=2r = 2r=2. Multiply the last term shown by 222.

    80×2=16080 \times 2 = 16080×2=160

  4. 4

    What is the common ratio of 100,50,25,12.5,…100, 50, 25, 12.5, \ldots100,50,25,12.5,…?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Divide a term by the one before it. Because the list decreases, the ratio lies between 000 and 111.

    r=50100=12r = \frac{50}{100} = \frac{1}{2}r=10050​=21​

  5. 5

    Using an=a1rn−1a_n = a_1 r^{n-1}an​=a1​rn−1, what is the 2nd term when a1=3a_1 = 3a1​=3 and r=4r = 4r=4?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    For the 2nd term, n=2n = 2n=2, so the exponent is n−1=1n - 1 = 1n−1=1.

    a2=3⋅41=12a_2 = 3 \cdot 4^{1} = 12a2​=3⋅41=12

    Adding the two numbers would give 777, but a geometric sequence multiplies.

  6. 6

    What is the 3rd term of a geometric sequence with a1=2a_1 = 2a1​=2 and r=3r = 3r=3?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    For the 3rd term, n=3n = 3n=3, so there are n−1=2n - 1 = 2n−1=2 factors of rrr.

    a3=2⋅32=2⋅9=18a_3 = 2 \cdot 3^{2} = 2 \cdot 9 = 18a3​=2⋅32=2⋅9=18

  7. 7

    Which sequence has common ratio r=13r = \tfrac{1}{3}r=31​?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A ratio of 13\tfrac{1}{3}31​ means each term is one third of the one before it, so the list decreases.

    927=13\frac{9}{27} = \frac{1}{3}279​=31​

    Only 27,9,3,127, 9, 3, 127,9,3,1 divides by 333 each step; the others multiply by 333 or by 222.

  8. 8

    A geometric sequence has a1=5a_1 = 5a1​=5 and r=2r = 2r=2. What is a2a_2a2​?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The second term is the first term times the common ratio.

    a2=5×2=10a_2 = 5 \times 2 = 10a2​=5×2=10

    The value 777 comes from adding instead of multiplying.

  9. 9

    A sequence starts 1,3,9,…1, 3, 9, \ldots1,3,9,…. What is the 5th term?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Here a1=1a_1 = 1a1​=1 and r=3r = 3r=3. The 5th term uses n−1=4n - 1 = 4n−1=4 factors of rrr.

    a5=1⋅34=81a_5 = 1 \cdot 3^{4} = 81a5​=1⋅34=81

    What is the 3rd term of a geometric sequence with a1=1a_1 = 1a1​=1 and r=5r = 5r=5?

    Solution

  11. 11

    In 3,6,12,24,483, 6, 12, 24, 483,6,12,24,48, which value is a2a_2a2​?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Count to position 222: 333 is first, 666 is second.

    a2=6a_2 = 6a2​=6

  12. 12

    Which two numbers describe a geometric sequence completely?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Every term is built from where you start and the constant factor you multiply by, and nothing else.

    an=a1rn−1a_n = a_1 r^{n-1}an​=a1​rn−1

    This formula uses only a1a_1a1​ and rrr, so those two numbers pin down the whole sequence.
