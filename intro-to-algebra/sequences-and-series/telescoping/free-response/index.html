    2. Hint 2

    b3−b1=−5.b_3-b_1=-5.b3​−b1​=−5.

    The three directed changes total 7−2−5=07-2-5=07−2−5=0, as a closed record should.

    Answer

    b3−b1=−5b_3-b_1=-5b3​−b1​=−5.

    Key idea

    Differences around a closed chain add to zero because every reading appears with both signs.

  5. Problem 5 The painted strips

    ∑s=719[(s+1)2−s2].\sum_{s=7}^{19}\left[(s+1)^2-s^2\right].s=7∑19​[(s+1)2−s2].

    Each −s2-s^2−s2 meets the +s2+s^2+s2 of the next strip, so every interior square area cancels.

    Only the largest square area and the smallest survive, leaving

    202−72=35120^2-7^2=351202−72=351

    square centimeters.

    13⋅27=351.13\cdot27=351.13⋅27=351.

  6. Problem 6 The target total

    Let Tn=∑k=1n1/[k(k+2)]T_n=\sum_{k=1}^{n}1/[k(k+2)]Tn​=∑k=1n​1/[k(k+2)] for positive integers nnn. Find the least nnn for which Tn>23T_n>\frac23Tn​>32​, and explain why no smaller index works.

    3. Hint 3

      The totals increase, so compare the indices on either side of the crossing.

    Full solution

  7. Problem 7 The nine differences

    For a positive integer mmm, nine differences satisfy

    ∑k=mm+8(k+1−k)=1.\sum_{k=m}^{m+8}(\sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt{k})=1.k=m∑m+8​(k+1​−k​)=1.

    Answer
