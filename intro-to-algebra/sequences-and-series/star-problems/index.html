  1. Problem 1 An impossible report and its nearest repairs

    ★ Stretch Difficulty: 1 of 3 stars, Stretch

    An increasing arithmetic sequence consists entirely of positive integers. A report says that its first four terms sum to 626262 and its next four terms sum to 190190190. Prove that the report is impossible.

    Keep 626262 unchanged. What is the smallest absolute change to 190190190 that makes the report possible? Find every corrected total achieving that smallest change, and give the corresponding first term and common difference.

    Builds on Arithmetic Sequences, Arithmetic Series

    1. Hint 1

      If the first term is aaa and the common difference is ddd, compare each of terms 555 through 888 with the term four places earlier.

    2. Hint 2

      The first total gives 2a+3d=312a+3d=312a+3d=31. Use integer parity before looking for the nearest possible second total.

    Answer

    The smallest change is 161616. The corrected totals are 174174174 with (a,d)=(5,7)(a,d)=(5,7)(a,d)=(5,7), and 206206206 with (a,d)=(2,9)(a,d)=(2,9)(a,d)=(2,9).

    Full solution

    Let the first term be the positive integer aaa and the common difference be the positive integer ddd.

    The first four terms sum to 4a+6d=624a+6d=624a+6d=62, or 2a+3d=312a+3d=312a+3d=31.

    Since 313131 is odd, ddd must be odd.

    Each of terms 5,6,7,85,6,7,85,6,7,8 is 4d4d4d more than its partner among terms 1,2,3,41,2,3,41,2,3,4.

    Their total is therefore 62+16d62+16d62+16d.

    A total of 190190190 would give d=(190−62)/16=8d=(190-62)/16=8d=(190−62)/16=8, contradicting the required odd parity.

    The nearest odd integers to 888 are 777 and 999, each one away.

    They give corrected totals 62+16⋅7=17462+16\cdot7=17462+16⋅7=174 and 62+16⋅9=20662+16\cdot9=20662+16⋅9=206, each differing from 190190190 by 161616.

    From a=(31−3d)/2a=(31-3d)/2a=(31−3d)/2, their first terms are 555 and 222, both positive.

    Thus both repairs actually give valid increasing positive-integer sequences.

    Every other allowable odd ddd lies farther from 888, so no smaller change is possible and these are all nearest repairs.

    Answer

    The smallest change is 161616. The corrected totals are 174174174 with (a,d)=(5,7)(a,d)=(5,7)(a,d)=(5,7), and 206206206 with (a,d)=(2,9)(a,d)=(2,9)(a,d)=(2,9).

    Key idea

    Aggregate sequence data must satisfy integer constraints; parity can detect an impossible report and certify a nearest repair.
