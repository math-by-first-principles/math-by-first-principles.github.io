    1. One shuttle, two rules . Foundational, 11 points. Question 1 of 5.

    Foundational 11 pts

    A shuttle van posts two rules on its door. A capacity sign reads: seats available for no more than 121212 passengers. A separate driver's rule says the van will not leave the curb until more than 666 passengers are aboard. Let ppp stand for the number of passengers currently on the van.
