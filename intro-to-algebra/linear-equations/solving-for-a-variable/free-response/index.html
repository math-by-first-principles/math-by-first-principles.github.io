  1. Problem 1 A cylinder's volume, aimed at its height

    The volume of a cylinder with radius rrr and height hhh is V=πr2hV = \pi r^2 hV=πr2h. Solve this formula for hhh, and state the condition your final step requires.

  3. Problem 3 The distance around a half-disc window

    A window has the shape of half a disc: a semicircle of radius rrr on top of a straight edge of length 2r2r2r. The distance around the window, the curved edge πr\pi rπr plus the straight edge 2r2r2r, is P=πr+2rP = \pi r + 2rP=πr+2r. Solve this formula for rrr.

    1. Hint 1

      The target rrr appears in two terms, πr\pi rπr and 2r2r2r. No single division removes it from both, so first arrange for rrr to appear only once.

    2. Hint 2

      Both terms share the factor rrr. The distributive law read backward writes πr+2r\pi r + 2rπr+2r as rrr times one quantity, and then one division by that quantity finishes the job.

    Answer

    r=Pπ+2r = \dfrac{P}{\pi + 2}r=π+2P​ (or r=P2+πr = \dfrac{P}{2 + \pi}r=2+πP​).

    Full solution

    The target rrr stands in two terms.

    Dividing both sides by π\piπ alone would turn them into r+2rπr + \dfrac{2r}{\pi}r+π2r​, which still holds rrr twice, so gather the two copies of rrr first.

    Both terms carry the factor rrr, so reading the distributive law backward gives

    P=r(π+2).P = r(\pi + 2).P=r(π+2).

    Expanding r(π+2)r(\pi + 2)r(π+2) gives back πr+2r\pi r + 2rπr+2r, which confirms the factoring.

    Now rrr appears once, multiplied by π+2\pi + 2π+2.

    Divide both sides by π+2\pi + 2π+2:

    r=Pπ+2.r = \frac{P}{\pi + 2}.r=π+2P​.

    The divisor π+2\pi + 2π+2 is a fixed number, a little more than 555, and it is not zero, so this rearrangement needs no condition on the letters.

    Answer

  4. Problem 4 A closed box, aimed at its height

  5. Problem 5 Where a seesaw balances

    Two children sit at the two ends of a seesaw that is LLL meters long. One weighs www kilograms and the other weighs vvv kilograms, where LLL, www and vvv are positive numbers. The seesaw balances when its pivot is ddd meters from the first child, where

    wd=v(L−d).wd = v(L - d).wd=v(L−d).

    Solve this balance condition for ddd. Then check your formula in the case where the two children weigh the same.

    1. Hint 1

      Expand the right side first, then look at where ddd appears: once on each side. One division can finish the job only once ddd stands in a single place.

    2. Hint 2

      Add vdvdvd to both sides so that every term containing ddd is on the left. Then factor ddd out of those terms and divide by what is left multiplying it.

    3. Hint 3

      For the check, put w=vw = vw=v into your formula and simplify. Where should the pivot of a seesaw carrying two equal weights be?

    Answer

    d=vLw+vd = \dfrac{vL}{w + v}d=w+vvL​. When the two children weigh the same it gives d=L2d = \dfrac{L}{2}d=2L​, the middle of the seesaw.

    Full solution

    Treat www, vvv and LLL as fixed numbers.

    Expand the right side:

    wd=vL−vd.wd = vL - vd.wd=vL−vd.

    The target now appears on both sides.

    Add vdvdvd to both sides, so that every term containing ddd is on the left:

    wd+vd=vL.wd + vd = vL.wd+vd=vL.

    Both terms on the left contain ddd, so factor it out:

    d(w+v)=vL.d(w + v) = vL.d(w+v)=vL.

    Divide both sides by w+vw + vw+v.

    That division needs w+v≠0w + v \neq 0w+v=0, and since both weights are positive, w+vw + vw+v is positive, so it is safe:

    d=vLw+v.d = \frac{vL}{w + v}.d=w+vvL​.

    For the check, put w=vw = vw=v:

    d=vLv+v=vL2v=L2.\begin{aligned} d &= \frac{vL}{v + v} \\ &= \frac{vL}{2v} \\ &= \frac{L}{2}. \end{aligned}d​=v+vvL​=2vvL​=2L​.​

    The last step cancels vvv, which is allowed because vvv is positive.

    So with equal weights the pivot sits at the middle of the seesaw, which is where two equal weights balance.

    The formula passes the check.

    Answer

    d=vLw+vd = \dfrac{vL}{w + v}d=w+vvL​. When the two children weigh the same it gives d=L2d = \dfrac{L}{2}d=2L​, the middle of the seesaw.

  8. Problem 8 Dana's price for a school group

    A museum charges ppp dollars for each of the nnn students in a school group, and 333 dollars more than that for each of the 222 teachers with them, so the group pays T=np+2(p+3)T = np + 2(p + 3)T=np+2(p+3) dollars in all.
