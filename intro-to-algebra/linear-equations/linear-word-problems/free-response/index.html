  1. Problem 1 Five integers in a row

    Five consecutive integers have a sum of 185185185. What is the largest of the five?

    1. Hint 1

      Consecutive integers are tied together: each one is 111 more than the one before it. Name just one of them with a letter, and every other one follows from it.

    2. Hint 2

      Let nnn be the smallest of the five. Write the other four in terms of nnn, add all five, and set the total equal to 185185185.

    3. Hint 3

      Solving gives the smallest integer, which is not the one asked for. Count up from it to the largest, and check the sum of all five against the words.

    Answer

    393939 (the five integers are 353535, 363636, 373737, 383838 and 393939).

    Full solution

    Let nnn be the smallest of the five integers.

    Each integer is 111 more than the one before it, so the five integers are nnn, n+1n + 1n+1, n+2n + 2n+2, n+3n + 3n+3 and n+4n + 4n+4.

    Adding them gives five nnn terms and the constants 1+2+3+4=101 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 101+2+3+4=10, so the sum is 5n+105n + 105n+10.

    The sum is 185185185, so subtract 101010 from both sides and divide both sides by 555:

    5n+10=1855n=175n=35.\begin{aligned} 5n + 10 &= 185 \\ 5n &= 175 \\ n &= 35. \end{aligned}5n+105nn​=185=175=35.​

    The five integers are 353535, 363636, 373737, 383838 and 393939.

    Check against the words: they are consecutive, and

    35+36+37+38+39=185.35 + 36 + 37 + 38 + 39 = 185.35+36+37+38+39=185.

    The question asks for the largest, not the smallest, so the answer is

    n+4=39.n + 4 = 39.n+4=39.

    Answer

    393939 (the five integers are 353535, 363636, 373737, 383838 and 393939).

    Key idea

    Name the smallest of a run of consecutive integers, write each of the others by adding 111 at a time, and report the one the question asks for.

  2. Problem 2 A square and a triangle with one perimeter

    A square and an equilateral triangle (a triangle whose three sides are equal) have the same perimeter. Each side of the triangle is 555 cm longer than each side of the square. Find the side length of each shape.

    1. Hint 1

      The two shapes are linked by one fact about their sides, so a single letter can describe both. The sentence about the perimeters is the one that states an equality.

    2. Hint 2

      Let sss be the side of the square in centimeters, so each side of the triangle is s+5s + 5s+5. The square's perimeter adds four equal sides, and the triangle's adds three.

    3. Hint 3

      Set the two perimeters equal and collect the sss terms on one side. Remember that sss is only the square's side, and the question asks about both shapes.

    Answer

    Each side of the square is 151515 cm and each side of the triangle is 202020 cm (both perimeters are 606060 cm).

    Full solution

    Let sss be the side length of the square, in centimeters.

    Each side of the triangle is 555 cm longer, so it is s+5s + 5s+5 centimeters.

    The square has four sides of length sss, so its perimeter is 4s4s4s.

    The triangle has three sides of length s+5s + 5s+5, so its perimeter is 3(s+5)3(s + 5)3(s+5).

    The two perimeters are equal:

    4s=3(s+5).4s = 3(s + 5).4s=3(s+5).

    Distribute the 333, then subtract 3s3s3s from both sides:

    4s=3s+15s=15.\begin{aligned} 4s &= 3s + 15 \\ s &= 15. \end{aligned}4ss​=3s+15=15.​

    So the square's side is 151515 cm, and the triangle's side is 15+5=2015 + 5 = 2015+5=20 cm.

    Check against the words: the square's perimeter is 4×15=604 \times 15 = 604×15=60 cm, the triangle's is 3×20=603 \times 20 = 603×20=60 cm, and 202020 is 555 more than 151515.

    Answer

    Each side of the square is 151515 cm and each side of the triangle is 202020 cm (both perimeters are 606060 cm).

    Key idea

    When two shapes are linked through their sides, name one side, write the other from it, and let the shared perimeter supply the equation.

  3. Problem 3 Walkers and bus riders

    In one class, 40%40\%40% of the students walk to school, and all the other students in the class take the bus. There are 999 more bus riders than walkers. How many students are in the class?

    1. Hint 1

      Both groups are percentages of the same whole class, so name the size of the class with a letter and describe both groups from it.

    2. Hint 2

      If 40%40\%40% of the class walk, the rest of the class, 60%60\%60% of it, take the bus. Write each group as a decimal times your letter.

    3. Hint 3

      The equality in the words compares the two groups: the bus riders minus the walkers is 999. Once you have the class size, work out both groups and check that comparison.

    Answer

    454545 students (181818 walk and 272727 take the bus).

    Full solution

    Let nnn be the number of students in the class.

    The walkers are 40%40\%40% of the class, which is 0.4n0.4n0.4n students.

    Everyone else takes the bus, and removing 40%40\%40% of the class leaves 60%60\%60% of it, so there are 0.6n0.6n0.6n bus riders.

    There are 999 more bus riders than walkers, so the bus riders minus the walkers is 999:

    0.6n−0.4n=9.0.6n - 0.4n = 9.0.6n−0.4n=9.

    Combine like terms, then divide both sides by 0.20.20.2:

    0.2n=9n=45.\begin{aligned} 0.2n &= 9 \\ n &= 45. \end{aligned}0.2nn​=9=45.​

    Check against the words: 40%40\%40% of 454545 is 0.4×45=180.4 \times 45 = 180.4×45=18 walkers, and the other 45−18=2745 - 18 = 2745−18=27 students take the bus.

    That is 27−18=927 - 18 = 927−18=9 more bus riders than walkers, as stated.

    So the class has 454545 students.

    Answer

    454545 students (181818 walk and 272727 take the bus).

    Key idea

    When a whole splits into a percentage and the rest, name the whole, write both parts as percentages of it, and translate the comparison between them.

  4. Problem 4 Three kinds of coin in one jar

    A jar holds only nickels (555 cents each), dimes (101010 cents each) and quarters (252525 cents each). There are twice as many dimes as nickels, and 333 more quarters than nickels. Altogether the coins are worth 444 dollars and 252525 cents. How many coins of each kind are in the jar?

    1. Hint 1

      Two of the three counts are described by comparing them with the nickels, so a single letter for the number of nickels can carry all three counts.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the number of dimes and the number of quarters in terms of the number of nickels. The value of each kind, in cents, is its count times the value of one coin.

    3. Hint 3

      Change the total to cents before writing the equation, so every value in it has the same unit. After solving, work out all three counts, not only the nickels.

    Answer

    777 nickels, 141414 dimes and 101010 quarters.

    Full solution

    Let nnn be the number of nickels.

    There are twice as many dimes, so there are 2n2n2n dimes.

    There are 333 more quarters than nickels, so there are n+3n + 3n+3 quarters.

    Work in cents, so that every value is a whole number: 444 dollars and 252525 cents is 425425425 cents.

    Each kind of coin is worth its count times the value of one coin, so the nickels are worth 5n5n5n cents, the dimes 10(2n)10(2n)10(2n) cents and the quarters 25(n+3)25(n + 3)25(n+3) cents.

    Together they are worth 425425425 cents:

    5n+10(2n)+25(n+3)=425.5n + 10(2n) + 25(n + 3) = 425.5n+10(2n)+25(n+3)=425.

    Multiply out the parentheses, collect like terms, subtract 757575 from both sides and divide both sides by 505050:

    5n+20n+25n+75=42550n+75=42550n=350n=7.\begin{aligned} 5n + 20n + 25n + 75 &= 425 \\ 50n + 75 &= 425 \\ 50n &= 350 \\ n &= 7. \end{aligned}5n+20n+25n+7550n+7550nn​=425=425=350=7.​

    So there are 777 nickels, 2×7=142 \times 7 = 142×7=14 dimes and 7+3=107 + 3 = 107+3=10 quarters.

    Check against the words: 141414 is twice 777, 101010 is 333 more than 777, and the coins are worth 35+140+250=42535 + 140 + 250 = 42535+140+250=425 cents, which is 444 dollars and 252525 cents.

    Answer

    777 nickels, 141414 dimes and 101010 quarters.

    Key idea

    Count every kind of coin from one letter, change every amount to one unit, and total the values as count times value per coin.

  5. Problem 5 An equation with its words missing

    A 969696 cm board is cut into three pieces. The second piece is twice as long as the first, and the third piece is 888 cm longer than the first. Someone translated this situation and wrote down only the equation

    x+2x+(x+8)=96.x + 2x + (x + 8) = 96.x+2x+(x+8)=96.

    Say what xxx stands for, units included, and which piece each of the other two terms on the left describes. Then find the length of the longest piece.

    1. Hint 1

      Only one of the pieces is described on its own; the other two are described by comparing them with it. That piece is the natural one for the letter to name.

    2. Hint 2

      Doubling a length and adding 888 to it are two different instructions; find the phrase of the situation that gives each one, and the piece that phrase describes.

    3. Hint 3

      The solved value is the length of one particular piece. Work out all three lengths before deciding which piece is the longest.

    Answer

    xxx is the first piece's length in centimeters, 2x2x2x is the second piece and x+8x + 8x+8 the third. The longest is the second piece, 444444 cm (the pieces are 222222, 444444 and 303030 cm).

    Full solution

    Only the first piece is described on its own terms; the other two are described by comparing them with it.

    So xxx is the length of the first piece, in centimeters.

    Read the left side one term at a time.

    The term xxx is the first piece.

    The phrase "twice as long as the first" doubles it, giving the second piece, 2x2x2x.

    The phrase "888 cm longer than the first" adds 888, giving the third piece, x+8x + 8x+8.

    The right side is the whole board: cutting loses no length, so the three pieces together make up the 969696 cm.

    Combine like terms, subtract 888 from both sides and divide both sides by 444:

    4x+8=964x=88x=22.\begin{aligned} 4x + 8 &= 96 \\ 4x &= 88 \\ x &= 22. \end{aligned}4x+84xx​=96=88=22.​

    The letter names the first piece, so 222222 cm is the first piece and not yet the answer.

    The second piece is 2×22=442 \times 22 = 442×22=44 cm and the third is 22+8=3022 + 8 = 3022+8=30 cm.

    Check against the words: 22+44+30=9622 + 44 + 30 = 9622+44+30=96, the second is twice the first, and the third is 888 cm longer than the first.

    The longest piece is the second, at 444444 cm.

    Answer

    xxx is the first piece's length in centimeters, 2x2x2x is the second piece and x+8x + 8x+8 the third. The longest is the second piece, 444444 cm (the pieces are 222222, 444444 and 303030 cm).

    Key idea

    In a sound translation each term traces back to a phrase of the words, and the solved value belongs to whichever quantity the letter names.

  6. Problem 6 Two names for the same pair

    Two numbers have a sum of 606060, and the larger is 141414 more than the smaller.

    Write and solve an equation for this situation twice: first naming the smaller number as your unknown, then naming the larger. Report the two numbers each time. Then explain why your two equations have different solutions even though they describe the same pair of numbers.

    1. Hint 1

      The letter is a name you choose for one quantity, not something the problem hands you. Before any equation, write a sentence saying which number your letter stands for.

    2. Hint 2

      Naming the smaller number, the larger is 141414 more than it. Naming the larger number, read the relationship from the other end: the smaller is 141414 less than the larger.

    3. Hint 3

      For the explanation, ask what each solved value is a value of. Two values that measure different quantities are not in competition, however different they look.

    Answer

    Naming the smaller nnn: n+(n+14)=60n + (n + 14) = 60n+(n+14)=60 gives n=23n = 23n=23. Naming the larger mmm: m+(m−14)=60m + (m - 14) = 60m+(m−14)=60 gives m=37m = 37m=37. Each route gives 232323 and 373737. The solutions differ: nnn is the smaller number and mmm the larger.

    Full solution

    First route: let nnn be the smaller number.

    The larger is 141414 more than the smaller, so it is n+14n + 14n+14.

    The sum of the two is 606060, so combine like terms, subtract 141414 from both sides and divide both sides by 222:

    n+(n+14)=602n+14=602n=46n=23.\begin{aligned} n + (n + 14) &= 60 \\ 2n + 14 &= 60 \\ 2n &= 46 \\ n &= 23. \end{aligned}n+(n+14)2n+142nn​=60=60=46=23.​

    So the smaller number is 232323, and the larger is

    23+14=37.23 + 14 = 37.23+14=37.

    Second route: let mmm be the larger number.

    Now the relationship is read from the other end: the smaller is 141414 less than the larger, so it is m−14m - 14m−14.

    The sum is still 606060, so combine like terms, add 141414 to both sides and divide both sides by 222:

    m+(m−14)=602m−14=602m=74m=37.\begin{aligned} m + (m - 14) &= 60 \\ 2m - 14 &= 60 \\ 2m &= 74 \\ m &= 37. \end{aligned}m+(m−14)2m−142mm​=60=60=74=37.​

    So the larger number is 373737, and the smaller is 37−14=2337 - 14 = 2337−14=23, the same pair as before.

    Check against the words: 23+37=6023 + 37 = 6023+37=60, and 373737 is 141414 more than 232323.

    The two solutions differ because the two letters name different quantities.

    The value n=23n = 23n=23 belongs to the smaller number and the value m=37m = 37m=37 to the larger, so they answer two different questions, and both routes report the same pair.

    A contradiction appears only if the meaning of each letter is dropped, which is why the method writes down what the letter stands for before any equation is written.

    Answer

    Naming the smaller nnn: n+(n+14)=60n + (n + 14) = 60n+(n+14)=60 gives n=23n = 23n=23. Naming the larger mmm: m+(m−14)=60m + (m - 14) = 60m+(m−14)=60 gives m=37m = 37m=37. Each route gives 232323 and 373737. The solutions differ: nnn is the smaller number and mmm the larger.

    Key idea

    A solved value belongs to the quantity its letter was defined to name, so write that definition down before writing the equation.

  7. Problem 7 A ride to the lake and back

    A cyclist leaves home at 8:00 in the morning and rides to a lake without stopping, at a steady 151515 kilometers per hour. She then rides home along the same road at a steady 101010 kilometers per hour. Her riding time for the whole trip, there and back, is 555 hours. At what time does she reach the lake, and how far is the lake from her home?

    1. Hint 1

      The ride out and the ride home cover the same stretch of road, so they cover the same distance, though not in the same time.

    2. Hint 2

      Let ttt be the number of hours the ride out takes. The two rides take 555 hours together, so the ride home takes 5−t5 - t5−t hours. Write each distance as rate times time.

    3. Hint 3

      Set the two distances equal and solve. The question asks for a time of day and a distance, so turn your value of ttt into a clock time and work out how far either ride goes.

    Answer

    She reaches the lake at 10:00 in the morning, and the lake is 303030 kilometers from her home.

    Full solution

    Let ttt be the number of hours the ride out to the lake takes.

    She rides for 555 hours in all, so the ride home takes the rest of that time, 5−t5 - t5−t hours.

    Distance is rate times time, d=rtd = rtd=rt.

    So the ride out covers 15t15t15t kilometers and the ride home covers 10(5−t)10(5 - t)10(5−t) kilometers.

    Both rides run along the same road between her home and the lake, so the two distances are equal:

    15t=10(5−t).15t = 10(5 - t).15t=10(5−t).

    Distribute the 101010, add 10t10t10t to both sides, and divide both sides by 252525:

    15t=50−10t25t=50t=2.\begin{aligned} 15t &= 50 - 10t \\ 25t &= 50 \\ t &= 2. \end{aligned}15t25tt​=50−10t=50=2.​

    The value t=2t = 2t=2 counts hours of riding out, so it answers neither question yet.

    Two hours after 8:00 is 10:00 in the morning, and in those 222 hours she rides 15×2=3015 \times 2 = 3015×2=30 kilometers.

    Check against the words: the ride home takes 5−2=35 - 2 = 35−2=3 hours at 101010 kilometers per hour, which covers 10×3=3010 \times 3 = 3010×3=30 kilometers, the same distance as the ride out, and the two rides take 2+3=52 + 3 = 52+3=5 hours in all.

    So she reaches the lake at 10:00 in the morning, and the lake is 303030 kilometers from her home.

    Answer

    She reaches the lake at 10:00 in the morning, and the lake is 303030 kilometers from her home.

    Key idea

    On a trip there and back along the same road, the two legs cover equal distances, and a known total time lets one letter give both travel times.

  8. Problem 8 Three times as old, and six times

    A father is 454545 years old today, and his son is 999.

    In how many years will the father be three times as old as his son? Then write and solve an equation for the father being six times as old as his son, and use its solution to decide whether that will happen at some future date.

    1. Hint 1

      Everyone ages at the same rate, so one letter can count the years from today for both of them.

    2. Hint 2

      In xxx years the father is 45+x45 + x45+x and the son is 9+x9 + x9+x. "Three times as old" multiplies the son's age, because he is the younger.

    3. Hint 3

      Solve the six-times equation the same way, then read the sign of xxx: a positive xxx is a date in the future, and a negative xxx is a date in the past.

    Answer

    In 999 years, when the father is 545454 and the son 181818. Six times: 45+x=6(9+x)45 + x = 6(9 + x)45+x=6(9+x) gives x=−1.8x = -1.8x=−1.8, which is 1.81.81.8 years ago; it happens at no future date.

    Full solution

    Let xxx be the number of years from today.

    Both ages grow by the same xxx, so in xxx years the father is 45+x45 + x45+x and the son is 9+x9 + x9+x.

    Three times as old multiplies the son's age, since he is the younger.

    Distribute the 333, subtract xxx and 272727 from both sides, and divide both sides by 222:

    45+x=3(9+x)45+x=27+3x18=2xx=9.\begin{aligned} 45 + x &= 3(9 + x) \\ 45 + x &= 27 + 3x \\ 18 &= 2x \\ x &= 9. \end{aligned}45+x45+x18x​=3(9+x)=27+3x=2x=9.​

    Check against the words: in 999 years the father is 545454 and the son is 181818, and

    54=3×18.54 = 3 \times 18.54=3×18.

    Six times as old sets up the same way.

    Distribute the 666, subtract xxx and 545454 from both sides, and divide both sides by 555:

    45+x=6(9+x)45+x=54+6x−9=5xx=−1.8.\begin{aligned} 45 + x &= 6(9 + x) \\ 45 + x &= 54 + 6x \\ -9 &= 5x \\ x &= -1.8. \end{aligned}45+x45+x−9x​=6(9+x)=54+6x=5x=−1.8.​

    This equation has exactly one solution, and it is negative.

    A negative number of years from today is a date in the past: 1.81.81.8 years ago the father was 43.243.243.2 and the son was 7.27.27.2, and

    43.2=6×7.2.43.2 = 6 \times 7.2.43.2=6×7.2.

    So the father was six times as old as his son 1.81.81.8 years ago, and there is no future date on which he is.

    Reasoning from the ages directly gives the same verdict.

    The father is always 363636 years older than his son, so being six times as old needs those 363636 years to be five times the son's age, which makes the son 36÷5=7.236 \div 5 = 7.236÷5=7.2 years old.

    Answer

    In 999 years, when the father is 545454 and the son 181818. Six times: 45+x=6(9+x)45 + x = 6(9 + x)45+x=6(9+x) gives x=−1.8x = -1.8x=−1.8, which is 1.81.81.8 years ago; it happens at no future date.

    Read a solved value back into the situation: a negative number of years from today is a date in the past, not a future one.

  9. Problem 9 Putting the discount back on

  10. Problem 10 One sum, two kinds of integer

    Decide whether there are three consecutive even integers with a sum of 969696, and whether there are three consecutive odd integers with a sum of 969696. Where such integers exist, find them; where they do not, explain why not.

    1. Hint 1

      Consecutive even integers and consecutive odd integers both step by 222, so both kinds can be written as nnn, n+2n + 2n+2 and n+4n + 4n+4. What differs is the kind of number nnn has to be.

    2. Hint 2

      Add nnn, n+2n + 2n+2 and n+4n + 4n+4, set the total equal to 969696, and solve. The equation does not know which kind of integer you had in mind.

    3. Hint 3

      Check your value of nnn against the words for each kind in turn.

    Answer

    Even: yes, 303030, 323232 and 343434. Odd: no three consecutive odd integers have a sum of 969696; the equation's only solution, n=30n = 30n=30, is not odd.

    Full solution

    Consecutive even integers step by 222, and so do consecutive odd integers.

    So for either kind, name the smallest nnn, and the three integers are nnn, n+2n + 2n+2 and n+4n + 4n+4.

    The kind of integer appears only as a condition on nnn: it must be even for the first question and odd for the second.

    The three integers have a sum of 969696, so combine like terms, subtract 666 from both sides and divide both sides by 333:

    n+(n+2)+(n+4)=963n+6=963n=90n=30.\begin{aligned} n + (n + 2) + (n + 4) &= 96 \\ 3n + 6 &= 96 \\ 3n &= 90 \\ n &= 30. \end{aligned}n+(n+2)+(n+4)3n+63nn​=96=96=90=30.​

    The equation has exactly one solution, n=30n = 30n=30, and it serves both questions.

    For the even question, 303030 is even, so the integers are 303030, 323232 and 343434.

    Check against the words: they are consecutive even integers, and

    30+32+34=96.30 + 32 + 34 = 96.30+32+34=96.

    For the odd question, the smallest integer would have to satisfy the same equation, and its only solution, 303030, is not odd.

    So no three consecutive odd integers have a sum of 969696.

    A second argument confirms it.

    An odd number plus an odd number is even, and adding a third odd number makes the total odd, so the sum of three odd integers is odd, and 969696 is even.

    Answer

    Even: yes, 303030, 323232 and 343434. Odd: no three consecutive odd integers have a sum of 969696; the equation's only solution, n=30n = 30n=30, is not odd.

    Key idea

    The form nnn, n+2n + 2n+2, n+4n + 4n+4 fits consecutive even and consecutive odd integers alike, so check that the solved nnn is the kind of integer the words ask for.
