    1. The exponent only remembers its remainder . Foundational, 10 points. Question 1 of 5.

    Foundational 10 pts

    Every power of iii is one of only four values. Use that on an exponent far too large to multiply out, and show why the pattern holds for every exponent, not just the ones anyone has checked.

    Part A

    Part B

    Part A

    Divide each exponent by 444 and keep the remainder, since the powers repeat every four steps.

    38=4×9+238 = 4 \times 9 + 238=4×9+2 (remainder 222):

    Part B

    The 202020 terms fall into five blocks of four (20=4×520 = 4 \times 520=4×5).
