  1. Problem 1 Matching powers

    Find the smallest whole number nnn greater than 100100100 for which in=i7i^n=i^7in=i7.

    1. Hint 1

      Powers with matching remainders on division by 444 have matching values.

    2. Hint 2

      Find the remainder of 777, then inspect the whole numbers just above 100100100.

    Answer

    n=103n=103n=103.

    Full solution

    The exponent 777 has remainder 333 on division by 444.

    i7=−ii^7=-ii7=−i

    Test the candidates in turn: 101101101 leaves remainder 111 and 102102102 leaves remainder 222, so neither matches.

    The next candidate does.

    103=4(25)+3103=4(25)+3103=4(25)+3

    Its remainder also gives −i-i−i, so it satisfies the condition and is the smallest permitted exponent.

    Answer

    n=103n=103n=103.

    Key idea

    Matching values of powers of iii can be found by matching exponent remainders.

  4. Problem 4 Repeated multiplication

    A calculation starts at −3i-3i−3i. Each step multiplies the current value by iii. What is the value after 999 steps?

    1. Hint 1

    Full solution

    Answer

    Key idea

  8. Problem 8 Two opposite bases

    For which whole numbers nnn is (−i)n=in(-i)^n=i^n(−i)n=in? Justify your answer.

    1. Hint 1

      Write −i-i−i as (−1)⋅i(-1)\cdot i(−1)⋅i, so the power splits into a power of −1-1−1 and a power of iii.

    2. Hint 2

      No power of iii is zero, so compare the two sides separately in the even and the odd cases.

    Answer

    Exactly the even whole numbers nnn, zero included, and no odd ones.

    Full solution

    Separate the base into its two factors with the power of a product, (ab)n=anbn(ab)^n=a^nb^n(ab)n=anbn.

    (−i)n=(−1)nin(-i)^n=(-1)^n i^n(−i)n=(−1)nin

    When nnn is even, (−1)n=1(-1)^n=1(−1)n=1, so the right side reduces to ini^nin and the two sides agree.

    At n=0n=0n=0 each side is a nonzero base raised to the power zero, so both equal 111.

    When nnn is odd, (−1)n=−1(-1)^n=-1(−1)n=−1, so the same identity gives

    (−i)n=−in(-i)^n=-i^n(−i)n=−in

    Agreement would then force in=−ini^n=-i^nin=−in, and hence in=0i^n=0in=0.

    Every power of iii is one of 111, iii, −1-1−1 and −i-i−i, none of them zero, so no odd exponent works.

    The identity therefore holds for the even whole numbers and for no others.

    Answer

    Exactly the even whole numbers nnn, zero included, and no odd ones.

    Key idea

    An even power removes the sign difference between iii and −i-i−i, while an odd power preserves it.

  9. Problem 9 A window of exponents

    Find every whole number nnn with 0≤n≤200\le n\le 200≤n≤20 for which in−4i^n\sqrt{-4}in−4​ is a positive real number.

    1. Hint 1

      Rewrite the square root in imaginary form, so the expression becomes one power of iii times a real factor.

    2. Hint 2

    Answer

    n=3n=3n=3, 777, 111111, 151515 and 191919.

    Full solution

    Convert the square root first.

    −4=2i\sqrt{-4}=2i−4​=2i

    The square root contributes one more factor of iii, so the exponent rises from nnn to n+1n+1n+1.

    in−4=2in+1i^n\sqrt{-4}=2i^{n+1}in−4​=2in+1

    The factor 222 is positive, so the value is a positive real exactly when in+1=1i^{n+1}=1in+1=1, which happens when n+1n+1n+1 is a multiple of 444.

    In the range 0≤n≤200\le n\le 200≤n≤20, the multiples of 444 available to n+1n+1n+1 are 444, 888, 121212, 161616 and 202020.

    Subtracting one from each gives the exponents n=3n=3n=3, 777, 111111, 151515 and 191919.

    Check n=15n=15n=15: the expression becomes 2i162i^{16}2i16, and 161616 is a multiple of 444, so the value is 222.

    Answer

    n=3n=3n=3, 777, 111111, 151515 and 191919.

    Key idea

    Folding a square root of a negative into the power of iii turns a question about the value into a question about the exponent's remainder.
