  1. Problem 1 A number and its square

    Three times the square of a positive number is 202020 more than seven times that number. Write an equation in standard form for the number nnn; do not solve it.

    1. Hint 1

      Each described quantity has to be written with the one letter that names the unknown.

    2. Hint 2

      Turn the comparison into an equation, then gather every term on one side so the other side is zero.

    Answer

    3n2−7n−20=03n^2-7n-20=03n2−7n−20=0, or equivalently −3n2+7n+20=0-3n^2+7n+20=0−3n2+7n+20=0.

    Full solution

    Three times the square of the number is 3n23n^23n2, and seven times the number is 7n7n7n.

    The comparison says

    3n2=7n+203n^2=7n+203n2=7n+20

    Move both right-hand terms across, leaving zero on the right.

    3n2−7n−20=03n^2-7n-20=03n2−7n−20=0

    The number is described as positive, so the equation is read with n>0n>0n>0.

    Answer

    3n2−7n−20=03n^2-7n-20=03n2−7n−20=0, or equivalently −3n2+7n+20=0-3n^2+7n+20=0−3n2+7n+20=0.

    Key idea

    Translating a comparison means writing every quantity with one unknown and gathering the terms on one side.

  3. Problem 3 Two areas

    A square has side length sss centimeters. A triangle has base 666 centimeters and height sss centimeters. The square has four times the triangle area. Find the side length sss.

    1. Hint 1

      Write each area using the same unknown length.

    2. Hint 2

      Set the two areas in the stated ratio and solve the equation that results.

    Answer

    s=12s=12s=12 centimeters.

    Full solution

    The triangle area is 3s3s3s square centimeters.

    The comparison gives

    s2=12ss^2=12ss2=12s

    Bring both terms to one side and factor out the common side length.

    s(s−12)=0s(s-12)=0s(s−12)=0

    The roots are 000 and 121212.

    A side length must be positive, so keep 121212.

    The square area is 144144144 and the triangle area is 363636 square centimeters, confirming the ratio.

    Answer

    s=12s=12s=12 centimeters.

    Key idea

    A comparison of areas can yield a zero root that the geometric situation excludes.

  4. Problem 4 A rising platform

    A ball starts 242424 feet above the ground with initial upward speed 404040 feet per second. At the same instant, a platform starts at the same height and rises steadily at 888 feet per second. Using the projectile model h=−16t2+v0t+h0h=-16t^2+v_0t+h_0h=−16t2+v0​t+h0​ for the ball, when after the start are the ball and platform at the same height again?

    1. Hint 1

      Write a height expression for each moving object.

    2. Hint 2

      Equate the heights and distinguish the starting instant from a later meeting.

    Answer

    222 seconds after the start.

    Full solution

    The ball height is −16t2+40t+24-16t^2+40t+24−16t2+40t+24 and the platform height is 8t+248t+248t+24, in feet.

    Equality gives

    −16t2+32t=0-16t^2+32t=0−16t2+32t=0

    Factor the common time.

    −16t(t−2)=0-16t(t-2)=0−16t(t−2)=0

    The roots are 000 and 222.

    The question asks for a later meeting, so the time is 222 seconds.

    At that instant both heights are 404040 feet.

    Answer

    222 seconds after the start.

    Key idea

    Matching two changing heights means equating their expressions and interpreting each resulting time.

  5. Problem 5 A trimmed square

    A square sheet measures 101010 centimeters on each side. A strip of width xxx is removed along one edge, and a strip of width 2x2x2x is removed along an adjacent edge. The remaining rectangle has area 484848 square centimeters. Find every value of xxx the sheet allows, and justify any root you discard.

    1. Hint 1

      The two remaining side lengths lose different amounts.

    2. Hint 2

      Multiply those lengths, solve the quadratic, and test each root against the sheet's 101010 centimeter sides.

    Answer

    x=2x=2x=2 centimeters; the root x=13x=13x=13 is rejected: it would leave side lengths of −3-3−3 and −16-16−16 centimeters.

    Full solution

    The remaining dimensions are 10−x10-x10−x and 10−2x10-2x10−2x centimeters.

    (10−x)(10−2x)=48(10-x)(10-2x)=48(10−x)(10−2x)=48

    Expand, move 484848 across, and divide by 222.

    x2−15x+26=0x^2-15x+26=0x2−15x+26=0

    Factor.

    (x−2)(x−13)=0(x-2)(x-13)=0(x−2)(x−13)=0

    The root 131313 would leave side lengths of −3-3−3 and −16-16−16 centimeters, which no sheet can have, so it is rejected.

    The root 222 leaves side lengths 888 and 666 centimeters, whose product is 484848 square centimeters.

    Answer

    x=2x=2x=2 centimeters; the root x=13x=13x=13 is rejected: it would leave side lengths of −3-3−3 and −16-16−16 centimeters.

    Key idea

    Every side remaining after a cut must stay positive when an algebraic root is interpreted.

  6. Problem 6 A triangular support

    A right triangle has legs xxx centimeters and 2x2x2x centimeters. Its hypotenuse is x+6x+6x+6 centimeters. Find the positive value of xxx exactly.

    1. Hint 1

      The hypotenuse square equals the sum of the leg squares.

    2. Hint 2

      After expanding, solve the quadratic and reject a nonpositive side length.

    Answer

    x=3+352x=\dfrac{3+3\sqrt5}{2}x=23+35​​ centimeters.

    Full solution

    The right triangle relation is

    x2+(2x)2=(x+6)2x^2+(2x)^2=(x+6)^2x2+(2x)2=(x+6)2

    Expanding and dividing by 444 gives

    x2−3x−9=0x^2-3x-9=0x2−3x−9=0

    The quadratic formula gives

    x=3±452x=\frac{3\pm\sqrt{45}}2x=23±45​​

    The negative choice is below zero.

    The positive choice is (3+35)/2(3+3\sqrt5)/2(3+35​)/2.

    For this value, x2=3x+9x^2=3x+9x2=3x+9, so 5x2=(x+6)25x^2=(x+6)^25x2=(x+6)2, checking the side relation.

    Answer

    x=3+352x=\dfrac{3+3\sqrt5}{2}x=23+35​​ centimeters.

    Key idea

    A right triangle model must satisfy both the squared side relation and positive side lengths.

  7. Problem 7 A sales target

    A vendor charges ppp dollars per item and sells 60−3p60-3p60−3p items, where ppp is a whole number with 5<p<205<p<205<p<20. Each item costs the vendor 555 dollars, and the total profit is the number of items sold times the amount by which the price exceeds that cost. Find every permitted price that gives a total profit of 108108108 dollars.

    1. Hint 1

      Both the number sold and the profit on one item change with the price, so the condition is a quadratic in ppp.

    2. Hint 2

      Multiply profit per item by the number sold, then test both roots against the price restriction.

    Answer

    888 dollars or 171717 dollars per item.

    Full solution

    The total profit condition is

    (p−5)(60−3p)=108(p-5)(60-3p)=108(p−5)(60−3p)=108

    Expand, gather terms, and divide by −3-3−3.

    p2−25p+136=0p^2-25p+136=0p2−25p+136=0

    Factor.

    (p−8)(p−17)=0(p-8)(p-17)=0(p−8)(p−17)=0

    Both prices are permitted whole numbers.

    At 888 dollars, profit is 3(36)=1083(36)=1083(36)=108 dollars; at 171717 dollars, it is 12(9)=10812(9)=10812(9)=108 dollars.

    Answer

    888 dollars or 171717 dollars per item.

    Key idea

    Total profit multiplies the profit on one item by the number sold, and both algebraic prices may be permitted.

  8. Problem 8 Two launch sites

    Two balls are launched with the same initial upward speed of 565656 feet per second. One starts at ground level and the other starts 262626 feet above the ground. A student says that if the lower ball never reaches 505050 feet, neither does the higher ball. Decide whether the claim is correct by examining the equations for height 505050 in the model h=−16t2+v0t+h0h=-16t^2+v_0t+h_0h=−16t2+v0​t+h0​.

    1. Hint 1

      The two models differ in their starting-height constants.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare the discriminants after setting each height to the target, and check that any real times are positive.

    Answer

    False; only the ball launched from 262626 feet reaches 505050 feet, at 0.50.50.5 second and at 333 seconds.

    Full solution

    Set the lower ball's height to the target.

    −16t2+56t=50-16t^2+56t=50−16t2+56t=50

    Move 505050 across and divide by −2-2−2.

    8t2−28t+25=08t^2-28t+25=08t2−28t+25=0

    Its discriminant is

    D=784−800D=784-800D=784−800

    This is −16-16−16, so no real time reaches the target.

    Now set the higher ball's height to the target.

    −16t2+56t+26=50-16t^2+56t+26=50−16t2+56t+26=50

    Move 505050 across and divide by −8-8−8.

    2t2−7t+3=02t^2-7t+3=02t2−7t+3=0

    Its discriminant is 49−24=2549-24=2549−24=25, which is positive, and the quadratic factors as

    (2t−1)(t−3)=0.(2t-1)(t-3)=0.(2t−1)(t−3)=0.

    The times are 0.50.50.5 second and 333 seconds, both positive and before the ball lands.

    Therefore the higher starting point changes whether the target is reached.

    Answer

    False; only the ball launched from 262626 feet reaches 505050 feet, at 0.50.50.5 second and at 333 seconds.

    Key idea

    Changing a starting height changes the discriminant of a target-height equation even when launch speeds match.

  9. Problem 9 A tile order

    A square arrangement uses the same whole number nnn of tiles in each row and column, with n>0n>0n>0. An order contains exactly the tiles for the square, one extra row of nnn tiles, and 666 spare tiles, for 303030 tiles altogether. A student concludes that no such arrangement is possible. Is the conclusion correct? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Write the total count as the square count plus the extra row plus the spares.

    2. Hint 2

      Bring every term to one side and solve the quadratic, then read each root against what nnn stands for.

    Answer

    Yes; no positive whole number nnn satisfies the order.

    Full solution

    The total count gives

    n2+n+6=30n^2+n+6=30n2+n+6=30

    Move 303030 across.

    n2+n−24=0n^2+n-24=0n2+n−24=0

    Its discriminant is 1+96=971+96=971+96=97, so the quadratic formula gives

    n=−1±972n=\frac{-1\pm\sqrt{97}}{2}n=2−1±97​​

    Since 81<97<10081<97<10081<97<100, the positive root lies between 444 and 4.54.54.5, so it is not a whole number, and the negative root is excluded by n>0n>0n>0.

    Checking directly, n=4n=4n=4 needs 262626 tiles and n=5n=5n=5 needs 363636 tiles, so no square arrangement fits an order of 303030.

    Answer

    Yes; no positive whole number nnn satisfies the order.

    Key idea

    A positive real root need not be a valid count when the unknown must be a whole number.

  10. Problem 10 A change in size

    A square originally has side length 888 centimeters. Each side changes by xxx centimeters, where a negative xxx means a decrease. The new area is 252525 square centimeters. Solving the area equation gives x=−3x=-3x=−3 and x=−13x=-13x=−13. A student rejects both because they are negative. Is that correct? Explain which changes are permitted.

    1. Hint 1

      The unknown describes a change, not the final side length.

    2. Hint 2

      Check the resulting side length for each candidate change.

    Answer

    No; x=−3x=-3x=−3 centimeters is permitted, and x=−13x=-13x=−13 centimeters is not.

    Full solution

    The final side length is 8+x8+x8+x, so the equation is

    (8+x)2=25(8+x)^2=25(8+x)2=25

    So 8+x=58+x=58+x=5 or 8+x=−58+x=-58+x=−5, giving x=−3x=-3x=−3 or x=−13x=-13x=−13, the two changes the statement reports.

    For x=−3x=-3x=−3, the actual side is 555 centimeters, which has area 252525 square centimeters.

    For x=−13x=-13x=−13, the proposed side is −5-5−5 centimeters, which is not a length.

    The negative change −3-3−3 is therefore valid.

    Answer

    No; x=−3x=-3x=−3 centimeters is permitted, and x=−13x=-13x=−13 centimeters is not.

    Key idea

    A negative change may be meaningful when it leaves the actual measured quantity positive.
