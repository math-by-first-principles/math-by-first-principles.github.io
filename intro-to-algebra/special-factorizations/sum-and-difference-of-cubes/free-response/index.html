    1. Cube, or not, and which sign goes where . Foundational, 9 points. Question 1 of 5.

    Foundational 9 pts

    A sum or a difference of two perfect cubes always factors the same way: a binomial times a trinomial, with SOAP deciding every sign. This question starts with recognizing the pattern and ends with defending one of its signs.

    1. A

    The answer

    Part A

    x3+216x^3+216x3+216 is a sum of cubes with a=x,b=6a=x,b=6a=x,b=6. x3−90x^3-90x3−90 is neither (909090 is not a perfect cube). x2−1000x^2-1000x2−1000 is neither (x2x^2x2 is a square term, not a cube).

    Part B

    64x3+27=(4x+3)(16x2−12x+9)64x^3+27=(4x+3)(16x^2-12x+9)64x3+27=(4x+3)(16x2−12x+9).

    Part C

    The middle sign must be minus, since a sum of cubes needs the middle term OPPOSITE the binomial's plus. Flipping it to plus would stop the two matching cross terms in the expansion from cancelling, so the product would no longer equal 64x3+2764x^3+2764x3+27.

    Worked solution

    Part A

    Test each expression against two requirements: both terms must be perfect cubes, and they must be joined by a plus or a minus sign.

    For x3+216x^3+216x3+216, x3=(x)3x^3=(x)^3x3=(x)3 and 216=63216=6^3216=63, and the terms are added.

    x3+216=x3+63x^3+216=x^3+6^3x3+216=x3+63

    Both conditions hold, so this is a sum of cubes with a=xa=xa=x and b=6b=6b=6.

    For x3−90x^3-90x3−90, the first term x3x^3x3 is a cube, but 909090 is not: 43=644^3=6443=64 and 53=1255^3=12553=125, and 909090 falls strictly between them. No whole number cubes to 909090, so this expression does not match the pattern.

    For x2−1000x^2-1000x2−1000, the number 1000=1031000=10^31000=103 is a perfect cube, but x2x^2x2 is a square, not a cube. A cube pattern needs BOTH terms to be cubes, so a mismatched power breaks it even when one term qualifies. This one is a difference of squares in disguise, not a difference of cubes.

    Part B

    Take the cube root of each term. Since 64x3=(4x)364x^3=(4x)^364x3=(4x)3 and 27=3327=3^327=33, this is a sum of cubes with a=4xa=4xa=4x and b=3b=3b=3. By SOAP, the binomial keeps the plus sign, and the trinomial's middle term takes the opposite (minus) sign, with a positive last term.

    64x3+27=(4x+3)((4x)2−(4x)(3)+32)=(4x+3)(16x2−12x+9).64x^3+27=(4x+3)\bigl((4x)^2-(4x)(3)+3^2\bigr)=(4x+3)(16x^2-12x+9).64x3+27=(4x+3)((4x)2−(4x)(3)+32)=(4x+3)(16x2−12x+9).

    The first trinomial term is (4x)2=16x2(4x)^2=16x^2(4x)2=16x2, the middle term is a single ab=12xab=12xab=12x with a minus sign, and the last term is 32=93^2=932=9.

    Part C

    The SOAP rule is not an arbitrary mnemonic; it reports what has to happen for the expansion to collapse to just two terms. Expand (4x+3)(16x2−12x+9)(4x+3)(16x^2-12x+9)(4x+3)(16x2−12x+9) and watch the middle terms:

    (4x+3)(16x2−12x+9)=64x3−48x2+36x+48x2−36x+27.(4x+3)(16x^2-12x+9)=64x^3-48x^2+36x+48x^2-36x+27.(4x+3)(16x2−12x+9)=64x3−48x2+36x+48x2−36x+27.

    The −48x2-48x^2−48x2 and +48x2+48x^2+48x2 are exact opposites and cancel, and so do +36x+36x+36x and −36x-36x−36x, leaving 64x3+2764x^3+2764x3+27.

    If the trinomial's middle sign were flipped to plus instead, the product (4x+3)(16x2+12x+9)(4x+3)(16x^2+12x+9)(4x+3)(16x2+12x+9) would pair +48x2+48x^2+48x2 with +48x2+48x^2+48x2 (reinforcing, not cancelling) and +36x+36x+36x with +36x+36x+36x. Neither middle pair would vanish, so the product would carry leftover x2x^2x2 and xxx terms and could not equal the two-term expression 64x3+2764x^3+2764x3+27. The minus sign is forced by the cancellation, not chosen by convention.

    In one line

    x3+216x^3+216x3+216 is a sum of cubes (a=x,b=6a=x,b=6a=x,b=6); x3−90x^3-90x3−90 and x2−1000x^2-1000x2−1000 are neither. 64x3+27=(4x+3)(16x2−12x+9)64x^3+27=(4x+3)(16x^2-12x+9)64x3+27=(4x+3)(16x2−12x+9), and the trinomial's middle sign must be minus because only that sign makes the expansion's cross terms cancel in opposite pairs.

    Worked solution

    Part A
