  1. Problem 1 Finding the missing constant

    The expression 8x3+C8x^3+C8x3+C has a sum-of-cubes factorization containing the binomial 2x+52x+52x+5. Find CCC.

    1. Hint 1

      A sum-of-cubes factorization has the form (a+b)(a2−ab+b2)(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2)(a+b)(a2−ab+b2), where aaa and bbb are the cube roots of the two terms.

    2. Hint 2

      Match 2x+52x+52x+5 against a+ba+ba+b to read off aaa and bbb, then write CCC in terms of bbb.

    Answer

    C=125C=125C=125.

    Full solution

    The cube roots are 2x2x2x and 555.

    The constant in the original sum is therefore

    C=53,C=5^3,C=53,

    giving C=125C=125C=125.

    The complete product is (2x+5)(4x2−10x+25)(2x+5)(4x^2-10x+25)(2x+5)(4x2−10x+25), whose constant is indeed 125125125.

    Answer

    C=125C=125C=125.

    Key idea

    The second term of the binomial is the cube root of the constant term, so cubing it recovers that constant.

  2. Problem 2 An adjusted product

    Simplify (6x−1)(36x2+6x+1)+1(6x-1)(36x^2+6x+1)+1(6x−1)(36x2+6x+1)+1.

    1. Hint 1

      The product has the sign pattern for a difference of cubes.

    2. Hint 2

      Identify the two cube roots before combining the added constant.

    Answer

    216x3216x^3216x3.

    Full solution

    The factors match the difference of cubes with terms 6x6x6x and 111, so the product is

    216x3−1.216x^3-1.216x3−1.

    Adding 111 cancels the constant, leaving 216x3216x^3216x3.

    At x=1x=1x=1 the original expression is 5⋅43+1=2165\cdot43+1=2165⋅43+1=216, which matches 216(1)3216(1)^3216(1)3.

    Answer

    216x3216x^3216x3.

    Key idea

    Recognizing a cube identity can expose a constant cancellation without a long expansion.

  3. Problem 3 A complete factorization

    Factor 81x3−37581x^3-37581x3−375 completely with integer coefficients.

    1. Hint 1

      Neither term is a perfect cube as written, so check for a common factor first.

    2. Hint 2

      Once the common factor is out, what is left inside the parentheses is a difference of cubes, and SOAP places its three signs.

    Answer

    3(3x−5)(9x2+15x+25)3(3x-5)(9x^2+15x+25)3(3x−5)(9x2+15x+25).

    Full solution

    Both terms are divisible by 333, since their digit sums 999 and 151515 are.

    Taking 333 out leaves

    27x3−12527x^3-12527x3−125

    inside the parentheses, and those two terms share no further common factor, so 333 was the greatest common factor.

    Since 27x3=(3x)327x^3=(3x)^327x3=(3x)3 and 125=53125=5^3125=53, that is a difference of cubes with a=3xa=3xa=3x and b=5b=5b=5.

    SOAP makes the binomial 3x−53x-53x−5, gives the trinomial the opposite middle sign with a single ab=15xab=15xab=15x, and keeps b2=25b^2=25b2=25 positive, so the complete factorization is

    3(3x−5)(9x2+15x+25).3(3x-5)(9x^2+15x+25).3(3x−5)(9x2+15x+25).

    Once a common factor is out and the cube identity is applied, the factorization is finished, so the trinomial is left alone.

    At x=1x=1x=1 the original expression is −294-294−294, and the product gives

    3(−2)(49)=−294.3(-2)(49)=-294.3(−2)(49)=−294.

    Answer

    3(3x−5)(9x2+15x+25)3(3x-5)(9x^2+15x+25)3(3x−5)(9x2+15x+25).

    Key idea

    SOAP places all three signs: the binomial takes the same sign, the middle term the opposite sign, and the last term stays positive.

  4. Problem 4 Combine, then factor

    Subtract 3x3+15363x^3+15363x3+1536 from 5x3+25605x^3+25605x3+2560, write the result as a completely factored product with integer coefficients, and evaluate it at x=1x=1x=1.

    1. Hint 1

      Combine like terms in the difference first.

    2. Hint 2

      Remove the numerical common factor, then recognize the remaining sum of cubes.

    Answer

    2(x+8)(x2−8x+64)2(x+8)(x^2-8x+64)2(x+8)(x2−8x+64); value at x=1x=1x=1: 102610261026.

    Full solution

    Subtracting term by term gives

    2x3+1024.2x^3+1024.2x3+1024.

    Removing the greatest common factor 222 leaves x3+512x^3+512x3+512 inside the parentheses.

    Since 512=83512=8^3512=83, that is a sum of cubes with a=xa=xa=x and b=8b=8b=8, so the complete product is

    2(x+8)(x2−8x+64).2(x+8)(x^2-8x+64).2(x+8)(x2−8x+64).

    At x=1x=1x=1 the factors give

    2(9)(57)=1026.2(9)(57)=1026.2(9)(57)=1026.

    Subtracting the two original expressions at x=1x=1x=1 gives 2565−1539=10262565-1539=10262565−1539=1026, which checks the result.

    Answer

    2(x+8)(x2−8x+64)2(x+8)(x^2-8x+64)2(x+8)(x2−8x+64); value at x=1x=1x=1: 102610261026.

    Key idea

    Combine like terms before factoring: a difference of two expressions can show a cube pattern that neither one shows on its own.

  5. Problem 5 Two related cube products

    Let P=(x+7)(x2−7x+49)P=(x+7)(x^2-7x+49)P=(x+7)(x2−7x+49) and Q=(x−7)(x2+7x+49)Q=(x-7)(x^2+7x+49)Q=(x−7)(x2+7x+49). Find P−QP-QP−Q and P+QP+QP+Q, each with like terms combined.

    1. Hint 1

      One product is a sum of cubes and the other is a difference of cubes.

    2. Hint 2

      Rewrite each using its two cube terms, then add and subtract.

    Answer

    P−Q=686P-Q=686P−Q=686; P+Q=2x3P+Q=2x^3P+Q=2x3.

    Full solution

    The cube identities give

    P=x3+343P=x^3+343P=x3+343

    and

    Q=x3−343.Q=x^3-343.Q=x3−343.

    Subtracting removes the cube terms and leaves 686686686, while adding removes the constants and leaves 2x32x^32x3.

    At x=1x=1x=1 the values are P=344P=344P=344 and Q=−342Q=-342Q=−342, so P−Q=686P-Q=686P−Q=686 and P+Q=2P+Q=2P+Q=2, which is 2(1)32(1)^32(1)3.

    Answer

    P−Q=686P-Q=686P−Q=686; P+Q=2x3P+Q=2x^3P+Q=2x3.

    Key idea

    Adding or subtracting a matched pair of cube products leaves twice the variable cube or twice the constant cube.

  6. Problem 6 A packing box

    A rectangular box has dimensions x+1x+1x+1 cm, x2−x+1x^2-x+1x2−x+1 cm, and 333 cm, where x>0x>0x>0. Its volume is 848484 cubic cm. Find xxx, showing how the product of the first two dimensions simplifies.

    1. Hint 1

      The first two dimensions form the factors of a sum of cubes.

    2. Hint 2

      Multiply that two-term result by the remaining dimension, then isolate the cube of xxx.

    Answer

    x=3x=3x=3; (x+1)(x2−x+1)=x3+1(x+1)(x^2-x+1)=x^3+1(x+1)(x2−x+1)=x3+1.

    Full solution

    The product of the first two dimensions is x3+1x^3+1x3+1.

    The volume condition is

    3(x3+1)=84.3(x^3+1)=84.3(x3+1)=84.

    Dividing by 333 and subtracting 111 gives x3=27x^3=27x3=27, so x=3x=3x=3.

    The dimensions are then 444 cm, 777 cm, and 333 cm, whose product is 848484 cubic cm.

    Answer

    x=3x=3x=3; (x+1)(x2−x+1)=x3+1(x+1)(x^2-x+1)=x^3+1(x+1)(x2−x+1)=x3+1.

    Key idea

    A cube factorization can simplify a three-dimensional volume condition before solving for a parameter.

  7. Problem 7 A missing multiplier

    Write 6x4−162x6x^4-162x6x4−162x as 2x2x2x times a second expression, then factor that second expression completely with integer coefficients.

    1. Hint 1

      Take the greatest common factor out of both terms before splitting off the 2x2x2x.

    2. Hint 2

      After 6x6x6x comes out, what is left inside the parentheses is a difference of cubes.

    Answer

    The second expression is 3x3−813x^3-813x3−81, that is 3(x3−27)3(x^3-27)3(x3−27), which factors as 3(x−3)(x2+3x+9)3(x-3)(x^2+3x+9)3(x−3)(x2+3x+9).

    Full solution

    The two terms share the factor 6x6x6x, and taking it out leaves

    x3−27x^3-27x3−27

    inside the parentheses.

    Writing 6x6x6x as 2x2x2x times 333 shows that the second expression is 3x3−813x^3-813x3−81, which is 3(x3−27)3(x^3-27)3(x3−27).

    Since 27=3327=3^327=33, that expression is a difference of cubes with a=xa=xa=x and b=3b=3b=3, so the complete factorization is

    3(x−3)(x2+3x+9).3(x-3)(x^2+3x+9).3(x−3)(x2+3x+9).

    No two integers have product 999 and sum 333, so the trinomial does not factor further.

    Multiplying by 2x2x2x returns 6x4−162x6x^4-162x6x4−162x, so nothing was divided away.

    At x=2x=2x=2 both forms give −228-228−228.

    Answer

    The second expression is 3x3−813x^3-813x3−81, that is 3(x3−27)3(x^3-27)3(x3−27), which factors as 3(x−3)(x2+3x+9)3(x-3)(x^2+3x+9)3(x−3)(x2+3x+9).

    Key idea

    Taking out the greatest common factor can reveal a difference of cubes inside an expression whose highest power is x4x^4x4.

  8. Problem 8 A forced middle coefficient

    For some positive number ddd and real constants K,mK,mK,m, the identity x3+K=(x+d)(x2+mx+9)x^3+K=(x+d)(x^2+mx+9)x3+K=(x+d)(x2+mx+9) holds for every real xxx. Find mmm, and name the two terms of the expanded right side that the identity forces to vanish once like terms are collected.

    1. Hint 1

      Two expressions that agree for every real xxx must have the same coefficient at each power of xxx.

    2. Hint 2

      Expand the right side, collect like terms, and compare coefficients with the left side power by power.

    Answer

    m=−3m=-3m=−3; the two terms that must vanish are the x2x^2x2 term and the xxx term of the expansion.

    Full solution

    Expand the right side: its x2x^2x2 coefficient is m+dm+dm+d, its xxx coefficient is 9+dm9+dm9+dm, and its constant is 9d9d9d.

    Since the two sides agree for every xxx, and the left side has no x2x^2x2 term and no xxx term, each of those two coefficients must be zero.

    From m+d=0m+d=0m+d=0 comes m=−dm=-dm=−d, and substituting that into 9+dm=09+dm=09+dm=0 gives

    9−d2=0.9-d^2=0.9−d2=0.

    Since d>0d>0d>0, this forces d=3d=3d=3, so m=−3m=-3m=−3.

    The identity is then x3+27=(x+3)(x2−3x+9)x^3+27=(x+3)(x^2-3x+9)x3+27=(x+3)(x2−3x+9), a sum of cubes whose middle terms cancel on expansion.

    Answer

    m=−3m=-3m=−3; the two terms that must vanish are the x2x^2x2 term and the xxx term of the expansion.

    Key idea

    Missing coefficients in a cube factorization are forced by the cancellation of both middle powers.

  9. Problem 9 A proposed replacement

    Let a,ba,ba,b be positive real numbers. A student replaces the trinomial in (a+b)(a2−ab+b2)(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2)(a+b)(a2−ab+b2) with (a−b)2(a-b)^2(a−b)2. Noor says this lowers the product by exactly ababab. Is Noor correct? Explain, and give the exact decrease.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare the original trinomial with the expanded square before multiplying by the common binomial.

    2. Hint 2

      The difference between the trinomials must also be multiplied by a+ba+ba+b.

    Answer

    No; the decrease is ab(a+b)ab(a+b)ab(a+b), not ababab.

    Full solution

    The replacement square is a2−2ab+b2a^2-2ab+b^2a2−2ab+b2.

    The original trinomial exceeds it by ababab, since its middle term is −ab-ab−ab rather than −2ab-2ab−2ab.

    Both trinomials are multiplied by the same factor a+ba+ba+b, so the decrease in the product is that ababab multiplied by a+ba+ba+b, which is

    ab(a+b).ab(a+b).ab(a+b).

    Noor compared the two trinomials but never multiplied by a+ba+ba+b, so the claimed decrease is too small.

    For a=3a=3a=3 and b=2b=2b=2 the products are 353535 and 555, a decrease of 303030, while ababab is only 666.

    Answer

    No; the decrease is ab(a+b)ab(a+b)ab(a+b), not ababab.

    Key idea

    A change inside one factor is multiplied by the other factor, so compare the products, not just the trinomials.

  10. Problem 10 Cubing a sum

    Ana writes (x+2)3=x3+8(x+2)^3=x^3+8(x+2)3=x3+8. Find every real xxx for which her equation is true, and explain why it is not an identity.

    1. Hint 1

      Cubing a sum is not the same as adding two cubes; the expansion of (x+2)3(x+2)^3(x+2)3 carries two middle terms.

    2. Hint 2

      Bring both sides together and factor what is left, rather than dividing, so that no solution is lost.

    Answer

    x=0x=0x=0 and x=−2x=-2x=−2; the equation is not an identity.

    Full solution

    Expanding (x+2)3(x+2)^3(x+2)3 as (x2+4x+4)(x+2)(x^2+4x+4)(x+2)(x2+4x+4)(x+2) and collecting terms gives

    x3+6x2+12x+8.x^3+6x^2+12x+8.x3+6x2+12x+8.

    Subtracting x3+8x^3+8x3+8 from both sides leaves

    6x2+12x=0.6x^2+12x=0.6x2+12x=0.

    Factoring the left side gives

    6x(x+2)=0,6x(x+2)=0,6x(x+2)=0,

    so x=0x=0x=0 or x=−2x=-2x=−2.

    Dividing by xxx instead of factoring would have lost the solution x=0x=0x=0.

    At x=0x=0x=0 both sides are 888, and at x=−2x=-2x=−2 both sides are 000.

    At x=1x=1x=1 the sides are 272727 and 999, so the equation fails there and cannot be an identity.

    Answer

    x=0x=0x=0 and x=−2x=-2x=−2; the equation is not an identity.

    Key idea

    A cube of a sum carries middle terms that a sum of cubes does not, so the two agree only at special inputs.
