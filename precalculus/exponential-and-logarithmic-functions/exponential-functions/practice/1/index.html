    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Every power is positive. The curve can get arbitrarily close to zero without attaining it and can grow arbitrarily large.

    4x>04^x>04x>0

    Zero is excluded; negative outputs do not occur; 4−1=1/44^{-1}=1/44−1=1/4 shows that values below 111 do occur.
