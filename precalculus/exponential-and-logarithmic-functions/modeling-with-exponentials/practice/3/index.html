    At one input, an observed output is 181818 and a model predicts 151515. What is the residual when the comparison is made in natural-log output values?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The transformed residual is observed log output minus predicted log output.

    ln⁡18−ln⁡15=ln⁡(6/5)\ln18-\ln15=\ln(6/5)ln18−ln15=ln(6/5)

    It is positive. The original-unit residual is three, but the logarithm of that difference is not the difference of the logarithms.

    A trial observes cooling from minute 000 through minute 888 in fixed surroundings and reports that the excess halves every 333 minutes. The surrounding temperature changes abruptly after minute 888. Which prediction is best supported by the original fixed-ambient model?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    6<86<86<8

    The halving applies to excess above ambient, and fixed-ambient exponential cooling from above does not cross that ambient at a finite time.
