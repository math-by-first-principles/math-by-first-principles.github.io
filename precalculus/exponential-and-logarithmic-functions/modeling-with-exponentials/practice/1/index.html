    Which axis choice is used to look for exponential behavior in positive output data?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    An exponential y=Pbxy=Pb^xy=Pbx satisfies a line equation after taking the logarithm of its positive output.

    Only the output axis is logarithmic. A logarithmic axis cannot include zero.

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution
