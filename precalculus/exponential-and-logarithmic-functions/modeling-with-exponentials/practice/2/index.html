    A positive amount is 323232 units at t=3t=3t=3 and 108108108 units at t=6t=6t=6. Which exponential model fits both readings?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The time gap is three and the total factor is 108/32=27/8108/32=27/8108/32=27/8.

    b3=27/8b^3=27/8b3=27/8

    Thus b=3/2b=3/2b=3/2. Anchoring at t=3t=3t=3 gives 32(3/2)t−332(3/2)^{t-3}32(3/2)t−3, which returns 108108108 when the exponent is three.

    A positive amount grows by a factor of 1.51.51.5 over 888 hours. What is the continuous coefficient kkk per hour for the same exponential curve?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    An eight-hour interval multiplies PektPe^{kt}Pekt by e8ke^{8k}e8k.

    e8k=1.5e^{8k}=1.5e8k=1.5

    Taking natural logarithms and dividing by eight gives k=ln⁡(1.5)/8k=\ln(1.5)/8k=ln(1.5)/8. Dividing the effective increase 0.50.50.5 by eight would assume additive rates.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    At x=1x=1x=1, the logarithm is zero, so A=2A=2A=2. The second point gives 2+2B=82+2B=82+2B=8.

    An object cools in a room at 18∘C18^\circ\text{C}18∘C. Its temperature is 82∘C82^\circ\text{C}82∘C initially and 50∘C50^\circ\text{C}50∘C after 555 minutes. Assuming exponential decay of excess, what temperature does the model predict at 151515 minutes?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The excess falls from 646464 to 323232 degrees in five minutes, so fifteen minutes is three half-lives.

    T(15)=18+64(1/2)3=26T(15)=18+64(1/2)^3=26T(15)=18+64(1/2)3=26

    Halving 828282 itself ignores ambient temperature; using two halvings would predict 343434 degrees.

    Three positive outputs at equally spaced times are 202020, 303030, and 424242. Which statement about an exact model A(t)=PbtA(t)=Pb^tA(t)=Pbt is correct?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Equal time gaps in an exponential model require equal ratios.

    30/20=1.530/20=1.530/20=1.5

    The next ratio is 42/30=1.442/30=1.442/30=1.4. Since these differ, no one constant factor fits all three exactly.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Square each residual before adding.

    SA=4+0+4=8S_A=4+0+4=8SA​=4+0+4=8

    For B, the total is 1+1+1=31+1+1=31+1+1=3. Opposite signs cancel in a sum of residuals, but not in their squared total.

    A model has continuous coefficient 0.240.240.24 per year. If the same curve is written with time mmm in months, what is the exponent?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The same elapsed time is t=m/12t=m/12t=m/12 years.

    Measurements at inputs x=2,4,8,16x=2,4,8,16x=2,4,8,16 give outputs 1,4,7,101,4,7,101,4,7,10. Which candidate fits all four?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Each doubling of the positive input adds three to the output. The logarithm log⁡2(x/2)\log_2(x/2)log2​(x/2) is 0,1,2,30,1,2,30,1,2,3 at the listed inputs.

    y=1+3log⁡2(x/2)y=1+3\log_2(x/2)y=1+3log2​(x/2)

    The unshifted logarithm gives 444 at x=2x=2x=2, and the linear and exponential candidates do not give 444 at x=4x=4x=4.

    A positive population is modeled exponentially from observations over days 000 through 444. A proposed day-202020 prediction exceeds the enclosure capacity, which is known to be fixed. What is the appropriate interpretation?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The formula may accept t=20t=20t=20, but the physical situation imposes another restriction. A prediction outside the observed interval is extrapolation.

    20>420>420>4

    Exceeding a fixed capacity shows that the unchanged growth assumption cannot remain valid that far ahead.
