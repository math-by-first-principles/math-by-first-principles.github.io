    For f(x)=−8x−5/6f(x)=-8x^{-5/6}f(x)=−8x−5/6, which combined domain-and-sign statement is correct?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The even reduced denominator excludes negative inputs. The reciprocal also excludes zero. At every remaining input the rooted power is positive.

    f(x)=−8(x6)5f(x)=\frac{-8}{(\sqrt[6]{x})^5}f(x)=(6x​)5−8​

    The outside negative coefficient, rather than the exponent's minus sign, makes these outputs negative.

  2. 2

    Which statement about f(x)=x−2/3f(x)=x^{-2/3}f(x)=x−2/3 on its natural real domain is correct?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The cube root accepts both signs, its square is positive away from zero, and the reciprocal excludes zero.

    f(−x)=1(−x3)2f(-x)=\frac1{(\sqrt[3]{-x})^2}f(−x)=(3−x​)21​

    Negating a cube root leaves its square unchanged, so f(-x)=f(x). The odd denominator alone does not make the full function odd.

  3. 3

    A polynomial on R\mathbb RR has positive even degree and positive leading coefficient. Which additional conclusion is guaranteed?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The leading term controls the far ends, while symmetry and bending can depend on the lower terms. For example, the polynomial below has an odd-power term.

    P(x)=x6+2x+5P(x)=x^6+2x+5P(x)=x6+2x+5

    Both ends rise but opposite inputs do not generally have equal outputs. The leading information alone also does not establish concavity everywhere.

    A polynomial has coefficient row −2,0,5,0,−1,3-2,0,5,0,-1,3−2,0,5,0,−1,3 in descending powers starting at x5x^5x5. For H(x)=x2P(x)+9H(x)=x^2P(x)+9H(x)=x2P(x)+9, what are the degree and far-right behavior of HHH?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The row starts with leading term -2x to the fifth power; zeros in the row do not erase its highest term. Multiplication by x squared raises its exponent by 2.

    Leading term of H=−2x7\text{Leading term of }H=-2x^7Leading term of H=−2x7

    Adding a constant cannot cancel that term. A negative leading coefficient makes the right end fall.

  5. 5

    A plotting window shows P(x)=−x5+500x2+20P(x)=-x^5+500x^2+20P(x)=−x5+500x2+20 positive near both window edges. Which assessment of its far ends is justified by its formula?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The negative fifth-power term controls the far ends even though a lower-power term has a large coefficient.

    P(x)=x5(−1+500x3+20x5)P(x)=x^5\left(-1+\frac{500}{x^3}+\frac{20}{x^5}\right)P(x)=x5(−1+x3500​+x520​)

    Far enough from zero, the bracket stays negative. The fifth power has opposite signs at opposite ends, giving left up and right down. A finite plot can hide that eventual pattern.

  6. 6

    A quadratic is sampled at x=−1,1,3,5x=-1,1,3,5x=−1,1,3,5 with outputs 12,4,8,2412,4,8,2412,4,8,24. What is its average rate from x=5x=5x=5 to x=7x=7x=7?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The first differences are -8, 4, and 16, each increasing by 12. The quadratic assumption justifies the next difference.

    16+12=2816+12=2816+12=28

    That increase occurs over an input width of 2.

    282=14\frac{28}{2}=14228​=14

    The next output would be 52, but the question requests the rate, not the output or its change.

  7. 7

    For Q(x)=ax2+bx+cQ(x)=ax^2+bx+cQ(x)=ax2+bx+c with a≠0a\ne0a=0, the average rates on successive intervals of width 444 decrease by 202020. What is the constant second output difference at that input step?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Each output difference equals the interval's rate times its width. Because every width is 4, a change of -20 in rates changes first differences by 4 times that amount.

    Δ2Q=4(−20)=−80\Delta^2Q=4(-20)=-80Δ2Q=4(−20)=−80

    The sign stays negative. Dividing by 4 would confuse converting an output difference into a rate with the reverse conversion needed here.

  8. 8

    Two consecutive intervals each have width 222. Their average rates are −4-4−4 and 999. What is the average rate across their combined interval?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Convert each rate into an output change before combining the intervals.

    Total change=2(−4)+2(9)=10\text{Total change}=2(-4)+2(9)=10Total change=2(−4)+2(9)=10 Total width=4\text{Total width}=4Total width=4 Average rate=104=52\text{Average rate}=\frac{10}{4}=\frac52Average rate=410​=25​

    Equal interval widths make this the arithmetic average of the two rates. Simply adding rates would miss the larger total width.

  9. 9

    A polynomial graph is rising through (2,7)(2,7)(2,7). Its secant slopes decrease throughout an interval just left of 222 and increase throughout an interval just right of 222, with a genuine concavity change there. What is (2,7)(2,7)(2,7)?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The stated slope patterns establish downward bending on the left and upward bending on the right. Their change defines an inflection. Rising through the point does not supply a switch between rising and falling, so no turning-point conclusion follows.

  11. 11

    A known quadratic q(x)=ax2+bx+cq(x)=ax^2+bx+cq(x)=ax2+bx+c has q(0)=2q(0)=2q(0)=2, q(2)=14q(2)=14q(2)=14, and q(4)=42q(4)=42q(4)=42. Which statement is correct?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The two-unit first differences are 12 and 28, giving second difference 16.

    2a(2)2=162a(2)^2=162a(2)2=16 a=2a=2a=2

    For any nonzero equal step, 2a times the squared step is positive. This describes increasing rates, which can still be negative on some intervals. A quadratic with nonzero constant term need not be a power function.

  12. 12

    The quadratic model q(x)=x2−x+6q(x)=x^2-x+6q(x)=x2−x+6 and another function ggg agree at inputs 0,1,2,30,1,2,30,1,2,3. No further information about ggg is given. Which claim is justified?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    An average rate uses only endpoint values. At any two distinct listed inputs those endpoint values match, so their rates match too.

    g(b)−g(a)b−a=q(b)−q(a)b−a\frac{g(b)-g(a)}{b-a}=\frac{q(b)-q(a)}{b-a}b−ag(b)−g(a)​=b−aq(b)−q(a)​

    This equality is established for listed endpoints a and b only. No value at an unlisted endpoint or whole-interval bending pattern has been supplied.
