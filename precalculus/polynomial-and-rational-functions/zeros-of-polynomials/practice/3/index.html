    A degree-6 polynomial has real coefficients. Its zero 8 has multiplicity 3, and −3+i-3+i−3+i has multiplicity 1. What must remain after including the forced conjugate?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The conjugate -3-i has multiplicity 1, so five copies are accounted for.

    3+1+1=53+1+1=53+1+1=5

    Exactly one copy remains. A further nonreal zero would need a second copy for its conjugate. It must therefore be real and simple, and it is not 8 because the multiplicity of 8 was stated exactly.

    A cubic with real coefficients and leading coefficient 1 has zero −2+5i-2+5i−2+5i and constant coefficient −58-58−58. What is its remaining real zero?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The forced pair gives the real quadratic factor (x+2)2+25=x2+4x+29(x+2)^2+25=x^2+4x+29(x+2)2+25=x2+4x+29. The leading coefficient 1 lets us write the cubic as (x2+4x+29)(x−r)(x^2+4x+29)(x-r)(x2+4x+29)(x−r). Its constant is -29r.

    −29r=−58-29r=-58−29r=−58

    Which evidence certifies a complete list for a degree-4 polynomial?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Four distinct exact zeros require four copies of factors, exhausting degree 4. Rounded candidates may not be exact, a real graph omits nonreal zeros, and a rational candidate list still needs testing.

    m1+m2+m3+m4=4m_1+m_2+m_3+m_4=4m1​+m2​+m3​+m4​=4

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    6=2(3)6=2(3)6=2(3)

    A degree-5 polynomial has real coefficients and exactly two distinct real zeros: −4-4−4 of multiplicity 1 and 6 of multiplicity 2. How many nonreal zeros does it have, counted with multiplicity?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The exact real multiplicities account for three copies.

    5−(1+2)=25-(1+2)=25−(1+2)=2

    No other real zero is allowed by the statement, so the two remaining copies are nonreal and form a conjugate pair. A picture without the multiplicity information would not establish this count.
