    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The correction has smaller numerator degree than denominator degree, so it shrinks toward zero far away.

    G(x)−(3x−2)=4x+1x2+7G(x)-(3x-2)=\frac{4x+1}{x^2+7}G(x)−(3x−2)=x2+74x+1​

    The polynomial part gives the slant line. Its growing linear term rules out a horizontal asymptote.
