    How many terms are in the geometric sequence 1,  12,  14,  …,  110241,\; \tfrac{1}{2},\; \tfrac{1}{4},\; \ldots,\; \tfrac{1}{1024}1,21​,41​,…,10241​?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Each term is (12)n−1\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^{n-1}(21​)n−1, and the last term is 11024=(12)10\tfrac{1}{1024} = \left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^{10}10241​=(21​)10, since 210=10242^{10} = 1024210=1024.

    n−1=10⟹n=11n - 1 = 10 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad n = 11n−1=10⟹n=11

    The terms are the powers (12)0\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^0(21​)0 through (12)10\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^{10}(21​)10, which is 111111 of them: one more than the highest exponent.

  2. 2

    Evaluate 1+2+4+8+⋯+10241 + 2 + 4 + 8 + \cdots + 10241+2+4+8+⋯+1024.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    With a=1a = 1a=1 and r=2r = 2r=2 the general term is 2 n−12^{\,n-1}2n−1, and the last term is 1024=2101024 = 2^{10}1024=210, so n−1=10n - 1 = 10n−1=10 and there are 111111 terms.

    S11=1(211−1)2−1=2048−1=2047S_{11} = \frac{1(2^{11} - 1)}{2 - 1} = 2048 - 1 = 2047S11​=2−11(211−1)​=2048−1=2047

    A run of doublings starting at 111 always adds to one less than the next power of two. Counting only 101010 terms would give 102310231023.

  3. 3

    Every term of a geometric sequence equals −3-3−3. What is the sum of its first 100100100 terms?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Identical terms mean the common ratio is 111, and the sum formula would divide by 1−r=01 - r = 01−r=0, so it cannot be used. Add the terms as they stand.

    S100=100⋅(−3)=−300S_{100} = 100 \cdot (-3) = -300S100​=100⋅(−3)=−300

    The sum is not undefined. When r=1r = 1r=1 the sum is nanana, here one hundred copies of −3-3−3. Only the formula fails, not the sum.

    A geometric sequence has a1=−2a_1 = -2a1​=−2 and r=−3r = -3r=−3. What is a6a_6a6​?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

  6. 6

    An account of 500050005000 dollars earns 666 percent interest a year, compounded annually. Which expression gives the balance after 101010 years?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Interest of 666 percent a year multiplies the balance by 1+0.06=1.061 + 0.06 = 1.061+0.06=1.06 every year, so the yearly balances form a geometric sequence with r=1.06r = 1.06r=1.06, not 0.060.060.06.

    balance after 10 years=5000(1.06)10\text{balance after } 10 \text{ years} = 5000(1.06)^{10}balance after 10 years=5000(1.06)10

    Multiplying by 0.060.060.06 would destroy almost all of the money each year. The expression 5000+10(0.06)(5000)5000 + 10(0.06)(5000)5000+10(0.06)(5000) adds the same amount annually, which is simple interest, not compounding.

  7. 7

    A geometric series has first term 555, last term 405405405, and common ratio 333. What is its sum?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The sum formula needs the number of terms, so get nnn from the last term.

    5⋅3 n−1=405⟹3 n−1=81=34⟹n=55 \cdot 3^{\,n-1} = 405 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3^{\,n-1} = 81 = 3^4 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad n = 55⋅3n−1=405⟹3n−1=81=34⟹n=5

    Now add the five terms.

    S5=5(35−1)3−1=5⋅2422=605S_5 = \frac{5(3^5 - 1)}{3 - 1} = \frac{5 \cdot 242}{2} = 605S5​=3−15(35−1)​=25⋅242​=605

    Checking by hand: 5+15+45+135+405=6055 + 15 + 45 + 135 + 405 = 6055+15+45+135+405=605. Forgetting to divide by r−1=2r - 1 = 2r−1=2 would give 121012101210.

    S12=12⋅7=84S_{12} = 12 \cdot 7 = 84S12​=12⋅7=84

  10. 10

    A sheet of paper 0.10.10.1 mm thick is folded in half 202020 times. Roughly how thick is the result?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Each fold doubles the thickness, so the thicknesses form a geometric sequence with r=2r = 2r=2 starting at 0.10.10.1 mm, and 202020 folds means 202020 doublings.

    0.1⋅220=0.1⋅1,048,576=104,857.6 mm0.1 \cdot 2^{20} = 0.1 \cdot 1{,}048{,}576 = 104{,}857.6 \text{ mm}0.1⋅220=0.1⋅1,048,576=104,857.6 mm

    Dividing by 100010001000 converts millimetres to metres, giving about 105105105 metres, roughly a thirty-storey building. Doubling twenty times multiplies by more than a million, which is why the answer feels impossible.

    A geometric sequence with a negative common ratio has a3=12a_3 = 12a3​=12 and a5=48a_5 = 48a5​=48. What is a4a_4a4​?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Terms two places apart differ by two multiplications, so a5=a3r2a_5 = a_3 r^2a5​=a3​r2.

    r2=4812=4⟹r=±2r^2 = \frac{48}{12} = 4 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad r = \pm 2r2=1248​=4⟹r=±2

    The ratio is negative, so r=−2r = -2r=−2, and the fourth term is one multiplication past the third.

    a4=12⋅(−2)=−24a_4 = 12 \cdot (-2) = -24a4​=12⋅(−2)=−24

    The run 12,−24,4812, -24, 4812,−24,48 has ratio −2-2−2 throughout, and the two sign changes cancel to leave a5a_5a5​ positive.
