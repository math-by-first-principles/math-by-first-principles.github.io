  1. 1

    Which of these sequences is geometric?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A sequence is geometric only when every quotient of consecutive terms is the same.

    105=2,2010=2,4020=2\frac{10}{5} = 2, \qquad \frac{20}{10} = 2, \qquad \frac{40}{20} = 2510​=2,1020​=2,2040​=2

    So 5,10,20,405, 10, 20, 405,10,20,40 has the constant ratio r=2r = 2r=2. The sequence 2,4,6,82, 4, 6, 82,4,6,8 has a constant difference instead, and in 1,4,9,161, 4, 9, 161,4,9,16 the quotients 4/1=44/1 = 44/1=4 and 9/4=2.259/4 = 2.259/4=2.25 disagree, as do those of 3,6,10,153, 6, 10, 153,6,10,15.

    A geometric sequence has a1=3a_1 = 3a1​=3 and r=5r = 5r=5. Which expression gives its nnnth term?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Reaching the nnnth term from the first means multiplying by rrr once for each of the n−1n - 1n−1 gaps between them.

    an=a1r n−1=3⋅5 n−1a_n = a_1 r^{\,n-1} = 3 \cdot 5^{\,n-1}an​=a1​rn−1=3⋅5n−1

    Check it at n=1n = 1n=1: the formula gives 3⋅50=33 \cdot 5^0 = 33⋅50=3, the first term, as it must. An exponent of nnn would wrongly report the first term as 151515.

  3. 3

    What is the common ratio of 96,  48,  24,  12,…96,\; 48,\; 24,\; 12,\ldots96,48,24,12,…?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Divide consecutive terms.

    4896=12,2448=12,1224=12\frac{48}{96} = \frac{1}{2}, \qquad \frac{24}{48} = \frac{1}{2}, \qquad \frac{12}{24} = \frac{1}{2}9648​=21​,4824​=21​,2412​=21​

    The terms are halving, so r=12r = \tfrac{1}{2}r=21​. A ratio smaller than 111 in size makes the terms shrink, and the ratio is positive because no term changes sign.

    What is the sum of the first five terms of 2,  4,  8,…2,\; 4,\; 8,\ldots2,4,8,…?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Here a=2a = 2a=2, r=2r = 2r=2, and n=5n = 5n=5.

    S5=2(25−1)2−1=2(32−1)=62S_5 = \frac{2(2^5 - 1)}{2 - 1} = 2(32 - 1) = 62S5​=2−12(25−1)​=2(32−1)=62

    Checking by hand: 2+4+8+16+32=622 + 4 + 8 + 16 + 32 = 622+4+8+16+32=62. Stopping after four terms would give 303030.

  5. 5

    A geometric sequence has a1=7a_1 = 7a1​=7 and r=3r = 3r=3. What is a3a_3a3​?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The third term sits two multiplications past the first, so the exponent is 3−1=23 - 1 = 23−1=2.

    a3=a1r2=7⋅32=7⋅9=63a_3 = a_1 r^2 = 7 \cdot 3^2 = 7 \cdot 9 = 63a3​=a1​r2=7⋅32=7⋅9=63

    The sequence starts 7,21,637, 21, 637,21,63.

    A geometric sequence starts at 100100100 and has ratio r=12r = \tfrac{1}{2}r=21​. What is its fifth term?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The fifth term needs 5−1=45 - 1 = 45−1=4 multiplications by 12\tfrac{1}{2}21​.

    a5=100(12)4=10016=6.25a_5 = 100 \left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^4 = \frac{100}{16} = 6.25a5​=100(21​)4=16100​=6.25

    The terms run 100,50,25,12.5,6.25100, 50, 25, 12.5, 6.25100,50,25,12.5,6.25, so the fifth is 6.256.256.25 and the fourth is 12.512.512.5.

  7. 7

    Is the sequence 3,  6,  9,  123,\; 6,\; 9,\; 123,6,9,12 geometric?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Test the ratios, not the differences.

    63=2,96=1.5\frac{6}{3} = 2, \qquad \frac{9}{6} = 1.536​=2,69​=1.5

    The two quotients disagree, so the sequence is not geometric. Its terms do all differ by the same amount (333 each time), which makes it arithmetic, so the reason offered in the third option is false.

  8. 8

    What is the common ratio of 12,  16,  118,…\tfrac{1}{2},\; \tfrac{1}{6},\; \tfrac{1}{18},\ldots21​,61​,181​,…?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Divide the second term by the first, remembering that dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal.

    r=1/61/2=16⋅21=13r = \frac{1/6}{1/2} = \frac{1}{6} \cdot \frac{2}{1} = \frac{1}{3}r=1/21/6​=61​⋅12​=31​

    The next pair agrees, since 1/181/6=13\dfrac{1/18}{1/6} = \tfrac{1}{3}1/61/18​=31​ as well, so r=13r = \tfrac{1}{3}r=31​.

  9. 9

    In the geometric sequence 3,  6,  12,  24,…3,\; 6,\; 12,\; 24,\ldots3,6,12,24,…, the value 242424 is which term?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Count the multiplications rather than guessing. Starting at 333, one doubling gives 666, two give 121212, and three give 242424.

    a4=3⋅24−1=3⋅8=24a_4 = 3 \cdot 2^{4-1} = 3 \cdot 8 = 24a4​=3⋅24−1=3⋅8=24

    Three multiplications land on the fourth term, because the exponent is always one less than the term number.

  10. 10

    What is the sum of the first three terms of 5,  15,  45,…5,\; 15,\; 45,\ldots5,15,45,…?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Here a=5a = 5a=5, r=3r = 3r=3, and n=3n = 3n=3.

    S3=5(33−1)3−1=5⋅262=65S_3 = \frac{5(3^3 - 1)}{3 - 1} = \frac{5 \cdot 26}{2} = 65S3​=3−15(33−1)​=25⋅26​=65

    Adding directly: 5+15+45=655 + 15 + 45 = 655+15+45=65.

  11. 11

    A geometric sequence has a1=2a_1 = 2a1​=2 and r=3r = 3r=3. What is a5a_5a5​?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The fifth term is four multiplications past the first, so the exponent is 444, not 555.

    a5=2⋅35−1=2⋅81=162a_5 = 2 \cdot 3^{5-1} = 2 \cdot 81 = 162a5​=2⋅35−1=2⋅81=162

    The sequence runs 2,6,18,54,1622, 6, 18, 54, 1622,6,18,54,162. Using 353^535 by mistake gives 486486486, which is the sixth term.

  12. 12

    A geometric sequence has a1=4a_1 = 4a1​=4 and a2=20a_2 = 20a2​=20. What is its common ratio?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The common ratio is any term divided by the one before it.

    r=a2a1=204=5r = \frac{a_2}{a_1} = \frac{20}{4} = 5r=a1​a2​​=420​=5

    Each term is five times the last, so the sequence continues 4,20,100,500,…4, 20, 100, 500, \ldots4,20,100,500,…. The value 161616 is the difference, which is what an arithmetic sequence would use.
