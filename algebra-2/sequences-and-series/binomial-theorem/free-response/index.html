    The answer

    Part A

    Row 999 is 1,9,36,84,126,126,84,36,9,11, 9, 36, 84, 126, 126, 84, 36, 9, 11,9,36,84,126,126,84,36,9,1.

    Part B

    The definition of (9k)\binom{9}{k}(k9​) does not depend on which row it sits in: it is the number of ways to choose which kkk of the 999 factors of (x+y)9(x+y)^9(x+y)9 donate a yyy instead of an xxx.     A choice of which 444 of the 999 factors donate a yyy automatically decides which 555 donate an xxx: fix one and the other is forced, since every factor donates exactly one letter.     Worked solution

    Part A

    Write out (n1)\binom{n}{1}(1n​) using the closed form and cancel (n−1)!(n-1)!(n−1)! against the tail of n!n!n!.

    (n1)=n!1!(n−1)!=n⋅(n−1)!(n−1)!=n\binom{n}{1} = \frac{n!}{1!(n-1)!} = \frac{n \cdot (n-1)!}{(n-1)!} = n(1n​)=1!(n−1)!n!​=(n−1)!n⋅(n−1)!​=n

    Do the same for (n2)\binom{n}{2}(2n​), canceling (n−2)!(n-2)!(n−2)! against the tail of n!n!n!.

    (n2)=n!2!(n−2)!=n(n−1)(n−2)!2⋅(n−2)!=n(n−1)2\binom{n}{2} = \frac{n!}{2!(n-2)!} = \frac{n(n-1)(n-2)!}{2 \cdot (n-2)!} = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}(2n​)=2!(n−2)!n!​=2⋅(n−2)!n(n−1)(n−2)!​=2n(n−1)​

    Part B

    Substitute the two expressions from part A into the given equation.

    n(n−1)2=5n\frac{n(n-1)}{2} = 5n2n(n−1)​=5n

    Multiply both sides by 222 to clear the fraction, then move everything to one side and factor.

    n(n−1)=10n⟹n2−11n=0⟹n(n−11)=0n(n-1) = 10n \quad\Longrightarrow\quad n^2 - 11n = 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad n(n-11)=0n(n−1)=10n⟹n2−11n=0⟹n(n−11)=0

    n=11n=11n=11

    Part C

    Pascal's rule builds one row from the row before it, always moving forward and never backward: to reach row 111111 you would have to build every row from 000 up to it in order, and the only way to know you have reached the right row is to already suspect the answer is 111111.
