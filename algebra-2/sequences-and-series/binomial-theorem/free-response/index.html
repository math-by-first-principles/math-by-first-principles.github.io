  1. Problem 1 A cubic expression

    Expand (x2+2y)3(x^2+2y)^3(x2+2y)3 fully.

    1. Hint 1

      Treat x2x^2x2 and 2y2y2y as the two complete terms of the binomial.

    2. Hint 2

      The cubic coefficients are 1,3,3,11,3,3,11,3,3,1; each power applies to the coefficient as well as the variable.

    Answer

    x6+6x4y+12x2y2+8y3x^6+6x^4y+12x^2y^2+8y^3x6+6x4y+12x2y2+8y3.

    Full solution

    Using A=x2A=x^2A=x2 and B=2yB=2yB=2y, the four contributions are A3A^3A3, 3A2B3A^2B3A2B, 3AB23AB^23AB2, and B3B^3B3.

    They simplify to x6x^6x6, 6x4y6x^4y6x4y, 12x2y212x^2y^212x2y2, and 8y38y^38y3.

    Thus, writing F=(x2+2y)3F=(x^2+2y)^3F=(x2+2y)3,

    F=x6+6x4y+12x2y2+8y3F=x^6+6x^4y+12x^2y^2+8y^3F=x6+6x4y+12x2y2+8y3

    At x=y=1x=y=1x=y=1, both the original and expanded expressions are 272727, providing a check.

    Answer

    x6+6x4y+12x2y2+8y3x^6+6x^4y+12x^2y^2+8y^3x6+6x4y+12x2y2+8y3.

    Key idea

    A binomial expansion raises each complete term, including its coefficient and internal exponent.

  2. Problem 2 A difference of powers

    Expand and simplify (x+1)4−(x−1)4(x+1)^4-(x-1)^4(x+1)4−(x−1)4.

    1. Hint 1

      The two expansions share their even-power terms.

    2. Hint 2

      Expand each fourth power, then subtract the entire second expansion.

    Answer

    8x3+8x8x^3+8x8x3+8x.

    Full solution

    The first expansion is x4+4x3+6x2+4x+1x^4+4x^3+6x^2+4x+1x4+4x3+6x2+4x+1.

    The second is x4−4x3+6x2−4x+1x^4-4x^3+6x^2-4x+1x4−4x3+6x2−4x+1.

    Subtracting cancels the terms in x4x^4x4, x2x^2x2, and the constants; the others double.

    (x+1)4−(x−1)4=8x3+8x(x+1)^4-(x-1)^4=8x^3+8x(x+1)4−(x−1)4=8x3+8x

    At x=1x=1x=1, both sides equal 161616.

    Answer

    8x3+8x8x^3+8x8x3+8x.

    Key idea

    Subtracting opposite-sign binomial expansions cancels the terms whose signs agree.

  3. Problem 3 A squared variable

    Find the coefficient of t7t^7t7 in (t+t2)5(t+t^2)^5(t+t2)5.

    Full solution

    The target 777 requires k=2k=2k=2.

    (52)=10\binom52=10(25​)=10

    Thus the term is 10t710t^710t7.

    Factoring out t5t^5t5 from t5(1+t)5t^5(1+t)^5t5(1+t)5 gives the same coefficient.

    Key idea

    When both binomial terms contain the variable, combine their exponents before choosing the term index.

  4. Problem 4 A paired product

    Expand (x+1)3(x−1)3(x+1)^3(x-1)^3(x+1)3(x−1)3 as a polynomial in descending powers of xxx.

    1. Hint 1

      Equal powers can be combined into one power of the product.

    2. Hint 2

      The product of x+1x+1x+1 and x−1x-1x−1 is a difference of squares.

    Answer

    x6−3x4+3x2−1x^6-3x^4+3x^2-1x6−3x4+3x2−1.

    Full solution

    Combine the two cubic powers.

    (x+1)3(x−1)3=(x2−1)3(x+1)^3(x-1)^3=(x^2-1)^3(x+1)3(x−1)3=(x2−1)3

    Now expand the cube using A=x2A=x^2A=x2 and B=−1B=-1B=−1.

    The result is x6−3x4+3x2−1x^6-3x^4+3x^2-1x6−3x4+3x2−1.

    Substituting x=1x=1x=1 gives zero on both sides, and x=0x=0x=0 gives −1-1−1 on both sides.

    Answer

    x6−3x4+3x2−1x^6-3x^4+3x^2-1x6−3x4+3x2−1.

    Key idea

    Combining a conjugate pair before expanding can reduce the number of binomial terms to handle.

  5. Problem 5 A coefficient balance

    Multiplying by 222 gives

    n2+n−132=0n^2+n-132=0n2+n−132=0

    so

    (n−11)(n+12)=0.(n-11)(n+12)=0.(n−11)(n+12)=0.

    The allowed positive integer is n=11n=11n=11.

    The coefficients are (111)=11\binom{11}1=11(111​)=11 and (112)=55\binom{11}2=55(211​)=55, and their sum is 666666.

    The negative root is not an allowable row number.

    Answer

  7. Problem 7 An unknown constant

    Let ccc be a nonzero real number. In (x+c)5(x+c)^5(x+c)5, the coefficient of x3x^3x3 is the negative of the coefficient of x2x^2x2. Find ccc and the two coefficients.

    1. Hint 1

      Write the two coefficients using the number of constant terms chosen.

    2. Hint 2

      The assumption c≠0c\ne0c=0 permits canceling a power of c in the coefficient equation.

    Answer

    c=−1c=-1c=−1; coefficient of x3x^3x3 is 101010, and coefficient of x2x^2x2 is −10-10−10.

    Full solution

    The coefficients are (52)c2=10c2\binom52c^2=10c^2(25​)c2=10c2 and

    (53)c3=10c3.\binom53c^3=10c^3.(35​)c3=10c3.

    The condition gives

    10c2=−10c310c^2=-10c^310c2=−10c3

    Since 10c2≠010c^2\ne010c2=0, division gives 1=−c1=-c1=−c, so c=−1c=-1c=−1.

    Substitution gives 101010 and −10-10−10, which have the required relationship.

    Answer

    c=−1c=-1c=−1; coefficient of x3x^3x3 is 101010, and coefficient of x2x^2x2 is −10-10−10.

    Key idea

    Comparing binomial coefficients can determine a parameter when any canceled factors are known to be nonzero.

  10. Problem 10 Signs of whole terms

    Let u<0u<0u<0 and v>0v>0v>0. Without expanding fully, determine the sign of every term in (2u−v)5(2u-v)^5(2u−v)5, and explain how this fits with its alternating coefficients.

    1. Hint 1

      The sign of a coefficient and the sign of a whole term are different questions.

    2. Hint 2

    Answer

    Every one of the six terms is negative; the coefficients alternate in sign, and so do the powers of uuu.

    Full solution

    k=0,1,…,5.k=0,1,\ldots,5.k=0,1,…,5.

    Since 2u<02u<02u<0 and −v<0-v<0−v<0, the term is a positive count times a product of 5−k5-k5−k plus kkk, that is five, negative factors, so every term is negative.

    The coefficients (5k)25−k(−1)k\binom5k2^{5-k}(-1)^k(k5​)25−k(−1)k alternate in sign with kkk, and the monomials u5−kvku^{5-k}v^ku5−kvk alternate in sign too, because u<0u<0u<0.

    The two alternations cancel, leaving every term negative.

    Answer

    Every one of the six terms is negative; the coefficients alternate in sign, and so do the powers of uuu.

    Key idea

    A term's sign depends on both its coefficient and the signs of the variables, so alternating coefficients need not give alternating terms.
