  1. Problem 1 A rule in squares

    The sequence an=(n+2)2−n2a_n=(n+2)^2-n^2an​=(n+2)2−n2 is defined for positive integers nnn. Find its common difference.

    1. Hint 1

      Simplify the rule before comparing consecutive terms.

    2. Hint 2

      The quadratic terms cancel when the difference of squares is expanded.

    Answer

    d=4d=4d=4.

    Full solution

    Expansion gives

    an=4n+4a_n=4n+4an​=4n+4

    Therefore replacing nnn with n+1n+1n+1 increases the value by 444 for every index.

    The first three terms are 8,12,168,12,168,12,16, consistent with that difference.

    Answer

    d=4d=4d=4.

    Key idea

    A rule that simplifies to a linear expression in the index defines an arithmetic sequence.

  2. Problem 2 A two-step change

    In an arithmetic sequence, a20−a7=39a_{20}-a_7=39a20​−a7​=39. Find a101−a99a_{101}-a_{99}a101​−a99​.

    1. Hint 1

      The given change covers thirteen equal steps.

    2. Hint 2

      The requested change covers two steps of that same size.

    Answer

    666.

    Full solution

    The common difference is d=39/13=3d=39/13=3d=39/13=3, since the index gap is 20−7=1320-7=1320−7=13.

    The requested gap is 101−99=2101-99=2101−99=2, so

    a101−a99=2(3)a_{101}-a_{99}=2(3)a101​−a99​=2(3)

    giving 666.

    Answer

    666.

    Key idea

    Differences between separated arithmetic terms depend on their index gap rather than their absolute positions.

  3. Problem 3 A length from a total

    A finite arithmetic sequence starts at −7-7−7, ends at 171717, and has sum 959595. How many terms does it contain?

    1. Hint 1

      The average of an arithmetic list equals the average of its two endpoints.

    2. Hint 2

      Divide the total by that average.

    Answer

    191919 terms.

    Full solution

    The endpoint average is −7+172=5\frac{-7+17}2=52−7+17​=5, and the sum of an arithmetic list is its number of terms times that average, so 95=5n95=5n95=5n and n=19n=19n=19.

    As a check, 191919 terms from −7-7−7 to 171717 take 181818 equal steps, so d=2418=43d=\frac{24}{18}=\frac43d=1824​=34​, a consistent common difference.

    Answer

    191919 terms.

    Key idea

    A nonzero endpoint average determines an arithmetic list's length from its total.

  4. Problem 4 Two conditions on entries

    An arithmetic sequence satisfies a2+a8=22a_2+a_8=22a2​+a8​=22 and a8−a2=−42a_8-a_2=-42a8​−a2​=−42. Find its first term and common difference, and check both conditions.

    1. Hint 1

      First recover the two terms from their combined value and their difference.

    2. Hint 2

      Adding the two given equations removes one of the unknown terms.

    3. Hint 3

      Count the steps between indices 222 and 888 before finding the common difference.

    Answer

    a1=39a_1=39a1​=39 and d=−7d=-7d=−7.

    Full solution

    Adding the two conditions gives

    2a8=−20a8=−10.\begin{aligned} 2a_8&=-20 \\ a_8&=-10. \end{aligned}2a8​a8​​=−20=−10.​

    The sum condition then gives a2=22−(−10)a_2=22-(-10)a2​=22−(−10), so a2=32a_2=32a2​=32.

    There are 8−2=68-2=68−2=6 steps between the two terms.

    Each step has the same difference, so

    d=−10−326=−7.\begin{aligned} d&=\frac{-10-32}{6} \\ &=-7. \end{aligned}d​=6−10−32​=−7.​

    The first term is one step before a2a_2a2​, giving a1=32+7a_1=32+7a1​=32+7, that is, 393939.

    For a check, this sequence gives a2=39−7=32a_2=39-7=32a2​=39−7=32 and a8=39+7(−7)a_8=39+7(-7)a8​=39+7(−7), which is −10-10−10.

    Their sum is 222222 and their difference is −42-42−42, as required.

    Answer

    a1=39a_1=39a1​=39 and d=−7d=-7d=−7.

    Key idea

    Combined information about two terms can be separated before their index gap determines the common difference.

  5. Problem 5 A descending schedule

    A printer schedules batches of 464646 labels at first and 333 fewer labels in each following batch, ending with a batch of 161616 labels. Planned batch 444 is canceled, and all the remaining planned batches are printed at their planned sizes. How many batches are printed, and how many labels are printed altogether?

    1. Hint 1

      Work out the complete planned arithmetic schedule first.

    2. Hint 2

      Then remove the canceled batch from both the count and the total.

    Answer

    101010 batches; 304304304 labels.

    Full solution

    The planned difference is d=−3d=-3d=−3.

    The number of steps is (16−46)/(−3)=10(16-46)/(-3)=10(16−46)/(−3)=10, so the plan has 111111 batches, and its total is

    S=112(46+16)S=\frac{11}{2}(46+16)S=211​(46+16)

    which is 341341341 labels.

    Planned batch 444 holds 46+3(−3)=3746+3(-3)=3746+3(−3)=37 labels.

    Removing it leaves 101010 batches and 341−37=304341-37=304341−37=304 labels.

    Answer

    101010 batches; 304304304 labels.

    Key idea

    Counting arithmetic terms requires one more than the number of equal changes between the endpoints, before any term is removed.

  6. Problem 6 Production and a rising target

    The total produced after nnn days is Sn=3n2+8nS_n=3n^2+8nSn​=3n2+8n items for integers n≥0n\ge0n≥0. The daily target is 484848 items on day one and rises by 444 items each day. Find the common difference in daily production and the first day on which production meets or exceeds that day's target. State the production and the target on that day.

    1. Hint 1

      The amount made on a day is the increase in the cumulative total on that day.

    2. Hint 2

      Subtract successive totals, write the daily target as an arithmetic term rule, and solve the comparison for a whole-number day.

    Answer

    d=6d=6d=6 items; day 202020, with production 125125125 items and target 124124124 items.

    Full solution

    The daily production is

    an=Sn−Sn−1.a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}.an​=Sn​−Sn−1​.

    Since Sn−1=3n2+2n−5S_{n-1}=3n^2+2n-5Sn−1​=3n2+2n−5, this gives an=6n+5a_n=6n+5an​=6n+5 for n≥1n\ge1n≥1, which rises by d=6d=6d=6 items per day.

    The target on day nnn is

    48+4(n−1)=4n+44.48+4(n-1)=4n+44.48+4(n−1)=4n+44.

    Production meets it when 6n+5≥4n+446n+5\ge4n+446n+5≥4n+44, that is, 2n≥392n\ge392n≥39, so the first such day is n=20n=20n=20.

    On day 202020 production is 125125125 items against a target of 124124124.

    On day 191919 it was 119119119 against 120120120, so day 202020 is the first.

    Answer

    d=6d=6d=6 items; day 202020, with production 125125125 items and target 124124124 items.

    Key idea

    Successive differences of a quadratic cumulative total give a linear term rule that can be compared with another arithmetic rule.

  7. Problem 7 An incomplete total

    A record pairs the 121212 terms of an arithmetic sequence from the ends inward. It includes a1+a12a_1+a_{12}a1​+a12​, a2+a11a_2+a_{11}a2​+a11​, a3+a10a_3+a_{10}a3​+a10​, a4+a9a_4+a_9a4​+a9​, and a5+a8a_5+a_8a5​+a8​, but omits a6+a7a_6+a_7a6​+a7​. The five recorded pair totals add to 170170170. Find the missing pair total and the sum S12S_{12}S12​ of all 121212 terms. Justify why every pair has the same total, and explain how adding the sequence in forward and reverse order gives the same sum.

    1. Hint 1

      Moving one position inward increases one member of a pair by the common difference and decreases the other by that amount.

    2. Hint 2

      Express ak+a13−ka_k+a_{13-k}ak​+a13−k​ in terms of the first term and common difference.

    3. Hint 3

      Use the five recorded pairs to recover the common pair total; forward and reversed rows together count the sum twice.

    Answer

    Missing pair total 343434; S12=204S_{12}=204S12​=204.

    Full solution

    Let the common difference be ddd.

    For an end-to-end pair, the two terms are a1+(k−1)da_1+(k-1)da1​+(k−1)d and a1+(12−k)da_1+(12-k)da1​+(12−k)d.

    Their step counts add to 111111, so

    ak+a13−k=2a1+11d.a_k+a_{13-k}=2a_1+11d.ak​+a13−k​=2a1​+11d.

    The right side does not depend on kkk, proving that every pair has the same total.

    Call that total CCC.

    The five recorded totals give

    5C=170C=34.\begin{aligned} 5C&=170 \\ C&=34. \end{aligned}5CC​=170=34.​

    Thus the omitted pair also totals 343434.

    The six pairs cover every term once, so their complete sum is 6(34)=2046(34)=2046(34)=204.

    Writing all 121212 terms forward and then in reverse produces 121212 columns, each totaling CCC, and counts the original sum twice.

    Therefore

    2S12=12(34)S12=204.\begin{aligned} 2S_{12}&=12(34) \\ S_{12}&=204. \end{aligned}2S12​S12​​=12(34)=204.​

    This agrees with restoring the missing pair:

    170+34=204.170+34=204.170+34=204.

    Answer

    Missing pair total 343434; S12=204S_{12}=204S12​=204.

    Key idea

    Equal end-to-end pair totals let an incomplete arithmetic-sum record determine the whole sum.

  8. Problem 8 An average claim

    For an arithmetic sequence with 151515 terms, a student claims its average equals a8a_8a8​. Is this true even if some terms are negative? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare terms the same distance from the middle index.

    2. Hint 2

      Each such pair differs from a8a_8a8​ by opposite multiples of ddd, which may both be zero.

    Answer

    Yes; the average is a8a_8a8​.

    Full solution

    The pairs a8−ja_{8-j}a8−j​ and a8+ja_{8+j}a8+j​ total 2a82a_82a8​ for

    j=1,2,…,7.j=1,2,\ldots,7.j=1,2,…,7.

    Seven pairs plus the middle term give

    S15=15a8S_{15}=15a_8S15​=15a8​

    Dividing by 151515 gives average a8a_8a8​.

    This argument uses differences and works regardless of the terms' signs.

    Answer

    Yes; the average is a8a_8a8​.

    Key idea

    The average of an odd-length arithmetic list is its middle term, including when terms are negative.

  10. Problem 10 A shifted list

    Full solution

    bn=an+10.b_n=a_n+10.bn​=an​+10.

    bn+1−bn=an+1−anb_{n+1}-b_n=a_{n+1}-a_nbn+1​−bn​=an+1​−an​

    because the added 101010 cancels from the subtraction.

    The difference remains −4-4−4; adding 101010 moves the entire list without changing the gaps.

    Answer

    No; the new common difference is −4-4−4.

    Key idea

    Adding the same constant to every arithmetic term preserves its common difference.
