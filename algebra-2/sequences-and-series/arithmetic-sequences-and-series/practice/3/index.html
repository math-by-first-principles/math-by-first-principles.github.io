    If aaa, bbb, ccc are three consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence, which statement must be true?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Consecutive terms means the two differences are the same, so b−a=c−bb - a = c - bb−a=c−b.

    Add a+ba + ba+b to both sides of that equation.

    b−a=c−b  ⇒  2b=a+cb - a = c - b \;\Rightarrow\; 2b = a + cb−a=c−b⇒2b=a+c

    The middle term is therefore the average of its neighbours, which is exactly what an arithmetic mean is. The other three statements all fail on a plain example such as 5,8,115, 8, 115,8,11, where a+c=16a + c = 16a+c=16 but b=8b = 8b=8, a+b=13a + b = 13a+b=13 but 2c=222c = 222c=22, and c−a=6c - a = 6c−a=6 but b=8b = 8b=8.

    How many three-digit numbers are divisible by 777?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The smallest three-digit multiple of 777 is 105=7⋅15105 = 7 \cdot 15105=7⋅15 and the largest is 994=7⋅142994 = 7 \cdot 142994=7⋅142, and they form an arithmetic sequence with d=7d = 7d=7.

    n=994−1057+1=8897+1=127+1=128n = \frac{994 - 105}{7} + 1 = \frac{889}{7} + 1 = 127 + 1 = 128n=7994−105​+1=7889​+1=127+1=128

    The 127127127 counts the steps between the ends, and the +1+1+1 counts the term you started on. Checking the multipliers agrees: they run from 151515 to 142142142, and 142−15+1=128142 - 15 + 1 = 128142−15+1=128.

    For the sequence 20,17,14,…20, 17, 14, \ldots20,17,14,…, which value of nnn makes the sum SnS_nSn​ as large as possible?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The running total grows as long as the terms being added are positive, and shrinks once they go negative. So find the last positive term.

    an=20+(n−1)(−3)=23−3na_n = 20 + (n-1)(-3) = 23 - 3nan​=20+(n−1)(−3)=23−3n

    This stays positive while n<233≈7.67n < \frac{23}{3} \approx 7.67n<323​≈7.67, so a7=2a_7 = 2a7​=2 is the last positive term and a8=−1a_8 = -1a8​=−1 is the first negative one.

    Adding a8a_8a8​ would pull the total down, so the maximum is at n=7n = 7n=7, where S7=72(20+2)=77S_7 = \frac{7}{2}(20 + 2) = 77S7​=27​(20+2)=77 (against S8=76S_8 = 76S8​=76).

    Three numbers are inserted between 444 and 242424 so that all five numbers form an arithmetic sequence. What are the three numbers?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    With three numbers inserted, the finished sequence has 555 terms, so there are 444 steps from the first to the last.

    d=24−45−1=204=5d = \frac{24 - 4}{5 - 1} = \frac{20}{4} = 5d=5−124−4​=420​=5

    Now step up from 444 by fives.

    4,  9,  14,  19,  244, \; 9, \; 14, \; 19, \; 244,9,14,19,24

    Dividing by 555 (the number of terms) instead of 444 (the number of steps) would give d=4d = 4d=4 and the wrong list 8,12,168, 12, 168,12,16.

    In an arithmetic sequence, a2+a14=30a_2 + a_{14} = 30a2​+a14​=30. What is a8a_8a8​?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    In an arithmetic sequence the total ai+aja_i + a_jai​+aj​ depends only on i+ji + ji+j, because

    ai+aj=(a1+(i−1)d)+(a1+(j−1)d)=2a1+(i+j−2)d.a_i + a_j = \big(a_1 + (i-1)d\big) + \big(a_1 + (j-1)d\big) = 2a_1 + (i + j - 2)d.ai​+aj​=(a1​+(i−1)d)+(a1​+(j−1)d)=2a1​+(i+j−2)d.

    Here 2+14=162 + 14 = 162+14=16 and 8+8=168 + 8 = 168+8=16, so the pair (a2,a14)(a_2, a_{14})(a2​,a14​) has the same total as (a8,a8)(a_8, a_8)(a8​,a8​).

    2a8=30  ⇒  a8=152a_8 = 30 \;\Rightarrow\; a_8 = 152a8​=30⇒a8​=15

    This is the same identity that makes every column of the forwards-and-backwards sum come out equal.

  11. 11

    Three consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence have sum 272727 and product 693693693. What is the largest of the three?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Name the terms m−dm - dm−d, mmm, and m+dm + dm+d, centring them on the middle one. The sum then collapses.

    (m−d)+m+(m+d)=3m=27  ⇒  m=9(m - d) + m + (m + d) = 3m = 27 \;\Rightarrow\; m = 9(m−d)+m+(m+d)=3m=27⇒m=9

    The outer two multiply to m2−d2m^2 - d^2m2−d2, so the product gives a second equation.

    9(81−d2)=693  ⇒  81−d2=77  ⇒  d2=49(81 - d^2) = 693 \;\Rightarrow\; 81 - d^2 = 77 \;\Rightarrow\; d^2 = 49(81−d2)=693⇒81−d2=77⇒d2=4

    So d=2d = 2d=2 or d=−2d = -2d=−2, and either way the three terms are 777, 999, and 111111. The largest is 111111.
