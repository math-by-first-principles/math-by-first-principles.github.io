    What is the value of ∑n=1∞5 n6 n+1\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{5^{\,n}}{6^{\,n+1}}n=1∑∞​6n+15n​?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Substituting n=1n = 1n=1 gives the first term 536\dfrac{5}{36}365​, and each step multiplies the numerator by 555 and the denominator by 666, so r=56r = \tfrac56r=65​.

    The structure is easier to see after pulling out a constant: since 6 n+1=6⋅6 n6^{\,n+1} = 6 \cdot 6^{\,n}6n+1=6⋅6n, the general term is 16(56) n\dfrac{1}{6}\left(\dfrac56\right)^{\,n}61​(65​)n.

    S=5361−56=53616=536⋅6=56S = \frac{\tfrac{5}{36}}{1 - \tfrac56} = \frac{\tfrac{5}{36}}{\tfrac16} = \frac{5}{36}\cdot 6 = \frac56S=1−65​365​​=61​365​​=365​⋅6=65​

    A ratio of 56\tfrac5665​ is close to 111, so this series converges slowly, but it converges.

  2. 2

    A patient takes a 200200200 mg dose of a drug once a day. In each day the body clears 34\tfrac3443​ of whatever is present, so 14\tfrac1441​ of it survives to the next dose. Over the long run, what amount does the quantity in the body just after a dose close in on?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Track what is left of each past dose at the moment of the newest one. Today's dose contributes 200200200 mg, yesterday's has been cut to 200(14)200\left(\tfrac14\right)200(41​), the one before to 200(14)2200\left(\tfrac14\right)^2200(41​)2, and so on.

    200+200(14)+200(14)2+⋯200 + 200\left(\tfrac14\right) + 200\left(\tfrac14\right)^2 + \cdots200+200(41​)+200(41​)2+⋯

    That is geometric with a=200a = 200a=200 and r=14r = \tfrac14r=41​, and ∣14∣<1\left|\tfrac14\right| < 1​41​​<1, so it converges.

    S=2001−14=20034=8003≈266.7 mgS = \frac{200}{1 - \tfrac14} = \frac{200}{\tfrac34} = \frac{800}{3} \approx 266.7 \text{ mg}S=1−41​200​=43​200​=3800​≈266.7 mg

    The level never exceeds this, and it never quite reaches it either. It closes in on it, which is exactly what a convergent series describes.

  3. 3

    A ball is dropped from 101010 meters, and after each bounce it rises to 35\tfrac3553​ of the height it fell from. What is the total vertical distance it travels before coming to rest?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Separate the trip into the first drop and the rebounds, because the first drop happens only once while every rebound height is travelled twice, once up and once down.

    The rebound heights are 666, then 6(35)6\left(\tfrac35\right)6(53​), and so on, a geometric series with a=6a = 6a=6 and r=35r = \tfrac35r=53​.

    sum of rebound heights=61−35=625=15\text{sum of rebound heights} = \frac{6}{1 - \tfrac35} = \frac{6}{\tfrac25} = 15sum of rebound heights=1−53​6​=52​6​=15

    Now assemble the total distance. It is the initial 101010 meter drop plus twice the rebound total.

    10+2(15)=40 meters10 + 2(15) = 40 \text{ meters}10+2(15)=40 meters

    Forgetting the factor of 222 gives 252525, and forgetting the initial drop gives 303030. Both are on the list.

  4. 4

    Written as a fraction in lowest terms, what is 0.45‾0.4\overline{5}0.45 (that is, 0.45555…0.45555\ldots0.45555…)?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The 444 does not repeat, so peel it off and treat only the 555s as a series.

    0.45‾=410+(5100+51000+⋯ )0.4\overline{5} = \frac{4}{10} + \left(\frac{5}{100} + \frac{5}{1000} + \cdots\right)0.45=104​+(1005​+10005​+⋯)

    The bracket has a=5100a = \tfrac{5}{100}a=1005​ and r=110r = \tfrac{1}{10}r=101​, so it sums to 5/1009/10=5100⋅109=118\dfrac{5/100}{9/10} = \dfrac{5}{100}\cdot\dfrac{10}{9} = \dfrac{1}{18}9/105/100​=1005​⋅910​=181​.

    0.45‾=25+118=3690+590=41900.4\overline{5} = \frac{2}{5} + \frac{1}{18} = \frac{36}{90} + \frac{5}{90} = \frac{41}{90}0.45=52​+181​=9036​+905​=9041​

    The decoy 4599\tfrac{45}{99}9945​ treats 454545 as the repeating block, but that is 0.454545…0.454545\ldots0.454545…, and 920\tfrac{9}{20}209​ is the terminating 0.450.450.45.

  5. 5

    For exactly which values of xxx does ∑n=1∞(x+25) n−1\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left(\tfrac{x+2}{5}\right)^{\,n-1}n=1∑∞​(5x+2​)n−1 converge?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The first term is (x+25)0=1\left(\tfrac{x+2}{5}\right)^0 = 1(5x+2​)0=1, never zero, so the criterion applies to the ratio r=x+25r = \tfrac{x+2}{5}r=5x+2​.

    ∣x+25∣<1  ⟺  ∣x+2∣<5\left|\frac{x+2}{5}\right| < 1 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; |x+2| < 5​5x+2​​<1⟺∣x+2∣<5

    Unpack the absolute value into a double inequality and subtract 222 throughout.

    −5<x+2<5  ⟺  −7<x<3-5 < x + 2 < 5 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; -7 < x < 3−5<x+2<5⟺−7<x<3

    The interval is centred at x=−2x = -2x=−2, where the ratio is 000, and it reaches 555 units in each direction. Sliding the centre is what the +2+2+2 does.

  6. 6

    What does ∑n=1∞(x+25) n−1\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left(\tfrac{x+2}{5}\right)^{\,n-1}n=1∑∞​(5x+2​)n−1 equal when x=1x = 1x=1?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Evaluate the ratio at x=1x = 1x=1.

    r=1+25=35r = \frac{1+2}{5} = \frac35r=51+2​=53​

    That value lies inside −7<x<3-7 < x < 3−7<x<3, so the series converges, and its first term is 111.

    S=11−35=125=52S = \frac{1}{1 - \tfrac35} = \frac{1}{\tfrac25} = \frac52S=1−53​1​=52​1​=25​

    Dividing by rrr rather than 1−r1 - r1−r would give 53\tfrac5335​, which is the trap here.

  7. 7

    An equilateral triangle has perimeter 363636. A second triangle is formed by joining the midpoints of its sides, so each of its sides is half as long, and the process repeats forever. What is the total perimeter of all the triangles?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Halving every side halves the perimeter, so the perimeters are 363636, 181818, 999, and so on: a geometric series with a=36a = 36a=36 and r=12r = \tfrac12r=21​.

    S=361−12=3612=72S = \frac{36}{1 - \tfrac12} = \frac{36}{\tfrac12} = 72S=1−21​36​=21​36​=72

    Any geometric series with ratio 12\tfrac1221​ totals exactly twice its first term, so the infinitely many triangles have the same total perimeter as two copies of the original.

  8. 8

    A triangle has area 100100100. The triangle formed by joining the midpoints of its sides has one quarter of that area, and the process repeats forever. What is the total area of all the triangles, the first one included?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The areas are 100100100, then 252525, then 254\tfrac{25}{4}425​, and so on, so this is geometric with a=100a = 100a=100 and r=14r = \tfrac14r=41​.

    S=1001−14=10034=4003≈133.3S = \frac{100}{1 - \tfrac14} = \frac{100}{\tfrac34} = \frac{400}{3} \approx 133.3S=1−41​100​=43​100​=3400​≈133.3

    The answer 1003\tfrac{100}{3}3100​ is the total of the inscribed triangles only, that is, the sum with the first term left out, and it is worth checking that 100+1003=4003100 + \tfrac{100}{3} = \tfrac{400}{3}100+3100​=3400​ as it should.

  9. 9

    What is the value of ∑n=2∞3(25) n\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} 3\left(\tfrac25\right)^{\,n}n=2∑∞​3(52​)n?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The index starts at n=2n = 2n=2, so the first term of this sum is the n=2n = 2n=2 term, and aaa in the formula must be that term.

    a=3(25)2=3⋅425=1225,r=25a = 3\left(\tfrac25\right)^2 = 3 \cdot \frac{4}{25} = \frac{12}{25}, \qquad r = \frac25a=3(52​)2=3⋅254​=2512​,r=52​

    Now apply the formula.

    S=12251−25=122535=1225⋅53=45S = \frac{\tfrac{12}{25}}{1 - \tfrac25} = \frac{\tfrac{12}{25}}{\tfrac35} = \frac{12}{25}\cdot\frac53 = \frac45S=1−52​2512​​=53​2512​​=2512​⋅35​=54​

    The value 1225\tfrac{12}{25}2512​ is only the first term, which is what you get by stopping too early.

  10. 10

    For which value of rrr does ∑n=1∞8r n−1\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} 8r^{\,n-1}n=1∑∞​8rn−1 equal 666?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The first term is 888, so the sum formula gives an equation in rrr.

    81−r=6  ⟹  1−r=86=43  ⟹  r=1−43=−13\frac{8}{1-r} = 6 \;\Longrightarrow\; 1 - r = \frac86 = \frac43 \;\Longrightarrow\; r = 1 - \frac43 = -\frac131−r8​=6⟹1−r=68​=34​⟹r=1−34​=−31​

    The sign is no surprise: the total 666 is smaller than the first term 888, so the later terms must be subtracting on balance, which requires a negative ratio. Its size is 13<1\tfrac13 < 131​<1, so the series does converge, and the answer is legitimate.

  11. 11

    The series 2+2(0.6)+2(0.6)2+⋯2 + 2(0.6) + 2(0.6)^2 + \cdots2+2(0.6)+2(0.6)2+⋯ has sum 555. What is the fewest number of terms you must add for the partial sum to exceed 4.994.994.99?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Exceeding 4.994.994.99 means the error is under 0.010.010.01, and the error after nnn terms is exactly S r n=5(0.6)nS\,r^{\,n} = 5(0.6)^nSrn=5(0.6)n.

    5(0.6)n<0.01  ⟺  (0.6)n<0.0025(0.6)^n < 0.01 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; (0.6)^n < 0.0025(0.6)n<0.01⟺(0.6)n<0.002

    Take logarithms and divide by log⁡(0.6)≈−0.2218\log(0.6) \approx -0.2218log(0.6)≈−0.2218, reversing the inequality because that number is negative.

    n>log⁡(0.002)log⁡(0.6)=−2.699−0.2218≈12.17n > \frac{\log(0.002)}{\log(0.6)} = \frac{-2.699}{-0.2218} \approx 12.17n>log(0.6)log(0.002)​=−0.2218−2.699​≈12.17

    So n=13n = 13n=13 terms are needed. Check the boundary: 5(0.6)12≈0.01095(0.6)^{12} \approx 0.01095(0.6)12≈0.0109, which leaves the partial sum at about 4.9894.9894.989, a whisker short, while 5(0.6)13≈0.00655(0.6)^{13} \approx 0.00655(0.6)13≈0.0065 clears the bar.

    What is the value of ∑n=1∞(12) n−1(13) n−1\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left(\tfrac12\right)^{\,n-1}\left(\tfrac13\right)^{\,n-1}n=1∑∞​(21​)n−1(31​)n−1?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Two powers with the same exponent combine into one, since x ky k=(xy) kx^{\,k}y^{\,k} = (xy)^{\,k}xkyk=(xy)k.

    (12) n−1(13) n−1=(16) n−1\left(\tfrac12\right)^{\,n-1}\left(\tfrac13\right)^{\,n-1} = \left(\tfrac16\right)^{\,n-1}(21​)n−1(31​)n−1=(61​)n−1

    So this is an ordinary geometric series with a=1a = 1a=1 and r=16r = \tfrac16r=61​, which converges.

    S=11−16=156=65S = \frac{1}{1 - \tfrac16} = \frac{1}{\tfrac56} = \frac65S=1−61​1​=65​1​=56​

    The two series on their own sum to 222 and to 32\tfrac3223​, and the answer is neither their sum 72\tfrac7227​ nor their product 333. The terms were multiplied one at a time, which is a different thing from multiplying the two totals, so combine the ratios first and sum once.
