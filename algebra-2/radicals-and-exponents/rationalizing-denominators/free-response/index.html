    A nonzero rational number DDD satisfies

    16−1=6+1D\frac1{\sqrt6-1}=\frac{\sqrt6+1}{D}6​−11​=D6​+1​

    Find DDD.

    1. Hint 1

    2. Hint 2

    Answer

    Full solution

    The original denominator is nonzero since 6>1\sqrt6>16​>1.

    A conjugate product determines the rational denominator in an equivalent fraction.

  8. Problem 8 Two kinds of conjugate

    For r=2+31r=2+\sqrt{31}r=2+31​, find its complex conjugate and its radical conjugate. Compute the product of rrr with each, and explain which product is rational.

    1. Hint 1

      Decide whether rrr has an imaginary part.

    2. Hint 2

      The radical conjugate changes the sign of the 31\sqrt{31}31​ term.

    Answer

    Complex conjugate 2+312+\sqrt{31}2+31​, product 35+43135+4\sqrt{31}35+431​; radical conjugate 2−312-\sqrt{31}2−31​, product −27-27−27. Only the product with the radical conjugate is rational.

    Full solution

    The number rrr is real, so its complex conjugate is rrr itself, and

    r⋅r=35+431r\cdot r=35+4\sqrt{31}r⋅r=35+431​

    since

    4+431+31=35+431.4+4\sqrt{31}+31=35+4\sqrt{31}.4+431​+31=35+431​.

    The radical conjugate changes the sign of the radical term.

    Since 4−31=−274-31=-274−31=−27,

    (2+31)(2−31)=−27(2+\sqrt{31})(2-\sqrt{31})=-27(2+31​)(2−31​)=−27

    Only the radical conjugate cancels the 31\sqrt{31}31​ terms, because the product of the two radical terms is −31-31−31, a rational number.

    For this rrr, the complex-conjugate product keeps the irrational term 4314\sqrt{31}431​.

    Answer

    Complex conjugate 2+312+\sqrt{31}2+31​, product 35+43135+4\sqrt{31}35+431​; radical conjugate 2−312-\sqrt{31}2−31​, product −27-27−27. Only the product with the radical conjugate is rational.
