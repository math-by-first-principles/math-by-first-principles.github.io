    Full solution

    383838.

    Full solution

    38⋅576=192\frac{38\cdot57}6=19^2638⋅57​=192

  4. Problem 4 The remaining rod

    A rod is 5175\sqrt{17}517​ cm long. Two pieces, each 68\sqrt{68}68​ cm long, are cut from it. Ignoring the cutting width, find the remaining length exactly.

    1. Hint 1

      Simplify the piece length before subtracting it twice.

    2. Hint 2

      68=4⋅1768=4\cdot1768=4⋅17, so each piece is a multiple of 17\sqrt{17}17​.

    Answer

    17\sqrt{17}17​ cm.

    Full solution

    Each piece has length 2172\sqrt{17}217​ cm.

    Together the pieces use 4174\sqrt{17}417​ cm.

    The remaining length is

    517−417=175\sqrt{17}-4\sqrt{17}=\sqrt{17}517​−417​=17​

    The removed and remaining lengths add back to the original rod length.

    Answer

    Key idea

    Simplifying radical lengths exposes the common unit needed for addition and subtraction.

  5. Problem 5 A rectangular card

    A rectangular card has side lengths 3+23+\sqrt23+2​ cm and 3−23-\sqrt23−2​ cm. Find its exact area and perimeter.

    1. Hint 1

      Area multiplies the side lengths, while perimeter adds two copies of each.

    2. Hint 2

      In the area product, the two mixed terms cancel.

    Answer

    Area: 777 square centimeters; perimeter: 121212 cm.

    Full solution

    Both sides are positive since 2<3\sqrt2<32​<3.

    The area is a difference of squares.

    A=9−2A=9-2A=9−2

    This gives 777 square centimeters.

    The sum of the side lengths is 666 cm, so

    P=12P=12P=12

    The radical terms cancel in the sum as well as in the area product.

    Answer

    Area: 777 square centimeters; perimeter: 121212 cm.

    Key idea

    A matching radical sum and difference can produce rational geometric measurements.

  9. Problem 9 The sign of a product

    For a,b≥0a,b\ge0a,b≥0, a student claims (a+b)(a−b)(\sqrt a+\sqrt b)(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)(a​+b​)(a​−b​) is zero or positive exactly when a≥ba\ge ba≥b. Is the claim true? Justify it.

    1. Hint 1

      The matching sum and difference eliminate the mixed radical terms.

    2. Hint 2

      The remaining expression compares the two radicands directly.

    Answer

    Yes, exactly when a≥ba\ge ba≥b.

    Full solution

    The principal roots exist under the given conditions.

    Their product simplifies to

    (a+b)(a−b)=a−b(\sqrt a+\sqrt b)(\sqrt a-\sqrt b)=a-b(a​+b​)(a​−b​)=a−b

    The difference a−ba-ba−b is zero or positive precisely when a≥ba\ge ba≥b.

    Equality includes a=ba=ba=b, including when both are zero.

    Answer

    Yes, exactly when a≥ba\ge ba≥b.

    Key idea

    A conjugate product can make its sign clear through a difference of radicands.
