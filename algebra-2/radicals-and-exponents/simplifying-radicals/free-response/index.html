    63x2=3∣x∣7\sqrt{63x^2} = 3|x|\sqrt 763x2​=3∣x∣7​.

    Worked solution

    Part A

    Split the radicand into a perfect square times a remainder. Since 63=9⋅763 = 9 \cdot 763=9⋅7,

    63x2=9x2⋅7.63x^2 = 9x^2 \cdot 7.63x2=9x2⋅7.

    Both factors on the right are non-negative for every real xxx, so the product rule applies:

    63x2=9x2 7=9 x2 7=3∣x∣7.\sqrt{63x^2} = \sqrt{9x^2}\,\sqrt 7 = \sqrt{9}\,\sqrt{x^2}\,\sqrt 7 = 3|x|\sqrt 7.63x2​=9x2​7​=9​x2​7​=3∣x∣7​.

    The bars are needed because x2\sqrt{x^2}x2​ is ∣x∣|x|∣x∣, not xxx: at x=−1x = -1x=−1 the original expression is 63≈7.94\sqrt{63} \approx 7.9463​≈7.94, a positive number, while 3(−1)7≈−7.943(-1)\sqrt7 \approx -7.943(−1)7​≈−7.94 would have the wrong sign.

    Part B

    The radicand 81x881x^881x8 is itself a perfect square, since 81x8=(9x4)281x^8 = (9x^4)^281x8=(9x4)2. Extracting it directly,

    81x8=(9x4)2=∣9x4∣.\sqrt{81x^8} = \sqrt{(9x^4)^2} = |9x^4|.81x8​=(9x4)2​=∣9x4∣.

    Now decide whether the bars change anything. 9x4≥09x^4 \ge 09x4≥0 for every real xxx, because x4x^4x4 is an even power and 999 is positive, so ∣9x4∣=9x4|9x^4| = 9x^4∣9x4∣=9x4 outright:

    81x8=9x4.\sqrt{81x^8} = 9x^4.81x8​=9x4.

    No bars are needed in the final answer, not because the identity a2=∣a∣\sqrt{a^2}=|a|a2​=∣a∣ stopped applying, but because the quantity inside those bars was already non-negative, so the bars had nothing to fix.

    Part C

    Halve the exponent inside the radical to find the exponent that would emerge: 14÷2=714 \div 2 = 714÷2=7, so x14=∣x7∣\sqrt{x^{14}} = |x^7|x14​=∣x7∣ before any bars are removed. The question of whether those bars survive comes down entirely to the parity of that emerging exponent, 777.

    An exponent that comes out even always leaves a non-negative quantity (xeven≥0x^{\text{even}} \ge 0xeven≥0 for every real xxx), so its bars can be dropped, as happened in part B. An exponent that comes out odd leaves a quantity that can still be negative, since an odd power carries the sign of xxx, so the bars are load-bearing there. Here the emerging exponent is 777, which is odd, so the bars stay:

    x14=∣x7∣=∣x∣7.\sqrt{x^{14}} = |x^7| = |x|^7.x14​=∣x7∣=∣x∣7.

    a≥0,b≥0(and b>0 for the quotient rule)a \ge 0, \qquad b \ge 0 \quad (\text{and } b > 0 \text{ for the quotient rule})a≥0,b≥0(and b>0 for the quotient rule)
