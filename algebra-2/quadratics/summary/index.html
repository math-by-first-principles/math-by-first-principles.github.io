Quadratic Functions and Equations: Chapter Review

A rapid review before the test: the chapter's vocabulary and notation, every formula with the conditions to use it, the standard problem types step by step, and the traps that cost points.

Vocabulary and notation

Problem types, step by step

Read a parabola's features from a given form

  1. Vertex form: read (h,k)(h, k)(h,k), flipping the inside sign. Standard: x=−b2ax = -\frac{b}{2a}x=−2ab​, then evaluate there. Factored: average the zeros, then evaluate.
  2. The sign of aaa gives the opening direction, the range, and the end behavior; the yyy-intercept is f(0)f(0)f(0), and the actual zeros come free only from factored form; Δ\DeltaΔ alone only counts how many real zeros there are, not their values.
  3. For an applied largest or smallest value the vertex is the answer: −b2a-\frac{b}{2a}−2ab​ says WHERE, the value there HOW MUCH. Discard anything outside the model's domain, such as a negative time.

e.g. y=−2(x+3)2+8y = -2(x + 3)^2 + 8y=−2(x+3)2+8: vertex (−3,8)(-3, 8)(−3,8), maximum 888, range (−∞,8](-\infty, 8](−∞,8]; and h=−16t2+64th = -16t^2 + 64th=−16t2+64t peaks at (2,64)(2, 64)(2,64).

Convert standard form to vertex form by completing the square

  1. Factor aaa out of the x2x^2x2 and xxx terms only; the constant stays outside.
  2. Inside, halve the new coefficient of xxx, square it, and add and subtract it.
  3. Write the square, then distribute aaa across BOTH it and the constant subtracted inside.
  4. Combine the constants, then expand to confirm the original.

e.g. 3x2−12x+5=3((x−2)2−4)+5=3(x−2)2−73x^2 - 12x + 5 = 3\big((x - 2)^2 - 4\big) + 5 = 3(x - 2)^2 - 73x2−12x+5=3((x−2)2−4)+5=3(x−2)2−7, vertex (2,−7)(2, -7)(2,−7).

Solve a quadratic equation

  1. Move every term to one side against 000, expanding first if it arrives as a product equal to a nonzero number.
  2. Pull out any common factor, a bare xxx included, rather than dividing by it.
  3. Factor if it splits on sight, a difference of squares x2−k2=(x−k)(x+k)x^2 - k^2 = (x - k)(x + k)x2−k2=(x−k)(x+k) or a perfect square included; take roots with a ±\pm± if already a square; otherwise use the formula.
  4. Simplify the radical, cancel any common factor of the whole fraction, and substitute each root into the ORIGINAL equation.

e.g. (x−1)(x−2)=6(x - 1)(x - 2) = 6(x−1)(x−2)=6 becomes (x−4)(x+1)=0(x - 4)(x + 1) = 0(x−4)(x+1)=0, so x=4x = 4x=4 or x=−1x = -1x=−1.

Count the real roots, or force a count

  1. Standard form first, reading aaa, bbb, ccc with their signs, substituting in parentheses.
  2. Compute Δ=b2−4ac\Delta = b^2 - 4acΔ=b2−4ac: positive gives two roots, zero one repeated, negative none over R\mathbb{R}R.
  3. To design a count, impose that condition on Δ\DeltaΔ and solve for the unknown coefficient.
  4. For a factoring question with integer coefficients, ask separately whether Δ\DeltaΔ is a perfect square.

e.g. x2+kx+9=0x^2 + kx + 9 = 0x2+kx+9=0 has exactly one real solution when k2−36=0k^2 - 36 = 0k2−36=0, so k=±6k = \pm 6k=±6.

Solve a quadratic inequality

  1. Move everything to one side against 000, threshold questions ("at least", "at most") included; if a<0a < 0a<0, multiply by −1-1−1 and REVERSE the symbol.
  2. Find the roots, the only places the sign can turn: an upward parabola is negative between two of them and positive outside, so keep the pieces the symbol asks for, or confirm with a test point strictly inside a piece, never a root.
  3. Degenerate cases: Δ=0\Delta = 0Δ=0 leaves a(x−r)2a(x - r)^2a(x−r)2, zero at rrr and positive elsewhere; Δ<0\Delta < 0Δ<0 leaves it positive everywhere.
  4. Answer as a set: brackets for ≤,≥\le, \ge≤,≥, parentheses for <,><, ><,>, pieces joined by ∪\cup∪.

e.g. x2−x−6≥0x^2 - x - 6 \ge 0x2−x−6≥0 has roots −2-2−2 and 333, so the solution set is (−∞,−2]∪[3,∞)(-\infty, -2] \cup [3, \infty)(−∞,−2]∪[3,∞).

Work with the roots without solving

  1. Write s=−bas = -\frac{b}{a}s=−ab​ and p=cap = \frac{c}{a}p=ac​.
  2. Given one root, subtract it from sss or divide it into ppp for the other; doing both checks it.
  3. Rewrite a symmetric expression in sss and ppp before substituting numbers.
  4. To build a quadratic from roots, use x2−sx+px^2 - sx + px2−sx+p, then scale to clear fractions.

e.g. One root of 2x2−7x+3=02x^2 - 7x + 3 = 02x2−7x+3=0 is 333 and s=72s = \tfrac{7}{2}s=27​, so the other is 12\tfrac{1}{2}21​.

Exam traps

  • Trap Distributing the factored-out aaa to the square but not to the constant subtracted inside: 2((x+3)2−9)+72\big((x + 3)^2 - 9\big) + 72((x+3)2−9)+7 finished as 2(x+3)2−22(x + 3)^2 - 22(x+3)2−2.

    Fix That −9-9−9 is inside, so it doubles to −18-18−18: 2x2+12x+7=2(x+3)2−112x^2 + 12x + 7 = 2(x + 3)^2 - 112x2+12x+7=2(x+3)2−11. Expand the finished form back.

  • Trap Splitting a product equal to something other than zero: (x−1)(x−2)=6(x - 1)(x - 2) = 6(x−1)(x−2)=6 read as x=7x = 7x=7 or x=8x = 8x=8.

    Fix Neither works (6⋅5=306 \cdot 5 = 306⋅5=30); only 000 pins a factor down. Expand, subtract 666, refactor: x=4x = 4x=4 or x=−1x = -1x=−1.

  • Trap Reading Δ≥0\Delta \ge 0Δ≥0 as "so it factors".

    Fix x2−2x^2 - 2x2−2 has Δ=8\Delta = 8Δ=8 and two real roots yet no rational factorization: that needs rational coefficients AND a perfect-square Δ\DeltaΔ.

  • Trap Reporting Δ<0\Delta < 0Δ<0 as "no roots", or as an automatically empty answer to an inequality.

    Fix Say no REAL roots. With a>0a > 0a>0, Δ<0\Delta < 0Δ<0 makes f(x)<0f(x) < 0f(x)<0 empty but f(x)>0f(x) > 0f(x)>0 all of R\mathbb{R}R: emptiness turns on the sign of aaa and the symbol.

  • Trap Collapsing an outside-the-roots answer into one interval, writing [−2,3][-2, 3][−2,3] for x2−x−6≥0x^2 - x - 6 \ge 0x2−x−6≥0.

    Fix That is the exact complement. Two rays need a union: (−∞,−2]∪[3,∞)(-\infty, -2] \cup [3, \infty)(−∞,−2]∪[3,∞).

  • Trap Multiplying an inequality by −1-1−1 and keeping the symbol.

    Fix The square is positive everywhere but 333, so the answer only punctures that point: (−∞,3)∪(3,∞)(-\infty, 3) \cup (3, \infty)(−∞,3)∪(3,∞).

  • Trap Writing the sum of the roots as ba\frac{b}{a}ab​, or reading off −b-b−b and ccc when a≠1a \neq 1a=1.

    Fix The sum is −ba-\frac{b}{a}−ab​, the product ca\frac{c}{a}ac​. For 2x2−7x+32x^2 - 7x + 32x2−7x+3 the sum is 72\tfrac{7}{2}27​, not 777.
