  1. Problem 1 A rearranged root equation

    The equation 3x(x−2)=x+23x(x-2)=x+23x(x−2)=x+2 has real roots. Find their sum and product without finding the roots individually.

    1. Hint 1

      The formulas use the coefficients after the equation is written with zero on one side.

    2. Hint 2

      Divide the linear and constant coefficients by the leading coefficient, applying the minus sign only to the sum.

    Answer

    Sum 73\frac7337​; product −23-\frac23−32​.

    Full solution

    Expand and move every term to one side: 3x(x−2)=3x2−6x3x(x-2)=3x^2-6x3x(x−2)=3x2−6x, so 3x2−6x=x+23x^2-6x=x+23x2−6x=x+2 becomes

    3x2−7x−2=03x^2-7x-2=03x2−7x−2=0

    Thus a=3a=3a=3, b=−7b=-7b=−7, c=−2c=-2c=−2, giving sum −b/a=7/3-b/a=7/3−b/a=7/3 and product c/a=−2/3c/a=-2/3c/a=−2/3.

    The discriminant is 49+24=73>049+24=73>049+24=73>0, consistent with the stated real roots.

    Answer

    Sum 73\frac7337​; product −23-\frac23−32​.

    Key idea

    Collect the equation before reading root totals from its coefficients.

  2. Problem 2 A shifted product

    The real roots of 2x2−5x−3=02x^2-5x-3=02x2−5x−3=0 are r1,r2r_1,r_2r1​,r2​. Find (r1+1)(r2+1)(r_1+1)(r_2+1)(r1​+1)(r2​+1) without solving for either root.

    1. Hint 1

      Expand the requested product into the root sum and product.

    2. Hint 2

      Read those two quantities from the coefficients.

    Answer

    222.

    Full solution

    The roots have sum 5/25/25/2 and product −3/2-3/2−3/2.

    Expanding (r1+1)(r2+1)=r1r2+r1+r2+1(r_1+1)(r_2+1)=r_1r_2+r_1+r_2+1(r1​+1)(r2​+1)=r1​r2​+r1​+r2​+1 and substituting gives

    Answer

    222.

    Key idea

    A product of equally shifted roots can be evaluated from their sum and product.

  3. Problem 3 A root at zero

    2. Hint 2

      Subtract the known root from the total.

    Answer

    −74-\frac74−47​.

    Full solution

    Subtracting the known zero gives

    r2=−74r_2=-\frac74r2​=−47​

    Answer

    −74-\frac74−47​.

    Key idea

    Use the sum when recovering a partner from a zero root.

  4. Problem 4 A family from two values

    Find all quadratic polynomials whose roots are −2-2−2 and 333 and whose coefficients are real. Give the family in standard form and state the restriction on its parameter.

    1. Hint 1

      The two roots determine factors but leave a common multiplier free.

    2. Hint 2

      Expand their product and retain every nonzero multiplier.

    Answer

    ax2−ax−6aa x^2-a x-6aax2−ax−6a, for every real a≠0a\ne0a=0.

    Full solution

    Every such quadratic has the form a(x+2)(x−3)a(x+2)(x-3)a(x+2)(x−3) with a≠0a\ne0a=0.

    Expanding gives

    f(x)=ax2−ax−6af(x)=ax^2-ax-6af(x)=ax2−ax−6a

    The nonzero condition preserves its degree and its zero set.

    Conversely, each member vanishes exactly at the two prescribed roots.

    Answer

    ax2−ax−6aa x^2-a x-6aax2−ax−6a, for every real a≠0a\ne0a=0.

    Key idea

    Prescribed roots determine a quadratic up to a nonzero scalar.

  5. Problem 5 A sum of shifted squares

    The real roots of x2−10x+7=0x^2-10x+7=0x2−10x+7=0 are r1,r2r_1,r_2r1​,r2​. Find (r1−5)2+(r2−5)2(r_1-5)^2+(r_2-5)^2(r1​−5)2+(r2​−5)2 without finding either root.

    1. Hint 1

      Expand the target and combine terms symmetric in the two roots.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the sum to remove the linear terms and the product to find the sum of squares.

    Answer

    363636.

    Full solution

    The sum is 101010 and the product is 777.

    Expand each shifted square.

    (r1−5)2=r12−10r1+25(r2−5)2=r22−10r2+25\begin{aligned} (r_1-5)^2&=r_1^2-10r_1+25 \\ (r_2-5)^2&=r_2^2-10r_2+25 \end{aligned}(r1​−5)2(r2​−5)2​=r12​−10r1​+25=r22​−10r2​+25​

    Adding these, the target equals

    (r12+r22)−10(r1+r2)+50(r_1^2+r_2^2)-10(r_1+r_2)+50(r12​+r22​)−10(r1​+r2​)+50

    First find r12+r22r_1^2+r_2^2r12​+r22​ from the sum and product.

    r12+r22=102−2(7)=86\begin{aligned} r_1^2+r_2^2&=10^2-2(7) \\ &=86 \end{aligned}r12​+r22​​=102−2(7)=86​

    Substituting into the expanded target gives

    86−10(10)+50=3686-10(10)+50=3686−10(10)+50=36

    The axis is x=5x=5x=5, and the two squared distances from it are equal.

    Answer

    363636.

    Key idea

    Symmetric combinations of shifted roots reduce to their original sum and product.

  6. Problem 6 Doubling both roots

    Let r1,r2r_1,r_2r1​,r2​ be the roots of x2+3x−2=0x^2+3x-2=0x2+3x−2=0. Find the monic quadratic whose roots are 2r12r_12r1​ and 2r22r_22r2​, in standard form.

    1. Hint 1

      Write the new roots 2r1,2r22r_1,2r_22r1​,2r2​ in terms of the old ones and substitute into the definitions of the sum and the product.

    2. Hint 2

      Insert the new sum and product into a monic quadratic.

    Answer

    x2+6x−8x^2+6x-8x2+6x−8.

    Full solution

    The original sum is −3-3−3 and product is −2-2−2.

    Doubling both roots gives a sum of −6-6−6 and product 4(−2)=−84(-2)=-84(−2)=−8.

    The required monic polynomial is

    x2+6x−8x^2+6x-8x2+6x−8

    As a check, it equals 4f(x/2)4f(x/2)4f(x/2) for f(x)=x2+3x−2f(x)=x^2+3x-2f(x)=x2+3x−2, so each original root doubled makes it zero.

    Answer

    x2+6x−8x^2+6x-8x2+6x−8.

    Key idea

    Scaling roots multiplies their sum by the scale and their product by its square.

    Full solution

    (r1−r2)2=22−4(−23)(r1−r2)2=203\begin{aligned} (r_1-r_2)^2&=2^2-4(-\tfrac23) \\ (r_1-r_2)^2&=\frac{20}{3} \end{aligned}(r1​−r2​)2(r1​−r2​)2​=22−4(−32​)=320​​

    The nonnegative square root is 215/32\sqrt{15}/3215​/3.

    Equivalently, Δ=60\Delta=60Δ=60 and the root distance is 60/3\sqrt{60}/360​/3, the same value.

    Answer

  9. Problem 9 A reported separation

    A quadratic with leading coefficient −3-3−3 has two real roots 4 units apart. A student reports its discriminant as −48-48−48 because the leading coefficient is negative. Is this correct? Find the discriminant.

    1. Hint 1

      The discriminant relates to the square of the leading coefficient and the square of the gap.

    2. Hint 2

      Track which signs survive squaring.

    Answer

    No; the discriminant is 144144144.

    Full solution

    The squared-root-gap identity is

    Δ=a2(r1−r2)2.\Delta=a^2(r_1-r_2)^2.Δ=a2(r1​−r2​)2.

    Here

    Δ=(−3)2(4)2Δ=144\begin{aligned} \Delta&=(-3)^2(4)^2 \\ \Delta&=144 \end{aligned}ΔΔ​=(−3)2(4)2=144​

    A negative leading coefficient reverses the parabola but does not make a squared distance negative.

    Answer

    No; the discriminant is 144144144.

    Key idea

    The discriminant measures squared root separation with a squared leading coefficient.

  10. Problem 10 A negative product target

    For every real sss and every real p<0p<0p<0, a designer expects two distinct real numbers whose sum is sss and whose product is ppp. Is that expectation valid? Explain using a quadratic that would have those roots.

    Answer

    Full solution

    The desired quadratic is

    x2−sx+p=0x^2-sx+p=0x2−sx+p=0

    Since s2≥0s^2\ge0s2≥0 and p<0p<0p<0,

    s2−4p>0s^2-4p>0s2−4p>0

    Therefore it has two distinct real roots.

    The coefficient formulas give their sum as sss and their product as ppp, so the construction meets both requirements.

    The general existence condition is s2−4p≥0s^2-4p\ge0s2−4p≥0, and these hypotheses satisfy it strictly.

    Answer

    Yes; x2−sx+p=0x^2-sx+p=0x2−sx+p=0 has two distinct real roots with the required sum and product.
