    Find the complex number zzz satisfying z+2i3=1−i\frac{z+2i}{3}=1-i3z+2i​=1−i. Give zzz in standard form.

    1. Hint 1

    2. Hint 2

    Answer

    Full solution

    z+2i=3−3iz+2i=3-3iz+2i=3−3i

    An equation with complex addition and real scaling is solved by undoing those operations in reverse order.

  10. Problem 10 A product comparison

    Can two non-real complex numbers that are not conjugates have a positive real product? Give an example or explain why none exists.

    1. Hint 1

      What must the real and imaginary parts of a product satisfy for the product to be a positive real number?

    2. Hint 2

    Answer

    Yes; iii and −3i-3i−3i are not conjugates and have product 333.

    Full solution

    The conjugate of iii is −i-i−i, so −3i-3i−3i is not its conjugate.

    Their product is

    i(−3i)=−3i2i(-3i)=-3i^2i(−3i)=−3i2

    Replacing i2i^2i2 by −1-1−1 gives 333, which is positive and real.

    Answer

    Yes; iii and −3i-3i−3i are not conjugates and have product 333.

    Key idea

    A sufficient condition for a positive real product need not be a necessary condition.
