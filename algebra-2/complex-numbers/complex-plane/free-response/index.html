    Worked solution

    Part A

    Two bounds pin the sum, and both come from the triangle inequality. Above,

    ∣z+w∣≤∣z∣+∣w∣=9+5=14,|z + w| \le |z| + |w| = 9 + 5 = 14,∣z+w∣≤∣z∣+∣w∣=9+5=14,

    since a detour through zzz is never shorter than the straight route.     Bounds are only worth having if they are reached, so exhibit a pair for each. Both moduli are satisfied by real numbers, which are the easiest to add:

    z=9, w=5 ⇒ ∣z+w∣=∣14∣=14,z=9, w=−5 ⇒ ∣z+w∣=∣4∣=4.z = 9, \ w = 5 \ \Rightarrow \ |z + w| = |14| = 14, \qquad z = 9, \ w = -5 \ \Rightarrow \ |z + w| = |4| = 4.z=9, w=5 ⇒ ∣z+w∣=∣14∣=14,z=9, w=−5 ⇒ ∣z+w∣=∣4∣=4.

    Part B

    One example is enough to sink a claim made about every pair, so choose the pair whose sum is easiest to measure exactly. Put one number on each axis: z=9iz = 9iz=9i has modulus 999 and w=5w = 5w=5 has modulus 555, so both conditions hold. Their sum is 5+9i5 + 9i5+9i, and

    Where does the classmate's reasoning go wrong? Lengths add only along a straight path. Walking from 000 to zzz and then a further ∣w∣|w|∣w∣ to z+wz + wz+w covers 9+5=149 + 5 = 149+5=14 units of travel, but the straight-line distance back to 000 is shorter unless the second leg continues in the direction of the first.     That is the condition, and the two examples confirm it. For the pair z=9z = 9z=9, w=5w = 5w=5 from part A, z w‾=45z\,\overline{w} = 45zw=45, a nonnegative real, and the bound was attained. Geometrically the equality case is the degenerate triangle in which the detour is no detour at all.

    Part C

    The bound is the triangle inequality itself, applied to a pair chosen so that the sum comes out to be zzz. The pair is z+wz + wz+w and −w-w−w, because

    (z+w)+(−w)=z.(z + w) + (-w) = z.(z+w)+(−w)=z.

    Applying the inequality to those two numbers, and using ∣−w∣=∣w∣|-w| = |w|∣−w∣=∣w∣ since the point −w-w−w is the same distance from 000 as www,

    ∣z∣≤∣z+w∣+∣w∣.|z| \le |z + w| + |w|.∣z∣≤∣z+w∣+∣w∣.

    Subtracting ∣w∣|w|∣w∣ from both sides, which is ordinary arithmetic on real numbers, gives ∣z+w∣≥∣z∣−∣w∣|z + w| \ge |z| - |w|∣z+w∣≥∣z∣−∣w∣. Nothing was assumed about zzz or www, so the bound holds for every pair.

    Now use it. With ∣z∣=9|z| = 9∣z∣=9 and ∣w∣=5|w| = 5∣w∣=5, every pair meeting the conditions satisfies

    ∣z+w∣≥9−5=4.|z + w| \ge 9 - 5 = 4.∣z+w∣≥9−5=4.
