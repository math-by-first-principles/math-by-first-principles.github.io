    What is ∣(2+i)(3−4i)(1+2i)∣|(2 + i)(3 - 4i)(1 + 2i)|∣(2+i)(3−4i)(1+2i)∣?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The modulus multiplies across products, so take each factor's modulus and multiply.

    ∣2+i∣=5,∣3−4i∣=5,∣1+2i∣=5|2 + i| = \sqrt{5}, \qquad |3 - 4i| = 5, \qquad |1 + 2i| = \sqrt{5}∣2+i∣=5​,∣3−4i∣=5,∣1+2i∣=5​

    Multiplying, 5⋅5⋅5=5⋅5=25\sqrt{5} \cdot 5 \cdot \sqrt{5} = 5 \cdot 5 = 255​⋅5⋅5​=5⋅5=25. No complex multiplication was ever needed, which is the whole point of multiplicativity.

    Solution

    Correct answer: A
