    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The two models agree for every ttt exactly when their one-year growth factors agree. Monthly compounding at 6%6\%6% multiplies by (1+0.0612)12=(1.005)12\left(1 + \frac{0.06}{12}\right)^{12} = (1.005)^{12}(1+120.06​)12=(1.005)12 each year, and the continuous model multiplies by ere^{r}er.

  12. 12

    Why is A=PertA = Pe^{rt}A=Pert called the continuous model?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The name records where the formula comes from. Substituting m=nrm = \frac{n}{r}m=rn​ into the discrete formula rewrites it as

    A=P[(1+1m)m]rtA = P\left[\left(1 + \frac{1}{m}\right)^{m}\right]^{rt}A=P[(1+m1​)m]rt

    and as the compounding gets finer, mmm grows and the bracket closes in on eee, leaving A=PertA = Pe^{rt}A=Pert. The rate rrr stays fixed throughout, and the irrationality of eee, though true, has nothing to do with the name.
