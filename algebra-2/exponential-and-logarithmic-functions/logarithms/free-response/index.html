  1. Problem 1 A combined reading

    Evaluate log⁡5(52−1)+5log⁡53\log_5(5^{\sqrt2-1})+5^{\log_5 3}log5​(52​−1)+5log5​3.

    1. Hint 1

      An exponential and a logarithm with matching bases undo each other.

    2. Hint 2

      In the first term the result is the exponent; in the second it is the positive argument.

    Answer

    2+2\sqrt2+22​+2.

    Full solution

    Both logarithms have positive arguments.

    The first term is 2−1\sqrt2-12​−1, and the second is 333.

    log⁡5(52−1)+5log⁡53=2+2\log_5(5^{\sqrt2-1})+5^{\log_5 3}=\sqrt2+2log5​(52​−1)+5log5​3=2​+2

    The two inverse laws do not require the exponent to be an integer.

    Answer

    2+2\sqrt2+22​+2.

    Key idea

    Cancellation depends on matching bases and valid arguments, not on whether the numbers are tidy.
