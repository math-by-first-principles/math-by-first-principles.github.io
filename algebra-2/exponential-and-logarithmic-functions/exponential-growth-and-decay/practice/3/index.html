    A population doubles every 101010 years. How long does it take to triple?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Write the model in doubling-time form, where the exponent counts doublings.

    A(t)=P⋅2t/10A(t) = P \cdot 2^{t/10}A(t)=P⋅2t/10

    Tripling means the multiplier reaches 333, and the starting amount cancels.

    2t/10=3⟹t10=log⁡3log⁡2≈1.5852^{t/10} = 3 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \frac{t}{10} = \frac{\log 3}{\log 2} \approx 1.5852t/10=3⟹10t​=log2log3​≈1.585

    So t≈15.85t \approx 15.85t≈15.85 years. Tripling takes more than one and a half doubling times, not one and a half times 101010 scaled by 3/23/23/2.

    In the model A=2000(1.03)4tA = 2000(1.03)^{4t}A=2000(1.03)4t, the variable ttt is measured in years. What is the annual growth rate?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Group the exponent so the model shows one factor per year. Since (1.03)4t=[(1.03)4]t(1.03)^{4t} = \left[(1.03)^4\right]^t(1.03)4t=[(1.03)4]t, the annual factor is (1.03)4(1.03)^4(1.03)4.

    (1.03)4≈1.1255(1.03)^4 \approx 1.1255(1.03)4≈1.1255

    Subtract 111 to get the rate: r≈0.1255r \approx 0.1255r≈0.1255, or about 12.55%12.55\%12.55% a year. The quoted 3%3\%3% is the rate per quarter, and multiplying it by four to get 12%12\%12% ignores the compounding within the year.

    Town A has 100010001000 people and grows 5%5\%5% a year. Town B has 150015001500 people and grows 2%2\%2% a year. In how many years do they have the same population?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Set the two models equal and collect the exponentials on one side.

    1000(1.05)t=1500(1.02)t⟹(1.051.02)t=1.51000(1.05)^t = 1500(1.02)^t \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \left(\frac{1.05}{1.02}\right)^t = 1.51000(1.05)t=1500(1.02)t⟹(1.021.05​)t=1.5

    The ratio of factors is itself a growth factor, about 1.0294121.0294121.029412, so take logarithms.

    Town A always catches up eventually: its larger factor beats any head start, given enough time.

  4. 4

    An investment doubles in 999 years with annual compounding. What is its annual growth rate?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The annual factor must produce a doubling after nine years, so undo the ninth power with a rational exponent.

    b9=2⟹b=21/9≈1.0801b^{9} = 2 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad b = 2^{1/9} \approx 1.0801b9=2⟹b=21/9≈1.0801

    Subtracting 111 gives r≈0.0801r \approx 0.0801r≈0.0801, about 8.01%8.01\%8.01% a year. The linear guess 100÷9≈11.1%100 \div 9 \approx 11.1\%100÷9≈11.1% is far too high, because it forgets that the interest starts earning interest.

    How long does 3000 dollars take to reach 5000 dollars at 5%5\%5% per year compounded monthly?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Monthly compounding gives a period factor of 1+0.05121 + \tfrac{0.05}{12}1+120.05​ and an exponent of 12t12t12t. Divide by the principal to isolate the multiplier.

    (1+0.0512)12t=50003000=53\left(1 + \frac{0.05}{12}\right)^{12t} = \frac{5000}{3000} = \frac{5}{3}(1+120.05​)12t=30005000​=35​

    Take logarithms and bring the exponent down.

    12t=log⁡53log⁡1.0041667≈0.2218490.0018058≈122.8512t = \frac{\log \frac{5}{3}}{\log 1.0041667} \approx \frac{0.221849}{0.0018058} \approx 122.8512t=log1.0041667log35​​≈0.00180580.221849​≈122.85

    Dividing by 121212 gives t≈10.24t \approx 10.24t≈10.24 years. Compounding annually instead would take about 10.4710.4710.47 years, slightly longer.

    A machine loses 25%25\%25% of its value each year. After how many whole years is it first worth less than 5%5\%5% of its original value?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The decay factor is b=0.75b = 0.75b=0.75, and the condition is (0.75)t<0.05(0.75)^t < 0.05(0.75)t<0.05. Solve the equality first to locate the crossing.

    t=log⁡0.05log⁡0.75≈−1.30103−0.124939≈10.41t = \frac{\log 0.05}{\log 0.75} \approx \frac{-1.30103}{-0.124939} \approx 10.41t=log0.75log0.05​≈−0.124939−1.30103​≈10.41

    The value drops below 5%5\%5% partway through the eleventh year, so the first whole year that satisfies the strict inequality is t=11t = 11t=11.     A population doubles every 666 years. By what percent does it grow in a single year?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The annual factor bbb must compound to 222 over six years, so undo the sixth power with a rational exponent.

    b6=2⟹b=21/6≈1.1225b^{6} = 2 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad b = 2^{1/6} \approx 1.1225b6=2⟹b=21/6≈1.1225

    The rate is r=b−1≈0.1225r = b - 1 \approx 0.1225r=b−1≈0.1225, about 12.25%12.25\%12.25% a year. Splitting the 100%100\%100% increase into six equal pieces gives about 16.7%16.7\%16.7%, which is too big, since compounding does part of the work for you.

    A credit card charges 12%12\%12% per year compounded monthly. What is the effective annual rate?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Each month multiplies the balance by 1+0.1212=1.011 + \tfrac{0.12}{12} = 1.011+120.12​=1.01, and twelve of those multiplications make a year.

    (1.01)12≈1.1268(1.01)^{12} \approx 1.1268(1.01)12≈1.1268

    Subtracting 111 gives an effective annual rate of about 12.68%12.68\%12.68%, more than the 12%12\%12% advertised. Compounding semiannually would give (1.06)2=1.1236(1.06)^2 = 1.1236(1.06)2=1.1236, an effective 12.36%12.36\%12.36%, so more frequent compounding costs the borrower more.

    The number 2.562.562.56 is the factor for the whole four-year gap, not for one year.

    A quantity decays exponentially, and after 444 years 60%60\%60% of it remains. By what percent does it decay each year?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Four years of decay leave a multiplier of 0.60.60.6, so the annual factor satisfies a fourth-power equation.

    b4=0.6⟹b=0.61/4≈0.8801b^{4} = 0.6 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad b = 0.6^{1/4} \approx 0.8801b4=0.6⟹b=0.61/4≈0.8801

    The rate is r=b−1≈−0.1199r = b - 1 \approx -0.1199r=b−1≈−0.1199, a decrease of about 12.0%12.0\%12.0% a year. Splitting the 40%40\%40% loss into four equal 10%10\%10% pieces is wrong: (0.9)4=0.6561(0.9)^4 = 0.6561(0.9)4=0.6561, which leaves too much behind.
