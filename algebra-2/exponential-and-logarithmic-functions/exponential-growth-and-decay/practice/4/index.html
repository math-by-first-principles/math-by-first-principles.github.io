    At 6%6\%6% per year compounded annually, how long does an investment take to triple?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Tripling means the multiplier reaches 333, and the principal cancels out of the equation.

    (1.06)t=3(1.06)^t = 3(1.06)t=3

    Take logarithms and bring the exponent down.

    t=log⁡3log⁡1.06≈0.4771210.025306≈18.85 yearst = \frac{\log 3}{\log 1.06} \approx \frac{0.477121}{0.025306} \approx 18.85 \text{ years}t=log1.06log3​≈0.0253060.477121​≈18.85 years

    The doubling time at the same rate is about 11.911.911.9 years, and tripling reasonably takes longer than that but far less than the linear guess of 300÷6=50300 \div 6 = 50300÷6=50 years.

    A car bought for 30000 dollars is worth 18000 dollars after 333 years. What is its annual rate of depreciation?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Three years of decay leave a multiplier of 1800030000=0.6\tfrac{18000}{30000} = 0.63000018000​=0.6, so the annual factor satisfies a cube equation.

    b3=0.6⟹b=0.61/3≈0.8434b^{3} = 0.6 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad b = 0.6^{1/3} \approx 0.8434b3=0.6⟹b=0.61/3≈0.8434

    The rate is r=b−1≈−0.1566r = b - 1 \approx -0.1566r=b−1≈−0.1566, a loss of about 15.7%15.7\%15.7% a year. Spreading the 40%40\%40% loss evenly to get about 13.3%13.3\%13.3% a year is too gentle, since each year's loss is taken from a smaller value than the last.

    An account grows from 4000 dollars to 6000 dollars in 555 years with annual compounding. What is the interest rate?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Five years of growth multiply the balance by 60004000=1.5\tfrac{6000}{4000} = 1.540006000​=1.5, so the annual factor satisfies a fifth-power equation.

    b5=1.5⟹b=1.51/5≈1.0845b^{5} = 1.5 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad b = 1.5^{1/5} \approx 1.0845b5=1.5⟹b=1.51/5≈1.0845

    The rate is about 8.45%8.45\%8.45% a year. Dividing the 50%50\%50% gain by 555 years to get 10%10\%10% overshoots, because (1.10)5=1.61(1.10)^5 = 1.61(1.10)5=1.61, well past 1.51.51.5.
