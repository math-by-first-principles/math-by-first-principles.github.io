  1. Problem 1 One recorded increase

    A positive quantity rises from 240240240 to 252252252 in one step. It will keep the same percentage increase in every later step. Find its factor per step.

    1. Hint 1

      The factor is the new amount divided by the old amount.

    2. Hint 2

      The increase is only part of the new amount; include the amount already present.

    Answer

    1.051.051.05.

    Full solution

    The old amount is positive, so division is valid.

    b=252240b=\frac{252}{240}b=240252​

    This is 1.051.051.05.

    The increase is 12/240=0.0512/240=0.0512/240=0.05 of the old amount, confirming a 5%5\%5% rate and a 1.051.051.05 factor.

    Answer

    1.051.051.05.

    Key idea

    A factor is the entire new amount as a multiple of the previous amount.
