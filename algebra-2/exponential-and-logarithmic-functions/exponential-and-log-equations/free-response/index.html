    Foundational 14 pts

    Part C

    Equating exponents needs one shared integer base that both numbers are whole-number powers of, and no such integer base connects 222 and 555.     Worked solution

    Part A

    Both 125125125 and 625625625 are powers of 555: 125=53125=5^{3}125=53 and 625=54625=5^{4}625=54. Rewrite each side on that base:

    3(x+1)=4(x−2)  ⟹  3x+3=4x−8  ⟹  x=11.3(x+1) = 4(x-2) \;\Longrightarrow\; 3x+3 = 4x-8 \;\Longrightarrow\; x = 11.3(x+1)=4(x−2)⟹3x+3=4x−8⟹x=11.

    Part B

    Both sides are powers of 333: 27=3327=3^{3}27=33 and 19=3−2\dfrac19=3^{-2}91​=3−2. Rewrite each side:

    33x=3−2(x−4).3^{3x} = 3^{-2(x-4)}.33x=3−2(x−4).

    3x=−2(x−4)=−2x+8  ⟹  5x=8  ⟹  x=85.3x = -2(x-4) = -2x+8 \;\Longrightarrow\; 5x = 8 \;\Longrightarrow\; x = \frac{8}{5}.3x=−2(x−4)=−2x+8⟹5x=8⟹x=58​.

    A fractional answer is not a sign of a mistake here; the exponents simply did not come out even.

    Part C

    Look back at what made parts A and B work. In each case, both sides could be rewritten as powers of a single base (555 in part A, 333 in part B), and it was only after that rewriting that the one-to-one theorem applied at all:

    bu=bv  ⟺  u=vfor one fixed base b>0, b≠1.b^{u}=b^{v} \iff u=v \quad \text{for one fixed base } b>0,\ b\neq1.bu=bv⟺u=vfor one fixed base b>0, b=1.

    The equation is not stuck, though, because a different one-to-one property is available. 2x2^{x}2x and 5x+15^{x+1}5x+1 are both positive for every real xxx, and the logarithm is itself one-to-one on positive numbers: log⁡bM=log⁡bN\log_b M=\log_b Nlogb​M=logb​N exactly when M=NM=NM=N, for M,N>0M,N>0M,N>0.
