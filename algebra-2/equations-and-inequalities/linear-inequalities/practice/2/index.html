    Solve −3x>12-3x > 12−3x>12.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Divide both sides by −3-3−3. Because the divisor is negative, reverse the symbol.

    −3x−3<12−3  ⇒  x<−4\frac{-3x}{-3} < \frac{12}{-3} \;\Rightarrow\; x < -4−3−3x​<−312​⇒x<−4

    The solution set is (−∞,−4)(-\infty, -4)(−∞,−4). Keeping the symbol as >>> would give the wrong half of the line.

    Solution

  10. 10

    Rewrite 'x>3x > 3x>3 or x>5x > 5x>5' as a single solution set.

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    An 'or' is the union of the two rays.

    (3,∞)∪(5,∞)=(3,∞)(3, \infty) \cup (5, \infty) = (3, \infty)(3,∞)∪(5,∞)=(3,∞)

    Every number above 555 is already above 333, so the larger ray sits inside the smaller-bound one, and the union is simply x>3x > 3x>3.
