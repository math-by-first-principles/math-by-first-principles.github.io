  1. Problem 1 A circle through two crossings

    ★ Stretch Difficulty: 1 of 3 stars, Stretch

    The circles x2+y2=25x^2+y^2=25x2+y2=25 and (x−6)2+y2=13(x-6)^2+y^2=13(x−6)2+y2=13 meet at two points AAA and BBB. Find every circle through both AAA and BBB that is tangent to the yyy-axis. Give its center and radius, and prove that your list is complete.

    Text description of this figure

    Full solution
