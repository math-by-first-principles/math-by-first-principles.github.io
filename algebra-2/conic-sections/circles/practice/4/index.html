    What are the center and radius of the circle x2+y2=6x+8yx^2 + y^2 = 6x + 8yx2+y2=6x+8y?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Move every variable term to the left so the equation is in general form: x2+y2−6x−8y=0x^2 + y^2 - 6x - 8y = 0x2+y2−6x−8y=0.

    Now complete both squares. Half of −6-6−6 is −3-3−3 (square 999), and half of −8-8−8 is −4-4−4 (square 161616).

    (x−3)2+(y−4)2=0+9+16=25(x - 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 0 + 9 + 16 = 25(x−3)2+(y−4)2=0+9+16=25

    The center is (3,4)(3, 4)(3,4) and the radius is 25=5\sqrt{25} = 525​=5. Since the constant term is 000, this circle passes through the origin, and indeed the center is 555 units from it.

  2. 2

    Where does the circle x2+y2−2x−8y−8=0x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 8y - 8 = 0x2+y2−2x−8y−8=0 meet the xxx-axis?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Every point on the xxx-axis has y=0y = 0y=0, so substitute y=0y = 0y=0 and solve the quadratic that remains.

    x2−2x−8=0  ⟹  (x−4)(x+2)=0x^2 - 2x - 8 = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; (x - 4)(x + 2) = 0x2−2x−8=0⟹(x−4)(x+2)=0

    The roots are x=4x = 4x=4 and x=−2x = -2x=−2, giving the points (4,0)(4, 0)(4,0) and (−2,0)(-2, 0)(−2,0).

    As a check, completing the square gives center (1,4)(1, 4)(1,4) and radius 555, and the distance from (1,4)(1, 4)(1,4) to (4,0)(4, 0)(4,0) is 9+16=5\sqrt{9 + 16} = 59+16​=5, so that point really is on the circle.

  3. 3

    How many points does the line y=4y = 4y=4 share with the circle x2+(y−1)2=9x^2 + (y - 1)^2 = 9x2+(y−1)2=9?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Substitute y=4y = 4y=4 into the circle and see how many values of xxx survive.

    x2+(4−1)2=9  ⟹  x2+9=9  ⟹  x2=0x^2 + (4 - 1)^2 = 9 \;\Longrightarrow\; x^2 + 9 = 9 \;\Longrightarrow\; x^2 = 0x2+(4−1)2=9⟹x2+9=9⟹x2=0

    The equation x2=0x^2 = 0x2=0 has the repeated root x=0x = 0x=0, which is the double-root signature of tangency. The line touches the circle at the single point (0,4)(0, 4)(0,4).

    That matches the picture: the center is (0,1)(0, 1)(0,1) with radius 333, so the highest point of the circle is exactly (0,4)(0, 4)(0,4).

  4. 4

    How many points do the circles (x−1)2+(y−2)2=4(x - 1)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 4(x−1)2+(y−2)2=4 and (x−7)2+(y−10)2=36(x - 7)^2 + (y - 10)^2 = 36(x−7)2+(y−10)2=36 share?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Read off the centers and radii: (1,2)(1, 2)(1,2) with r1=2r_1 = 2r1​=2, and (7,10)(7, 10)(7,10) with r2=6r_2 = 6r2​=6. Measure the distance between the centers.

    d=(7−1)2+(10−2)2=36+64=10d = \sqrt{(7 - 1)^2 + (10 - 2)^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10d=(7−1)2+(10−2)2​=36+64​=10

    Compare that with the sum of the radii, 2+6=82 + 6 = 82+6=8. The centers are 101010 apart, farther than the two circles can reach toward each other.

    d=10>8=r1+r2d = 10 > 8 = r_1 + r_2d=10>8=r1​+r2​

    The circles lie entirely outside one another and share no points at all.

    What is the smallest distance from the origin to a point on the circle (x−8)2+(y−6)2=25(x - 8)^2 + (y - 6)^2 = 25(x−8)2+(y−6)2=25?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The center is (8,6)(8, 6)(8,6) and the radius is 25=5\sqrt{25} = 525​=5. Measure how far the center sits from the origin.

    82+62=100=10\sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{100} = 1082+62​=100​=10

    Since 10>510 > 510>5, the origin lies outside the circle. Points of the circle sit between 10−510 - 510−5 and 10+510 + 510+5 from the origin, and the nearest one is on the segment joining the origin to the center.

    10−5=510 - 5 = 510−5=5

    The least distance is 555. It is not 000, because the origin is not on the circle: substituting gives 64+36=10064 + 36 = 10064+36=100, not 252525.

  6. 6

    For which values of kkk does the line y=ky = ky=k cut the circle x2+y2−6x−4y−12=0x^2 + y^2 - 6x - 4y - 12 = 0x2+y2−6x−4y−12=0 at exactly two points?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Complete the square to see the circle plainly. Half of −6-6−6 is −3-3−3 (square 999), and half of −4-4−4 is −2-2−2 (square 444).

    (x−3)2+(y−2)2=12+9+4=25(x - 3)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 12 + 9 + 4 = 25(x−3)2+(y−2)2=12+9+4=25

    The center is (3,2)(3, 2)(3,2) and the radius is 555, so the circle occupies the horizontal band from y=2−5y = 2 - 5y=2−5 up to y=2+5y = 2 + 5y=2+5. Substituting y=ky = ky=k leaves (x−3)2=25−(k−2)2(x - 3)^2 = 25 - (k - 2)^2(x−3)2=25−(k−2)2, which has two real solutions exactly when the right side is strictly positive.

    (k−2)2<25  ⟹  −3<k<7(k - 2)^2 < 25 \;\Longrightarrow\; -3 < k < 7(k−2)2<25⟹−3<k<7

    At k=−3k = -3k=−3 and k=7k = 7k=7 the line is tangent, giving one point, not two, so those endpoints are excluded.

  7. 7

    Which circle passes through (2,0)(2, 0)(2,0), (0,2)(0, 2)(0,2), and (−2,0)(-2, 0)(−2,0)?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Substitute the three points into x2+y2+Dx+Ey+F=0x^2 + y^2 + Dx + Ey + F = 0x2+y2+Dx+Ey+F=0. From (2,0)(2, 0)(2,0): 4+2D+F=04 + 2D + F = 04+2D+F=0. From (−2,0)(-2, 0)(−2,0): 4−2D+F=04 - 2D + F = 04−2D+F=0. Subtracting these gives 4D=04D = 04D=0, so D=0D = 0D=0, and then F=−4F = -4F=−4.

    From (0,2)(0, 2)(0,2): 4+2E+F=04 + 2E + F = 04+2E+F=0, and with F=−4F = -4F=−4 this gives E=0E = 0E=0.

    x2+y2−4=0  ⟹  x2+y2=4x^2 + y^2 - 4 = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; x^2 + y^2 = 4x2+y2−4=0⟹x2+y2=4

    The circle is centered at the origin with radius 222, and each of the three given points is exactly 222 units from (0,0)(0, 0)(0,0).

  8. 8

    Which equation has a graph consisting of exactly one point?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Every choice has D=2D = 2D=2 and E=−6E = -6E=−6, so completing the square always gives (x+1)2+(y−3)2=10−F(x + 1)^2 + (y - 3)^2 = 10 - F(x+1)2+(y−3)2=10−F, and only the constant changes.

    A sum of two squares is zero only when both squares vanish, so the graph is a single point exactly when the right side is 000.

    10−F=0  ⟹  F=1010 - F = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; F = 1010−F=0⟹F=10

    With F=10F = 10F=10 the lone solution is (−1,3)(-1, 3)(−1,3). The constants 666 and 000 leave positive right sides (circles), and 141414 leaves a negative one (no graph at all).

    A chord of the circle x2+y2=100x^2 + y^2 = 100x2+y2=100 lies along the line y=6y = 6y=6. How long is the chord?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Substitute y=6y = 6y=6 into the circle to locate the endpoints.

    x2+36=100  ⟹  x2=64  ⟹  x=±8x^2 + 36 = 100 \;\Longrightarrow\; x^2 = 64 \;\Longrightarrow\; x = \pm 8x2+36=100⟹x2=64⟹x=±8

    The endpoints are (−8,6)(-8, 6)(−8,6) and (8,6)(8, 6)(8,6), which share a yyy-coordinate, so the chord is horizontal and its length is the gap between the xxx-values.

    8−(−8)=168 - (-8) = 168−(−8)=16

    The chord is 161616 long. Answering 888 would report only the half-chord, which is the leg of the right triangle with hypotenuse 101010 and other leg 666.

    What are the center and radius of the circle 9x2+9y2−6x+18y−26=09x^2 + 9y^2 - 6x + 18y - 26 = 09x2+9y2−6x+18y−26=0?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The squared terms share the coefficient 999, so divide the whole equation by 999 before anything else.

    x2+y2−23x+2y−269=0x^2 + y^2 - \frac{2}{3}x + 2y - \frac{26}{9} = 0x2+y2−32​x+2y−926​=0

    Complete both squares. Half of −23-\frac{2}{3}−32​ is −13-\frac{1}{3}−31​ (square 19\frac{1}{9}91​), and half of 222 is 111 (square 111).

    (x−13)2+(y+1)2=269+19+1=3+1=4\left(x - \frac{1}{3}\right)^2 + (y + 1)^2 = \frac{26}{9} + \frac{1}{9} + 1 = 3 + 1 = 4(x−31​)2+(y+1)2=926​+91​+1=3+1=4

    The center is (13,−1)\left(\frac{1}{3}, -1\right)(31​,−1) and the radius is 4=2\sqrt{4} = 24​=2. Fractions in the center are perfectly normal; what is not allowed is skipping the division by 999.
