    Where is the focus of the parabola (x−3)2=4(y+1)(x - 3)^2 = 4(y + 1)(x−3)2=4(y+1)?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Match against (x−h)2=4p(y−k)(x - h)^2 = 4p(y - k)(x−h)2=4p(y−k). The vertex is (3,−1)(3, -1)(3,−1) and the coefficient on the right is 4p4p4p.

    4p=4  ⟹  p=14p = 4 \;\Longrightarrow\; p = 14p=4⟹p=1

    The axis is vertical, so the focus is p=1p = 1p=1 unit above the vertex.

    (h,  k+p)=(3,  −1+1)=(3,0)(h, \; k + p) = (3, \; -1 + 1) = (3, 0)(h,k+p)=(3,−1+1)=(3,0)

    The distractor (3,−2)(3,-2)(3,−2) is the directrix quoted as a point; the directrix is the line y=−2y = -2y=−2.

  2. 2

    What is the directrix of the parabola (y+2)2=−12(x−1)(y + 2)^2 = -12(x - 1)(y+2)2=−12(x−1)?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The square is on yyy, so the axis is horizontal and the form is (y−k)2=4p(x−h)(y-k)^2 = 4p(x-h)(y−k)2=4p(x−h) with vertex (1,−2)(1, -2)(1,−2).

    4p=−12  ⟹  p=−34p = -12 \;\Longrightarrow\; p = -34p=−12⟹p=−3

    For a horizontal axis the directrix is the vertical line x=h−px = h - px=h−p.

    x=1−(−3)=4x = 1 - (-3) = 4x=1−(−3)=4

    Since p<0p < 0p<0 the curve opens left, so the directrix must be the line on the right, at x=4x = 4x=4.

  3. 3

    Find the equation of the parabola with focus (2,5)(2, 5)(2,5) and directrix y=1y = 1y=1.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The directrix is horizontal, so the axis is vertical and the form is (x−h)2=4p(y−k)(x-h)^2 = 4p(y-k)(x−h)2=4p(y−k). The vertex is the midpoint between the focus and the directrix, measured along the axis x=2x = 2x=2.

    k=5+12=3  ⟹  vertex (2,3)k = \frac{5 + 1}{2} = 3 \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{vertex } (2, 3)k=25+1​=3⟹vertex (2,3)

    The signed distance from the vertex up to the focus is p=5−3=2p = 5 - 3 = 2p=5−3=2, so 4p=84p = 84p=8.

    (x−2)2=8(y−3)(x-2)^2 = 8(y-3)(x−2)2=8(y−3)

    The focus is above the directrix, so p>0p > 0p>0 and the curve opens upward.

  4. 4

    Find the equation of the parabola with focus (−1,2)(-1, 2)(−1,2) and directrix x=3x = 3x=3.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The directrix is vertical, so the axis is horizontal and the form is (y−k)2=4p(x−h)(y-k)^2 = 4p(x-h)(y−k)2=4p(x−h). The vertex is midway between the focus and the directrix along the axis y=2y = 2y=2.

    h=−1+32=1  ⟹  vertex (1,2)h = \frac{-1 + 3}{2} = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{vertex } (1, 2)h=2−1+3​=1⟹vertex (1,2)

    The signed distance from the vertex to the focus is p=−1−1=−2p = -1 - 1 = -2p=−1−1=−2, so 4p=−84p = -84p=−8.

    (y−2)2=−8(x−1)(y-2)^2 = -8(x-1)(y−2)2=−8(x−1)

    The focus is to the left of the vertex, so the curve opens left, which is what the negative ppp records.

  5. 5

    What is the vertex of the parabola y=2x2−8x+5y = 2x^2 - 8x + 5y=2x2−8x+5?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Complete the square, factoring the leading coefficient out of the xxx terms first.

    y=2(x2−4x)+5=2((x−2)2−4)+5=2(x−2)2−3y = 2(x^2 - 4x) + 5 = 2\bigl((x-2)^2 - 4\bigr) + 5 = 2(x-2)^2 - 3y=2(x2−4x)+5=2((x−2)2−4)+5=2(x−2)2−3

    In vertex form y=a(x−h)2+ky = a(x-h)^2 + ky=a(x−h)2+k the vertex is (h,k)=(2,−3)(h,k) = (2, -3)(h,k)=(2,−3). Multiplying the −4-4−4 back through by the 222 is the step most often skipped: it turns +5+5+5 into −3-3−3.

  6. 6

    Which point is equidistant from the point (0,2)(0, 2)(0,2) and the line y=−2y = -2y=−2?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    These are the focus and directrix of a parabola, so test each candidate against the definition. For (4,2)(4,2)(4,2), the distance to the point is

    (4−0)2+(2−2)2=16=4,\sqrt{(4-0)^2 + (2-2)^2} = \sqrt{16} = 4,(4−0)2+(2−2)2​=16​=4,

    and the distance to the line y=−2y = -2y=−2 is ∣2−(−2)∣=4|2 - (-2)| = 4∣2−(−2)∣=4. The two agree, so (4,2)(4,2)(4,2) is on the curve.

    The others fail: (2,4)(2,4)(2,4) is 8≈2.83\sqrt{8} \approx 2.838​≈2.83 from the point but 666 from the line, (8,4)(8,4)(8,4) is 68≈8.25\sqrt{68} \approx 8.2568​≈8.25 from the point but 666 from the line, and (2,1)(2,1)(2,1) is 5≈2.24\sqrt{5} \approx 2.245​≈2.24 from the point but 333 from the line.

    Find the equation of the parabola with vertex (−3,4)(-3, 4)(−3,4) and focus (−3,7)(-3, 7)(−3,7).

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The focus is directly above the vertex, so the axis is vertical and the form is (x−h)2=4p(y−k)(x-h)^2 = 4p(y-k)(x−h)2=4p(y−k) with (h,k)=(−3,4)(h,k) = (-3,4)(h,k)=(−3,4). The signed distance from the vertex to the focus is

    p=7−4=3  ⟹  4p=12.p = 7 - 4 = 3 \;\Longrightarrow\; 4p = 12.p=7−4=3⟹4p=12.

    (x+3)2=12(y−4)(x+3)^2 = 12(y-4)(x+3)2=12(y−4)

    The coefficient is 4p=124p = 124p=12, not p=3p = 3p=3, and the square sits on xxx because the curve opens vertically.

  8. 8

    What is the vertex of the parabola y2−4y−8x+28=0y^2 - 4y - 8x + 28 = 0y2−4y−8x+28=0?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The equation is quadratic in yyy and linear in xxx, the signature of a sideways parabola. Isolate the yyy terms and complete the square.

    y2−4y=8x−28y^2 - 4y = 8x - 28y2−4y=8x−28

    Half of −4-4−4 is −2-2−2, and (−2)2=4(-2)^2 = 4(−2)2=4, so add 444 to both sides.

    (y−2)2=8x−24=8(x−3)(y - 2)^2 = 8x - 24 = 8(x - 3)(y−2)2=8x−24=8(x−3)

    Comparing with (y−k)2=4p(x−h)(y-k)^2 = 4p(x-h)(y−k)2=4p(x−h) gives h=3h = 3h=3 and k=2k = 2k=2, so the vertex is (3,2)(3,2)(3,2). Keep the order straight: hhh is the xxx-coordinate even though the yyy term was squared.

  9. 9

    Where is the focus of the parabola (y−1)2=4(x+2)(y - 1)^2 = 4(x + 2)(y−1)2=4(x+2)?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The square is on yyy, so the axis is horizontal. Matching (y−k)2=4p(x−h)(y-k)^2 = 4p(x-h)(y−k)2=4p(x−h) gives vertex (−2,1)(-2, 1)(−2,1) and

    4p=4  ⟹  p=1.4p = 4 \;\Longrightarrow\; p = 1.4p=4⟹p=1.

    For a horizontal axis the focus is at (h+p,  k)(h + p, \; k)(h+p,k), moving along the axis, not up.

    (−2+1,  1)=(−1,1)(-2 + 1, \; 1) = (-1, 1)(−2+1,1)=(−1,1)

    The distractor (−3,1)(-3,1)(−3,1) is where the directrix crosses the axis, and (−2,2)(-2,2)(−2,2) mistakenly moves the focus vertically.

  10. 10

    A satellite dish is built by rotating a parabola about its axis. Where should the receiver be placed?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Signals arriving from a distant satellite are effectively parallel to the axis of the dish. The reflective property says that every ray parallel to the axis, wherever it strikes the surface, reflects through one single point.

    parallel ray  →  reflects    focus\text{parallel ray} \;\xrightarrow{\;\text{reflects}\;}\; \text{focus}parallel rayreflects​focus

    So all the collected energy is concentrated at the focus, which is where the receiver goes. That property is the reason the point is named the focus at all.

    The parabola y=ax2y = ax^2y=ax2 has focus (0,18)\left(0, \tfrac18\right)(0,81​). What is aaa?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    For a parabola with its vertex at the origin, the coefficient and the focus distance are linked by a=14pa = \tfrac{1}{4p}a=4p1​.

    The focus is at (0,18)\left(0, \tfrac18\right)(0,81​), so p=18p = \tfrac18p=81​.

    a=14p=14⋅18=112=2a = \frac{1}{4p} = \frac{1}{4 \cdot \tfrac18} = \frac{1}{\tfrac12} = 2a=4p1​=4⋅81​1​=21​1​=2

    Check it from the other side: y=2x2y = 2x^2y=2x2 rearranges to x2=12yx^2 = \tfrac12 yx2=21​y, so 4p=124p = \tfrac124p=21​ and p=18p = \tfrac18p=81​, as required. Reading a=18a = \tfrac18a=81​ straight off the focus is the trap here.

  12. 12

    A parabola has vertex (2,−1)(2, -1)(2,−1) and directrix y=2y = 2y=2. Where is its focus?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The directrix is horizontal, so the axis is vertical and the directrix is the line y=k−py = k - py=k−p.

    −1−p=2  ⟹  p=−3-1 - p = 2 \;\Longrightarrow\; p = -3−1−p=2⟹p=−3

    A negative ppp says the focus lies below the vertex, which fits: the directrix is above it, and a parabola always runs away from its directrix.

    (h,  k+p)=(2,  −1+(−3))=(2,−4)(h, \; k + p) = (2, \; -1 + (-3)) = (2, -4)(h,k+p)=(2,−1+(−3))=(2,−4)

    The focus and the directrix sit on opposite sides of the vertex, each 333 units away.
