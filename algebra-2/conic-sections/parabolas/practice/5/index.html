    Where is the focus of the parabola y=−2x2+8x−5y = -2x^2 + 8x - 5y=−2x2+8x−5?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Complete the square, factoring the −2-2−2 out of the xxx terms.

    y=−2(x2−4x)−5=−2((x−2)2−4)−5=−2(x−2)2+3y = -2(x^2 - 4x) - 5 = -2\bigl((x-2)^2 - 4\bigr) - 5 = -2(x-2)^2 + 3y=−2(x2−4x)−5=−2((x−2)2−4)−5=−2(x−2)2+3

    The vertex is (2,3)(2,3)(2,3). Now isolate the square to expose 4p4p4p: subtract 333 and divide by −2-2−2.

    (x−2)2=−12(y−3)  ⟹  4p=−12  ⟹  p=−18(x-2)^2 = -\tfrac12 (y - 3) \;\Longrightarrow\; 4p = -\tfrac12 \;\Longrightarrow\; p = -\tfrac18(x−2)2=−21​(y−3)⟹4p=−21​⟹p=−81​

    The focus is ppp from the vertex, and ppp is negative because the curve opens downward.

    (2,  3−18)=(2,238)\left(2, \; 3 - \tfrac18\right) = \left(2, \tfrac{23}{8}\right)(2,3−81​)=(2,823​)

    The distractor 258\tfrac{25}{8}825​ is where the directrix sits, on the other side of the vertex.

  2. 2

    A parabolic arch is 202020 m wide at its base and 555 m high at the centre. How high is the arch at a point 555 m horizontally from the centre?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Put the vertex at the top of the arch, at (0,5)(0,5)(0,5), so the curve is x2=4p(y−5)x^2 = 4p(y - 5)x2=4p(y−5) and opens downward. The base is 202020 m wide, so the arch meets the ground 101010 m either side of the centre, at (10,0)(10, 0)(10,0).

    102=4p(0−5)  ⟹  100=−20p  ⟹  4p=−2010^2 = 4p(0 - 5) \;\Longrightarrow\; 100 = -20p \;\Longrightarrow\; 4p = -20102=4p(0−5)⟹100=−20p⟹4p=−20

    The arch is therefore x2=−20(y−5)x^2 = -20(y-5)x2=−20(y−5). Set x=5x = 5x=5:

    25=−20(y−5)  ⟹  y−5=−1.25  ⟹  y=3.7525 = -20(y - 5) \;\Longrightarrow\; y - 5 = -1.25 \;\Longrightarrow\; y = 3.7525=−20(y−5)⟹y−5=−1.25⟹y=3.75

    The height is 3.753.753.75 m. Halving the distance from the centre does not halve the drop: the drop scales with the square of the horizontal distance, so it falls only a quarter as far as it does at the base.

    The parabola (y−2)2=4p(x+1)(y - 2)^2 = 4p(x + 1)(y−2)2=4p(x+1) passes through (2,6)(2, 6)(2,6). What is its directrix?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Substitute the point to find ppp.

    (6−2)2=4p(2+1)  ⟹  16=12p  ⟹  p=43(6-2)^2 = 4p(2+1) \;\Longrightarrow\; 16 = 12p \;\Longrightarrow\; p = \tfrac43(6−2)2=4p(2+1)⟹16=12p⟹p=34​

    The axis is horizontal with vertex (−1,2)(-1, 2)(−1,2), so the directrix is the vertical line x=h−px = h - px=h−p.

    x=−1−43=−73x = -1 - \tfrac43 = -\tfrac73x=−1−34​=−37​

    Since p>0p > 0p>0 the curve opens right, so the directrix must lie to the left of the vertex, which confirms the negative value. The distractor 13\tfrac1331​ is the focus's xxx-coordinate, h+ph + ph+p.

  4. 4

    The parabola (x+2)2=4p(y−3)(x + 2)^2 = 4p(y - 3)(x+2)2=4p(y−3) has directrix y=5y = 5y=5. What is ppp?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The vertex is (−2,3)(-2, 3)(−2,3), and for a vertical axis the directrix is the line y=k−py = k - py=k−p.

    3−p=5  ⟹  p=−23 - p = 5 \;\Longrightarrow\; p = -23−p=5⟹p=−2

    The sign is the whole answer here. The directrix sits above the vertex, so the focus must sit below it, at (−2,1)(-2, 1)(−2,1), and the curve opens downward. The distractor −8-8−8 is 4p4p4p, the coefficient, not ppp.

  5. 5

    What are the endpoints of the chord of x2=8yx^2 = 8yx2=8y that passes through the focus and is parallel to the directrix?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    From 4p=84p = 84p=8 we get p=2p = 2p=2, so the focus is (0,2)(0, 2)(0,2). The directrix is horizontal, so a chord parallel to it is the horizontal line through the focus, y=2y = 2y=2. Substitute that height into the equation.

    x2=8(2)=16  ⟹  x=±4x^2 = 8(2) = 16 \;\Longrightarrow\; x = \pm 4x2=8(2)=16⟹x=±4

    The endpoints are (−4,2)(-4, 2)(−4,2) and (4,2)(4, 2)(4,2). Their separation is 888, which is exactly the focal width ∣4p∣=8|4p| = 8∣4p∣=8, as it must be.

  6. 6

    Which of these parabolas has its focus at the origin?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Each has ∣4p∣=4|4p| = 4∣4p∣=4, so ∣p∣=1|p| = 1∣p∣=1: the focus is one unit from the vertex in every case. Find each vertex and step off ppp.

    x2=4(y+1) ⁣:  vertex (0,−1),  p=1  ⟹  focus (0,−1+1)=(0,0)x^2 = 4(y+1)\colon\; \text{vertex } (0,-1), \; p = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{focus } (0, -1 + 1) = (0,0)x2=4(y+1):vertex (0,−1),p=1⟹focus (0,−1+1)=(0,0)

    That is the one. The others land elsewhere: x2=4(y−1)x^2 = 4(y-1)x2=4(y−1) has vertex (0,1)(0,1)(0,1) and focus (0,2)(0,2)(0,2); x2=−4(y+1)x^2 = -4(y+1)x2=−4(y+1) has vertex (0,−1)(0,-1)(0,−1) but p=−1p = -1p=−1, so its focus is (0,−2)(0,-2)(0,−2); and y2=4(x−1)y^2 = 4(x-1)y2=4(x−1) opens sideways from vertex (1,0)(1,0)(1,0), giving focus (2,0)(2,0)(2,0).

    A point PPP on the parabola x2=4yx^2 = 4yx2=4y is 555 units from the focus. What are the possible coordinates of PPP?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    From 4p=44p = 44p=4 we get p=1p = 1p=1, so the directrix is y=−1y = -1y=−1 and the focal distance of a point on the curve is y+1y + 1y+1.

    y+1=5  ⟹  y=4y + 1 = 5 \;\Longrightarrow\; y = 4y+1=5⟹y=4

    Now find the matching xxx values on the curve.

    x2=4(4)=16  ⟹  x=±4x^2 = 4(4) = 16 \;\Longrightarrow\; x = \pm 4x2=4(4)=16⟹x=±4

    Both (4,4)(4,4)(4,4) and (−4,4)(-4,4)(−4,4) work, by symmetry about the axis. Check one: the distance from (4,4)(4,4)(4,4) to the focus (0,1)(0,1)(0,1) is 16+9=5\sqrt{16 + 9} = 516+9​=5.

    Which point on the parabola x2=8yx^2 = 8yx2=8y lies closest to the focus, and how far from the focus is it?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    From 4p=84p = 84p=8 we get p=2p = 2p=2, so the focus is (0,2)(0,2)(0,2) and the directrix is y=−2y = -2y=−2. For a point on the curve the focal distance equals the distance to the directrix.

    PF=y+2PF = y + 2PF=y+2

    Every point of x2=8yx^2 = 8yx2=8y has y≥0y \ge 0y≥0, so y+2y + 2y+2 is smallest when y=0y = 0y=0, at the vertex.

    PFmin⁡=0+2=2PF_{\min} = 0 + 2 = 2PFmin​=0+2=2

    The closest point is the vertex (0,0)(0,0)(0,0), exactly ∣p∣=2|p| = 2∣p∣=2 units from the focus. The point (4,2)(4,2)(4,2) is on the curve and genuinely 444 units from the focus, but it is not the closest; and (0,2)(0,2)(0,2) is the focus itself, which does not lie on the parabola at all.

    The parabola x2=8yx^2 = 8yx2=8y is translated 333 units right and 222 units down. Where is the focus of the image?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Find the original focus first. From 4p=84p = 84p=8 we get p=2p = 2p=2, and the vertex is the origin, so the focus is (0,2)(0,2)(0,2).

    A translation slides every feature of the figure by the same vector, since it preserves all distances. Adding 333 to xxx and subtracting 222 from yyy:

    (0+3,  2−2)=(3,0)(0 + 3, \; 2 - 2) = (3, 0)(0+3,2−2)=(3,0)

    The image has equation (x−3)2=8(y+2)(x-3)^2 = 8(y+2)(x−3)2=8(y+2), with vertex (3,−2)(3,-2)(3,−2) and focus 222 above it, at (3,0)(3,0)(3,0), which agrees.

    What is the vertex of the parabola 2x2+12x−y+20=02x^2 + 12x - y + 20 = 02x2+12x−y+20=0?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Solve for yyy first, since yyy appears to the first power.

    y=2x2+12x+20y = 2x^2 + 12x + 20y=2x2+12x+20

    Now complete the square, factoring the 222 out of the xxx terms.

    y=2(x2+6x)+20=2((x+3)2−9)+20=2(x+3)2+2y = 2(x^2 + 6x) + 20 = 2\bigl((x+3)^2 - 9\bigr) + 20 = 2(x+3)^2 + 2y=2(x2+6x)+20=2((x+3)2−9)+20=2(x+3)2+2

    In vertex form y=a(x−h)2+ky = a(x-h)^2 + ky=a(x−h)2+k, the vertex is (−3,2)(-3, 2)(−3,2). Remember to multiply the −9-9−9 by the outside 222 before combining it with the 202020, which is what turns 202020 into 222.

    A parabola has its vertex at the origin, opens to the right, and the chord through its focus perpendicular to the axis has endpoints (1,2)(1, 2)(1,2) and (1,−2)(1, -2)(1,−2). What is its equation?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Opening right from the origin means y2=4pxy^2 = 4pxy2=4px with p>0p > 0p>0. That chord is the focal chord, so its endpoints lie at the focus's xxx-coordinate, giving p=1p = 1p=1 straight away.

    Confirm with the curve itself. At x=px = px=p the equation gives

    y2=4p⋅p=4p2  ⟹  y=±2p,y^2 = 4p \cdot p = 4p^2 \;\Longrightarrow\; y = \pm 2p,y2=4p⋅p=4p2⟹y=±2p,

    so the endpoints are (p,±2p)(p, \pm 2p)(p,±2p). Matching (1,±2)(1, \pm 2)(1,±2) gives p=1p = 1p=1 from both coordinates, a consistent answer.

    y2=4(1)x=4xy^2 = 4(1)x = 4xy2=4(1)x=4x

    The chord's length is 4=∣4p∣4 = |4p|4=∣4p∣, the focal width, as expected.

    Find the equation of the parabola with focus (2,1)(2, 1)(2,1) and directrix y=−3y = -3y=−3.

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The directrix is horizontal, so the axis is vertical and the form is (x−h)2=4p(y−k)(x-h)^2 = 4p(y-k)(x−h)2=4p(y−k). The vertex is midway between the focus and the directrix.

    k=1+(−3)2=−1  ⟹  vertex (2,−1)k = \frac{1 + (-3)}{2} = -1 \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{vertex } (2, -1)k=21+(−3)​=−1⟹vertex (2,−1)

    The signed distance from the vertex up to the focus is p=1−(−1)=2p = 1 - (-1) = 2p=1−(−1)=2, so 4p=84p = 84p=8.

    (x−2)2=8(y+1)(x-2)^2 = 8(y+1)(x−2)2=8(y+1)

    Verify from the definition: (x−2)2+(y−1)2=∣y+3∣\sqrt{(x-2)^2 + (y-1)^2} = |y+3|(x−2)2+(y−1)2​=∣y+3∣ squares to (x−2)2+y2−2y+1=y2+6y+9(x-2)^2 + y^2 - 2y + 1 = y^2 + 6y + 9(x−2)2+y2−2y+1=y2+6y+9, which simplifies to (x−2)2=8y+8(x-2)^2 = 8y + 8(x−2)2=8y+8. The two agree.
