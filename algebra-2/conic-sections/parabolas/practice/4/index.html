  1. 1

    Find the equation of the set of points equidistant from the point (0,−3)(0, -3)(0,−3) and the line y=3y = 3y=3.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    This is a parabola with focus (0,−3)(0,-3)(0,−3) and directrix y=3y = 3y=3. Write the distance condition for (x,y)(x,y)(x,y) and square both sides, which is reversible since both sides are distances.

    x2+(y+3)2=∣y−3∣\sqrt{x^2 + (y+3)^2} = |y - 3|x2+(y+3)2​=∣y−3∣

    x2+y2+6y+9=y2−6y+9x^2 + y^2 + 6y + 9 = y^2 - 6y + 9x2+y2+6y+9=y2−6y+9

    The y2y^2y2 terms and the constants cancel, leaving x2=−12yx^2 = -12yx2=−12y.

    The standard form confirms it: 4p=−124p = -124p=−12 gives p=−3p = -3p=−3, so the focus is 333 below the vertex at the origin, and the curve opens downward, away from the directrix above.

  2. 2

    What is the directrix of the parabola y=3x2−12x+13y = 3x^2 - 12x + 13y=3x2−12x+13?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Complete the square, factoring the 333 out of the xxx terms.

    y=3(x2−4x)+13=3((x−2)2−4)+13=3(x−2)2+1y = 3(x^2 - 4x) + 13 = 3\bigl((x-2)^2 - 4\bigr) + 13 = 3(x-2)^2 + 1y=3(x2−4x)+13=3((x−2)2−4)+13=3(x−2)2+1

    The vertex is (2,1)(2,1)(2,1). Isolate the square to expose 4p4p4p: subtract 111 and divide by 333.

    (x−2)2=13(y−1)  ⟹  4p=13  ⟹  p=112(x-2)^2 = \tfrac13 (y - 1) \;\Longrightarrow\; 4p = \tfrac13 \;\Longrightarrow\; p = \tfrac{1}{12}(x−2)2=31​(y−1)⟹4p=31​⟹p=121​

    The directrix is ppp below the vertex, at y=k−py = k - py=k−p.

    y=1−112=1112y = 1 - \tfrac{1}{12} = \tfrac{11}{12}y=1−121​=1211​

    The distractor 1312\tfrac{13}{12}1213​ is the focus's height, on the other side of the vertex.

  3. 3

    A point PPP on the parabola y2=12xy^2 = 12xy2=12x is 777 units from the focus. What is the xxx-coordinate of PPP?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    From 4p=124p = 124p=12 we get p=3p = 3p=3, so this sideways parabola has focus (3,0)(3,0)(3,0) and directrix x=−3x = -3x=−3. For a point on the curve, the distance to the focus equals the distance to the directrix, which for a vertical directrix is measured horizontally.

    PF=x+3PF = x + 3PF=x+3

    Set that equal to 777:

    x+3=7  ⟹  x=4x + 3 = 7 \;\Longrightarrow\; x = 4x+3=7⟹x=4

    Check it: at x=4x = 4x=4, y2=48y^2 = 48y2=48, and the distance from (4,48)(4, \sqrt{48})(4,48​) to (3,0)(3,0)(3,0) is 1+48=7\sqrt{1 + 48} = 71+48​=7.

  4. 4

    Where is the focus of the parabola x=−y28x = -\dfrac{y^2}{8}x=−8y2​?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Clear the fraction to reach the standard form. Multiplying by −8-8−8 gives y2=−8xy^2 = -8xy2=−8x.

    4p=−8  ⟹  p=−24p = -8 \;\Longrightarrow\; p = -24p=−8⟹p=−2

    The square is on yyy, so the axis is horizontal and the focus is at (p,0)(p, 0)(p,0), moving along the xxx-axis.

    (−2,  0)(-2, \; 0)(−2,0)

    The negative ppp opens the curve to the left, toward its focus. The distractor (2,0)(2,0)(2,0) is where the directrix x=2x = 2x=2 crosses the axis, and (0,−2)(0,-2)(0,−2) mistakenly treats the curve as vertical.

  5. 5

    A parabola with a horizontal axis has vertex (1,−3)(1, -3)(1,−3) and passes through (9,1)(9, 1)(9,1). Where is its focus?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A horizontal axis with vertex (1,−3)(1,-3)(1,−3) means (y+3)2=4p(x−1)(y + 3)^2 = 4p(x - 1)(y+3)2=4p(x−1). Substitute the point (9,1)(9,1)(9,1).

    (1+3)2=4p(9−1)  ⟹  16=32p  ⟹  p=12(1 + 3)^2 = 4p(9 - 1) \;\Longrightarrow\; 16 = 32p \;\Longrightarrow\; p = \tfrac12(1+3)2=4p(9−1)⟹16=32p⟹p=21​

    For a horizontal axis the focus is at (h+p,  k)(h + p, \; k)(h+p,k), so it moves sideways from the vertex, never up.

    (1+12,  −3)=(32,−3)\left(1 + \tfrac12, \; -3\right) = \left(\tfrac32, -3\right)(1+21​,−3)=(23​,−3)

    The answer (1,−52)\left(1, -\tfrac52\right)(1,−25​) makes the classic error of shifting the focus vertically away from the vertex, which is what the other standard form does.

  6. 6

    A parabolic microphone dish is 606060 cm across and 151515 cm deep at the centre. How far from the vertex is its focus?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Put the vertex at the origin with the dish opening upward, so its cross-section is x2=4pyx^2 = 4pyx2=4py. Across 606060 cm means the rim is 303030 cm either side of the axis, and 151515 cm deep puts the rim at height 151515, so (30,15)(30, 15)(30,15) lies on the curve.

    302=4p(15)  ⟹  900=60p  ⟹  p=1530^2 = 4p(15) \;\Longrightarrow\; 900 = 60p \;\Longrightarrow\; p = 15302=4p(15)⟹900=60p⟹p=15

    The focus is 151515 cm from the vertex, which happens to be exactly level with the rim here. Using the full width 606060 in place of the half-width 303030 is the standard trap and would give a focus four times too far away.

  7. 7

    For which value of kkk is the line y=x+ky = x + ky=x+k tangent to the parabola x2=4yx^2 = 4yx2=4y?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Substitute the line into the parabola and collect everything on one side.

    x2=4(x+k)  ⟹  x2−4x−4k=0x^2 = 4(x + k) \;\Longrightarrow\; x^2 - 4x - 4k = 0x2=4(x+k)⟹x2−4x−4k=0

    Tangency means the line meets the curve exactly once, so this quadratic must have a repeated root, which happens exactly when the discriminant is zero.

    (−4)2−4(1)(−4k)=16+16k=0  ⟹  k=−1(-4)^2 - 4(1)(-4k) = 16 + 16k = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; k = -1(−4)2−4(1)(−4k)=16+16k=0⟹k=−1

    Check it: with k=−1k = -1k=−1 the quadratic is x2−4x+4=(x−2)2=0x^2 - 4x + 4 = (x-2)^2 = 0x2−4x+4=(x−2)2=0, a double root at x=2x = 2x=2. For k>−1k > -1k>−1 the discriminant is positive and the line cuts the curve twice; for k<−1k < -1k<−1 it misses entirely.

    What is the focal width of the parabola y=x26y = \dfrac{x^2}{6}y=6x2​?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Clear the fraction: multiplying by 666 gives x2=6yx^2 = 6yx2=6y, which is x2=4pyx^2 = 4pyx2=4py with

    4p=6  ⟹  p=32.4p = 6 \;\Longrightarrow\; p = \tfrac32.4p=6⟹p=23​.

    The focal width is ∣4p∣=6|4p| = 6∣4p∣=6, the coefficient itself.

    Confirm it by cutting the curve at the focus's height y=32y = \tfrac32y=23​: then x2=6⋅32=9x^2 = 6 \cdot \tfrac32 = 9x2=6⋅23​=9, so x=±3x = \pm 3x=±3 and the chord runs from (−3,32)(-3, \tfrac32)(−3,23​) to (3,32)(3, \tfrac32)(3,23​), a length of 666. The distractor 32\tfrac3223​ is ppp, only a quarter of the width.

    A parabola of the form x2=4pyx^2 = 4pyx2=4py has focal width 101010 and opens downward. What is ppp?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The focal width is ∣4p∣|4p|∣4p∣, so start from the magnitude.

    ∣4p∣=10  ⟹  ∣p∣=104=52|4p| = 10 \;\Longrightarrow\; |p| = \tfrac{10}{4} = \tfrac52∣4p∣=10⟹∣p∣=410​=25​

    Now fix the sign. Opening downward means the focus lies below the vertex, so ppp is negative.

    p=−52p = -\tfrac52p=−25​

    The curve is x2=−10yx^2 = -10yx2=−10y. The distractor −10-10−10 is 4p4p4p rather than ppp, and 52\tfrac5225​ has the right size but opens the curve the wrong way.

  10. 10

    The point P=(4,2)P = (4, 2)P=(4,2) lies on the parabola x2=8yx^2 = 8yx2=8y. The tangent at PPP is the perpendicular bisector of the segment joining the focus to which point?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The tangent at PPP bisects the segment from the focus FFF to DDD, the foot of the perpendicular from PPP to the directrix. Since 4p=84p = 84p=8, we have p=2p = 2p=2, so the directrix is y=−2y = -2y=−2.

    The foot of the perpendicular from P=(4,2)P = (4,2)P=(4,2) to a horizontal line keeps the same xxx-coordinate.

    D=(4,−2)D = (4, -2)D=(4,−2)

    And the definition checks out: PD=∣2−(−2)∣=4PD = |2 - (-2)| = 4PD=∣2−(−2)∣=4, while PFPFPF, the distance from (4,2)(4,2)(4,2) to (0,2)(0,2)(0,2), is also 444. Because PPP is equidistant from FFF and DDD, it lies on the perpendicular bisector of FDFDFD, which is the tangent, and that mirror is what reflects an incoming ray from DDD's direction toward FFF.

  11. 11

    A parabola has its vertex at the origin, its axis along the yyy-axis, and it passes through (3,−3)(3, -3)(3,−3). What is its directrix?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    An axis along the yyy-axis with the vertex at the origin means x2=4pyx^2 = 4pyx2=4py. Substitute the point (3,−3)(3,-3)(3,−3).

    32=4p(−3)  ⟹  9=−12p  ⟹  p=−343^2 = 4p(-3) \;\Longrightarrow\; 9 = -12p \;\Longrightarrow\; p = -\tfrac3432=4p(−3)⟹9=−12p⟹p=−43​

    The directrix of x2=4pyx^2 = 4pyx2=4py is the line y=−py = -py=−p.

    y=−(−34)=34y = -\left(-\tfrac34\right) = \tfrac34y=−(−43​)=43​

    The curve passes through a point below the vertex, so it opens downward, which puts the directrix above the vertex and the focus below it at (0,−34)\left(0, -\tfrac34\right)(0,−43​). The choice y=−34y = -\tfrac34y=−43​ quotes that focus height instead of the directrix.

    A parabola has its vertex at the origin, opens upward, and passes through (6,3)(6, 3)(6,3). A point PPP on this parabola is 121212 units from the focus. What is the yyy-coordinate of PPP?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Find the curve first. Opening upward from the origin means x2=4pyx^2 = 4pyx2=4py, and the point (6,3)(6,3)(6,3) is on it.

    62=4p(3)  ⟹  36=12p  ⟹  p=36^2 = 4p(3) \;\Longrightarrow\; 36 = 12p \;\Longrightarrow\; p = 362=4p(3)⟹36=12p⟹p=3

    So the parabola is x2=12yx^2 = 12yx2=12y and its directrix is y=−3y = -3y=−3. For any point on the curve, the focal distance equals the distance to the directrix.

    PF=y+3=12  ⟹  y=9PF = y + 3 = 12 \;\Longrightarrow\; y = 9PF=y+3=12⟹y=9

    The answer is y=9y = 9y=9, where x2=108x^2 = 108x2=108. Reading 121212 straight off as the height forgets that the focal distance is measured from y=−3y = -3y=−3, not from the vertex.
