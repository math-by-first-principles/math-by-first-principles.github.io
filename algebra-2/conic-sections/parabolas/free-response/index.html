  1. Problem 1 A point and line

    The figure gives a focus and a directrix. Find the signed value of ppp for their parabola.

    Text description of this figure

    A grid with the x-axis from -5 to 3 and the y-axis from -2 to 6 on equal scales, a grid line and a label at every integer. A single point labeled F sits at x equals -1 and y equals 4. A dashed horizontal line, labeled directrix, is drawn along the x-axis, at height 0, across the whole grid. No parabola, vertex or distance is drawn.

    1. Hint 1

      The vertex lies halfway from the focus to the directrix along the axis.

    2. Hint 2

      Read the two heights, find their midpoint, and measure upward from that midpoint to the focus.

    Answer

    p=2p=2p=2.

    Full solution

    The focus has height 444 and the directrix has height 000, so the vertex height is

    k=4+02k=\frac{4+0}{2}k=24+0​ k=2.k=2.k=2.

    The focus is above the vertex, so

    p=4−2p=4-2p=4−2 p=2.p=2.p=2.

    The vertex is also 222 units above the directrix.

    Answer

    p=2p=2p=2.

    Key idea

    The signed value of ppp measures the displacement from the vertex toward the focus along the axis.

    2∣p∣=7.2|p|=7.2∣p∣=7.

    The required distance is 7 cm.

    Answer

    Key idea

    The separation of the focus and directrix is half the focal width.

  5. Problem 5 Moving a reflector

    The parabola y2+10y+20x+65=0y^2+10y+20x+65=0y2+10y+20x+65=0 is translated so that its focus becomes (−4,6)(-4,6)(−4,6). Find the translation and the equation of the translated curve in standard form.

    1. Hint 1

      A translation moves the focus and vertex by the same displacement.

    2. Hint 2

      Complete the square in y to locate the original vertex and focus, then compare the focus coordinates.

    Answer

    Full solution

    Completing the square, y2+10y=(y+5)2−25y^2+10y=(y+5)^2-25y2+10y=(y+5)2−25, so

    (y+5)2=−20x−40(y+5)^2=-20x-40(y+5)2=−20x−40

    that is,

    (y+5)2=−20(x+2).(y+5)^2=-20(x+2).(y+5)2=−20(x+2).

    The vertex is (−2,−5)(-2,-5)(−2,−5) and 4p=−204p=-204p=−20, so p=−5p=-5p=−5 and the focus is (−7,−5)(-7,-5)(−7,−5).

    Reaching (−4,6)(-4,6)(−4,6) requires adding (3,11)(3,11)(3,11).

    The vertex becomes (1,6)(1,6)(1,6) and p is unchanged, giving

    (y−6)2=−20(x−1).(y-6)^2=-20(x-1).(y−6)2=−20(x−1).

    Its focus is (1−5,6)(1-5,6)(1−5,6), which is (−4,6)(-4,6)(−4,6), as required.

  6. Problem 6 A chord across the curve

    A vertical parabola opens upward. Its chord through the focus, parallel to the directrix, has endpoints (−5,7)(-5,7)(−5,7) and (7,7)(7,7)(7,7). Find its focus, directrix, and standard equation.

    Focus (1,7)(1,7)(1,7); directrix y=1y=1y=1; (x−1)2=12(y−4)(x-1)^2=12(y-4)(x−1)2=12(y−4).

    Full solution

    The chord midpoint is (1,7)(1,7)(1,7) and its length is 121212, so

    p=124p=\frac{12}{4}p=412​ p=3.p=3.p=3.

    The vertex is (1,4)(1,4)(1,4) and the directrix is y=1y=1y=1.

    Hence

    (x−1)2=12(y−4).(x-1)^2=12(y-4).(x−1)2=12(y−4).

    At both endpoints the two sides equal 363636, confirming the chord.

    Answer

    Focus (1,7)(1,7)(1,7); directrix y=1y=1y=1; (x−1)2=12(y−4)(x-1)^2=12(y-4)(x−1)2=12(y−4).

  10. Problem 10 A covered mirror

    An upward parabolic mirror receives light traveling downward parallel to its axis. Its left half is covered, while its right half keeps the same shape. A student says the uncovered half will no longer direct all the rays it reflects through the original focus. Is this claim correct? Explain using the distance condition.

    1. Hint 1

      Which geometric facts does the reflection argument use at a single point?

    2. Hint 2

      For an uncovered point P, compare PF with the perpendicular distance from P to the directrix.

    Answer

    No; every ray reflected by the uncovered half still passes through the original focus.

    Full solution

    Covering part of the mirror does not move its focus, directrix, or remaining points.

    At each uncovered point,

    PF=PD.PF=PD.PF=PD.

    The tangent there exchanges F and D as mirror images.

    A ray arriving parallel to the axis travels along the line PD and reflects along PF toward F.

    The covered half reduces the collected light but does not change where the remaining reflected rays meet.

    Answer

    No; every ray reflected by the uncovered half still passes through the original focus.

    Key idea

    The focusing property of a parabola holds at each reflecting point separately.
