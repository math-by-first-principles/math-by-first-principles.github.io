    A hyperbola has foci (0,±10)(0, \pm 10)(0,±10), and every point on it is exactly 121212 units further from one focus than from the other. What is its equation?

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The foci are on the yyy-axis, so the curve opens up and down and c=10c = 10c=10. The constant difference is 2a2a2a by definition.

    2a=12⟹a=62a = 12 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a = 62a=12⟹a=6

    Since a=6<10=ca = 6 < 10 = ca=6<10=c, the curve exists. Now find b2b^2b2 by subtracting:

    b2=c2−a2=100−36=64b^2 = c^2 - a^2 = 100 - 36 = 64b2=c2−a2=100−36=64

    The equation is y236−x264=1\frac{y^2}{36} - \frac{x^2}{64} = 136y2​−64x2​=1. Note that a2=36a^2 = 36a2=36 goes under the positive term, and c2=100c^2 = 100c2=100 never appears in the equation at all.

    A rectangular hyperbola is centred at the origin with a vertex at (0,5)(0, 5)(0,5). What is its equation?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The vertex is on the yyy-axis, so the curve opens up and down with a=5a = 5a=5. Rectangular means a=ba = ba=b, so b=5b = 5b=5 too.

    y225−x225=1⟹y2−x2=25\frac{y^2}{25} - \frac{x^2}{25} = 1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y^2 - x^2 = 2525y2​−25x2​=1⟹y2−x2=25

    Writing x2−y2=25x^2 - y^2 = 25x2−y2=25 instead would put the vertices on the xxx-axis, which contradicts the given vertex. The asymptotes are y=±xy = \pm xy=±x either way, which is exactly why the asymptotes cannot tell the two apart.

    At the vertex x=ax = ax=a, how far below the asymptote y=baxy = \dfrac{b}{a}xy=ab​x does the hyperbola x2a2−y2b2=1\dfrac{x^2}{a^2} - \dfrac{y^2}{b^2} = 1a2x2​−b2y2​=1 sit?

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Two hyperbolas are centred at the origin and open left and right. One has e=1.05e = 1.05e=1.05 and the other has e=3e = 3e=3. Which one has the wider-opening branches?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Dividing b2=c2−a2b^2 = c^2 - a^2b2=c2−a2 by a2a^2a2 shows that the asymptote slope depends on eccentricity alone.

    ba=e2−1\frac{b}{a} = \sqrt{e^2 - 1}ab​=e2−1​

    For e=1.05e = 1.05e=1.05 the slope is 0.1025≈0.32\sqrt{0.1025} \approx 0.320.1025​≈0.32, so the asymptotes are nearly flat and the branches are narrow and sharply pointed. For e=3e = 3e=3 the slope is 8≈2.83\sqrt{8} \approx 2.838​≈2.83, so the asymptotes are steep and the branches flare out. The value of aaa only scales the picture; it never changes the slope, so eccentricity settles the question on its own.
