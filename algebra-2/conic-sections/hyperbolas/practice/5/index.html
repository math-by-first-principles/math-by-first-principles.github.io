    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    What is the graph of x24−y29=0\dfrac{x^2}{4} - \dfrac{y^2}{9} = 04x2​−9y2​=0?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    With 000 on the right, the left side is a difference of squares and factors.

    (x2−y3)(x2+y3)=0⟹y=±32x\left(\frac{x}{2} - \frac{y}{3}\right)\left(\frac{x}{2} + \frac{y}{3}\right) = 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y = \pm\frac{3}{2}x(2x​−3y​)(2x​+3y​)=0⟹y=±23​x

    A product is zero exactly when one of its factors is, so the graph is the pair of lines y=±32xy = \pm\frac{3}{2}xy=±23​x, which are precisely the asymptotes of x24−y29=1\frac{x^2}{4} - \frac{y^2}{9} = 14x2​−9y2​=1. That is why replacing the 111 with 000 is a legitimate way to find asymptotes rather than a memory trick.
