  2. Problem 2 Two measured distances

    1. Hint 1

      The constant absolute difference of focal distances is the full transverse-axis length.

    2. Hint 2

      Subtract the shorter distance from the longer distance.

    Answer

    13 units.

    Full solution

    The absolute difference is

    ∣23−10∣=13.|23-10|=13.∣23−10∣=13.

    This is the constant 2a2a2a, which is also the length of the transverse axis, so the required length is 131313 units.

    Answer

    13 units.

    Key idea

    A hyperbola's transverse-axis length equals its constant absolute focal-distance difference.

  8. Problem 8 Two geometry records

    Answer
