  1. Problem 1 Reordered variables

    Write the augmented matrix of 2z−x=72z-x=72z−x=7, y=3x+1y=3x+1y=3x+1, and z−y=4z-y=4z−y=4, using columns in the order x,y,zx,y,zx,y,z.

    1. Hint 1

      Put all variable terms on the left in the named order.

    2. Hint 2

      A missing variable still needs a zero in its column.

    Answer

    [−1027−31010−114]\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} -1 & 0 & 2 & 7 \\ -3 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 & 4 \end{array}\right]​−1−30​01−1​201​714​​.

    Full solution

    Rewrite the equations as −x+2z=7-x+2z=7−x+2z=7, −3x+y=1-3x+y=1−3x+y=1, and −y+z=4-y+z=4−y+z=4.

    The constant column stays on the right.

    [−1027−31010−114]\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} -1 & 0 & 2 & 7 \\ -3 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 & 4 \end{array}\right]​−1−30​01−1​201​714​​

    Each zero keeps the absent variable in its correct place.

    Answer

    [−1027−31010−114]\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} -1 & 0 & 2 & 7 \\ -3 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & -1 & 1 & 4 \end{array}\right]​−1−30​01−1​201​714​​.

    Key idea

    Keep a fixed variable order when translating equations into matrix rows.

  2. Problem 2 An encoded equation

    The row [2−106]\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}2&-1&0&6\end{array}\right][2​−1​0​6​] uses variable order p,q,rp,q,rp,q,r. Write the equation it records.

    1. Hint 1

      Each coefficient multiplies the variable in its named column.

    2. Hint 2

      The entry after the bar is the right side.

    Answer

    2p−q=62p-q=62p−q=6.

    Full solution

    The row records 2p−q+0r=62p-q+0r=62p−q+0r=6.

    Removing the zero term gives

    2p−q=62p-q=62p−q=6

    The absent rrr term does not shift the qqq coefficient into another column.

    Answer

    2p−q=62p-q=62p−q=6.

    Key idea

    A zero entry records an absent variable without changing column order.

  3. Problem 3 Scaling a record

    Replace R1R_1R1​ by −12R1-\frac12R_1−21​R1​ in the augmented matrix shown. Give the resulting matrix.

    [2−46−132]\left[\begin{array}{cc|c} 2 & -4 & 6 \\ -1 & 3 & 2 \end{array}\right][2−1​−43​62​]

    1. Hint 1

      Apply the multiplier to every entry in the named row.

    2. Hint 2

      Leave the other row unchanged.

    Answer

    [−12−3−132]\left[\begin{array}{cc|c} -1 & 2 & -3 \\ -1 & 3 & 2 \end{array}\right][−1−1​23​−32​].

    Full solution

    Multiplying the top row by −1/2-1/2−1/2 gives (−1,2∣−3)(-1,2\mid-3)(−1,2∣−3).

    Hence the new matrix is

    [−12−3−132]\left[\begin{array}{cc|c} -1 & 2 & -3 \\ -1 & 3 & 2 \end{array}\right][−1−1​23​−32​]

    Multiplying its first row by −2-2−2 recovers the original record.

    Answer

    [−12−3−132]\left[\begin{array}{cc|c} -1 & 2 & -3 \\ -1 & 3 & 2 \end{array}\right][−1−1​23​−32​].

    Key idea

    Scaling a row multiplies its constant along with its coefficients.

  4. Problem 4 Three linked records

    Write an augmented matrix, using columns in the order x,y,zx,y,zx,y,z, and solve y+2z=5y+2z=5y+2z=5, x−y=1x-y=1x−y=1, and 2x+z=82x+z=82x+z=8 by row reduction.

    1. Hint 1

      A row beginning with xxx makes a useful top row.

    2. Hint 2

      After clearing the xxx column, clear the yyy column below its pivot.

    Answer

    Augmented matrix [01251−1012018]\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 0 & 1 & 2 & 5 \\ 1 & -1 & 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 & 8 \end{array}\right]​012​1−10​201​518​​; (x,y,z)=(103,73,43)(x,y,z)=(\tfrac{10}{3},\tfrac73,\tfrac43)(x,y,z)=(310​,37​,34​).

    Full solution

    In variable order x,y,zx,y,zx,y,z, the matrix is

    [01251−1012018]\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 0 & 1 & 2 & 5 \\ 1 & -1 & 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 & 8 \end{array}\right]​012​1−10​201​518​​

    Swap the first two rows.

    Replace R3R_3R3​ by R3−2R1R_3-2R_1R3​−2R1​, then by R3−2R2R_3-2R_2R3​−2R2​.

    [1−101012500−3−4]\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 & 5 \\ 0 & 0 & -3 & -4 \end{array}\right]​100​−110​02−3​15−4​​

    The bottom row gives z=4/3z=4/3z=4/3.

    Then

    y=5−2z=7/3x=y+1=10/3\begin{aligned} y&=5-2z \\ &=7/3 \\ x&=y+1 \\ &=10/3 \end{aligned}yx​=5−2z=7/3=y+1=10/3​

    Check: 7/3+8/3=57/3+8/3=57/3+8/3=5, 10/3−7/3=110/3-7/3=110/3−7/3=1, and

    20/3+4/3=8.20/3+4/3=8.20/3+4/3=8.

    Answer

    Augmented matrix [01251−1012018]\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 0 & 1 & 2 & 5 \\ 1 & -1 & 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 & 8 \end{array}\right]​012​1−10​201​518​​; (x,y,z)=(103,73,43)(x,y,z)=(\tfrac{10}{3},\tfrac73,\tfrac43)(x,y,z)=(310​,37​,34​).

    A row swap can place a useful pivot before the forward elimination sweep.

  5. Problem 5 A third measurement

    The augmented matrix below uses columns x,yx,yx,y. Reduce it and describe its solution set.

    [101012115]\left[\begin{array}{cc|c} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 & 5 \end{array}\right]​101​011​125​​

    1. Hint 1

      There are more rows than variables, so one equation may conflict rather than repeat.

    2. Hint 2

      Combine the first two rows and compare with the third.

    Answer

    No solution; the system is inconsistent.

    Full solution

    Replace R3R_3R3​ by R3−R1−R2R_3-R_1-R_2R3​−R1​−R2​.

    [101012002]\left[\begin{array}{cc|c} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{array}\right]​100​010​122​​

    The bottom row reads 0=20=20=2, which is false for every x,yx,yx,y.

    The first two equations alone give x=1x=1x=1 and y=2y=2y=2, but the third equation x+y=5x+y=5x+y=5 requires 1+2=51+2=51+2=5, which fails, so the system has no solution.

    Answer

    No solution; the system is inconsistent.

    Key idea

    A zero row on the left with a nonzero constant is a contradiction: the system has no solution regardless of the other rows.

  6. Problem 6 A short record

    A system in x,y,zx,y,zx,y,z has the augmented matrix below. Find all its solutions and name the free variable.

    [12140026]\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & 1 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & 6 \end{array}\right][10​20​12​46​]

    1. Hint 1

      The second row fixes a variable even though its pivot skips a column.

    2. Hint 2

      Let the variable without a pivot be a real parameter.

    Answer

    yyy is free; (x,y,z)=(1−2t,t,3)(x,y,z)=(1-2t,t,3)(x,y,z)=(1−2t,t,3) for real ttt.

    Full solution

    The bottom row gives 2z=62z=62z=6, hence z=3z=3z=3.

    There is no pivot in the yyy column, so let y=ty=ty=t.

    The first row then gives

    x=1−2tx=1-2tx=1−2t

    Substitution gives (1−2t)+2t+3=4(1-2t)+2t+3=4(1−2t)+2t+3=4 and 2(3)=62(3)=62(3)=6 for all ttt.

    Answer

    yyy is free; (x,y,z)=(1−2t,t,3)(x,y,z)=(1-2t,t,3)(x,y,z)=(1−2t,t,3) for real ttt.

    Key idea

    In a consistent system's row-echelon form, a variable column without a pivot identifies a free variable.

  7. Problem 7 Completing the constants

    For real ccc, the augmented matrix below uses columns x,y,zx,y,zx,y,z in that order. Determine all ccc giving a consistent system and describe the solution set for those ccc.

    [1−10200132−21c]\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 3 \\ 2 & -2 & 1 & c \end{array}\right]​102​−10−2​011​23c​​

    1. Hint 1

      Compare the third row with a combination of the first two.

    2. Hint 2

      Keep the constant column in the same subtraction as the coefficients.

    Answer

    c=7c=7c=7; (x,y,z)=(2+t,t,3)(x,y,z)=(2+t,t,3)(x,y,z)=(2+t,t,3) for real ttt.

    Full solution

    Replace R3R_3R3​ by R3−2R1−R2R_3-2R_1-R_2R3​−2R1​−R2​, understood as two successive legal operations.

    [1−1020013000c−7]\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & -1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & c-7 \end{array}\right]​100​−100​010​23c−7​​

    The last row is false unless c=7c=7c=7.

    At c=7c=7c=7, it vanishes; z=3z=3z=3 and x−y=2x-y=2x−y=2 remain.

    Letting y=ty=ty=t gives the stated family, whose third left side is

    2(2+t)−2t+3=7.2(2+t)-2t+3=7.2(2+t)−2t+3=7.

    Answer

    c=7c=7c=7; (x,y,z)=(2+t,t,3)(x,y,z)=(2+t,t,3)(x,y,z)=(2+t,t,3) for real ttt.

    Key idea

    A parameter in the constant column can decide whether the pivot-free variable is free or the system is inconsistent.

  8. Problem 8 A proposed update

    For the equations x=2x=2x=2 and y=5y=5y=5, a student simultaneously replaces both rows by their sum and keeps two copies of x+y=7x+y=7x+y=7. They say the solution set is unchanged. Is that correct? Justify with a pair of values.

    1. Hint 1

      A permitted addition keeps the row being added available elsewhere.

    2. Hint 2

      Test a point on the new line that fails one original equation.

    Answer

    No; for example, (0,7)(0,7)(0,7) solves the new system but not the original.

    Full solution

    The originals force the unique pair (2,5)(2,5)(2,5).

    The simultaneous replacements both say

    x+y=7x+y=7x+y=7

    The pair (0,7)(0,7)(0,7) satisfies this twice but fails x=2x=2x=2.

    Replacing both rows in this way loses the information needed to recover the original rows.

    Answer

    No; for example, (0,7)(0,7)(0,7) solves the new system but not the original.

    Key idea

    Adding one row to another preserves information when the source row remains available.

  9. Problem 9 Checking an update

    A student replaces R3R_3R3​ by R3+4R1R_3+4R_1R3​+4R1​ while leaving R1R_1R1​ and R2R_2R2​ fixed. They claim the original R3R_3R3​ can be recovered by subtracting 4R14R_14R1​ from the new R3R_3R3​. Is this correct? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Check whether the row needed to undo the change is still present.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the change followed by the proposed reverse operation.

    Answer

    Yes.

    Full solution

    The first row is unchanged and still available.

    The proposed reverse gives

    (R3+4R1)−4R1=R3(R_3+4R_1)-4R_1=R_3(R3​+4R1​)−4R1​=R3​

    Every entry, including the constant, returns to its original value.

    Thus the original and changed systems have exactly the same solutions.

    Answer

    Yes.

    Key idea

    A reversible row operation preserves the entire system’s solution set.

  10. Problem 10 Counting available information

    A consistent system has five equations in three variables. Its row-echelon form has two zero rows and a pivot in each variable column. Another consistent system has two equations in three variables and no zero rows. Explain which system has a unique solution.

    1. Hint 1

      The decisive count concerns variable columns, not the number of zero rows.

    2. Hint 2

      Two nonzero rows can supply at most two pivots.

    Answer

    The five-equation system has a unique solution; the two-equation system has infinitely many solutions.

    Full solution

    The first system has three variable pivots, so back-substitution fixes all three variables despite the redundant rows.

    The second has at most two pivots for three variables, so at least one variable is free.

    It is consistent, hence that free variable supplies infinitely many solutions.

    Answer

    The five-equation system has a unique solution; the two-equation system has infinitely many solutions.

    Key idea

    For a consistent system, every variable needs a pivot for the solution to be unique.
