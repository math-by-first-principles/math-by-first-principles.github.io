  1. 1

    Which equation has x=3x = 3x=3 as an extraneous root?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    An extraneous root is a candidate that the clearing step produces and the domain then rejects, so you need an equation whose cleared form gives x=3x = 3x=3 while 333 is itself excluded.

    xx−3=3x−3  ⇒  x=3, and x=3 is excluded\frac{x}{x-3} = \frac{3}{x-3} \;\Rightarrow\; x = 3, \text{ and } x = 3 \text{ is excluded}x−3x​=x−33​⇒x=3, and x=3 is excluded

    Now rule the others out. The equation xx−3=2x−3\frac{x}{x-3} = \frac{2}{x-3}x−3x​=x−32​ clears to x=2x = 2x=2, which is in its domain and therefore genuine. The equation xx+3=3x+3\frac{x}{x+3} = \frac{3}{x+3}x+3x​=x+33​ does clear to x=3x = 3x=3, but its excluded value is −3-3−3, so there x=3x = 3x=3 is a genuine solution. And 3x=1x−3\frac{3}{x} = \frac{1}{x-3}x3​=x−31​ clears to 3(x−3)=x3(x-3) = x3(x−3)=x, giving x=92x = \frac{9}{2}x=29​, so it never produces 333 as a candidate at all.

  2. 2

    Solve x+2x=xx−2\dfrac{x+2}{x} = \dfrac{x}{x-2}xx+2​=x−2x​.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The excluded values are 000 and 222. Multiplying both sides by the LCD x(x−2)x(x-2)x(x−2) gives

    (x+2)(x−2)=x2  ⇒  x2−4=x2  ⇒  −4=0(x+2)(x-2) = x^2 \;\Rightarrow\; x^2 - 4 = x^2 \;\Rightarrow\; -4 = 0(x+2)(x−2)=x2⇒x2−4=x2⇒−4=0

    The variable cancels completely and leaves a false statement, so the cleared equation has no roots. There are no candidates to check, so the equation has no solution.

    Solve 5x2+4x+3+2x+1=3x+3\dfrac{5}{x^2+4x+3} + \dfrac{2}{x+1} = \dfrac{3}{x+3}x2+4x+35​+x+12​=x+33​.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Factor the quadratic denominator: x2+4x+3=(x+1)(x+3)x^2 + 4x + 3 = (x+1)(x+3)x2+4x+3=(x+1)(x+3). The excluded values are −1-1−1 and −3-3−3, and the LCD is (x+1)(x+3)(x+1)(x+3)(x+1)(x+3).

    Multiplying every term by the LCD gives 5+2(x+3)=3(x+1)5 + 2(x+3) = 3(x+1)5+2(x+3)=3(x+1).

    2x+11=3x+3  ⇒  x=82x + 11 = 3x + 3 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 82x+11=3x+3⇒x=8

    The candidate 888 is in the domain, so it is genuine. Checking: 599+29=599+2299=2799=311\frac{5}{99} + \frac{2}{9} = \frac{5}{99} + \frac{22}{99} = \frac{27}{99} = \frac{3}{11}995​+92​=995​+9922​=9927​=113​, which matches the right side.

  4. 4

    Two crews clear a field together in 444 hours. Alone, the faster crew would take 666 hours less than the slower crew. How long does the slower crew take alone?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Let ttt be the hours the slower crew needs alone, so the faster crew needs t−6t - 6t−6, and their rates add:

    1t+1t−6=14\frac{1}{t} + \frac{1}{t-6} = \frac{1}{4}t1​+t−61​=41​

    The excluded values are 000 and 666. Multiplying every term by the LCD 4t(t−6)4t(t-6)4t(t−6) gives 4(t−6)+4t=t(t−6)4(t-6) + 4t = t(t-6)4(t−6)+4t=t(t−6), so t2−14t+24=0t^2 - 14t + 24 = 0t2−14t+24=0 and (t−12)(t−2)=0(t-12)(t-2) = 0(t−12)(t−2)=0.

    Both candidates are in the domain and satisfy the equation, but t=2t = 2t=2 would make the faster crew take 2−6=−42 - 6 = -42−6=−4 hours, which is impossible, so the story rejects it. The slower crew takes 121212 hours, the faster takes 666, and 112+16=14\frac{1}{12} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{4}121​+61​=41​ confirms it.

  5. 5

    Solve xx−4−4x=16x2−4x\dfrac{x}{x-4} - \dfrac{4}{x} = \dfrac{16}{x^2-4x}x−4x​−x4​=x2−4x16​.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Factor first: x2−4x=x(x−4)x^2 - 4x = x(x-4)x2−4x=x(x−4), so the excluded values are 000 and 444, and the LCD is x(x−4)x(x-4)x(x−4).

    Multiplying every term by the LCD gives x2−4(x−4)=16x^2 - 4(x-4) = 16x2−4(x−4)=16, and the constants cancel:

    x2−4x+16=16  ⇒  x2−4x=0  ⇒  x(x−4)=0x^2 - 4x + 16 = 16 \;\Rightarrow\; x^2 - 4x = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; x(x-4) = 0x2−4x+16=16⇒x2−4x=0⇒x(x−4)=0

    The candidates are 000 and 444, and both are excluded. Every candidate is extraneous, so the equation has no solution.

  6. 6

    Solve 3x+2+1x−2=8x2−4\dfrac{3}{x+2} + \dfrac{1}{x-2} = \dfrac{8}{x^2-4}x+23​+x−21​=x2−48​.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The excluded values are −2-2−2 and 222, and the LCD is (x+2)(x−2)(x+2)(x-2)(x+2)(x−2).

    Multiplying every term by the LCD gives 3(x−2)+(x+2)=83(x-2) + (x+2) = 83(x−2)+(x+2)=8.

    4x−4=8  ⇒  x=34x - 4 = 8 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 34x−4=8⇒x=3

    The candidate 333 is in the domain, so it is genuine. Checking: 35+11=85\frac{3}{5} + \frac{1}{1} = \frac{8}{5}53​+11​=58​, which matches 89−4\frac{8}{9-4}9−48​.

  7. 7

    Tap A takes 555 hours longer to fill a tank than tap B does. Together they fill it in 666 hours. How long does tap B take alone?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Let ttt be the hours tap B needs alone, so tap A needs t+5t + 5t+5, and the rates add:

    1t+1t+5=16\frac{1}{t} + \frac{1}{t+5} = \frac{1}{6}t1​+t+51​=61​

    The excluded values are 000 and −5-5−5. Multiplying every term by the LCD 6t(t+5)6t(t+5)6t(t+5) gives 6(t+5)+6t=t(t+5)6(t+5) + 6t = t(t+5)6(t+5)+6t=t(t+5), so t2−7t−30=0t^2 - 7t - 30 = 0t2−7t−30=0 and (t−10)(t+3)=0(t-10)(t+3) = 0(t−10)(t+3)=0.

    Both candidates are in the domain and satisfy the equation, but a tap cannot take −3-3−3 hours, so the story rejects that one. Tap B takes 101010 hours and tap A takes 151515, and 110+115=16\frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{15} = \frac{1}{6}101​+151​=61​ confirms it.

  8. 8

    The reciprocals of two consecutive positive integers add to 712\dfrac{7}{12}127​. What is the smaller integer?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Call the smaller integer nnn, so the larger is n+1n + 1n+1, and the excluded values are 000 and −1-1−1:

    1n+1n+1=712\frac{1}{n} + \frac{1}{n+1} = \frac{7}{12}n1​+n+11​=127​

    Multiplying every term by the LCD 12n(n+1)12n(n+1)12n(n+1) gives 12(n+1)+12n=7n(n+1)12(n+1) + 12n = 7n(n+1)12(n+1)+12n=7n(n+1), so 7n2−17n−12=07n^2 - 17n - 12 = 07n2−17n−12=0 and (7n+4)(n−3)=0(7n+4)(n-3) = 0(7n+4)(n−3)=0.

    The candidates are n=3n = 3n=3 and n=−47n = -\frac{4}{7}n=−74​. Both lie in the domain, but only 333 is a positive integer, so the story rejects the other. Checking: 13+14=712\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{7}{12}31​+41​=127​.

    A car covers 150150150 km at a constant speed. Had it gone 101010 km/h faster, the trip would have taken 303030 minutes less. What was its speed?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Let vvv be the speed in km/h. The actual time is 150v\frac{150}{v}v150​ and the faster time is 150v+10\frac{150}{v+10}v+10150​, and the first exceeds the second by half an hour:

    150v−150v+10=12\frac{150}{v} - \frac{150}{v+10} = \frac{1}{2}v150​−v+10150​=21​

    The excluded values are 000 and −10-10−10. Multiplying every term by the LCD 2v(v+10)2v(v+10)2v(v+10) gives 300(v+10)−300v=v(v+10)300(v+10) - 300v = v(v+10)300(v+10)−300v=v(v+10), so v2+10v−3000=0v^2 + 10v - 3000 = 0v2+10v−3000=0 and (v−50)(v+60)=0(v-50)(v+60) = 0(v−50)(v+60)=0.

    Both candidates satisfy the equation, but a speed of −60-60−60 km/h cannot describe this car, so the story rejects it. The speed was 505050 km/h: the trip took 333 hours, and at 606060 km/h it would have taken 2.52.52.5 hours.

  10. 10

    Solve 2x−2+xx+2=x2+4x2−4\dfrac{2}{x-2} + \dfrac{x}{x+2} = \dfrac{x^2+4}{x^2-4}x−22​+x+2x​=x2−4x2+4​.

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The excluded values are 222 and −2-2−2, and the LCD is (x−2)(x+2)(x-2)(x+2)(x−2)(x+2).

    Multiplying every term by the LCD gives 2(x+2)+x(x−2)=x2+42(x+2) + x(x-2) = x^2 + 42(x+2)+x(x−2)=x2+4, and expanding the left side,

    x2+4=x2+4x^2 + 4 = x^2 + 4x2+4=x2+4

    The variable cancels into a statement true for every value, so every value in the domain solves the original equation. The solution set is every real number except the two excluded values, which stay excluded because the original equation says nothing at all there.

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    The excluded value is x=4x = 4x=4. Multiplying every term by x−4x - 4x−4, including the 111, gives a+(x−4)=xa + (x-4) = xa+(x−4)=x, and the variable cancels:

    a−4=0a - 4 = 0a−4=0

    If a≠4a \neq 4a=4 this is a false statement, so the cleared equation has no roots and the original has no solution. If a=4a = 4a=4 it is true for every xxx, so every value in the domain solves the original. Only a=4a = 4a=4 gives infinitely many solutions.
