    Simplify x2−16x2−x−6⋅x+2x−4\frac{x^2-16}{x^2-x-6} \cdot \frac{x+2}{x-4}x2−x−6x2−16​⋅x−4x+2​ and state every restriction.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Factor, and read the restrictions off the original denominators while they are factored.

    x2−16=(x−4)(x+4),x2−x−6=(x−3)(x+2)x^2-16 = (x-4)(x+4), \qquad x^2-x-6 = (x-3)(x+2)x2−16=(x−4)(x+4),x2−x−6=(x−3)(x+2)

    The denominators are (x−3)(x+2)(x-3)(x+2)(x−3)(x+2) and x−4x-4x−4, so x≠3x \ne 3x=3, x≠−2x \ne -2x=−2, and x≠4x \ne 4x=4. Now multiply and cancel.

    (x−4)(x+4)(x−3)(x+2)⋅x+2x−4=x+4x−3\frac{(x-4)(x+4)}{(x-3)(x+2)} \cdot \frac{x+2}{x-4} = \frac{x+4}{x-3}(x−3)(x+2)(x−4)(x+4)​⋅x−4x+2​=x−3x+4​

    The simplified form shows only x≠3x \ne 3x=3. The other two restrictions rode on the factors x+2x+2x+2 and x−4x-4x−4, which cancelled, so they must be carried by hand.

  2. 2

    Simplify x2−25x+1÷x−5x+1\frac{x^2-25}{x+1} \div \frac{x-5}{x+1}x+1x2−25​÷x+1x−5​ and state every restriction.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Collect the restrictions from the original quotient first. Both denominators are x+1x+1x+1, so x≠−1x \ne -1x=−1. The divisor x−5x+1\frac{x-5}{x+1}x+1x−5​ is zero exactly when x−5=0x - 5 = 0x−5=0, and you cannot divide by zero, so x≠5x \ne 5x=5.

    Now flip and multiply, using x2−25=(x−5)(x+5)x^2-25 = (x-5)(x+5)x2−25=(x−5)(x+5).

    (x−5)(x+5)x+1⋅x+1x−5=x+5\frac{(x-5)(x+5)}{x+1} \cdot \frac{x+1}{x-5} = x+5x+1(x−5)(x+5)​⋅x−5x+1​=x+5

    The answer is the polynomial x+5x+5x+5, which is defined everywhere and therefore advertises none of its restrictions. Both x≠−1x \ne -1x=−1 and x≠5x \ne 5x=5 are still true of the original expression.

    Simplify x2−x−12x+1÷(x−4)\frac{x^2-x-12}{x+1} \div (x-4)x+1x2−x−12​÷(x−4).

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Factor the numerator, and write the polynomial divisor over 111.

    x2−x−12=(x−4)(x+3)x^2-x-12 = (x-4)(x+3)x2−x−12=(x−4)(x+3)

    Flip the divisor and multiply.

    (x−4)(x+3)x+1⋅1x−4=(x−4)(x+3)(x+1)(x−4)=x+3x+1\frac{(x-4)(x+3)}{x+1} \cdot \frac{1}{x-4} = \frac{(x-4)(x+3)}{(x+1)(x-4)} = \frac{x+3}{x+1}x+1(x−4)(x+3)​⋅x−41​=(x+1)(x−4)(x−4)(x+3)​=x+1x+3​

  4. 4

    Simplify x2+6x+9x2−9⋅x−3x\frac{x^2+6x+9}{x^2-9} \cdot \frac{x-3}{x}x2−9x2+6x+9​⋅xx−3​.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The first numerator is a perfect square and the first denominator is a difference of squares.

    x2+6x+9=(x+3)2,x2−9=(x−3)(x+3)x^2+6x+9 = (x+3)^2, \qquad x^2-9 = (x-3)(x+3)x2+6x+9=(x+3)2,x2−9=(x−3)(x+3)

    Multiply across, then cancel one x+3x+3x+3 and the x−3x-3x−3.

    (x+3)2(x−3)(x+3)⋅x−3x=(x+3)2(x−3)x(x−3)(x+3)=x+3x\frac{(x+3)^2}{(x-3)(x+3)} \cdot \frac{x-3}{x} = \frac{(x+3)^2(x-3)}{x(x-3)(x+3)} = \frac{x+3}{x}(x−3)(x+3)(x+3)2​⋅xx−3​=x(x−3)(x+3)(x+3)2(x−3)​=xx+3​

    The restrictions are x≠3x \ne 3x=3, x≠−3x \ne -3x=−3, and x≠0x \ne 0x=0.

  5. 5

    Simplify x−5x+2⋅x2+2x5−x\frac{x-5}{x+2} \cdot \frac{x^2+2x}{5-x}x+2x−5​⋅5−xx2+2x​.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Factor, and notice that 5−x5-x5−x is the opposite of x−5x-5x−5.

    x2+2x=x(x+2),5−x=−(x−5)x^2+2x = x(x+2), \qquad 5-x = -(x-5)x2+2x=x(x+2),5−x=−(x−5)

    Now multiply across. The factors x−5x-5x−5 and x+2x+2x+2 both cancel, and the minus sign survives.

    x−5x+2⋅x(x+2)−(x−5)=x(x−5)(x+2)−(x+2)(x−5)=−x\frac{x-5}{x+2} \cdot \frac{x(x+2)}{-(x-5)} = \frac{x(x-5)(x+2)}{-(x+2)(x-5)} = -xx+2x−5​⋅−(x−5)x(x+2)​=−(x+2)(x−5)x(x−5)(x+2)​=−x

    The restrictions are x≠−2x \ne -2x=−2 and x≠5x \ne 5x=5.

  6. 6

    Which values of xxx must be excluded from x+7x−1÷x+3x−6\frac{x+7}{x-1} \div \frac{x+3}{x-6}x−1x+7​÷x−6x+3​?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A quotient carries three conditions. The dividend's denominator gives x−1≠0x - 1 \ne 0x−1=0, so x≠1x \ne 1x=1. The divisor's denominator gives x−6≠0x - 6 \ne 0x−6=0, so x≠6x \ne 6x=6. The divisor's numerator may not be zero either, since dividing by zero is undefined.

    x+3=0  ⇒  x=−3is excludedx + 3 = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; x = -3 \quad \text{is excluded}x+3=0⇒x=−3is excluded

    The dividend's numerator x+7x+7x+7 is the one polynomial that is free to vanish, so x=−7x = -7x=−7 is allowed.

    Simplify 2x2+5x−3x2−9⋅x+32x−1\frac{2x^2+5x-3}{x^2-9} \cdot \frac{x+3}{2x-1}x2−92x2+5x−3​⋅2x−1x+3​.

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Factor both quadratics.

    2x2+5x−3=(2x−1)(x+3),x2−9=(x−3)(x+3)2x^2+5x-3 = (2x-1)(x+3), \qquad x^2-9 = (x-3)(x+3)2x2+5x−3=(2x−1)(x+3),x2−9=(x−3)(x+3)

    Multiply across and cancel 2x−12x-12x−1 along with one copy of x+3x+3x+3.

    (2x−1)(x+3)(x−3)(x+3)⋅x+32x−1=(2x−1)(x+3)(x+3)(x−3)(x+3)(2x−1)=x+3x−3\frac{(2x-1)(x+3)}{(x-3)(x+3)} \cdot \frac{x+3}{2x-1} = \frac{(2x-1)(x+3)(x+3)}{(x-3)(x+3)(2x-1)} = \frac{x+3}{x-3}(x−3)(x+3)(2x−1)(x+3)​⋅2x−1x+3​=(x−3)(x+3)(2x−1)(2x−1)(x+3)(x+3)​=x−3x+3​

    The restrictions are x≠3x \ne 3x=3, x≠−3x \ne -3x=−3, and x≠12x \ne \tfrac{1}{2}x=21​.

    Simplify xx−1⋅x−1x+2⋅x+2x+5\frac{x}{x-1} \cdot \frac{x-1}{x+2} \cdot \frac{x+2}{x+5}x−1x​⋅x+2x−1​⋅x+5x+2​.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The rule extends to any number of factors: multiply all the numerators and all the denominators.

    x(x−1)(x+2)(x−1)(x+2)(x+5)\frac{x(x-1)(x+2)}{(x-1)(x+2)(x+5)}(x−1)(x+2)(x+5)x(x−1)(x+2)​

    The factors x−1x-1x−1 and x+2x+2x+2 each appear on both the top and the bottom, so both cancel.

    x(x−1)(x+2)(x−1)(x+2)(x+5)=xx+5\frac{x(x-1)(x+2)}{(x-1)(x+2)(x+5)} = \frac{x}{x+5}(x−1)(x+2)(x+5)x(x−1)(x+2)​=x+5x​

    The restrictions are x≠1x \ne 1x=1, x≠−2x \ne -2x=−2, and x≠−5x \ne -5x=−5.

    The expression x2−1x−3⋅x−3x+1\frac{x^2-1}{x-3} \cdot \frac{x-3}{x+1}x−3x2−1​⋅x+1x−3​ simplifies to x−1x-1x−1. What is its value at x=3x = 3x=3?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The simplified form x−1x-1x−1 would give 3−1=23 - 1 = 23−1=2, but the question asks about the original expression, and at x=3x = 3x=3 its first denominator is zero.

    32−13−3=80\frac{3^2-1}{3-3} = \frac{8}{0}3−332−1​=08​

    Division by zero is undefined, so the original expression has no value at x=3x = 3x=3. Cancelling the factor x−3x-3x−3 removed the evidence, not the restriction: the answer is x−1x-1x−1 with x≠3x \ne 3x=3 and x≠−1x \ne -1x=−1.

    The product x+2x−7⋅x−7x+1\frac{x+2}{x-7} \cdot \frac{x-7}{x+1}x−7x+2​⋅x+1x−7​ simplifies to x+2x+1\frac{x+2}{x+1}x+1x+2​. Which restriction does the simplified form fail to show?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The original has denominators x−7x-7x−7 and x+1x+1x+1, so the restrictions are x≠7x \ne 7x=7 and x≠−1x \ne -1x=−1.

    x+2x−7⋅x−7x+1=x+2x+1\frac{x+2}{x-7} \cdot \frac{x-7}{x+1} = \frac{x+2}{x+1}x−7x+2​⋅x+1x−7​=x+1x+2​

    The simplified form has only the denominator x+1x+1x+1, so it still shows x≠−1x \ne -1x=−1 but has lost every trace of x≠7x \ne 7x=7. That restriction rode on the factor x−7x-7x−7, which cancelled. (x=−2x = -2x=−2 merely makes a numerator zero, which is allowed.)
