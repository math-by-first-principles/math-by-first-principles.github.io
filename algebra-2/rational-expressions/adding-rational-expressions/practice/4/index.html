    Simplify the complex fraction 1x+1+11x+1−1\dfrac{\frac{1}{x+1} + 1}{\frac{1}{x+1} - 1}x+11​−1x+11​+1​.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Combine the top and the bottom, each over the denominator x+1x+1x+1.

    1x+1+1=1+(x+1)x+1=x+2x+1,1x+1−1=1−(x+1)x+1=−xx+1\frac{1}{x+1} + 1 = \frac{1 + (x+1)}{x+1} = \frac{x+2}{x+1}, \qquad \frac{1}{x+1} - 1 = \frac{1 - (x+1)}{x+1} = \frac{-x}{x+1}x+11​+1=x+11+(x+1)​=x+1x+2​,x+11​−1=x+11−(x+1)​=x+1−x​

    Divide by multiplying by the reciprocal; the x+1x+1x+1 factors cancel.

    x+2x+1⋅x+1−x=−x+2x\frac{x+2}{x+1} \cdot \frac{x+1}{-x} = -\frac{x+2}{x}x+1x+2​⋅−xx+1​=−xx+2​

    The minus comes from 1−(x+1)=−x1 - (x+1) = -x1−(x+1)=−x, so the sign is decided in the bottom. The restrictions are x≠−1x \neq -1x=−1 and x≠0x \neq 0x=0.

  2. 2

    Write x−1x+2+x+2x−1\dfrac{x-1}{x+2} + \dfrac{x+2}{x-1}x+2x−1​+x−1x+2​ as a single fraction.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The LCD is (x+2)(x−1)(x+2)(x-1)(x+2)(x−1), so each numerator is multiplied by the factor its fraction is missing.

    (x−1)2+(x+2)2(x+2)(x−1)\frac{(x-1)^2 + (x+2)^2}{(x+2)(x-1)}(x+2)(x−1)(x−1)2+(x+2)2​

    Expand both squares: (x2−2x+1)+(x2+4x+4)(x^2 - 2x + 1) + (x^2 + 4x + 4)(x2−2x+1)+(x2+4x+4).

    2x2+2x+5(x+2)(x−1)\frac{2x^2 + 2x + 5}{(x+2)(x-1)}(x+2)(x−1)2x2+2x+5​

    The numerator has no real roots, so it shares no factor with the denominator and the fraction is in lowest terms.

  3. 3

    What is the least common denominator of 1x2−9\dfrac{1}{x^2-9}x2−91​, 1x2−6x+9\dfrac{1}{x^2-6x+9}x2−6x+91​, and 1x+3\dfrac{1}{x+3}x+31​?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Factor all three denominators.

    x2−9=(x−3)(x+3),x2−6x+9=(x−3)2,x+3x^2 - 9 = (x-3)(x+3), \qquad x^2 - 6x + 9 = (x-3)^2, \qquad x + 3x2−9=(x−3)(x+3),x2−6x+9=(x−3)2,x+3

    The distinct factors are x−3x-3x−3 and x+3x+3x+3. The highest power of x−3x-3x−3 in a single denominator is 222, and the highest power of x+3x+3x+3 is 111.

    LCD=(x−3)2(x+3)\text{LCD} = (x-3)^2(x+3)LCD=(x−3)2(x+3)

    The choice (x−3)(x+3)(x-3)(x+3)(x−3)(x+3) is not a common denominator at all, because (x−3)2(x-3)^2(x−3)2 does not divide it. Multiplying all three denominators together would give (x−3)3(x+3)2(x-3)^3(x+3)^2(x−3)3(x+3)2, a common denominator that carries two surplus factors.

    Which values of xxx must be excluded from the domain of 1x2−2x−8+1x2−x−12\dfrac{1}{x^2-2x-8} + \dfrac{1}{x^2-x-12}x2−2x−81​+x2−x−121​?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Factor both denominators.

    x2−2x−8=(x−4)(x+2),x2−x−12=(x−4)(x+3)x^2 - 2x - 8 = (x-4)(x+2), \qquad x^2 - x - 12 = (x-4)(x+3)x2−2x−8=(x−4)(x+2),x2−x−12=(x−4)(x+3)

    Set every factor to zero. The shared factor x−4x-4x−4 contributes x=4x=4x=4 once, and the other two contribute x=−2x=-2x=−2 and x=−3x=-3x=−3.

    x=4,x=−2,x=−3x = 4, \quad x = -2, \quad x = -3x=4,x=−2,x=−3

  5. 5

    What is the least common denominator of 3x3−x\dfrac{3}{x^3-x}x3−x3​ and 5x2+x\dfrac{5}{x^2+x}x2+x5​?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Factor both denominators completely, starting by pulling out the common xxx.

    x3−x=x(x2−1)=x(x−1)(x+1),x2+x=x(x+1)x^3 - x = x(x^2-1) = x(x-1)(x+1), \qquad x^2 + x = x(x+1)x3−x=x(x2−1)=x(x−1)(x+1),x2+x=x(x+1)

    Every factor of the second denominator already appears in the first, so the first denominator is itself the LCD.

    LCD=x(x−1)(x+1)\text{LCD} = x(x-1)(x+1)LCD=x(x−1)(x+1)

    The product of the two denominators, x2(x−1)(x+1)2x^2(x-1)(x+1)^2x2(x−1)(x+1)2, carries a surplus xxx and a surplus x+1x+1x+1.

  6. 6

    Simplify 1x−1−1x+1x2−x\dfrac{1}{x-1} - \dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{1}{x^2-x}x−11​−x1​+x2−x1​.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Factor x2−x=x(x−1)x^2 - x = x(x-1)x2−x=x(x−1), which is the LCD of all three. Build the first two up and bracket the numerator being subtracted.

    x−(x−1)+1x(x−1)\frac{x - (x-1) + 1}{x(x-1)}x(x−1)x−(x−1)+1​

    Distribute the minus sign across both terms of x−1x-1x−1.

    x−x+1+1x(x−1)=2x(x−1)\frac{x - x + 1 + 1}{x(x-1)} = \frac{2}{x(x-1)}x(x−1)x−x+1+1​=x(x−1)2​

    Failing to distribute gives x−x−1+1=0x - x - 1 + 1 = 0x−x−1+1=0, a wrong answer that looks suspiciously tidy.

  7. 7

    Constants AAA and BBB satisfy Ax−2+Bx+3=x+13x2+x−6\dfrac{A}{x-2} + \dfrac{B}{x+3} = \dfrac{x+13}{x^2+x-6}x−2A​+x+3B​=x2+x−6x+13​ for every xxx in the domain. What are AAA and BBB?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Factor the right-hand denominator: x2+x−6=(x−2)(x+3)x^2 + x - 6 = (x-2)(x+3)x2+x−6=(x−2)(x+3), which is exactly the LCD on the left. Combining the left side gives

    A(x+3)+B(x−2)(x−2)(x+3)=x+13(x−2)(x+3),\frac{A(x+3) + B(x-2)}{(x-2)(x+3)} = \frac{x+13}{(x-2)(x+3)},(x−2)(x+3)A(x+3)+B(x−2)​=(x−2)(x+3)x+13​,

    so the numerators agree at every allowed xxx. That makes them equal as polynomials: their difference has degree at most 111 and vanishes at infinitely many values, and only the zero polynomial can do that. A polynomial identity holds at every xxx with no exceptions, including the two values the original fractions bar, so you may substitute those values freely even though the fractions themselves are undefined there.

    Setting x=2x=2x=2 kills the BBB term and gives 5A=155A = 155A=15; setting x=−3x=-3x=−3 kills the AAA term and gives −5B=10-5B = 10−5B=10.

    A=3,B=−2A = 3, \qquad B = -2A=3,B=−2

    Check: 3(x+3)−2(x−2)=3x+9−2x+4=x+133(x+3) - 2(x-2) = 3x + 9 - 2x + 4 = x + 133(x+3)−2(x−2)=3x+9−2x+4=x+13.

    Simplify the complex fraction 1x−1x+11x(x+1)\dfrac{\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{x+1}}{\frac{1}{x(x+1)}}x(x+1)1​x1​−x+11​​.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Combine the top over the LCD x(x+1)x(x+1)x(x+1), bracketing the fraction being subtracted.

    1x−1x+1=(x+1)−xx(x+1)=1x(x+1)\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{x+1} = \frac{(x+1) - x}{x(x+1)} = \frac{1}{x(x+1)}x1​−x+11​=x(x+1)(x+1)−x​=x(x+1)1​

    The top and the bottom are now the same fraction, so multiplying by the reciprocal of the bottom gives

    1x(x+1)⋅x(x+1)1=1.\frac{1}{x(x+1)} \cdot \frac{x(x+1)}{1} = 1.x(x+1)1​⋅1x(x+1)​=1.

    The restrictions are x≠0x \neq 0x=0 and x≠−1x \neq -1x=−1, which the answer 111 shows no trace of.

    Write xx+3+9x2−9\dfrac{x}{x+3} + \dfrac{9}{x^2-9}x+3x​+x2−99​ as a single fraction.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Factor x2−9=(x−3)(x+3)x^2 - 9 = (x-3)(x+3)x2−9=(x−3)(x+3), which is the LCD. The first fraction is missing the factor x−3x-3x−3, not x+3x+3x+3.

    x(x−3)(x−3)(x+3)+9(x−3)(x+3)=x2−3x+9x2−9\frac{x(x-3)}{(x-3)(x+3)} + \frac{9}{(x-3)(x+3)} = \frac{x^2 - 3x + 9}{x^2-9}(x−3)(x+3)x(x−3)​+(x−3)(x+3)9​=x2−9x2−3x+9​

    A student combines 4x+1−2x−3\dfrac{4}{x+1} - \dfrac{2}{x-3}x+14​−x−32​ over the LCD (x+1)(x−3)(x+1)(x-3)(x+1)(x−3) and writes the numerator as 4(x−3)−2(x+1)=4x−12−2x+2=2x−104(x-3) - 2(x+1) = 4x - 12 - 2x + 2 = 2x - 104(x−3)−2(x+1)=4x−12−2x+2=2x−10. What is the correct numerator?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The minus sign in front of 2(x+1)2(x+1)2(x+1) applies to the whole product, so both of its terms change sign.

    4(x−3)−2(x+1)=4x−12−2x−24(x-3) - 2(x+1) = 4x - 12 - 2x - 24(x−3)−2(x+1)=4x−12−2x−2

    Collect the like terms.

    2x−142x - 142x−14

    The student turned −2-2−2 into +2+2+2, which is the single most common error in this lesson. Bracketing the numerator being subtracted before expanding prevents it.

    One pipe fills a tank in xxx hours, so it fills 1x\frac{1}{x}x1​ of the tank each hour. A second pipe takes x+2x+2x+2 hours. Running together, they fill 1x+1x+2\dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{1}{x+2}x1​+x+21​ of the tank each hour. Write that as a single fraction.

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The denominators share no factor, so the LCD is x(x+2)x(x+2)x(x+2). Build each fraction up by the factor it is missing.

    x+2x(x+2)+xx(x+2)=(x+2)+xx(x+2)\frac{x+2}{x(x+2)} + \frac{x}{x(x+2)} = \frac{(x+2) + x}{x(x+2)}x(x+2)x+2​+x(x+2)x​=x(x+2)(x+2)+x​

    Collect the numerator.

    2x+2x(x+2)\frac{2x+2}{x(x+2)}x(x+2)2x+2​

    Adding the tops and the bottoms separately would give 22x+2\frac{2}{2x+2}2x+22​, which is not even close: at x=2x=2x=2 the true rate is 68\frac{6}{8}86​ of a tank per hour, while that wrong form gives 13\frac{1}{3}31​.
