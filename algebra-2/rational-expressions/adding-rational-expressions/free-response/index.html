  1. Problem 1 A shared denominator

    Write 2x2+x+8x2+x+5−3x2+x+5\frac{2x^2+x+8}{x^2+x+5}-\frac{3}{x^2+x+5}x2+x+52x2+x+8​−x2+x+53​ as one fraction in lowest terms, and state its real domain.

    1. Hint 1

      The two terms already use the same denominator.

    2. Hint 2

      Subtract the full second numerator, then check for a shared factor.

    Answer

    2x2+x+5x2+x+5\frac{2x^2+x+5}{x^2+x+5}x2+x+52x2+x+5​; all real xxx.

    Full solution

    The common denominator is x2+x+5=(x+12)2+194x^2+x+5=(x+\frac12)^2+\frac{19}4x2+x+5=(x+21​)2+419​, which is positive for every real xxx.

    Combining the numerators gives

    2x2+x+8−3x2+x+5=2x2+x+5x2+x+5\frac{2x^2+x+8-3}{x^2+x+5}=\frac{2x^2+x+5}{x^2+x+5}x2+x+52x2+x+8−3​=x2+x+52x2+x+5​

    Neither quadratic has a real zero, since their discriminants 1−401-401−40 and 1−201-201−20 are negative, so neither has a linear factor over the reals.

    The numerator is not a constant multiple of the denominator, since

    2(x2+x+5)=2x2+2x+10.2(x^2+x+5)=2x^2+2x+10.2(x2+x+5)=2x2+2x+10.

    So they share no factor, and the fraction is in lowest terms.

    Answer

    2x2+x+5x2+x+5\frac{2x^2+x+5}{x^2+x+5}x2+x+52x2+x+5​; all real xxx.

    Key idea

    Subtracting fractions with a shared denominator changes the numerator, not the denominator.

  2. Problem 2 Preparing a denominator

    For 112(x−1)2+118(x−1)3\frac{1}{12(x-1)^2}+\frac{1}{18(x-1)^3}12(x−1)21​+18(x−1)31​, write the least common denominator in factored form.

    1. Hint 1

      The numerical coefficient and the polynomial power must both accommodate the two denominators.

    2. Hint 2

      Take the least common multiple of 121212 and 181818, and the higher power of x−1x-1x−1.

    Answer

    36(x−1)336(x-1)^336(x−1)3.

    Full solution

    The numerical least common multiple is 363636.

    The highest power of x−1x-1x−1 present in a denominator is 333.

    Thus

    L=36(x−1)3L=36(x-1)^3L=36(x−1)3

    Dividing LLL by the two denominators gives 3(x−1)3(x-1)3(x−1) and 222, respectively, confirming that each divides it.

    Answer

    36(x−1)336(x-1)^336(x−1)3.

    Key idea

    A least common denominator combines the numerical least common multiple with each factor's highest required power.

  3. Problem 3 A nested fraction

    This entire denominator is zero at x=52x=\frac52x=25​, so that input is excluded too.

    Dividing by 2x−5x−4\frac{2x-5}{x-4}x−42x−5​ means multiplying by x−42x−5\frac{x-4}{2x-5}2x−5x−4​, which gives (x+8)(x−4)2x−5\frac{(x+8)(x-4)}{2x-5}2x−5(x+8)(x−4)​, with both restrictions retained.

    At x=1x=1x=1, the original is 92−1=9\frac9{2-1}=92−19​=9, and the new form gives

    9(−3)−3=9.\frac{9(-3)}{-3}=9.−39(−3)​=9.

    Answer

    (x+8)(x−4)2x−5\frac{(x+8)(x-4)}{2x-5}2x−5(x+8)(x−4)​, with x≠4,52x\ne4,\frac52x=4,25​.

  4. Problem 4 Cost per printed copy

    A print shop charges 90 dollars for a run of x+4x+4x+4 copies and 78 dollars for a run of 2x+12x+12x+1 copies, where xxx is a positive integer. Write the first cost per copy minus the second as a single fraction in lowest terms, and find that difference at x=6x=6x=6.

    1. Hint 1

      Divide each total charge by its own copy count before subtracting.

    2. Hint 2

      Use (x+4)(2x+1)(x+4)(2x+1)(x+4)(2x+1) as a common denominator.

    Answer

    6(17x−37)(x+4)(2x+1)\frac{6(17x-37)}{(x+4)(2x+1)}(x+4)(2x+1)6(17x−37)​ dollars per copy; 333 dollars per copy at x=6x=6x=6.

    Full solution

    The required difference is 90x+4−782x+1\frac{90}{x+4}-\frac{78}{2x+1}x+490​−2x+178​.

    Both denominators are positive for positive integer xxx.

    Over the common denominator, the numerator is

    90(2x+1)−78(x+4)=102x−222\begin{aligned}&90(2x+1)-78(x+4)\\&=102x-222\end{aligned}​90(2x+1)−78(x+4)=102x−222​

    Thus the difference is 6(17x−37)(x+4)(2x+1)\frac{6(17x-37)}{(x+4)(2x+1)}(x+4)(2x+1)6(17x−37)​, in lowest terms because 17x−3717x-3717x−37 is zero only at 3717\frac{37}{17}1737​, which is neither −4-4−4 nor −12-\frac12−21​.

    At x=6x=6x=6, the original costs are 999 and 666 dollars per copy, whose difference is 333 dollars per copy, and

    6⋅6510⋅13=3.\frac{6\cdot65}{10\cdot13}=3.10⋅136⋅65​=3.

    Answer

    6(17x−37)(x+4)(2x+1)\frac{6(17x-37)}{(x+4)(2x+1)}(x+4)(2x+1)6(17x−37)​ dollars per copy; 333 dollars per copy at x=6x=6x=6.

    Key idea

    A difference of average costs requires subtracting ratios over a shared denominator.

  5. Problem 5 Three contributions

    Write 1x2+1+2(x2+1)2−12\frac{1}{x^2+1}+\frac{2}{(x^2+1)^2}-\frac{1}{2}x2+11​+(x2+1)22​−21​ as a single fraction in lowest terms for real xxx.

    1. Hint 1

      A common denominator must accommodate every denominator in the expression.

    2. Hint 2

      Use a denominator containing two copies of the quadratic and the numerical factor 222.

    3. Hint 3

      Expand only the combined numerator, keeping brackets around the term being subtracted.

    Answer

    5−x42(x2+1)2\frac{5-x^4}{2(x^2+1)^2}2(x2+1)25−x4​; all real xxx.

    Full solution

    The least common denominator is 2(x2+1)22(x^2+1)^22(x2+1)2, positive for all real xxx.

    The rewritten numerators are 2(x2+1)2(x^2+1)2(x2+1), 444, and (x2+1)2(x^2+1)^2(x2+1)2.

    Their combination is

    2(x2+1)+4−(x2+1)2=5−x42(x^2+1)+4-(x^2+1)^2=5-x^42(x2+1)+4−(x2+1)2=5−x4

    The result is (5−x4)/(2(x2+1)2)(5-x^4)/(2(x^2+1)^2)(5−x4)/(2(x2+1)2).

    The numerator is not divisible by x2+1x^2+1x2+1: replacing x2x^2x2 by −1-1−1 in its remainder gives 444, not 000.

    No common factor remains.

    Answer

    5−x42(x2+1)2\frac{5-x^4}{2(x^2+1)^2}2(x2+1)25−x4​; all real xxx.

    Key idea

    Repeated denominator factors need their highest power once in the common denominator.

  6. Problem 6 A ratio of two combinations

    Simplify 2/(x−14)−1/(x+15)1+2/(x+15)\frac{2/(x-14)-1/(x+15)}{1+2/(x+15)}1+2/(x+15)2/(x−14)−1/(x+15)​ for real xxx, retaining every restriction.

    1. Hint 1

      Combine the numerator and denominator separately.

    2. Hint 2

      Determine whether the combined denominator can be zero before taking its reciprocal.

    Answer

    x+44(x−14)(x+17)\frac{x+44}{(x-14)(x+17)}(x−14)(x+17)x+44​, with x≠14,−15,−17x\ne14,-15,-17x=14,−15,−17.

    Full solution

    The inner denominators exclude 141414 and −15-15−15.

    Combining the top over (x−14)(x+15)(x-14)(x+15)(x−14)(x+15) gives

    N=2(x+15)−(x−14)(x−14)(x+15)=x+44(x−14)(x+15)\begin{aligned}N&=\frac{2(x+15)-(x-14)}{(x-14)(x+15)}\\&=\frac{x+44}{(x-14)(x+15)}\end{aligned}N​=(x−14)(x+15)2(x+15)−(x−14)​=(x−14)(x+15)x+44​​

    Combining the bottom gives

    D=x+15+2x+15=x+17x+15D=\frac{x+15+2}{x+15}=\frac{x+17}{x+15}D=x+15x+15+2​=x+15x+17​

    The denominator DDD is zero at x=−17x=-17x=−17, so that input is excluded as well.

    Dividing NNN by DDD gives

    x+44(x−14)(x+15)⋅x+15x+17=x+44(x−14)(x+17)\begin{aligned}&\frac{x+44}{(x-14)(x+15)}\cdot\frac{x+15}{x+17}\\&=\frac{x+44}{(x-14)(x+17)}\end{aligned}​(x−14)(x+15)x+44​⋅x+17x+15​=(x−14)(x+17)x+44​​

    with

    At x=0x=0x=0, the original top is −17−115=−22105-\frac17-\frac1{15}=-\frac{22}{105}−71​−151​=−10522​ and the bottom is 1715\frac{17}{15}1517​, giving −22119-\frac{22}{119}−11922​, as does 44(−14)(17)\frac{44}{(-14)(17)}(−14)(17)44​.

    Answer

    x+44(x−14)(x+17)\frac{x+44}{(x-14)(x+17)}(x−14)(x+17)x+44​, with x≠14,−15,−17x\ne14,-15,-17x=14,−15,−17.

  7. Problem 7 A polynomial difference

    Simplify x2+xx2−1−1x−1\frac{x^2+x}{x^2-1}-\frac1{x-1}x2−1x2+x​−x−11​ for real xxx. State which excluded input or inputs no longer appear in the reduced denominator.

    1. Hint 1

      The quadratic denominator contains the other denominator as a factor.

    2. Hint 2

      Combine over the factored quadratic and then factor the entire numerator.

    Answer

    111, with x≠−1,1x\ne-1,1x=−1,1; both exclusions disappear from the reduced denominator.

    Full solution

    The original denominators exclude −1-1−1 and 111.

    Over (x−1)(x+1)(x-1)(x+1)(x−1)(x+1), the numerator is

    x2+x−(x+1)=x2−1x^2+x-(x+1)=x^2-1x2+x−(x+1)=x2−1

    Cancel the nonzero factors on the domain to obtain 111.

    Since the reduced denominator is 111, neither exclusion remains visible, but both still apply to the original difference.

    Answer

    111, with x≠−1,1x\ne-1,1x=−1,1; both exclusions disappear from the reduced denominator.

    Key idea

    Subtracting rational expressions can cancel a denominator without restoring any excluded input.

  9. Problem 9 A sum that keeps its sign

  10. Problem 10 Reduce first, or not

    Let S=x−16(x−16)(x+14)+x+14(x+14)(x−15)S=\frac{x-16}{(x-16)(x+14)}+\frac{x+14}{(x+14)(x-15)}S=(x−16)(x+14)x−16​+(x+14)(x−15)x+14​. Explain why each is a valid choice at its stage, and write SSS as a single fraction in lowest terms with its domain.

    1. Hint 1

      Record the excluded inputs from the fractions as written before reducing anything.

    2. Hint 2

      Each fraction can be reduced on its own before any common denominator is chosen.

    Answer

    As written the LCD is (x−16)(x+14)(x−15)(x-16)(x+14)(x-15)(x−16)(x+14)(x−15); after reducing it is (x+14)(x−15)(x+14)(x-15)(x+14)(x−15). S=2x−1(x+14)(x−15)S=\frac{2x-1}{(x+14)(x-15)}S=(x+14)(x−15)2x−1​, with x≠16,−14,15x\ne16,-14,15x=16,−14,15.

    Full solution

    As written, the denominators are (x−16)(x+14)(x-16)(x+14)(x−16)(x+14) and (x+14)(x−15)(x+14)(x-15)(x+14)(x−15), so the LCD is (x−16)(x+14)(x−15)(x-16)(x+14)(x-15)(x−16)(x+14)(x−15) and the domain is

    x≠16,−14,15.x\ne16,-14,15.x=16,−14,15.

    On that domain each fraction reduces: x−16(x−16)(x+14)=1x+14\frac{x-16}{(x-16)(x+14)}=\frac1{x+14}(x−16)(x+14)x−16​=x+141​ and

    x+14(x+14)(x−15)=1x−15.\frac{x+14}{(x+14)(x-15)}=\frac1{x-15}.(x+14)(x−15)x+14​=x−151​.
