  1. Problem 1 Quadratic specifications

    Write the quadratic polynomial with leading coefficient 444 whose two roots, counted with multiplicity, have sum 333 and product −2-2−2. Give expanded form.

    1. Hint 1

      The sum and product determine the two remaining coefficients.

    2. Hint 2

      Expand 4(x−r1)(x−r2)4(x-r_1)(x-r_2)4(x−r1​)(x−r2​) before inserting the given values.

    Answer

    4x2−12x−84x^2-12x-84x2−12x−8.

    Full solution

    For two roots the expansion is

    p(x)=4x2−4(r1+r2)x+4r1r2\begin{aligned} p(x) &= 4x^2-4(r_1+r_2)x \\ &\quad +4r_1r_2 \end{aligned}p(x)​=4x2−4(r1​+r2​)x+4r1​r2​​

    The stated values give

    p(x)=4x2−12x−8p(x)=4x^2-12x-8p(x)=4x2−12x−8

    Its root sum is −(−12)/4=3-(-12)/4=3−(−12)/4=3 and product is −8/4=−2-8/4=-2−8/4=−2, checking both conditions.

    Answer

    4x2−12x−84x^2-12x-84x2−12x−8.

    Key idea

    The quadratic sum and product rules come from matching the expansion of two linear factors.

  2. Problem 2 A fifth-degree coefficient

    The five complex roots of 2x5+3x4−4x3+6x2−x+72x^5+3x^4-4x^3+6x^2-x+72x5+3x4−4x3+6x2−x+7 are listed with multiplicity. Find the sum of all products of three different entries of this list.

    1. Hint 1

      A product of three root entries belongs to the coefficient three powers below the leading term.

    2. Hint 2

    Answer

    −3-3−3.

    Full solution

    The requested quantity is e3e_3e3​.

    It is attached to the x2x^2x2 coefficient, since 5−3=25-3=25−3=2.

    The formula gives

    e3=−62e_3=-\frac{6}{2}e3​=−26​ e3=−3e_3=-3e3​=−3

    Answer

    Key idea

    The coefficient position and the number of chosen roots determine both the symmetric sum and its sign.

  3. Problem 3 A root of zero, expanded

    Answer

    3x3+9x2−30x3x^3+9x^2-30x3x3+9x2−30x (that is, a=3a=3a=3, b=9b=9b=9, c=−30c=-30c=−30, d=0d=0d=0).

  5. Problem 5 Shifted root values

  6. Problem 6 Differences between roots

    The roots of x3−x2−2x+5x^3-x^2-2x+5x3−x2−2x+5 are r,s,tr,s,tr,s,t, including any repeats. Find (r−s)2+(r−t)2+(s−t)2(r-s)^2+(r-t)^2+(s-t)^2(r−s)2+(r−t)2+(s−t)2 without finding the roots.

    1. Hint 1

      Expand the three squares and collect the same kinds of terms.

    2. Hint 2

      Each root square appears twice and each pair product appears with coefficient −2-2−2.

    Answer

    141414.

    Full solution

    The root sum is e1=1e_1=1e1​=1 and pair sum is e2=−2e_2=-2e2​=−2.

    The sum of squares is

    r2+s2+t2=12−2(−2)r^2+s^2+t^2=1^2-2(-2)r2+s2+t2=12−2(−2) r2+s2+t2=5r^2+s^2+t^2=5r2+s2+t2=5

    If DDD is the requested total, expansion gives

    D=2(r2+s2+t2)−2e2D=2(r^2+s^2+t^2)-2e_2D=2(r2+s2+t2)−2e2​ D=2(5)−2(−2)D=2(5)-2(-2)D=2(5)−2(−2) D=14D=14D=14

    The calculation includes the full complex root list and does not require the roots themselves.

    Answer

    141414.

    Key idea

    A symmetric combination of root differences can be computed by expanding it into symmetric sums.

  7. Problem 7 A coefficient condition

    For p(x)=2x4−3x3+5x2+cx+6p(x)=2x^4-3x^3+5x^2+cx+6p(x)=2x4−3x3+5x2+cx+6, the sum of the reciprocals of all four roots equals twice the sum of the roots. Find ccc.

  9. Problem 9 Interpreting a negative total

    A student reads the roots r,sr,sr,s of x2+2x+5x^2+2x+5x2+2x+5 and says: "Their squares add to a negative number, so the calculation must be wrong." Decide whether the objection is valid without solving for the roots, and state r2+s2r^2+s^2r2+s2.

    r2+s2=(r+s)2−2rsr^2+s^2=(r+s)^2-2rsr2+s2=(r+s)2−2rs r2+s2=−6r^2+s^2=-6r2+s2=−6

    The negative total is not a contradiction.

    The discriminant is 22−4(1)(5)=−162^2-4(1)(5)=-1622−4(1)(5)=−16, so the roots are nonreal, and their squares are not constrained to be nonnegative real numbers.

    Answer

    Invalid; r2+s2=−6r^2+s^2=-6r2+s2=−6.
