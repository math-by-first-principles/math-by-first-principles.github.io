    An orthogonal polygon is formed by removing an upper right rectangular corner 121212 cm wide and 999 cm deep from a 272727 cm by 161616 cm rectangle. Find its area and perimeter.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The area loses the corner. Its two new orthogonal edges replace equal total horizontal and vertical lengths, leaving the perimeter unchanged.

    A=27(16)−12(9)A=324P=2(27+16)P=86\begin{aligned} A&=27(16)-12(9) \\ A&=324 \\ P&=2(27+16) \\ P&=86 \end{aligned}AAPP​=27(16)−12(9)=324=2(27+16)=86​

    A 121212 cm by 777 cm rectangle and a right triangle with legs 121212 cm and 555 cm are joined along their full 121212 cm sides. Their interiors are disjoint. Find the union area and perimeter.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The triangle hypotenuse is 131313 cm by Pythagoras. Add the two areas; remove both copies of the common side from the perimeter sum.

    A=12(7)+12(12)(5)A=114P=2(12+7)+(12+5+13)−24P=44\begin{aligned} A&=12(7)+\tfrac12(12)(5) \\ A&=114 \\ P&=2(12+7)+(12+5+13)-24 \\ P&=44 \end{aligned}AAPP​=12(7)+21​(12)(5)=114=2(12+7)+(12+5+13)−24=44​

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D
