    Two rectangles have disjoint interiors and share part of an edge. Their dimensions are 888 cm by 777 cm and 333 cm by 666 cm. What is the area of their union?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Their interiors do not overlap, so add their rectangular areas.

    A=8(7)+3(6)A=74\begin{aligned}A&=8(7)+3(6)\\A&=74\end{aligned}AA​=8(7)+3(6)=74​

    Solution

    A=11(4)+12(11)(6)A=77\begin{aligned}A&=11(4)+\tfrac12(11)(6)\\A&=77\end{aligned}AA​=11(4)+21​(11)(6)=77​
  4. 4

    An L-shaped polygon has horizontal and vertical sides. Its full width is 222222 cm, and its upper horizontal side is 999 cm. What is the horizontal width of the absent upper right corner?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The retained upper width and absent width span the full bottom width.

    22−9=1322-9=1322−9=13
  5. 5

    An L-shaped polygon has horizontal and vertical sides. Its full height is 151515 cm. The lower right vertical side is 666 cm. What is the vertical depth of the absent upper right corner?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The absent depth and lower side span the same height as the full left side.

    15−6=915-6=915−6=9

    Two polygons with perimeters 282828 cm and 363636 cm have disjoint interiors and meet along exactly one 555 cm segment. What is their union perimeter?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The sum counts the shared segment twice. Neither copy is exposed.

    P=28+36−2(5)P=54\begin{aligned}P&=28+36-2(5)\\P&=54\end{aligned}PP​=28+36−2(5)=54​

    When does subtracting the area of a removed polygon from the area of an outer polygon give the retained area?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Every removed part must first belong to the whole. Under that condition, area addition can be rearranged as

    Aretained=Aouter−AremovedA_{\text{retained}}=A_{\text{outer}}-A_{\text{removed}}Aretained​=Aouter​−Aremoved​
