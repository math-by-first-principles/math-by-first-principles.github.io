  1. Problem 1 The clipped corner

    An 181818 cm by 888 cm rectangle has its upper right corner removed along a diagonal. The removed right triangle has perpendicular legs 555 cm and 666 cm along the rectangle sides. Find the retained area.

    Text description of this figure

    Schematic rectangle 18 cm wide and 8 cm high with its upper right corner removed along a diagonal. The removed right triangle has horizontal leg 5 cm and vertical leg 6 cm. The shaded polygon is retained. No scale is supplied.

    1. Hint 1

      Identify the familiar whole and the part that is absent.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the perpendicular legs to find the missing triangle area.

    Answer

    129 cm2129\text{ cm}^2129 cm2.

    Full solution

    The outer rectangle has area 18(8)=14418(8)=14418(8)=144 square centimeters.

    The removed right triangle has area 12(5)(6)=15\tfrac12(5)(6)=1521​(5)(6)=15 square centimeters.

    Subtract the excluded region once.

    A=144−15A=144-15A=144−15 A=129 cm2.A=129\text{ cm}^2.A=129 cm2.

    Answer

    129 cm2129\text{ cm}^2129 cm2.

    Key idea

    A convenient containing figure can make subtraction shorter than splitting the retained region.

  2. Problem 2 The joined rectangles

    A 777 cm by 999 cm rectangle and a 121212 cm by 444 cm rectangle have disjoint interiors. Their lower sides are aligned, and they meet along the entire 444 cm side of the second rectangle, with no other contact. Find the area and perimeter of their union.

    Text description of this figure

    Two rectangles with disjoint interiors, 7 by 9 cm and 12 by 4 cm, joined along the entire side of length 4 cm of the second rectangle. Their lower edges are aligned. The shared edge is dashed. No scale is supplied.

    1. Hint 1

      The same join has different roles in area and perimeter.

    2. Hint 2

      Add rectangular areas, but remove both copies of the shared side from the sum of their perimeters.

    Answer

    A=111 cm2P=56 cm\begin{aligned}A&=111\text{ cm}^2\\P&=56\text{ cm}\end{aligned}AP​=111 cm2=56 cm​

    Full solution

    The shared segment has no area.

    The union area is

    A=7(9)+12(4)A=7(9)+12(4)A=7(9)+12(4) A=111 cm2.A=111\text{ cm}^2.A=111 cm2.

    The rectangle perimeters are 323232 cm and 323232 cm.

    Their sum counts the 444 cm shared segment twice, although it is internal.

    Remove both copies.

    P=32+32−2(4)P=32+32-2(4)P=32+32−2(4) P=56 cm.P=56\text{ cm}.P=56 cm.

    Answer

    A=111 cm2P=56 cm\begin{aligned}A&=111\text{ cm}^2\\P&=56\text{ cm}\end{aligned}AP​=111 cm2=56 cm​

    Key idea

    A join contributes zero area and removes two copies of its length from the perimeter sum.

  3. Problem 3 The staircase outline

    An orthogonal polygon has the boundary steps shown. In order from its upper left vertex, travel right 666 cm, down 444 cm, right 999 cm, down 777 cm, right 555 cm, down 555 cm, left xxx cm, and up yyy cm to the start. Find xxx, yyy, its perimeter, and its area.

    Text description of this figure

    A schematic orthogonal staircase boundary. Starting at the upper left and following the boundary: right 6 cm, down 4 cm, right 9 cm, down 7 cm, right 5 cm, down 5 cm, left x cm, and up y cm back to the start. No scale is supplied.

    1. Hint 1

      A closed path has equal total distances in opposite horizontal directions and in opposite vertical directions.

    2. Hint 2

      Use vertical cuts at the two inward corners to find three rectangles.

    3. Hint 3

      The rectangle widths are the three rightward steps; determine each full height above the common bottom.

    Answer

    x=20 cmy=16 cmP=72 cmA=229 cm2\begin{aligned}x&=20\text{ cm}\\y&=16\text{ cm}\\P&=72\text{ cm}\\A&=229\text{ cm}^2\end{aligned}xyPA​=20 cm=16 cm=72 cm=229 cm2​

    Full solution

    Closure horizontally gives

    x=6+9+5x=6+9+5x=6+9+5 x=20.x=20.x=20.

    Closure vertically gives

    y=4+7+5y=4+7+5y=4+7+5 y=16.y=16.y=16.

    The total rightward travel is 202020 cm and total downward travel is 161616 cm.

    Returning to the start adds the same distances.

    P=2(20+16)P=2(20+16)P=2(20+16) P=72 cm.P=72\text{ cm}.P=72 cm.

    Vertical cuts from the inward corners to the bottom give rectangles of widths 666, 999, and 555 cm.

    Their heights are 161616, 121212, and 555 cm respectively, since the second top is 444 cm below the first and the third top is another 777 cm down.

    Their interiors do not overlap and cover the polygon.

    A=6(16)+9(12)+5(5)A=6(16)+9(12)+5(5)A=6(16)+9(12)+5(5) A=229 cm2.A=229\text{ cm}^2.A=229 cm2.

    Answer

    x=20 cmy=16 cmP=72 cmA=229 cm2\begin{aligned}x&=20\text{ cm}\\y&=16\text{ cm}\\P&=72\text{ cm}\\A&=229\text{ cm}^2\end{aligned}xyPA​=20 cm=16 cm=72 cm=229 cm2​

    Key idea

    Aligned steps recover hidden full lengths and the heights needed for an additive split.

  4. Problem 4 The recessed entrance

    A room follows a 9.69.69.6 m by 7.47.47.4 m rectangle except for a rectangular entrance recess 3.23.23.2 m wide and 2.52.52.5 m deep, opening into the middle of one 9.69.69.6 m side. All edges are horizontal or vertical. Find the floor area and the baseboard length if a 1.11.11.1 m doorway occupies part of the recess bottom. All other boundary edges receive baseboard.

    Text description of this figure

    Schematic rectangular outline 9.6 by 7.4 m with a rectangular notch 3.2 m wide and 2.5 m deep opening into the middle of its top side. All sides are horizontal or vertical. The notch is not a closed hole. No scale is supplied.

    1. Hint 1

      The recess is open to the outside rather than a closed interior hole.

    2. Hint 2

      Its bottom replaces an equal length on the original side, and its two depth edges add boundary.

    3. Hint 3

      Subtract the doorway only after finding the complete new boundary.

    Answer

    A=63.04 m2baseboard=37.9 m\begin{aligned}A&=63.04\text{ m}^2\\\text{baseboard}&=37.9\text{ m}\end{aligned}Abaseboard​=63.04 m2=37.9 m​

    Full solution

    Subtract the rectangular recess from the full floor rectangle.

    A=9.6(7.4)−3.2(2.5)A=9.6(7.4)-3.2(2.5)A=9.6(7.4)−3.2(2.5) A=63.04 m2.A=63.04\text{ m}^2.A=63.04 m2.

    The original rectangle perimeter is 343434 m.

    The missing 3.23.23.2 m top segment is replaced by the 3.23.23.2 m recess bottom.

    The two 2.52.52.5 m depth edges are additional.

    P=34+2(2.5)P=34+2(2.5)P=34+2(2.5) P=39 m.P=39\text{ m}.P=39 m.

    The doorway takes 1.11.11.1 m of that boundary but receives no baseboard.

    39−1.1=37.9 m.39-1.1=37.9\text{ m}.39−1.1=37.9 m.

    Answer

    A=63.04 m2baseboard=37.9 m\begin{aligned}A&=63.04\text{ m}^2\\\text{baseboard}&=37.9\text{ m}\end{aligned}Abaseboard​=63.04 m2=37.9 m​

    Key idea

    An open side recess adds two depth edges even when the removed and replacement widths cancel.

  5. Problem 5 The two unit systems

    A 353535 cm by 222222 cm rectangle loses an upper right rectangular corner measuring 120120120 mm by 808080 mm. Its horizontal notch width is 120120120 mm. Find the remaining area in square meters and its perimeter in meters. Use 101010 mm equal to 111 cm and 100100100 cm equal to 111 m. Explain the conversion factors.

    Text description of this figure

    Schematic L-shaped polygon with horizontal and vertical sides. Total width 35 cm, total height 22 cm. The removed upper right rectangular corner is 120 mm wide and 80 mm deep. Dashed lines show the original top and right edges of that corner. No scale is supplied.

    1. Hint 1

      Convert the notch lengths into the same unit as the outside lengths before finding any area.

    2. Hint 2

      A corner notch replaces equal horizontal and vertical boundary lengths.

    3. Hint 3

      A one-meter square has one hundred centimeters in each of two directions.

    Answer

    A=0.0674 m2P=1.14 m\begin{aligned}A&=0.0674\text{ m}^2\\P&=1.14\text{ m}\end{aligned}AP​=0.0674 m2=1.14 m​

    Full solution

    The notch is 121212 cm by 888 cm.

    Both follow from dividing the millimeter lengths by 101010.

    Subtract areas in square centimeters.

    A=35(22)−12(8)A=35(22)-12(8)A=35(22)−12(8) A=674 cm2.A=674\text{ cm}^2.A=674 cm2.

    A one-meter square has 100100100 cm along each side, so it contains 100(100)100(100)100(100) square centimeters.

    674÷10000=0.0674 m2.674\div10000=0.0674\text{ m}^2.674÷10000=0.0674 m2.

    The corner removal replaces a 121212 cm horizontal part and an 888 cm vertical part by equal new lengths.

    The perimeter is

    P=2(35+22)P=2(35+22)P=2(35+22) P=114 cm.P=114\text{ cm}.P=114 cm.

    Perimeter is one length, so divide by 100100100 only once.

    114 cm=1.14 m.114\text{ cm}=1.14\text{ m}.114 cm=1.14 m.

    Answer

    A=0.0674 m2P=1.14 m\begin{aligned}A&=0.0674\text{ m}^2\\P&=1.14\text{ m}\end{aligned}AP​=0.0674 m2=1.14 m​

    Key idea

    Square-unit conversion uses both directions, while converting a boundary length uses one.

  6. Problem 6 The shared boundary rule

    Two simple polygons have disjoint interiors and meet along exactly one boundary segment of positive length sss. They have no other contact. Their perimeters are P1P_1P1​ and P2P_2P2​. Prove that the perimeter of their union is P1+P2−2sP_1+P_2-2sP1​+P2​−2s.

    Text description of this figure

    Two schematic simple polygons joined along a single vertical segment s. Their interiors lie on opposite sides of that segment. Their perimeters before joining are P one and P two. The common segment is dashed and there is no other contact. No scale is supplied.

    1. Hint 1

      Follow how many times the common segment occurs before and after joining.

    2. Hint 2

      First test the counting with perimeters 343434 cm and 464646 cm and a shared 999 cm side.

    3. Hint 3

      Every nonshared boundary segment remains exposed; only the two counted copies of the join disappear.

    Answer

    P=P1+P2−2sP=P_1+P_2-2sP=P1​+P2​−2s.

    Full solution

    For the numerical instance, the sum 34+4634+4634+46 is 808080 cm and includes the 999 cm join twice.

    Both copies become internal, leaving

    80−2(9)=62 cm.80-2(9)=62\text{ cm}.80−2(9)=62 cm.

    For the general polygons, each nonshared boundary part belongs to exactly one original perimeter and remains on the union boundary, because their interiors are disjoint and they have no other contact.

    The shared segment belongs to each perimeter, so the sum counts its length twice.

    No part of that shared segment is exposed after joining.

    Removing its two counted copies gives

    P=P1+P2−2s.P=P_1+P_2-2s.P=P1​+P2​−2s.

    Nothing in this boundary accounting depended on the sample lengths.

    Answer

    P=P1+P2−2sP=P_1+P_2-2sP=P1​+P2​−2s.

    Key idea

    Perimeter addition must account for how often each exposed or internal segment was counted.

  7. Problem 7 The notched trapezoid

    A trapezoid has parallel bases 212121 cm and 131313 cm and perpendicular height 101010 cm. A rectangular notch 555 cm wide opens into the middle of the longer base and extends perpendicularly into the trapezoid. The notch lies entirely within it, and the retained area is 135135135 square centimeters. Find the notch depth.

    Text description of this figure

    Schematic trapezoid with horizontal parallel bases 13 cm at the top and 21 cm at the bottom, and perpendicular height 10 cm. A rectangular notch of width 5 cm and unknown depth d opens into the middle of the bottom base. The notch lies wholly within the trapezoid. No scale is supplied.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare the retained area with the area of the complete trapezoid.

    2. Hint 2

      The missing area is five times the notch depth.

    Answer

    777 cm.

    Full solution

    The complete trapezoid area is

    A=21+132(10)A=\frac{21+13}{2}(10)A=221+13​(10) A=170 cm2.A=170\text{ cm}^2.A=170 cm2.

    The removed rectangle therefore has area

    170−135=35 cm2.170-135=35\text{ cm}^2.170−135=35 cm2.

    If its depth is ddd cm,

    5d=355d=355d=35 d=7.d=7.d=7.

    The depth is positive and below the full 101010 cm height.

    With the stated containment of the notch, it gives the described retained region.

    Answer

    777 cm.

    Key idea

    An area deficit gives a missing product, which can reveal a notch dimension.

  8. Problem 8 The two openings

    A 242424 cm by 171717 cm rectangle contains two holes strictly inside it. They have no contact. One hole is a right triangle with perpendicular legs 666 cm and 888 cm; the other is a square of side 555 cm. Find the retained area and the total boundary length including both holes.

    Text description of this figure

    They are separate, so

    A=24(17)−24−25A=24(17)-24-25A=24(17)−24−25 A=359 cm2.A=359\text{ cm}^2.A=359 cm2.

    Its boundary is 242424 cm.

    The outer rectangle perimeter is 828282 cm, and the square hole perimeter is 202020 cm.

    Include both inner boundaries.

    Ptotal=82+24+20P_{\text{total}}=82+24+20Ptotal​=82+24+20 Ptotal=126 cm.P_{\text{total}}=126\text{ cm}.Ptotal​=126 cm.

    Answer

    A=359 cm2Ptotal=126 cm\begin{aligned}A&=359\text{ cm}^2\\P_{\text{total}}&=126\text{ cm}\end{aligned}APtotal​​=359 cm2=126 cm​
