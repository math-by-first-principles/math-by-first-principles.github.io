    A convex trapezoid has perpendicular height 666 cm and area 114114114 square cm. Its longer parallel base exceeds its shorter base by 101010 cm. Find the shorter base.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Let the shorter base be bbb cm, so the longer is b+10b+10b+10 cm.

    114=2b+102⋅6,38=2b+10,b=14.\begin{aligned} 114&=\frac{2b+10}{2}\cdot6,\\ 38&=2b+10,\\ b&=14. \end{aligned}11438b​=22b+10​⋅6,=2b+10,=14.​
  2. 2

    In a rhombus each side is 101010 cm and one full diagonal is 121212 cm. Find the area.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Each half of the known diagonal is 666 cm. A right triangle with hypotenuse 101010 cm has other leg 888 cm, so the second full diagonal is 161616 cm.

    A=12⋅12⋅16,A=96.\begin{aligned} A&=\frac12\cdot12\cdot16, \\ A&=96. \end{aligned}AA​=21​⋅12⋅16,=96.​
  3. 3

    In convex kite ABCDABCDABCD, the equal-side pairs meet at AAA and CCC. Diagonals meet at OOO. Given BOBOBO is 555 cm, AOAOAO is 444 cm, and area 757575 square cm, find OCOCOC.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    AC bisects BD, so BD=10BD=10BD=10 cm. First find the full diagonal AC.

    AC=2⋅7510,AC=15OC=15−4,OC=11.\begin{aligned} AC&=\frac{2\cdot75}{10}, \\ AC&=15 \\ OC&=15-4, \\ OC&=11. \end{aligned}ACACOCOC​=102⋅75​,=15=15−4,=11.​

    In a convex quadrilateral, diagonal ACACAC is 121212 cm and area 102102102 square cm. The perpendicular distance from BBB to ACACAC is 555 cm. Find the perpendicular distance from DDD to ACACAC.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The two triangle heights add in the area computation.

    102=12⋅12⋅(5+h),h=17−5,h=12.\begin{aligned} 102&=\frac12\cdot12\cdot(5+h), \\ h&=17-5, \\ h&=12. \end{aligned}102hh​=21​⋅12⋅(5+h),=17−5,=12.​

    Solution

    A convex kite has area 126126126 square cm. Its full diagonal ACACAC is 212121 cm, and AC bisects BD at O. The point O divides AC into parts 666 cm and 151515 cm. Find BO.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The area determines the full diagonal BD; then use its bisection.

    BD=2⋅12621,BD=12BO=12÷2,BO=6.\begin{aligned} BD&=\frac{2\cdot126}{21}, \\ BD&=12 \\ BO&=12\div2, \\ BO&=6. \end{aligned}BDBDBOBO​=212⋅126​,=12=12÷2,=6.​

  11. 11

    A convex trapezoid has midsegment 141414 cm and area 112112112 square cm. Its longer base is 181818 cm. Find its height and shorter base.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The midsegment is the average of the bases, so area equals midsegment times height.

    h=112÷14,h=8b=2⋅14−18,b=10.\begin{aligned} h&=112\div14, \\ h&=8 \\ b&=2\cdot14-18, \\ b&=10. \end{aligned}hhbb​=112÷14,=8=2⋅14−18,=10.​

    Solution
