    Solution

    A rhombus has area 105105105 square cm and one full diagonal 141414 cm. Find the other full diagonal.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A rhombus satisfies the perpendicularity condition.

    d=2⋅10514,d=15.\begin{aligned} d&=\frac{2\cdot105}{14}, \\ d&=15. \end{aligned}dd​=142⋅105​,=15.​

    In convex kite ABCDABCDABCD, the equal-side pairs meet at AAA and CCC. Diagonals meet at OOO. Given AOAOAO is 333 cm, OCOCOC is 888 cm, and BOBOBO is 666 cm, find the kite area.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    AC bisects BD, so the full lengths are 111111 cm and 121212 cm.

    A=12⋅11⋅12,A=66.\begin{aligned} A&=\frac12\cdot11\cdot12, \\ A&=66. \end{aligned}AA​=21​⋅11⋅12,=66.​

    A convex quadrilateral has a diagonal 181818 cm long. The perpendicular distances of the other vertices from its line are 222 cm and 777 cm. Find its area.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The diagonal is a base for each triangle.

    A=12⋅18⋅2+12⋅18⋅7,A=81.\begin{aligned} A&=\frac12\cdot18\cdot2+\frac12\cdot18\cdot7,\\ A&=81. \end{aligned}AA​=21​⋅18⋅2+21​⋅18⋅7,=81.​

    A square has a full diagonal of 121212 cm. Find its area.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The two diagonals of a square are equal and perpendicular.

    A=12⋅12⋅12,A=72.\begin{aligned} A&=\frac12\cdot12\cdot12, \\ A&=72. \end{aligned}AA​=21​⋅12⋅12,=72.​

    A convex trapezoid has bases 777 cm and 191919 cm and perpendicular height 121212 cm. Its midsegment is 131313 cm. Which area computation is justified?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The midsegment length is the average of the parallel bases, as established in Chapter 8.

    A=7+192⋅12,A=156.\begin{aligned} A&=\frac{7+19}{2}\cdot12, \\ A&=156. \end{aligned}AA​=27+19​⋅12,=156.​

    A convex quadrilateral has perpendicular diagonals meeting at OOO. One diagonal is 202020 cm long. The other is split at OOO into 444 cm and 999 cm. Find its area.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    A=12⋅20⋅(4+9),A=130.\begin{aligned} A&=\frac12\cdot20\cdot(4+9), \\ A&=130. \end{aligned}AA​=21​⋅20⋅(4+9),=130.​

    Solution
