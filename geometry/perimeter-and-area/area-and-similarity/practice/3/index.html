    A nonoverlapping triangle partition is carried through a similarity of positive length factor k. Which expression gives the image area if the original triangle areas are a, b, and c?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    k2a+k2b+k2c=k2(a+b+c).k^2a+k^2b+k^2c=k^2(a+b+c).k2a+k2b+k2c=k2(a+b+c).

  11. 11

    Two nondegenerate triangles are known to be similar. Their corresponding altitudes have equal positive lengths. Which conclusion must hold?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Corresponding altitude lengths scale by the length factor. Equal positive altitudes force that factor to be 111.

    The triangles therefore have the same shape and size, so they are congruent and have equal areas.
