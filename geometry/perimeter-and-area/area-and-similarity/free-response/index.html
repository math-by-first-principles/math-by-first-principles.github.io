  1. Problem 1 A direct enlargement

    A simple polygon has area 18 cm218\text{ cm}^218 cm2. A similar image is made using length scale factor 5/25/25/2 to take the original to its image. Find its area.

    Text description of this figure

    Original and image polygons, schematic. Original: Area 18 square cm. Image: Length factor 5/2. Similarity is supplied in the text.

    1. Hint 1

      Area depends on how two independent length dimensions change.

    2. Hint 2

      Square the length scale factor before multiplying the original area.

    Answer

    112.5 cm2112.5\text{ cm}^2112.5 cm2, or 2252 cm2\tfrac{225}{2}\text{ cm}^22225​ cm2.

    Full solution

    The area factor is the square of the length factor.

    k2=254.k^2=\frac{25}{4}.k2=425​.

    Multiply the original area by that factor.

    Aimage=254⋅18.A_{\text{image}}=\frac{25}{4}\cdot18.Aimage​=425​⋅18. Aimage=112.5 cm2.A_{\text{image}}=112.5\text{ cm}^2.Aimage​=112.5 cm2.

    Answer

    112.5 cm2112.5\text{ cm}^2112.5 cm2, or 2252 cm2\tfrac{225}{2}\text{ cm}^22225​ cm2.

    Key idea

    Area uses the square of a positive length scale factor.

  2. Problem 2 Corresponding measurements

    Two simple polygons are similar. An original side of 222222 cm corresponds to an image side of 141414 cm. The original perimeter is 666666 cm and its area is 242 cm2242\text{ cm}^2242 cm2.

    Find the image perimeter.

    Find the image area.

    Text description of this figure

    Original and image polygons, schematic. Original: Side 22 cm. Image: Matching side 14 cm. Similarity is supplied in the text.

    1. Hint 1

      Keep image measurements over original measurements.

    2. Hint 2

      Perimeter uses the resulting factor once; area uses its square.

    Answer

    Image perimeter:

    42 cm.42\text{ cm}.42 cm.

    Image area:

    98 cm2.98\text{ cm}^2.98 cm2.

    Full solution

    The length scale factor taking the original to its image is

    k=1422.k=\frac{14}{22}.k=2214​. k=711.k=\frac7{11}.k=117​.

    For perimeter, multiply once.

    Pimage=711⋅66.P_{\text{image}}=\frac7{11}\cdot66.Pimage​=117​⋅66. Pimage=42 cm.P_{\text{image}}=42\text{ cm}.Pimage​=42 cm.

    For area, multiply by the squared factor.

    Aimage=49121⋅242.A_{\text{image}}=\frac{49}{121}\cdot242.Aimage​=12149​⋅242. Aimage=98 cm2.A_{\text{image}}=98\text{ cm}^2.Aimage​=98 cm2.

    Answer

    Image perimeter:

    42 cm.42\text{ cm}.42 cm.

    Image area:

    98 cm2.98\text{ cm}^2.98 cm2.

    Key idea

    An ordered side ratio determines perimeter and area factors when similarity is known.

  3. Problem 3 From areas to a side

    Two triangles are similar, with area ratio 36:4936:4936:49 in the order original:image. An original side is 181818 cm. Find the corresponding image side and explain why similarity is needed.

    Text description of this figure

    Two similar triangles, schematic. Their area ratio, in the order original:image, is 36:49. The diagram does not encode the unknown length scale factor.

    1. Hint 1

      Areas determine a corresponding length ratio only for similar shapes.

    2. Hint 2

      Take the positive square root, keeping image area over original area.

    Answer

    Image side: 212121 cm. Similarity is needed to link corresponding lengths to the area ratio.

    Full solution

    Image area divided by original area is 49/3649/3649/36.

    Similarity gives the positive length factor

    k=4936.k=\sqrt{\frac{49}{36}}.k=3649​​. k=76.k=\frac76.k=67​.

    Multiply the original side by that factor.

    image side=76⋅18.\text{image side}=\frac76\cdot18.image side=67​⋅18. image side=21 cm.\text{image side}=21\text{ cm}.image side=21 cm.

    Area by itself measures the enclosed region and does not fix its shape.

    The given similarity supplies the common factor for corresponding lengths.

    Answer

    Image side: 212121 cm. Similarity is needed to link corresponding lengths to the area ratio.

    Key idea

    An inverse area calculation depends on an established similarity.

  4. Problem 4 The outer portion

    In nondegenerate triangle ABCABCABC, DDD lies between AAA and BBB, EEE lies between AAA and CCC, and DEDEDE is parallel to BCBCBC. The ratio AD:ABAD:ABAD:AB is 3:53:53:5.

    The area of ABCABCABC is 200 cm2200\text{ cm}^2200 cm2. Find the area inside ABCABCABC but outside triangle ADEADEADE.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle ABC with D on AB and E on AC. DE is parallel to BC. The drawing is schematic; the length ratio is supplied in the problem.

    1. Hint 1

      Parallel lines connect the inner triangle to the whole triangle.

    2. Hint 2

      AA gives similarity, so square the inner-to-whole length ratio.

    3. Hint 3

      Subtract the inner area from the whole area.

    Answer

    128 cm2128\text{ cm}^2128 cm2.

    Full solution

    The triangles share angle A.

    Parallel DE and BC give a second pair of equal angles, so ADE is similar to ABC by AA.

    Let A△ADEA_{\triangle ADE}A△ADE​ denote the inner triangle area.

    The inner-to-whole length factor is 3/53/53/5, so

    A△ADE=925⋅200.A_{\triangle ADE}=\frac9{25}\cdot200.A△ADE​=259​⋅200. A△ADE=72 cm2.A_{\triangle ADE}=72\text{ cm}^2.A△ADE​=72 cm2.

    The inner triangle lies inside ABC because D and E lie on its sides.

    Subtract its area.

    Aoutside=200−72.A_{\text{outside}}=200-72.Aoutside​=200−72. Aoutside=128 cm2.A_{\text{outside}}=128\text{ cm}^2.Aoutside​=128 cm2.

    Answer

    128 cm2128\text{ cm}^2128 cm2.

    Key idea

    A parallel cut makes a similar triangle whose area fraction is a squared length fraction.

  5. Problem 5 A resized emblem

    A flat polygonal emblem is enlarged uniformly by length scale factor 1.51.51.5. The original face has area 54 cm254\text{ cm}^254 cm2 and its boundary has length 303030 cm.

    Find the enlarged face area.

    Find the length of trim needed to follow its boundary once, with no allowance for joins.

    Text description of this figure

    Original and image polygons, schematic. Original: Area 54; P = 30 cm. Image: Length factor 1.5. Similarity is supplied in the text.

    1. Hint 1

      Uniform enlargement gives similar faces.

    2. Hint 2

      Use separate factors for the face area and the boundary length.

    Answer

    Face area:

    121.5 cm2.121.5\text{ cm}^2.121.5 cm2.

    Trim length:

    45 cm.45\text{ cm}.45 cm.

    Full solution

    The face area uses the squared factor.

    Aimage=1.52⋅54.A_{\text{image}}=1.5^2\cdot54.Aimage​=1.52⋅54. Aimage=121.5 cm2.A_{\text{image}}=121.5\text{ cm}^2.Aimage​=121.5 cm2.

    The trim follows the perimeter and uses the length factor once.

    Pimage=1.5⋅30.P_{\text{image}}=1.5\cdot30.Pimage​=1.5⋅30. Pimage=45 cm.P_{\text{image}}=45\text{ cm}.Pimage​=45 cm.

    Answer

    Face area:

    121.5 cm2.121.5\text{ cm}^2.121.5 cm2.

    Trim length:

    45 cm.45\text{ cm}.45 cm.

  6. Problem 6 A preserved fraction

    A simple polygon of positive area is partitioned into two polygonal pieces with nonoverlapping interiors. Both pieces have positive areas. A similarity carries the whole polygon and the partition to an image with positive length factor kkk.

    Prove that the first piece occupies the same fraction of the total area in the original and in the image.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare the area of the piece to the area of the whole in each figure.

    2. Hint 2

      Both numerator and denominator acquire the same area factor.

    3. Hint 3

      The common factor can be canceled because it is positive.

    Answer

    The first piece occupies the same fraction of the total area in the original and in the image.

    Full solution

    Let A1A_1A1​ be the first piece area and AAA the whole original area.

    The original fraction is A1/AA_1/AA1​/A.

    The similarity carries each piece to its image without creating an overlap or a gap.

    Every area has factor k2k^2k2, so the image fraction is

    k2A1k2A.\frac{k^2A_1}{k^2A}.k2Ak2A1​​.

    Since k>0k>0k>0 and A>0A>0A>0, cancellation is valid.

    k2A1k2A=A1A.\frac{k^2A_1}{k^2A}=\frac{A_1}{A}.k2Ak2A1​​=AA1​​.

    Answer

    The first piece occupies the same fraction of the total area in the original and in the image.

    Key idea

    The same area factor on a part and its whole preserves their area fraction.

  7. Problem 7 Check the calculation

    Similar polygons have original area 128 cm2128\text{ cm}^2128 cm2 and image area 392 cm2392\text{ cm}^2392 cm2. The original perimeter is 484848 cm. Examine the following calculation.

    Step 1.

    k2=392128.k^2=\frac{392}{128}.k2=128392​. k2=4916.k^2=\frac{49}{16}.k2=1649​.

    Step 2.

    k=4916.k=\frac{49}{16}.k=1649​.

    Step 3.

    Pimage=48⋅4916.P_{\text{image}}=48\cdot\frac{49}{16}.Pimage​=48⋅1649​.

    Identify the first invalid step, repair it, and find the image perimeter.

    1. Hint 1

      Distinguish the length factor from its square.

    2. Hint 2

      Check which step first stops following from the preceding information.

    3. Hint 3

      Use the positive square root before scaling perimeter.

    Answer

    Step 2 is first invalid. Correct length factor:

    k=74.k=\tfrac74.k=47​.

    Image perimeter:

    84 cm.84\text{ cm}.84 cm.

    Full solution

    Step 1 correctly uses image area over original area and simplifies the ratio.

    Step 2 incorrectly treats k2k^2k2 as kkk.

    Take the positive square root instead.

    k=4916.k=\sqrt{\frac{49}{16}}.k=1649​​. k=74.k=\frac74.k=47​.

    Then scale the original perimeter by the length factor.

    Pimage=48⋅74.P_{\text{image}}=48\cdot\frac74.Pimage​=48⋅47​. Pimage=84 cm.P_{\text{image}}=84\text{ cm}.Pimage​=84 cm.

    Answer

    Step 2 is first invalid. Correct length factor:

    k=74.k=\tfrac74.k=47​.

    Image perimeter:

    84 cm.84\text{ cm}.84 cm.

    Key idea

    Recover the length factor before applying an area ratio to a perimeter.

  8. Problem 8 Equal area and shape

    A rectangle is 555 cm by 121212 cm. Construct a numerical counterexample to the statement that any rectangle with the same area must be similar to it. Give positive whole-number side lengths for a second rectangle, verify equal areas, and show that the shapes are not similar.

    1. Hint 1

      Equal products can come from different pairs of side lengths.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare longer-side to shorter-side ratios to test rectangle similarity.

    Answer

    One possible second rectangle is 333 cm by 202020 cm. Both areas are 60 cm260\text{ cm}^260 cm2, but the side ratios 12/512/512/5 and 20/320/320/3 differ. Other valid counterexamples are accepted.

    Full solution

    The given rectangle has area

    A=5⋅12.A=5\cdot12.A=5⋅12. A=60 cm2.A=60\text{ cm}^2.A=60 cm2.

    A 333 cm by 202020 cm rectangle also has area

    A=3⋅20.A=3\cdot20.A=3⋅20. A=60 cm2.A=60\text{ cm}^2.A=60 cm2.

    For similarity, longer sides must correspond and shorter sides must correspond.

    The ratios 12/512/512/5 and 20/320/320/3 are unequal, so these rectangles are not similar.

    Equal area therefore does not guarantee similarity.

    Answer

    One possible second rectangle is 333 cm by 202020 cm. Both areas are 60 cm260\text{ cm}^260 cm2, but the side ratios 12/512/512/5 and 20/320/320/3 differ. Other valid counterexamples are accepted.

    Key idea

    A counterexample satisfies the given condition while failing the proposed conclusion.

  9. Problem 9 A percentage comparison

    A similar image of a polygon has 44%44\%44% more area than the original. Find the percentage increase in its perimeter. Explain why using 44%44\%44% as the perimeter increase would not follow.

    1. Hint 1

      Translate the area increase into an area multiplying factor.

    2. Hint 2

      The length factor is the positive square root of that factor.

    3. Hint 3

      Subtract one from the perimeter factor before converting to a percentage.

    Answer

    The perimeter increases by 20%20\%20%. The 44%44\%44% increase describes area, which uses the square of the length factor.

    Full solution

    An area increase of 44%44\%44% gives area factor 1.441.441.44.

    Similarity gives

    k=1.44.k=\sqrt{1.44}.k=1.44​. k=1.2.k=1.2.k=1.2.

    Perimeter uses factor 1.21.21.2, so it becomes 120%120\%120% of its original value.

    Its increase is 20%20\%20%.

    The area factor includes the change in two dimensions.

    A perimeter adds lengths and uses the length factor only once.

    Answer

    The perimeter increases by 20%20\%20%. The 44%44\%44% increase describes area, which uses the square of the length factor.

    Key idea

    Convert an area percentage to a factor before taking its positive square root.

  10. Problem 10 Recover and transfer

    Two simple polygons are similar. The original area is 63 cm263\text{ cm}^263 cm2 and the image area is 175 cm2175\text{ cm}^2175 cm2. The image perimeter is 959595 cm. A diagonal of the original is 999 cm long.

    Find the original perimeter.

    Find the corresponding image diagonal.

    Text description of this figure

    Original and image polygons, schematic. Original: Area 63; diagonal 9 cm. Image: Area 175; P = 95 cm. Similarity is supplied in the text.

    1. Hint 1

      First recover the positive factor that multiplies original lengths to give image lengths.

    2. Hint 2

      To return from an image perimeter to the original, divide by that factor.

    3. Hint 3

      To move an original diagonal to its image, multiply by that factor.

    Answer

    Original perimeter:

    57 cm.57\text{ cm}.57 cm.

    Image diagonal:

    15 cm.15\text{ cm}.15 cm.

    Full solution

    The area ratio is image over original.

    k2=17563.k^2=\frac{175}{63}.k2=63175​. k2=259.k^2=\frac{25}{9}.k2=925​. k=53.k=\frac53.k=35​.

    The known perimeter belongs to the image.

    Divide by 5/35/35/3 to recover the original.

    Poriginal=95⋅35.P_{\text{original}}=95\cdot\frac35.Poriginal​=95⋅53​. Poriginal=57 cm.P_{\text{original}}=57\text{ cm}.Poriginal​=57 cm.

    The known diagonal belongs to the original.

    Corresponding diagonals are lengths and use the same factor.

    dimage=53⋅9.d_{\text{image}}=\frac53\cdot9.dimage​=35​⋅9. dimage=15 cm.d_{\text{image}}=15\text{ cm}.dimage​=15 cm.

    Answer

    Original perimeter:

    57 cm.57\text{ cm}.57 cm.

    Image diagonal:

    15 cm.15\text{ cm}.15 cm.

    Key idea

    After recovering a length factor, check which direction each requested measurement travels.
