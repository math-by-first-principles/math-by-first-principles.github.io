  1. Problem 1 Small-square units

    A unit square is divided into four rows and four columns of congruent squares. A rectangle is made from five columns and three rows of these small squares.

    Find its area in square units.

    Text description of this figure

    A rectangle has five columns and three rows of small squares. Beside it, a unit square has four columns and four rows of congruent small squares. Every small square in both grids is the same size.

    1. Hint 1

      Find the area of one small square from the unit square.

    2. Hint 2

      Count the small squares in the rectangle, then combine their areas.

    Answer

    1516\tfrac{15}{16}1615​ square unit.

    Full solution

    The unit square contains 16 congruent small squares.

    By congruence and addition, each small square has area 116\tfrac1{16}161​ square unit.

    The rectangle contains 15 of them.

    A=15⋅116A=15\cdot\frac1{16}A=15⋅161​ A=1516A=\frac{15}{16}A=1615​

    square unit.

    Answer

    1516\tfrac{15}{16}1615​ square unit.

    Key idea

    The unit square fixes the area of a tile before the tiles are counted.

  2. Problem 2 A perpendicular measurement

    A triangle has a 16 cm base and a corresponding perpendicular height of 9 cm.

    Find its area.

    Text description of this figure

    Schematic triangle, not drawn to scale. Base 16 cm, perpendicular height 9 cm.

    1. Hint 1

      Relate the triangle to two congruent copies in a parallelogram.

    2. Hint 2

      Take half the product of the matching base and height.

    Answer

    72 cm272\text{ cm}^272 cm2.

    Full solution

    A parallelogram with the same base and height is filled by two congruent copies of the triangle.

    Its area is 16⋅916\cdot916⋅9 square centimeters.

    One triangle has half that area.

    A=12⋅16⋅9A=\frac12\cdot16\cdot9A=21​⋅16⋅9 A=72 cm2.A=72\text{ cm}^2.A=72 cm2.

    Answer

    72 cm272\text{ cm}^272 cm2.

    Key idea

    A triangle has half the area of its paired parallelogram.

    Answer

  4. Problem 4 Parallelogram dimensions

    A parallelogram has area 152 cm2152\text{ cm}^2152 cm2, perpendicular height 8 cm, and adjacent side 10 cm.

    Find its chosen base length and perimeter.

    Text description of this figure

    Schematic parallelogram, not drawn to scale, with base b, perpendicular height 8 cm, and adjacent side 10 cm.

    1. Hint 1

      The area and perpendicular height determine the matching base.

    2. Hint 2

      Divide the area by the height, then use the two pairs of opposite sides for perimeter.

    Answer

    b=19 cm,P=58 cm.\begin{aligned}b&=19\text{ cm},\\P&=58\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}bP​=19 cm,=58 cm.​

    Full solution

    The unknown base b matches the 8 cm perpendicular height.

    152=8b152=8b152=8b b=19 cm.b=19\text{ cm}.b=19 cm.

    The adjacent side is 10 cm, and opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal.

    P=2(19+10)P=2(19+10)P=2(19+10) P=58 cm.P=58\text{ cm}.P=58 cm.

    Answer

    b=19 cm,P=58 cm.\begin{aligned}b&=19\text{ cm},\\P&=58\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}bP​=19 cm,=58 cm.​

    Key idea

    Area and perpendicular height determine the base; the boundary side lengths determine perimeter.

  5. Problem 5 A height beyond the side

    Triangle ABC has side AB of length 10 cm. The perpendicular from C to line AB meets it at H beyond B. The lengths BH and CH are 5 cm and 12 cm, respectively.

    Find the area of triangle ABC. Explain whether the segment BH is included in the chosen base.

    Text description of this figure

    Schematic triangle ABC with base AB 10 cm and perpendicular CH 12 cm. The foot H is beyond B on the extension of AB. The drawing is not to scale.

    1. Hint 1

      Keep the chosen side AB separate from its supporting line.

    2. Hint 2

      The perpendicular CH is the height, even though its foot is outside the triangle.

    Answer

    A=60 cm2.A=60\text{ cm}^2.A=60 cm2.

    Segment BH is not included in base AB.

    Full solution

    The chosen base AB has length 10 cm.

    Extending its line locates the foot H but does not change that side length.

    The matching height CH is 12 cm.

    A=12⋅10⋅12A=\frac12\cdot10\cdot12A=21​⋅10⋅12 A=60 cm2.A=60\text{ cm}^2.A=60 cm2.

    Answer

    A=60 cm2.A=60\text{ cm}^2.A=60 cm2.

    Segment BH is not included in base AB.

    Key idea

    Extend a side line to locate the height without extending the measured base.

  6. Problem 6 Choose the hypotenuse

    A right triangle has legs 7 cm and 24 cm, and hypotenuse 25 cm.

    Find its area, then find its altitude to the hypotenuse.

    Text description of this figure

    Schematic triangle, not drawn to scale. Base 24 cm, perpendicular height 7 cm.

    1. Hint 1

      One pair of perpendicular sides is already known.

    2. Hint 2

      After finding the area with the legs, use the hypotenuse as a new base.

    Answer

    A=84 cm2,h=16825 cm.\begin{aligned}A&=84\text{ cm}^2,\\h&=\frac{168}{25}\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}Ah​=84 cm2,=25168​ cm.​

    Here h is the altitude to the hypotenuse.

    Full solution

    The legs give the area directly.

    A=12⋅7⋅24A=\frac12\cdot7\cdot24A=21​⋅7⋅24 A=84 cm2.A=84\text{ cm}^2.A=84 cm2.

    Let h be the altitude to the 25 cm hypotenuse.

    The area stays the same when the base changes.

    84=12⋅25h84=\frac12\cdot25h84=21​⋅25h h=16825 cm.h=\frac{168}{25}\text{ cm}.h=25168​ cm.

    Answer

    A=84 cm2,h=16825 cm.\begin{aligned}A&=84\text{ cm}^2,\\h&=\frac{168}{25}\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}Ah​=84 cm2,=25168​ cm.​

    Here h is the altitude to the hypotenuse.

  7. Problem 7 Exact irrational lengths

    A rectangle has positive side lengths 5\sqrt55​ cm and 20\sqrt{20}20​ cm. Find its exact area. State whether finite rational-square tiling proves the rectangle formula for these side lengths in this lesson.

    Text description of this figure

    Schematic rectangle, not drawn to scale, with sides √5 cm and √20 cm.

    1. Hint 1

      Distinguish the proved rational case from the accepted extension.

    2. Hint 2

      Multiply the positive lengths and simplify the product of the square roots.

    Answer

    A=10 cm2.A=10\text{ cm}^2.A=10 cm2.

    The rectangle formula for these irrational lengths is accepted here; finite rational-square tiling does not prove this case.

    Full solution

    Both side lengths are irrational.

    Our finite rational tiling proof therefore does not establish the formula for this rectangle; we use the explicitly accepted extension.

    Multiply the side lengths.

    A=5⋅20A=\sqrt5\cdot\sqrt{20}A=5​⋅20​ A=100A=\sqrt{100}A=100​ A=10 cm2.A=10\text{ cm}^2.A=10 cm2.

    Answer

    A=10 cm2.A=10\text{ cm}^2.A=10 cm2.

    The rectangle formula for these irrational lengths is accepted here; finite rational-square tiling does not prove this case.

    Key idea

    State the accepted foundation when a proof does not cover the lengths being used.

    For base AC, let hhh be the perpendicular height from B.

    90=12⋅15h90=\frac12\cdot15h90=21​⋅15h h=12 cm.h=12\text{ cm}.h=12 cm.

    Answer

    A=90 cm2,h=12 cm.\begin{aligned}A&=90\text{ cm}^2,\\h&=12\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}Ah​=90 cm2,=12 cm.​

  10. Problem 10 Reconstruct the region

    Triangle ABC has horizontal base AB of length 12 units. The perpendicular CH has length 9 units, with H on AB and AH of length 3 units.

    Draw a congruent copy by a half turn about the midpoint of BC, naming the image of A point D. Describe a cut and translation that turns the paired region into a rectangle. Find the original triangle area and justify why this reconstruction gives it.

    Text description of this figure

    Schematic triangle ABC with base AB 12 units and perpendicular CH 9 units. The foot H is on side AB. The drawing is not to scale.

    1. Hint 1

      Track the images of both endpoints of each side under the half turn.

    2. Hint 2

      The paired region is a parallelogram. Its upper left corner is directly above H, which lies inside AB.

    3. Hint 3

      A perpendicular cut removes a corner triangle that fits on the other end after a translation by the base length.

    Answer

    The copies form parallelogram ABDC. Cut along CH and translate the corner triangle ACH by 12 units toward B to complete a 12 by 9 rectangle. The original triangle area is 545454 square units.

    Full solution

    The half turn interchanges B and C and sends A to D.

    It sends AB to parallel side DC and AC to parallel side DB, so the two congruent triangles fill parallelogram ABDC without interior overlap.

    Since AH is 3 units and AB is 12 units, the cut CH lies within the parallelogram.

    Translate triangle ACH 12 units along AB.

    Parallel sides make its horizontal width and perpendicular height match the corner missing on the right, so the pieces fill a rectangle with sides 12 and 9.

    Congruence preserves each moved piece area, and addition preserves their total.

    The paired region has area

    12⋅9=10812\cdot9=10812⋅9=108

    square units.

    The original triangle and its half-turn copy are congruent, so their areas are equal.

    The original therefore has area

    108÷2=54108\div2=54108÷2=54

    square units.

    Answer

    The copies form parallelogram ABDC. Cut along CH and translate the corner triangle ACH by 12 units toward B to complete a 12 by 9 rectangle. The original triangle area is 545454 square units.

    Key idea

    A reconstruction needs congruent pieces, no gaps or overlaps, and the correct total number of copies.
