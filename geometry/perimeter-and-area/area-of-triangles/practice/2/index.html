    A triangle has area 99 cm299\text{ cm}^299 cm2 and base 18 cm. What is its corresponding height?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The triangle formula includes a factor of one half. Double the area before dividing by the base.

    18h=198,h=11 cm.\begin{aligned} 18h&=198,\\ h&=11\text{ cm}. \end{aligned}18hh​=198,=11 cm.​
  2. 2

    A triangle has area 52.5 m252.5\text{ m}^252.5 m2 and perpendicular height 7 m. What is its matching base?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Recover base times height by doubling the area.

    7b=105,b=15 m.\begin{aligned} 7b&=105,\\ b&=15\text{ m}. \end{aligned}7bb​=105,=15 m.​
  3. 3

    A parallelogram has area 117 cm2117\text{ cm}^2117 cm2 and chosen base 13 cm. Find the matching height.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Parallelogram area is base times perpendicular height, without a factor of one half.

    h=117÷13,h=9 cm.\begin{aligned} h&=117\div13,\\ h&=9\text{ cm}. \end{aligned}hh​=117÷13,=9 cm.​

    A triangle has base 72\tfrac7227​ cm and perpendicular height 83\tfrac8338​ cm. What is its area?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Multiply the matching lengths, then take half.

    A=12⋅72⋅83,A=143 cm2.\begin{aligned} A&=\frac12\cdot\frac72\cdot\frac83,\\ A&=\frac{14}{3}\text{ cm}^2. \end{aligned}AA​=21​⋅27​⋅38​,=314​ cm2.​
  5. 5

    A right triangle has a 6 cm leg and a 10 cm hypotenuse. What is its area?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Use Pythagoras to find the other perpendicular leg.

    h2=102−62,h2=64.\begin{aligned} h^2&=10^2-6^2,\\ h^2&=64. \end{aligned}h2h2​=102−62,=64.​

    The positive leg length is 8 cm.

    A=12⋅6⋅8,A=24 cm2.\begin{aligned} A&=\frac12\cdot6\cdot8,\\ A&=24\text{ cm}^2. \end{aligned}AA​=21​⋅6⋅8,=24 cm2.​
  6. 6

    An isosceles triangle has equal sides of 17 cm and base 16 cm. Its height to that base is 15 cm. Which pair gives its area and perimeter?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Use the height for area.

    A=12⋅16⋅15,A=120 cm2.\begin{aligned} A&=\frac12\cdot16\cdot15,\\ A&=120\text{ cm}^2. \end{aligned}AA​=21​⋅16⋅15,=120 cm2.​

    Use the three side lengths for perimeter.

    P=17+17+16,P=50 cm.\begin{aligned} P&=17+17+16,\\ P&=50\text{ cm}. \end{aligned}PP​=17+17+16,=50 cm.​
  7. 7

    A triangle has a side of length 12 cm with corresponding height 12 cm. Another side has length 18 cm. What height matches the 18 cm side?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    First use the supplied matching base and height.

    A=12⋅12⋅12.A=\frac12\cdot12\cdot12.A=21​⋅12⋅12. A=72 cm2.A=72\text{ cm}^2.A=72 cm2.

    The same triangle area must result from the 18 cm side and its own height.

    18h=144.18h=144.18h=144. h=8 cm.h=8\text{ cm}.h=8 cm.
  8. 8

    A rectangle is 72\tfrac7227​ units by 53\tfrac5335​ units. Use squares of side 16\tfrac1661​ unit. How many tiles fill it, and what is the total area?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The side lengths contain 21 and 10 sixth-unit intervals. Thus there are 210 tiles, each with area one thirty-sixth square unit.

    A=210⋅136,A=356.\begin{aligned} A&=210\cdot\frac1{36},\\ A&=\frac{35}{6}. \end{aligned}AA​=210⋅361​,=635​.​
  9. 9

    Two congruent copies of a triangle form a parallelogram without gaps or interior overlaps. The parallelogram has base 17 cm and height 6 cm. What is the area of one triangle?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Congruence makes the two triangular areas equal. Their sum is the parallelogram area.

    A=17⋅62,A=51 cm2.\begin{aligned} A&=\frac{17\cdot6}{2},\\ A&=51\text{ cm}^2. \end{aligned}AA​=217⋅6​,=51 cm2.​
  10. 10

    For a chosen triangle base AB, what additional measurement determines the area?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A base must be paired with its perpendicular height from the opposite vertex. A side length, midpoint distance, or base extension does not by itself supply that height.

  11. 11

    Only two side lengths of a triangle, 13 cm and 14 cm, are given. What is needed to calculate its area by the base-height formula using the 14 cm side?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The 13 cm side need not be perpendicular to the 14 cm side. The missing information for this formula is the matching perpendicular height.

    A=12144,A=6 cm2.\begin{aligned} A&=\frac12\sqrt{144},\\ A&=6\text{ cm}^2. \end{aligned}AA​=21​144​,=6 cm2.​
