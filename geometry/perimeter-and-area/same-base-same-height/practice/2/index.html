    D lies inside BC with BD to DC equal to 3 to 5. Triangle ABC has area 104 square cm. What are the areas of ABD and ADC, respectively?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The eight base-ratio parts transfer to eight equal area parts. Each part is 104 divided by 8, or 13 square cm.

    AABD=3(13)AABD=39 cm2AADC=5(13)AADC=65 cm2.\begin{aligned}A_{ABD}&=3(13)\\A_{ABD}&=39\text{ cm}^2\\A_{ADC}&=5(13)\\A_{ADC}&=65\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}AABD​AABD​AADC​AADC​​=3(13)=39 cm2=5(13)=65 cm2.​

    Two triangles have equal perpendicular heights and equal areas. Their bases, in cm, are 2x+32x+32x+3 and 5x−95x-95x−9. What is x?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Equal heights and equal areas force equal bases.

    2x+3=5x−912=3xx=4.\begin{aligned}2x+3&=5x-9\\12&=3x\\x&=4.\end{aligned}2x+312x​=5x−9=3x=4.​

  4. 4

    Two triangles have equal included angles of 150 degrees. Their enclosing sides are 8 cm and 13 cm in the first, and 12 cm and 26 cm in the second. If the first area is 26 square cm, what is the second?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The obtuse angle still gives proportional perpendicular heights to the supporting base lines. Compare the products.

    A2A1=12(26)8(13)A2A1=3.\begin{aligned}\frac{A_2}{A_1}&=\frac{12(26)}{8(13)}\\\frac{A_2}{A_1}&=3.\end{aligned}A1​A2​​A1​A2​​​=8(13)12(26)​=3.​

    Therefore the second area is 3 times 26, or 78 square cm.

    In the median partition of ABC, small triangle GBD has area 11.5 square cm. What are the area of quadrilateral AEGF and the area of ABC, respectively?

    Two triangles have equal positive heights. Their bases are x+2x+2x+2 and 2x−12x-12x−1, in cm, and their ordered area ratio is 3 to 4. What is x?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Transfer the area ratio to the bases, then cross-multiply.

    x+22x−1=344x+8=6x−3x=5.5.\begin{aligned}\frac{x+2}{2x-1}&=\frac34\\4x+8&=6x-3\\x&=5.5.\end{aligned}2x−1x+2​4x+8x​=43​=6x−3=5.5.​

    The bases are then 7.5 cm and 10 cm, both positive.

    D lies inside BC. The ratio BD to DC is 11 to 6, and ABC has area 68 square cm. The perpendicular foot from A lies beyond C. What are the areas of ABD and ADC, respectively?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Both heights reach the same supporting line BC, so an outside foot does not change the ratio. There are 17 parts, each of area 4 square cm.

    AABD=11(4)AABD=44 cm2AADC=6(4)AADC=24 cm2.\begin{aligned}A_{ABD}&=11(4)\\A_{ABD}&=44\text{ cm}^2\\A_{ADC}&=6(4)\\A_{ADC}&=24\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}AABD​AABD​AADC​AADC​​=11(4)=44 cm2=6(4)=24 cm2.​

    Solution

  11. 11

    Two right triangles have enclosing perpendicular sides 6 cm and 22 cm in the first, and 8 cm and 11 cm in the second. What is the first-to-second area ratio?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Their equal included angles are right angles, so the two pairs of sides are base-height pairs. The factors one half cancel.

    A1A2=6(22)8(11)A1A2=32.\begin{aligned}\frac{A_1}{A_2}&=\frac{6(22)}{8(11)}\\\frac{A_1}{A_2}&=\frac32.\end{aligned}A2​A1​​A2​A1​​​=8(11)6(22)​=23​.​
