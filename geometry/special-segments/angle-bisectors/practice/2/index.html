  1. 1

    P lies on an internal bisector. Its perpendicular distances to the two side lines are (4x−3)(4x-3)(4x−3) cm and (2x+9)(2x+9)(2x+9) cm. Find their common length.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Set the perpendicular lengths equal.

    4x−3=2x+92x=12x=6.\begin{aligned}4x-3&=2x+9\\2x&=12\\x&=6.\end{aligned}4x−32xx​=2x+9=12=6.​

    Substitute into either expression.

    4(6)−3=21 cm.4(6)-3=21\text{ cm}.4(6)−3=21 cm.
  2. 2

    I is the incenter and IF is perpendicular to side AB. AI is 17 cm and AF is 15 cm. What is the inradius?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    AI is the hypotenuse of right triangle AFI.

    IF2=172−152IF2=64IF=8 cm.\begin{aligned}IF^2&=17^2-15^2\\IF^2&=64\\IF&=8\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}IF2IF2IF​=172−152=64=8 cm.​

    The positive perpendicular length IF is the inradius.

  3. 3

    I is the incenter of triangle ABC, with F the perpendicular foot on AB. Why does using radius IA fail to draw the incircle?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    In right triangle AFI, IA is the hypotenuse and IF is a leg. Radius IA would reach beyond side line AB rather than just reach its foot.

  4. 4

    P is inside angle UOV. Its perpendicular feet are X,Y and PX equals PY. Which congruence test applies directly once both feet are known to be on the forward rays?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The right triangles share hypotenuse OP and have equal perpendicular legs PX and PY. These are exactly the HL hypotheses.

  5. 5

    Which construction is sufficient to locate the incenter of a nondegenerate triangle?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Concurrency puts the intersection of two internal bisectors on the third one. One bisector leaves many possible points; perpendicular bisectors locate a different center.

  6. 6

    I is inside triangle ABC and lies on the internal bisectors at A and B. Its perpendicular distance to AB is 4.6 cm. What is its perpendicular distance to BC?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The bisector at B gives equal distances to lines BA and BC. The given distance to AB is therefore also the distance to BC.

  7. 7

    The first compass arc at O has radius 5 cm. The later arcs centered at U and V both have radius 7 cm and meet at X strictly inside the angle. Is OX an internal bisector?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    SSS uses three matching pairs.

    OU=OVUX=VXOX=OX.\begin{aligned}OU&=OV\\UX&=VX\\OX&=OX.\end{aligned}OUUXOX​=OV=VX=OX.​

    The first pair and second pair do not need to have the same length as each other.

  8. 8

    Triangle ABC has ∠A=52∘\angle A=52^\circ∠A=52∘ and ∠B=68∘\angle B=68^\circ∠B=68∘. I is its incenter. What is ∠ACI\angle ACI∠ACI?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    First use the triangle angle sum.

    ∠C=180∘−52∘−68∘∠C=60∘.\begin{aligned}\angle C&=180^\circ-52^\circ-68^\circ\\\angle C&=60^\circ.\end{aligned}∠C∠C​=180∘−52∘−68∘=60∘.​

    CI halves that angle.

    ∠ACI=30∘.\angle ACI=30^\circ.∠ACI=30∘.
  9. 9

    In triangle ABC, I is the incenter, ∠A=40∘\angle A=40^\circ∠A=40∘ and ∠B=80∘\angle B=80^\circ∠B=80∘. What is ∠AIB\angle AIB∠AIB?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Triangle AIB has angles 20 degrees at A and 40 degrees at B, because AI and BI are internal bisectors.

    ∠AIB=180∘−20∘−40∘∠AIB=120∘.\begin{aligned}\angle AIB&=180^\circ-20^\circ-40^\circ\\\angle AIB&=120^\circ.\end{aligned}∠AIB∠AIB​=180∘−20∘−40∘=120∘.​
  10. 10

    The first arc gives OU=OV. Later arcs use unequal radii and meet inside the angle at X. Which SSS equality is no longer guaranteed?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The unequal later settings need not give UX=VX. The original equal radii and the shared side still remain, but two matching side pairs do not establish SSS.

  11. 11

    For the incenter I, the perpendicular foot F to line AB lies on ray AB and also on ray BA. Where must F lie?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The overlap of ray AB with ray BA is segment AB. The acute half-angles at the vertices rule out endpoints, so the foot is strictly between A and B.

  12. 12

    Which statement completes the concurrency proof? I is interior, its distances to BC and CA are equal, so ...

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The two equal perpendicular distances concern the side lines of angle C, and I lies inside that angle. Its interior-point converse identifies the third internal bisector.
