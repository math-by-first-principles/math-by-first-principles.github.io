    M is the midpoint of AB, and PM is perpendicular to AB. AB is 20 cm long and PM is 24 cm long. Find PA.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Half of AB is 10 cm. Triangle PMA is right at M.

    PA2=102+242PA2=676PA=26 cm\begin{aligned} PA^2&=10^2+24^2\\ PA^2&=676\\ PA&=26\text{ cm} \end{aligned}PA2PA2PA​=102+242=676=26 cm​

  4. 4

    Triangle ABC is right at A. Its circumradius is 5 cm, and leg AC is 6 cm. Find leg AB.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The hypotenuse is twice the circumradius, so it is 10 cm. Use the known leg and hypotenuse in Pythagoras.

    AB2=102−62AB2=64AB=8 cm\begin{aligned} AB^2&=10^2-6^2\\ AB^2&=64\\ AB&=8\text{ cm} \end{aligned}AB2AB2AB​=102−62=64=8 cm​
  5. 5

    A and B are 14 cm apart. Circles centered at A and B each have radius 25 cm and meet at two distinct points X and Y. What is the length of XY?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Let M be the midpoint of AB. Both compass crossings lie on its perpendicular bisector, on opposite sides of AB. Half the segment is 7 cm, and AX is a 25 cm radius. Pythagoras in right triangle AMX gives

    MX2=252−72MX2=576MX=24 cm.\begin{aligned} MX^2&=25^2-7^2\\ MX^2&=576\\ MX&=24\text{ cm}. \end{aligned}MX2MX2MX​=252−72=576=24 cm.​

    The same calculation in triangle AMY gives a midpoint-to-crossing distance of 24 cm on the other side of AB. Add the two parts.

    XY=24+24XY=48 cm.\begin{aligned} XY&=24+24\\ XY&=48\text{ cm}. \end{aligned}XYXY​=24+24=48 cm.​
  6. 6

    Equal-radius circles centered at A and B meet at distinct points X and Y. Why does joining X and Y construct the perpendicular bisector of AB?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Both X and Y are equally far from A and B because the circles have equal radii. Both therefore lie on the perpendicular bisector, and their joining line is that line.

  7. 7

    A perpendicular is required at P on line l. Which first step supplies endpoints whose midpoint is P?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A circle centered at P gives equal distances to its two intersections with the line. Since those intersections lie on opposite sides, P is their midpoint.

  8. 8

    P is off a line. A circle centered at P touches the line once at T. Why must its radius be increased for the two-crossing perpendicular construction?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    With only T there is no nonzero segment AB whose perpendicular bisector can be used. Increasing the radius produces two distinct line intersections.

  9. 9

    A triangle has angles 38 degrees, 57 degrees, and 85 degrees. Where is its circumcenter?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    All three angles are acute. Applying the midpoint-parallel location argument to each side puts the center in all three interior half-planes.

  10. 10

    A line passes through the midpoint of AB and meets AB at 50 degrees. Which description is correct?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Passing through the midpoint gives segment bisection. The 50 degree angle fails perpendicularity, which requires a right angle.

  11. 11

    One given nonidentity rotation supplies two nonzero, nonparallel point-image chords. Which construction locates its center?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Each chord bisector contains the given rotation center by equidistance. Nonparallel chords produce nonparallel bisectors, so they have one intersection.

  12. 12

    To construct a circumcircle, two side perpendicular bisectors meet at O. Which compass setting is correct?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The concurrency proof makes all three center-to-vertex distances equal. Using any one of them as the radius draws the circle through every vertex.
