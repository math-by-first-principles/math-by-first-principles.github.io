Perpendicular Bisectors and the Circumcenter: Practice

12 multiple-choice questions, progressively harder.

Level 1 · Foundational 0 / 12 answered
Question 1 of 12
  1. 1

    What defines the perpendicular bisector of a nonzero segment?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Both conditions are required: the line passes through the midpoint and meets the segment at a right angle.

  2. 2

    M is the midpoint of AB, and PM is perpendicular to AB. Which statement follows?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    P lies on the perpendicular bisector of AB, so the forward theorem gives equal distances to A and B.

  3. 3

    Q is equally far from distinct points A and B. Where must Q lie?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The converse places every point with equal endpoint distances on the perpendicular bisector. Q can be the midpoint or a point off AB.

  4. 4

    A segment is 18 cm long. How far is its midpoint from each endpoint?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A midpoint divides the segment into two equal lengths.

    18÷2=9 cm18\div2=9\text{ cm}18÷2=9 cm
  5. 5

    P lies on the perpendicular bisector of AB. PA is 16 cm. What is PB?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The two endpoint distances are equal by the forward theorem. Therefore PB is 16 cm.

  6. 6

    Equal-radius circles centered at A and B are used to construct the perpendicular bisector of a 10 cm segment. Which radius gives two distinct crossings?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The radius must be greater than half the endpoint separation.

    10÷2=5 cm10\div2=5\text{ cm}10÷2=5 cm

    Only 6 cm is greater than 5 cm.

  7. 7

    What does a locus describe?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A locus contains every point satisfying the condition and excludes every point that fails it.

  8. 8

    Which triangle lines meet at the circumcenter?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The concurrency theorem proved in this lesson identifies the common point of the three side perpendicular bisectors.

  9. 9

    Which distances are guaranteed equal at a triangle circumcenter O?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The circumcenter is equally far from all three vertices. Equal distances to side lines describe a different condition.

  10. 10

    Where is the circumcenter of a right triangle?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The right-case midpoint-parallel argument puts the center at the hypotenuse midpoint.

  11. 11

    What is the circumcircle of a nondegenerate triangle?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A circumcircle has the circumcenter as its center and one common vertex distance as its radius.

  12. 12

    For one given rotation, A maps to a different point A prime. Why does the center lie on the perpendicular bisector of their chord?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Rotation preserves distance from its center. The converse turns those equal distances into membership of the chord bisector.
