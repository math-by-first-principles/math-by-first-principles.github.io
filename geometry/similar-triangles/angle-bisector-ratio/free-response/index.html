  4. Problem 4 Choose the actual bisector intersection

    AB=15AC=24BC=26.\begin{aligned}AB&=15\\AC&=24\\BC&=26.\end{aligned}ABACBC​=15=24=26.​

    Candidate D2D_2D2​ gives 11/1511/1511/15, which differs from 5/85/85/8.

    Answer

  6. Problem 6 Explain the auxiliary equal length

      Transfer the two half-angle equalities to E,C, then identify the first and leftover pieces on BE.

    Answer

    AE=AC,=25.\begin{aligned}AE&=AC,\\&=25.\end{aligned}AE​=AC,=25.​

    The actual bisector makes the two half-angles equal.

    AE=AC,=25.\begin{aligned}AE&=AC,\\&=25.\end{aligned}AE​=AC,=25.​

    In triangle BECBECBEC, the cut ADADAD is parallel to ECECEC, with both cut points interior.
