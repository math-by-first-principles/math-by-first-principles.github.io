The Angle-Bisector Ratio Theorem: Practice

12 multiple-choice questions, progressively harder.

Level 1 · Foundational 0 / 12 answered
Question 1 of 12
  1. 1

    In nondegenerate triangle ABC, AD internally bisects angle BAC and D is strictly between B,C. Which equality follows?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The opposite-side part beside B matches AB; the part beside C matches AC. Both ratios must keep that endpoint order.

  2. 2

    What does it mean for AD to be the internal bisector of angle BAC in a nondegenerate triangle?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Internal refers to the ray inside angle BAC. Its intersection with the opposite side is interior, and the ray makes two equal angles at A.

  3. 3

    AD is an internal bisector and AB:AC=5:11.AB:AC=5:11.AB:AC=5:11. What is BD:DC?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Use the same B-then-C order: BD:DC equals AB:AC. The whole-side fraction would be 5/16, which is a different comparison.

  4. 4

    AD is an internal bisector and AB=AC.AB=AC.AB=AC. What follows for D?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The adjacent-side ratio is 1, so BD/DC=1.BD/DC=1.BD/DC=1. Since D is interior, equal parts make it the midpoint.

  5. 5

    An internal bisector divides an angle measuring 74∘74^\circ74∘. What is each half-angle measure?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The definition halves the angle measure.

    74∘÷2=37∘.74^\circ\div 2=37^\circ.74∘÷2=37∘.
  6. 6

    Which lengths does this ratio theorem compare?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    BD and DC lie along the opposite side; AB and AC are the two adjacent side lengths. No perpendicular-distance theorem is used.

  7. 7

    In the parallel proof, which line is drawn through C?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The parallel through C meets BA extended at E. Its angle pairs produce an isosceles triangle and a parallel cut in triangle BEC.

  8. 8

    In that auxiliary construction, which point order on the extended side is correct?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    E lies on BA extended beyond A. This makes A an interior point of BE when side-splitter is used in triangle BEC.

  9. 9

    Why does the proof establish AE=ACAE=ACAE=AC?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Parallel-line angle pairs transfer the equal half-angles at A to E and C. The earlier isosceles converse gives their opposite sides equal.

  10. 10

    D is strictly between B,C and BD/DC=AB/ACBD/DC=AB/ACBD/DC=AB/AC in a nondegenerate triangle. What does the converse conclude?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The interior point and matching adjacent-side ratio are exactly the hypotheses of the angle-bisector ratio converse.

  11. 11

    If AD internally bisects angle BAC, which reciprocal form is valid?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Taking the reciprocal on both sides preserves equality. Reversing only one ratio generally does not.

  12. 12

    Why is AB/AC not automatically a dilation scale factor here?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A dilation scale factor compares an image length with its corresponding original length. AB and AC are adjacent sides of a single triangle.
