  1. 1

    AD is an internal bisector and BD/BC=7/18.BD/BC=7/18.BD/BC=7/18. What is AB:AC?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The whole has eighteen ratio parts; BD uses seven and DC uses eleven. The adjacent-side ratio equals the part-to-part ratio 7:11.

    Solution

    For a whole L and ratio r, the first part is uniquely determined.

    AB=12AC=17\begin{aligned}AB&=12\\AC&=17\end{aligned}ABAC​=12=17​

    in a triangle with internal bisector AD. Why is the proposed ordered similarity ABD to ACD impossible?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    In this proposed matching A goes to A, B to C and D to D. Corresponding side ratios must agree, but AC/AB=17/12AC/AB=17/12AC/AB=17/12 differs from AD/AD=1.AD/AD=1.AD/AD=1. Equal half-angles alone do not supply similarity.

    A record lists

    AB=6AC=14BC=7\begin{aligned}AB&=6\\AC&=14\\BC&=7\end{aligned}ABACBC​=6=14=7​

    and positive side parts in ratio 3:7. Can these measurements describe a nondegenerate triangle with an internal bisector?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A proportional division cannot repair impossible outer side lengths.

    6+7=13<14.6+7=13<14.6+7=13<14.

    The longest listed side exceeds the sum of the other two.

    For an internal bisector,

    BD=5DC=8.\begin{aligned}BD&=5\\DC&=8.\end{aligned}BDDC​=5=8.​

    If AC exceeds AB by 9, find AB.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The adjacent sides have five and eight ratio parts. Their difference is three parts.

    one part=9÷3,=3,AB=5⋅3,=15.\begin{aligned}\text{one part}&=9\div 3,\\&=3,\\AB&=5\cdot 3,\\&=15.\end{aligned}one partAB​=9÷3,=3,=5⋅3,=15.​
  7. 7

    BE internally bisects angle ABC and E is strictly between A,C. Which ordered ratio is correct?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The part next to A pairs with BA, while the part next to C pairs with BC. Changing the bisected vertex changes which side is opposite, but not the endpoint-matching rule.

  8. 8

    A proposed use of the converse takes D beyond C on line BC. What must be checked before applying this lesson’s converse?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The ordinary internal converse here assumes an interior division of the opposite side. An extension point does not satisfy that hypothesis, so this converse cannot be cited.

  9. 9

    In a nondegenerate triangle, an internal bisector has

    AB=pAC=qBC=L.\begin{aligned}AB&=p\\AC&=q\\BC&=L.\end{aligned}ABACBC​=p=q=L.​

    Which formula gives BD?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The whole side has p+q ratio parts. BD occupies the p-part share, giving pL/(p+q); the denominator q alone belongs to a part-to-part ratio.

  10. 10

    An internal bisector AD meets BC at its midpoint. Which reasoning proves AB=ACAB=ACAB=AC?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The midpoint gives equal positive parts. The direct angle-bisector ratio theorem then makes the adjacent sides equal; no additional triangle-center result is used.

  11. 11

    In the proof, B,A,E are collinear in order, D is interior to BC, and AD is parallel to EC. Which ordered AA similarity follows from the parallel cut?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    B is the shared vertex, A matches E along BE, and D matches C along BC. The order BAD therefore matches BEC.

  12. 12

    An explorer reports BD/DC≈0.734 and AB/AC≈0.734. What does that observation establish?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Measurements and rounded displays are useful evidence. The auxiliary parallel, equal-angle argument and side-splitter establish the exact theorem for every permitted triangle.
