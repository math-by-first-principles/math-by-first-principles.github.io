    AD internally bisects angle BAC. If

    AB=10AC=18BD=6\begin{aligned}AB&=10\\AC&=18\\BD&=6\end{aligned}ABACBD​=10=18=6​

    , find DC.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Pair BD with AB and DC with AC.

    6DC=1018,DC=10.8.\begin{aligned}\frac 6{DC}&=\frac{10}{18},\\DC&=10.8.\end{aligned}DC6​DC​=1810​,=10.8.​

    AB=14AC=25BC=31.2.\begin{aligned}AB&=14\\AC&=25\\BC&=31.2.\end{aligned}ABACBC​=14=25=31.2.​

    AD is the internal bisector. What is BD?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The whole base contains 14+25 ratio parts; BD uses fourteen of them.

    BD=31.2⋅1439,=11.2.\begin{aligned}BD&=31.2\cdot\frac{14}{39},\\&=11.2.\end{aligned}BD​=31.2⋅3914​,=11.2.​

  3. 3

    An internal bisector gives

    BD=8DC=12.\begin{aligned}BD&=8\\DC&=12.\end{aligned}BDDC​=8=12.​

    If AC=27AC=27AC=27, find AB.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The opposite-side parts have ratio two to three, so AB is two thirds of AC.

    AB=27⋅812,=18.\begin{aligned}AB&=27\cdot\frac 8{12},\\&=18.\end{aligned}AB​=27⋅128​,=18.​

    AB=16AC=22BD=12DC=16.5\begin{aligned}AB&=16\\AC&=22\\BD&=12\\DC&=16.5\end{aligned}ABACBDDC​=16=22=12=16.5​

    , what is justified?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Compare the ratios in the same order.

    1216.5=811,1622=811.\begin{aligned}\frac{12}{16.5}&=\frac 8{11},\\\frac{16}{22}&=\frac 8{11}.\end{aligned}16.512​2216​​=118​,=118​.​

    The interior hypothesis allows the converse.

  6. 6

    AD internally bisects angle BAC, with

    AB=3x+1AC=4x+2BD=5DC=7.\begin{aligned}AB&=3x+1\\AC&=4x+2\\BD&=5\\DC&=7.\end{aligned}ABACBDDC​=3x+1=4x+2=5=7.​

    Find x.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Use the fixed endpoint order before cross-multiplication.

    7(3x+1)=5(4x+2),21x+7=20x+10,x=3.\begin{aligned}7(3x+1)&=5(4x+2),\\21x+7&=20x+10,\\x&=3.\end{aligned}7(3x+1)21x+7x​=5(4x+2),=20x+10,=3.​

    The resulting side lengths are 101010, 141414, and 121212, which satisfy the triangle inequality.

    Solution

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

  9. 9

    AD internally bisects angle BAC and D is the midpoint of BC. If

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

  10. 10

    In the auxiliary parallel proof,

    AB=9AC=16BC=20.\begin{aligned}AB&=9\\AC&=16\\BC&=20.\end{aligned}ABACBC​=9=16=20.​

    CE is parallel to the internal bisector AD, and B,A,E are in order. Find AE.

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Equal parallel-line half-angle pairs make triangle AEC isosceles. Thus AE equals AC, which is 16; BE would instead be 9+16=25.

  12. 12

    AD is an internal bisector. If AB is shorter than AC, what follows?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The positive ratio AB/AC is less than 1, so BD/DC is less than 1. The theorem preserves the comparison between the paired lengths.
