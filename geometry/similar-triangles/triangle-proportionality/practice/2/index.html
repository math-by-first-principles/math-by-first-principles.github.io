    D,E are interior side points in triangle ABC and DE is parallel to BC. If

    AD=6DB=11AE=9\begin{aligned}AD&=6\\DB&=11\\AE&=9\end{aligned}ADDBAE​=6=11=9​

    , find EC.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Use first part over leftover part on both sides.

    611=9EC,EC=16.5.\begin{aligned}\frac 6{11}&=\frac 9{EC},\\EC&=16.5.\end{aligned}116​EC​=EC9​,=16.5.​
  2. 2

    In a parallel triangle cut,

    AD=5AB=18.\begin{aligned}AD&=5\\AB&=18.\end{aligned}ADAB​=5=18.​

    If AC=27AC=27AC=27, find AE.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Use part-to-whole on both sides.

    AE27=518,AE=7.5.\begin{aligned}\frac{AE}{27}&=\frac 5{18},\\AE&=7.5.\end{aligned}27AE​AE​=185​,=7.5.​
  3. 3

    D lies between A,B and E between A,C. The lengths are

    AD=8DB=14AE=12EC=21.\begin{aligned}AD&=8\\DB&=14\\AE&=12\\EC&=21.\end{aligned}ADDBAEEC​=8=14=12=21.​

    What follows?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Both part-to-part ratios reduce to four sevenths.

    814=47,1221=47.\begin{aligned}\frac 8{14}&=\frac 47,\\\frac{12}{21}&=\frac 47.\end{aligned}148​2112​​=74​,=74​.​

    The nondegenerate triangle and interior conditions allow the side-splitter converse.

  4. 4

    D and E are midpoints of AB and AC. If

    BC=6x−2DE=4x−6\begin{aligned}BC&=6x-2\\DE&=4x-6\end{aligned}BCDE​=6x−2=4x−6​

    , find x.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The midsegment length is half the third side.

    4x−6=12(6x−2),4x−6=3x−1,x=5.\begin{aligned}4x-6&=\tfrac 12(6x-2),\\4x-6&=3x-1,\\x&=5.\end{aligned}4x−64x−6x​=21​(6x−2),=3x−1,=5.​

    The resulting lengths are 14 and 28, both positive.

  5. 5

    The outer triangle has

    AB=18AC=26BC=30.\begin{aligned}AB&=18\\AC&=26\\BC&=30.\end{aligned}ABACBC​=18=26=30.​

    D,E,F are the midpoints of AB,AC,BC. Which list gives DE,EF,FD in that order?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    DE matches BC, EF matches AB, and FD matches AC. Halve each corresponding outer length.

    DE=15,EF=9,FD=13.\begin{aligned}DE&=15,\\EF&=9,\\FD&=13.\end{aligned}DEEFFD​=15,=9,=13.​
  6. 6

    Three parallel cross-lines cut two transversals at A,B,C and P,Q,R in order. If

    AB=5BC=9PQ=8\begin{aligned}AB&=5\\BC&=9\\PQ&=8\end{aligned}ABBCPQ​=5=9=8​

    , find QR.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The second-to-first transversal factor is eight fifths for each corresponding gap.

    QR=85⋅9,=14.4.\begin{aligned}QR&=\frac 85\cdot 9,\\&=14.4.\end{aligned}QR​=58​⋅9,=14.4.​

  7. 7

    The two transversals themselves are parallel. They meet three parallel cross-lines at A,B,C and P,Q,R in order. If

    AB=4BC=7\begin{aligned}AB&=4\\BC&=7\end{aligned}ABBC​=4=7​

    , what are PQ and QR?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    ASA congruence in each strip gives equality of the corresponding intercepted lengths.

    PQ=4,QR=7.\begin{aligned}PQ&=4,\\QR&=7.\end{aligned}PQQR​=4,=7.​
  8. 8

    To construct AX:XB=3:5AX:XB=3:5AX:XB=3:5 with equal auxiliary steps, how many total steps are needed and which step gets the parallel?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The first part uses three steps and the leftover part five, so the whole auxiliary length needs eight. The parallel through the third step transfers the first three parts to AX.

  9. 9

    A segment is divided into six equal parts by the auxiliary-ray construction. What ratio does the third division point make with the two parts of the segment?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The third of six equal parts leaves three equal parts on the other side.

    3:(6−3)=3:3,=1:1.\begin{aligned}3:(6-3)&=3:3,\\&=1:1.\end{aligned}3:(6−3)​=3:3,=1:1.​

  10. 10

    Two supplied positive lengths have ratio 4:9. The construction transfers that ratio to AX:XB on a segment AB of length 39. Find AX.

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The whole has thirteen ratio parts, and AX uses four of them.

    AX=413⋅39,=12.\begin{aligned}AX&=\frac 4{13}\cdot 39,\\&=12.\end{aligned}AX​=134​⋅39,=12.​

  11. 11

    D is interior to AB, but E lies beyond C on ray AC. Before using the ordinary side-splitter converse from this lesson, which hypothesis fails?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The ordinary converse used here requires both points to lie inside their named sides. E beyond C does not meet that hypothesis.

  12. 12

    An interior triangle cut has

    AD:DB=5:7AE=15.\begin{aligned}AD:DB&=5:7\\AE&=15.\end{aligned}AD:DBAE​=5:7=15.​

    What is the whole length AC if DE is parallel to BC?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    AE is five of the twelve parts of the whole AC.

    AC=15⋅125,=36.\begin{aligned}AC&=15\cdot\frac{12}{5},\\&=36.\end{aligned}AC​=15⋅512​,=36.​

    The leftover EC would be 21; that is not the requested whole length.
