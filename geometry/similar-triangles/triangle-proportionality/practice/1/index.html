    In nondegenerate triangle ABCABCABC, DDD lies between A,BA,BA,B, EEE between A,CA,CA,C, and DE∥BCDE\parallel BCDE∥BC. Which part-to-part equality follows?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The side-splitter theorem compares the first part with the leftover part on both sides. Thus AD/DB equals AE/EC.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    The whole side contains both ratio parts.

    ADAB=22+9,=211.\begin{aligned}\frac{AD}{AB}&=\frac{2}{2+9},\\&=\frac 2{11}.\end{aligned}ABAD​​=2+92​,=112​.​

  3. 3

    For the ordinary side-splitter converse in a nondegenerate triangle ABC with AD/DB=AE/ECAD/DB=AE/ECAD/DB=AE/EC, which point-location condition is required?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The converse in this lesson concerns a nondegenerate triangle with D between A and B and E between A and C. The positive interior division ratios then determine a unique parallel.

  4. 4

    A segment joins the midpoints of two sides of a triangle. Which pair of conclusions is guaranteed?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The midsegment theorem supplies both direction and length. It is parallel to the third side and has half its length.

  5. 5

    A triangle has third side length 383838 cm. What is the length of the midsegment parallel to it?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A midsegment is half its parallel side.

    12⋅38=19 cm.\frac 12\cdot 38=19\text{ cm}.21​⋅38=19 cm.
  6. 6

    D,E,F are the midpoints of AB,AC,BCAB,AC,BCAB,AC,BC respectively. Which ordered statement always gives the natural central-to-outer matching?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    DE matches CB, EF matches BA, and FD matches AC. Their shared endpoints give D to C, E to B, and F to A.

  7. 7

    Three parallel cross-lines meet transversals in corresponding order A,B,CA,B,CA,B,C and P,Q,RP,Q,RP,Q,R. Which equality follows?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    AB and PQ are the gaps between the first two cross-lines. BC and QR are the gaps between the last two. Compare them in the same order.

  8. 8

    Why must the compass opening stay fixed when marking equal auxiliary steps?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Each step is copied with the same compass opening, so all auxiliary step lengths are equal. The opening can be any convenient positive length.

  9. 9

    A construction marks nine equal auxiliary steps and joins the last point to B. The parallel through step 4 meets AB at X. What is AX:XB?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Four steps belong to the first part and the remaining five to the leftover part.

    AX:XB=4:(9−4),=4:5.\begin{aligned}AX:XB&=4:(9-4),\\&=4:5.\end{aligned}AX:XB​=4:(9−4),=4:5.​

  10. 10

    Which tools are enough for the exact segment-division construction taught here?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The compass copies equal or supplied lengths; angle copying constructs parallels. The unmarked straightedge draws the required lines without measuring a quotient.

  11. 11

    A parallel to BC through the midpoint D of AB meets AC at E in a nondegenerate triangle. What follows?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The division AD:DB is 1:1. The direct side-splitter theorem gives AE:EC=1:1AE:EC=1:1AE:EC=1:1, so E is the midpoint of AC.

  12. 12

    To construct a ratio from two supplied positive segment lengths a and b, what is copied consecutively on the auxiliary ray?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Copy the supplied segments in order, join the last point V to B, and draw the parallel through U. Side-splitter transfers AU:UV to AX:XB.
