    A dilation followed by earlier congruence establishes similarity without assuming the side ratios that are still to be proved.

  10. Problem 10 Two conclusions from one configuration

    In nondegenerate triangle ABC, D lies strictly between B and C and E lies strictly between B and A.

    The outer side lengths are

    AB=30AC=42BC=36\begin{gathered}AB=30\\AC=42\\BC=36\end{gathered}AB=30AC=42BC=36​

    The side parts are

    BD=15DC=21BE=12.5EA=17.5\begin{gathered}BD=15\\DC=21\\BE=12.5\\EA=17.5\end{gathered}BD=15DC=21BE=12.5EA=17.5​

    (a) Prove that AD internally bisects angle BAC.

    (b) Prove that DE is parallel to AC.

    Explain which two kinds of segment ratio support the two conclusions, even though both comparisons reduce to the same numerical value.

    Text description of this figure

    The outer triangle is ABC. D is an interior point of BC and E an interior point of BA. Segment AD and segment DE have been drawn, but no angle halves are marked equal and no parallel arrows appear. The given side-part and outer-side lengths are in the statement. The schematic drawing leaves both requested conclusions to be established.

    1. Hint 1

      For part (a), compare the parts of BC with the adjacent sides AB and AC in the same endpoint order.

    2. Hint 2

      For part (b), regard B as the common vertex of the whole triangle and compare the interior divisions on BC and BA.

    3. Hint 3

      Distinguish an opposite-side-to-adjacent-side comparison from a comparison of the two divided sides.

    Answer

    (a) AD internally bisects angle BAC. (b)

    DE∥AC\begin{gathered}DE\parallel AC\end{gathered}DE∥AC​

    The first conclusion compares opposite-side parts with adjacent sides; the second compares first and leftover parts on BC and BA.

    Full solution

    (a) Compare the opposite-side parts with the adjacent sides.

    BDDC=1521BDDC=57ABAC=3042ABAC=57\begin{gathered}\frac{BD}{DC}=\frac{15}{21}\\\frac{BD}{DC}=\frac57\\\frac{AB}{AC}=\frac{30}{42}\\\frac{AB}{AC}=\frac57\end{gathered}DCBD​=2115​DCBD​=75​ACAB​=4230​ACAB​=75​​

    The ratios agree, D is interior to BC, and the outer triangle is nondegenerate.

    The angle-bisector ratio converse therefore proves that AD internally bisects angle BAC.

    (b) View the whole triangle in the order BCA.

    D is inside BC and E is inside BA.

    Their first-part-to-leftover ratios are

    BDDC=57BEEA=12.517.5BEEA=57\begin{gathered}\frac{BD}{DC}=\frac57\\\frac{BE}{EA}=\frac{12.5}{17.5}\\\frac{BE}{EA}=\frac57\end{gathered}DCBD​=75​EABE​=17.512.5​EABE​=75​​

    The two divided-side ratios agree, so the side-splitter converse gives

    DE∥CA\begin{gathered}DE\parallel CA\end{gathered}DE∥CA​

    The first comparison uses the adjacent whole sides AB and AC to identify an internal angle bisector.

    The second uses the interior divisions of BC and BA to identify a parallel cut.

    Equal numerical ratios do not make those two theorem hypotheses interchangeable.

    Answer

    Choose a converse by the geometric roles of its ratios, retaining every interior-point and nondegeneracy condition.
