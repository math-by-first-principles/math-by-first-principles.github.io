  3. Problem 3 Recover an unstated correspondence

  5. Problem 5 Compare and complete the side data

    Two triangles have side lengths 8,13,168, 13, 168,13,16 and 12,19.5,2512, 19.5, 2512,19.5,25, with no correspondence specified.

    (a) Decide whether the original pair is similar, considering all possible correspondences.

    (b) Keep the lengths 121212 and 19.519.519.5 in the second triangle. Replace only its length 252525 so that the triangles become similar.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare the sorted side lengths; a positive factor cannot reverse their size order.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the common factor from the first two size ranks to find the required third length.

    Answer

    Part (a). They are not similar. Part (b). Replace 252525 by 242424; the factor is 1.51.51.5.

    Full solution

    (a) The positive triples both form triangles.

    Sorted side matching is necessary for any similarity.

    The first two ratios are

    12/8=1.519.5/13=1.5\begin{aligned}12/8&=1.5\\19.5/13&=1.5\end{aligned}12/819.5/13​=1.5=1.5​

    , but 25/1625/1625/16 differs from 1.51.51.5.

    Thus no correspondence works.

    (b) The retained side pairs require factor 1.51.51.5.

    The third side must therefore be

    1.5⋅16=24.1.5\cdot 16=24.1.5⋅16=24.

    The repaired second triple 12,19.5,2412, 19.5, 2412,19.5,24 satisfies the triangle inequality and all three ratios are 1.51.51.5, so SSS now establishes similarity.

    Answer

    Part (a). They are not similar. Part (b). Replace 252525 by 242424; the factor is 1.51.51.5.

    Key idea

    Repair a failed SSS test by enforcing the same factor on the remaining side.

  6. Problem 6 Determine the algebraic side condition
