  1. 1

    Under a valid simultaneous shadow model, a vertical staff of height 1.25 m has its own 0.750 m shadow. A vertical tree's own shadow is 12.0 m. Estimate its height.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Right base angles and equal parallel-ray tip angles give AA. Match height with height and own shadow with own shadow.

    H=12.0⋅1.250.750,=20.0 m.\begin{aligned}H&=12.0\cdot\frac{1.25}{0.750},\\&=20.0\text{ m}.\end{aligned}H​=12.0⋅0.7501.25​,=20.0 m.​

  3. 3

    A horizontal mirror lies on level ground between observer and vertical object. Equal-angle reflection applies. Eye height is 1.50 m; the observer-to-mirror ground leg is 2.5 m and the object-to-mirror ground leg 14.0 m. Find object height.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The right angles and equal mirror angles give AA. Object height over eye height equals object ground leg over observer ground leg.

    H=1.50⋅14.02.5,=8.4 m.\begin{aligned}H&=1.50\cdot\frac{14.0}{2.5},\\&=8.4\text{ m}.\end{aligned}H​=1.50⋅2.514.0​,=8.4 m.​

  4. 4

    In a valid horizontal-mirror model, the object is 18.0 m tall, eye height is 1.65 m, and object-to-mirror ground distance is 24.0 m. Find the observer-to-mirror ground distance.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    AA matches vertical heights and ground legs. Keep the observer triangle first in both ratios.

    d=24.0⋅1.6518.0,=2.2 m.\begin{aligned}d&=24.0\cdot\frac{1.65}{18.0},\\&=2.2\text{ m}.\end{aligned}d​=24.0⋅18.01.65​,=2.2 m.​

  5. 5

    A,B,C are collinear in order; T,B,D are collinear in order. AT and CD are perpendicular to bank AC. If

    AB=30.0 mBC=18.0 mCD=10.2 m\begin{aligned}AB&=30.0\text{ m}\\BC&=18.0\text{ m}\\CD&=10.2\text{ m}\end{aligned}ABBCCD​=30.0 m=18.0 m=10.2 m​

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Right angles at A,C and vertical angles at B give AA. AT matches CD and AB matches CB.

    AT=10.2⋅30.018.0,=17.0 m.\begin{aligned}AT&=10.2\cdot\frac{30.0}{18.0},\\&=17.0\text{ m}.\end{aligned}AT​=10.2⋅18.030.0​,=17.0 m.​

  8. 8

    A similar enlargement multiplies each corresponding edge by 1.75. Its original perimeter is 28 cm. Find the image perimeter.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Adding the corresponding scaled edges multiplies the whole boundary sum by 1.75.

    Pimage=1.75⋅28,=49 cm.\begin{aligned}P_{\rm image}&=1.75\cdot 28,\\&=49\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}Pimage​​=1.75⋅28,=49 cm.​

    Two triangular signs are similar. A side of 8 cm on the original matches 11 cm on the image. The original perimeter is 40 cm. Find the image perimeter.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Pimage=40⋅118,=55 cm.\begin{aligned}P_{\rm image}&=40\cdot\frac{11}{8},\\&=55\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}Pimage​​=40⋅811​,=55 cm.​

    A uniform plan scale is 1:750. A real gate is 2.7 m long. Find its drawing length in millimeters.

    Solution
