  1. 1

    A dilation has center O(1,−2)O(1,-2)O(1,−2) and scale factor 12\frac 1221​. Find the image of P(5,4)P(5, 4)P(5,4).

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    From the center to P, the displacement is four right and six up. Halve it and add the center back.

    x′=1+12(5−1),=3,y′=−2+12(4+2),=1.\begin{aligned}x'&=1+\tfrac 12(5-1),\\&=3,\\y'&=-2+\tfrac 12(4+2),\\&=1.\end{aligned}x′y′​=1+21​(5−1),=3,=−2+21​(4+2),=1.​
  2. 2

    A dilation has scale factor 1.81.81.8. The image of a segment has length 12.612.612.6 cm. What is the original segment length?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The image length equals the factor times the original length. Undo the multiplication.

    L=12.61.8,=7 cm.\begin{aligned}L&=\frac{12.6}{1.8},\\&=7\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}L​=1.812.6​,=7 cm.​

  3. 3

    A dilation centered at the origin has scale factor 34\frac 3443​. What is the image of the horizontal line y=4y=4y=4?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Every point on the original line has vertical coordinate four. Its image vertical coordinate is therefore three.

    y′=34⋅4,=3.\begin{aligned}y'&=\frac 34\cdot 4,\\&=3.\end{aligned}y′​=43​⋅4,=3.​

    The image horizontal line contains all real horizontal coordinates, so it is the entire line y = 3.

  4. 4

    A dilation has center (−2,3)(-2, 3)(−2,3) and scale factor 1.51.51.5. Where does (2,−1)(2,-1)(2,−1) map?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Scale the displacements from the center, not the raw point coordinates.

    x′=−2+1.5(2+2),=4,y′=3+1.5(−1−3),=−3.\begin{aligned}x'&=-2+1.5(2+2),\\&=4,\\y'&=3+1.5(-1-3),\\&=-3.\end{aligned}x′y′​=−2+1.5(2+2),=4,=3+1.5(−1−3),=−3.​
  5. 5

    Line mmm passes through a dilation's center OOO. The factor is 222. Which statement is correct?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The two rays of the line from O map onto themselves. Individual positive distances double, so only O stays in its original location.

    Solution

  7. 7

    Points O,P,P′O,P,P'O,P,P′ occur in that order on one ray. A dilation centered at OOO sends PPP to P′P'P′. If

    OP=4PP′=6\begin{aligned}OP&=4\\PP'&=6\end{aligned}OPPP′​=4=6​

    , what is its scale factor?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    First add the two adjacent distances to get the distance from O to the image.

    OP′=4+6,=10,k=104,=52.\begin{aligned}OP'&=4+6,\\&=10,\\k&=\frac{10}{4},\\&=\frac 52.\end{aligned}OP′k​=4+6,=10,=410​,=25​.​
  8. 8

    Points O,P′,PO,P',PO,P′,P occur in that order on one ray. A dilation centered at OOO sends PPP to P′P'P′. If

    OP′=9P′P=6\begin{aligned}OP'&=9\\P'P&=6\end{aligned}OP′P′P​=9=6​

    , what is its scale factor?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The whole original distance includes both adjacent pieces. Divide the image distance by that whole distance.

    OP=9+6,=15,k=915,=35.\begin{aligned}OP&=9+6,\\&=15,\\k&=\frac 9{15},\\&=\frac 35.\end{aligned}OPk​=9+6,=15,=159​,=53​.​
  9. 9

    A segment of length 2t+22t+22t+2 has an image of length 4t−24t-24t−2 under a dilation with scale factor 1.51.51.5. Find ttt.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The entire original expression is multiplied by the factor.

    4t−2=1.5(2t+2),4t−2=3t+3,t=5.\begin{aligned}4t-2&=1.5(2t+2),\\4t-2&=3t+3,\\t&=5.\end{aligned}4t−24t−2t​=1.5(2t+2),=3t+3,=5.​

    This gives positive lengths 12 and 18, whose image-to-original ratio is 1.5.

  10. 10

    A dilation has center (2,−1)(2,-1)(2,−1) and scale factor 333. What is the image of the line y=−1y=-1y=−1?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The center lies on the line y = -1. A line through the center maps onto the same line as a set, even though most of its points move.

  11. 11

    Which coordinate rule gives a dilation centered at the origin with scale factor 14\frac 1441​?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Multiply both coordinates by the same factor. Dividing by four is multiplying by one quarter.

    (x,y)⟼(14x,14y).(x,y)\longmapsto(\tfrac 14x,\tfrac 14y).(x,y)⟼(41​x,41​y).
  12. 12

    A dilation with scale factor 333 sends (2,1)(2, 1)(2,1) to (8,7)(8, 7)(8,7). What is its center?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Let the center be (a,b) and use the two displacement rules.

    8=a+3(2−a),a=−1,7=b+3(1−b),b=−2.\begin{aligned}8&=a+3(2-a),\\a&=-1,\\7&=b+3(1-b),\\b&=-2.\end{aligned}8a7b​=a+3(2−a),=−1,=b+3(1−b),=−2.​
