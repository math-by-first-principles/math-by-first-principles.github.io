  1. Problem 1 A point on its ray

    A dilation with center OOO and scale factor 47\frac 4774​ sends a point PPP to P′P'P′. The distance OPOPOP is 777 cm.

    Find OP′OP'OP′ and state whether P′P'P′ lies between OOO and PPP or beyond PPP.

    1. Hint 1

      Use the distance from the center to the point.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare the positive scale factor with one to decide the order.

    Answer

    OP′=4OP'=4OP′=4 cm; P′P'P′ lies between OOO and PPP.

    Full solution

    Multiply the original distance by the scale factor.

    OP′=47⋅7,=4 cm.\begin{aligned}OP'&=\frac 47\cdot 7,\\&=4\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}OP′​=74​⋅7,=4 cm.​

    The factor is positive and below one, so the image lies on ray OPOPOP, closer to OOO than PPP is.

    Thus P′P'P′ lies between OOO and PPP.

    Answer

    OP′=4OP'=4OP′=4 cm; P′P'P′ lies between OOO and PPP.

    Key idea

    A reduction changes distance from the chosen center while keeping the original ray.

  2. Problem 2 Three vertices from the origin

    Triangle ABCABCABC has vertices A(−4,−2)A(-4,-2)A(−4,−2), B(2,−2)B(2,-2)B(2,−2) and C(0,6)C(0, 6)C(0,6). It is dilated about the origin with scale factor 34\frac 3443​.

    (a) Find A′A'A′ and B′B'B′.

    (b) Find C′C'C′ and classify the dilation.

    1. Hint 1

      Identify the center before choosing the coordinate rule.

    2. Hint 2

      Multiply both coordinates of each vertex by the factor, including negative coordinates.

    Answer

    Part (a).

    A′=(−3,−1.5)B′=(1.5,−1.5)\begin{gathered}A'=(-3,-1.5)\\B'=(1.5,-1.5)\end{gathered}A′=(−3,−1.5)B′=(1.5,−1.5)​

    Part (b).

    C′=(0,4.5)C'=(0, 4.5)C′=(0,4.5)

    This is a reduction.

    Full solution

    (a) Multiply each coordinate by three quarters.

    A′=(−3,−1.5),B′=(1.5,−1.5).\begin{aligned}A'&=(-3,-1.5),\\B'&=(1.5,-1.5).\end{aligned}A′B′​=(−3,−1.5),=(1.5,−1.5).​

    (b) Zero stays zero when multiplied, and six becomes four and a half.

    C′=(0,4.5).C'=(0, 4.5).C′=(0,4.5).

    The positive factor 34\frac 3443​ is less than one, so this is a reduction.

    Answer

    Part (a).

    A′=(−3,−1.5)B′=(1.5,−1.5)\begin{gathered}A'=(-3,-1.5)\\B'=(1.5,-1.5)\end{gathered}A′=(−3,−1.5)B′=(1.5,−1.5)​

    Part (b).

    C′=(0,4.5)C'=(0, 4.5)C′=(0,4.5)

    This is a reduction.

    The origin rule multiplies both coordinates by the same number.

  3. Problem 3 Lengths and angles carry different information

    A dilation centered at OOO sends PPP to P′P'P′, with

    (b) A segment YZYZYZ has length 151515 mm. Find Y′Z′Y'Z'Y′Z′.

    (c) An angle at YYY measures 84∘84^\circ84∘. Find its image measure.

      First identify which given distance belongs to the image.

    k=85k=\frac 85k=58​

    (c) Angles are preserved, so the image angle measures 84∘84^\circ84∘.

    Answer

  4. Problem 4 Two points around a shifted center

    A dilation has center O(−3,2)O(-3, 2)O(−3,2) and scale factor 12\frac 1221​.

    (a) Find the image of P(1,8)P(1, 8)P(1,8).

    (b) Find the image of Q(−5,0)Q(-5, 0)Q(−5,0).

    1. Hint 1

      Measure each horizontal and vertical displacement from O.

    2. Hint 2

      Halve the displacements, then add the center coordinates back.

    Answer

    Part (a).

    P′=(−1,5)P'=(-1, 5)P′=(−1,5)

    Part (b).

    Q′=(−4,1)Q'=(-4, 1)Q′=(−4,1)

    Full solution

    (a) The displacement to PPP is four right and six up.

    After halving it, start two right and three up from OOO.

    P′=(−1,5).P'=(-1, 5).P′=(−1,5).

    (b) The displacement to QQQ is two left and two down.

    Half of that is one left and one down from OOO.

    Q′=(−4,1).Q'=(-4, 1).Q′=(−4,1).

    Answer

    Part (a).

    P′=(−1,5)P'=(-1, 5)P′=(−1,5)

    Part (b).

    Q′=(−4,1)Q'=(-4, 1)Q′=(−4,1)

    Key idea

    Scale displacements from the center rather than the raw coordinates.

  5. Problem 5 A line remains while its points move

    A dilation has center O(1,−2)O(1,-2)O(1,−2) and scale factor 0.40.40.4.

    (a) Describe the image of the line y=−2y=-2y=−2 and find the image of R(6,−2)R(6,-2)R(6,−2) on it.

    (b) Find the image of the line y=3y=3y=3 and explain whether it is the same line or a distinct parallel line.

    1. Hint 1

      Check whether each line contains the center.

    2. Hint 2

      For a horizontal line, every point has the same y-coordinate. Apply the vertical coordinate rule to that number.

    Answer

    Part (a). The image line is y=−2y=-2y=−2.

    R′=(3,−2)R'=(3,-2)R′=(3,−2)

    Part (b). The image is y=0y=0y=0, a distinct parallel line.

    Full solution

    (a) The line y=−2y=-2y=−2 contains OOO, so it maps onto itself as a set.

    The point RRR still moves.

    x′=1+0.4(6−1),=3,y′=−2+0.4(−2+2),=−2.\begin{aligned}x'&=1+0.4(6-1),\\&=3,\\y'&=-2+0.4(-2+2),\\&=-2.\end{aligned}x′y′​=1+0.4(6−1),=3,=−2+0.4(−2+2),=−2.​

    (b) All original points have vertical displacement five from the center.

    y′=−2+0.4(3+2),=0.\begin{aligned}y'&=-2+0.4(3+2),\\&=0.\end{aligned}y′​=−2+0.4(3+2),=0.​

    The horizontal coordinates still range over every real number, so the image is the entire line y=0y=0y=0.

    It is distinct from, and parallel to, y=3y=3y=3.

    Answer

    Part (a). The image line is y=−2y=-2y=−2.

    R′=(3,−2)R'=(3,-2)R′=(3,−2)

    Part (b). The image is y=0y=0y=0, a distinct parallel line.

    Key idea

    An unchanged line as a set need not have unchanged individual points.

  6. Problem 6 Two labels to determine

    A dilation has scale factor 1.51.51.5. All labeled lengths below are positive.

    (a) A segment has length 5u−15u-15u−1 and its image has length 7u+37u+37u+3. Find uuu and both lengths.

    (b) An original angle measures (2v+9)∘(2v+9)^\circ(2v+9)∘ and its image measures (5v−39)∘(5v-39)^\circ(5v−39)∘. Find vvv and the angle measure.

    1. Hint 1

      Decide which quantities are multiplied and which stay equal.

    2. Hint 2

      In part (a), multiply the whole original length expression by 1.5. In part (b), set the two angle expressions equal.

    Answer

    Part (a).

    u=9u=9u=9

    The original length is 444444 and the image length is 666666. Part (b).

    v=16v=16v=16

    Both angles measure 41∘41^\circ41∘.

    Full solution

    (a) Apply the factor to the original length.

    7u+3=1.5(5u−1),7u+3=7.5u−1.5,4.5=0.5u,u=9.\begin{aligned}7u+3&=1.5(5u-1),\\7u+3&=7.5u-1.5,\\4.5&=0.5u,\\u&=9.\end{aligned}7u+37u+34.5u​=1.5(5u−1),=7.5u−1.5,=0.5u,=9.​

    Substitute to check the positive lengths.

    5(9)−1=44,7(9)+3=66.\begin{aligned}5(9)-1&=44,\\7(9)+3&=66.\end{aligned}5(9)−17(9)+3​=44,=66.​

    (b) A dilation preserves the angle measure.

    2v+9=5v−39,48=3v,v=16.\begin{aligned}2v+9&=5v-39,\\48&=3v,\\v&=16.\end{aligned}2v+948v​=5v−39,=3v,=16.​

    Substituting sixteen into either expression gives an angle of 41∘41^\circ41∘.

    Answer

    Part (a).

    u=9u=9u=9

    The original length is 444444 and the image length is 666666. Part (b).

    v=16v=16v=16

    Both angles measure 41∘41^\circ41∘.

    The geometric quantity determines whether to scale an expression or equate it.

  7. Problem 7 Test the fixed-point claim

    Consider the claim “Every point on a line through a dilation's center is fixed.”

    Refute it with one positive scale factor and one point away from the center. State the correct fixed-point rule, including the special factor.

    1. Hint 1

      An unchanged line and an unchanged point are different statements.

    2. Hint 2

      Choose a factor other than one and a point whose distance from the center is easy to multiply.

    Answer

    For example, center O at coordinate 0 on a number line, factor 3, and point P at 2 give image P′ at 6, so P is not fixed. For factor 1 every point is fixed; for any other positive factor only the center is fixed.

    Full solution

    Choose a number line with OOO at 000 and PPP at 222.

    Under scale factor 333, the image is at 666.

    OP′=3⋅2,=6.\begin{aligned}OP'&=3\cdot 2,\\&=6.\end{aligned}OP′​=3⋅2,=6.​

    The point at 222 is not fixed, so the universal claim is false.

    The line still maps onto itself as a set.

    At factor 111, every distance from the center stays the same and every point is fixed.

    For a positive factor different from 111, each positive distance changes, leaving only the center fixed.

    Answer

    For example, center O at coordinate 0 on a number line, factor 3, and point P at 2 give image P′ at 6, so P is not fixed. For factor 1 every point is fixed; for any other positive factor only the center is fixed.

    Key idea

    A single moving point can refute a claim that every point is fixed.

  8. Problem 8 Infer the missing center

    A dilation with scale factor 32\frac 3223​ sends P(4,6)P(4, 6)P(4,6) to P′(7,10)P'(7, 10)P′(7,10).

    (a) Find the center O(a,b)O(a,b)O(a,b).

    (b) Use that center to find the image of Q(0,2)Q(0, 2)Q(0,2).

    1. Hint 1

      The displacement from the center is scaled before the center is added back.

    2. Hint 2

      Write one equation using the horizontal coordinates and one using the vertical coordinates.

    Answer

    Part (a).

    O=(−2,−2)O=(-2,-2)O=(−2,−2)

    Part (b).

    Q′=(1,4)Q'=(1, 4)Q′=(1,4)

    Full solution

    (a) Use the coordinate rule with the unknown center.

    7=a+32(4−a),7=6−12a,a=−2.\begin{aligned}7&=a+\tfrac 32(4-a),\\7&=6-\tfrac 12a,\\a&=-2.\end{aligned}77a​=a+23​(4−a),=6−21​a,=−2.​

    Apply the same reasoning vertically.

    10=b+32(6−b),10=9−12b,b=−2.\begin{aligned}10&=b+\tfrac 32(6-b),\\10&=9-\tfrac 12b,\\b&=-2.\end{aligned}1010b​=b+23​(6−b),=9−21​b,=−2.​

    (b) Measure the displacement from (−2,−2)(-2,-2)(−2,−2) to Q(0,2)Q(0, 2)Q(0,2), scale it, and add the center back.

    x′=−2+32(0+2),=1,y′=−2+32(2+2),=4.\begin{aligned}x'&=-2+\tfrac 32(0+2),\\&=1,\\y'&=-2+\tfrac 32(2+2),\\&=4.\end{aligned}x′y′​=−2+23​(0+2),=1,=−2+23​(2+2),=4.​

    Answer

    Part (a).

    O=(−2,−2)O=(-2,-2)O=(−2,−2)

    Part (b).

    Q′=(1,4)Q'=(1, 4)Q′=(1,4)

    Key idea

    One point-image pair and a factor different from one can determine a center.

  10. Problem 10 Repair a stretched triangle

    Triangle ABCABCABC has vertices A(0,0)A(0, 0)A(0,0), B(2,0)B(2, 0)B(2,0) and C(0,3)C(0, 3)C(0,3). A transformation records A′(1,−3)A'(1,-3)A′(1,−3), B′(5,−3)B'(5,-3)B′(5,−3) and C′(1,0)C'(1, 0)C′(1,0).

    (a) Explain why these three images cannot come from one dilation with any center.

    (b) Keep A′A'A′ and B′B'B′ as recorded. Find a center and a positive scale factor that give these two mappings, and replace C′C'C′ so that the entire triangle image comes from that dilation.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare an original horizontal length with its image, then do the same for a vertical length.

    2. Hint 2

      For part (b), the horizontal segment determines the factor. Use A and its image to find the center.

    3. Hint 3

      After finding the center, apply the same coordinate rule to C.

    Answer

    Part (a). The length factors are 222 for ABABAB and 111 for ACACAC, so no dilation works. Part (b). Use factor 222 and center (−1,3)(-1, 3)(−1,3). The corrected third image is

    C′=(1,3).C'=(1, 3).C′=(1,3).

    Full solution

    (a) The horizontal length ABABAB is 222, while A′B′A'B'A′B′ is 444, so that pair requires factor 222.

    The vertical length ACACAC is 333, while A′C′A'C'A′C′ is also 333, so that pair requires factor 111.

    One dilation cannot satisfy both.

    (b) Keep the factor 222 required by ABABAB and its image.

    Use A(0,0)A(0, 0)A(0,0) mapping to (1,−3)(1,-3)(1,−3) to find the center.

    1=a+2(0−a),a=−1,−3=b+2(0−b),b=3.\begin{aligned}1&=a+2(0-a),\\a&=-1,\\-3&=b+2(0-b),\\b&=3.\end{aligned}1a−3b​=a+2(0−a),=−1,=b+2(0−b),=3.​

    This center sends B(2,0)B(2, 0)B(2,0) to (5,−3)(5,-3)(5,−3) as required.

    Apply it to C(0,3)C(0, 3)C(0,3).

    x′=−1+2(0+1),=1,y′=3+2(3−3),=3.\begin{aligned}x'&=-1+2(0+1),\\&=1,\\y'&=3+2(3-3),\\&=3.\end{aligned}x′y′​=−1+2(0+1),=1,=3+2(3−3),=3.​

    Replace the recorded third image by (1,3)(1, 3)(1,3).

    Now every vertex uses the same center and factor.

    Answer

    Part (a). The length factors are 222 for ABABAB and 111 for ACACAC, so no dilation works. Part (b). Use factor 222 and center (−1,3)(-1, 3)(−1,3). The corrected third image is

    C′=(1,3).C'=(1, 3).C′=(1,3).

    Key idea

    Unequal length factors rule out a dilation; repairing it requires one consistent point rule.
