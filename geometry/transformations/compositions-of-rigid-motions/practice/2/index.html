  1. 1

    First rotate P(−3,4)P(-3,4)P(−3,4) through 90∘90^\circ90∘ clockwise about the origin, then translate two right and five down. What is P′′P''P′′?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The clockwise quarter-turn uses (y,−x)(y,-x)(y,−x), so the intermediate point is P′(4,3)P'(4,3)P′(4,3).

    Apply the translation to that point.

    x′′=4+2=6x''=4+2=6x′′=4+2=6

    y′′=3−5=−2y''=3-5=-2y′′=3−5=−2

    Thus P′′(6,−2)P''(6,-2)P′′(6,−2).

  2. 2

    A point NNN is first reflected across the yyy-axis, then translated three right and one up. Its final image is N′′(−5,4)N''(-5,4)N′′(−5,4). Find NNN.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Undo the slide first: subtract 3 from the horizontal coordinate and 1 from the height.

    x′=−5−3=−8x'=-5-3=-8x′=−5−3=−8

    y′=4−1=3y'=4-1=3y′=4−1=3

    Reflect N′(−8,3)N'(-8,3)N′(−8,3) across the same y-axis to recover N(8,3)N(8,3)N(8,3).

  3. 3

    Reflect first across x=4x=4x=4, then across x=−1x=-1x=−1. What single motion does this give, and where does J(7,2)J(7,2)J(7,2) finish?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The second mirror is five units left of the first, so the translation is ten left.

    2(5)=102(5)=102(5)=10

    The horizontal coordinate becomes 7−10=−37-10=-37−10=−3, and height stays 2. A direct reflection check gives J′(1,2)J'(1,2)J′(1,2), then J′′(−3,2)J''(-3,2)J′′(−3,2).

  4. 4

    Two distinct parallel mirrors produce an 18 cm translation when used successively. What is their perpendicular separation?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Two parallel reflections translate through twice the gap. Divide the travel distance by 2.

    18÷2=918\div2=918÷2=9

    The mirrors are 9 cm apart.

  5. 5

    The chosen directed angle from mirror m to mirror n is 120 degrees counterclockwise. They meet at O. First reflect in m, then in n. Which rotation gives the same final mapping?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Double the directed angle between the chosen rays.

    2(120∘)=240∘2(120^\circ)=240^\circ2(120∘)=240∘

    The composition turns counterclockwise about O. Choosing the opposite ray of a mirror would change the result by a full turn, without changing the final image.

  6. 6

    Reflect V(6,−2)V(6,-2)V(6,−2) across both coordinate axes. Compare the two possible orders.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Across the x-axis first, the point becomes (6,2)(6,2)(6,2); reflecting next across the y-axis gives (−6,2)(-6,2)(−6,2).

    Across the y-axis first, it becomes (−6,−2)(-6,-2)(−6,−2); the x-axis reflection again gives (−6,2)(-6,2)(−6,2).

    Both coordinates reverse signs.

    (x,y)↦(−x,−y)(x,y)\mapsto(-x,-y)(x,y)↦(−x,−y)

    The perpendicular mirrors commute as a half-turn about the origin.

  7. 7

    A glide first reflects R(−1,−3)R(-1,-3)R(−1,−3) across y=2y=2y=2, then translates five units right. What is R′′R''R′′?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The horizontal mirror changes height to twice 2 minus the old height.

    y′=4−(−3)=7y'=4-(-3)=7y′=4−(−3)=7

    The intermediate point is R′(−1,7)R'(-1,7)R′(−1,7). Add 5 to its horizontal coordinate.

    −1+5=4-1+5=4−1+5=4

    Thus R′′(4,7)R''(4,7)R′′(4,7).

  8. 8

    A reflection across x=−1x=-1x=−1 and a translation four units up form a glide. What is its rule in either order?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The vertical mirror changes only the horizontal coordinate.

    x′=2(−1)−x=−2−xx'=2(-1)-x=-2-xx′=2(−1)−x=−2−x

    The parallel translation adds 4 to the height. Since one step acts across the mirror and the other along it, either order gives the displayed rule.

  9. 9

    A rigid sequence maps triangle JKLJKLJKL exactly onto triangle UVWUVWUVW, with J↦UJ\mapsto UJ↦U, K↦VK\mapsto VK↦V, and L↦WL\mapsto WL↦W. Given JK=8JK=8JK=8 cm and m∠JKL=49∘m\angle JKL=49^\circm∠JKL=49∘, what follows?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The endpoints J and K correspond to U and V, so distance preservation gives UV=8UV=8UV=8 cm.

    The vertex K corresponds to V. Angle preservation gives

    m∠UVW=49∘.m\angle UVW=49^\circ.m∠UVW=49∘.

    Both properties survive every rigid step.

  10. 10

    A specified reflection followed by a specified translation maps all three vertices of one triangle to the corresponding vertices of another. Why does this match the whole triangle?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The given steps are actual rigid motions, which take each straight side to the segment between its image endpoints. The same fold and slide carry the enclosed interior with that boundary. Thus this sequence supplies the required whole-triangle match.

  11. 11

    A reflection followed by a rotation maps segment ABABAB onto CDCDCD. Given AB=3t+4AB=3t+4AB=3t+4 cm and CD=28CD=28CD=28 cm, find ttt.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Each step preserves the segment length, so the initial and final lengths agree.

    3t+4=283t+4=283t+4=28

    Subtract 4 and divide by 3.

    3t=243t=243t=24

    t=8t=8t=8

  12. 12

    A glide is performed by first reflecting across m, then translating six units in a specified direction along m. Which ordered sequence undoes it?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Reverse the steps and invert each motion. First undo the parallel translation with its opposite displacement. Then reflect in the same mirror, which undoes the original reflection.
