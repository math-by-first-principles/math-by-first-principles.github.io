Question 1 of 12
  1. 1

    Which instruction defines one rotation of the plane?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A rotation uses one center, one angle, and one direction for the whole plane. Each off-center point stays at its own radius, and the center stays fixed.

  2. 2

    Rotate JJJ through 55∘55^\circ55∘ counterclockwise about OOO. Given OJ=4.6OJ=4.6OJ=4.6 cm, what is OJ′OJ'OJ′?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A rotation keeps a point at the same distance from its center.

    OJ′=OJOJ'=OJOJ′=OJ

    Thus OJ′=4.6OJ'=4.6OJ′=4.6 cm. The 55 degrees describes the turn, not a length.

  3. 3

    A rotation turns the plane 73∘73^\circ73∘ clockwise about CCC. Which point stays fixed?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The center has radius zero and maps to itself. Every other point turns 73 degrees around a positive-radius circle, so it moves.

  4. 4

    Using counterclockwise as positive, what does an angle of −270∘-270^\circ−270∘ describe?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A negative sign chooses clockwise direction. The magnitude gives the amount of turning.

    ∣−270∣=270|-270|=270∣−270∣=270

    The instruction is 270 degrees clockwise. Its final image also agrees with a 90-degree counterclockwise turn, but the stated motion is longer.

  5. 5

    Rotate L(−4,6)L(-4,6)L(−4,6) through 90∘90^\circ90∘ counterclockwise about the origin. What is L′L'L′?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Use the counterclockwise quarter-turn rule (x,y)↦(−y,x)(x,y)\mapsto(-y,x)(x,y)↦(−y,x).

    (−4,6)↦(−6,−4)(-4,6)\mapsto(-6,-4)(−4,6)↦(−6,−4)

  6. 6

    Rotate M(7,−1)M(7,-1)M(7,−1) through 180∘180^\circ180∘ about the origin. What is M′M'M′?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A half-turn reverses both coordinate directions. Negate both coordinates.

    (7,−1)↦(−7,1)(7,-1)\mapsto(-7,1)(7,−1)↦(−7,1)

  7. 7

    Rotate N(−3,−5)N(-3,-5)N(−3,−5) through 90∘90^\circ90∘ clockwise about the origin. What is N′N'N′?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Use the clockwise quarter-turn rule (x,y)↦(y,−x)(x,y)\mapsto(y,-x)(x,y)↦(y,−x).

    (−3,−5)↦(−5,3)(-3,-5)\mapsto(-5,3)(−3,−5)↦(−5,3)

  8. 8

    Rotate K(1,−6)K(1,-6)K(1,−6) through 270∘270^\circ270∘ clockwise about the origin. What is K′K'K′?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A 270-degree clockwise turn reaches the same final ray as a 90-degree counterclockwise turn. Use (−y,x)(-y,x)(−y,x).

    (1,−6)↦(6,1)(1,-6)\mapsto(6,1)(1,−6)↦(6,1)

  9. 9

    A rotation maps a 10 cm segment and a 38-degree angle. What are their corresponding image measurements?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A rotation preserves distance and angle measure directly. The image segment remains 10 cm long, and the corresponding image angle remains 38 degrees.

  10. 10

    The order U,V,WU,V,WU,V,W around a noncollinear triangle is clockwise. What is the order U′,V′,W′U',V',W'U′,V′,W′ after a rotation?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A rotation turns the marked face without flipping it. It preserves the clockwise or counterclockwise order of corresponding triangle vertices, whichever direction the figure itself turns.

  11. 11

    What does a full 360-degree rotation about any chosen center do?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A full turn returns every point to its starting ray at its original radius.

    P′=PP'=PP′=P

    This is an identity rotation; every point is fixed.

  12. 12

    A new ray from OOO already marks the required 42∘42^\circ42∘ clockwise turn from OPOPOP. What next locates P′P'P′?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The angle and direction have already chosen the new ray. The remaining rotation condition is the unchanged radius, so copy length OP from O along that ray.
