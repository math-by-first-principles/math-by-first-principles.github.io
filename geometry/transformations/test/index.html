    One translation sends P to Q and also sends Q to R. The three points are distinct. Which conclusion is guaranteed?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    You chose B: This puts P between Q and R, although the second equal displacement continues away from P.

    You chose C: This turns the second displacement through 90 degrees instead of copying the common direction.

    You chose D: This treats a translation with distinct point and image as the zero translation.

    Its required target is D(5,0)D(5,0)D(5,0).

    Choice y=xy=xy=x.

    The horizontal change from A-prime to D is

    5−(−1)=6.5-(-1)=6.5−(−1)=6.

    Choice y=xy=xy=x.

    The vertical change is

    0−(−2)=2.0-(-2)=2.0−(−2)=2.

    Thus the required translation is (6,2)(6,2)(6,2), which sends A-prime to D.

    Choice y=xy=xy=x.

    B first becomes B′(−1,3)B'(-1,3)B′(−1,3).

    Applying that same slide gives (5,5)=E(5,5)=E(5,5)=E.

    Choice y=xy=xy=x.

    C first becomes C′(4,−2)C'(4,-2)C′(4,−2).

    The same slide gives (10,0)=F(10,0)=F(10,0)=F.

    All three required vertex matches work.

    Choice y=−xy=-xy=−x.

    Reflecting A gives (1,2)(1,2)(1,2).

    The required horizontal change to D is 5−1=45-1=45−1=4, and the required vertical change is 0−2=−20-2=-20−2=−2.

    This forces translation (4,−2)(4,-2)(4,−2) for this mirror choice.

    Choice y=−xy=-xy=−x.

    B first becomes (1,−3)(1,-3)(1,−3).

    The forced slide sends it to (5,−5)(5,-5)(5,−5), which is not E(5,5)E(5,5)E(5,5).

    No different translation can fix this B match while retaining the required A-to-D match, so this mirror choice fails.

    Choose reflection across y=xy=xy=x followed by translation (6,2)(6,2)(6,2).

    Each rigid step takes a straight segment to the segment between its endpoint images.

    The three verified vertex matches therefore send AB to DE, BC to EF, and CA to FD, covering every boundary side.

    Dot check.

    P first becomes P′(1,−1)P'(1,-1)P′(1,−1).

    Its final horizontal coordinate is 1+6=71+6=71+6=7 and its final height is −1+2=1-1+2=1−1+2=1.

    Hence P′′(7,1)P''(7,1)P′′(7,1).

    Dot check.

    The given target dot is at (8,1), a different position.

    Both dot positions are strictly inside the target triangle and off its boundary, so the unmatched image cannot be supplied by a boundary point.

    The chosen sequence fails for the complete figures.

    Repair.

    Move only Q to (7,1).

    Now the chosen sequence sends A to D, B to E, C to F, and P to the repaired Q.

    It covers each target side and its one dot, so the entire starting figure maps exactly onto the repaired target.

    Repair.

    Both steps are rigid.

    This complete mapping establishes congruence of the repaired marked figures, with all four correspondences specified.

    Answer

    Reflect across y=xy=xy=x, then translate by (6,2)(6,2)(6,2); all three vertex matches work. The y=−xy=-xy=−x choice fails. The complete match fails: P maps to (7,1)(7,1)(7,1). Move Q to (7,1)(7,1)(7,1); the repaired figures are congruent.

    Key idea

    A valid sequence must carry every included component, even when the boundary vertices already match.
