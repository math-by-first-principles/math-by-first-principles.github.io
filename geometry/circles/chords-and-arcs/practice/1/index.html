Question 1 of 12
  1. 1

    Which description defines a chord of a circle?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A chord is a finite segment whose two distinct endpoints lie on the circle.

    An interior segment need not reach the circle, the boundary is curved, and a whole line is not a segment.

  4. 4

    Two distinct circles are concentric. Which description must be true?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Concentric means that the center is shared.

    If the radii were also equal, the point sets would be the same circle. Distinct concentric circles therefore have different radii.

  5. 5

    Circle O has radius 8 cm. Which circle must be congruent to it?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Congruent circles have equal radii. A different center can be matched by translation.

    The diameter-14 circle has radius 7 cm. The other offered radii are 12 cm and 16 cm, so only radius 8 cm works.

  6. 6

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Divide target radius by original radius.

    k=156.k=\frac{15}{6}.k=615​.

    The factor is five halves. Multiplying 6 by that factor gives 15.

    A circle has radius 10 cm. A chord is 6 cm from its center, measured perpendicularly. Find the full chord length.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The radius, perpendicular distance, and half chord form a right triangle.

    h2=102−62,h2=64,h=8.\begin{aligned}h^2&=10^2-6^2,\\h^2&=64,\\h&=8.\end{aligned}h2h2h​=102−62,=64,=8.​

    Double the positive half length to get 16 cm.

  12. 12

    What is the perpendicular distance from a circle center to any diameter?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A diameter passes through the center.

    The distance from a point on a line to that line is zero. This boundary case does not form a nondegenerate center-to-chord right triangle.
