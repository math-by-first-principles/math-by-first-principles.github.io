    In convex cyclic ABCDABCDABCD, the whole angles at A,CA,CA,C measure (4x+9)∘(4x+9)^\circ(4x+9)∘ and (2x+3)∘(2x+3)^\circ(2x+3)∘. Find xxx.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The opposite angles are supplementary.

    (4x+9)+(2x+3)=180,6x+12=180,x=28.\begin{aligned}(4x+9)+(2x+3)&=180,\\6x+12&=180,\\x&=28.\end{aligned}(4x+9)+(2x+3)6x+12x​=180,=180,=28.​

    The resulting angles are 121 and 59 degrees, both valid convex angles.

  2. 2

    In convex cyclic ABCDABCDABCD, angles ABC,CDAABC,CDAABC,CDA measure (5x−8)∘(5x-8)^\circ(5x−8)∘ and (3x+12)∘(3x+12)^\circ(3x+12)∘. Find xxx.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    B and D are opposite.

    (5x−8)+(3x+12)=180,8x+4=180,x=22.\begin{aligned}(5x-8)+(3x+12)&=180,\\8x+4&=180,\\x&=22.\end{aligned}(5x−8)+(3x+12)8x+4x​=180,=180,=22.​

    The angles are 102 and 78 degrees, so their measures are valid.

  3. 3

    In convex cyclic ABCDABCDABCD, side BCBCBC is extended through CCC to EEE. Exterior angle DCEDCEDCE measures (3x+5)∘(3x+5)^\circ(3x+5)∘, and interior angle BADBADBAD measures (5x−23)∘(5x-23)^\circ(5x−23)∘. Find xxx.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The exterior angle equals the opposite interior angle.

    3x+5=5x−23,28=2x,x=14.\begin{aligned}3x+5&=5x-23,\\28&=2x,\\x&=14.\end{aligned}3x+528x​=5x−23,=2x,=14.​

    Both named angles then measure 47 degrees.

    Convex cyclic ABCDABCDABCD has m∠A=82∘m\angle A=82^\circm∠A=82∘ and m∠B=119∘m\angle B=119^\circm∠B=119∘. Which pair gives the whole angles at C,DC,DC,D?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Subtract each given angle from 180 degrees using the opposite pair.

    m∠C=98∘,m∠D=61∘.\begin{gathered}m\angle C=98^\circ,\\m\angle D=61^\circ.\end{gathered}m∠C=98∘,m∠D=61∘.​
  5. 5

    Convex ABCDABCDABCD has diagonal ACACAC. The givens are m∠BAC=31∘m\angle BAC=31^\circm∠BAC=31∘, m∠BCA=65∘m\angle BCA=65^\circm∠BCA=65∘, and m∠CAD=42∘m\angle CAD=42^\circm∠CAD=42∘. What value of m∠ACDm\angle ACDm∠ACD makes the quadrilateral cyclic?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Add the two pieces at A to obtain 73 degrees. The whole angle at C must therefore be 107 degrees. Its known piece is 65 degrees.

    m∠ACD=180∘−73∘−65∘,m∠ACD=42∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle ACD&=180^\circ-73^\circ-65^\circ,\\m\angle ACD&=42^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠ACDm∠ACD​=180∘−73∘−65∘,=42∘.​

    With this value the opposite-angle sum is 180 degrees, so the converse applies.

  6. 6

    Convex cyclic ABCDABCDABCD has m∠BCD=118∘m\angle BCD=118^\circm∠BCD=118∘. Diagonal AC bisects the whole angle BAD. Find m∠CADm\angle CADm∠CAD.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The whole angle at A is supplementary to the whole angle at C. Diagonal AC then splits the whole angle at A into two equal parts.

    m∠BAD=180∘−118∘,m∠BAD=62∘,m∠CAD=62∘/2,m∠CAD=31∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle BAD&=180^\circ-118^\circ,\\m\angle BAD&=62^\circ,\\m\angle CAD&=62^\circ/2,\\m\angle CAD&=31^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠BADm∠BADm∠CADm∠CAD​=180∘−118∘,=62∘,=62∘/2,=31∘.​
  7. 7

    Convex ABCDABCDABCD has BCBCBC extended through CCC to EEE. Both ∠DCE\angle DCE∠DCE and ∠BAD\angle BAD∠BAD measure 68∘68^\circ68∘. Which conclusion follows?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The linear pair makes the whole angle at C 112 degrees. It is supplementary to the opposite angle at A.

    68∘+112∘=180∘.68^\circ+112^\circ=180^\circ.68∘+112∘=180∘.

    The converse proves cyclicity.

  8. 8

    Only consecutive angles A=77∘A=77^\circA=77∘ and B=103∘B=103^\circB=103∘ are known in convex ABCDABCDABCD. What can be decided about cyclicity from these data alone?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The converse needs an opposite pair. A and B are consecutive, so their supplementary sum does not settle cyclicity. For instance, the remaining angles could be C=103, D=77, satisfying the cyclic test, or C=77, D=103 in an oblique parallelogram, failing it.

  9. 9

    In convex cyclic ABCDABCDABCD, the whole angle at A is twice the whole angle at C. Find the whole angle at C.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Write the angle at C as t degrees. The opposite angle at A is 2t degrees.

    2t+t=180,t=60.\begin{aligned}2t+t&=180,\\t&=60.\end{aligned}2t+tt​=180,=60.​

    Convex cyclic ABCDABCDABCD has AB=ADAB=ADAB=AD and m∠BCD=112∘m\angle BCD=112^\circm∠BCD=112∘. Find m∠ABDm\angle ABDm∠ABD.

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The opposite whole angle BAD is 68 degrees. In isosceles triangle ABD, the base angles at B,D are equal.

    m∠ABD=(180∘−68∘)/2,m∠ABD=56∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle ABD&=(180^\circ-68^\circ)/2,\\m\angle ABD&=56^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠ABDm∠ABD​=(180∘−68∘)/2,=56∘.​
