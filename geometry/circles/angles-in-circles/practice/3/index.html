    Points A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D occur in order around a circle. Chords AC,BDAC,BDAC,BD meet at XXX. Side lines AB,DCAB,DCAB,DC meet at external PPP with orders P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B and P,D,CP,D,CP,D,C. Far arc BCBCBC excludes A,DA,DA,D; near arc DADADA excludes B,CB,CB,C. Angle AXBAXBAXB is 86∘86^\circ86∘, and angle APDAPDAPD is 41∘41^\circ41∘. Find the far arc BCBCBC.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The inside angle makes arcs AB and CD total 172 degrees, leaving 188 degrees for BC and DA. Their difference is 82 degrees.

    b+d=188,b−d=82,2b=270,b=135.\begin{aligned}b+d&=188,\\b-d&=82,\\2b&=270,\\b&=135.\end{aligned}b+db−d2bb​=188,=82,=270,=135.​

    The near arc is 53 degrees; arcs AB and CD can each be 86 degrees.

  2. 2

    Points A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D occur in order around a circle. Chords AC,BDAC,BDAC,BD meet at XXX. Side lines AB,DCAB,DCAB,DC meet at external PPP with orders P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B and P,D,CP,D,CP,D,C. Far arc BCBCBC excludes A,DA,DA,D; near arc DADADA excludes B,CB,CB,C. Angle AXBAXBAXB is 98∘98^\circ98∘, angle APDAPDAPD is 27∘27^\circ27∘, and consecutive arc ABABAB is 67∘67^\circ67∘. Find the inscribed angle BADBADBAD.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The inside angle makes arcs AB and CD total 196 degrees, so arc CD is 129 degrees. The remaining arcs BC and DA total 164 degrees. Their difference is twice the exterior angle, or 54 degrees.

    b+d=164,b−d=54,b=109,d=55.\begin{aligned}b+d&=164,\\b-d&=54,\\b&=109,\\d&=55.\end{aligned}b+db−dbd​=164,=54,=109,=55.​

    The inscribed angle BAD excludes A and intercepts the arc from B through C to D. That arc measures 238 degrees.

    m∠BAD=(109∘+129∘)/2,m∠BAD=119∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle BAD&=(109^\circ+129^\circ)/2,\\m\angle BAD&=119^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠BADm∠BAD​=(109∘+129∘)/2,=119∘.​

    All four positive arcs total 360 degrees.

    Ray PTPTPT is tangent at TTT, and a secant has order P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B. Near arc TATATA excludes BBB; far arc BTBTBT excludes AAA. The degree measures of near and far arcs are 3x+23x+23x+2 and 7x+187x+187x+18. Angle TPATPATPA is 48∘48^\circ48∘. Find arc ABABAB excluding TTT.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Use the angle difference first.

    (7x+18)−(3x+2)=96,4x+16=96,x=20.\begin{aligned}(7x+18)-(3x+2)&=96,\\4x+16&=96,\\x&=20.\end{aligned}(7x+18)−(3x+2)4x+16x​=96,=96,=20.​

    The near and far arcs are 62 and 158 degrees. The remaining arc is

    m(arc AB)=360∘−62∘−158∘,m(arc AB)=140∘.\begin{aligned}m(\text{arc }AB)&=360^\circ-62^\circ-158^\circ,\\m(\text{arc }AB)&=140^\circ.\end{aligned}m(arc AB)m(arc AB)​=360∘−62∘−158∘,=140∘.​

    Rays PT,PUPT,PUPT,PU are tangent to one circle at distinct T,UT,UT,U, with PPP strictly outside. In triangle PTUPTUPTU, angle PTUPTUPTU measures (2x+3)∘(2x+3)^\circ(2x+3)∘, and angle TPUTPUTPU measures (3x+6)∘(3x+6)^\circ(3x+6)∘. Point RRR lies on the major arc TUTUTU. Find major arc TRUTRUTRU.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The two base angles select the same minor arc, so both measure 2x+3 degrees.

    2(2x+3)+(3x+6)=180,7x+12=180,x=24.\begin{aligned}2(2x+3)+(3x+6)&=180,\\7x+12&=180,\\x&=24.\end{aligned}2(2x+3)+(3x+6)7x+12x​=180,=180,=24.​

    The minor arc is twice the 51-degree base angle, or 102 degrees. Therefore

    m(arc TRU)=360∘−102∘,m(arc TRU)=258∘.\begin{aligned}m(\text{arc }TRU)&=360^\circ-102^\circ,\\m(\text{arc }TRU)&=258^\circ.\end{aligned}m(arc TRU)m(arc TRU)​=360∘−102∘,=258∘.​

    Points A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D occur in that order around a circle. Chords AC,BDAC,BDAC,BD cross at interior PPP. Angles APB,BPCAPB,BPCAPB,BPC measure (4x−2)∘,(2x+8)∘(4x-2)^\circ,(2x+8)^\circ(4x−2)∘,(2x+8)∘. Consecutive arc ABABAB is 88∘88^\circ88∘. Find consecutive arc CDCDCD.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The adjacent angles form a straight angle.

    (4x−2)+(2x+8)=180,6x+6=180,x=29,m∠APB=114∘.\begin{aligned}(4x-2)+(2x+8)&=180,\\6x+6&=180,\\x&=29,\\m\angle APB&=114^\circ.\end{aligned}(4x−2)+(2x+8)6x+6xm∠APB​=180,=180,=29,=114∘.​

    Double this inside angle and subtract arc AB.

    m(arc CD)=228∘−88∘,m(arc CD)=140∘.\begin{aligned}m(\text{arc }CD)&=228^\circ-88^\circ,\\m(\text{arc }CD)&=140^\circ.\end{aligned}m(arc CD)m(arc CD)​=228∘−88∘,=140∘.​

The consecutive arcs AB,BC,CD,DAAB,BC,CD,DAAB,BC,CD,DA measure 30∘,270∘,30∘,30∘30^\circ,270^\circ,30^\circ,30^\circ30∘,270∘,30∘,30∘. Find the angle between rays PAPAPA and PDPDPD.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The far route BC is major and excludes both near points. The positive arcs sum to 360 degrees.

    m∠APD=(270∘−30∘)/2,m∠APD=120∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle APD&=(270^\circ-30^\circ)/2,\\m\angle APD&=120^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠APDm∠APD​=(270∘−30∘)/2,=120∘.​

    The acute 60-degree angle would use one reversed ray.

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Two secants from external PPP have orders P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B and P,D,CP,D,CP,D,C. The circle order is A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D. Far arc BCBCBC excludes A,DA,DA,D; near arc DADADA excludes B,CB,CB,C. Angle APDAPDAPD is 54∘54^\circ54∘. The far and near arc measures, in degrees, are 5x+415x+415x+41 and 2x+172x+172x+17. Find the far arc.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Twice the exterior angle is the arc difference.

    (5x+41)−(2x+17)=108,3x+24=108,x=28.\begin{aligned}(5x+41)-(2x+17)&=108,\\3x+24&=108,\\x&=28.\end{aligned}(5x+41)−(2x+17)3x+24x​=108,=108,=28.​

    The requested route is

    m(far arc BC)=5(28)∘+41∘,m(far arc BC)=181∘.\begin{aligned}m(\text{far arc }BC)&=5(28)^\circ+41^\circ,\\m(\text{far arc }BC)&=181^\circ.\end{aligned}m(far arc BC)m(far arc BC)​=5(28)∘+41∘,=181∘.​

    The near arc is 73 degrees, leaving 106 degrees for AB and CD.
