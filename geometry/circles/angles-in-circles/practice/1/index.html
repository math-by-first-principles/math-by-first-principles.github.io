  1. 1

    Points A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D occur in that order around a circle. Chords AC,BDAC,BDAC,BD cross at interior PPP. Consecutive arcs ABABAB and CDCDCD measure 62∘62^\circ62∘ and 104∘104^\circ104∘. Find m∠APBm\angle APBm∠APB.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Angle APB faces arcs AB and CD, as does its vertical partner.

    m∠APB=(62∘+104∘)/2,m∠APB=83∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle APB&=(62^\circ+104^\circ)/2,\\m\angle APB&=83^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠APBm∠APB​=(62∘+104∘)/2,=83∘.​
  2. 2

    Points A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D occur in that order around a circle. Chords AC,BDAC,BDAC,BD cross at interior PPP. Which two consecutive arcs determine ∠BPC\angle BPC∠BPC by a half-sum?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The rays PB,PC face arc BC. Their opposite rays PD,PA face arc DA. These are the arcs of the vertical pair. Writing b,d for their degree measures gives

    m∠BPC=(b+d)/2.m\angle BPC=(b+d)/2.m∠BPC=(b+d)/2.
  3. 3

    Two secants from external PPP have orders P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B and P,D,CP,D,CP,D,C. The circle order is A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D. Far arc BCBCBC excludes A,DA,DA,D; near arc DADADA excludes B,CB,CB,C. The far arc is 188∘188^\circ188∘ and the near arc is 52∘52^\circ52∘. Find m∠APDm\angle APDm∠APD.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The angle is outside, between the rays toward the circle. Use far minus near.

    m∠APD=(188∘−52∘)/2,m∠APD=68∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle APD&=(188^\circ-52^\circ)/2,\\m\angle APD&=68^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠APDm∠APD​=(188∘−52∘)/2,=68∘.​
  4. 4

    Ray PTPTPT is tangent at TTT, and a secant has order P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B. Near arc TATATA excludes BBB; far arc BTBTBT excludes AAA. The near arc is 44∘44^\circ44∘ and the far arc is 156∘156^\circ156∘. Find m∠TPAm\angle TPAm∠TPA.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The tangent and secant start at the same external point. Their selected arcs give

    m∠TPA=(156∘−44∘)/2,m∠TPA=56∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle TPA&=(156^\circ-44^\circ)/2,\\m\angle TPA&=56^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠TPAm∠TPA​=(156∘−44∘)/2,=56∘.​
  5. 5

    Rays PT,PUPT,PUPT,PU are tangent to one circle at distinct T,UT,UT,U, with PPP strictly outside. The minor arc TUTUTU measures 122∘122^\circ122∘. Find m∠TPUm\angle TPUm∠TPU.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The angle between the tangent rays and the minor contact arc sum to 180 degrees.

    m∠TPU=180∘−122∘,m∠TPU=58∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle TPU&=180^\circ-122^\circ,\\m\angle TPU&=58^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠TPUm∠TPU​=180∘−122∘,=58∘.​
  6. 6

    Two chords cross strictly inside a circle, away from its center. Which rule gives an angle at their intersection?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Join two chord endpoints to form a triangle. The angle at the chord crossing is exterior to that triangle, so it adds two inscribed angles. If their intercepted arc measures are a,c, the angle is

    θ=(a+c)/2.\theta=(a+c)/2.θ=(a+c)/2.

    Points A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D occur in that order around a circle. Chords AC,BDAC,BDAC,BD cross at interior PPP. Angle APBAPBAPB is 87∘87^\circ87∘, and consecutive arc CDCDCD is 98∘98^\circ98∘. Find consecutive arc ABABAB.

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Double the inside angle to recover the sum of arcs AB and CD. Subtract the known arc.

    m(arc AB)=2(87∘)−98∘,m(arc AB)=76∘.\begin{aligned}m(\text{arc }AB)&=2(87^\circ)-98^\circ,\\m(\text{arc }AB)&=76^\circ.\end{aligned}m(arc AB)m(arc AB)​=2(87∘)−98∘,=76∘.​

    Two secants from external PPP have orders P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B and P,D,CP,D,CP,D,C. The circle order is A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D. Far arc BCBCBC excludes A,DA,DA,D; near arc DADADA excludes B,CB,CB,C. The exterior angle is 42∘42^\circ42∘, and the near arc is 56∘56^\circ56∘. Find the far arc.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Twice the angle is far minus near. Add the near arc to that difference.

    m(far arc BC)=2(42∘)+56∘,m(far arc BC)=140∘.\begin{aligned}m(\text{far arc }BC)&=2(42^\circ)+56^\circ,\\m(\text{far arc }BC)&=140^\circ.\end{aligned}m(far arc BC)m(far arc BC)​=2(42∘)+56∘,=140∘.​
  9. 9

    Points A,V,BA,V,BA,V,B lie on a circle. The arc from AAA to BBB excluding VVV measures 146∘146^\circ146∘. Find m∠AVBm\angle AVBm∠AVB.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The vertex is on the circle. Use half of the single arc excluding V.

    m∠AVB=146∘/2,m∠AVB=73∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle AVB&=146^\circ/2,\\m\angle AVB&=73^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠AVBm∠AVB​=146∘/2,=73∘.​

    Points A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D occur in that order around a circle. Chords AC,BDAC,BDAC,BD cross at interior PPP. Angle APBAPBAPB measures 113∘113^\circ113∘. Find the adjacent angle BPCBPCBPC.

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Rays PA and PC are opposite. The two adjacent angles therefore form a straight angle.

    m∠BPC=180∘−113∘,m∠BPC=67∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle BPC&=180^\circ-113^\circ,\\m\angle BPC&=67^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠BPCm∠BPC​=180∘−113∘,=67∘.​

    Rays PT,PUPT,PUPT,PU are tangent to one circle at distinct T,UT,UT,U, with PPP strictly outside. Angle TPUTPUTPU measures 109∘109^\circ109∘. Find the minor arc TUTUTU.

    Solution

    m(minor arc TU)=180∘−109∘,m(minor arc TU)=71∘.\begin{aligned}m(\text{minor arc }TU)&=180^\circ-109^\circ,\\m(\text{minor arc }TU)&=71^\circ.\end{aligned}m(minor arc TU)m(minor arc TU)​=180∘−109∘,=71∘.​

    Two secants from external PPP have orders P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B and P,D,CP,D,CP,D,C. The circle order is A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D. Which route is the far arc in the half-difference rule for ∠APD\angle APD∠APD?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The second intersections are B and C. The far route between them must avoid both first intersections A and D. The near arc instead joins A and D.

    m∠APD=12(far arc−near arc).m\angle APD=\tfrac12(\text{far arc}-\text{near arc}).m∠APD=21​(far arc−near arc).
