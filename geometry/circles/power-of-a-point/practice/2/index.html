Question 1 of 12
  1. 1

    Chords have orders A,P,BA,P,BA,P,B and C,P,DC,P,DC,P,D. The lengths PA,PBPA,PBPA,PB are x,x+2x,x+2x,x+2, and PC,PDPC,PDPC,PD are 3,83,83,8. Here xxx is a length. Find xxx.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The chord products give

    x(x+2)=24,(x−4)(x+6)=0.\begin{aligned}x(x+2)&=24,\\(x-4)(x+6)&=0.\end{aligned}x(x+2)(x−4)(x+6)​=24,=0.​

    The candidates are 4 and -6. Since x itself is a length, only 4 is valid; the first chord parts are 4 and 6.

  2. 2

    A secant has external length xxx and inside chord length 777. A tangent from the same point has length 30\sqrt{30}30​. Here xxx is a length. Find xxx.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The whole secant length is x+7.

    x(x+7)=30,(x−3)(x+10)=0.\begin{aligned}x(x+7)&=30,\\(x-3)(x+10)&=0.\end{aligned}x(x+7)(x−3)(x+10)​=30,=0.​

    Only the positive candidate 3 is a length. It gives external 3 and whole 10.

  3. 3

    Secants have orders P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B and P,C,DP,C,DP,C,D. The lengths PA,AB,PCPA,AB,PCPA,AB,PC are 6,10,86,10,86,10,8. Find CDCDCD.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The first whole secant is 16.

    6(16)=8(PD),PD=12,CD=12−8,CD=4.\begin{aligned}6(16)&=8(PD),\\PD&=12,\\CD&=12-8,\\CD&=4.\end{aligned}6(16)PDCDCD​=8(PD),=12,=12−8,=4.​
  4. 4

    A tangent has length 55\sqrt{55}55​. A secant from the same external point has external length 555. Find its inside chord length.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The tangent square is 55. Divide by the external part to find the whole, then subtract.

    PB=55/5,PB=11,AB=11−5,AB=6.\begin{aligned}PB&=55/5,\\PB&=11,\\AB&=11-5,\\AB&=6.\end{aligned}PBPBABAB​=55/5,=11,=11−5,=6.​
  5. 5

    A circle has radius 121212 and an interior point at center distance 999. Which pair gives signed power and positive chord-part product?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The signed value is negative.

    Pow⁡(P)=92−122,Pow⁡(P)=−63.\begin{aligned}\operatorname{Pow}(P)&=9^2-12^2,\\\operatorname{Pow}(P)&=-63.\end{aligned}Pow(P)Pow(P)​=92−122,=−63.​

    Inside, the positive product is 63. Each component appears in two options, so both signs must be decided.

  6. 6

    A circle has radius 121212. The signed power of external PPP is 818181. Find OPOPOP.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Add radius squared to signed power.

    OP2=81+144,OP2=225,OP=15.\begin{aligned}OP^2&=81+144,\\OP^2&=225,\\OP&=15.\end{aligned}OP2OP2OP​=81+144,=225,=15.​

    The distance exceeds the radius.

  7. 7

    A circle has radius 777 and OPOPOP is 111. A chord through PPP has PAPAPA of 777. Find PBPBPB.

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The point is inside. Its positive product is 48, and a part of length 7 is geometrically possible since it lies between 6 and 8, the nearest and farthest circle distances.

    7(PB)=72−12,7(PB)=48,PB=487.\begin{aligned}7(PB)&=7^2-1^2,\\7(PB)&=48,\\PB&=\frac{48}{7}.\end{aligned}7(PB)7(PB)PB​=72−12,=48,=748​.​
  8. 8

    A tangent segment has length x+1x+1x+1. A secant from the same external point has external length 222 and whole length 181818. Find the valid parameter xxx.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The tangent square is 36, so its positive length is 6.

    (x+1)2=36,x+1=6,x=5.\begin{aligned}(x+1)^2&=36,\\x+1&=6,\\x&=5.\end{aligned}(x+1)2x+1x​=36,=6,=5.​

    The other algebraic candidate, -7, makes the tangent length -6 and is invalid.

  9. 9

    An external secant has lengths x+8x+8x+8 to the near endpoint and x+12x+12x+12 to the far endpoint. A tangent from the same point has length 353\sqrt535​. Here xxx is a parameter. Find every valid xxx.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The tangent square is 45.

    (x+8)(x+12)=45,(x+3)(x+17)=0.\begin{aligned}(x+8)(x+12)&=45,\\(x+3)(x+17)&=0.\end{aligned}(x+8)(x+12)(x+3)(x+17)​=45,=0.​

    At x=-3 the lengths are 5 and 9, positive and correctly ordered. At x=-17 the lengths are -9 and -5. Only -3 fits.

  10. 10

    For two distinct secants with orders P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B and P,C,DP,C,DP,C,D, which ordered similarity matches triangle PADPADPAD?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The angle at P is shared. The angles at D and B intercept arc AC, so D matches B. The remaining match is A to C.

    △PAD∼△PCB.\triangle PAD\sim\triangle PCB.△PAD∼△PCB.
  11. 11

    A tangent at TTT and a secant P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B start at external PPP. Which ordered similarity matches triangle PTAPTAPTA?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The common angle matches P to P. The tangent-chord angle at T matches the inscribed angle at B, so T matches B and A matches T.

    △PTA∼△PBT.\triangle PTA\sim\triangle PBT.△PTA∼△PBT.
  12. 12

    Point PPP coincides with endpoint AAA on a circle, and a secant has the other endpoint BBB. Which factor makes PA⋅PBPA\cdot PBPA⋅PB zero?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The distance from P to A is zero because they are the same point. B is a distinct circle point, so PB is positive. The zero product is the on-circle boundary.

    PA=0,PA⋅PB=0.\begin{aligned}PA&=0,\\PA\cdot PB&=0.\end{aligned}PAPA⋅PB​=0,=0.​
