  1. 1

    Secants have orders P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B and P,C,DP,C,DP,C,D. The lengths PA,AB,PC,PDPA,AB,PC,PDPA,AB,PC,PD are x,x+6,4,27x,x+6,4,27x,x+6,4,27, respectively. Here xxx itself is a length. Find xxx.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The first whole secant is 2x+6.

    x(2x+6)=4(27),x2+3x−54=0,(x−6)(x+9)=0.\begin{aligned}x(2x+6)&=4(27),\\x^2+3x-54&=0,\\(x-6)(x+9)&=0.\end{aligned}x(2x+6)x2+3x−54(x−6)(x+9)​=4(27),=0,=0.​

    Only x=6 is positive. Its external and whole lengths are 6 and 18, with product 108. The other candidate gives negative lengths.

  2. 2

    A tangent from PPP has length x+3x+3x+3. A secant has external length xxx and inside chord length 151515. Find the valid xxx.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The secant whole is x+15.

    (x+3)2=x(x+15),x2+6x+9=x2+15x,9=9x,x=1.\begin{aligned}(x+3)^2&=x(x+15),\\x^2+6x+9&=x^2+15x,\\9&=9x,\\x&=1.\end{aligned}(x+3)2x2+6x+99x​=x(x+15),=x2+15x,=9x,=1.​

    The external, whole, and tangent lengths are 1, 16, and 4. They are positive and the product is 16.

  3. 3

    Chords have orders A,P,BA,P,BA,P,B and C,P,DC,P,DC,P,D. Their lengths are x+6x+6x+6, 6−x6-x6−x, 555, and 777, respectively. Here xxx is a parameter. Which gives every valid xxx?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The chord equation gives

    (x+6)(6−x)=35,36−x2=35,x2=1.\begin{aligned}(x+6)(6-x)&=35,\\36-x^2&=35,\\x^2&=1.\end{aligned}(x+6)(6−x)36−x2x2​=35,=35,=1.​

    At x=1 the first chord parts are 7 and 5. At x=-1 they are 5 and 7. Both candidates give positive lengths in the inside order. At either 6 or -6, one part is zero and the product is zero.

  4. 4

    A circle has radius 131313 and OPOPOP is 555. A chord through PPP has PAPAPA of 999. Find PBPBPB.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    P is inside. The positive chord product is 144.

    PA⋅PB=132−52,9(PB)=144,PB=16.\begin{aligned}PA\cdot PB&=13^2-5^2,\\9(PB)&=144,\\PB&=16.\end{aligned}PA⋅PB9(PB)PB​=132−52,=144,=16.​
  5. 5

    An external point has signed power 919191 and center distance 101010. Find the diameter of the circle.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Recover radius squared, then double the radius.

    91=102−r2,r2=9,r=3,2r=6.\begin{aligned}91&=10^2-r^2,\\r^2&=9,\\r&=3,\\2r&=6.\end{aligned}91r2r2r​=102−r2,=9,=3,=6.​
  6. 6

    Chord ABABAB has length 141414 and contains interior PPP. Write PA=xPA=xPA=x, so PBPBPB is 14−x14-x14−x. Another chord through PPP has parts 444 and 101010. Which lists every possible PAPAPA?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The product equation is

    x(14−x)=40,x2−14x+40=0,(x−4)(x−10)=0.\begin{aligned}x(14-x)&=40,\\x^2-14x+40&=0,\\(x-4)(x-10)&=0.\end{aligned}x(14−x)x2−14x+40(x−4)(x−10)​=40,=0,=0.​

    Both 4 and 10 lie strictly between 0 and 14, so each gives positive parts. Negative values fail both the equation and the length requirement.

  7. 7

    Secants have orders P,A,BP,A,BP,A,B and P,C,DP,C,DP,C,D. The lengths PA,PB,PC,CDPA,PB,PC,CDPA,PB,PC,CD are 2,18,3,92,18,3,92,18,3,9. A tangent from PPP touches at TTT. Which pair gives ABABAB and PTPTPT?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The first inside chord is whole minus external. The second product checks the first.

    AB=18−2,AB=16,PC⋅PD=3(3+9),PC⋅PD=36,PT=6.\begin{aligned}AB&=18-2,\\AB&=16,\\PC\cdot PD&=3(3+9),\\PC\cdot PD&=36,\\PT&=6.\end{aligned}ABABPC⋅PDPC⋅PDPT​=18−2,=16,=3(3+9),=36,=6.​

    Both components must be correct.

  8. 8

    From △PTA∼△PBT\triangle PTA\sim\triangle PBT△PTA∼△PBT, which proportion directly gives the tangent-secant product for a general configuration?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Read the order P to P, T to B, A to T. Thus PT corresponds to PB, and PA corresponds to PT. The selected proportion cross-multiplies to the tangent square equaling external times whole.

    PTPB=PAPT,PT2=PA⋅PB.\begin{aligned}\frac{PT}{PB}&=\frac{PA}{PT},\\PT^2&=PA\cdot PB.\end{aligned}PBPT​PT2​=PTPA​,=PA⋅PB.​
  9. 9

    A point PPP is 555 units from the center of a circle and has signed power −50-50−50. Find the radius.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Use the signed definition, keeping its negative value.

    −50=52−r2,r2=75,r=53.\begin{aligned}-50&=5^2-r^2,\\r^2&=75,\\r&=5\sqrt3.\end{aligned}−50r2r​=52−r2,=75,=53​.​

    This radius exceeds 5, consistent with an interior point.

  10. 10

    A diameter lies on a secant ray in order P,A,O,BP,A,O,BP,A,O,B, where OOO is the center. The lengths PA,PBPA,PBPA,PB are 7,197,197,19. Find OPOPOP.

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The center is the midpoint of the diameter, hence halfway between the distances to A and B from P.

    OP=7+192,OP=13.\begin{aligned}OP&=\frac{7+19}{2},\\OP&=13.\end{aligned}OPOP​=27+19​,=13.​
  11. 11

    A secant from external PPP has near and far lengths x+2x+2x+2 and x+9x+9x+9. Its tangent segment has length 2152\sqrt{15}215​. Here xxx is a parameter. Find the valid xxx.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The tangent square is 60.

    (x+2)(x+9)=60,(x−3)(x+14)=0.\begin{aligned}(x+2)(x+9)&=60,\\(x-3)(x+14)&=0.\end{aligned}(x+2)(x+9)(x−3)(x+14)​=60,=0.​

    At x=3, the lengths are 5 and 12. At x=-14, the lengths are -12 and -5. Only 3 gives positive lengths in order.

  12. 12

    A circle has radius 85\sqrt{85}85​. An interior point PPP has center distance 666. An exterior point QQQ has center distance 111111. Which pair gives the positive chord-part product at PPP and a tangent length from QQQ?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    For the inside point, subtract its squared center distance from radius squared. For the outside point, reverse that subtraction and take the positive root.

    PA⋅PB=85−36,PA⋅PB=49,QT2=121−85,QT=6.\begin{aligned}PA\cdot PB&=85-36,\\PA\cdot PB&=49,\\QT^2&=121-85,\\QT&=6.\end{aligned}PA⋅PBPA⋅PBQT2QT​=85−36,=49,=121−85,=6.​
