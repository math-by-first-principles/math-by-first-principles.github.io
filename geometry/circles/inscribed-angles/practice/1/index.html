  1. 1

    Which description defines an inscribed angle?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    An inscribed angle needs a vertex on the circle and two chord rays through two other distinct points. The other choices fail the vertex or chord conditions.

    Points A,V,B,WA,V,B,WA,V,B,W occur in that order around a circle. Which arc is intercepted by ∠AVB\angle AVB∠AVB?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The intercepted arc must join A to B without passing through V. Only arc AWB has those endpoints and excludes V.

  3. 3

    An inscribed angle intercepts an arc of 132∘132^\circ132∘. What is its measure?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Halve the intercepted arc.

    132∘/2=66∘.132^\circ/2=66^\circ.132∘/2=66∘.
  4. 4

    An inscribed angle measures 41∘41^\circ41∘. What is the measure of its intercepted arc?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Double the inscribed angle.

    2(41∘)=82∘.2(41^\circ)=82^\circ.2(41∘)=82∘.
  5. 5

    The major arc AXBAXBAXB excluding VVV measures 238∘238^\circ238∘. Find m∠AVBm\angle AVBm∠AVB.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Use the stated arc, which excludes the vertex.

    m∠AVB=238∘/2.m\angle AVB=238^\circ/2.m∠AVB=238∘/2.

    The angle measures 119 degrees.

  6. 6

    ABABAB is a diameter, and CCC is another point on the circle. Find m∠ACBm\angle ACBm∠ACB.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The intercepted arc is a semicircle.

    m∠ACB=180∘/2.m\angle ACB=180^\circ/2.m∠ACB=180∘/2.

    The angle is 90 degrees.

  7. 7

    Distinct points A,B,CA,B,CA,B,C lie on a circle. If m∠ABC=90∘m\angle ABC=90^\circm∠ABC=90∘, which segment is a diameter?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Angle ABC intercepts a semicircle with endpoints A and C. The semicircle converse makes AC a diameter.

    m(arc AC excluding B)=180∘.m(\text{arc }AC\text{ excluding }B)=180^\circ.m(arc AC excluding B)=180∘.
  8. 8

    P,QP,QP,Q lie on the same open arc between A,BA,BA,B. If m∠APB=57∘m\angle APB=57^\circm∠APB=57∘, find m∠AQBm\angle AQBm∠AQB.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Both vertices exclude the same arc AB, so their angles are equal.

    m∠AQB=57∘.m\angle AQB=57^\circ.m∠AQB=57∘.
  9. 9

    P,RP,RP,R lie on opposite open arcs between A,BA,BA,B. If m∠APB=39∘m\angle APB=39^\circm∠APB=39∘, find m∠ARBm\angle ARBm∠ARB.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Their intercepted arcs total a full circle, so their angles total 180 degrees.

    m∠ARB=180∘−39∘.m\angle ARB=180^\circ-39^\circ.m∠ARB=180∘−39∘.

    The angle is 141 degrees.
