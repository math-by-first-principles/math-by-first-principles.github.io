    Diameter VDVDVD is outside ∠AVB\angle AVB∠AVB, with ray VAVAVA between VDVDVD and VBVBVB. Angle DVBDVBDVB measures 71∘71^\circ71∘, and DVADVADVA measures 26∘26^\circ26∘. Find arc ABABAB excluding VVV.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Subtract to obtain the inscribed angle of 45 degrees, then double to obtain its arc.

    m(AB^)=2(71∘−26∘).m(\widehat{AB})=2(71^\circ-26^\circ).m(AB)=2(71∘−26∘).

    The arc measures 90 degrees.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The whole angle is half the arc, or 87 degrees. Add its parts.

    (x+7)+(2x−4)=87,3x=84,x=28.\begin{aligned}(x+7)+(2x-4)&=87,\\3x&=84,\\x&=28.\end{aligned}(x+7)+(2x−4)3xx​=87,=84,=28.​
  3. 3

    VAVAVA is a diameter with center OOO. In triangle VOBVOBVOB, the angle at OOO measures (4x+12)∘(4x+12)^\circ(4x+12)∘, and the angle at BBB measures (x+6)∘(x+6)^\circ(x+6)∘. Find arc BABABA excluding VVV.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The radii make the two base angles equal.

    (4x+12)+2(x+6)=180,6x=156,x=26.\begin{aligned}(4x+12)+2(x+6)&=180,\\6x&=156,\\x&=26.\end{aligned}(4x+12)+2(x+6)6xx​=180,=156,=26.​

    The central angle VOB is 116 degrees. Subtract from the straight angle along the diameter.

    m(BA^)=180∘−116∘.m(\widehat{BA})=180^\circ-116^\circ.m(BA)=180∘−116∘.

    The requested arc measures 64 degrees.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The angle at Q excludes the minor arc AB, so that full arc measures 164 degrees. Since R lies on it, subtract arc AR.

    m(RB^)=2(82∘)−76∘.m(\widehat{RB})=2(82^\circ)-76^\circ.m(RB)=2(82∘)−76∘.

    Arc RB measures 88 degrees.

  5. 5

    An inscribed angle measures (4x+30)∘(4x+30)^\circ(4x+30)∘. Its intercepted major arc measures (7x+80)∘(7x+80)^\circ(7x+80)∘. Find xxx.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The intercepted arc is twice the angle, including when it is major.

    2(4x+30)=7x+80,8x+60=7x+80,x=20.\begin{aligned}2(4x+30)&=7x+80,\\8x+60&=7x+80,\\x&=20.\end{aligned}2(4x+30)8x+60x​=7x+80,=7x+80,=20.​

    This gives an angle of 110 degrees and a major arc of 220 degrees, both valid.

  6. 6

    Triangle ABCABCABC is inscribed in a circle and has a right angle at CCC. Its angles at A,BA,BA,B measure (4x+8)∘(4x+8)^\circ(4x+8)∘ and (6x+2)∘(6x+2)^\circ(6x+2)∘. Find arc ACACAC excluding BBB.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The other two angles total 90 degrees.

    (4x+8)+(6x+2)=90,x=8.\begin{aligned}(4x+8)+(6x+2)&=90,\\x&=8.\end{aligned}(4x+8)+(6x+2)x​=90,=8.​

    The angle at B is 50 degrees, so double it for its intercepted arc.

    m(arc AC excluding B)=2(50∘).m(\text{arc }AC\text{ excluding }B)=2(50^\circ).m(arc AC excluding B)=2(50∘).

    The arc measures 100 degrees.

    Opposite tangent rays TR,TSTR,TSTR,TS form angles with chord TATATA of (3x+11)∘(3x+11)^\circ(3x+11)∘ and (5x+9)∘(5x+9)^\circ(5x+9)∘, respectively. Ray TRTRTR lies on the same side of line OTOTOT as AAA. Find minor arc TATATA.

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The two tangent rays form a straight angle.

    (3x+11)+(5x+9)=180,x=20.\begin{aligned}(3x+11)+(5x+9)&=180,\\x&=20.\end{aligned}(3x+11)+(5x+9)x​=180,=20.​

    Ray TR selects the minor arc. Its angle is 71 degrees.

    m(TA^)=2(71∘).m(\widehat{TA})=2(71^\circ).m(TA)=2(71∘).

    The minor arc measures 142 degrees.

    Ray TSTSTS is tangent at TTT on the opposite side of line OTOTOT from AAA. Given m∠STA=94∘m\angle STA=94^\circm∠STA=94∘, point BBB on major arc TATATA, and m∠TAB=31∘m\angle TAB=31^\circm∠TAB=31∘, find m∠ATBm\angle ATBm∠ATB.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The tangent ray selects the major arc of 188 degrees, leaving a minor arc of 172 degrees. Vertex B selects that minor arc, so its inscribed angle is 86 degrees. Now use the triangle sum.

    m∠ATB=180∘−86∘−31∘.m\angle ATB=180^\circ-86^\circ-31^\circ.m∠ATB=180∘−86∘−31∘.

    The angle at T measures 63 degrees.
