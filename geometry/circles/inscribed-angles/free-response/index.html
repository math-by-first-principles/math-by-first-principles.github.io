  7. Problem 7 Recognize a diameter

    Answer

    This angle is 90∘90^\circ90∘, so its intercepted arc ABABAB excluding CCC measures 180∘180^\circ180∘.

    By the converse of the semicircle theorem, ABABAB is a diameter of this circle.

    Every diameter passes through its center OOO.

    Answer

    Yes. Segment ABABAB is a diameter and passes through OOO.

    Key idea

    A proved right angle can establish a diameter by the converse.
