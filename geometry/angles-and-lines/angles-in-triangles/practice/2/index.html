  7. 7

    Which condition allows the accepted parallel foundation to supply a line through C parallel to AB in the angle-sum proof?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A triangle has three noncollinear vertices. Therefore C is outside the line through A and B.

    The accepted foundation supplies a parallel through an external point in the same plane. The construction does not depend on equal sides or an acute angle.
