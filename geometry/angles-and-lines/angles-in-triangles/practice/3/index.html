    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    In triangle ACDACDACD, rays ADADAD and ABABAB agree, so its angle at AAA is 33∘33^\circ33∘.

    m∠ADC=180∘−33∘−41∘=106∘.m\angle ADC=180^\circ-33^\circ-41^\circ=106^\circ.m∠ADC=180∘−33∘−41∘=106∘.

    m∠CDB=180∘−106∘=74∘.m\angle CDB=180^\circ-106^\circ=74^\circ.m∠CDB=180∘−106∘=74∘.

    Triangle CDBCDBCDB has 59∘59^\circ59∘ at BBB, so m∠DCB=180∘−74∘−59∘=47∘.m\angle DCB=180^\circ-74^\circ-59^\circ=47^\circ.m∠DCB=180∘−74∘−59∘=47∘.

  3. 3

    A triangle has angle measures (2x+1)∘(2x+1)^\circ(2x+1)∘, (4x−3)∘(4x-3)^\circ(4x−3)∘, and (5x+6)∘(5x+6)^\circ(5x+6)∘. Which describes the triangle after solving for xxx?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The three variable terms total 11x11x11x, and the constant terms total 444. The triangle sum therefore gives 11x+4=180.11x+4=180.11x+4=180.

    Subtract 444 and divide by 111111 to obtain x=16x=16x=16. Substitution gives angle measures 33∘,61∘,86∘33^\circ,61^\circ,86^\circ33∘,61∘,86∘.

    Their sum is 180∘180^\circ180∘ and each is less than 90∘90^\circ90∘, so the triangle is acute with largest angle 86∘86^\circ86∘.

    Two angles of a nondegenerate triangle are complementary. Which argument establishes its angle classification?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Complementary means the two measures total 90∘90^\circ90∘. The triangle sum leaves 180∘−90∘=90∘.180^\circ-90^\circ=90^\circ.180∘−90∘=90∘.

    So the remaining angle is right, and the triangle is right. The two complementary angles need not be congruent.
