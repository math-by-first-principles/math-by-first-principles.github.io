  1. Problem 1 Name the angles from the extension

    Triangle GHKGHKGHK has HKHKHK extended beyond KKK to LLL, with H,K,LH,K,LH,K,L in that order. Name the exterior angle at KKK, its adjacent interior angle, and its two remote interior angles.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle GHK has vertices G, H and K. Side HK continues on the same line beyond K to L. Arcs show angle positions without names or measures.

    1. Hint 1

      Locate the vertex where the side continues beyond the triangle.

    2. Hint 2

      The remote interior angles are the ones at the other two triangle vertices.

    Answer

    Exterior ∠GKL\angle GKL∠GKL; adjacent interior ∠GKH\angle GKH∠GKH; remote interiors ∠HGK\angle HGK∠HGK and ∠GHK\angle GHK∠GHK (reversing the first and third letters of any angle name is equivalent).

    Full solution

    The continued side ends at KKK and runs toward LLL, so ∠GKL\angle GKL∠GKL is the exterior angle.

    It uses the triangle side KGKGKG and the extended ray KLKLKL.

    The interior angle at the same vertex is ∠GKH\angle GKH∠GKH.

    Rays KHKHKH and KLKLKL are opposite, so this interior angle and the exterior angle form a linear pair.

    The remote interior angles are at the other vertices, GGG and HHH.

    Their names are ∠HGK\angle HGK∠HGK and ∠GHK\angle GHK∠GHK.

    Each name keeps its vertex in the middle.

    Answer

    Exterior ∠GKL\angle GKL∠GKL; adjacent interior ∠GKH\angle GKH∠GKH; remote interiors ∠HGK\angle HGK∠HGK and ∠GHK\angle GHK∠GHK (reversing the first and third letters of any angle name is equivalent).

    Key idea

    A chosen exterior angle fixes one adjacent interior angle and two remote interior angles.

    Keeping this choice only at BBB mixes directions and does not describe the original boundary traversal.

    Case B.

    The positive measures alone cannot verify the direction record.

    To repair it, restore the original left exterior choice at BBB, or reverse the entire traversal and use right exterior choices at all three vertices.

    Answer

    Case A: at A,B,CA,B,CA,B,C, the turns are 127∘,119∘,114∘127^\circ,119^\circ,114^\circ127∘,119∘,114∘, totaling 360∘360^\circ360∘. Case B: the claim is false; the sum remains 360∘360^\circ360∘, but the replacement reverses the turn direction at BBB.

    Key idea

    A correct numerical exterior-angle total does not by itself check whether the chosen turns follow one traversal consistently.
