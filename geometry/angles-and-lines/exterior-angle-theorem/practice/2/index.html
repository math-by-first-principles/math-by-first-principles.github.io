  5. 5

    A measurement record for a triangle gives an exterior angle of 87∘87^\circ87∘ and one of its remote interior angles as 92∘92^\circ92∘. Which conclusion follows?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The exterior angle theorem would give the other remote measure as

    87∘−92∘=−5∘.87^\circ-92^\circ=-5^\circ.87∘−92∘=−5∘.

    A triangle's interior measures are positive. Equivalently, the exterior angle inequality requires the exterior to exceed each remote angle, which this record violates.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Let the adjacent interior measure be ccc, so the exterior measure is 3c3c3c. They form a linear pair.

    c+3c=180∘c+3c=180^\circc+3c=180∘

    Thus c=45∘c=45^\circc=45∘ and the exterior measure is 3(45∘)=135∘3(45^\circ)=135^\circ3(45∘)=135∘.
