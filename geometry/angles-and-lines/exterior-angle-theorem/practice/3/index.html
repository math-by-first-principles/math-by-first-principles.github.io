    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The exterior turns total 360∘360^\circ360∘.

    (4x+9)+(3x+21)+(5x+6)=360(4x+9)+(3x+21)+(5x+6)=360(4x+9)+(3x+21)+(5x+6)=360

    This gives 12x+36=36012x+36=36012x+36=360, so x=27x=27x=27. The exterior turns are 117∘117^\circ117∘, 102∘102^\circ102∘, and 141∘141^\circ141∘.

    In triangle ABCABCABC, DDD lies strictly between AAA and BBB, and EEE lies strictly between AAA and CCC. Segment DEDEDE is drawn. Given m∠BAC=47∘m\angle BAC=47^\circm∠BAC=47∘, m∠AED=61∘m\angle AED=61^\circm∠AED=61∘, and m∠ABC=59∘m\angle ABC=59^\circm∠ABC=59∘, what is m∠BDEm\angle BDEm∠BDE?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

  4. 4

    Two triangles each have a chosen non-reflex exterior angle. Compared with the first triangle, the second has one remote interior angle 8∘8^\circ8∘ larger and the other remote interior angle 8∘8^\circ8∘ smaller. Both triangles have positive interior measures. How do the exterior measures compare?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Let the first remote measures be aaa and bbb. Their sum is the first exterior measure.

    The second remote measures are a+8∘a+8^\circa+8∘ and b−8∘b-8^\circb−8∘. Adding cancels the increase against the decrease, giving the second exterior measure

    e2=a+b.e_2=a+b.e2​=a+b.

    The exterior angle theorem therefore gives equal exterior measures.

    Which record passes both necessary checks for three non-reflex exterior angles, one at each vertex of a triangle: each lies strictly between 0∘0^\circ0∘ and 180∘180^\circ180∘, and their sum is 360∘360^\circ360∘?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The correct record has three measures strictly between 0∘0^\circ0∘ and 180∘180^\circ180∘. Its total checks in two additions.

    98∘+141∘=239∘98^\circ+141^\circ=239^\circ98∘+141∘=239∘

    239∘+121∘=360∘239^\circ+121^\circ=360^\circ239∘+121∘=360∘

    The first wrong total is 350∘350^\circ350∘, and the last is 370∘370^\circ370∘. The record with 180∘180^\circ180∘ passes the sum check but fails the strict upper bound; its adjacent interior measure would be zero.

    In triangle ABCABCABC, the interior angle at CCC measures 58∘58^\circ58∘. A non-reflex exterior angle at AAA is 24∘24^\circ24∘ larger than a non-reflex exterior angle at BBB. What is the exterior measure at AAA?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The exterior measure at CCC is 180∘−58∘=122∘180^\circ-58^\circ=122^\circ180∘−58∘=122∘. The other two exterior measures therefore total 360∘−122∘=238∘360^\circ-122^\circ=238^\circ360∘−122∘=238∘.

    If the exterior at BBB has measure uuu, the one at AAA has measure u+24∘u+24^\circu+24∘. Thus

    2u+24∘=238∘.2u+24^\circ=238^\circ.2u+24∘=238∘.

    Subtracting 24∘24^\circ24∘ and dividing by 2 gives u=107∘u=107^\circu=107∘.

    The requested exterior measure at AAA is 107∘+24∘=131∘107^\circ+24^\circ=131^\circ107∘+24∘=131∘.

  9. 9

    In a proof about triangle ABCABCABC, a point DDD is known only to lie outside the triangle. A proposed argument writes m∠ACB+m∠ACD=180∘m\angle ACB+m\angle ACD=180^\circm∠ACB+m∠ACD=180∘. Which additional given justifies that line?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    To use the linear-pair rule, the angles must be adjacent with opposite noncommon rays. They already share ray CACACA.

    The order B,C,DB,C,DB,C,D makes CBCBCB and CDCDCD opposite rays and places the two angles on opposite sides of CACACA. A point merely outside the triangle does not establish that structure.

  10. 10

    One remote interior angle for a triangle's chosen exterior angle is a right angle. Which conclusion must hold?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Let the other positive remote measure be bbb. The exterior angle theorem gives

    e=90∘+b.e=90^\circ+b.e=90∘+b.

    Thus the exterior measure is greater than 90∘90^\circ90∘.

    It is also less than 180∘180^\circ180∘, since its adjacent interior measure is positive. Thus the exterior angle is obtuse. Its supplement is positive and less than 90∘90^\circ90∘, so the adjacent interior angle is acute.

    A triangle's exterior angle measures (2y+29)∘(2y+29)^\circ(2y+29)∘, and its remote interior angles measure (y+13)∘(y+13)^\circ(y+13)∘ and (y+16)∘(y+16)^\circ(y+16)∘. Does the exterior angle theorem determine a unique real value of yyy?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Substitution into the theorem gives

    (y+13)+(y+16)=2y+29(y+13)+(y+16)=2y+29(y+13)+(y+16)=2y+29

    Combining terms gives 2y+29=2y+292y+29=2y+292y+29=2y+29, which is true for every yyy. Angle positivity still limits the allowed values, but it does not select just one. For instance, y=1y=1y=1 and y=2y=2y=2 each give three positive interior measures and a non-reflex exterior angle.
