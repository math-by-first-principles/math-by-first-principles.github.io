  1. 1

    Triangle PQRPQRPQR has QRQRQR extended beyond RRR to SSS, with Q,R,SQ,R,SQ,R,S in that order. Which are the remote interior angles for ∠PRS\angle PRS∠PRS?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The exterior angle has vertex RRR. Its remote interior angles are the triangle's interior angles at the other vertices, PPP and QQQ.

    The interior angle ∠PRQ\angle PRQ∠PRQ is adjacent to the exterior angle, so it is not remote.

    Triangle ABCABCABC has BCBCBC extended beyond CCC to DDD, with B,C,DB,C,DB,C,D in that order. If m∠BAC=26∘m\angle BAC=26^\circm∠BAC=26∘ and m∠ABC=83∘m\angle ABC=83^\circm∠ABC=83∘, what is m∠ACDm\angle ACDm∠ACD?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The angles at AAA and BBB are remote interior angles for the exterior angle at CCC. Add their measures by the exterior angle theorem.

    26∘+83∘=109∘26^\circ+83^\circ=109^\circ26∘+83∘=109∘

    Triangle ABCABCABC has BCBCBC extended beyond CCC to DDD, with B,C,DB,C,DB,C,D in that order. If m∠ACD=142∘m\angle ACD=142^\circm∠ACD=142∘ and m∠BAC=68∘m\angle BAC=68^\circm∠BAC=68∘, what is m∠ABCm\angle ABCm∠ABC?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The exterior measure is the sum of the two remote measures.

    142∘=68∘+m∠ABC142^\circ=68^\circ+m\angle ABC142∘=68∘+m∠ABC

    Subtract the known remote measure to get m∠ABC=74∘m\angle ABC=74^\circm∠ABC=74∘. The 38∘38^\circ38∘ supplement of the exterior angle is instead the adjacent angle at CCC.

  4. 4

    Triangle ABCABCABC has BCBCBC extended beyond CCC to DDD, with B,C,DB,C,DB,C,D in that order. If m∠ACB=113∘m\angle ACB=113^\circm∠ACB=113∘, what is m∠ACDm\angle ACDm∠ACD?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The given interior angle is adjacent to the exterior angle. Their noncommon rays CBCBCB and CDCDCD are opposite, so they form a linear pair.

    m∠ACD=180∘−113∘=67∘m\angle ACD=180^\circ-113^\circ=67^\circm∠ACD=180∘−113∘=67∘

    A triangle has a side extended at one vertex. The remote interior angles for that exterior angle measure 44∘44^\circ44∘ and 63∘63^\circ63∘. What is its measure?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The given angles are the two remote interior angles. Their sum is the exterior measure, not its supplement.

    44∘+63∘=107∘44^\circ+63^\circ=107^\circ44∘+63∘=107∘

  6. 6

    A triangle's exterior angle has measure eee, and its remote interior angles have measures aaa and bbb. Which must be true?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The exterior angle theorem gives e=a+be=a+be=a+b. Both remote measures are positive in a triangle.

    Subtract each remote measure in turn.

    e−a=b>0∘e-a=b>0^\circe−a=b>0∘

    e−b=a>0∘e-b=a>0^\circe−b=a>0∘

    Both differences are positive, proving both inequalities.

    A path follows the three sides of a triangle once, taking one non-reflex left exterior turn at every vertex. Two turns measure 151∘151^\circ151∘ and 104∘104^\circ104∘. What is the third?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    These are three consistently oriented exterior turns, one per vertex, so their measures total 360∘360^\circ360∘.

    360∘−151∘−104∘=105∘360^\circ-151^\circ-104^\circ=105^\circ360∘−151∘−104∘=105∘

  9. 9

    At one vertex of a triangle, either incident side can be extended beyond the vertex, making two non-reflex exterior angles. How are their measures related?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Let the interior measure be ccc. Each side extension makes its exterior angle a linear pair with that same interior angle.

    Each exterior measure is the same supplement.

    e1=180∘−ce_1=180^\circ-ce1​=180∘−c

    e2=180∘−ce_2=180^\circ-ce2​=180∘−c

    Thus the two exterior angles have equal measures. The choices can represent opposite turning directions.

  10. 10

    In triangle ABCABCABC, a distinct point DDD lies outside the triangle. Which extra condition makes ∠ACD\angle ACD∠ACD the exterior angle formed by continuing side BCBCBC past CCC?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A side continuation must lie on the original side's line, beyond its endpoint. The order B,C,DB,C,DB,C,D makes rays CBCBCB and CDCDCD opposite.

    That order gives the linear pair between ∠ACB\angle ACB∠ACB and ∠ACD\angle ACD∠ACD. Merely placing DDD outside the triangle does not give it.

    Triangle ABCABCABC has BCBCBC extended beyond CCC to DDD, with B,C,DB,C,DB,C,D in that order. The measures in degrees are m∠BAC=2x+7m\angle BAC=2x+7m∠BAC=2x+7, m∠ABC=3x+8m\angle ABC=3x+8m∠ABC=3x+8, and m∠ACD=7x−5m\angle ACD=7x-5m∠ACD=7x−5. Which equation follows directly from the exterior angle theorem?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The angles at AAA and BBB are remote; their sum equals the exterior angle at CCC.

    (2x+7)+(3x+8)=7x−5(2x+7)+(3x+8)=7x-5(2x+7)+(3x+8)=7x−5

    The exterior angle is not one of the triangle's three interior angles. Neither remote angle is its adjacent linear-pair partner.

  12. 12

    An exterior angle of a triangle measures 90∘90^\circ90∘. One of its remote interior angles measures 36∘36^\circ36∘. What is the other remote measure?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The remote measures add to the exterior measure.

    90∘−36∘=54∘90^\circ-36^\circ=54^\circ90∘−36∘=54∘

    The adjacent interior angle is also 90∘90^\circ90∘, but it is not the requested remote angle.
