    Text description of this figure

  5. Problem 5 An exterior sum equation

    In the numbered diagram, ℓ∥m\ell\parallel mℓ∥m, m∠2=(3y+5)∘m\angle2=(3y+5)^\circm∠2=(3y+5)∘, and m∠7=(7y+15)∘m\angle7=(7y+15)^\circm∠7=(7y+15)∘. Find yyy and both angle measures. Justify the equation.

    Text description of this figure

    Two parallel horizontal lines l above m are crossed by a transversal rising from lower left to upper right.       Identify whether the two exterior angles lie on the same or opposite sides of the transversal.

    2. Hint 2

    10y+20=180.10y+20=180.10y+20=180.

    Both are positive and less than 180∘180^\circ180∘; their sum is 180∘180^\circ180∘.

    Answer

    y=16y=16y=16; m∠2=53∘m\angle2=53^\circm∠2=53∘ and m∠7=127∘m\angle7=127^\circm∠7=127∘.

    m∠B=52∘.m\angle B=52^\circ.m∠B=52∘.

    Angle C is vertical to angle B at the lower crossing, so m∠C=52∘m\angle C=52^\circm∠C=52∘ as well.

    Answer

    m∠B=52∘m\angle B=52^\circm∠B=52∘ and m∠C=52∘m\angle C=52^\circm∠C=52∘.

  9. Problem 9 A total is not the whole check

    A transversal crosses two given distinct parallel lines. Let L be the sum of the two interior angles on its left and R the sum on its right. Compare these proposed records.

    A. L=180∘L=180^\circL=180∘ and R=180∘R=180^\circR=180∘.

    B. L=186∘L=186^\circL=186∘ and R=174∘R=174^\circR=174∘.

    Which record is ruled out by the angle form of Euclid's parallel postulate, and why? State what this check says about the other record, without reversing an angle theorem.

    1. Hint 1

      Check each side separately, not only the combined total.

    2. Hint 2

      The angle postulate predicts an intersection when one same-side interior sum is below 180 degrees.

    3. Hint 3

      A record agreeing with a necessary condition is not, by that fact alone, a proof of parallelism.

    Answer

    B is ruled out because its right sum is below 180∘180^\circ180∘; A is not ruled out by this check.

    Full solution

    A.

    Both proposed sums equal 180∘180^\circ180∘, so neither triggers the postulate's below-180∘180^\circ180∘ condition.

    The record agrees with the necessary sums for the given parallel lines.

    This check does not reverse the theorem to prove parallelism from a record.

    B.

    The total still equals 360∘360^\circ360∘, but R=174∘R=174^\circR=174∘ is below 180∘180^\circ180∘.

    The angle postulate forces the crossed lines to meet on the right side.

    That contradicts the given parallelism, so record B is ruled out.

    Answer

    B is ruled out because its right sum is below 180∘180^\circ180∘; A is not ruled out by this check.

    Key idea

    The 360-degree combined total and the prohibition of either side being below 180 degrees are both needed.
