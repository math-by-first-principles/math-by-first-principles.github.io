    In the numbered diagram, ℓ∥m\ell\parallel mℓ∥m. The measures in degrees are m∠1=2x+1m\angle1=2x+1m∠1=2x+1, m∠2=3x+19m\angle2=3x+19m∠2=3x+19, and m∠5=4y−7m\angle5=4y-7m∠5=4y−7. Find (x,y)(x,y)(x,y).

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Angles 1 and 2 form a linear pair, so 2x+1+3x+19=1802x+1+3x+19=1802x+1+3x+19=180.

    5x+20=1805x+20=1805x+20=180

    Thus x=32x=32x=32 and angle 1 measures 65 degrees. Corresponding angle 5 also measures 65 degrees.

    4y−7=654y-7=654y−7=65

    Adding 7 and dividing by 4 gives y=18y=18y=18.

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    In the diagram, ℓ∥m\ell\parallel mℓ∥m, and transversals meet at PPP on ℓ\ellℓ. The marked interior angles at QQQ and RRR measure 42∘42^\circ42∘ and 61∘61^\circ61∘. Find m∠QPRm\angle QPRm∠QPR.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Along PQPQPQ, alternate interior congruence places 42 degrees between the leftward ray of ℓ\ellℓ and PQPQPQ. Along PRPRPR, it places 61 degrees between PRPRPR and the rightward ray of ℓ\ellℓ.

    These two angles and ∠QPR\angle QPR∠QPR fill the straight angle below ℓ\ellℓ.

    m∠QPR=180∘−42∘−61∘m\angle QPR=180^\circ-42^\circ-61^\circm∠QPR=180∘−42∘−61∘

    Therefore angle QPR measures 77 degrees.

  5. 5

    For the numbered diagram with ℓ∥m\ell\parallel mℓ∥m, which substitution proves that the same-side exterior angles 1,81,81,8 are supplementary?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Corresponding angles give equal measures for angles 1 and 5. The local linear pair gives m∠5+m∠8=180∘m\angle5+m\angle8=180^\circm∠5+m∠8=180∘.

    Substituting the equal measure of angle 1 yields the required exterior sum. No equality between angles 1 and 8 has been assumed.

  6. 6

    A transversal is moved so that it still crosses the same two distinct parallel lines at distinct points. Which conclusion remains guaranteed for every such position?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Changing the transversal can change each individual measure. The hypotheses of the proved same-side interior theorem still hold at every allowed position.

    Therefore each of its two interior sums remains 180 degrees. Parallelism does not force eight right angles or a fixed individual measure.

    At the lower crossing, angles 5 and 7 measure 51 degrees. The upper neighboring measures are 146 degrees and the lower neighboring measures are 129 degrees, so the other proposed equalities fail.

  8. 8

    Point PPP is outside line ℓ\ellℓ. Lines aaa and bbb lie in the plane of PPP and ℓ\ellℓ, pass through PPP, and are both parallel to ℓ\ellℓ. What does the accepted uniqueness foundation imply?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The foundation guarantees exactly one parallel to ℓ\ellℓ through the external point PPP. Both aaa and bbb satisfy that same requirement.

    Uniqueness means they cannot be distinct lines, so the two names describe one line.

  9. 9

    In a proposed parallel-line configuration, the two same-side interior sums are 183∘183^\circ183∘ and 177∘177^\circ177∘. Why is the configuration impossible even though the sums total 360∘360^\circ360∘?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Their total is indeed 360 degrees, as required by angle addition. But the angle postulate also applies to either side separately.

    Since 177 degrees is below 180 degrees, the crossed lines must meet on that side. That contradicts the parallel hypothesis.

    Two transversals cross the same two distinct parallel lines. Along ttt, a corresponding pair has measures (7p+5)∘(7p+5)^\circ(7p+5)∘ and (3p+49)∘(3p+49)^\circ(3p+49)∘. Along uuu, a same-side exterior pair has measures (8q+10)∘(8q+10)^\circ(8q+10)∘ and (6q+30)∘(6q+30)^\circ(6q+30)∘. Find p+qp+qp+q.

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The corresponding pair along ttt is congruent, so 7p+5=3p+497p+5=3p+497p+5=3p+49. Subtracting gives 4p=444p=444p=44, hence p=11p=11p=11.

    The same-side exterior pair along uuu is supplementary.

    8q+10+6q+30=1808q+10+6q+30=1808q+10+6q+30=180

    This gives 14q=14014q=14014q=140, hence q=10q=10q=10. Thus p+q=21p+q=21p+q=21. The ttt angles each measure 82 degrees and the uuu angles each measure 90 degrees.

  11. 11

    Which proof chain derives alternate interior congruence from the accepted foundations without borrowing a converse or a later triangle theorem?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    For parallel lines the postulate excludes sums below 180 degrees, and the total of 360 degrees fixes both interior sums. A linear pair and one of these sums have a common supplement, giving corresponding congruence.

    Vertical angles connect the corresponding pair to the alternate interior pair. Each result is established before the next uses it.
