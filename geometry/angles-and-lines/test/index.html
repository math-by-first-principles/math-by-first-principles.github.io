    Since QS continues the direction RQ and X lies on P's side of RQ, SQX and QRP occupy corresponding positions.

    The crossings at Q and R are distinct, and all constructed objects lie in the triangle's plane.

    The corresponding-angle converse gives ℓ∥PR\ell\parallel PRℓ∥PR.

    The lines are distinct because Q is outside PR.

    A different correctly performed construction is acceptable if its retained arcs establish an exact angle condition and its stated pair satisfies a proved converse.

    Approximate visual agreement alone is not the justification.

    Answer

    A constructed line ℓ\ellℓ through Q with ℓ∥PR\ell\parallel PRℓ∥PR, retained copy arcs, and a valid converse pair; for example, ∠SQX\angle SQX∠SQX and ∠QRP\angle QRP∠QRP.

    Key idea

    A parallel construction needs an exact copied-angle record and the positions that make a converse apply.
