  1. 1

    Distinct coplanar lines ℓ,m\ell,mℓ,m are cut by ttt at distinct points, as numbered in the diagram. Corresponding angles 1 and 5 measure (5x−11)∘(5x-11)^\circ(5x−11)∘ and (3x+27)∘(3x+27)^\circ(3x+27)∘. What value makes the lines parallel?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    If the lines are parallel, the forward corresponding-angle theorem forces the measures to be equal. Thus every suitable value must satisfy

    5x−11=3x+275x-11=3x+275x−11=3x+27

    Subtract 3x3x3x and add 11 to get 2x=382x=382x=38, so x=19x=19x=19 is the only solution. At this value both measures are 84∘84^\circ84∘, which is valid. Conversely, their equality establishes parallelism by the corresponding-angle converse.

  2. 2

    Distinct coplanar lines ℓ,m\ell,mℓ,m are cut by ttt at distinct points, as numbered in the diagram. Same-side interior angles 3 and 6 measure (2x+17)∘(2x+17)^\circ(2x+17)∘ and (4x+13)∘(4x+13)^\circ(4x+13)∘. What value establishes parallelism?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    If the lines are parallel, the forward same-side interior theorem forces a supplementary pair. Every suitable value must therefore satisfy

    (2x+17)+(4x+13)=180(2x+17)+(4x+13)=180(2x+17)+(4x+13)=180

    Combining terms gives 6x+30=1806x+30=1806x+30=180, then 6x=1506x=1506x=150 and the unique solution x=25x=25x=25. The measures become 67∘67^\circ67∘ and 113∘113^\circ113∘. Conversely, these valid supplementary angles establish parallelism by the same-side interior converse.

  3. 3

    Distinct coplanar lines ℓ,m\ell,mℓ,m are cut by ttt at distinct points, as numbered in the diagram. Alternate exterior angles 2 and 8 measure (3x+4)∘(3x+4)^\circ(3x+4)∘ and (5x−30)∘(5x-30)^\circ(5x−30)∘. Which result makes the lines parallel?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    If the lines are parallel, the forward alternate exterior theorem forces equal measures. Every suitable value must satisfy

    3x+4=5x−303x+4=5x-303x+4=5x−30

    Adding 30 and subtracting 3x3x3x gives 34=2x34=2x34=2x, so the unique solution is x=17x=17x=17. Substitution gives 55∘55^\circ55∘ for each. Conversely, these valid congruent alternate exterior angles establish parallelism by the converse.

  4. 4

    In one plane, ℓ∥m\ell\parallel mℓ∥m and t⊥ℓt\perp\ellt⊥ℓ. One angle at ttt and mmm measures (8x−22)∘(8x-22)^\circ(8x−22)∘. Find xxx.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The perpendicular transversal theorem makes ttt perpendicular to mmm. The angle must be right.

    8x−22=908x-22=908x−22=90

    Add 22 and divide by 8 to obtain x=14x=14x=14. Substitution gives 90∘90^\circ90∘.

  5. 5

    Distinct coplanar lines ℓ,m\ell,mℓ,m are cut by ttt at distinct points, as numbered in the diagram. A proposed corresponding pair has measures (x+200)∘(x+200)^\circ(x+200)∘ and (3x+100)∘(3x+100)^\circ(3x+100)∘. Equating them gives x=50x=50x=50. Why does this not produce the stated non-reflex angles?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    At x=50x=50x=50, each expression gives a measure outside the valid range.

    50+200=25050+200=25050+200=250

    Each numbered sector at a crossing of distinct lines measures strictly between 0∘0^\circ0∘ and 180∘180^\circ180∘. The algebraic equality therefore fails the geometric conditions.

  6. 6

    Pairwise distinct coplanar lines satisfy ℓ∥m\ell\parallel mℓ∥m and n⊥mn\perp mn⊥m. Which conclusion and reason are valid?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Line nnn meets mmm at a right angle. Since ℓ∥m\ell\parallel mℓ∥m and all three lines are coplanar, the proved crossing result shows that nnn also meets ℓ\ellℓ, making nnn a transversal.

    The perpendicular transversal theorem now gives n⊥ℓn\perp\elln⊥ℓ.

  7. 7

    Distinct coplanar lines ℓ,m\ell,mℓ,m are cut by ttt at distinct points, as numbered in the diagram. Angle 1 measures 102∘102^\circ102∘. Which additional given supplies a corresponding-angle converse?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Angle 5 corresponds to angle 1 at the other crossing. Its stated 102∘102^\circ102∘ measure would give a congruent corresponding pair.

    The other facts follow locally at the upper crossing and do not constrain mmm.

  8. 8

    While copying an angle for a parallel construction, why must the new ray be placed on the specified side of the transversal?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    An angle measure and a starting ray can give rays on opposite sides.

    Choosing the specified side places the copied angle in the required corresponding position. Equality of measures without the angle positions would not justify the intended converse.

  9. 9

    In the corresponding-angle converse proof, a known parallel nnn passes through PPP. The candidate ℓ\ellℓ makes the same angle with the same ray of ttt on the same side. What justifies n=ℓn=\elln=ℓ?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The angle-measure foundation fixes a unique ray on a chosen side for an opening strictly between 0∘0^\circ0∘ and 180∘180^\circ180∘.

    The selected rays of nnn and ℓ\ellℓ coincide, so their full lines are the same. Because nnn is parallel to the original line, so is ℓ\ellℓ.

  10. 10

    Point PPP is outside mmm, PH⊥mPH\perp mPH⊥m, and HHH lies on mmm. The distance from PPP to mmm is 32 cm, and PH=5k−3PH=5k-3PH=5k−3 cm. Find kkk.

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The perpendicular segment supplies the point-to-line distance, so PH=32PH=32PH=32.

    5k−3=325k-3=325k−3=32

    Add 3 and divide by 5 to get k=7k=7k=7. Then 5(7)−3=325(7)-3=325(7)−3=32.

  11. 11

    Three pairwise distinct coplanar lines satisfy ℓ∥n\ell\parallel nℓ∥n and m∥nm\parallel nm∥n. What follows?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    If ℓ\ellℓ and mmm intersected at a point outside nnn, two distinct parallels to nnn would pass through that point.

    That contradicts the accepted uniqueness of a parallel. The distinct coplanar lines therefore do not meet and are parallel.

  12. 12

    A protractor reading suggests two corresponding angles are about 73∘73^\circ73∘. Which statement describes what this evidence supplies?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A protractor has limited precision, and the readings were explicitly approximate.

    The converse requires congruent angles, an exact condition. A measurement can check or suggest the drawing without proving that condition.
