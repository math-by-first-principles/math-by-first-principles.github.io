    An isosceles triangle has a vertex angle of 84 degrees. What is each base angle?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The two base angles are equal and use the angle sum remaining after the vertex angle.

    (180∘−84∘)/2=48∘.(180^\circ-84^\circ)/2=48^\circ.(180∘−84∘)/2=48∘.

    A triangle has three congruent interior angles. Which side classification must apply?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Apply the converse of the base-angle theorem to two pairs of equal angles. Their opposite sides are all equal, so the triangle is equilateral.

    AB=BC.BC=CA.\begin{aligned} AB&=BC. \\ BC&=CA. \end{aligned}ABBC​=BC.=CA.​

  8. 8

    What defines a median of a triangle?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A median is identified by its vertex endpoint and the midpoint of the opposite side. Perpendicularity is not part of its definition.

  9. 9

    What defines an altitude from a triangle vertex?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    An altitude is defined by a right angle with the line of the opposite side. Its foot can be on that side or its extension.

    In triangle XYZ, angles Y and Z are congruent. Which equality follows?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The side opposite Y is XZ, and the side opposite Z is XY. The converse gives equality of those two sides.

    XY=XZ.XY=XZ.XY=XZ.
