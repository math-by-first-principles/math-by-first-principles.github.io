    Triangle ABC has the equal sides

    AB=AC.\begin{aligned} AB&=AC. \end{aligned}AB​=AC.​

    Angle BAC measures 100 degrees. D lies inside BC, with

    AD=BD.\begin{aligned} AD&=BD. \end{aligned}AD​=BD.​

    What is angle DAC?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The base angles of ABC are 40 degrees. The extra equal-side pair is

    AD=BD.\begin{aligned} AD&=BD. \end{aligned}AD​=BD.​

    Triangle ABD therefore has equal opposite angles at B and A, so angle BAD is 40 degrees.

    m∠DAC=100∘−40∘.m∠DAC=60∘.\begin{aligned} m\angle DAC&=100^\circ-40^\circ. \\ m\angle DAC&=60^\circ. \end{aligned}m∠DACm∠DAC​=100∘−40∘.=60∘.​

    Answer choices for question 4

  5. 5

    In triangle ABC, angles B and C are congruent. The conclusion to be proved is

    AB=AC.\begin{aligned} AB&=AC. \end{aligned}AB​=AC.​

    Compare triangle BCA with triangle CBA. Which fact provides the included side for ASA without assuming that conclusion?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The two given equal angles are at the endpoints B and C. The side between them is BC, which is the same segment when named CB. The following equality is the conclusion and cannot be used as a given:

    AB=AC.\begin{aligned} AB&=AC. \end{aligned}AB​=AC.​

    The included-side fact is

    BC=CB.\begin{aligned} BC&=CB. \end{aligned}BC​=CB.​

    Two isosceles triangles each have a vertex angle of 104 degrees. First triangle: both equal sides are 13 centimeters.

    Second triangle: both equal sides are 17 centimeters.

    Which statement is justified?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Each pair of base angles splits the remaining 76 degrees.

    (180∘−104∘)/2=38∘.(180^\circ-104^\circ)/2=38^\circ.(180∘−104∘)/2=38∘.

    The equal sides measure 13 centimeters in one triangle and 17 centimeters in the other, preventing congruence.

  11. 11

    The median from the vertex between the equal sides divides an isosceles triangle into congruent halves. The two angles at its foot are both t degrees and form a linear pair. Which equation establishes that this median is also an altitude?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A linear pair totals 180 degrees, and congruence makes its two angles equal.

    t+t=180.t+t=180.t+t=180.

    Thus each angle is 90 degrees and the median is perpendicular to the base.

    In triangle ABC, D lies inside BC, AD is perpendicular to BC, angle BAD is 30 degrees, and angle DAC is 40 degrees. What does this show about a claim that every altitude halves the angle at its starting vertex?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The angle parts are 30 and 40 degrees, so AD does not bisect angle BAC. The smaller triangles have remaining angles of 60 and 50 degrees, consistent with a triangle.

    30∘+40∘=70∘.30^\circ+40^\circ=70^\circ.30∘+40∘=70∘.
