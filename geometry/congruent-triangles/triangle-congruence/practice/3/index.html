  1. 1

    Two scalene triangles have

    AB=5BC=9CA=11PQ=11QR=5RP=9.\begin{aligned} AB&=5 \\ BC&=9 \\ CA&=11 \\ PQ&=11 \\ QR&=5 \\ RP&=9. \end{aligned}ABBCCAPQQRRP​=5=9=11=11=5=9.​

    Which ordered statement follows from these side pairs?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    At AAA, lengths 5 and 11 meet; they meet at QQQ in the other triangle.

    At BBB, lengths 5 and 9 meet; they meet at RRR.

    Thus CCC matches PPP, giving

    ABC↔QRP\begin{aligned} ABC&\leftrightarrow QRP \end{aligned}ABC​↔QRP​

    All three corresponding side pairs match, so SSS applies.

  2. 2

    Triangles ABCABCABC and DEFDEFDEF have corresponding lengths

    AB=x+4DE=2yBC=x+yEF=17.\begin{aligned} AB&=x+4 \\ DE&=2y \\ BC&=x+y \\ EF&=17. \end{aligned}ABDEBCEF​=x+4=2y=x+y=17.​

    The third side pair is already equal. Which values complete SSS?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The required equations are

    x+4=2yx+y=17\begin{aligned} x+4&=2y \\ x+y&=17 \end{aligned}x+4x+y​=2y=17​

    From the second,

    x=17−y.\begin{aligned} x&=17-y. \end{aligned}x​=17−y.​

    Substitute in the first to get

    21−y=2y\begin{aligned} 21-y&=2y \end{aligned}21−y​=2y​

    The resulting values are:

    y=7x=10.\begin{aligned} y&=7 \\ x&=10. \end{aligned}yx​=7=10.​

    Check: the first pair is 14 and 14, and the second is 17 and 17.

  3. 3

    After equal bases are aligned, a third vertex must lie on a radius-12 circle about the first endpoint and a radius-17 circle about the second. Why is the intersection condition useful?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Membership in the first circle gives one length and membership in the second gives the other:

    AC=12BC=17\begin{aligned} AC&=12 \\ BC&=17 \end{aligned}ACBC​=12=17​

    A common point satisfies both conditions needed for the SSS construction.

  4. 4

    Which argument completes the reflected case of SSS without changing the matched base?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The base line fixes both matched endpoints under reflection. The remaining vertex goes to the opposite-height position, described by

    (x,y)↦(x,−y)\begin{aligned} (x,y)&\mapsto(x,-y) \end{aligned}(x,y)​↦(x,−y)​

    Reflect the entire triangle, not just one vertex.

  5. 5

    Triangles KLMKLMKLM and RSTRSTRST are claimed congruent in that order. The given side data are:

    KL=RSKL=16LM=STLM=18KM=20\begin{aligned} KL&=RS \\ KL&=16 \\ LM&=ST \\ LM&=18 \\ KM&=20 \end{aligned}KLKLLMLMKM​=RS=16=ST=18=20​

    Which value of RTRTRT makes SSS apply?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The missing pair is KMKMKM and RTRTRT, so it requires

    RT=KMRT=20\begin{aligned} RT&=KM \\ RT&=20 \end{aligned}RTRT​=KM=20​
  6. 6

    A construction uses fixed base UVUVUV and two circles centered at UUU and VVV with fixed positive radii. The circles meet at WWW above the base line and W′W'W′ below. Which reason shows that these are two placements of one triangle shape up to congruence?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Each base endpoint stays fixed while reflection across line UVUVUV exchanges the two third vertices. The matching distance conditions give three equal side pairs, including

    UW=UW′\begin{aligned} UW&=UW' \end{aligned}UW​=UW′​

    The reflection supplies a whole-figure rigid match.

    The side lengths in centimeters are:

    MN=14NP=19MP=22\begin{aligned} MN&=14 \\ NP&=19 \\ MP&=22 \end{aligned}MNNPMP​=14=19=22​

    What is the perimeter of △YZX\triangle YZX△YZX?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The name YZXYZXYZX still describes the same second triangle.

    Its three side lengths are 14, 19, and 22 cm by congruence, so

    14+19+22=55\begin{aligned} 14+19+22&=55 \end{aligned}14+19+22​=55​
  8. 8

    A triangle and a four-sided frame each have all their side lengths fixed. Which conclusion is justified?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Three side lengths determine a triangle up to rigid motion by SSS. The four-sided equal-strip example can change a corner angle while retaining s,s,s,ss,s,s,ss,s,s,s as its side-length list, so the same conclusion does not follow for the frame.

  9. 9

    Two actual triangles have matching side lengths. Which extra measurement is required before SSS can establish congruence?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The three matching side pairs are exactly the data SSS needs. Its conclusion is a whole-triangle rigid match, so no separate angle measurement is required. The distance between the drawings does not affect that conclusion.

  10. 10

    In the SSS justification, the two equations are

    x2+y2=r2.(x−d)2+y2=s2.\begin{aligned} x^2+y^2&=r^2. \\ (x-d)^2+y^2&=s^2. \end{aligned}x2+y2(x−d)2+y2​=r2.=s2.​

    Subtracting the first from the second gives

    d2−2dx=s2−r2.\begin{aligned} d^2-2dx&=s^2-r^2. \end{aligned}d2−2dx​=s2−r2.​

    Why must the base length ddd be nonzero?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The next step is

    x=d2+r2−s22d\begin{aligned} x&=\frac{d^2+r^2-s^2}{2d} \end{aligned}x​=2dd2+r2−s2​​

    This division needs

    d≠0.\begin{aligned} d&\ne0. \end{aligned}d​=0.​

    Distinct base endpoints give the stronger condition

    d>0.\begin{aligned} d&>0. \end{aligned}d​>0.​
  11. 11

    a=4.\begin{aligned} a&=4. \end{aligned}a​=4.​

    The two matching expressions are

    First expression: 3a+2.Second expression: 5a−6.\begin{aligned} \text{First expression: }&3a+2. \\ \text{Second expression: }&5a-6. \end{aligned}First expression: Second expression: ​3a+2.5a−6.​

    What check confirms this side pair?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Substitute separately:

    3(4)+2=145(4)−6=14\begin{aligned} 3(4)+2&=14 \\ 5(4)-6&=14 \end{aligned}3(4)+25(4)−6​=14=14​

  12. 12

    Which statement accurately limits the conclusion of SSS?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    SSS begins with triangles, not arbitrary proposed lengths or figures. For a triangle the perpendicular height is nonzero:

    y≠0\begin{aligned} y\ne0 \end{aligned}y=0​

    Its side pairs must follow one consistent vertex match; position and orientation need not agree.
