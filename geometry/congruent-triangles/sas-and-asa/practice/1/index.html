  1. 1

    Which angle is included between sides PQPQPQ and PRPRPR?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The sides meet at P.

    The vertex of an angle is its middle letter, so the included angle is

    ∠QPR\angle QPR∠QPR

  2. 2

    Which side is included between the angles at DDD and FFF in triangle DEFDEFDEF?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The included side joins the two named angle vertices.

    Here those endpoints give

    DFDFDF

  3. 3

    Triangles ABCABCABC and DEFDEFDEF have these given matching parts:

    AB=DEAC=DF∠A≅∠D\begin{aligned} AB&=DE \\ AC&=DF \\ \angle A&\cong\angle D \end{aligned}ABAC∠A​=DE=DF≅∠D​

    Which criterion uses exactly these givens?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The two given sides meet at A and D, which are the given angle vertices. These are two side pairs and their included angle pair.

    SAS gives:

    △ABC≅△DEF.\triangle ABC\cong\triangle DEF.△ABC≅△DEF.
  4. 4

    Triangles LMNLMNLMN and XYZXYZXYZ have these given matching parts:

    ∠L≅∠XLN=XZ∠N≅∠Z\begin{aligned} \angle L&\cong\angle X \\ LN&=XZ \\ \angle N&\cong\angle Z \end{aligned}∠LLN∠N​≅∠X=XZ≅∠Z​

    Which criterion uses exactly these givens?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    LN joins L and N, and XZ joins X and Z. The given sides lie between the two known angle vertices.

    ASA gives:

    △LMN≅△XYZ.\triangle LMN\cong\triangle XYZ.△LMN≅△XYZ.

    Solution

    Angles at A,CA,CA,C match angles at D,FD,FD,F, respectively. Which equality supplies the included-side pair for ASA?

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A proposed SAS construction uses two positive side lengths but an included angle of 0∘0^\circ0∘. What happens?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    At a zero-degree included angle, the two rays coincide.

    All three vertices lie on one line.

    A triangle needs an included angle satisfying

    0∘<θθ<180∘\begin{aligned} 0^\circ&<\theta \\ \theta&<180^\circ \end{aligned}0∘θ​<θ<180∘​

    Solution

    Which information is sufficient for SAS?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    SAS fixes two distances from a shared vertex and the angle between their rays. The required matching parts are:

    AB=DEAC=DF∠A≅∠D\begin{aligned} AB&=DE \\ AC&=DF \\ \angle A&\cong\angle D \end{aligned}ABAC∠A​=DE=DF≅∠D​

    A congruence match sends AAA to TTT, BBB to RRR, and CCC to SSS. Complete the ordered statement:

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Keep the images in the order of A, B, C.

  11. 11

    In an SAS construction, the base is drawn and the included-angle ray starts at its left endpoint. What fixes the third vertex on that ray?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The second side length measures from the included-angle vertex.

    If that vertex is A and the prescribed length is 6 cm, place C so that

    AC=6 cm.AC=6\text{ cm}.AC=6 cm.

    Exactly one point on the ray has that distance.

  12. 12

    When constructing a triangle from a side and its two endpoint angles, how should the two new rays open?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The rays must meet on the chosen side of the base to enclose the intended triangle.

    Each endpoint angle must be positive, and the sum of the two endpoint angles must be less than 180∘180^\circ180∘.
