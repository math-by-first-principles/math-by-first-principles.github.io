    ∠B=72∘AB=13 cm\begin{aligned} \angle B&=72^\circ \\ AB&=13\text{ cm} \end{aligned}∠BAB​=72∘=13 cm​

    Triangle DEFDEFDEF has these givens:

    ∠D=50∘∠E=72∘DE=13 cm\begin{aligned} \angle D&=50^\circ \\ \angle E&=72^\circ \\ DE&=13\text{ cm} \end{aligned}∠D∠EDE​=50∘=72∘=13 cm​

    Which criterion establishes congruence in the listed order?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The interior angle at A and its exterior angle form a straight angle:

    180∘−130∘=50∘.180^\circ-130^\circ=50^\circ.180∘−130∘=50∘.

    AB and DE join the matching 50-degree and 72-degree vertices. ASA establishes:

    △ABC≅△DEF.\triangle ABC\cong\triangle DEF.△ABC≅△DEF.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    4x−9=2x+17.4x-9=2x+17.4x−9=2x+17.

    What value of xxx makes the ASA data match in the listed order?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles.

    Thus

    3x+24=132.3x+24=132.3x+24=132.

    Subtract 24 and divide by 3:

    x=36.x=36.x=36.

    This gives angles A and B of 50 and 82 degrees, matching D and E.

    Which assessment of a direct SAS argument is correct?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Solution

    Triangle ABCABCABC has vertices A(0,0)A(0,0)A(0,0), B(4,0)B(4,0)B(4,0), and C(0,7)C(0,7)C(0,7). Triangle DEFDEFDEF has vertices D(2,1)D(2,1)D(2,1), E(2,5)E(2,5)E(2,5), and F(−5,1)F(-5,1)F(−5,1). Which argument establishes congruence in that order without finding the slanted side lengths?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Read horizontal and vertical segment lengths from the coordinate differences.

    AB and DE each have length 4.

    AC and DF each have length 7.

    At A and D, a horizontal side meets a vertical side, so both included angles are right.

    A pair of right angles alone would not fix the side lengths or establish congruence.
