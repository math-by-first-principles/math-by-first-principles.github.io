    Find xxx and explain which correspondence you used.

    1. Hint 1

    2. Hint 2

    Full solution

    The ordered match sends KKK to PPP and MMM to QQQ.

    Set the lengths equal:

    2x+9=41.\begin{aligned}2x+9&=41\end{aligned}.2x+9​=41​.

    Subtract 9 and divide by 2 to obtain:

    x=16.\begin{aligned}x&=16\end{aligned}.x​=16​.

    The corresponding lengths both equal 414141 cm.

    Answer

    x=16x=16x=16; KM corresponds to PQ.

    Use the established vertex order before translating a target into algebra.

  5. Problem 5 Transfer a distance across a pond

  7. Problem 7 Choose a useful added segment

    A four-sided figure HJKLHJKLHJKL has every corner pointing outward. The given side pairs are:

    HJ=HLKJ=KL.\begin{aligned}HJ&=HL \\ KJ&=KL\end{aligned}.HJKJ​=HL=KL​.

    Text description of this figure

    1. Hint 1

      Look for two triangles whose sides can use both given equality pairs.

    2. Hint 2

      Which segment between opposite corners would be shared by those two triangles?

    Answer

    Full solution

    HJ=HLJK=LK.\begin{aligned}HJ&=HL \\ JK&=LK\end{aligned}.HJJK​=HL=LK​.

  10. Problem 10 Pass a result into a second congruence

    Triangles RABRABRAB and SCDSCDSCD each have three noncollinear vertices. Their positive side lengths satisfy:

    RA=SCRB=SDAB=CD.\begin{aligned}RA&=SC \\ RB&=SD \\ AB&=CD\end{aligned}.RARBAB​=SC=SD=CD​.

    AE=CF.\begin{aligned}AE&=CF\end{aligned}.AE​=CF​.

    EB=FD.\begin{aligned}EB&=FD\end{aligned}.EB​=FD​.

      Compare rays AR and AE, and compare rays CS and CF. What does each point order tell you?

    3. Hint 3

      For the second comparison, combine the proved included-angle equality with the given side pairs.

    ∠RAB≅∠SCD.\angle RAB\cong\angle SCD.∠RAB≅∠SCD.

    R↔SA↔CB↔D.\begin{aligned}R&\leftrightarrow S \\ A&\leftrightarrow C \\ B&\leftrightarrow D\end{aligned}.RAB​↔S↔C↔D​.
