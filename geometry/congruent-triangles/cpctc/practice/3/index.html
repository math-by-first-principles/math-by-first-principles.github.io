  1. 1

    Lines ACACAC and BDBDBD cross at OOO. Given

    OA=OCOC=14OB=ODOD=19\begin{aligned}OA&=OC \\ OC&=14 \\ OB&=OD \\ OD&=19\end{aligned}OAOCOBOD​=OC=14=OD=19​

    which conclusion follows by SAS then CPCTC?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The included vertical angles at OOO are congruent.

    Together with the two given side pairs, they give

    △AOB≅△COD\begin{aligned}\triangle AOB&\cong\triangle COD\end{aligned}△AOB​≅△COD​

    by SAS.

    CPCTC gives the remaining side equality:

    AB=CD.\begin{aligned}AB&=CD.\end{aligned}AB​=CD.​
  2. 2

    A four-sided figure PQRSPQRSPQRS, with every corner pointing outward, has

    PQ=PSRQ=RS.\begin{aligned}PQ&=PS \\ RQ&=RS\end{aligned}.PQRQ​=PS=RS​.

    After drawing PRPRPR, what proves that it bisects the interior angle at PPP?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The two given side pairs and

    PR=PR\begin{aligned}PR&=PR\end{aligned}PR​=PR​

    establish

    △PQR≅△PSR.\begin{aligned}\triangle PQR&\cong\triangle PSR\end{aligned}.△PQR​≅△PSR​.

    CPCTC gives

    ∠QPR≅∠SPR.\begin{aligned}\angle QPR&\cong\angle SPR.\end{aligned}∠QPR​≅∠SPR.​

    The diagonal lies inside the angle because every corner points outward.

    The proved equality of the two parts establishes angle bisection.

  3. 3

    In △ABC\triangle ABC△ABC and △DEF\triangle DEF△DEF,

    AB=DE∠A≅∠D∠C≅∠F.\begin{aligned}AB&=DE \\ \angle A&\cong\angle D \\ \angle C&\cong\angle F\end{aligned}.AB∠A∠C​=DE≅∠D≅∠F​.

    Which criterion establishes congruence before the following conclusion?

    BC=EF.\begin{aligned}BC&=EF\end{aligned}.BC​=EF​.
    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The known angles are at the first and third vertices, while ABABAB and DEDEDE are not their included sides.

    AAS gives

    △ABC≅△DEF.\begin{aligned}\triangle ABC&\cong\triangle DEF.\end{aligned}△ABC​≅△DEF.​

    CPCTC then gives the target side equality.

  4. 4

    A proof establishes

    △LMN≅△UVW.\begin{aligned}\triangle LMN&\cong\triangle UVW\end{aligned}.△LMN​≅△UVW​.

    If

    m∠N=4x+3 degreesm∠W=7x−18 degrees\begin{aligned}m\angle N&=4x+3\text{ degrees} \\ m\angle W&=7x-18\text{ degrees}\end{aligned}m∠Nm∠W​=4x+3 degrees=7x−18 degrees​

    find the common angle measure.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    CPCTC gives

    4x+3=7x−18.\begin{aligned}4x+3&=7x-18.\end{aligned}4x+3​=7x−18.​

    Thus

    x=7.\begin{aligned}x&=7\end{aligned}.x​=7​.

    Substitution gives

    4(7)+3=31,\begin{aligned}4(7)+3&=31,\end{aligned}4(7)+3​=31,​

    so each angle measures 31∘31^\circ31∘.

  5. 5

    In an indirect-distance model, the accessible triangle reproduces only one sighting angle and the shared baseline. Why can the target length not yet be transferred by ASA?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The shared baseline supplies one side pair,

    BC=BC.\begin{aligned}BC&=BC.\end{aligned}BC​=BC.​

    With only one angle pair, the ASA data are incomplete.

    CPCTC cannot manufacture the missing evidence before congruence is established.

  6. 6

    Two right triangles have right angles at BBB and EEE, with

    AC=DFAB=DE.\begin{aligned}AC&=DF \\ AB&=DE\end{aligned}.ACAB​=DF=DE​.

    After matching them by HL, which side pair may be concluded by CPCTC?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The hypotenuses pair as

    AC↔DF\begin{aligned}AC&\leftrightarrow DF\end{aligned}AC​↔DF​

    and the named legs pair as

    AB↔DE.\begin{aligned}AB&\leftrightarrow DE\end{aligned}.AB​↔DE​.

    Hence the ordered match is

    ABC↔DEF\begin{aligned}ABC&\leftrightarrow DEF\end{aligned}ABC​↔DEF​

    and the remaining pair is

    BC=EF.\begin{aligned}BC&=EF.\end{aligned}BC​=EF.​
  7. 7

    A proof has established

    △ABC≅△DEF.\begin{aligned}\triangle ABC&\cong\triangle DEF\end{aligned}.△ABC​≅△DEF​.

    Which equality compares the angles opposite corresponding sides ABABAB and DEDEDE?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The angle opposite ABABAB is at CCC; the angle opposite DEDEDE is at FFF.

    Mapping all three letters gives

    ACB↔DFE.\begin{aligned}ACB&\leftrightarrow DFE.\end{aligned}ACB​↔DFE.​

    CPCTC justifies their congruence.

  8. 8

    A compass procedure preserves the two radii of a source triangle but changes the transferred chord length. Which conclusion is justified?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The proof needs three side pairs, including

    AB=DE.\begin{aligned}AB&=DE.\end{aligned}AB​=DE.​

    Changing the transferred chord removes that pair.

    The two matching radii alone do not establish the required triangle congruence.

  9. 9

    In a figure,

    △ABE≅△ACD\begin{aligned}\triangle ABE&\cong\triangle ACD\end{aligned}△ABE​≅△ACD​

    has been proved. If

    BE=3z+8CD=5z−10\begin{aligned}BE&=3z+8 \\ CD&=5z-10\end{aligned}BECD​=3z+8=5z−10​

    find the common side length.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The correspondence gives

    BE=CD.\begin{aligned}BE&=CD\end{aligned}.BE​=CD​.

    Therefore

    3z+8=5z−10,\begin{aligned}3z+8&=5z-10,\end{aligned}3z+8​=5z−10,​

    so

    z=9.\begin{aligned}z&=9\end{aligned}.z​=9​.

    Substitute to obtain

    3(9)+8=35.\begin{aligned}3(9)+8&=35.\end{aligned}3(9)+8​=35.​

    The other expression also equals 35.

  10. 10

    A diagonal, a segment joining opposite corners, is drawn in a four-sided figure with every corner pointing outward. Which claim is available solely because the diagonal was drawn?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The added diagonal is a side of each resulting triangle.

    Its length equals itself in the two triangles. Bisection and perpendicularity require additional givens or proofs.

  11. 11

    Suppose a first triangle congruence proves

    TU=VW.\begin{aligned}TU&=VW\end{aligned}.TU​=VW​.

    A second pair has

    RT=SVRU=SW.\begin{aligned}RT&=SV \\ RU&=SW\end{aligned}.RTRU​=SV=SW​.

    What criterion now establishes the following congruence?

    △RTU≅△SVW.\begin{aligned}\triangle RTU&\cong\triangle SVW\end{aligned}.△RTU​≅△SVW​.
    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The first-stage result supplies the third side pair.

    Together the data are

    RT=SV,RU=SW,TU=VW.\begin{aligned}RT&=SV, \\ RU&=SW, \\ TU&=VW.\end{aligned}RTRUTU​=SV,=SW,=VW.​

    These match

    R↔ST↔VU↔W\begin{aligned}R&\leftrightarrow S \\ T&\leftrightarrow V \\ U&\leftrightarrow W\end{aligned}RTU​↔S↔V↔W​

    so SSS applies.

  12. 12

    Which statement best explains why a copied angle can be justified without a protractor measurement?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The compass settings give three side equalities.

    SSS then establishes

    △OAB≅△PDE,\begin{aligned}\triangle OAB&\cong\triangle PDE,\end{aligned}△OAB​≅△PDE,​

    and CPCTC gives the corresponding angle equality.

    A measurement can check the drawing, but it is not the exact proof.
