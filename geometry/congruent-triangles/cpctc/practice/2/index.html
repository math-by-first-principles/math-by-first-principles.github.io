  1. 1

    A proof gives

    △KLM≅△RST.\triangle KLM\cong\triangle RST.△KLM≅△RST.

    If

    KL=2x+7RS=25\begin{aligned}KL&=2x+7 \\ RS&=25\end{aligned}KLRS​=2x+7=25​

    find xxx.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    CPCTC gives equality of KLKLKL and RSRSRS:

    2x+7=25.\begin{aligned}2x+7&=25.\end{aligned}2x+7​=25.​

    Subtract 7 and divide by 2, obtaining

    x=9.\begin{aligned}x&=9.\end{aligned}x​=9.​
  2. 2

    The ordered congruence is:

    △ABC≅△EFD\triangle ABC\cong\triangle EFD△ABC≅△EFD

    The given angle measure is:

    m∠F=68∘m\angle F=68^\circm∠F=68∘

    Find m∠Bm\angle Bm∠B.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The second vertices correspond, so BBB matches FFF.

    By CPCTC, their angle measures are equal:

    m∠B=m∠Fm∠F=68∘.\begin{aligned}m\angle B&=m\angle F \\ m\angle F&=68^\circ.\end{aligned}m∠Bm∠F​=m∠F=68∘.​
  3. 3

    A four-sided figure JKLMJKLMJKLM, with every corner pointing outward, has

    JK=JMKL=ML.\begin{aligned}JK&=JM \\ KL&=ML\end{aligned}.JKKL​=JM=ML​.

    Which auxiliary segment creates a pair of triangles that can be matched immediately by SSS?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Drawing JLJLJL creates △JKL\triangle JKL△JKL and △JML\triangle JML△JML.

    The givens supply two side pairs; the third is

    JL=JL.\begin{aligned}JL&=JL.\end{aligned}JL​=JL.​

    SSS establishes their congruence.

  4. 4

    After proving

    △JKL≅△JML\begin{aligned}\triangle JKL&\cong\triangle JML\end{aligned}△JKL​≅△JML​

    which equality is a valid CPCTC conclusion?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The correspondence fixes JJJ and LLL and sends KKK to MMM.

    Thus the angle mapping is

    KJL↔MJL.\begin{aligned}KJL&\leftrightarrow MJL.\end{aligned}KJL​↔MJL.​

    The other choices do not pair corresponding parts.

  5. 5

    Points A,B,DA,B,DA,B,D lie on one ray in that order, and A,C,EA,C,EA,C,E lie on a second ray in that order. The two rays form an opening strictly between 0∘0^\circ0∘ and 180∘180^\circ180∘. Given

    AB=ACAD=AE\begin{aligned}AB&=AC \\ AD&=AE\end{aligned}ABAD​=AC=AE​

    which ordered triangle pair is matched by SAS using the shared opening at AAA?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Use the short pair

    AB=AC\begin{aligned}AB&=AC\end{aligned}AB​=AC​

    and long pair

    AE=AD.\begin{aligned}AE&=AD\end{aligned}.AE​=AD​.

    Their included opening is the same at AAA.

    The correct order is

    ABE↔ACD.\begin{aligned}ABE&\leftrightarrow ACD.\end{aligned}ABE​↔ACD.​

    The collinear triples ABDABDABD and ACEACEACE are not triangles.

  6. 6

    In the angle-copy construction,

    OA=OBOB=PDPD=PEPE=11 cmAB=DEDE=13 cm.\begin{aligned}OA&=OB \\ OB&=PD \\ PD&=PE \\ PE&=11\text{ cm} \\ AB&=DE \\ DE&=13\text{ cm}\end{aligned}.OAOBPDPEABDE​=OB=PD=PE=11 cm=DE=13 cm​.

    Which pair of triangles is matched in the order needed to compare the angles at OOO and PPP?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The two equal radii meet at OOO in the source and PPP in the target.

    The chord ABABAB corresponds to DEDEDE, giving

    OAB↔PDE.\begin{aligned}OAB&\leftrightarrow PDE.\end{aligned}OAB​↔PDE.​

    This matches all three side pairs.

  7. 7

    Triangles ABCABCABC and DBCDBCDBC share side BCBCBC and have matching angles at BBB and CCC. If

    BD=37 m\begin{aligned}BD&=37\text{ m}\end{aligned}BD​=37 m​

    what is ABABAB in the exact model?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    ASA gives

    △ABC≅△DBC.\begin{aligned}\triangle ABC&\cong\triangle DBC\end{aligned}.△ABC​≅△DBC​.

    By CPCTC the matching side lengths satisfy

    AB=DBDB=37.\begin{aligned}AB&=DB \\ DB&=37.\end{aligned}ABDB​=DB=37.​
  8. 8

    A proof establishes

    △PQR≅△XYZ.\begin{aligned}\triangle PQR&\cong\triangle XYZ\end{aligned}.△PQR​≅△XYZ​.

    The corresponding lengths are

    PR=2t+1XZ=5t−20.\begin{aligned}PR&=2t+1 \\ XZ&=5t-20\end{aligned}.PRXZ​=2t+1=5t−20​.

    Find ttt.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The corresponding side equality gives

    2t+1=5t−20.\begin{aligned}2t+1&=5t-20.\end{aligned}2t+1​=5t−20.​

    Rearranging gives

    3t=21\begin{aligned}3t&=21\end{aligned}3t​=21​

    hence

    t=7.\begin{aligned}t&=7.\end{aligned}t​=7.​

    Both lengths are then 15.

  9. 9

    Two triangle drawings overlap. What must a separated redraw preserve for use in the proof?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A redraw may move copies for clarity, but it must retain the same mathematical data.

    A known side pair such as

    AB=DE\begin{aligned}AB&=DE\end{aligned}AB​=DE​

    remains the same pair; redrawing does not create new givens.

  10. 10

    Lines ACACAC and BDBDBD cross at OOO, with

    OA=OCOB=OD.\begin{aligned}OA&=OC \\ OB&=OD\end{aligned}.OAOB​=OC=OD​.

    Which fact supplies the included angle pair for SAS in △AOB\triangle AOB△AOB and △COD\triangle COD△COD?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The rays OAOAOA and OCOCOC are opposite, as are OBOBOB and ODODOD.

    Thus

    ∠AOB≅∠COD\begin{aligned}\angle AOB&\cong\angle COD\end{aligned}∠AOB​≅∠COD​

    by the vertical-angle theorem.

    These are the included angles between the given side pairs.

  11. 11

    A two-stage congruence argument has established one pair of triangles and then, by CPCTC, a side pair needed for a second comparison. What must happen before CPCTC can be used on the second pair?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The first match supplies only its own corresponding parts.

    The second pair needs a criterion establishing its own statement, such as

    △LMN≅△RST.\begin{aligned}\triangle LMN&\cong\triangle RST.\end{aligned}△LMN​≅△RST.​

    Only then can CPCTC compare that pair’s remaining parts.

  12. 12

    In an angle copy, which equality would be an invalid reason for SAS if it is the very result being proved?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The desired angle equality is the conclusion, not an initial datum.

    Use the three transferred side pairs for SSS first. CPCTC then gives the corresponding angle equality:

    ∠AOB≅∠DPE.\begin{aligned}\angle AOB&\cong\angle DPE.\end{aligned}∠AOB​≅∠DPE.​
