  1. 1

    Triangles ABC and DEF have the following data:

    m∠A=49∘m∠D=49∘m∠B=58∘m∠E=58∘BC=EF\begin{aligned} m\angle A&=49^\circ \\ m\angle D&=49^\circ \\ m\angle B&=58^\circ \\ m\angle E&=58^\circ \\ BC&=EF \end{aligned}m∠Am∠Dm∠Bm∠EBC​=49∘=49∘=58∘=58∘=EF​

    Which criterion directly describes the given data?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    BC is not between the given angles at A and B, and EF occupies the corresponding position. Two angles and a nonincluded side give AAS.

  2. 2

    In triangles KLM and RST, angles K and R are congruent and angles L and S are congruent. Which equality would allow AAS?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The angle matching fixes K to R, L to S, and M to T. Thus KM corresponds to RT, and this side is nonincluded between the given angle vertices.

  3. 3

    Two AAS-congruent triangles have corresponding sides of lengths 4x+34x+34x+3 and 7x−187x-187x−18. Find x.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Once congruence is established, corresponding lengths match.

    4x+3=7x−18.4x+3=7x-18.4x+3=7x−18.

    Thus

    21=3xx=7\begin{aligned} 21&=3x \\ x&=7 \end{aligned}21x​=3x=7​

    Both side lengths are 31.

  4. 4

    Triangles ABC and DEF are right at B and E. Which side equality must be the hypotenuse equality for HL?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The hypotenuses are opposite the right-angle vertices B and E, so they are AC and DF.

    AB=DEBC=EF\begin{aligned} AB&=DE \\ BC&=EF \end{aligned}ABBC​=DE=EF​

    If

    AC=5t−6DF=2t+15\begin{aligned} AC&=5t-6 \\ DF&=2t+15 \end{aligned}ACDF​=5t−6=2t+15​

    what is t?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The given side pairs are one leg and the hypotenuse, so HL applies. It gives

    AC=DF5t−6=2t+15.\begin{aligned} AC&=DF \\ 5t-6&=2t+15. \end{aligned}AC5t−6​=DF=2t+15.​

    3t=21t=7\begin{aligned} 3t&=21 \\ t&=7 \end{aligned}3tt​=21=7​
  6. 6

    Two right triangles each have one leg of length 11 and their other legs of length 20. Which criterion can establish their congruence without first finding the hypotenuse?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The known legs meet at the right angle. Two equal side pairs and the included equal right angles give SAS directly.

  7. 7

    Triangles PQR and XYZ have angles

    m∠P=32∘m∠X=32∘m∠Q=97∘m∠Y=97∘\begin{aligned} m\angle P&=32^\circ \\ m\angle X&=32^\circ \\ m\angle Q&=97^\circ \\ m\angle Y&=97^\circ \end{aligned}m∠Pm∠Xm∠Qm∠Y​=32∘=32∘=97∘=97∘​

    If

    PQ=XYPQ=XYPQ=XY

    which criterion fits the given side?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    PQ joins the two given angle vertices, and XY joins their corresponding vertices. It is the included side, so this is ASA.

  8. 8

    Two equiangular triangles have perimeters 36 and 69 centimeters. Which statement is correct?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Equiangular triangles are equilateral. Divide each perimeter by three.

    36/3=12.69/3=23.\begin{aligned} 36/3&=12. \\ 69/3&=23. \end{aligned}36/369/3​=12.=23.​

    Different corresponding lengths prevent congruence.

  9. 9

    A compass construction fixes AC, a ray from A, and a circle centered at C. The circle meets the ray at distinct B1 and B2, both beyond A. Why can AB1 and AB2 not be equal?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Along one ray, a distance from its endpoint identifies only one point. Since B1 and B2 are distinct, their distances from A differ.

  10. 10

    For two triangles, the following are the only givens:

    AB=DE.AC=DF.∠B≅∠E.\begin{aligned} AB&=DE. \\ AC&=DF. \\ \angle B&\cong\angle E. \end{aligned}ABAC∠B​=DE.=DF.≅∠E.​

    What conclusion is justified?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The known angle is not between the two named sides. No right-angle condition is supplied. These are unrestricted SSA data, so no general congruence conclusion follows.

  11. 11

    In the HL overlay, angles BCF and CFB are congruent because

    BC=BFBC=BFBC=BF

    What fact then makes the two small angles at B congruent?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The small triangles already share equal angle measures at C and F, and both have 90 degrees at A. Their remaining angle measures must therefore match.

  12. 12

    Two triangles are proposed with a matching corresponding side pair and angles of 118 and 62 degrees in each triangle. Can these measurements describe nondegenerate triangles for an AAS comparison?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A nondegenerate triangle needs a positive third interior angle.

    180∘−118∘−62∘=0∘.180^\circ-118^\circ-62^\circ=0^\circ.180∘−118∘−62∘=0∘.

    These measurements cannot describe such a triangle.
