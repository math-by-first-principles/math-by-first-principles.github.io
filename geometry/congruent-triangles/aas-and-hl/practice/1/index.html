  2. 2

    AAS uses two corresponding angles and which side information?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    AAS is angle-angle-side. The known side is nonincluded, and it must respect the same correspondence as the known angles.

  3. 3

    In triangle ABC, which two sides are not included between angles A and C?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The side joining the two given angle vertices A and C is AC. It is the included side. The other two sides, AB and BC, are both nonincluded for this angle pair.

  4. 4

    Which side of a right triangle is its hypotenuse?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The right-angle location determines the hypotenuse. It is the side across from that angle, regardless of orientation.

  5. 5

    Which given data are enough for the HL criterion?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    HL requires a right triangle on both sides of the comparison, the hypotenuse pair, and one corresponding leg pair.

  6. 6

    Which list is not a triangle congruence criterion in general?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Equilateral triangles with different side lengths all have 60-degree angles. Their equal angles do not make their side lengths match.

  7. 7

    What does the failure of SSA as a general congruence criterion mean?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A single fixed SSA construction with two noncongruent solutions disproves the guarantee. Some other SSA data may fit one or no triangle.
