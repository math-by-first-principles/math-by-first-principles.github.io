    The given circle has radius 9.59.59.5 cm. A compass arc centered at A with opening OA selects B on the circle. What is the perimeter of triangle OAB?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Both OA and OB are radii of the given circle. AB is a radius of the compass circle with the same opening, so all three lengths are 9.5 cm.

    P=3(9.5).P=3(9.5).P=3(9.5). P=28.5 cm.P=28.5\text{ cm}.P=28.5 cm.
  2. 2

    Two consecutive radius-length compass steps locate B and then C after A. What is the smaller central angle AOC?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Each step gives an equilateral center triangle and a 60-degree turn. The first two turns total less than a straight angle, so they form the smaller angle.

    ∠AOC=2(60∘).\angle AOC=2(60^\circ).∠AOC=2(60∘). ∠AOC=120∘.\angle AOC=120^\circ.∠AOC=120∘.
  3. 3

    After five consecutive radius-length steps from A around a circle, what additional forward turn returns the radius to its initial direction?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Five steps have used five equilateral center angles. The remaining part of a full turn is

    360∘−5(60∘)=60∘.360^\circ-5(60^\circ)=60^\circ.360∘−5(60∘)=60∘.

    This sixth step returns to the original ray and hence to its unique circle point A.

  4. 4

    A given circle has diameter 20 cm. A compass step places a chord of length 10 cm on it. Which reason establishes a 60-degree smaller central angle?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The circle radius is half its diameter. The two radii and the constructed chord are all 10 cm, making an equilateral center triangle.

    r=20÷2.r=20\div2.r=20÷2. r=10 cm.r=10\text{ cm}.r=10 cm.

    Its three equal angles share 180 degrees, giving a 60-degree center angle.

  5. 5

    Consecutive hexagon vertices A and B have been marked with a fixed opening equal to the circle radius. An arc centered at B with that same opening meets the given circle at A and at a different point X. Which action continues the construction in the same direction?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A is the preceding vertex, so returning to it would undo the first step. The other crossing X belongs to the given circle and is one compass opening from B. Name it C and retain the opening for the next step.

    BC=r.BC=r.BC=r.
  6. 6

    An equilateral triangle is made by joining alternate vertices of a constructed regular hexagon in a circle of radius 3 cm. What is one triangle side?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A triangle side subtends 120 degrees. Halving its center triangle gives a 30-60-90 right triangle with hypotenuse 3 cm. Double the longer leg to recover the chord.

    s=2(332).s=2\left(\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}\right).s=2(233​​). s=33 cm.s=3\sqrt3\text{ cm}.s=33​ cm.
  7. 7

    A constructed hexagon has vertices A, B, C, D, E, F in order. Which two vertices complete an equilateral triangle starting at B?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Starting at B, skip C to reach D, then skip E to reach F. Each pair of successive chosen vertices is two hexagon steps apart, including F back to B.

    2(60∘)=120∘.2(60^\circ)=120^\circ.2(60∘)=120∘.

    The three equal central angles give three equal chords.

    For a square construction, diameter AC is 13 cm. Helper circles centered at A and C each use opening AC, meeting at U and V. What is AU + CU?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    U lies on both helper circles. Each distance to a helper center equals the unchanged opening, 13 cm.

    AU+CU=13+13.AU+CU=13+13.AU+CU=13+13. AU+CU=26 cm.AU+CU=26\text{ cm}.AU+CU=26 cm.

    ABCD is the square obtained from two perpendicular diameters of circle O. What is angle ABO?

    A square is constructed inside a given circle of radius 9 cm. What is the length of one square diagonal?

    Solution

    d=2(9).d=2(9).d=2(9). d=18 cm.d=18\text{ cm}.d=18 cm.
