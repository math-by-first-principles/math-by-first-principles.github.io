  9. 9

    How many different equilateral triangles have all three vertices among the vertices of a given regular hexagon? Count each set of three vertices once.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Equal triangle sides on the same circle subtend equal minor central angles. The three equal gaps must each span two hexagon steps, because the six steps must be shared equally.

    6÷3=2 steps per gap.6\div3=2\text{ steps per gap}.6÷3=2 steps per gap.

    The choices are ACE and BDF. Starting at a different vertex of either triangle does not produce a new set.
