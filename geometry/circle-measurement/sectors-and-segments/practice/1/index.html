    An annulus has outer radius 6 cm and inner radius 2 cm. What is its exact area?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Subtract the inner disk from the outer disk.

    A=π(62−22),A=32π cm2.\begin{aligned} A&=\pi(6^2-2^2),\\ A&=32\pi\text{ cm}^2. \end{aligned}AA​=π(62−22),=32π cm2.​

  10. 10

    Which pieces make up the complete boundary of a proper sector?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Following the sector from one endpoint goes along a radius to the center, along the other radius to the second endpoint, and back along the selected arc. A chord belongs to a circular segment instead.
