    A circle's radius increases by 15%15\%15%. Which pair gives the percentage increases in circumference and area?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The length factor is 1.15. Circumference uses that factor; area uses its square.

    1.152=1.32251.15^2=1.32251.152=1.3225

    The new area is 132.25 percent of the old area, an increase of 32.25 percent.
