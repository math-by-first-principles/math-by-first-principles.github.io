    For one circle, the circumference is 2π(x−2)2\pi(x-2)2π(x−2) cm and the area is π(7x−20)\pi(7x-20)π(7x−20) square cm. Which real x-values can describe this circle?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The circumference gives radius x-2, which must be positive. Equate its square with the area divided by pi.

    (x−2)2=7x−20(x-2)^2=7x-20(x−2)2=7x−20 (x−3)(x−8)=0(x-3)(x-8)=0(x−3)(x−8)=0

    Both x=3 and x=8 give positive radii and the stated positive areas.

    Let AAA be a disk area and let T=Cr/2T=Cr/2T=Cr/2. Which information certifies that the absolute difference between A and T is less than 0.01 square unit?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Two numbers inside the same interval differ by less than its width.

    ∣A−T∣<U−I|A-T|<U-I∣A−T∣<U−I

    When that width is less than 0.01, the desired error follows. A side count, a one-sided inequality or coarse rounding alone does not control the error.
