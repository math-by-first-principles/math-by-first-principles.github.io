    A circle has positive radius (x+8)(x+8)(x+8) cm. A selected arc, traced once, has positive central turn (x−3)(x-3)(x−3) radians and length 606060 cm. Find xxx.

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The radius-times-turn product equals the stated arc length.

    (x+8)(x−3)=60,x2+5x−84=0,(x+12)(x−7)=0.\begin{aligned} (x+8)(x-3)&=60,\\ x^2+5x-84&=0,\\ (x+12)(x-7)&=0. \end{aligned}(x+8)(x−3)x2+5x−84(x+12)(x−7)​=60,=0,=0.​

    The candidates are 7 and -12. The negative candidate gives a negative radius and turn. For x equal to 7, the radius is 15 cm and the turn is 4 radians, below one full turn; their product is 60 cm.
