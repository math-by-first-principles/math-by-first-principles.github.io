    A triangle has sides 262626 and 333333 meeting at 72∘72^\circ72∘. Find the third side to the nearest tenth.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The third side ddd is across from the given angle.

    d2=262+332−2(26)(33)cos⁡72∘,d≈35.1.\begin{aligned}d^2&=26^2+33^2\\&\quad-2(26)(33)\cos72^\circ,\\d&\approx35.1.\end{aligned}d2d​=262+332−2(26)(33)cos72∘,≈35.1.​

    The sides of a triangle measure 131313, 202020, and 242424. Is the angle opposite the longest side acute, right, or obtuse?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The two shorter lengths sum to 333333, so the lengths form a triangle. For the angle TTT opposite 242424, the cosine has a negative numerator.

    132+202−242=−7.13^2+20^2-24^2=-7.132+202−242=−7.

    The denominator is positive, so the angle is obtuse.

    Solution
