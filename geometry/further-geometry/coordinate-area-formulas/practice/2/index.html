    A pentagon has boundary vertices A(−2,1)A(-2,1)A(−2,1), B(2,−1)B(2,-1)B(2,−1), C(6,2)C(6,2)C(6,2), D(4,5)D(4,5)D(4,5), and E(0,4)E(0,4)E(0,4). What is its ordinary area?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The differences in boundary order are 000, 101010, 222222, 161616, and 888. The last difference comes from E→AE\to AE→A.

    2S=0+10+22+16+8S=28.\begin{aligned}2S&=0+10+22+16+8\\S&=28.\end{aligned}2SS​=0+10+22+16+8=28.​

    The ordinary area is 28 square units.

  4. 4

    An edge moves 5 units left. Its two endpoints are above both y=−1y=-1y=−1 and y=−4y=-4y=−4. By how much does its signed trapezoid contribution increase when the reference line changes from y=−1y=-1y=−1 to y=−4y=-4y=−4?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Each endpoint height increases by 3, so the average height increases by 3. The signed width is positive 5.

    ΔE=5⋅3ΔE=15.\begin{aligned}\Delta E&=5\cdot3\\\Delta E&=15.\end{aligned}ΔEΔE​=5⋅3=15.​

    Rectangle corners A(−3,−1)A(-3,-1)A(−3,−1), B(5,−1)B(5,-1)B(5,−1), C(5,3)C(5,3)C(5,3), and D(−3,3)D(-3,3)D(−3,3) are joined in the order B,D,A,CB,D,A,CB,D,A,C and closed at BBB. Which pair crosses?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The listed order uses both rectangle diagonals, BDBDBD and ACACAC. Their midpoints agree.

    MBD=(1,1)MAC=(1,1).\begin{aligned}M_{BD}&=(1,1)\\M_{AC}&=(1,1).\end{aligned}MBD​MAC​​=(1,1)=(1,1).​

    The diagonals meet at that point. The other two segments are opposite sides of the rectangle.

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The two new edges go from PPP to QQQ and from QQQ to RRR. Compute both differences.

    dPQ=1⋅0−5(−2)dPQ=10dQR=5⋅2−9⋅0dQR=10.\begin{aligned}d_{PQ}&=1\cdot0-5(-2)\\d_{PQ}&=10\\d_{QR}&=5\cdot2-9\cdot0\\d_{QR}&=10.\end{aligned}dPQ​dPQ​dQR​dQR​​=1⋅0−5(−2)=10=5⋅2−9⋅0=10.​

  10. 10

    A strip of width 2 contains two separate pieces of a polygon. Each piece has a straight upper edge and a straight lower edge across the entire strip. For the lower piece, the vertical gaps at the two ends are 3 and 3. For the upper piece, the gaps are 2 and 5. What area lies in the strip?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    With straight edges the gap changes linearly across the strip, so its average is the average of the two end gaps. Multiply each average by the strip width and add the results.

    K=2⋅3+32+2⋅2+52K=13.\begin{aligned}K&=2\cdot\tfrac{3+3}{2}+2\cdot\tfrac{2+5}{2}\\K&=13.\end{aligned}KK​=2⋅23+3​+2⋅22+5​=13.​

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The trapezoid contribution is zero because the signed width is zero. Twice that contribution is the sum of the given cross-product difference and the endpoint difference bib_ibi​.

    0=20+bibi=−20.\begin{aligned}0&=20+b_i\\b_i&=-20.\end{aligned}0bi​​=20+bi​=−20.​

    Some product differences of a simple polygon in boundary order are negative, but their total is positive. Which claim follows?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The positive total is twice the signed area.

    2S>0.2S>0.2S>0.

    Individual product differences can have either sign. The positive total identifies counterclockwise orientation.
