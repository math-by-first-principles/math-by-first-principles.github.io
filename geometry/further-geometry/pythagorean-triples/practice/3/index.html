    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Removing 333 from the inputs removes a factor 999 from the outputs. The reduced pair (11,7)(11,7)(11,7) is coprime and odd in both entries, so its output still has a factor 222.

    112+72=170.11^2+7^2=170.112+72=170.

    The hypotenuse 170170170 has exactly one factor of 222. No odd prime divides all the reduced outputs, since it would divide both reduced parameters.

    The odd leg of a primitive triple is a prime. Which relation between the parameters follows?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Factor the formula for the odd leg.

    a=(m−n)(m+n).a=(m-n)(m+n).a=(m−n)(m+n).

    Both factors are positive integers, and the second exceeds 111. Since their product is prime, the smaller factor is 111.

    For a right triangle with integer sides, a prime divides one leg and the hypotenuse. Why must it divide the other leg?

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Call the given leg aaa and the other leg bbb.

    b2=c2−a2.b^2=c^2-a^2.b2=c2−a2.

    The prime divides both terms on the right, so it divides their difference. A prime dividing b2b^2b2 must divide bbb.
