    Which ordered triple does the parameter pair (12,5)(12,5)(12,5) give when the square difference is written first?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Square both parameters before adding or subtracting. Use the doubled product for the other leg.

    a=144−25=119b=2⋅12⋅5=120c=144+25=169.\begin{aligned}a&=144-25\\&=119\\[2pt]b&=2\cdot12\cdot5\\&=120\\[2pt]c&=144+25\\&=169.\end{aligned}abc​=144−25=119=2⋅12⋅5=120=144+25=169.​
  2. 2

    The pair (16,10)(16,10)(16,10) generates a scaled triple. Which triple remains after division by its GCF? Use square-difference leg first.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The parameters share 222, and dividing both by 222 gives the coprime pair (8,5)(8,5)(8,5) of opposite parity. Each original output is 444 times its new partner.

    a=64−25=39b=2⋅8⋅5=80c=64+25=89.\begin{aligned}a&=64-25\\&=39\\[2pt]b&=2\cdot8\cdot5\\&=80\\[2pt]c&=64+25\\&=89.\end{aligned}abc​=64−25=39=2⋅8⋅5=80=64+25=89.​
  3. 3

    For the generated triple from parameter pair (17,9)(17,9)(17,9), what is the GCF of the three entries?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The parameter squares are odd, so all three outputs are even. The sum of two odd squares leaves remainder 222 when divided by 444.

    172+92=370.17^2+9^2=370.172+92=370.

    No odd prime divides both the square sum and difference because it would divide both 171717 and 999. Therefore 222 is the full GCF.

    The product of 121212 and 272727 is a perfect square. Why does this example fall outside the claim that two coprime factors of a square must be squares?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The two factors share 333. The exponent of 333 in the product is even, although the exponent in each factor is odd.

    12=22⋅327=3312⋅27=182.\begin{aligned}12&=2^2\cdot3\\27&=3^3\\12\cdot27&=18^2.\end{aligned}122712⋅27​=22⋅3=33=182.​
  8. 8

    An odd prime divides both the square-sum output and the square-difference output. What must it also divide?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The prime divides the sum and difference of those outputs.

    c+a=2m2c−a=2n2.\begin{aligned}c+a&=2m^2\\c-a&=2n^2.\end{aligned}c+ac−a​=2m2=2n2.​

    As it is odd, it must divide both parameter squares and therefore both parameters.

  9. 9

    A generated triple has c−a=200c-a=200c−a=200, where aaa is the square-difference leg. The parameters differ by 111. What is its even leg?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Subtract the square-difference formula from the square-sum formula to isolate the smaller parameter square.

    2n2=200n2=100n=10m=11.\begin{aligned}2n^2&=200\\n^2&=100\\n&=10\\m&=11.\end{aligned}2n2n2nm​=200=100=10=11.​

    Then use the doubled product.

    2⋅11⋅10=220.2\cdot11\cdot10=220.2⋅11⋅10=220.

  11. 11

    In the reverse proof, suppose a prime divides both the half-sum and the half-difference of the hypotenuse and odd leg. Which consequence rules this out for a primitive triple?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The prime would divide the sum ccc and the difference aaa. It would then divide the even leg square.

    b2=c2−a2.b^2=c^2-a^2.b2=c2−a2.

    A prime factor of b2b^2b2 also divides bbb. A shared prime among all three sides contradicts GCF 111.

  12. 12

    A valid pair has m>n>0m>n>0m>n>0. What happens if their positions are interchanged in all three formulas?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The product and the sum are unchanged by switching order. The difference reverses.

    n2−m2=−(m2−n2).n^2-m^2=-(m^2-n^2).n2−m2=−(m2−n2).

    It becomes negative, so the interchanged inputs do not give three positive lengths.
