  1. Problem 1 Three outputs from two inputs

    Use the parameter pair (13,10)(13,10)(13,10) to generate a Pythagorean triple.

    Identify the hypotenuse and explain why the triple is primitive.

    1. Hint 1

      The square sum gives the longest length.

    2. Hint 2

      Check both the GCF and the parity of the input pair.

    Answer

    The triple is (69,260,269)(69,260,269)(69,260,269) with hypotenuse 269269269. It is primitive because 131313 and 101010 are coprime and have opposite parity.

    Full solution

    Compute the difference of the squares to find one leg.

    132−102=6913^2-10^2=69132−102=69

    Double the product for the other leg.

    2⋅13⋅10=2602\cdot13\cdot10=2602⋅13⋅10=260

    Add the squares to find the hypotenuse.

    132+102=26913^2+10^2=269132+102=269

    The prime 131313 has no common factor greater than 111 with 101010, and one parameter is odd while the other is even.

    The triple is primitive.

    Answer

    The triple is (69,260,269)(69,260,269)(69,260,269) with hypotenuse 269269269. It is primitive because 131313 and 101010 are coprime and have opposite parity.

    Key idea

    The square sum is the hypotenuse, while both input tests determine primitivity.

    The prime factors of 888888 are 222 and 111111.

    Neither divides 105105105, so no prime divides all three quotients.

    The quotient has GCF 111, so the divisor 444 was the full GCF of the original lengths and the scale factor is 444.

  3. Problem 3 Two ways to get a scaled output

    For each case, state which primitive condition fails, find the GCF of the outputs, and give the primitive triple. The parameters are positive with m>nm>nm>n.

    Case A uses the parameter pair (19,5)(19,5)(19,5).

    Case B uses the parameter pair (24,21)(24,21)(24,21).

    1. Hint 1

      Check parity and common factors separately for each input pair.

    2. Hint 2

      In Case B, remove the common factor from the inputs before using the three formulas.

    3. Hint 3

      In Case A, the sum of two odd squares can be divided by two but not by four.

    Answer

    Case A fails opposite parity, has GCF 222, and reduces to (168,95,193)(168,95,193)(168,95,193). Case B fails coprimality, has GCF 999, and reduces to (15,112,113)(15,112,113)(15,112,113). Either order of the legs is valid.

    Full solution

    Case A.

    The parameters are coprime but both odd, and the outputs are (336,190,386)(336,190,386)(336,190,386).

    The square sum 386386386 is even and has no factor of 444.

    No odd prime can divide all Case A outputs because it would have to divide both parameter squares.

    The GCF is 222, so division gives (168,95,193)(168,95,193)(168,95,193).

    Case B.

    The parameters have opposite parity but share GCF 333.

    Dividing them by 333 gives (8,7)(8,7)(8,7), which is coprime and of opposite parity.

    The Case B outputs from (8,7)(8,7)(8,7) are (15,112,113)(15,112,113)(15,112,113) and have GCF 111.

    Each entry generated by the original pair is nine times the corresponding entry, so the original GCF is 999.

    Answer

    Case A fails opposite parity, has GCF 222, and reduces to (168,95,193)(168,95,193)(168,95,193). Case B fails coprimality, has GCF 999, and reduces to (15,112,113)(15,112,113)(15,112,113). Either order of the legs is valid.

    Key idea

    A shared parameter factor enters the outputs squared, while same parity supplies a common factor of two.

    Taking positive roots gives

    (m,n)=(13,8).(m,n)=(13,8).(m,n)=(13,8).

    The doubled product gives the even leg.

    2⋅13⋅8=2082\cdot13\cdot8=2082⋅13⋅8=208

    Answer

  5. Problem 5 Build the largest copy with a fixed stock

    A model right-triangle frame has side lengths 848484, 187187187, and 205205205 cm. A shop will make a larger frame by multiplying each of the three model lengths by the same integer greater than one.

    The shop has 1,5001{,}5001,500 cm of stock, and cutting wastes no material. Find the greatest allowable scale factor, the three frame lengths, and the unused stock.

    Text description of this figure

    The right triangle has a horizontal leg labeled 187 cm, a vertical leg labeled 84 cm, and a hypotenuse labeled 205 cm.

    1. Hint 1

      The stock limit applies to the sum of all three lengths.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare consecutive integer multiples of the model perimeter near the limit.

    Answer

    The greatest scale factor is 333. The frame lengths are 252252252, 561561561, and 615615615 cm, and 727272 cm of stock remains.

    Full solution

    Add the three model lengths.

    84+187+205=47684+187+205=47684+187+205=476

    A scale factor of 333 uses 1,4281{,}4281,428 cm, and a scale factor of 444 would use 1,9041{,}9041,904 cm.

    Larger factors only increase the perimeter.

    Multiply each model length by 333 to get 252252252, 561561561, and 615615615 cm.

    Subtract the stock used from the stock available.

    1500−1428=721500-1428=721500−1428=72

    Answer

    The greatest scale factor is 333. The frame lengths are 252252252, 561561561, and 615615615 cm, and 727272 cm of stock remains.

    Key idea

    The perimeter of an integer-scale copy grows by the same factor as its sides.

  6. Problem 6 An input from a short list

    For Euclid's formulas, fix m=15m=15m=15. The other parameter nnn must be one of 444, 666, 777, or 101010.

    Select every choice that makes a primitive triple. Give the side lengths for each selected choice, and state why each rejected choice fails.

    1. Hint 1

      The odd parameter 151515 needs an even partner for opposite parity.

    2. Hint 2

      An even partner can still share one of the prime factors of 151515.

    Answer

    Only n=4n=4n=4 works, producing (209,120,241)(209,120,241)(209,120,241). The input 666 shares 333 with 151515, 777 has the same parity as 151515, and 101010 shares 555 with 151515.

    Full solution

    Choice 444 is even and has no common prime factor with 151515.

    Its generated triple is primitive.

    Compute the three outputs.

    152−42=2092⋅15⋅4=120152+42=241\begin{aligned}15^2-4^2&=209\\2\cdot15\cdot4&=120\\15^2+4^2&=241\end{aligned}152−422⋅15⋅4152+42​=209=120=241​

    Choice 666 is even but shares the prime 333 with 151515.

    Choice 777 is coprime to 151515 but both inputs are odd.

    Choice 101010 is even but shares the prime 555 with 151515.

    Answer

    Only n=4n=4n=4 works, producing (209,120,241)(209,120,241)(209,120,241). The input 666 shares 333 with 151515, 777 has the same parity as 151515, and 101010 shares 555 with 151515.

    Key idea

    An input pair must pass both tests before its output is primitive.

  7. Problem 7 Two measurements about the same sides

    Answer

    The parameters are (17,12)(17,12)(17,12). The odd leg is 145145145, the even leg is 408408408, and the hypotenuse is 433433433.

    Full solution

    (m,n)=(17,12).(m,n)=(17,12).(m,n)=(17,12).

    The two square values determine the odd leg and hypotenuse, and the roots determine the even leg.

    289−144=1452⋅17⋅12=408289+144=433\begin{aligned}289-144&=145\\2\cdot17\cdot12&=408\\289+144&=433\end{aligned}289−1442⋅17⋅12289+144​=145=408=433​

  8. Problem 8 A square product with shared primes

    A right triangle has integer side lengths 525525525, 864864864, and 1,0111{,}0111,011. Kai calculates two values from the hypotenuse and the odd leg.

    The half-sum is 768768768.

    The half-difference is 243243243.

    Their product is 4322432^24322. Kai concludes that each factor is a square because the product is a square.

    Explain the gap in Kai's argument. Find the primitive triple, its two parameters, and the scale factor to the given triangle.

    1. Hint 1

      For the separate factors to inherit paired primes, they need an additional property.

    2. Hint 2

      Check what happens when you divide every original side by 333.

    3. Hint 3

      Use the odd leg and hypotenuse of the reduced triangle.

    Answer

    The factors share 333, so the square product does not force each factor to be square. The primitive triple is (175,288,337)(175,288,337)(175,288,337), its parameters are (16,9)(16,9)(16,9), and the scale factor is 333.

    Full solution

    The prime factorizations are

    768=28⋅3243=35\begin{aligned}768&=2^8\cdot3\\243&=3^5\end{aligned}768243​=28⋅3=35​

    The copies of 333 pair up in the product because the exponents add to six.

    Neither factor has an even exponent of 333 on its own.

    Divide all three sides by 333 to get (175,288,337)(175,288,337)(175,288,337).

    The prime factors of 175175175 are 555 and 777, neither of which divides 288288288.

    The quotient is primitive and 333 is the full GCF.

    Find the half-sum and half-difference using the new hypotenuse and odd leg.

    (337+175)/2=256(337−175)/2=81\begin{aligned}(337+175)/2&=256\\(337-175)/2&=81\end{aligned}(337+175)/2(337−175)/2​=256=81​

    Their positive roots give

    (m,n)=(16,9).(m,n)=(16,9).(m,n)=(16,9).

    Answer

    The factors share 333, so the square product does not force each factor to be square. The primitive triple is (175,288,337)(175,288,337)(175,288,337), its parameters are (16,9)(16,9)(16,9), and the scale factor is 333.

    Key idea

    Remove the full scale factor before using the argument about coprime factors of a square.

  9. Problem 9 A whole family from neighboring integers

    Take any positive integer nnn and use m=n+1m=n+1m=n+1 in Euclid's formulas.

    Prove that the output is primitive. Show that its hypotenuse is one unit longer than its even leg, and give an expression for its odd leg in terms of nnn.

    1. Hint 1

      What would a shared divisor of two consecutive integers have to divide?

    2. Hint 2

      To compare the hypotenuse and even leg, subtract their formulas and look for a binomial square.

    3. Hint 3

      The odd leg uses the difference of the two parameter squares.

    Answer

    The output is primitive, its hypotenuse exceeds its even leg by 111, and its odd leg is 2n+12n+12n+1.

    Full solution

    A shared divisor of nnn and n+1n+1n+1 must divide their difference, which is 111.

    The inputs are coprime.

    They are consecutive, so exactly one is even.

    The two primitive conditions therefore hold for every positive integer nnn.

    Subtract the even-leg expression from the hypotenuse expression.

    c−b=m2+n2−2mn=(m−n)2=1\begin{aligned}c-b&=m^2+n^2-2mn\\&=(m-n)^2\\&=1\end{aligned}c−b​=m2+n2−2mn=(m−n)2=1​

    For the odd leg, factor the difference of squares.

    a=(m−n)(m+n)=1⋅(2n+1)=2n+1\begin{aligned}a&=(m-n)(m+n)\\&=1\cdot(2n+1)\\&=2n+1\end{aligned}a​=(m−n)(m+n)=1⋅(2n+1)=2n+1​

    Answer

    The output is primitive, its hypotenuse exceeds its even leg by 111, and its odd leg is 2n+12n+12n+1.

    Key idea

    Consecutive parameters make the square of their difference exactly one.

  10. Problem 10 Prove that the recovery always works

    Let a primitive right triangle have positive integer sides and hypotenuse ccc.

    Part A. Prove that exactly one leg is even and that ccc is odd. Call the odd leg aaa and the even leg bbb.

    Part B. Let uuu and vvv be the half-sum and half-difference of ccc and aaa, respectively. Prove they are positive coprime integers whose product is a square.

    Part C. Explain why uuu and vvv must each be a square, and show how their positive roots give all three side lengths. Justify coprimality and opposite parity of the roots.

    1. Hint 1

      For Part A, compare the remainders of even and odd squares after dividing by four.

    2. Hint 2

      For Part B, a prime shared by the two half-values would divide their sum and difference.

    3. Hint 3

      For Part C, count how often each prime occurs in the product and in the one factor where it appears.

    Answer

    Part A gives one even leg and an odd hypotenuse. Part B gives positive coprime integers with product (b/2)2(b/2)^2(b/2)2. Part C gives coprime roots of opposite parity with

    a=m2−n2b=2mnc=m2+n2\begin{aligned}a&=m^2-n^2\\b&=2mn\\c&=m^2+n^2\end{aligned}abc​=m2−n2=2mn=m2+n2​

    Full solution

    Part A.

    Two even legs force an even hypotenuse and a shared factor of 222.

    Two odd legs make the sum of their squares leave remainder 222 on division by 444, whereas squares leave 000 or 111.

    One leg is therefore odd and one is even.

    Their square sum is odd, so the hypotenuse is odd.

    Part B.

    The lengths ccc and aaa are both odd and c>a>0c>a>0c>a>0, so uuu and vvv are positive integers with u>vu>vu>v.

    Their product is

    uv=(c+a)(c−a)/4=(c2−a2)/4=(b/2)2\begin{aligned}uv&=(c+a)(c-a)/4\\&=(c^2-a^2)/4\\&=(b/2)^2\end{aligned}uv​=(c+a)(c−a)/4=(c2−a2)/4=(b/2)2​

    A prime shared by uuu and vvv would divide their sum ccc and their difference aaa.

    It would then divide b2b^2b2 and therefore bbb, which is impossible for a primitive triple.

    Part C.

    In the square product every prime occurs an even number of times.

    Since each prime occurs in just one factor, its exponent in that factor is even.

    The factors are squares.

    Let mmm and nnn be the positive roots of uuu and vvv.

    They satisfy m>n>0m>n>0m>n>0, share no prime factors, and have opposite parity because their squares add to the odd hypotenuse.

    Recover the lengths from the difference, positive square root of the product, and sum.

    a=m2−n2b=2mnc=m2+n2\begin{aligned}a&=m^2-n^2\\b&=2mn\\c&=m^2+n^2\end{aligned}abc​=m2−n2=2mn=m2+n2​

    Answer

    Part A gives one even leg and an odd hypotenuse. Part B gives positive coprime integers with product (b/2)2(b/2)^2(b/2)2. Part C gives coprime roots of opposite parity with

    a=m2−n2b=2mnc=m2+n2\begin{aligned}a&=m^2-n^2\\b&=2mn\\c&=m^2+n^2\end{aligned}abc​=m2−n2=2mn=m2+n2​

    Key idea

    Coprimality is what lets the square product split into two square factors.
