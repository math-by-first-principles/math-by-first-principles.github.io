    The center and external point are 464646 mm apart. Which compass opening can draw midpoint arcs that cross in two places?

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The compass opening must exceed half the length of OP.

    The midpoint MMM of OPOPOP is 999 units from PPP. What is the diameter of the auxiliary circle used to construct the tangents?

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The compass opening from M to P is a radius. The diameter has twice that length.

    The circles in the diagram cross at T1T_1T1​ and T2T_2T2​. How would the line through those two points meet the given solid circle?

    Solution

  8. 8

    Point PPP coincides with the center OOO of a positive-radius circle. How many tangent lines pass through PPP?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The center-to-point distance is smaller than the positive radius rrr.

    OP=0<r.OP=0<r.OP=0<r.

    Every line through the center cuts a diameter, so each line crosses the circle twice.

  9. 9

    Point PPP lies on a ray starting at OOO, beyond that ray's crossing with the given circle. How many tangent lines through PPP can the diameter construction produce?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Call the ray's crossing with the circle A. P is beyond A, so its center distance exceeds the radius.

    OP>OAOA=r.\begin{gathered}OP>OA\\OA=r.\end{gathered}OP>OAOA=r.​

    The auxiliary circle gives two distinct contact points.
