    Two rods measure 14 cm and 18 cm. A third rod measures 32 cm. What happens when you try to make a triangle?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The shorter lengths total exactly the longest length.

    14+18=32.14+18=32.14+18=32.

    The two shorter rods must stretch in a straight line to meet. A nondegenerate triangle requires a strict inequality.

  6. 6

    Why is the factor s−cs-cs−c positive in a nondegenerate triangle?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Twice the factor equals the combined length of aaa and bbb, less ccc.

    2(s−c)=a+b−c.2(s-c)=a+b-c.2(s−c)=a+b−c.

    The strict triangle inequality makes the right side positive.
