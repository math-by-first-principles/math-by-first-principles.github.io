    A triangular panel has sides 25 cm, 26 cm, and 37 cm. A mount needs the perpendicular height to the 37 cm side to exceed 17 cm. Which conclusion follows?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The three lengths are positive, and 51 exceeds 37. Half the perimeter is 44 cm, leaving differences of 19 cm, 18 cm, and 7 cm. With lengths in centimeters, Heron's formula gives the squared area.

    K2=44⋅19⋅18⋅7,K2=105336.\begin{aligned} K^2&=44\cdot19\cdot18\cdot7,\\ K^2&=105336. \end{aligned}K2K2​=44⋅19⋅18⋅7,=105336.​

    The 37 cm side and its height obey 37h=2K37h=2K37h=2K. Squaring that relation gives the squared value of 37h37h37h.

    (37h)2=4K2,(37h)2=421344.\begin{aligned} (37h)^2&=4K^2,\\ (37h)^2&=421344. \end{aligned}(37h)2(37h)2​=4K2,=421344.​

    A height of 17 cm would give the following square.

    (37⋅17)2=395641.(37\cdot17)^2=395641.(37⋅17)2=395641.

    The actual square is larger. Since both heights are positive, the height exceeds 17 cm.
