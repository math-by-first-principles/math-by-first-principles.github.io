  1. Problem 1 The side across from the smaller angle

    In triangle ABCABCABC, angles AAA and CCC measure 58∘58^\circ58∘ and 81∘81^\circ81∘, respectively. Side ABABAB is 20 cm long.

    Find BCBCBC to the nearest tenth of a centimeter. Explain why it should be shorter than ABABAB.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle ABC has angles 58 degrees at A and 81 degrees at C. The base AB is 20 centimeters long. Side BC is requested.

    1. Hint 1

      Identify the angle across from ABABAB.

    2. Hint 2

      The requested length BCBCBC is across from angle AAA. Compare the two corresponding ratios.

    Answer

    BC≈17.2 cm.BC\approx17.2\text{ cm}.BC≈17.2 cm.

    Side BCBCBC lies opposite the smaller angle.

    Full solution

    Use the known pair of ABABAB and angle CCC.

    Side BCBCBC faces angle AAA, so the two ratios give

    BCsin⁡58∘=20sin⁡81∘.\frac{BC}{\sin58^\circ}=\frac{20}{\sin81^\circ}.sin58∘BC​=sin81∘20​.

    Solve for the length and round.

    BC=20sin⁡58∘sin⁡81∘ cm≈17.2 cm.\begin{aligned}BC&=\frac{20\sin58^\circ}{\sin81^\circ}\text{ cm}\\&\approx17.2\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}BC​=sin81∘20sin58∘​ cm≈17.2 cm.​

    Angle AAA is smaller than angle CCC.

    The side across from the smaller angle is shorter.

    Answer

    BC≈17.2 cm.BC\approx17.2\text{ cm}.BC≈17.2 cm.

    Side BCBCBC lies opposite the smaller angle.

    Key idea

    Label each side by the angle across from it before writing a proportion.

    Find CCC and the lengths of BCBCBC and CACACA. Round lengths to the nearest tenth of a meter.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle ABC has angles 64 degrees at A and 56 degrees at B. The side between them is 23 meters long.

    Use angle BBB for CACACA.

    CA=23sin⁡56∘sin⁡60∘ m≈22.0 m.\begin{aligned}CA&=23\frac{\sin56^\circ}{\sin60^\circ}\text{ m}\\&\approx22.0\text{ m}.\end{aligned}CA​=23sin60∘sin56∘​ m≈22.0 m.​

    Answer

  3. Problem 3 A fixed angle and four radii

    Angle AAA measures 45∘45^\circ45∘ and ACACAC is 20 units long. Point BBB must lie on the ray from AAA that makes this angle with ACACAC.

    For each case, state how many nondegenerate triangles fit. Explain where the circle centered at CCC meets the ray.

    (a) The radius is 13 units.

    (b) The radius is 10210\sqrt{2}102​ units.

    (c) The radius is 17 units.

    (d) The radius is 20 units.

    1. Hint 1

      Find the perpendicular distance from CCC to the ray.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare each radius with both the perpendicular distance and the distance from CCC to AAA.

    Answer

    (a)Zero.(b)One, at the foot.(c)Two, both past A.(d)One past A.\begin{aligned}\text{(a)}\quad&\text{Zero.}\\\text{(b)}\quad&\text{One, at the foot.}\\\text{(c)}\quad&\text{Two, both past }A.\\\text{(d)}\quad&\text{One past }A.\end{aligned}(a)(b)(c)(d)​Zero.One, at the foot.Two, both past A.One past A.​

    Full solution

    The perpendicular distance is

    h=20sin⁡45∘=102.\begin{aligned}h&=20\sin45^\circ\\&=10\sqrt{2}.\end{aligned}h​=20sin45∘=102​.​

    (a) The radius 13 is below the height because its square is below 200.

    The circle misses the line.

    (b) The radius equals the height.

    The only intersection is at the perpendicular foot, strictly ahead of AAA.

    (c) The square of 17 is above 200, so 17 exceeds the height.

    Since 17 is also below 20, the two crossings are both ahead of AAA.

    (d) One crossing is at AAA, since the radius equals ACACAC.

    The farther crossing makes a triangle.

    Answer

    (a)Zero.(b)One, at the foot.(c)Two, both past A.(d)One past A.\begin{aligned}\text{(a)}\quad&\text{Zero.}\\\text{(b)}\quad&\text{One, at the foot.}\\\text{(c)}\quad&\text{Two, both past }A.\\\text{(d)}\quad&\text{One past }A.\end{aligned}(a)(b)(c)(d)​Zero.One, at the foot.Two, both past A.One past A.​

    Key idea

    For an acute given angle, compare the radius with both the perpendicular height and the distance to the ray start.

  4. Problem 4 Survey an island station

    Stations AAA and BBB are 118 m apart on one shore. They sight the same station CCC on an island in the horizontal plane of AAA and BBB. At AAA, the angle from the outward baseline ray pointing away from BBB toward CCC is 119∘119^\circ119∘. At BBB, the interior angle is 47∘47^\circ47∘.

    Find ACACAC and BCBCBC to the nearest tenth of a meter. State which shore station is closer.

    Text description of this figure

    Stations A and B are 118 meters apart. At A, the 119-degree exterior reading starts along the extension away from B and ends at the sight line to C. At B, the 47-degree interior reading starts along BA.

    1. Hint 1

      The outward ray at AAA and ray ABABAB form a straight angle.

    2. Hint 2

      After finding the interior angle at AAA, use the angle sum to get the angle opposite the measured baseline.

    Answer

    AC≈90.7 m.BC≈108.5 m.\begin{aligned}AC&\approx90.7\text{ m}.\\BC&\approx108.5\text{ m}.\end{aligned}ACBC​≈90.7 m.≈108.5 m.​

    Station AAA is closer.

    Full solution

    The two baseline rays from AAA are opposite, so the interior angle is

    A=180∘−119∘=61∘.\begin{aligned}A&=180^\circ-119^\circ\\&=61^\circ.\end{aligned}A​=180∘−119∘=61∘.​

    The angle across from the baseline is

    C=180∘−61∘−47∘=72∘.\begin{aligned}C&=180^\circ-61^\circ-47^\circ\\&=72^\circ.\end{aligned}C​=180∘−61∘−47∘=72∘.​

    Segment ACACAC faces angle BBB.

    AC=118sin⁡47∘sin⁡72∘ m≈90.7 m.\begin{aligned}AC&=118\frac{\sin47^\circ}{\sin72^\circ}\text{ m}\\&\approx90.7\text{ m}.\end{aligned}AC​=118sin72∘sin47∘​ m≈90.7 m.​

    Segment BCBCBC faces angle AAA.

    BC=118sin⁡61∘sin⁡72∘ m≈108.5 m.\begin{aligned}BC&=118\frac{\sin61^\circ}{\sin72^\circ}\text{ m}\\&\approx108.5\text{ m}.\end{aligned}BC​=118sin72∘sin61∘​ m≈108.5 m.​

    Station AAA is closer because ACACAC is shorter.

    This agrees with BBB being the smaller shore angle.

    Answer

    AC≈90.7 m.BC≈108.5 m.\begin{aligned}AC&\approx90.7\text{ m}.\\BC&\approx108.5\text{ m}.\end{aligned}ACBC​≈90.7 m.≈108.5 m.​

    Station AAA is closer.

    Key idea

    Convert an exterior reading to the interior angle before finding the opposite pair for the baseline.

  5. Problem 5 Recover both triangles

    In triangle ABCABCABC, angle AAA is 22∘22^\circ22∘. The lengths of aaa and bbb are 15 cm and 27 cm, respectively.

    Find every possible set of measurements for BBB, CCC, and ccc. Give angles and lengths to the nearest tenth in degrees and centimeters.

    1. Hint 1

      The sine of BBB comes from the known opposite pair A,aA,aA,a and the length bbb.

    2. Hint 2

      Try the inverse sine angle and its supplement. In each case, find the remaining angle before solving for ccc.

    Answer

    Case 1.

    B≈42.4∘.C≈115.6∘.c≈36.1 cm.\begin{aligned}B&\approx42.4^\circ.\\C&\approx115.6^\circ.\\c&\approx36.1\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}BCc​≈42.4∘.≈115.6∘.≈36.1 cm.​

    Case 2.

    B≈137.6∘.C≈20.4∘.c≈14.0 cm.\begin{aligned}B&\approx137.6^\circ.\\C&\approx20.4^\circ.\\c&\approx14.0\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}BCc​≈137.6∘.≈20.4∘.≈14.0 cm.​

    Full solution

    The opposite pairs give

    sin⁡B=27sin⁡22∘15.\sin B=\frac{27\sin22^\circ}{15}.sinB=1527sin22∘​.

    Case 1.

    Inverse sine gives about 42.4∘42.4^\circ42.4∘ for BBB.

    Subtract AAA and the unrounded value of BBB from 180∘180^\circ180∘.

    This gives a positive CCC of about 115.6∘115.6^\circ115.6∘.

    Case 1 length.

    c=15sin⁡Csin⁡22∘ cm≈36.1 cm.\begin{aligned}c&=\frac{15\sin C}{\sin22^\circ}\text{ cm}\\&\approx36.1\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}c​=sin22∘15sinC​ cm≈36.1 cm.​

    Case 2.

    The supplement gives about 137.6∘137.6^\circ137.6∘ for BBB.

    It leaves a positive CCC of about 20.4∘20.4^\circ20.4∘.

    Case 2 length.

    c=15sin⁡Csin⁡22∘ cm≈14.0 cm.\begin{aligned}c&=\frac{15\sin C}{\sin22^\circ}\text{ cm}\\&\approx14.0\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}c​=sin22∘15sinC​ cm≈14.0 cm.​

    There are exactly two triangle angles with the calculated sine, so these cases exhaust the possibilities.

    Answer

    Case 1.

    B≈42.4∘.C≈115.6∘.c≈36.1 cm.\begin{aligned}B&\approx42.4^\circ.\\C&\approx115.6^\circ.\\c&\approx36.1\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}BCc​≈42.4∘.≈115.6∘.≈36.1 cm.​

  6. Problem 6 Two bases and one height

    Angle AAA is 30∘30^\circ30∘, side ACACAC is 40 cm, and side BCBCBC is 29 cm. The two possible triangles share the same ACACAC, with both choices of BBB on the same ray from AAA.

    (a) Find the two exact lengths of ABABAB.

    (b) Find the exact difference between the areas of the two triangles.

    Text description of this figure

    The ray starts at A and makes a 30-degree angle with AC. AC is 40 centimeters long. The circle centered at C has radius 29 centimeters.

    1. Hint 1

      Drop the perpendicular from CCC to the ray and name its foot HHH.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the right triangle with hypotenuse BCBCBC to find the distance from HHH to either crossing.

    3. Hint 3

      Both areas use the same height. Find the difference between the two bases.

    Answer

    (a)

    cnear=(203−21) cm.cfar=(203+21) cm.\begin{aligned}c_{\text{near}}&=(20\sqrt{3}-21)\text{ cm}.\\c_{\text{far}}&=(20\sqrt{3}+21)\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}cnear​cfar​​=(203​−21) cm.=(203​+21) cm.​

    (b)

    420 cm2.420\text{ cm}^2.420 cm2.

    Full solution

    (a) The perpendicular height is

    CH=40sin⁡30∘=20 cm.\begin{aligned}CH&=40\sin30^\circ\\&=20\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}CH​=40sin30∘=20 cm.​

    The signed projection is positive and gives

    AH=40cos⁡30∘=203 cm.\begin{aligned}AH&=40\cos30^\circ\\&=20\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}AH​=40cos30∘=203​ cm.​

    Both crossings are the same distance from HHH.

    With BCBCBC as hypotenuse,

    HB=292−202 cm=21 cm.\begin{aligned}HB&=\sqrt{29^2-20^2}\text{ cm}\\&=21\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}HB​=292−202​ cm=21 cm.​

    Since AHAHAH exceeds 21 cm, both crossings are past AAA.

    Subtract 21 cm to get the nearer base and add it for the farther base.

    (b) The farther base is 42 cm longer.

    Multiply half that difference by the common height.

    Kfar−Knear=12(42)(20) cm2=420 cm2.\begin{aligned}K_{\text{far}}-K_{\text{near}}&=\tfrac12(42)(20)\text{ cm}^2\\&=420\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}Kfar​−Knear​​=21​(42)(20) cm2=420 cm2.​

    Answer

    (a)

    cnear=(203−21) cm.cfar=(203+21) cm.\begin{aligned}c_{\text{near}}&=(20\sqrt{3}-21)\text{ cm}.\\c_{\text{far}}&=(20\sqrt{3}+21)\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}cnear​cfar​​=(203​−21) cm.=(203​+21) cm.​

    (b)

    420 cm2.420\text{ cm}^2.420 cm2.

    Key idea

    Two crossing points equidistant from the foot give bases that differ by twice that distance.

  7. Problem 7 A sine value that passes only the first check

    (b) Angle AAA leaves only 45∘45^\circ45∘ for BBB and CCC together.

    Both candidates for BBB exceed that amount, so each leaves a negative angle at CCC.

    (c) The perpendicular foot lies behind the start.

    All points past AAA are farther from CCC than AAA is, so a radius of 10 cannot reach the ray.

    (a)58.1∘, 121.9∘.(b)Neither angle fits.(c)No ray intersection.\begin{aligned}\text{(a)}\quad&58.1^\circ,\ 121.9^\circ.\\\text{(b)}\quad&\text{Neither angle fits.}\\\text{(c)}\quad&\text{No ray intersection.}\end{aligned}(a)(b)(c)​58.1∘, 121.9∘.Neither angle fits.No ray intersection.​

  8. Problem 8 An area identity with a missing step

    Triangle ABCABCABC is nondegenerate. Twice its area can be written in one way at AAA and another way at CCC.

    2K=bcsin⁡A.2K=bc\sin A.2K=bcsinA. 2K=absin⁡C.2K=ab\sin C.2K=absinC.

    (a) Use these equal expressions to prove that sin⁡A/a\sin A/asinA/a equals sin⁡C/c\sin C/csinC/c. Explain why the division is allowed.

    (b) Apply the relationship to a triangle with aaa of length 15, ccc of length 19, and sine of CCC equal to 4/54/54/5. Give the sine of AAA exactly.

    1. Hint 1

      Which product contains all three side lengths?

    2. Hint 2

      For part (b), solve the equation between the sine-over-side ratios for the missing sine.

    Answer

    (a) Division by the positive product abcabcabc gives

    sin⁡Aa=sin⁡Cc.\frac{\sin A}{a}=\frac{\sin C}{c}.asinA​=csinC​.

    (b)

    sin⁡A=1219.\sin A=\frac{12}{19}.sinA=1912​.

    Full solution

    (a) The two expressions equal the same 2K2K2K.

    All three side lengths are positive, so dividing each expression by their product preserves equality.

    The factors bbb and ccc cancel from the first expression, leaving sin⁡A/a\sin A/asinA/a.

    The factors aaa and bbb cancel from the second, leaving sin⁡C/c\sin C/csinC/c.

    The two quotients are equal.

    (b) Substitute the lengths and the sine of CCC, then multiply by 15.

    sin⁡A=1519⋅45=1219.\begin{aligned}\sin A&=\frac{15}{19}\cdot\frac45\\&=\frac{12}{19}.\end{aligned}sinA​=1915​⋅54​=1912​.​

    Answer

    (a) Division by the positive product abcabcabc gives

    sin⁡Aa=sin⁡Cc.\frac{\sin A}{a}=\frac{\sin C}{c}.asinA​=csinC​.

    (b)

    sin⁡A=1219.\sin A=\frac{12}{19}.sinA=1912​.

    Key idea

    Dividing equivalent area expressions by the product of the sides leaves the opposite-pair ratios.

  9. Problem 9 Line tangency behind the start

    Angle AAA is 135∘135^\circ135∘ and ACACAC is 14 units long. Point BBB must lie on the ray from AAA making that angle with ACACAC. The diagram draws the ray solid and its backward extension dashed.

    (b) The radius is 14 units.

    (c) The radius is 15 units.

    Text description of this figure

      Where is the perpendicular foot from CCC to the full line through the ray?

    2. Hint 2

      Compare the distance from CCC to AAA with distances from CCC to points farther along the forward ray.

  10. Problem 10 Choose the next field check

    A marker BBB is somewhere along a straight maintenance route starting at AAA.
