  1. 1

    In triangle ABCABCABC, AAA measures 42∘42^\circ42∘, aaa is 13 cm, and BBB measures 59∘59^\circ59∘. Which expression gives bbb in centimeters?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The supplied pair is aaa with AAA. Compare it to bbb with BBB.

    bsin⁡59∘=13sin⁡42∘.\frac{b}{\sin59^\circ}=\frac{13}{\sin42^\circ}.sin59∘b​=sin42∘13​.

    Multiply by the sine of BBB.

    Angles AAA and CCC are 57∘57^\circ57∘ and 68∘68^\circ68∘. Side ACACAC is 18 cm. Find BCBCBC to the nearest tenth of a centimeter.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The known side is opposite BBB.

    B=180∘−57∘−68∘=55∘.\begin{aligned}B&=180^\circ-57^\circ-68^\circ\\&=55^\circ.\end{aligned}B​=180∘−57∘−68∘=55∘.​

    Side BCBCBC is opposite AAA.

    BC=18sin⁡57∘sin⁡55∘ cm≈18.4 cm.\begin{aligned}BC&=18\frac{\sin57^\circ}{\sin55^\circ}\text{ cm}\\&\approx18.4\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}BC​=18sin55∘sin57∘​ cm≈18.4 cm.​

    In triangle ABCABCABC, AAA is 52∘52^\circ52∘, BBB is 101∘101^\circ101∘, and aaa is 17 m. Find ccc to the nearest tenth of a meter.

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    First find the angle opposite ccc.

  4. 4

    Stations AAA and BBB are 92 m apart and both sight target CCC in their horizontal plane. The interior angle at AAA is 47∘47^\circ47∘. At BBB, the angle from the baseline extension pointing away from AAA to the sight line is 125∘125^\circ125∘. Which expression gives BCBCBC in meters?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The interior angle at BBB is supplementary to the stated reading.

    B=180∘−125∘=55∘.\begin{aligned}B&=180^\circ-125^\circ\\&=55^\circ.\end{aligned}B​=180∘−125∘=55∘.​ C=180∘−47∘−55∘=78∘.\begin{aligned}C&=180^\circ-47^\circ-55^\circ\\&=78^\circ.\end{aligned}C​=180∘−47∘−55∘=78∘.​

    The target distance BCBCBC pairs with AAA. The known baseline pairs with CCC.

  5. 5

    Two triangles both have angles of 36∘36^\circ36∘ at AAA and 81∘81^\circ81∘ at BBB. The length of ABABAB in the second is twice its length in the first. How does the distance ACACAC change?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Both triangles have the same BBB and CCC. For each triangle the ratio is

    ACAB=sin⁡Bsin⁡C.\frac{AC}{AB}=\frac{\sin B}{\sin C}.ABAC​=sinCsinB​.

    Doubling the baseline doubles ACACAC.

  8. 8

    In an acute SSA setup, the radius aaa equals the perpendicular distance from CCC to ray ABABAB. The values of aaa and bbb are 4 and 11. What is ccc exactly?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The circle touches the ray at BBB, which makes angle BBB a right angle. The length bbb is the hypotenuse.

    c2=112−42=105.\begin{aligned}c^2&=11^2-4^2\\&=105.\end{aligned}c2​=112−42=105.​

    Use the positive square root.

  9. 9

    In an SSA problem AAA is acute and a>ba>ba>b. Why can the supplementary candidate for BBB never give a second triangle?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The circle is centered at CCC, and the start AAA is at distance bbb from CCC.

    a>b.a>b.a>b.

    The circle surrounds AAA. One line crossing lies on each side of AAA.

    In an SSA calculation, A=74∘A=74^\circA=74∘ and the two candidates for BBB are 85∘85^\circ85∘ and 95∘95^\circ95∘. What is the smaller valid value of CCC?

    Solution

    Both choices for BBB give positive remaining angles.
