    A ray at an obtuse angle meets the unit semicircle at height yyy, where 0<y<10<y<10<y<1. Which expression is its signed horizontal projection?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The unit radius and its projections satisfy the Pythagorean theorem. The horizontal coordinate must take the negative root because the angle is obtuse.

    x2=1−y2,x=−1−y2.\begin{aligned} x^2&=1-y^2,\\ x&=-\sqrt{1-y^2}. \end{aligned}x2x​=1−y2,=−1−y2​.​

    Two rigid rods are joined at a hinge. The acute setting 36∘36^\circ36∘ gives a target triangle area. Which other setting strictly between 0∘0^\circ0∘ and 180∘180^\circ180∘ gives exactly the same area?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    With the rod lengths fixed, the area fixes the height on the unit semicircle. A horizontal line at that height meets the semicircle in the original point and its reflection in the vertical axis.

    180∘−36∘=144∘180^\circ-36^\circ=144^\circ180∘−36∘=144∘

    In triangle ABCABCABC, angle AAA is acute and ABABAB is horizontal to the right. The length of ACACAC is bbb, and the length of ABABAB is ccc. When does the perpendicular from CCC to line ABABAB land strictly beyond BBB?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The foot is at horizontal coordinate bcos⁡Ab\cos AbcosA measured from AAA. Point BBB is at horizontal coordinate ccc. The foot lies strictly beyond BBB when its coordinate is greater.

    bcos⁡A>cb\cos A>cbcosA>c

    The included angle of a triangle increases from 98∘98^\circ98∘ to 166∘166^\circ166∘, while the two side lengths at the angle stay fixed. Why does the area decrease?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The upper-left arc descends from the top toward the left endpoint. The sine stays positive but decreases. The base and the length used to scale the height stay fixed.

    sin⁡166∘<sin⁡98∘\sin166^\circ<\sin98^\circsin166∘<sin98∘

    The area of a triangle is exactly half the product of two of its side lengths. What must be true of the angle between those two sides?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The area formula already contains half the product. Both side lengths are positive, so equality requires the sine to be 111. This occurs at the top of the unit semicircle, at 90∘90^\circ90∘.

    sin⁡90∘=1\sin90^\circ=1sin90∘=1
