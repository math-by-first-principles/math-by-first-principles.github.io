  1. Problem 1 Read a point left of the axis

    The ray for angle θ\thetaθ meets the upper unit semicircle at (−15113,112113)\left(-\tfrac{15}{113},\tfrac{112}{113}\right)(−11315​,113112​).

    Find cos⁡θ\cos\thetacosθ and sin⁡θ\sin\thetasinθ. Classify θ\thetaθ as acute, right, or obtuse and explain how the coordinates tell you.

    1. Hint 1

      Recall which coordinate each function reads.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare the point with the vertical axis through the top of the semicircle.

    Answer

    Cosine is −15113-\tfrac{15}{113}−11315​. Sine is 112113\tfrac{112}{113}113112​. The angle is obtuse.

    Full solution

    Cosine is the signed horizontal projection.

    cos⁡θ=−15113\cos\theta=-\frac{15}{113}cosθ=−11315​

    Sine is the vertical coordinate.

    sin⁡θ=112113\sin\theta=\frac{112}{113}sinθ=113112​

    The point lies left of the vertical axis, so its ray has turned beyond a right angle.

    The angle is obtuse.

    Answer

    Cosine is −15113-\tfrac{15}{113}−11315​. Sine is 112113\tfrac{112}{113}113112​. The angle is obtuse.

    Key idea

    The horizontal coordinate distinguishes the acute and obtuse parts of the semicircle.

  2. Problem 2 Reflect a unit point

    An acute angle TTT has cosine 1161\tfrac{11}{61}6111​ and sine 6061\tfrac{60}{61}6160​.

    Find the coordinates of the unit point for 180∘−T180^\circ-T180∘−T. Describe the reflection that takes one unit point to the other.

    1. Hint 1

      A supplementary angle completes a straight angle with the original turn.

    2. Hint 2

      Reflect the radius over the vertical line through the origin and track both coordinates.

    Answer

    The point is (−1161,6061)\left(-\tfrac{11}{61},\tfrac{60}{61}\right)(−6111​,6160​). The reflection is in the vertical axis.

    Full solution

    The original point is (1161,6061)\left(\tfrac{11}{61},\tfrac{60}{61}\right)(6111​,6160​).

    Reflect its radius in the vertical axis.

    The reflected ray makes the original angle with the negative horizontal axis, so its turn from the positive axis is 180∘−T180^\circ-T180∘−T.

    Reflection keeps the vertical coordinate and reverses the horizontal one.

    The reflected point is (−1161,6061)\left(-\tfrac{11}{61},\tfrac{60}{61}\right)(−6111​,6160​).

    Answer

    The point is (−1161,6061)\left(-\tfrac{11}{61},\tfrac{60}{61}\right)(−6111​,6160​). The reflection is in the vertical axis.

    Key idea

    Reflection in the vertical axis preserves height and reverses the signed projection.

  3. Problem 3 Exact area at an obtuse corner

    In triangle ABCABCABC, ABABAB is 272727 cm long and ACACAC is 161616 cm long. Angle AAA measures 120∘120^\circ120∘.

    Find the exact area.

    Text description of this figure

    The 27-centimeter horizontal side and the 16-centimeter slanted side meet at angle A, which measures 120 degrees.

    1. Hint 1

      Trace the two given sides to their shared vertex.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the acute supplement to get the exact sine.

    Answer

    1083 cm2108\sqrt3\text{ cm}^21083​ cm2.

    Full solution

    The given sides meet at AAA, so use the sine of 120∘120^\circ120∘.

    The supplement of 120∘120^\circ120∘ is 60∘60^\circ60∘.

    The supplementary angles have the same sine.

    sin⁡120∘=32\sin120^\circ=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}sin120∘=23​​

    Substitute in the area formula.

    K=12(27)(16)32,K=1083 cm2\begin{aligned}K&=\tfrac12(27)(16)\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\\ K&=108\sqrt3\text{ cm}^2\end{aligned}KK​=21​(27)(16)23​​,=1083​ cm2​

    Answer

    1083 cm2108\sqrt3\text{ cm}^21083​ cm2.

    Key idea

    Use the sine of the included angle, or the equal sine of its supplement.

  4. Problem 4 Recover the length of a pennant edge

    A triangular pennant has area 63 ft263\text{ ft}^263 ft2. Its base is 141414 ft long. At the left end, the base is extended left past the pennant. The angle between that extension and the pennant's slanted edge at this corner is 62∘62^\circ62∘.

    Find the length of the slanted edge at this corner to the nearest tenth of a foot.

    1. Hint 1

      The angle measured from the base extension is supplementary to the included angle.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the area as one half times the base, the unknown side, and the appropriate sine.

    Answer

    About 10.210.210.2 ft.

    Full solution

    The included angle and the 62∘62^\circ62∘ exterior angle add to a straight angle.

    Their sines are equal.

    Let uuu be the slanted length in feet.

    Substitute the measured area and base.

    63=12(14)usin⁡62∘63=\tfrac12(14)u\sin62^\circ63=21​(14)usin62∘

    Divide by the coefficient of the unknown length.

    Use degree mode and round the final result.

    u=9sin⁡62∘,u≈10.2\begin{aligned}u&=\frac9{\sin62^\circ},\\ u&\approx10.2\end{aligned}uu​=sin62∘9​,≈10.2​

    Answer

    About 10.210.210.2 ft.

    Key idea

    An exterior angle along the base extension supplies the same sine as the included angle.

  5. Problem 5 An acute angle with an exterior foot

    In triangle ABCABCABC, ABABAB is horizontal and points right from AAA. Its length is 343434 units. The side ACACAC has length 414141 units and points above the base line. Angle AAA is acute and has cosine 4041\tfrac{40}{41}4140​.

    Locate the foot DDD of the perpendicular from CCC to line ABABAB relative to segment ABABAB. Find the exact area.

    1. Hint 1

      Use cosine to find the horizontal coordinate of the foot measured from A.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the right triangle with hypotenuse AC to find the height. Then use base AB.

    Answer

    The foot is 666 units beyond BBB. The area is 153153153 square units.

    Full solution

    The horizontal projection of ACACAC gives the distance from AAA to DDD.

    AD=41(4041),AD=40\begin{aligned}AD&=41\left(\frac{40}{41}\right),\\ AD&=40\end{aligned}ADAD​=41(4140​),=40​

    Point BBB is 343434 units right of AAA.

    Therefore DDD lies 666 units beyond BBB.

    Use the right triangle ACDACDACD to find the positive height hhh.

    h2=412−402,h=9\begin{aligned}h^2&=41^2-40^2,\\ h&=9\end{aligned}h2h​=412−402,=9​

    The base of triangle ABCABCABC still measures 343434.

    K=12(34)(9),K=153\begin{aligned}K&=\tfrac12(34)(9),\\ K&=153\end{aligned}KK​=21​(34)(9),=153​

    Answer

    The foot is 666 units beyond BBB. The area is 153153153 square units.

    Key idea

    An acute angle at the left endpoint places the foot to its right, possibly past the other endpoint.

  6. Problem 6 Survey a garden in two pieces

    A surveyor stands at PPP, between endpoints AAA and BBB of the straight boundary of a triangular garden ABCABCABC. Distances PAPAPA, PBPBPB, and PCPCPC are 888 m, 171717 m, and 121212 m, respectively. Angle BPCBPCBPC measures 41∘41^\circ41∘.

    Write expressions for the areas of APCAPCAPC and BPCBPCBPC using the sine of the same angle. Find the total area to the nearest tenth of a square meter.

    Text description of this figure

    From P, segment PA runs 8 meters left, PB runs 17 meters right, and PC runs 12 meters up and right at 41 degrees from PB.

    1. Hint 1

      Rays PAPAPA and PBPBPB point in opposite directions. Relate the two angles at PPP to a straight angle.

    2. Hint 2

      The two pieces use different bases but the same 12-meter side. Add their areas before rounding.

    Answer

    KAPC=48sin⁡41∘ m2KBPC=102sin⁡41∘ m2KABC≈98.4 m2\begin{gathered}K_{APC}=48\sin41^\circ\text{ m}^2\\ K_{BPC}=102\sin41^\circ\text{ m}^2\\ K_{ABC}\approx98.4\text{ m}^2\end{gathered}KAPC​=48sin41∘ m2KBPC​=102sin41∘ m2KABC​≈98.4 m2​

    Full solution

    Angles APCAPCAPC and BPCBPCBPC are supplementary.

    Use sin⁡41∘\sin41^\circsin41∘ for both.

    The first piece has sides of lengths 8 and 12 at its included angle.

    KAPC=48sin⁡41∘K_{APC}=48\sin41^\circKAPC​=48sin41∘

    The second piece has sides of lengths 17 and 12 at its included angle.

    KBPC=102sin⁡41∘K_{BPC}=102\sin41^\circKBPC​=102sin41∘

    Add the two areas and round the total.

    KABC=150sin⁡41∘,KABC≈98.4 m2\begin{aligned}K_{ABC}&=150\sin41^\circ,\\ K_{ABC}&\approx98.4\text{ m}^2\end{aligned}KABC​KABC​​=150sin41∘,≈98.4 m2​

    Answer

    KAPC=48sin⁡41∘ m2KBPC=102sin⁡41∘ m2KABC≈98.4 m2\begin{gathered}K_{APC}=48\sin41^\circ\text{ m}^2\\ K_{BPC}=102\sin41^\circ\text{ m}^2\\ K_{ABC}\approx98.4\text{ m}^2\end{gathered}KAPC​=48sin41∘ m2KBPC​=102sin41∘ m2KABC​≈98.4 m2​

    Key idea

    A straight baseline can give supplementary included angles in two adjoining triangles.

  7. Problem 7 Choose the shortest support

    A 323232 cm rod is fixed horizontally. A second rod hinges at its left end and can turn through any angle strictly between 0∘0^\circ0∘ and 180∘180^\circ180∘. The two free ends and the hinge must enclose a triangle of area 250 cm2250\text{ cm}^2250 cm2.

    Find the shortest possible length of the second rod. State the hinge angle that achieves it and justify why a shorter rod cannot work.

    1. Hint 1

      First determine the perpendicular height required by the fixed base and target area.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare that height with the largest possible height for a rod of a given length.

    Answer

    The minimum length is 1258\tfrac{125}{8}8125​ cm, achieved at 90∘90^\circ90∘.

    Full solution

    Solve the base-height formula for the required height in centimeters.

    250=12(32)h,h=1258\begin{aligned}250&=\tfrac12(32)h,\\ h&=\tfrac{125}{8}\end{aligned}250h​=21​(32)h,=8125​​

    The tip can rise at most the full length of the second rod.

    Every shorter rod gives a height below the required value.

    A rod of length 1258\tfrac{125}{8}8125​ cm standing vertically has exactly the required height.

    It meets the fixed rod at 90∘90^\circ90∘.

    Answer

    The minimum length is 1258\tfrac{125}{8}8125​ cm, achieved at 90∘90^\circ90∘.

    Key idea

    The shortest hinged side for a prescribed height reaches straight up.

  8. Problem 8 Recover two possible projections

    Side ABABAB is horizontal to the right from AAA and measures 424242 units. Vertex CCC lies above that line at a distance of 373737 units from AAA. Triangle ABCABCABC has area 735735735 square units.

    Find every possible signed horizontal projection of ACACAC from AAA. Explain why each is possible.

    1. Hint 1

      The given base and area determine the vertical coordinate of C.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the Pythagorean theorem with the fixed length AC. Consider both sides of the vertical line through A.

    Answer

    The projections are +12+12+12 units and −12-12−12 units.

    Full solution

    The area gives the height of CCC above the base line.

    735=12(42)h,h=35\begin{aligned}735&=\tfrac12(42)h,\\ h&=35\end{aligned}735h​=21​(42)h,=35​

    Let xxx be the signed horizontal projection.

    A right triangle with vertical leg 353535 and hypotenuse 373737 gives

    x2=372−352,x2=144,x=±12\begin{aligned}x^2&=37^2-35^2,\\ x^2&=144,\\ x&=\pm12\end{aligned}x2x2x​=372−352,=144,=±12​

    The two positions are reflections across the vertical line through AAA.

    Both are 373737 units from AAA and 353535 units above the base line.

    Both therefore give the specified area.

    Answer

    The projections are +12+12+12 units and −12-12−12 units.

    Key idea

    A height can determine the magnitude of a projection while leaving its sign open.

  9. Problem 9 Change two sides while preserving area

    A triangle has sides of lengths sss and ttt meeting at an angle θ\thetaθ strictly between 0∘0^\circ0∘ and 180∘180^\circ180∘. A redesign replaces the sss-side with one of length 54s\tfrac54s45​s and opens the hinge to the supplement of θ\thetaθ.

    By what factor must the ttt-side change to preserve the area? Justify your result for every allowed θ\thetaθ.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare the original and new sines using the supplementary-angle relationship.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the new side length as a factor times the old one. Match the two products.

    Answer

    Multiply the ttt-side length by 45\tfrac4554​.

    Full solution

    The two included angles have equal positive sines.

    The areas can therefore be equal exactly when the products of the given sides agree.

    Let fff be the factor multiplying ttt.

    Write the product of the two new lengths and set it equal to the old product.

    (54s)(ft)=st\left(\tfrac54s\right)(ft)=st(45​s)(ft)=st

    The original side lengths are positive, so divide by ststst.

    54f=1,f=45\begin{aligned}\tfrac54 f&=1,\\ f&=\tfrac45\end{aligned}45​ff​=1,=54​​

    This factor restores the original product for every allowed θ\thetaθ, so it also restores the original area.

    Answer

    Multiply the ttt-side length by 45\tfrac4554​.

    Key idea

    For supplementary included angles, equal areas require equal products of the adjacent side lengths.

  10. Problem 10 Compare the sides opposite two hinge settings

    A hinge at AAA keeps ABABAB horizontal to the right with length 333333 units. A 292929-unit arm reaches C1C_1C1​ when its angle is acute. The unit point on that ray is (2029,2129)\left(\tfrac{20}{29},\tfrac{21}{29}\right)(2920​,2921​). The arm then opens to the supplementary angle, reaching C2C_2C2​ above the base line.

    Give the two signed horizontal projections of the arm. Compare the areas of triangles ABC1ABC_1ABC1​ and ABC2ABC_2ABC2​, and decide which third side, BC1BC_1BC1​ or BC2BC_2BC2​, is longer. Explain the comparison using projections and heights.

    1. Hint 1

      Reflect the acute unit point in the vertical axis, then scale both points to the arm length.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare the horizontal distances from B to the two feet. Both right triangles for the third sides have the same height.

    Answer

    The projections are +20+20+20 and −20-20−20 units. The areas are equal, and BC2BC_2BC2​ is longer.

    Full solution

    Reflection gives the second unit point (−2029,2129)\left(-\tfrac{20}{29},\tfrac{21}{29}\right)(−2920​,2921​).

    Multiplying each unit point by the arm length gives C1=(20,21)C_1=(20,21)C1​=(20,21).

    C2=(−20,21)C_2=(-20,21)C2​=(−20,21)

    The signed projections are +20+20+20 and −20-20−20 units.

    Both triangles have the same base and the same 212121-unit height, so their areas agree.

    Point BBB is 333333 units to the right of AAA.

    The first foot lies between AAA and BBB, so its horizontal distance from BBB is 131313 units.

    The second foot lies left of AAA, so its horizontal distance from BBB is 535353 units.

    Each third side is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with a 212121-unit vertical leg.

    The second right triangle has the longer horizontal leg, so the Pythagorean theorem gives BC2>BC1BC_2>BC_1BC2​>BC1​.

    Answer

    The projections are +20+20+20 and −20-20−20 units. The areas are equal, and BC2BC_2BC2​ is longer.

    Key idea

    Equal heights preserve area with a fixed base, while opposite projections can change the third side.
