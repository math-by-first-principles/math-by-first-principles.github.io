  1. 1

    In triangle ABC, which side is opposite angle B?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The opposite side is the side not touching the angle's vertex.

    AC does not touch B, so AC is opposite angle B.

  2. 2

    Triangle ABC has angles of 40, 65, and 75 degrees at A, B, and C, respectively. Which side is longest?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The largest given angle is at C.

    The longest side faces that angle, so it is AB.

    AB>AC.AB>AC.AB>AC. AB>BC.AB>BC.AB>BC.

    An existing triangle ABC has side lengths AB of 8, BC of 13, and CA of 10 units. Which angle is largest?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The longest side is BC, with length 13.

    Its opposite angle is A, so angle A is largest.

    m∠A>m∠B.m\angle A>m\angle B.m∠A>m∠B. m∠A>m∠C.m\angle A>m\angle C.m∠A>m∠C.

    Triangle PQR has angles P, Q, and R measuring 50, 60, and 70 degrees, respectively. Which side is shortest?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The smallest angle is P, measuring 50 degrees.

    The side opposite P is QR, so QR is shortest.

    QR<PR.QR<PR.QR<PR. QR<PQ.QR<PQ.QR<PQ.

    In an existing triangle KLM, KL and LM each measure 9 units and KM measures 14 units. Which angles are equal?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The equal sides KL and LM meet at L.

    Their opposite angles are M and K. The isosceles base-angle theorem makes those angles equal.

    m∠K=m∠M.m\angle K=m\angle M.m∠K=m∠M.

    In triangle ABC, BC is extended through C to D. Angle ACD is 120 degrees and angle A is 42 degrees. Find angle B.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The exterior angle equals the sum of the remote angles A and B.

    m∠B=120∘−42∘.m\angle B=120^\circ-42^\circ.m∠B=120∘−42∘. m∠B=78∘.m\angle B=78^\circ.m∠B=78∘.
  7. 7

    Triangle LMN is right at M. Which side is the hypotenuse?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The hypotenuse lies opposite the right angle.

    M is the right-angle vertex, so LN is the hypotenuse.

  8. 8

    P is outside a line. H is its perpendicular foot, and Q is another point on the line. Which segment is shorter?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Triangle PHQ is right at H.

    PQ is its hypotenuse, so

    PH<PQ.PH<PQ.PH<PQ.
  9. 9

    P is outside a line. The perpendicular segment PH has length 5 units, and Q is another point on the line. Which claimed length of PQ is impossible?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    PQ is the hypotenuse of right triangle PHQ.

    PQ>5.PQ>5.PQ>5.

    A length of 4 units is too short.

  10. 10

    Triangle ABC and triangle XYZ are unrelated. Angle A is larger than angle X. What can you conclude about the opposite lengths BC and YZ?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The side-angle ordering theorem compares parts within one triangle.

    An angle comparison between unrelated triangles does not determine their side lengths.

  11. 11

    In triangle ABC, angle A is smaller than angle B. Which comparison must hold?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    BC is opposite angle A, and AC is opposite angle B.

    The smaller angle faces the shorter side, so

    BC<AC.BC<AC.BC<AC.
  12. 12

    An exterior angle of a triangle measures 60 degrees. What is the measure of its adjacent interior angle?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The adjacent interior angle and the exterior angle form a straight angle.

    180∘−60∘=120∘.180^\circ-60^\circ=120^\circ.180∘−60∘=120∘.

    Here the exterior angle is smaller than the adjacent interior angle. The exterior-angle inequality applies to remote interior angles.
