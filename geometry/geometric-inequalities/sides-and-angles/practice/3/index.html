  1. 1

    In triangle ABC, AC is longer than AB. To begin the cutoff proof, D is chosen on ray AC so AD equals AB. Why is D strictly between A and C?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The chosen distance AD equals the positive length AB.

    Since AB is less than AC, moving that distance from A along ray AC reaches D before C. No midpoint or angle bisector is assumed.

    0<AD.0<AD.0<AD. AD<AC.AD<AC.AD<AC.
  2. 2

    In the cutoff construction, D lies between A and C and AD equals AB. Which triangle has angle BDA as an exterior angle?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    For triangle BDC, side DC extends through D along ray DA.

    Angle BDA is therefore exterior to triangle BDC, with remote angles DBC and BCD.

  3. 3

    In triangle ABC, angle B is larger than angle C. An indirect proof considers the possibility AC<ABAC<ABAC<AB. What contradiction follows?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    If AB were the longer side, the longer-side result would make its opposite angle C larger than angle B.

    That is the reverse of the given strict comparison.

    m∠C>m∠B.m\angle C>m\angle B.m∠C>m∠B.
  4. 4

    Triangle ABC has angle measures

    m∠A=(2x+10)∘.m\angle A=(2x+10)^\circ.m∠A=(2x+10)∘. m∠B=(x+20)∘.m\angle B=(x+20)^\circ.m∠B=(x+20)∘. m∠C=(150−3x)∘.m\angle C=(150-3x)^\circ.m∠C=(150−3x)∘.

    For which real x do these form a triangle with BC strictly longest?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    BC is opposite A, so A must exceed B and C.

    Comparing A with B gives

    x>10.x>10.x>10.

    Comparing A with C gives

    5x>140.5x>140.5x>140.

    Thus x must exceed 28. All angles are positive exactly when the third angle also remains positive, requiring x less than 50 in this range. Their sum is already 180 degrees.

    28<x<50.28<x<50.28<x<50.
  5. 5

    An isosceles triangle ABC has AB equal to AC and vertex angle

    m∠A=(2x+20)∘.m\angle A=(2x+20)^\circ.m∠A=(2x+20)∘.

    Which value of x gives a valid triangle with base BC strictly longer than either equal side?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The equal base angles each measure

    m∠B=(80−x)∘.m\angle B=(80-x)^\circ.m∠B=(80−x)∘.

    BC is longer than AB when A exceeds C, which has the same measure as B.

    2x+20>80−x.2x+20>80-x.2x+20>80−x. x>20.x>20.x>20.

    Positive base angles also require x less than 80. Of the choices, only 25 satisfies both conditions.

  6. 6

    In triangle ABC, angle A is larger than angle B, and angles B and C are equal. Which pair of side comparisons follows?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The angles opposite BC, AC, and AB are A, B, and C.

    A is larger than B, so

    BC>AC.BC>AC.BC>AC.

    B and C are equal, so

    AC=AB.AC=AB.AC=AB.
  7. 7

    H, Q, and R lie in that order on a line. P is outside the line and PH is perpendicular to it. Which strict comparison follows?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Angle PQR is exterior to right triangle PHQ, because QR extends QH through Q.

    It exceeds the right angle at H, so angle PQR is obtuse. It is therefore the largest angle of triangle PQR.

    Its opposite side PR is longer than PQ:

    PR>PQ.PR>PQ.PR>PQ.

    PH=5.PH=5.PH=5. PQ=4x−1.PQ=4x-1.PQ=4x−1. PR=2x+7.PR=2x+7.PR=2x+7.

    Solution

  9. 9

    In an existing triangle ABC, AB is 5 units, AC is 8 units, and angle B is 80 degrees. Which statement about angle C must be true?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    AC is longer than AB.

    Their opposite angles are B and C, so angle B exceeds angle C. The exact measure of C has not been found.

    m∠C<80∘.m\angle C<80^\circ.m∠C<80∘.
  10. 10

    For the converse theorem, suppose angle B is larger than angle C in triangle ABC. Which two alternatives must be ruled out before concluding AC>ABAC>ABAC>AB?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Two real lengths are equal, one is smaller, or one is larger.

    To leave AC greater than AB as the only possibility, rule out equality and AC less than AB. Isosceles base angles and the longer-side theorem provide the contradictions.

  11. 11

    A triangle has an exterior angle of 70 degrees and a remote interior angle of 25 degrees. How does that exterior angle compare with its adjacent interior angle?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The adjacent interior angle forms a straight angle with the exterior one.

    180∘−70∘=110∘.180^\circ-70^\circ=110^\circ.180∘−70∘=110∘.

    The difference is

    110∘−70∘=40∘.110^\circ-70^\circ=40^\circ.110∘−70∘=40∘.

    The exterior angle is smaller. Its 25-degree remote angle does not change that adjacent-angle calculation.

  12. 12

    Triangle ABC has AB>ACAB>ACAB>AC. Which additional fact would force BC to be strictly longest?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The existing comparison makes AB longer than AC.

    If angle A exceeds angle C, then their opposite sides satisfy

    BC>AB.BC>AB.BC>AB.

    Combining the comparisons puts BC ahead of both other sides.
