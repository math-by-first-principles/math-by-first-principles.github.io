    Triangle ABC has angle A of 46 degrees and angle B of 59 degrees. Which side is longest?

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    An existing triangle ABC has AB of 12 units, BC of 9 units, and CA of 15 units. Order its angles from smallest to largest.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The sides, from shortest to longest, are BC, AB, and AC.

    Their opposite angles are A, C, and B, respectively.

    m∠A<m∠C.m\angle A<m\angle C.m∠A<m∠C. m∠C<m∠B.m\angle C<m\angle B.m∠C<m∠B.

    An existing triangle ABC has angle B larger than angle C. Its lengths satisfy

    AC=3x+2.AC=3x+2.AC=3x+2. AB=x+10.AB=x+10.AB=x+10.

    Which restriction follows?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    AC faces the larger angle, so

    3x+2>x+10.3x+2>x+10.3x+2>x+10.

    Subtract x and 2 to obtain

    2x>8.2x>8.2x>8.

    Divide by 2:

    x>4.x>4.x>4.
  4. 4

    An existing triangle ABC has angle B larger than angle C. Side AC is 7 units long and AB has length 3x−23x-23x−2. Which is the sharp upper bound from the angle comparison?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    AC must exceed AB, since it faces the larger angle.

    7>3x−2.7>3x-2.7>3x−2.

    Add 2 and divide by 3:

    x<3.x<3.x<3.

    Equality is excluded because the opposite angles are unequal.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The interior angles A, B, and C are 75, 43, and 62 degrees, respectively. A is largest, so BC is longest.

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

  8. 8

    P is outside a line, H is its perpendicular foot, and Q is a different point on the line. The lengths are

    PH=10.PH=10.PH=10. PQ=2x+4.PQ=2x+4.PQ=2x+4.

    Which inequality is equivalent to PQ being longer than PH?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The slanted segment PQ is the hypotenuse.

    2x+4>10.2x+4>10.2x+4>10.

    Subtract 4, then divide by 2:

    x>3.x>3.x>3.

    Equality would make a slanted segment as short as the perpendicular.

  9. 9

    Three points A, B, and C lie on a line, and P is outside the line. Their distances from P are 12, 9, and 7 units, respectively. What does this establish?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The given distances put C ahead of A and B.

    They do not compare C with every other point on the line. A closer perpendicular foot may lie somewhere else.

  10. 10

    In triangle ABC, BC is extended through C to D. The measures are

    m∠ACD=(2x+25)∘.m\angle ACD=(2x+25)^\circ.m∠ACD=(2x+25)∘. m∠A=(x+40)∘.m\angle A=(x+40)^\circ.m∠A=(x+40)∘.

    Which is the sharp lower bound on x forced by the exterior angle inequality?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The exterior angle exceeds remote angle A.

    2x+25>x+40.2x+25>x+40.2x+25>x+40.

    Subtract x and 25:

    x>15.x>15.x>15.

    At 15, the other remote angle would have zero measure.

  11. 11

    In triangle ABC, BC is longer than AC. In triangle BCD, BC is longer than BD. Which conclusion about AC and BD follows?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Both AC and BD are shorter than BC.

    Two lengths can both be below a third without having a determined order relative to each other.

  12. 12

    Point P lies on line ℓ\ellℓ. What is its shortest distance to the line?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The point P itself is available as an endpoint on the line.

    Its distance from itself is zero. Every different point on the line has positive distance from P, so the minimum is zero.
