    BC=7x−3.BC=7x-3.BC=7x−3. EF=3x+21.EF=3x+21.EF=3x+21.

    Which necessary restriction follows?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The hinge theorem makes BC longer than EF.

    7x−3>3x+21.7x-3>3x+21.7x−3>3x+21.

  3. 3

    Triangles ABC and ADC share AC and have

    AB=AD.AB=AD.AB=AD.

    Angle BAC measures 46 degrees; angle DAC measures 71 degrees. Which relation follows?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The marked arms match, and AC supplies the common second arm.

    Triangle ADC has the larger included angle, so its third side is longer.

    DC>BC.DC>BC.DC>BC.

    Two triangles have matching arms of lengths 6 and 10 units. The second third side is 11 units. The first has the larger included angle and third side x. Which is the full possible range of x?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The hinge theorem gives the lower comparison.

    x>11.x>11.x>11.

    The triangle inequality bounds the first third side below the sum of its arms.

    x<16.x<16.x<16.

    The difference bound is 4 and is already satisfied. Every length in the remaining interval forms a triangle and has the larger opening by the converse.

    11<x<16.11<x<16.11<x<16.

    BX<5+3.BX<5+3.BX<5+3.

    Answer choices for question 11
