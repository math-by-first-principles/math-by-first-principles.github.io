    Two triangles have the proposed first pair

    AB=2p+7.AB=2p+7.AB=2p+7. DE=p+15.DE=p+15.DE=p+15.

    Their second pair AC and DF is equal. The included angles at A and D measure 50 and 90 degrees. Which value and conclusion hold when the first pair matches?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Equality of the first arms requires

    2p+7=p+15.2p+7=p+15.2p+7=p+15.

    Subtract p and subtract 7.

    p=8.p=8.p=8.

    Now the equal pairs surround a smaller angle at A, so

    BC<EF.BC<EF.BC<EF.
  2. 2

    Two triangles have matching arms of lengths 7 and 12 units. Their third sides are x+4x+4x+4 and 2x−12x-12x−1 units. The first included angle is smaller. Which is the full possible range of x?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The hinge comparison requires

    x+4<2x−1.x+4<2x-1.x+4<2x−1.

    This gives x greater than 5. Each third side must also be between the difference 5 and the sum 19.

    For the first third side,

    1<x<15.1<x<15.1<x<15.

    For the second third side,

    3<x<10.3<x<10.3<x<10.

    Combining all restrictions gives

    5<x<10.5<x<10.5<x<10.

    Every x in this interval yields two valid triangles; their third-side order gives the required angle order by the converse.

  7. 7

    The two triangles have

    AB=DE.AB=DE.AB=DE.

    Also,

    AC=DF.AC=DF.AC=DF.

    Which statement is false?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The hinge theorem and its converse compare the included angles, not every angle.

    If every corresponding angle in one triangle were larger, its angle sum would exceed the other triangle’s 180-degree sum. Both sums must be 180 degrees.

  8. 8

    Three triangles have matching arms of lengths 8 and 11 units. Their included angles are 30, 60, and 120 degrees, with opposite third sides a, b, and c. Which pair of restrictions is guaranteed?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The 120-degree opening exceeds the 60-degree opening, so the hinge theorem gives

    c>b.c>b.c>b.

    The triangle inequality also keeps the third side strictly below the sum of the arms.

    c<8+11.c<8+11.c<8+11.

    Thus c is less than 19.

  10. 10

    The two triangles have

    AB=DE.AB=DE.AB=DE.

    Also,

    AC=DF.AC=DF.AC=DF.

    Which comparison of the sums of the other two angles is equivalent to BC being longer than EF?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The converse hinge theorem makes the included angle at A larger than the one at D.

    m∠A>m∠D.m\angle A>m\angle D.m∠A>m∠D.

    Subtracting each included angle from 180 degrees leaves the corresponding sum of the other two angles. A larger subtracted angle leaves a smaller sum.

    Conversely, suppose the sum at B and C is smaller than the sum at E and F. Subtract each sum from 180 degrees. The smaller sum leaves the larger included angle at A. With the two matched side pairs, the forward hinge theorem then gives

    BC>EF.BC>EF.BC>EF.

    This proves both directions of the equivalence.

  11. 11

    Two triangles have matching arms of lengths 9 and 14 units. The second third side is 16 units. The first has a larger included angle and third side 2n+12n+12n+1 units, where n is a positive integer. How many values of n are possible?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The hinge theorem gives

    2n+1>16.2n+1>16.2n+1>16.

    The triangle inequality gives the upper bound

    2n+1<23.2n+1<23.2n+1<23.

    The difference bound is already satisfied. Solve the two inequalities.

    152<n<11.\frac{15}{2}<n<11.215​<n<11.

    The possible integers are 8, 9, and 10, so there are 3.

    The corresponding third sides are 17, 19, and 21. Each is strictly between the arm difference 5 and arm sum 23, so each forms a triangle with arms 9 and 14. Each also exceeds 16, so the converse hinge theorem gives the required larger included angle. All three values occur.
