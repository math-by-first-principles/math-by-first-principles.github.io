Question 1 of 12
  1. 1

    Proposed side lengths are v minus 2, v plus 3, and 17 units. Which real values of v make a nondegenerate triangle?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Comparing the first two sides with 17 gives

    (v−2)+(v+3)>17.(v-2)+(v+3)>17.(v−2)+(v+3)>17.

    Hence

    v>8.v>8.v>8.

    The other pair comparisons simplify to 15 greater than 3 and 20 greater than negative 2, which hold. All lengths are positive for v greater than 8, so there is no extra upper bound.

  2. 2

    A nondegenerate triangle has side expressions u plus 1, 2u plus 3, and 16 units. What is the complete interval for u?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The pair of variable sides must exceed 16:

    3u+4>16.3u+4>16.3u+4>16.

    This gives u greater than 4. The pair involving u plus 1 and 16 gives

    u+17>2u+3.u+17>2u+3.u+17>2u+3.

    This gives u less than 14. The third comparison is weaker, and positivity holds throughout:

    4<u<14.4<u<14.4<u<14.

    The endpoints make flat side triples.

  3. 3

    An isosceles triangle is proposed with equal sides r and r, and base 3r minus 8 units. What is the complete valid range of real r?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The base must be positive:

    3r−8>0.3r-8>0.3r−8>0.

    The equal sides together must exceed the base:

    2r>3r−8.2r>3r-8.2r>3r−8.

    These give

    83<r<8.\frac83<r<8.38​<r<8.

    The comparisons of an equal side with the other side plus the base hold precisely because the base is positive.

  4. 4

    A length x must complete both triangles: one with fixed sides 6 and 13, and one with fixed sides 11 and 15. If x is a positive multiple of 4, how many values are possible?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The first triangle requires

    7<x<19.7<x<19.7<x<19.

    The second requires

    4<x<26.4<x<26.4<x<26.

    The common interval is the first one. Its positive multiples of 4 are 8, 12, and 16, so there are 3.

  5. 5

    A triangle has perimeter 40 units and side lengths x, 13, and 27 minus x units. If x is an integer, how many values are possible?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The comparison with the x side gives

    13+(27−x)>x.13+(27-x)>x.13+(27−x)>x.

    Thus x is less than 20. The comparison with the 27 minus x side gives

    x+13>27−x.x+13>27-x.x+13>27−x.

    Thus x is greater than 7. The third comparison is already true. The integers are 8 through 19, giving

    19−8+1=12.19-8+1=12.19−8+1=12.

    These values also make all lengths positive.

  6. 6

    For triangle PQR, extend QP beyond P to S and copy PR onto PS. The extension proof compares angles in triangle QRS. Which original side-sum inequality does the resulting QS greater than QR prove?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Because Q, P, and S are in order,

    QS=QP+PS.QS=QP+PS.QS=QP+PS.

    The copied length replaces PS by PR. Thus the proved comparison with QR is

    PQ+PR>QR.PQ+PR>QR.PQ+PR>QR.

    The other inequalities can be proved by relabeling, but they are not this construction's conclusion.

  7. 7

    Could a nondegenerate quadrilateral have consecutive side lengths 3, 4, 5, and 13 units?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The three shorter sides total

    3+4+5=12.3+4+5=12.3+4+5=12.

    A polygon side must be strictly shorter than the route around its other sides. A 13-unit closing side violates that requirement:

    13>12.13>12.13>12.

    The proposed quadrilateral cannot exist.

  8. 8

    A and B are 20 units apart. A route from A through P and Q to B has leg lengths 3x, x plus 2, and 6 units. A, P, and Q are noncollinear. Which restriction follows?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The first bend makes the entire broken route strictly longer than the direct segment:

    3x+(x+2)+6>20.3x+(x+2)+6>20.3x+(x+2)+6>20.

    Simplify:

    4x+8>20.4x+8>20.4x+8>20.

    Hence

    x>3.x>3.x>3.

    This is a necessary restriction from the path comparison. It does not determine the route's angles.

  9. 9

    Let p be the perimeter of three positive proposed triangle sides. Which condition on the longest side is equivalent to the three strict triangle inequalities?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Write the longest side as c. The other two sides sum to p minus c, so the needed comparison is

    p−c>c.p-c>c.p−c>c.

    Rearrange:

    c<p2.c<\frac p2.c<2p​.

    For positive lengths, checking the longest side suffices. Equality at half the perimeter is a flat boundary.

  10. 10

    A, Q, P, and B are collinear in that order. AP measures 9 units, PQ measures 4 units, and QB measures 11 units. What are the direct distance AB and the length of the route A to P to Q to B?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Since Q is between A and P,

    AQ=9−4.AQ=9-4.AQ=9−4.

    Thus AQ is 5 units. Segment addition then gives

    AB=5+11.AB=5+11.AB=5+11.

    The direct distance is 16 units. The specified route has length

    9+4+11=24.9+4+11=24.9+4+11=24.

    It travels the segment QP twice extra, adding 8 units.

  11. 11

    An isosceles triangle has perimeter 32 units and all three side lengths are positive integers. How many base lengths are possible?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Let b be the base. Each equal side has length

    32−b2.\frac{32-b}{2}.232−b​.

    Integer equal sides require b to be even. The two equal sides together must exceed the base:

    32−b>b.32-b>b.32−b>b.

    Thus

    0<b<16.0<b<16.0<b<16.

    The even integers are 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, and 14: seven possibilities. Each gives positive equal sides and satisfies the other inequalities.

  12. 12

    Four distinct points A, P, Q, and B are collinear. A route A to P to Q to B has leg lengths 4, 6, and 9 units. Which value cannot be the direct distance AB?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The route has total length

    4+6+9=19.4+6+9=19.4+6+9=19.

    The direct distance cannot exceed this, so 20 is impossible. The other choices occur on a number line.

    Distance 19: Put A, P, Q, and B at successive positions 0, 4, 10, and 19.

    Distance 11: Put them at 0, negative 4, 2, and 11.

    Distance 7: Put them at 0, 4, negative 2, and 7.

    The order of travel can include reversals, so collinearity alone does not force distance 19.
