  1. 1

    An acute angle of 47 degrees in a right triangle faces a 6.5 m leg. Find the hypotenuse to the nearest tenth of a meter.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The known length is opposite, and the unknown is the hypotenuse.

    sin⁡47∘=6.5/h,h=6.5/sin⁡47∘,h≈8.9 m.\begin{aligned}\sin47^\circ&=6.5/h,\\h&=6.5/\sin47^\circ,\\h&\approx8.9\text{ m}.\end{aligned}sin47∘hh​=6.5/h,=6.5/sin47∘,≈8.9 m.​
  2. 2

    A right triangle has an acute angle of 63 degrees and an adjacent leg of 14 cm. Find the opposite leg to the nearest tenth.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The original two leg roles give tangent.

    x=14tan⁡63∘,x≈27.5 cm.\begin{aligned}x&=14\tan63^\circ,\\x&\approx27.5\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}xx​=14tan63∘,≈27.5 cm.​

    A tangent greater than one is valid because the opposite leg is longer than the adjacent leg.

  3. 3

    A right triangle has hypotenuse 18 units and an 11-unit leg adjacent to theta. Find theta to the nearest tenth of a degree.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Use adjacent divided by hypotenuse as the inverse-cosine input.

    θ=cos⁡−1(11/18),θ≈52.3∘.\begin{aligned}\theta&=\cos^{-1}(11/18),\\\theta&\approx52.3^\circ.\end{aligned}θθ​=cos−1(11/18),≈52.3∘.​
  4. 4

    A record describes theta as an acute angle in a nondegenerate right triangle and gives sin⁡θ=1\sin\theta=1sinθ=1. What should you conclude?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A positive leg is shorter than the hypotenuse, so an acute sine ratio must satisfy

    0<sin⁡θ<1.0<\sin\theta<1.0<sinθ<1.

    The input 1 is a boundary value corresponding to 90 degrees, not an acute angle.

  5. 5

    The legs of a right triangle are 9 and 22 units. Theta is opposite the 22-unit leg. Which expression finds theta directly?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The known pair is opposite and adjacent. Their positive ratio may exceed one.

    θ=tan⁡−1(22/9).\theta=\tan^{-1}(22/9).θ=tan−1(22/9).

    Reversing the ratio finds the complementary angle; sine and cosine cannot use this ratio greater than one for an acute angle.

  6. 6

    A right triangle has an adjacent leg of 12 cm and hypotenuse of 0.2 m relative to theta. Which expression gives theta in degrees?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Convert the hypotenuse to the same unit: 0.2 m is 20 cm. The adjacent-to-hypotenuse ratio gives

    θ=cos⁡−1(12/20).\theta=\cos^{-1}(12/20).θ=cos−1(12/20).

    The input is dimensionless and strictly between zero and one.

  7. 7

    Which supplied data are impossible for a nondegenerate right triangle?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A hypotenuse must be longer than either leg. The impossible record has

    9<10.9<10.9<10.

    The other sets each describe at least one valid right triangle. The angles-only set describes many sizes rather than an impossibility.

  8. 8

    A right triangle has legs 15 and 20 units. Which pair gives the angles opposite those legs, respectively, to the nearest tenth of a degree?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    For the angle opposite 15, use opposite divided by adjacent.

    α=tan⁡−1(15/20).\alpha=\tan^{-1}(15/20).α=tan−1(15/20).

    This rounds to 36.9 degrees. Subtract the stored inverse result from 90 degrees for the other angle, which rounds to 53.1 degrees.

    A solver has sin⁡35∘=12/h\sin35^\circ=12/hsin35∘=12/h. Which expression correctly isolates h?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Multiply by h before dividing by the positive sine value.

    hsin⁡35∘=12,h=12/sin⁡35∘.\begin{aligned}h\sin35^\circ&=12,\\h&=12/\sin35^\circ.\end{aligned}hsin35∘h​=12,=12/sin35∘.​
  10. 10

    Which workflow follows the original data most accurately when an inverse angle is used in a later side calculation?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The inverse expression defines an exact acute angle for the original ratio. For example, if the opposite and adjacent legs are u and v, retain

    θ=tan⁡−1(u/v).\theta=\tan^{-1}(u/v).θ=tan−1(u/v).

    Then use the stored value in later calculations. Rounding earlier changes the input to that next calculation.

  11. 11

    The acute angles theta and phi of a right triangle are complementary. If cos⁡θ=0.25\cos\theta=0.25cosθ=0.25, find phi to the nearest tenth of a degree.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The sine of the complementary angle equals the original cosine.

    sin⁡ϕ=0.25.\sin\phi=0.25.sinϕ=0.25.

    Use inverse sine in degree mode.

    ϕ=sin⁡−1(0.25),ϕ≈14.5∘.\begin{aligned}\phi&=\sin^{-1}(0.25),\\\phi&\approx14.5^\circ.\end{aligned}ϕϕ​=sin−1(0.25),≈14.5∘.​
  12. 12

    For an acute-angle calculation, what does tan⁡−1(2.4)\tan^{-1}(2.4)tan−1(2.4) produce in degree mode, to the nearest tenth?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A tangent ratio compares two positive legs, so an input greater than one is allowed.

    θ=tan⁡−1(2.4).\theta=\tan^{-1}(2.4).θ=tan−1(2.4).

    Degree-mode evaluation gives approximately 67.4 degrees, an acute angle.
