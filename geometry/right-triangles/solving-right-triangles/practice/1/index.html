  1. 1

    A right triangle has a selected acute angle theta. The known side is its adjacent leg A, and the unknown is its hypotenuse H. Which equation uses those two sides directly?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Adjacent leg divided by hypotenuse is cosine.

    cos⁡θ=AH.\cos\theta=\frac AH.cosθ=HA​.

    The angle must be selected before assigning side roles.

  2. 2

    A right triangle has hypotenuse 13 cm and a 24-degree acute angle. Find the leg opposite that angle to the nearest tenth of a centimeter.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The opposite-to-hypotenuse ratio is sine.

    x=13sin⁡24∘,x≈5.3 cm.\begin{aligned}x&=13\sin24^\circ,\\x&\approx5.3\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}xx​=13sin24∘,≈5.3 cm.​

    The result is shorter than the hypotenuse.

  3. 3

    A right triangle has a 52-degree acute angle and an adjacent leg of 9 m. Find its hypotenuse to the nearest tenth of a meter.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Cosine places the unknown hypotenuse below the known adjacent leg.

    cos⁡52∘=9/h,h=9/cos⁡52∘,h≈14.6 m.\begin{aligned}\cos52^\circ&=9/h,\\h&=9/\cos52^\circ,\\h&\approx14.6\text{ m}.\end{aligned}cos52∘hh​=9/h,=9/cos52∘,≈14.6 m.​
  4. 4

    A right triangle has a 31-degree acute angle and an adjacent leg of 12 units. Find the opposite leg to the nearest tenth.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The two relevant sides are opposite and adjacent, so use tangent.

    tan⁡31∘=x/12,x=12tan⁡31∘,x≈7.2.\begin{aligned}\tan31^\circ&=x/12,\\x&=12\tan31^\circ,\\x&\approx7.2.\end{aligned}tan31∘xx​=x/12,=12tan31∘,≈7.2.​
  5. 5

    For an acute angle theta, what does θ=sin⁡−1(0.8)\theta=\sin^{-1}(0.8)θ=sin−1(0.8) mean?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Inverse sine takes the side ratio as input and returns the acute angle.

    sin⁡θ=0.8.\sin\theta=0.8.sinθ=0.8.

    In degree mode the angle is approximately 53.1 degrees; taking a reciprocal would not undo sine.

  6. 6

    A right triangle has hypotenuse 19 units and a 7-unit leg opposite theta. Find theta to the nearest tenth of a degree.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The given pair is opposite and hypotenuse.

    sin⁡θ=7/19,θ=sin⁡−1(7/19),θ≈21.6∘.\begin{aligned}\sin\theta&=7/19,\\\theta&=\sin^{-1}(7/19),\\\theta&\approx21.6^\circ.\end{aligned}sinθθθ​=7/19,=sin−1(7/19),≈21.6∘.​
  7. 7

    One acute angle in a right triangle is 28.6 degrees. Find the other acute angle.

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The two acute angles add to a right angle.

    90∘−28.6∘=61.4∘.90^\circ-28.6^\circ=61.4^\circ.90∘−28.6∘=61.4∘.
  8. 8

    Which exact calculator check is appropriate for degree mode?

  9. 9

    A right triangle has legs 10 and 17 cm. Theta is opposite the 10 cm leg. Find theta to the nearest tenth of a degree.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Use the original opposite and adjacent legs.

    tan⁡θ=10/17,θ=tan⁡−1(10/17),θ≈30.5∘.\begin{aligned}\tan\theta&=10/17,\\\theta&=\tan^{-1}(10/17),\\\theta&\approx30.5^\circ.\end{aligned}tanθθθ​=10/17,=tan−1(10/17),≈30.5∘.​
  10. 10

    Which input can inverse sine use to recover an acute angle in a nondegenerate right triangle?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    An acute angle has a positive opposite leg shorter than the hypotenuse. Thus

    0<sin⁡θ<1.0<\sin\theta<1.0<sinθ<1.

    Only 0.42 lies in this strict range. The boundary value 1 would give a right angle, not an acute one.

  11. 11

    A right triangle has hypotenuse 16 cm and a leg of 9 cm. Find the other leg to the nearest tenth of a centimeter.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The unknown is a leg, so subtract squares.

    x2=162−92,x=175,x≈13.2 cm.\begin{aligned}x^2&=16^2-9^2,\\x&=\sqrt{175},\\x&\approx13.2\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}x2xx​=162−92,=175​,≈13.2 cm.​
  12. 12

    A right triangle has one acute angle of 42 degrees. Which additional datum fixes its size?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    An angle fixes shape, while a side length fixes scale. For hypotenuse 11, the opposite leg is fixed by

    O=11sin⁡42∘.O=11\sin42^\circ.O=11sin42∘.

    The other options restate shape information that is already determined.
