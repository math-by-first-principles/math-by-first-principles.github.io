  1. 1

    A right triangle has opposite leg 5.5 cm and adjacent leg 12.3 cm relative to theta. Which expression correctly finds its hypotenuse without an intermediate rounded angle?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The direct inverse angle uses opposite over adjacent.

    θ=tan⁡−1(5.5/12.3).\theta=\tan^{-1}(5.5/12.3).θ=tan−1(5.5/12.3).

    The hypotenuse follows from adjacent over cosine.

    h=12.3/cos⁡θ.h=12.3/\cos\theta.h=12.3/cosθ.

    Nesting these expressions preserves the original data. Pythagoras checks the result at about 13.5 cm.

  2. 2

    Triangle ABC is right at C, with BC of 8 units and CA of 15 units. Which expression finds angle B directly from the two legs?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Relative to B, CA is opposite and BC is adjacent. Thus

    m∠B=tan⁡−1(15/8).m\angle B=\tan^{-1}(15/8).m∠B=tan−1(15/8).

    The hypotenuse is not needed. The positive input greater than one is valid for inverse tangent.

  3. 3

    A record calls a 6-unit side the hypotenuse and an 8-unit side the leg opposite an acute angle. What is the appropriate conclusion?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The given side roles already conflict with the hypotenuse being longest.

    8>6.8>6.8>6.

    The proposed inverse-sine input would also exceed one. Reversing the ratio or changing functions changes the stated roles rather than validating them.

  4. 4

    A right triangle has an acute angle of 42 degrees and perimeter 50 cm. Which expression gives its hypotenuse?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    If the hypotenuse is h, the two legs are h times sine and h times cosine. Add all three sides.

    50=hsin⁡42∘+hcos⁡42∘+h.50=h\sin42^\circ+h\cos42^\circ+h.50=hsin42∘+hcos42∘+h.

    Factor the common h and divide.

    h=501+sin⁡42∘+cos⁡42∘.h=\frac{50}{1+\sin42^\circ+\cos42^\circ}.h=1+sin42∘+cos42∘50​.
  5. 5

    Which pair of ratios can describe the same acute angle in a nondegenerate right triangle?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A triangle with positive legs 3 and 4 has hypotenuse 5. For the angle opposite 3, its sine is 3/5 and its cosine is 4/5. Their identity check is

    (3/5)2+(4/5)2=1.(3/5)^2+(4/5)^2=1.(3/5)2+(4/5)2=1.

    The equal 3/5 pair fails this identity. The other two choices contain sine or cosine values greater than one.

  6. 6

    An acute angle in a right triangle has cos⁡θ=0.08\cos\theta=0.08cosθ=0.08. Its adjacent leg is 2.5 m. Which expression finds the hypotenuse directly, without first finding theta?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Use the given cosine ratio as it stands.

    0.08=2.5/h,h=2.5/0.08.\begin{aligned}0.08&=2.5/h,\\h&=2.5/0.08.\end{aligned}0.08h​=2.5/h,=2.5/0.08.​

    The hypotenuse is 31.25 m. An inverse operation is unnecessary when the needed ratio is already known.

  7. 7

    A right triangle has opposite leg 16 units and adjacent leg 7 units relative to theta. Which direct inverse expression has valid side roles and input?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Opposite divided by adjacent gives tangent.

    θ=tan⁡−1(16/7).\theta=\tan^{-1}(16/7).θ=tan−1(16/7).

    Its input is positive, so it determines an acute angle even though the ratio exceeds one. Sine and cosine cannot use that input; reversing the tangent ratio gives the other acute angle.

  8. 8

    Exact side data give sin⁡θ=0.9995\sin\theta=0.9995sinθ=0.9995 for an acute angle. Which procedure preserves the original valid angle calculation?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The original ratio lies strictly between zero and one, so it gives an acute angle.

    θ=sin⁡−1(0.9995).\theta=\sin^{-1}(0.9995).θ=sin−1(0.9995).

    This is approximately 88.1881 degrees. Rounding the input to one first produces the nonacute boundary angle of 90 degrees instead.

  9. 9

    A nondegenerate right triangle is said to have an acute angle theta with cos⁡θ=−0.2\cos\theta=-0.2cosθ=−0.2. What is the correct response?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Both the adjacent leg and the hypotenuse are positive, with the leg shorter. Thus

    0<cos⁡θ<1.0<\cos\theta<1.0<cosθ<1.

    The stated negative ratio cannot describe this acute-angle triangle. Removing a sign would change the record.

  10. 10

    Which information uniquely determines all side lengths of a nondegenerate right triangle?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The angle fixes shape, while the perimeter fixes scale. With hypotenuse h and perimeter P,

    P=h(1+sin⁡θ+cos⁡θ).P=h(1+\sin\theta+\cos\theta).P=h(1+sinθ+cosθ).

    The factor in parentheses is positive, so it gives one positive h. The other choices leave a size or shape freedom.
