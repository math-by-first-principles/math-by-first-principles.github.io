  1. Problem 1 Triangle ABC

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The hypotenuse ABABAB is 181818 cm, and angle AAA measures 41∘41^\circ41∘.

    Find both legs to the nearest tenth of a centimeter and angle BBB exactly.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle ABC is right at C. Side CB is labeled BC, side AC is labeled CA, and hypotenuse AB is labeled 18 cm. The marked angle at A is labeled 41°.

    1. Hint 1

      Identify each leg relative to the given angle at AAA.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the original hypotenuse with sine for the opposite leg and cosine for the adjacent leg.

    3. Hint 3

      The acute angles add to a right angle.

    Answer

    BC≈11.8 cm,CA≈13.6 cm,m∠B=49∘.\begin{aligned}BC&\approx11.8\text{ cm},\\CA&\approx13.6\text{ cm},\\m\angle B&=49^\circ.\end{aligned}BCCAm∠B​≈11.8 cm,≈13.6 cm,=49∘.​

    Full solution

    Relative to AAA, side BCBCBC is opposite and CACACA is the adjacent leg.

    Both use the known hypotenuse directly.

    The sine equation gives

    sin⁡41∘=BC18,BC=18sin⁡41∘,BC≈11.8 cm.\begin{aligned}\sin41^\circ&=\frac{BC}{18},\\BC&=18\sin41^\circ,\\BC&\approx11.8\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}sin41∘BCBC​=18BC​,=18sin41∘,≈11.8 cm.​

    The cosine equation gives

    cos⁡41∘=CA18,CA=18cos⁡41∘,CA≈13.6 cm.\begin{aligned}\cos41^\circ&=\frac{CA}{18},\\CA&=18\cos41^\circ,\\CA&\approx13.6\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}cos41∘CACA​=18CA​,=18cos41∘,≈13.6 cm.​

    Subtract the given acute angle from a right angle.

    m∠B=90∘−41∘m\angle B=90^\circ-41^\circm∠B=90∘−41∘

    Thus angle BBB is exactly 49∘49^\circ49∘.

    Each leg is shorter than 181818 cm.

    The leg opposite the smaller angle is shorter, as expected.

    Answer

    BC≈11.8 cm,CA≈13.6 cm,m∠B=49∘.\begin{aligned}BC&\approx11.8\text{ cm},\\CA&\approx13.6\text{ cm},\\m\angle B&=49^\circ.\end{aligned}BCCAm∠B​≈11.8 cm,≈13.6 cm,=49∘.​

    Key idea

    Calculate each requested leg from the original given side to avoid carrying a rounded intermediate length.

  2. Problem 2 A rotated configuration

    Triangle PQRPQRPQR is right at QQQ. Angle PPP measures 58∘58^\circ58∘, and the opposite leg QRQRQR is 6.86.86.8 m.

    Find PRPRPR and PQPQPQ to the nearest tenth of a meter.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle PRQ is right at Q. Side QR is labeled 6.8 m, side PQ is labeled PQ, and hypotenuse PR is labeled PR. The marked angle at P is labeled 58°.

    1. Hint 1

      The known side is opposite the selected angle; decide which ratio contains it and each unknown.

    2. Hint 2

      Sine places the hypotenuse in the denominator. Tangent places the adjacent leg in the denominator.

    3. Hint 3

      Solve each fraction equation before entering numbers into the calculator.

    Answer

    PR≈8.0 m,PQ≈4.2 m.\begin{aligned}PR&\approx8.0\text{ m},\\PQ&\approx4.2\text{ m}.\end{aligned}PRPQ​≈8.0 m,≈4.2 m.​

    Full solution

    The hypotenuse is PRPRPR, opposite the right angle at QQQ.

    Sine gives

    sin⁡58∘=6.8PR,PR=6.8sin⁡58∘,PR≈8.0 m.\begin{aligned}\sin58^\circ&=\frac{6.8}{PR},\\PR&=\frac{6.8}{\sin58^\circ},\\PR&\approx8.0\text{ m}.\end{aligned}sin58∘PRPR​=PR6.8​,=sin58∘6.8​,≈8.0 m.​

    Relative to PPP, the adjacent leg is PQPQPQ.

    Use tangent.

    tan⁡58∘=6.8PQ,PQ=6.8tan⁡58∘,PQ≈4.2 m.\begin{aligned}\tan58^\circ&=\frac{6.8}{PQ},\\PQ&=\frac{6.8}{\tan58^\circ},\\PQ&\approx4.2\text{ m}.\end{aligned}tan58∘PQPQ​=PQ6.8​,=tan58∘6.8​,≈4.2 m.​

    The opposite leg is longer than the adjacent leg, consistent with the given angle exceeding 45∘45^\circ45∘.

    Both legs are shorter than the hypotenuse.

    Answer

    PR≈8.0 m,PQ≈4.2 m.\begin{aligned}PR&\approx8.0\text{ m},\\PQ&\approx4.2\text{ m}.\end{aligned}PRPQ​≈8.0 m,≈4.2 m.​

    Key idea

    When an unknown lies below the fraction bar, multiply through by that unknown before dividing by the function value.

  3. Problem 3 Three starting points

    For each case, give a direct equation or expression that uses the original given data to find the requested quantity. Explain the side roles or theorem that justify it. Do not evaluate the expressions.

    Case A: Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC, with hypotenuse ABABAB of 272727 units and leg BCBCBC of 111111 units. Find angle AAA.

    Case B: Triangle DEFDEFDEF is right at EEE, angle DDD measures 34∘34^\circ34∘, and DEDEDE is 151515 units. Find EFEFEF.

    Case C: Triangle JKLJKLJKL is right at KKK, with legs JKJKJK of 999 units and KLKLKL of 141414 units. Find JLJLJL.

    1. Hint 1

      For each case, decide first whether the requested result is an angle or a length.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the known side pair directly for an inverse operation; use a known angle with the relevant ratio for a side.

    3. Hint 3

      Two known legs and a missing third side give a direct Pythagorean calculation.

    Answer

    Case A:

    m∠A=sin⁡−1(11/27)m\angle A=\sin^{-1}(11/27)m∠A=sin−1(11/27)

    Case B:

    EF=15tan⁡34∘EF=15\tan34^\circEF=15tan34∘

    Case C:

    JL=92+142JL=\sqrt{9^2+14^2}JL=92+142​

    Equivalent correct equations are acceptable.

    Full solution

    Case A: BCBCBC is opposite AAA and ABABAB is the hypotenuse.

    Their ratio is between zero and one.

    m∠A=sin⁡−1(11/27)m\angle A=\sin^{-1}(11/27)m∠A=sin−1(11/27)

    Case B: DEDEDE is adjacent to DDD, and EFEFEF is opposite it.

    tan⁡34∘=EF/15\tan34^\circ=EF/15tan34∘=EF/15

    Thus

    EF=15tan⁡34∘EF=15\tan34^\circEF=15tan34∘

    Case C: JLJLJL is the hypotenuse.

    Pythagoras uses the two given legs.

    JL=92+142JL=\sqrt{9^2+14^2}JL=92+142​

    Answer

  4. Problem 4 Triangle UVW

    Triangle UVWUVWUVW is right at VVV. Its hypotenuse UWUWUW is 313131 cm and leg VWVWVW is 121212 cm.

    Find the remaining side to the nearest tenth of a centimeter and both acute angles to the nearest tenth of a degree.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle UWV is right at V. Side VW is labeled 12 cm, side UV is labeled UV, and hypotenuse UW is labeled 31 cm. The marked angle at U is labeled ?.

    1. Hint 1

      The remaining side is a leg; subtract squares for its exact length.

    2. Hint 2

      The original 121212 cm leg lies opposite angle UUU. Use its ratio with the original hypotenuse to find that angle.

    3. Hint 3

      Keep the inverse result unrounded when subtracting it from 90∘90^\circ90∘.

    Answer

    UV≈28.6 cm,m∠U≈22.8∘,m∠W≈67.2∘.\begin{aligned}UV&\approx28.6\text{ cm},\\m\angle U&\approx22.8^\circ,\\m\angle W&\approx67.2^\circ.\end{aligned}UVm∠Um∠W​≈28.6 cm,≈22.8∘,≈67.2∘.​

    Full solution

    Find the other leg by Pythagoras.

    UV2=312−122,UV=817,UV≈28.6 cm.\begin{aligned}UV^2&=31^2-12^2,\\UV&=\sqrt{817},\\UV&\approx28.6\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}UV2UVUV​=312−122,=817​,≈28.6 cm.​

    The known opposite-to-hypotenuse ratio gives angle UUU directly.

    m∠U=sin⁡−1(12/31)m\angle U=\sin^{-1}(12/31)m∠U=sin−1(12/31)

    In degree mode, the angle is approximately 22.773972∘22.773972^\circ22.773972∘.

    m∠W=90∘−m∠Um\angle W=90^\circ-m\angle Um∠W=90∘−m∠U

    The requested angle values are 22.8∘22.8^\circ22.8∘ and 67.2∘67.2^\circ67.2∘.

    The longer leg UVUVUV faces the larger angle WWW, consistent with side-angle ordering.

    Answer

    UV≈28.6 cm,m∠U≈22.8∘,m∠W≈67.2∘.\begin{aligned}UV&\approx28.6\text{ cm},\\m\angle U&\approx22.8^\circ,\\m\angle W&\approx67.2^\circ.\end{aligned}UVm∠Um∠W​≈28.6 cm,≈22.8∘,≈67.2∘.​

  5. Problem 5 Two proposed routes

    A right triangle has an acute angle of 38∘38^\circ38∘ and an adjacent leg of 181818 units. Its hypotenuse is hhh.

    Plan A uses h=18/cos⁡38∘h=18/\cos38^\circh=18/cos38∘.

    Plan B first finds the opposite leg as 18tan⁡38∘18\tan38^\circ18tan38∘, then uses h=182+(18tan⁡38∘)2h=\sqrt{18^2+(18\tan38^\circ)^2}h=182+(18tan38∘)2​.

    Decide whether each plan is mathematically valid without intermediate rounding. Choose a more direct plan and explain one precision risk in the other plan. You do not need a decimal result.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle ABC is right at C. Side CB is labeled opposite, side AC is labeled 18, and hypotenuse AB is labeled h. The marked angle at A is labeled 38°.

    1. Hint 1

      Check each equation against the roles of the sides, rather than assuming one plan must be wrong.

    2. Hint 2

      One plan uses the adjacent leg and hypotenuse directly. The other builds a third side first.

    3. Hint 3

      A rounded opposite leg changes what is squared in the second plan.

    Answer

    Both plans are valid without intermediate rounding. Plan A is more direct. Rounding the opposite leg before applying Pythagoras in Plan B can alter the final result.

    Full solution

    Plan A correctly uses cosine because 181818 is the adjacent leg and hhh is the hypotenuse.

    cos⁡38∘=18/h\cos38^\circ=18/hcos38∘=18/h

    Solving gives the displayed Plan A expression.

    Plan B correctly uses tangent to find the opposite leg from the adjacent leg.

    Applying Pythagoras to those two legs then gives its displayed expression.

    Both plans describe the same triangle.

    Their exact expressions agree.

    Plan A finds the requested side in one ratio equation and does not require an extra side.

    Plan B remains valid if its full expression or unrounded stored opposite leg is retained.

    Replacing that leg by a rounded decimal before squaring introduces avoidable error.

    Answer

    Both plans are valid without intermediate rounding. Plan A is more direct. Rounding the opposite leg before applying Pythagoras in Plan B can alter the final result.

    Key idea

    A more direct method can reduce intermediate calculations without making other exact methods invalid.

  6. Problem 6 A ratio and a length

    A right triangle has hypotenuse 343434 m. Relative to an acute angle θ\thetaθ, its opposite-to-adjacent leg ratio is 7:97:97:9.

    Find both acute angles to the nearest tenth of a degree and both legs to the nearest tenth of a meter.

    1. Hint 1

      The leg ratio fixes the shape; the hypotenuse fixes its size.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the inverse operation associated with opposite divided by adjacent.

    3. Hint 3

      Keep that unrounded angle when using the given hypotenuse to recover each leg.

    Answer

    θ≈37.9∘,ϕ≈52.1∘,O≈20.9 m,A≈26.8 m.\begin{aligned}\theta&\approx37.9^\circ,\\\phi&\approx52.1^\circ,\\O&\approx20.9\text{ m},\\A&\approx26.8\text{ m}.\end{aligned}θϕOA​≈37.9∘,≈52.1∘,≈20.9 m,≈26.8 m.​

    Here ϕ\phiϕ is the other acute angle, and O,AO,AO,A are opposite and adjacent to θ\thetaθ.

    Full solution

    The opposite-to-adjacent ratio gives

    θ=tan⁡−1(7/9)\theta=\tan^{-1}(7/9)θ=tan−1(7/9)

    This positive input produces an acute angle.

    Keep its stored value.

    The complementary angle is

    ϕ=90∘−θ\phi=90^\circ-\thetaϕ=90∘−θ

    The angles round to 37.9∘37.9^\circ37.9∘ and 52.1∘52.1^\circ52.1∘.

    Use the original hypotenuse and unrounded angle to find the opposite leg.

    O=34sin⁡θO=34\sin\thetaO=34sinθ

    This rounds to 20.920.920.9 m.

    For the adjacent leg,

    A=34cos⁡θA=34\cos\thetaA=34cosθ

    This rounds to 26.826.826.8 m.

    An exact check can use a common positive part size sss.

    The legs are 7s7s7s and 9s9s9s, so

    130s2=342130s^2=34^2130s2=342

    Their positive lengths therefore have ratio 7:97:97:9 and the stated hypotenuse.

    Answer

    θ≈37.9∘,ϕ≈52.1∘,O≈20.9 m,A≈26.8 m.\begin{aligned}\theta&\approx37.9^\circ,\\\phi&\approx52.1^\circ,\\O&\approx20.9\text{ m},\\A&\approx26.8\text{ m}.\end{aligned}θϕOA​≈37.9∘,≈52.1∘,≈20.9 m,≈26.8 m.​

    Here ϕ\phiϕ is the other acute angle, and O,AO,AO,A are opposite and adjacent to θ\thetaθ.

  7. Problem 7 A calculator interpretation

    A right triangle has hypotenuse 202020 cm and a 121212 cm leg opposite acute angle θ\thetaθ.

    Noor writes sin⁡θ=12/20\sin\theta=12/20sinθ=12/20.

    She then writes θ=(12/20)−1\theta=(12/20)^{-1}θ=(12/20)−1, interpreting the inverse-sine key as a reciprocal, and reports about 1.7∘1.7^\circ1.7∘.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle ABC is right at C. Side CB is labeled 12 cm, side AC is labeled adjacent, and hypotenuse AB is labeled 20 cm. The marked angle at A is labeled θ.

      An inverse function reverses the angle-to-ratio operation; a reciprocal reverses a division.

    2. Hint 2

      Use the inverse associated with the ratio in Noor's first line, in degree mode.

    3. Hint 3

      Apply sine to the unrounded angle and compare with the original ratio.

    Answer

    The reciprocal of 12/2012/2012/20 is a number, not its inverse-sine angle. The correct angle is θ≈36.9∘\theta\approx36.9^\circθ≈36.9∘. Its unrounded value has sine 0.60.60.6, the original ratio.

    Full solution

    The first line is valid because 121212 is opposite and 202020 is the hypotenuse.

  8. Problem 8 An incomplete record

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC, and side BCBCBC is 888 units. No other side or angle measurement is given.

    Is this enough information to determine the other two sides and acute angles? Justify your decision by giving two noncongruent right triangles that satisfy the record. For each example, state the other leg and the hypotenuse exactly; angle calculations are not required.

    1. Hint 1

      The other leg has not been fixed by the record.

    2. Hint 2

      Choose two different positive values for that leg and use Pythagoras to complete each triangle.

    3. Hint 3

      Each example must retain the given 888-unit leg, while a different side length makes the two triangles noncongruent.

    Answer

    The data are insufficient. One example has other leg 666 units and hypotenuse 101010 units. A second has other leg 151515 units and hypotenuse 171717 units. Other valid pairs of distinct completions are acceptable.

    Full solution

    The right angle and one leg leave the other positive leg free.

    Choose CACACA as 666 units in the first completion.

    Pythagoras gives

    AB=82+62AB=\sqrt{8^2+6^2}AB=82+62​

    Its positive length is 101010 units.

    Choose CACACA as 151515 units in the second completion.

    Then

    AB=82+152AB=\sqrt{8^2+15^2}AB=82+152​

    The hypotenuse is 171717 units.

    Each construction has perpendicular positive legs and the required 888-unit side.

    Their different hypotenuses show that they are not congruent.

    Their opposite-to-adjacent ratios at AAA are also different, so the acute angles are not fixed either.

    Thus the record does not determine all missing quantities.

    Answer

  10. Problem 10 Two summaries

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The hypotenuse ABABAB is exactly 161616 cm, and angle AAA is exactly 27∘27^\circ27∘.

    Report I lists BCBCBC as 7.37.37.3 cm, CACACA as 14.314.314.3 cm, and angle BBB as 63∘63^\circ63∘.

    Report II lists BCBCBC as 14.314.314.3 cm, CACACA as 7.37.37.3 cm, and angle BBB as 63∘63^\circ63∘.

    Each listed leg is intended to be rounded to the nearest tenth. Choose the report consistent with the original data. Explain why a Pythagorean check alone cannot distinguish these two reports, then use the selected reference angle to justify your choice.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle ABC is right at C. Side CB is labeled BC, side AC is labeled CA, and hypotenuse AB is labeled 16 cm. The marked angle at A is labeled 27°.

    1. Hint 1

      The two reports have the same pair of leg values, assigned to different sides.

    2. Hint 2

      Relate the smaller given acute angle to the smaller opposite side.

    3. Hint 3

      Use the original hypotenuse with the sine and cosine of the original angle to check the side assignments.

    Answer

    Report I is consistent. Pythagoras is unchanged when the two leg values are exchanged. Relative to AAA, BCBCBC is the opposite leg and rounds to 7.37.37.3 cm; CACACA is adjacent and rounds to 14.314.314.3 cm.

    Full solution

    Both reports give the correct complementary angle because

    90∘−27∘=63∘90^\circ-27^\circ=63^\circ90∘−27∘=63∘

    Pythagoras adds the two leg squares, so swapping their names leaves that sum unchanged.

    Moreover, the reported sides are rounded; an exact squared equality is not expected from them.

    Angle AAA is smaller than angle BBB, so its opposite side BCBCBC must be the smaller leg.

    This already supports Report I.

    Check using the original angle and hypotenuse.

    BC=16sin⁡27∘,BC≈7.263848 cm,CA=16cos⁡27∘,CA≈14.256104 cm.\begin{aligned}BC&=16\sin27^\circ,\\BC&\approx7.263848\text{ cm},\\CA&=16\cos27^\circ,\\CA&\approx14.256104\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}BCBCCACA​=16sin27∘,≈7.263848 cm,=16cos27∘,≈14.256104 cm.​

    These values round to the side assignments in Report I.

    Report II exchanges opposite and adjacent without changing the reference angle.

    Answer

    Report I is consistent. Pythagoras is unchanged when the two leg values are exchanged. Relative to AAA, BCBCBC is the opposite leg and rounds to 7.37.37.3 cm; CACACA is adjacent and rounds to 14.314.314.3 cm.

    Key idea

    A side-square check verifies lengths together, while the reference angle checks which leg has which name.
