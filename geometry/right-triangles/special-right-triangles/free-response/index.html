  1. Problem 1 Two equal legs

    A 45-45-90 triangle has hypotenuse 19219\sqrt2192​ mm.

    Find each leg and the exact perimeter.

    Text description of this figure

    Right triangle CAB has its marked right angle at C. The two legs have matching ticks. Hypotenuse AB is labeled 19√2 mm.

    1. Hint 1

      Identify the factor connecting a leg to the hypotenuse.

    2. Hint 2

      Recover one leg by dividing by that factor, then add two equal legs and the hypotenuse.

    Answer

    Each leg is 191919 mm. The perimeter is (38+192)(38+19\sqrt2)(38+192​) mm.

    Full solution

    Each leg is the hypotenuse divided by 2\sqrt22​.

    s=1922.s=\frac{19\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}.s=2​192​​.

    Therefore each leg is 191919 mm.

    Add both equal legs and the hypotenuse.

    P=38+192 mm.P=38+19\sqrt2\text{ mm}.P=38+192​ mm.

    The two leg squares total 722722722, which equals the square of 19219\sqrt2192​, so the lengths check.

    Answer

    Each leg is 191919 mm. The perimeter is (38+192)(38+19\sqrt2)(38+192​) mm.

    Key idea

    Reverse the hypotenuse multiplier before adding the sides.

  4. Problem 4 A midpoint join

    On paper, draw an equilateral triangle ABCABCABC with each side labeled 222222 cm. Use a convenient drawing scale. Mark the midpoint MMM of BCBCBC and join AAA to MMM.

    Add the equal-length and angle markings that can be justified. Explain why AMAMAM is perpendicular to BCBCBC and bisects angle AAA. Label both half-base lengths and the exact altitude.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare the two smaller triangles using three side pairs.

    2. Hint 2

      Equal corresponding angles at M form a straight angle. The equal vertex parts divide the equilateral angle.

    3. Hint 3

      Once the right angle is justified, the equilateral side is the hypotenuse of either half.

    Answer

    SSS justifies the matching halves. Both half-bases are 111111 cm, both vertex parts are 30∘30^\circ30∘, and both angles at MMM are 90∘90^\circ90∘. The altitude is 11311\sqrt3113​ cm.

    Full solution

    Mark all three outer sides with one tick pattern.

    Mark the two half-base segments with a different pattern.

    The triangles ABMABMABM and ACMACMACM have equal outer sides, equal base halves, and shared AMAMAM, so they are congruent by SSS.

    Corresponding angles AMBAMBAMB and AMCAMCAMC are equal.

    Since BBB, MMM, and CCC are collinear, those angles sum to 180∘180^\circ180∘ and each is 90∘90^\circ90∘.

    Add right-angle marks only after this reasoning.

    The two parts at AAA correspond under the congruence.

    They split the equilateral 60∘60^\circ60∘ angle, so each is 30∘30^\circ30∘.

    Add matching angle arcs or the two angle labels.

    The half-base lengths are each 111111 cm.

    In either 30-60-90 half, the altitude is the long leg.

    AM=113 cm.AM=11\sqrt3\text{ cm}.AM=113​ cm.

    Answer

    SSS justifies the matching halves. Both half-bases are 111111 cm, both vertex parts are 30∘30^\circ30∘, and both angles at MMM are 90∘90^\circ90∘. The altitude is 11311\sqrt3113​ cm.

    Key idea

    Construction markings should record proved facts, not visual guesses.

  5. Problem 5 A length relationship

    A nondegenerate right triangle has two acute angles of 45∘45^\circ45∘ and one leg of length s>0s>0s>0.

    Derive the hypotenuse length from the angle information and Pythagoras. Derive the reverse formula for sss when the hypotenuse is hhh. Check your formulas for a leg of 131313 cm.

    Text description of this figure

    Right triangle CAB has its marked right angle at C. The two legs have matching ticks. Side CA is labeled s. Hypotenuse AB is labeled h.

    1. Hint 1

      Equal angles have equal opposite sides.

    2. Hint 2

      Apply Pythagoras after identifying both legs. Use the positive root because these quantities are lengths.

    3. Hint 3

      To reverse the relationship, divide by the nonzero multiplier.

    Answer

    h=s2.s=h2.\begin{aligned}h&=s\sqrt2.\\s&=\frac{h}{\sqrt2}.\end{aligned}hs​=s2​.=2​h​.​

    For a 131313 cm leg, the hypotenuse is 13213\sqrt2132​ cm and the reverse formula returns 131313 cm.

    Full solution

    As a numerical check, equal acute angles force both legs to be 131313 cm.

    Pythagoras gives

    h2=132+132.h^2=13^2+13^2.h2=132+132.

    Thus

    h2=338.h^2=338.h2=338.

    The positive root is 13213\sqrt2132​ cm.

    For a general positive leg sss, equal opposite angles again force the other leg to be sss.

    Pythagoras gives

    h2=2s2.h^2=2s^2.h2=2s2.

    Taking the positive root gives

    h=s2.h=s\sqrt2.h=s2​.

    The multiplier is positive, so division recovers the leg.

    s=h2.s=\frac{h}{\sqrt2}.s=2​h​.

    With hypotenuse 13213\sqrt2132​ cm this returns 131313 cm.

    Answer

  6. Problem 6 An altitude measurement

    Find its exact side length and perimeter.

    Text description of this figure

    1. Hint 1

      The altitude is the long leg in either congruent half of the equilateral triangle.

    2. Hint 2

      Recover half a side first, then double it. The frame has three equal sides.

    Answer

    Side length 10310\sqrt3103​ cm; perimeter 30330\sqrt3303​ cm.

    Full solution

    Let sss be a half-base length.

    Since the altitude is the long leg,

    s3=15.s\sqrt3=15.s3​=15.

    Divide and rationalize.

    s=53 cm.s=5\sqrt3\text{ cm}.s=53​ cm.

    An equilateral side is twice the half-base.

    e=103 cm.e=10\sqrt3\text{ cm}.e=103​ cm.

    The perimeter contains three equal sides.

    P=303 cm.P=30\sqrt3\text{ cm}.P=303​ cm.

    A check in one half gives short-leg square 757575 plus altitude square 225225225, totaling 300300300, the square of the side length.

    Answer

    Side length 10310\sqrt3103​ cm; perimeter 30330\sqrt3303​ cm.

    Key idea

    An equilateral altitude gives half a side before it gives a whole side.

  7. Problem 7 The two projected pieces

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC, angle AAA is 30∘30^\circ30∘, and hypotenuse ABABAB is 343434 cm. The altitude from CCC meets ABABAB at HHH.

    Find AHAHAH and HBHBHB exactly. Then explain why AH:HB=3:1AH:HB=3:1AH:HB=3:1 for every right triangle with these angle measures, whatever its size.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle ABC is right at C. H is the foot of the altitude on AB. AB is 34 cm; AH is AH; HB is HB. CA is AC. CB is BC. Angle A is 30°.

    1. Hint 1

      Find both legs from the special side ratio before finding their projections.

    2. Hint 2

    3. Hint 3

    Answer

    AH=25.5AH=25.5AH=25.5 cm and HB=8.5HB=8.5HB=8.5 cm. In general, AH=3c/4AH=3c/4AH=3c/4 and HB=c/4HB=c/4HB=c/4, so AH:HB=3:1AH:HB=3:1AH:HB=3:1.

    Full solution

    The short leg opposite 30∘30^\circ30∘ is BC=17BC=17BC=17 cm.

    The long leg is AC=173AC=17\sqrt3AC=173​ cm.

    Apply the leg projection relationships.

    AH=AC2AB=86734=25.5 cm.HB=BC2AB=28934=8.5 cm.\begin{aligned}AH&=\frac{AC^2}{AB}=\frac{867}{34}=25.5\text{ cm}.\\HB&=\frac{BC^2}{AB}=\frac{289}{34}=8.5\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}AHHB​=ABAC2​=34867​=25.5 cm.=ABBC2​=34289​=8.5 cm.​

    Their sum is the full 343434 cm hypotenuse.

    Now let AB=c>0AB=c>0AB=c>0.

    The corresponding legs are c3/2c\sqrt3/2c3​/2 and c/2c/2c/2.

    Hence

    AH=(c3/2)2c=3c4.HB=(c/2)2c=c4.\begin{aligned}AH&=\frac{(c\sqrt3/2)^2}{c}=\frac{3c}{4}.\\HB&=\frac{(c/2)^2}{c}=\frac{c}{4}.\end{aligned}AHHB​=c(c3​/2)2​=43c​.=c(c/2)2​=4c​.​

    Dividing the two projected lengths gives AH:HB=3:1AH:HB=3:1AH:HB=3:1.

    The leg ratio is 3:1\sqrt3:13​:1; the projection ratio uses their squares, so it is 3:13:13:1.

  9. Problem 9 Recognizing the special shape

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. Its hypotenuse ABABAB is 464646 cm, and leg BCBCBC is 232323 cm.

    Find ACACAC exactly. Prove that angle AAA is 30∘30^\circ30∘ and angle BBB is 60∘60^\circ60∘ by comparing with a known special triangle; do not assume a converse of the side-ratio rule.

    Would knowing only that two sides of a triangle are 232323 cm and 464646 cm justify the same conclusion? Test the possible third side 303030 cm and explain.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle ABC is right at C. Hypotenuse AB is 46 cm, and BC is 23 cm. No acute angle measures are marked.

    1. Hint 1

      Use Pythagoras to find the positive length of AC.

    2. Hint 2

      A 30-60-90 triangle with short leg 23 cm supplies a comparison triangle with three known side lengths.

    3. Hint 3

      Check triangle existence before comparing the largest-side square in the second case.

    Answer

    Full solution

    Pythagoras applies because the right angle is given.

    AC2=462−232=1587.AC=233 cm.\begin{aligned}AC^2&=46^2-23^2=1587.\\AC&=23\sqrt3\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}AC2AC​=462−232=1587.=233​ cm.​

    The lesson proof gives a 30-60-90 half with sides 232323, 23323\sqrt3233​, and 464646 cm.

    All three side lengths match triangle ABCABCABC.

    SSS proves congruence.

    The angle opposite the 232323 cm side is 30∘30^\circ30∘, so A=30∘A=30^\circA=30∘; the remaining acute angle BBB is 60∘60^\circ60∘.

    For sides 232323, 303030, and 464646, the existence check passes because 23+30>4623+30>4623+30>46.

    462=2116.232+302=1429.\begin{aligned}46^2&=2116.\\23^2+30^2&=1429.\end{aligned}462232+302​=2116.=1429.​

    The longest-side square is greater, so that triangle is obtuse, not right.

    Thus the two given side lengths alone do not force a 30-60-90 triangle.

  10. Problem 10 A shared length

    A square has side length a>0a>0a>0. An equilateral triangle has a side equal in length to a diagonal of the square.

    The equilateral triangle's altitude is 10610\sqrt6106​ cm. Find the square's side length, its perimeter, and the equilateral triangle's perimeter. Explain the chain of side relationships and check the altitude exactly.

    1. Hint 1

      The shared length has different roles: square diagonal and equilateral side.

    2. Hint 2

      Recover the equilateral side from its altitude, then recover the square side from that diagonal.

    3. Hint 3

      Use four equal sides for the square perimeter and three for the equilateral perimeter.

    Answer

    Square side 202020 cm; square perimeter 808080 cm; equilateral perimeter 60260\sqrt2602​ cm.

    Full solution

    Let ddd be the common length of the square diagonal and the equilateral side.

    The equilateral altitude is half its side times 3\sqrt33​, so

    d32=106.\frac{d\sqrt3}{2}=10\sqrt6.2d3​​=106​.

    Multiply by 222 and divide by 3\sqrt33​.

    d=202 cm.d=20\sqrt2\text{ cm}.d=202​ cm.

    A square diagonal is 2\sqrt22​ times its side.

    Therefore

    a=2022.a=\frac{20\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}.a=2​202​​.

    Thus the square side is 202020 cm.

    The square has four equal sides, so its perimeter is 808080 cm.

    The equilateral triangle has three equal sides of length 20220\sqrt2202​ cm, so its perimeter is 60260\sqrt2602​ cm.

    Check the equilateral altitude.

    h=202⋅32.h=\frac{20\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt3}{2}.h=2202​⋅3​​.

    This is exactly 10610\sqrt6106​ cm, the stated altitude.

    Answer

    Square side 202020 cm; square perimeter 808080 cm; equilateral perimeter 60260\sqrt2602​ cm.

    Key idea

    Follow a shared segment through its role in each figure before combining the two ratios.
