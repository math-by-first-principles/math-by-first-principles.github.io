  1. Problem 1 Triangle RST

    Triangle RSTRSTRST has a right angle at SSS. Side RSRSRS is 555 cm and side STSTST is 373\sqrt737​ cm.

    Identify the hypotenuse and find its exact length in simplest radical form.

    Text description of this figure

    Triangle RTS has RS labeled 5 cm, ST labeled 3√7 cm, and RT labeled RT. The right angle is at S.

    1. Hint 1

      Locate the side that does not touch the right-angle vertex.

    2. Hint 2

      Square each leg, including the outside coefficient of the radical, and add.

    3. Hint 3

      After taking the positive root, look for a perfect-square factor in the radicand.

    Answer

    The hypotenuse is RTRTRT, with exact length 2222\sqrt{22}222​ cm, equivalently 88\sqrt{88}88​ cm.

    Full solution

    The right angle is at SSS, so the side across from it is RTRTRT.

    The two given sides are its legs.

    Apply the theorem.

    RT2=52+(37)2RT^2=5^2+(3\sqrt7)^2RT2=52+(37​)2

    Squaring the radical length gives 636363.

    Thus

    RT2=25+63RT^2=25+63RT2=25+63

    Take the positive root and simplify.

    RT=88,RT=222.\begin{aligned}RT&=\sqrt{88},\\RT&=2\sqrt{22}.\end{aligned}RTRT​=88​,=222​.​

    Answer

    The hypotenuse is RTRTRT, with exact length 2222\sqrt{22}222​ cm, equivalently 88\sqrt{88}88​ cm.

    Key idea

    The right-angle vertex identifies the hypotenuse before any numbers enter the equation.

    Full solution

    The answer is shorter than the hypotenuse because its square is 414141, less than 909090.

    Answer

    A missing leg is found by subtracting squares, even when a given side is a radical.

  3. Problem 3 An asserted length

    Line 1: t2=82+112t^2=8^2+11^2t2=82+112.

    Line 2: t=64+121t=\sqrt{64+121}t=64+121​.

    Line 4: t=19t=19t=19.

    Explain the error in Line 3 and replace the incorrect conclusion with the exact hypotenuse. Give a direct squared-value check showing that 191919 cannot be the hypotenuse.

    1. Hint 1

    2. Hint 2

      Compare the square of the claimed length with the sum of the leg squares.

    3. Hint 3

      Keep the sum under one radical before simplifying.

    Answer

    Full solution

    Lines 1 and 2 correctly identify and solve for the positive hypotenuse.

    Line 3 treats a root of a sum as a sum of roots, a rule that does not hold here.

    Add inside the one radical.

    t=185t=\sqrt{185}t=185​

    The radicand is the product of 555 and 373737, so it has no perfect-square factor greater than 111.

    Square the proposed answer 191919.

    192=36119^2=361192=361

    Compare it with the required square.

    82+112=1858^2+11^2=18582+112=185

    The squares differ, so 191919 cannot be the hypotenuse.

    Answer

    Line 3 distributes a square root over a sum, which is invalid. The correct hypotenuse is 185\sqrt{185}185​ units. The claimed 191919 units fails because 192=36119^2=361192=361, whereas the leg-square sum is 185185185.

  4. Problem 4 Two prior relations

    Triangle ABCABCABC is nondegenerate and right at CCC. Let aaa name BCBCBC, bbb name CACACA, and ccc name ABABAB. The altitude CHCHCH meets the open segment ABABAB at HHH, with ppp naming AHAHAH and qqq naming HBHBHB.

    Use these prior results from similarity and segment addition.

    b2=cpb^2=cpb2=cp.

    a2=cqa^2=cqa2=cq.

    p+q=cp+q=cp+q=c.

    Prove the Pythagorean theorem from these relations. Explain exactly where the fact that HHH lies inside ABABAB is used.

    Text description of this figure

    ABC is right at C. The altitude CH meets the interior of AB at H. AH is p, HB is q, CA is b, BC is a, AB is c, and CH is h.

    1. Hint 1

      Both leg-square products share the same whole hypotenuse.

    2. Hint 2

      Add the two products and factor out that common length.

    3. Hint 3

      The two interior pieces fill one whole hypotenuse.

    Answer

    The result is

    a2+b2=c2a^2+b^2=c^2a2+b2=c2

    The interior foot allows the two hypotenuse pieces to be replaced by the whole hypotenuse, by segment addition.

    Full solution

    Add the two leg-square relations.

    a2+b2=cq+cpa^2+b^2=cq+cpa2+b2=cq+cp

    Factor out the common hypotenuse.

    a2+b2=c(q+p)a^2+b^2=c(q+p)a2+b2=c(q+p)

    The interior position of HHH means that the adjacent lengths AHAHAH and HBHBHB partition ABABAB.

    This is the step that gives

    q+p=cq+p=cq+p=c

    Substitute the whole hypotenuse for the sum of its pieces.

    a2+b2=c2a^2+b^2=c^2a2+b2=c2

    Answer

    The result is

    a2+b2=c2a^2+b^2=c^2a2+b2=c2

    The interior foot allows the two hypotenuse pieces to be replaced by the whole hypotenuse, by segment addition.

    The similarity proof adds two products with a common hypotenuse, then uses segment addition.

  5. Problem 5 Four proposed frames

    For each proposed set of side lengths, determine whether a nondegenerate triangle exists. Classify each existing triangle as acute, right, or obtuse. Show the existence check before the squared comparison.

    Case A: 101010, 121212, and 171717 cm.

    Case B: 444, 101010, and 151515 cm.

    Case C: 777, 101010, and 121212 cm.

    Case D: 141414, 484848, and 505050 cm.

    1. Hint 1

      First compare the longest length with the sum of the other two.

    2. Hint 2

      For each set that closes, compare the longest-side square with the sum of the other two squares.

    3. Hint 3

      A greater longest-side square means an angle larger than a right angle, while equality gives a right angle.

    Answer

    Case A is obtuse. Case B does not form a nondegenerate triangle. Case C is acute. Case D is right.

    Full solution

    172=289,102+122=244.\begin{aligned}17^2&=289,\\10^2+12^2&=244.\end{aligned}172102+122​=289,=244.​

    The longest-side square is larger, so Case A is obtuse.

    Case B fails the existence check.

    4+10<154+10<154+10<15

    It receives no angle classification.

    Case C exists because 7+10>127+10>127+10>12.

    Compare

    122=144,72+102=149.\begin{aligned}12^2&=144,\\7^2+10^2&=149.\end{aligned}12272+102​=144,=149.​

    The longest-side square is smaller, so Case C is acute.

  6. Problem 6 Three fixed lengths

    Triangle PQRPQRPQR has side lengths PQPQPQ of 999 units, QRQRQR of 404040 units, and PRPRPR of 414141 units. No angle is given.

    Prove that angle PQRPQRPQR is right by constructing a separate right triangle and using SSS. State how the Pythagorean theorem is used without assuming the original triangle is right.

    1. Hint 1

      Make a comparison triangle whose right angle is known from its construction.

    2. Hint 2

      Copy the 999-unit and 404040-unit sides onto perpendicular rays.

    3. Hint 3

      Find the constructed hypotenuse, then match all three sides to the given triangle.

    Answer

    Construct a right triangle with legs 999 and 404040 units. Its hypotenuse is 414141 units by the theorem. SSS congruence with PQRPQRPQR makes angle PQRPQRPQR right; the theorem was applied only to the constructed triangle.

    Full solution

    Construct triangle XYZXYZXYZ with a right angle at YYY, side XYXYXY of 999 units, and side YZYZYZ of 404040 units.

    Copying positive lengths onto perpendicular rays makes this a nondegenerate right triangle.

    Call its hypotenuse ddd.

    The theorem applies here because the construction supplies the right angle.

    d2=92+402,d2=1681,d=41.\begin{aligned}d^2&=9^2+40^2,\\d^2&=1681,\\d&=41.\end{aligned}d2d2d​=92+402,=1681,=41.​

    The corresponding sides PQPQPQ and XYXYXY both have length 999.

    Sides QRQRQR and YZYZYZ both have length 404040.

    Sides PRPRPR and XZXZXZ both have length 414141.

    SSS proves that triangles PQRPQRPQR and XYZXYZXYZ are congruent in that order.

    Angle PQRPQRPQR therefore matches the right angle XYZXYZXYZ.

    No right angle in the original triangle was assumed.

    Its right angle follows after congruence.

    Answer

    Construct a right triangle with legs 999 and 404040 units. Its hypotenuse is 414141 units by the theorem. SSS congruence with PQRPQRPQR makes angle PQRPQRPQR right; the theorem was applied only to the constructed triangle.

    Key idea

    A constructed right triangle lets the established theorem supply the third side needed for a noncircular converse proof.

  8. Problem 8 Four written lines

    A triangle has side lengths 111111, 131313, and 202020 units. A student writes this argument.

    Line 1: 11+13>2011+13>2011+13>20, so a nondegenerate triangle with these lengths exists.

    Line 2: 202=112+13220^2=11^2+13^2202=112+132, because 202020 is the longest side.

    Line 3: 202=29020^2=290202=290, by evaluating the right side of Line 2.

    Line 4: The triangle is right by the converse of the Pythagorean theorem.

    Identify the first invalid line, explain why its stated reason fails, and repair the argument to give the correct angle classification.

    1. Hint 1

      The longest side tells you which square to compare; it does not force equality.

    2. Hint 2

      Check the lines in order and distinguish an assumed equality from an arithmetic consequence.

    3. Hint 3

      Evaluate the longest-side square independently of the other square sum.

    Answer

    Line 2 is the first invalid line. A longest side need not satisfy the right-triangle equality. The valid triangle is obtuse because its longest-side square is 400400400, greater than the other square sum of 290290290.

    Full solution

    Line 1 is valid: the positive lengths pass the strict triangle inequality.

    Line 2 is the first invalid line.

    Knowing the longest side selects the comparison side but gives no right-angle hypothesis.

    The student has applied the theorem before knowing the triangle is right.

    Compute the two quantities separately.

    202=400,112+132=290.\begin{aligned}20^2&=400,\\11^2+13^2&=290.\end{aligned}202112+132​=400,=290.​

    The longest-side square is greater than the sum of the other two squares.

    The angle opposite the 202020-unit side is obtuse, so the triangle is obtuse.

    Answer

    Line 2 is the first invalid line. A longest side need not satisfy the right-triangle equality. The valid triangle is obtuse because its longest-side square is 400400400, greater than the other square sum of 290290290.

    Key idea

    The first unsupported equality can invalidate an argument even when the arithmetic after it is performed correctly.

  9. Problem 9 Three designs

    Design A must be acute.
