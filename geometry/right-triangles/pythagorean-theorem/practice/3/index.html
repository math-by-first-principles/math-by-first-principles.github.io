    Positive lengths 888, 151515, and zzz are proposed, with z≥15z\ge15z≥15. Which condition gives an obtuse nondegenerate triangle?

    Solution

    Existence requires

    z<8+15.z<8+15.z<8+15.

  4. 4

    To prove the converse, construct a right triangle with legs equal to two sides of the given triangle. After proving the third sides equal, which congruence criterion transfers the right angle?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Call the given side lengths a, b, and c, with c the side opposite the angle being tested. The constructed triangle has legs a and b and hypotenuse d. Applying the theorem only to it gives

    d2=a2+b2.d^2=a^2+b^2.d2=a2+b2.

    The converse hypothesis makes the right side equal to the square of c. Positivity gives

    d=c.d=c.d=c.

    All three corresponding side lengths now match, which is SSS. The original triangle was not known to be right; its right angle follows after congruence.

    Solution
