    Triangle DEFDEFDEF is right at EEE. Which side is its hypotenuse?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The given right angle is at E.

    m∠DEF=90∘.m\angle DEF=90^\circ.m∠DEF=90∘.

    The hypotenuse is the side opposite that angle, the side not touching E. Thus it is DF.

  11. 11

    In a right triangle, why is the hypotenuse the longest side?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Name the other two positive angle measures alpha and beta. The triangle angle sum gives

    α+β=90∘.\alpha+\beta=90^\circ.α+β=90∘.

    Each is smaller than the right angle. By side-angle ordering, the side opposite the right angle is longest.
