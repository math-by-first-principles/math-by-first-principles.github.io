  3. 3

    Acute angles θ\thetaθ and ϕ\phiϕ are complementary. Evaluate the following expression exactly.

    sin⁡θcos⁡ϕ+cos⁡θsin⁡ϕ\sin\theta\cos\phi+\cos\theta\sin\phisinθcosϕ+cosθsinϕ

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Use the complementary-angle relations in the two products.

    cos⁡ϕ=sin⁡θ.\cos\phi=\sin\theta.cosϕ=sinθ. sin⁡ϕ=cos⁡θ.\sin\phi=\cos\theta.sinϕ=cosθ.

    The expression becomes the sum of squared sine and cosine.

    sin⁡2θ+cos⁡2θ=1.\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1.sin2θ+cos2θ=1.

    It is not the unsquared sum. Both products are positive, and the identity fixes their sum at 111.

    Which expression equals (sin⁡θ+cos⁡θ)2(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^2(sinθ+cosθ)2 for every acute θ\thetaθ?

    Solution

    (sin⁡θ+cos⁡θ)2=sin⁡2θ+cos⁡2θ+2sin⁡θcos⁡θ.\begin{aligned} (\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^2 &=\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta\\ &\quad+2\sin\theta\cos\theta. \end{aligned}(sinθ+cosθ)2​=sin2θ+cos2θ+2sinθcosθ.​

    The sum of the two squares is 111, so the result is 1+2sin⁡θcos⁡θ1+2\sin\theta\cos\theta1+2sinθcosθ. The cross term is positive for acute angles. Omitting it or dropping its cosine factor changes the value.

  7. 7

    In a right triangle, the leg opposite acute θ\thetaθ is longer than its adjacent leg. Which comparison must hold?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Dividing the longer positive leg by the shorter gives a ratio above 111.

    tan⁡θ>1.\tan\theta>1.tanθ>1.

    Sine and cosine use the same positive hypotenuse denominator, so their order follows their leg numerators.

    sin⁡θ>cos⁡θ.\sin\theta>\cos\theta.sinθ>cosθ.

    Reversing either comparison contradicts the stated leg ordering.

  8. 8

    Which expression equals tan⁡2θ\tan^2\thetatan2θ for every acute θ\thetaθ?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Square the quotient identity.

    tan⁡2θ=sin⁡2θcos⁡2θ.\tan^2\theta=\frac{\sin^2\theta}{\cos^2\theta}.tan2θ=cos2θsin2θ​.

    Use the squared-value identity to replace the denominator.

    cos⁡2θ=1−sin⁡2θ.\cos^2\theta=1-\sin^2\theta.cos2θ=1−sin2θ.

    This gives the stated expression. The denominator is positive for acute angles. Adding instead of subtracting, reversing the quotient, or failing to square its numerator gives the wrong expressions.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The given ratios determine shape but leave the scale free. If the hypotenuse is 13k13k13k, the two ratios require opposite leg 5k5k5k and adjacent leg 12k12k12k.

    5k13k=513.\frac{5k}{13k}=\frac5{13}.13k5k​=135​. 12k13k=1213.\frac{12k}{13k}=\frac{12}{13}.13k12k​=1312​.

  12. 12

    For an acute angle θ\thetaθ, it is given that sin⁡θ>cos⁡θ\sin\theta>\cos\thetasinθ>cosθ. Which of its sine and tangent values must exceed 111?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Cosine is positive, so dividing the given inequality by it gives

    sin⁡θcos⁡θ>1.\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}>1.cosθsinθ​>1.

    That quotient is tangent. Sine remains strictly below 111 because it is a leg divided by the longer hypotenuse. Thus only tangent exceeds 111.
