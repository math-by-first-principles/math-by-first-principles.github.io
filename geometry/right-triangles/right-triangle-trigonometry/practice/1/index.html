Question 1 of 12
  1. 1

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. Use AAA as the acute reference angle. Which side is opposite the selected angle?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The opposite leg lies across from the selected acute angle. For angle AAA, that side is BCBCBC.

    CACACA is the adjacent leg, and ABABAB is the hypotenuse.

  2. 2

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. Use AAA as the acute reference angle. Which side is its adjacent leg?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The adjacent leg meets the selected angle and the right angle. Thus CACACA is adjacent to AAA.

    Although ABABAB also touches AAA, it is the hypotenuse and is excluded from adjacent leg.

  3. 3

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. Use AAA as the acute reference angle. Which side is the hypotenuse?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The hypotenuse is opposite the right angle at CCC, so it is ABABAB.

    It stays the hypotenuse when either acute angle is selected. The leg roles can exchange, but the right angle does not move.

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. Use AAA as the acute reference angle. Find sin⁡A\sin AsinA from these lengths.

    BC=9BC=9BC=9

    CA=40CA=40CA=40

    AB=41AB=41AB=41

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Sine uses opposite leg divided by hypotenuse.

    sin⁡A=941.\sin A=\frac9{41}.sinA=419​.

    The other ratios give cosine, tangent, or the reciprocal of the required sine.

    Solution

    In a right triangle, acute θ\thetaθ has opposite leg 888 units and adjacent leg 333 units. What is tan⁡θ\tan\thetatanθ?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Tangent divides the opposite leg by the adjacent leg.

    tan⁡θ=83.\tan\theta=\frac83.tanθ=38​.

    A tangent can exceed 111. The reciprocal reverses the legs; the expressions with 73\sqrt{73}73​ use the hypotenuse instead.

  7. 7

    A right triangle is enlarged by factor 444, with its selected acute angle kept fixed. What happens to the sine of that angle?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Both the opposite leg and the hypotenuse are multiplied by 444.

    4a4c=ac.\frac{4a}{4c}=\frac ac.4c4a​=ca​.

    Their common factor cancels, so sine stays unchanged. The other factors confuse a length or squared length with a ratio.

  8. 8

    Two nondegenerate right triangles have the same acute reference angle. Why may their corresponding side ratios be equated?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    They have a matching right angle and the given matching acute angle, so AA proves similarity.

    Similarity gives proportional corresponding sides. It allows different sizes, so congruence, equal side lengths, and equal perimeters need not follow.
