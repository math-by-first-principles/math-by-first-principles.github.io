    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. The hypotenuse is 404040 cm, and BCBCBC is twice CACACA. Find CHCHCH.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Squaring the leg ratio and dividing their geometric-mean relations gives

    HBAH=4.\frac{HB}{AH}=4.AHHB​=4.

    The whole contains five equal parts, so AH=8AH=8AH=8 cm and HB=32HB=32HB=32 cm.

    CH2=8⋅32.CH^2=8\cdot32.CH2=8⋅32.

    Taking the positive root gives CH=16CH=16CH=16 cm. Halving the whole assumes equal pieces; 888 is only one piece, and 16516\sqrt5165​ is the longer leg.

  2. 2

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets the interior of hypotenuse ABABAB at HHH. Find CHCHCH exactly from the following lengths.

    CA=14CA=14CA=14 cm

    AH=7AH=7AH=7 cm

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The leg relation first determines the whole hypotenuse.

    142=7⋅AB.14^2=7\cdot AB.142=7⋅AB.

    Thus AB=28AB=28AB=28 cm and HB=21HB=21HB=21 cm. Use the two pieces.

    CH2=7⋅21.CH^2=7\cdot21.CH2=7⋅21.

    Therefore CH=73CH=7\sqrt3CH=73​ cm. Using ABABAB instead of HBHBHB returns the given leg 141414, while using CACACA as a piece gives 727\sqrt272​. The other leg is 14314\sqrt3143​.

  3. 3

    Triangle XYZXYZXYZ is right at XXX. The altitude XHXHXH meets the interior of hypotenuse YZYZYZ. Find XYXYXY exactly from the following pieces.

    YH=3YH=3YH=3 cm

    HZ=15HZ=15HZ=15 cm

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The whole hypotenuse is YZ=18YZ=18YZ=18 cm. Leg XYXYXY touches the 333 cm piece at YYY.

    XY2=18⋅3.XY^2=18\cdot3.XY2=18⋅3.

    Thus XY=36XY=3\sqrt6XY=36​ cm. The altitude is 353\sqrt535​ cm, and the other leg is 3303\sqrt{30}330​ cm.

  4. 4

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. The ratio CA:CHCA:CHCA:CH is 5:45:45:4, and AB=25AB=25AB=25 cm. Find AHAHAH.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Divide the leg and altitude relations. The positive factor AHAHAH cancels.

    CA2CH2=ABHB.\frac{CA^2}{CH^2}=\frac{AB}{HB}.CH2CA2​=HBAB​.

    The given length ratio has squared ratio 25/1625/1625/16, so

    2516=25HB.\frac{25}{16}=\frac{25}{HB}.1625​=HB25​.

    Thus HB=16HB=16HB=16 cm. The requested piece is

    AH=25−16.AH=25-16.AH=25−16.

    Therefore AH=9AH=9AH=9 cm. Using the unsquared ratio would give the incorrect piece HB=20HB=20HB=20.

  5. 5

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets the interior of hypotenuse ABABAB at HHH. A measurement record gives these exact lengths.

    AH=6AH=6AH=6 cm

    HB=10HB=10HB=10 cm

    CH=7CH=7CH=7 cm

    Which conclusion is justified?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The exact measurements must satisfy the altitude relation.

    CH2=AH⋅HB.CH^2=AH\cdot HB.CH2=AH⋅HB.

    But the recorded square is 494949 and the recorded product is 606060. They differ, so no right triangle with the stated altitude can have all three measurements.

    An altitude can meet unequal pieces. Merely lying between their lengths or producing right angles does not replace the required correspondence.

  6. 6

    Triangle ABCABCABC has angles 40∘40^\circ40∘ at AAA and BBB, and 100∘100^\circ100∘ at CCC. Its altitude CHCHCH meets the interior of ABABAB. Which statement is true?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Each small triangle has a 40∘40^\circ40∘ endpoint angle and a right angle at HHH. Its remaining angle is 50∘50^\circ50∘.

    The small triangles therefore share angle set 40∘40^\circ40∘, 50∘50^\circ50∘, 90∘90^\circ90∘ and are similar by AA. The whole has angle set 40∘40^\circ40∘, 40∘40^\circ40∘, 100∘100^\circ100∘, so it is similar to neither small triangle.

    The original triangle's right-angle hypothesis is essential to the three-triangle result.

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. If m∠ACH=63∘m\angle ACH=63^\circm∠ACH=63∘, what is m∠HCBm\angle HCBm∠HCB?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The two small angles at CCC make the whole right angle.

    m∠HCB=90∘−63∘.m\angle HCB=90^\circ-63^\circ.m∠HCB=90∘−63∘.

    Thus ∠HCB\angle HCB∠HCB measures 27∘27^\circ27∘. The angle is not a copy or half of ∠ACH\angle ACH∠ACH; 117∘117^\circ117∘ would be its supplement, not its complement.

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. The pieces satisfy AH:HB=9:16AH:HB=9:16AH:HB=9:16. What is the ratio BC:CABC:CABC:CA?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Divide the two leg relationships.

    BC2CA2=HBAH.\frac{BC^2}{CA^2}=\frac{HB}{AH}.CA2BC2​=AHHB​.

    Thus the squared leg ratio is 16:916:916:9. Both lengths are positive, so taking square roots gives

    BC:CA=4:3.BC:CA=4:3.BC:CA=4:3.

    The piece order is reversed because BCBCBC touches HBHBHB, while CACACA touches AHAHAH.

  9. 9

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. Use these length names.

    a=BCb=CAc=ABp=AHq=HB.\begin{aligned} a&=BC\\ b&=CA\\ c&=AB\\ p&=AH\\ q&=HB. \end{aligned}abcpq​=BC=CA=AB=AH=HB.​

    Why does dividing the leg relationships give the following ratio?

    a2b2=qp.\frac{a^2}{b^2}=\frac qp.b2a2​=pq​.
    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The leg relations give a2=cqa^2=cqa2=cq and b2=cpb^2=cpb2=cp. Divide them using positive lengths.

    a2b2=cqcp.\frac{a^2}{b^2}=\frac{cq}{cp}.b2a2​=cpcq​.

    Cancel the common nonzero factor ccc.

    a2b2=qp.\frac{a^2}{b^2}=\frac qp.b2a2​=pq​.

    Similarity gives proportional sides; it does not require equal sides or a midpoint.

  10. 10

    Two right triangles each have a hypotenuse altitude of 121212 cm.

    The first has hypotenuse pieces 888 cm and 181818 cm.

    The second has hypotenuse pieces 666 cm and 242424 cm.

    Which comparison is correct?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    For each triangle, the ratio of squared legs equals the ratio of the corresponding pieces. Since 18>818>818>8 and 24>624>624>6, compare longer leg with shorter leg.

    For the first triangle,

    longer legshorter leg=188.\frac{\text{longer leg}}{\text{shorter leg}}=\sqrt{\frac{18}{8}}.shorter leglonger leg​=818​​.

    This ratio is 3/23/23/2. For the second, the ratio is 24/6=2\sqrt{24/6}=224/6​=2. Different within-triangle side ratios rule out similarity, even though both altitudes are 121212 cm.

    The ratios 9:49:49:4 and 4:14:14:1 are squared ratios, not leg ratios.

  11. 11

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets the interior of hypotenuse ABABAB at HHH. Find AHAHAH from the following lengths.

    CA=10CA=10CA=10 cm

    CH=8CH=8CH=8 cm

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Write p=AHp=AHp=AH and q=HBq=HBq=HB. The leg relation and altitude relation give

    CA2=p(p+q).CA^2=p(p+q).CA2=p(p+q). CH2=pq.CH^2=pq.CH2=pq.

    Subtract the second equality from the first. The terms pqpqpq cancel.

    p2=100−64.p^2=100-64.p2=100−64.

    Thus p2=36p^2=36p2=36, so the positive length AHAHAH is 666 cm.

    The value 2\sqrt22​ comes from taking the square root of 10−810-810−8 instead of subtracting squared lengths. The value 164\sqrt{164}164​ adds the squares. The value 25/225/225/2 divides the leg square by the altitude, which is not the relevant leg relationship.

  12. 12

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. Which proportion must always follow from the ordered similarity △ABC∼△ACH\triangle ABC\sim\triangle ACH△ABC∼△ACH?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The ordered matching is AAA to AAA, BBB to CCC, and CCC to HHH. The original side BCBCBC matches CHCHCH, while ABABAB matches ACACAC.

    Keeping original length over image length gives

    BCCH=ABAC.\frac{BC}{CH}=\frac{AB}{AC}.CHBC​=ACAB​.

    Using AHAHAH as the match for BCBCBC, or reversing only one ratio, breaks the correspondence.
