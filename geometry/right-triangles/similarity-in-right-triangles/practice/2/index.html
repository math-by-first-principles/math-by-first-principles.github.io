    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. The whole hypotenuse is 161616 cm, and AH=7AH=7AH=7 cm. Find CHCHCH exactly.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    First recover the other hypotenuse piece.

    HB=16−7.HB=16-7.HB=16−7.

    Thus HB=9HB=9HB=9 cm. Now use both pieces.

    CH2=7⋅9.CH^2=7\cdot9.CH2=7⋅9.

    The positive root gives CH=37CH=3\sqrt7CH=37​ cm. Using the whole 161616 in place of the other piece gives 474\sqrt747​, which is the leg CACACA.

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets the interior of hypotenuse ABABAB at HHH. Find HBHBHB from the following lengths.

    AB=13AB=13AB=13 cm

    CA=39CA=\sqrt{39}CA=39​ cm

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The given leg touches AHAHAH.

    39=13⋅AH.39=13\cdot AH.39=13⋅AH.

    So AH=3AH=3AH=3 cm. The requested piece is the other one.

    HB=13−3.HB=13-3.HB=13−3.

    Therefore HB=10HB=10HB=10 cm. The altitude would be 30\sqrt{30}30​ cm; 39/1039/1039/10 uses a piece where the whole hypotenuse belongs.

  3. 3

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets the interior of hypotenuse ABABAB at HHH. Find ABABAB from the following lengths.

    CH=12CH=12CH=12 cm

    HB=18HB=18HB=18 cm

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Use the altitude to recover the missing piece.

    122=18⋅AH.12^2=18\cdot AH.122=18⋅AH.

    This gives AH=8AH=8AH=8 cm. Add the two pieces.

    AB=8+18.AB=8+18.AB=8+18.

    Thus the whole hypotenuse is 262626 cm. The 888 is only one piece; adding or multiplying the original givens does not find the whole.

  4. 4

    Triangle RSTRSTRST is right at SSS. The perpendicular SDSDSD meets the interior of hypotenuse RTRTRT. Find RSRSRS from the following lengths.

    RD=4RD=4RD=4 cm

    DT=12DT=12DT=12 cm

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The hypotenuse is RTRTRT, whose pieces add to 161616 cm. Leg RSRSRS touches RDRDRD, the 444 cm piece.

    RS2=16⋅4.RS^2=16\cdot4.RS2=16⋅4.

    Taking the positive root gives RS=8RS=8RS=8 cm. The altitude is 434\sqrt343​ cm, and the other leg is 838\sqrt383​ cm.

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. The pieces satisfy

    AH:HB=2:7AH:HB=2:7AH:HB=2:7

    AB=27AB=27AB=27 cm. Find CHCHCH exactly.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    There are nine equal ratio parts in the whole 272727 cm, so each part is 333 cm.

    AH=6 cm.AH=6\text{ cm}.AH=6 cm. HB=21 cm.HB=21\text{ cm}.HB=21 cm.

    Use their product.

    CH2=6⋅21.CH^2=6\cdot21.CH2=6⋅21.

    Thus CH=314CH=3\sqrt{14}CH=314​ cm. The sum of the ratio numbers is not a length, and 14\sqrt{14}14​ omits the scale of the pieces.

  6. 6

    A right triangle and its hypotenuse altitude are enlarged by a dilation with factor 333. How does the altitude length change?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A dilation multiplies every length by its factor. The geometric-mean relation agrees: each hypotenuse piece triples, so their product is multiplied by 999.

    hnew2=9hold2.h_{\text{new}}^2=9h_{\text{old}}^2.hnew2​=9hold2​.

    Both lengths are positive, giving hnew=3holdh_{\text{new}}=3h_{\text{old}}hnew​=3hold​. The factor 999 belongs to squared lengths.

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. Use these length names.

    a=BCa=BCa=BC

    c=ABc=ABc=AB

    What is the scale factor from the whole triangle ABCABCABC to triangle CBHCBHCBH, using corresponding hypotenuses?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The ordered similarity is

    △ABC∼△CBH.\triangle ABC\sim\triangle CBH.△ABC∼△CBH.

    The original hypotenuse ABABAB matches the image hypotenuse CBCBCB. Image length divided by original length gives

    k=ac.k=\frac ac.k=ca​.

    The reciprocal reverses the direction. The other choices use sides that do not correspond in this mapping.

  8. 8

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. Use these length names.

    a=BCa=BCa=BC

    b=CAb=CAb=CA

    p=AHp=AHp=AH

    q=HBq=HBq=HB

    Which statement must hold?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Both squared legs use the same positive whole hypotenuse ccc.

    a2=cq.a^2=cq.a2=cq. b2=cp.b^2=cp.b2=cp.

    Multiplying by ccc preserves order, and positive lengths have the same order as their squares. Thus the longer leg touches the longer piece: a>ba>ba>b exactly when q>pq>pq>p.

    The ratio q/pq/pq/p is the ratio of squared legs. Taking its positive square root gives a/ba/ba/b.

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. Use these length names.

    c=ABc=ABc=AB

    p=AHp=AHp=AH

    h=CHh=CHh=CH

    Which equation expresses the altitude relation using only these three lengths?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The missing piece is the whole minus AHAHAH.

    HB=c−p.HB=c-p.HB=c−p.

    The altitude uses the two pieces.

    h2=p(c−p).h^2=p(c-p).h2=p(c−p).

    The products cpcpcp and c(c−p)c(c-p)c(c−p) give the squares of the legs. The sum p+cp+cp+c does not express the altitude relation.

  11. 11

    Triangle ABCABCABC is right at CCC. The altitude CHCHCH meets hypotenuse ABABAB at interior point HHH. Suppose AH=HBAH=HBAH=HB. Which expression equals CHCHCH in terms of ABABAB?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The equal pieces each have length AB/2AB/2AB/2. Their geometric mean is the same positive number.

    CH2=(AB2)2.CH^2=\left(\frac{AB}{2}\right)^2.CH2=(2AB​)2.

    Therefore CH=AB/2CH=AB/2CH=AB/2. No special-triangle ratio is needed; this follows directly from the altitude relation.

    Thus AB=39AB=39AB=39 cm. The 131313 omits the common scale, 181818 is the altitude, and 117117117 uses x2x^2x2 in a length.
