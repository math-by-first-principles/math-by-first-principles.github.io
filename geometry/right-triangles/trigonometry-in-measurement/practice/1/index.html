  1. 1

    An observer looks upward at a tower top. From which ray is the angle of elevation measured?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Elevation measures the upward turn of a sight line from the horizontal through the eye. If its acute angle from the upward vertical is called ϕ\phiϕ, that angle complements the elevation θ\thetaθ.

    θ+ϕ=90∘.\theta+\phi=90^\circ.θ+ϕ=90∘.
  2. 2

    A sight line is 26 degrees below the horizontal through an eye. What is its angle with the downward vertical?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The horizontal and downward vertical form a right angle. Remove the depression angle.

    90∘−26∘=64∘.90^\circ-26^\circ=64^\circ.90∘−26∘=64∘.
  3. 3

    On level ground, a vertical post top is 9.0 m above an observer’s eye. The eye is 1.7 m above the post’s base level. What is the whole post height?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The whole height contains the part below the eye and the rise above it.

    H=1.7+9.0.H=10.7 m.\begin{aligned}H&=1.7+9.0.\\H&=10.7\text{ m}.\end{aligned}HH​=1.7+9.0.=10.7 m.​

    An observer and a vertical tree stand on the same level ground in one vertical plane. Eye height is 1.51.51.5 m, horizontal run is 20.020.020.0 m, and elevation is 30.0∘30.0^\circ30.0∘. Estimate the whole height to the nearest tenth.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The tangent rise starts at eye level. Add the eye height only after finding that rise.

    H=1.5+20tan⁡30∘.H≈13.0 m.\begin{aligned}H&=1.5+20\tan30^\circ.\\H&\approx13.0\text{ m}.\end{aligned}HH​=1.5+20tan30∘.≈13.0 m.​

    A lookout eye is 18.018.018.0 m above a horizontal water surface. A target on that surface is seen at a depression angle of 40.0∘40.0^\circ40.0∘. Find the horizontal distance to the nearest tenth of a meter.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Parallel horizontal references transfer the depression angle to the target’s elevation angle.

    tan⁡40∘=18d.d=18tan⁡40∘.d≈21.5 m.\begin{aligned}\tan40^\circ&=\frac{18}{d}.\\d&=\frac{18}{\tan40^\circ}.\\d&\approx21.5\text{ m}.\end{aligned}tan40∘dd​=d18​.=tan40∘18​.≈21.5 m.​

    A straight ramp is 9.09.09.0 m long along its surface and makes 10.0∘10.0^\circ10.0∘ with the horizontal. What is its vertical rise to the nearest tenth of a meter?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The ramp surface is the hypotenuse, so sine connects it with the rise.

    r=9sin⁡10∘.r≈1.6 m.\begin{aligned}r&=9\sin10^\circ.\\r&\approx1.6\text{ m}.\end{aligned}rr​=9sin10∘.≈1.6 m.​

  8. 8

    Two points at different heights lie in a vertical plane. The angle of depression from the higher point is 32 degrees. What is the angle of elevation back to it from the lower point, using horizontal references?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The two horizontals are parallel and the sight line is their transversal. Alternate interior angles match.

    θelevation=32∘.\theta_{\rm elevation}=32^\circ.θelevation​=32∘.

    A straight ramp has positive rise and positive horizontal run. Which length is greatest?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Let rise be rrr, run ddd, and surface length LLL. Both legs are positive, so the hypotenuse is longer than either.

    L2=r2+d2.L>r.L>d.\begin{aligned}L^2&=r^2+d^2.\\L&>r.\\L&>d.\end{aligned}L2LL​=r2+d2.>r.>d.​

    A straight ramp has an 18 percent gradient. Find its inclination from horizontal to the nearest tenth of a degree.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Turn the percent into a rise/run ratio, then use the acute inverse tangent in degree mode.

    tan⁡θ=0.18.θ=tan⁡−1(0.18).θ≈10.2∘.\begin{aligned}\tan\theta&=0.18.\\\theta&=\tan^{-1}(0.18).\\\theta&\approx10.2^\circ.\end{aligned}tanθθθ​=0.18.=tan−1(0.18).≈10.2∘.​

    A straight ramp has horizontal run 4.00 m and rise 0.50 m. What is its sloped length to the nearest hundredth of a meter?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The run and rise are the perpendicular legs.

    L=4.002+0.502.L≈4.03 m.\begin{aligned}L&=\sqrt{4.00^2+0.50^2}.\\L&\approx4.03\text{ m}.\end{aligned}LL​=4.002+0.502​.≈4.03 m.​
