    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    You chose A: You match the whole hypotenuse to a small leg; the small hypotenuse is CB.

    You chose C: You match sides because both meet the point C, rather than following the ordered angle correspondence.

    You chose D: You select a hypotenuse piece from the other small triangle, although AH is not a side of the whole triangle ABC.

    △ABC∼△CBH.\triangle ABC\sim\triangle CBH.△ABC∼△CBH.

    The vertex pairs are A with C, B with B, and C with H. Thus whole side AC corresponds to small side CH.

    Schematic, not for measuring. ABC is right at C. Its perpendicular CH meets AB at interior H. AH is 17 units and HB is 19 units. The whole hypotenuse and both legs have no numerical labels. B is the requested acute reference angle.

      Each hypotenuse piece is paired with the leg at its own endpoint; first identify the whole hypotenuse.

    3. Hint 3

    The two pieces fill the whole hypotenuse.

    Adding 17 and 19 gives

    AB=36.AB=36.AB=36.

    Leg CA uses the piece at A.

    CA2=36⋅17CA=617.\begin{gathered}CA^2=36\cdot17\\CA=6\sqrt{17}.\end{gathered}CA2=36⋅17CA=617​.​

    BC2=36⋅19BC=619.\begin{gathered}BC^2=36\cdot19\\BC=6\sqrt{19}.\end{gathered}BC2=36⋅19BC=619​.​

    For reference angle B, CA is opposite, BC is adjacent, and AB is the hypotenuse.

    The sine ratio gives
