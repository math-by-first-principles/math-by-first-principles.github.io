    A panel shop can use only rectangular dimensions, in whole centimeters, with both sides at least 333 cm. A board edge constraint requires length plus width to be at most 131313 cm. With length named as the longer side, which panel has the proven greatest area?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    For each shorter side, the longest allowed longer side gives its best area. The shorter side is at least 333 cm. If it were at least 777 cm, the longer side would also be at least 777 cm, making the sum at least 141414 cm. This leaves 333, 444, 555, and 666 cm as the possible shorter sides.

    3(10)=30,4(9)=36,5(8)=40,6(7)=42.\begin{aligned}3(10)&=30,\\4(9)&=36,\\5(8)&=40,\\6(7)&=42.\end{aligned}3(10)4(9)5(8)6(7)​=30,=36,=40,=42.​

    These exhaust the choices for the shorter side, so 777 cm by 666 cm is optimal.

    One shipping shelf has room for 242424 cm across and 181818 cm front to back. Pack 777 cm by 444 cm cartons with aligned edges in horizontal rows. Each row uses a single orientation, but rows may differ. Cartons can touch and fit in one layer. What is the proven maximum for this family?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A 444 cm deep row fits 333 cartons and a 777 cm deep row fits 666. The number of 777 cm rows can only be 000, 111, or 222.

    0(6)+4(3)=12,1(6)+2(3)=12,2(6)+1(3)=15.\begin{aligned}0(6)+4(3)&=12,\\1(6)+2(3)&=12,\\2(6)+1(3)&=15.\end{aligned}0(6)+4(3)1(6)+2(3)2(6)+1(3)​=12,=12,=15.​

    The final arrangement uses all 181818 cm of depth and reaches the maximum.

    A museum display case has fixed inside height and base area. In its model, the board to be minimized has area C+2u+5vC+2u+5vC+2u+5v, where CCC is fixed, uuu and vvv are positive base lengths in centimeters, and uv=40uv=40uv=40. All positive real lengths satisfying this product are allowed. Which dimensions attain the minimum, with the length uuu given first?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The product of the two variable terms is fixed at 400400400. Their squared sum is smallest when their difference is zero.

    (2u+5v)2=1600+(2u−5v)2.(2u+5v)^2=1600+(2u-5v)^2.(2u+5v)2=1600+(2u−5v)2.

    Equality requires each term to be 202020, giving 10 cm for uuu and 4 cm for vvv. Both lengths are positive and their product is 404040.

    Two tent plans require 282828 and 313131 square meters of fabric respectively, before cutting waste. Waste adds 15%15\%15% of the first plan's required area and 4%4\%4% of the second plan's. With at most 32.332.332.3 square meters to purchase, which plans are feasible?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    28(1.15)=32.2,31(1.04)=32.24.\begin{aligned}28(1.15)&=32.2,\\31(1.04)&=32.24.\end{aligned}28(1.15)31(1.04)​=32.2,=32.24.​

    An arts fair has room for exactly 333 equal vendor rows. Row lengths can be 111111 m or 141414 m. All booths are 222 m wide along a row, with a 111 m gap between neighbors and no required gap at the ends. How many more booths fit in total if the rows are lengthened from 111111 m to 141414 m?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    For nnn booths, there are n−1n-1n−1 gaps. The row length used is

    2n+(n−1)=3n−1.2n+(n-1)=3n-1.2n+(n−1)=3n−1.

    The 111111 m row fits four booths exactly. The 141414 m row fits five exactly. The change adds one booth per row, or three altogether.

    A packing table shows that 191919 cards is the best count in full-width rows. An independent area calculation shows that at most 191919 cards of this size can fit inside the container without overlap, even in other orientations. What follows?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The row layout achieves 191919. The independent upper bound applies to the whole permitted set, so no arrangement can beat that count. Together these facts prove the optimum.

    A bin is 252525 cm deep. It uses one layer of nonoverlapping rows, which may be 666 cm or 999 cm deep. A 666 cm row holds 444 pots at most, and a 999 cm row holds 777 at most. All full rows that meet the depth limit are permitted. If the available depth drops to 242424 cm, what happens to the maximum?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    For depths 252525 and 242424 cm, the only possible counts of 999 cm rows are zero, one, or two. With zero, each depth fits four 666 cm rows. With one, each fits two. With two, each fits one.

    4(4)=16,7+2(4)=15,2(7)+4=18.\begin{aligned}4(4)&=16,\\7+2(4)&=15,\\2(7)+4&=18.\end{aligned}4(4)7+2(4)2(7)+4​=16,=15,=18.​

    Two deep rows and one shallow row use exactly 242424 cm, so the best count survives the reduction.

    For two positive panel dimensions xxx and yyy, the material charge in a model is 5(x+y)5(x+y)5(x+y) dollars, with xxx and yyy in centimeters. The design requires xy=81xy=81xy=81. A test finds a feasible pair with charge 909090 dollars. What argument proves this charge is a minimum?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The product fixes the constant term in the identity.

    (x+y)2=324+(x−y)2.(x+y)^2=324+(x-y)^2.(x+y)2=324+(x−y)2.

    Because x+yx+yx+y is positive, the sum is at least 181818. The charge is therefore at least 909090 dollars. The feasible test pair reaches this bound, proving the minimum.
