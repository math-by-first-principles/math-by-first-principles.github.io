  1. 1

    Brook has 1,260 residents across 9 square kilometers. Harbor has 1,520 residents across 8 square kilometers. By how much does Harbor’s average population density exceed Brook’s?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Find each whole-region rate, then subtract.

    1260÷9=1401520÷8=190190−140=50.\begin{gathered}1260\div9=140\\1520\div8=190\\190-140=50.\end{gathered}1260÷9=1401520÷8=190190−140=50.​

    Harbor averages 50 more residents per square kilometer.

    A survey recorded 450 plants in a rectangular plot 12 m by 25 m. A second plot has the same average plant density and an area of 184 square meters. How many plants does the second plot contain?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The first plot covers 12(25)=30012(25)=30012(25)=300 square meters. Use its average rate for the second plot as stated.

    450÷300=1.51.5(184)=276 plants.\begin{gathered}450\div300=1.5\\1.5(184)=276\text{ plants}.\end{gathered}450÷300=1.51.5(184)=276 plants.​

    An island has 840 residents. A 3-square-kilometer reserve with no residents makes up one quarter of the island’s area. What is the average population density of the part outside the reserve?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The reserve is one quarter of the whole area, so the island covers 4(3)=124(3)=124(3)=12 square kilometers. All 840 residents live in the other 12−3=912-3=912−3=9 square kilometers.

    840÷9=9313.840\div9=93\tfrac13.840÷9=9331​.

    The average is in residents per square kilometer.

    A storage tub with a rectangular interior measures 50 cm by 36 cm along its inside base. The inside rim is 28 cm above the bottom and a fill mark is 22 cm above it. How many liters can the tub hold up to the fill mark?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Use the 22 cm fill depth. Convert the cubic centimeters to liters.

    50(36)(22)=39600 cm339600÷1000=39.6 L.\begin{gathered}50(36)(22)=39600\text{ cm}^3\\39600\div1000=39.6\text{ L}.\end{gathered}50(36)(22)=39600 cm339600÷1000=39.6 L.​

    Solution

    Solution

  8. 8

    A model reservoir and its full-size version have similar interiors at model-to-actual length scale 1:5. If the full-size version holds 40 liters to the rim, how many cubic centimeters does the model hold to the rim?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Convert 40 liters to 40,000 cubic centimeters. Since the full-size length is five times the model length, divide the actual capacity by the cube of five.

    40000÷53=320 cm3.40000\div5^3=320\text{ cm}^3.40000÷53=320 cm3.

    Solution
