  3. 3

    A number-line segment has one endpoint at −15-15−15 and midpoint at −4-4−4. What is the other endpoint's coordinate?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The midpoint is 111111 units to the right of −15-15−15. Continue the same distance to the right.

    −4+11=7-4+11=7−4+11=7

    The midpoint is between the endpoints.

    Point SSS has coordinate −6-6−6. A point TTT on the same number line is 999 units from SSS. What are all possible coordinates of TTT?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A given distance allows a point in either direction on the line.

    −6−9=−15-6-9=-15−6−9=−15

    −6+9=3-6+9=3−6+9=3

    Both coordinates are 999 units from −6-6−6.

    Distinct collinear points occur in order AAA, BBB, CCC. Which equation uses the betweenness correctly?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The middle point is BBB, so ABABAB and BCBCBC are the two adjacent parts. Their sum is the whole length ACACAC.

  10. 10

    A ruler records a segment to the nearest millimeter as 525252 mm. Which statement respects the precision of this measurement?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A physical reading has limited precision. Recording nearest millimeters supports an approximate value of 525252 millimeters, not an exact claim with arbitrarily many decimal places.

  12. 12

    Point MMM lies on AB‾\overline{AB}AB and satisfies AM=MBAM=MBAM=MB. Point NNN lies outside AB‾\overline{AB}AB and satisfies AN=NBAN=NBAN=NB. Which point meets the definition of its midpoint?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A midpoint must lie on the segment and divide it into equal lengths. Both conditions are stated for MMM.

    Although AN=NBAN=NBAN=NB, point NNN lacks the required location on the segment, so it is not the midpoint.
