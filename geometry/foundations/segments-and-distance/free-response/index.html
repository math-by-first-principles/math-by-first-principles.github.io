  1. Problem 1 Separate the two notations

    A segment with endpoints AAA and BBB has length 12.512.512.5 centimeters.

    Write a statement using ABABAB for its length.

    Another segment CD‾\overline{CD}CD is congruent to it.

    Write a congruence statement and state CDCDCD.

    1. Hint 1

      The segment and its numerical measurement use different notation.

    2. Hint 2

      Use bars and the congruence symbol for the objects; use an equals sign for the lengths.

    Answer

    AB=12.5AB=12.5AB=12.5 cm; AB‾≅CD‾\overline{AB}\cong\overline{CD}AB≅CD; CD=12.5CD=12.5CD=12.5 cm.

    Full solution

    The unmarked pair ABABAB denotes length, so AB=12.5AB=12.5AB=12.5 centimeters.

    The bars identify the two segments: AB‾≅CD‾\overline{AB}\cong\overline{CD}AB≅CD.

    By the definition of segment congruence, their lengths are equal, so CD=12.5CD=12.5CD=12.5 centimeters too.

    Answer

    AB=12.5AB=12.5AB=12.5 cm; AB‾≅CD‾\overline{AB}\cong\overline{CD}AB≅CD; CD=12.5CD=12.5CD=12.5 cm.

    Key idea

    Congruence describes geometric objects, while equality compares their lengths.

  2. Problem 2 Why the midpoint average works

    Points PPP and QQQ on one number line have coordinates 717171 and −54-54−54, respectively.

    Calculate the average of their coordinates.

    Show that the point MMM at this coordinate lies between the endpoints and has equal distances to them.

    Then explain why swapping the endpoint names changes neither the average nor the midpoint's location.

    1. Hint 1

      A midpoint must lie on the segment and have equal distances to its endpoints.

    2. Hint 2

      Check the coordinate order and both endpoint gaps. For the last part, compare a+ba+ba+b with b+ab+ab+a before dividing by two.

    Answer

    The average is 8.58.58.5. It lies between −54-54−54 and 717171, and PM=MQ=62.5PM=MQ=62.5PM=MQ=62.5 units, so MMM is the midpoint. Swapping the endpoints gives the same average and therefore the same point.

    Full solution

    Compute the average:

    71+(−54)2=8.5.\frac{71+(-54)}{2}=8.5.271+(−54)​=8.5.

    The strict order −54<8.5<71-54<8.5<71−54<8.5<71 places MMM between QQQ and PPP.

    The distance from PPP is

    PM=71−8.5=62.5.PM=71-8.5=62.5.PM=71−8.5=62.5.

    The distance from QQQ is

    QM=8.5−(−54)=62.5.QM=8.5-(-54)=62.5.QM=8.5−(−54)=62.5.

    The location and the equal distances together meet the midpoint definition.

    Swapping the endpoint coordinates gives

    −54+712=8.5.\frac{-54+71}{2}=8.5.2−54+71​=8.5.

    In general, a+b=b+aa+b=b+aa+b=b+a, so dividing either sum by two gives the same average.

    Under the accepted coordinate correspondence, the same coordinate identifies the same point.

    Answer

    The midpoint average gives an equal-distance point between the endpoints, independent of which endpoint is named first.

  5. Problem 5 Locate the halfway point

    The endpoints of a number-line segment are at −18-18−18 and 999. Find the midpoint coordinate and the length of each half.

    1. Hint 1

      A midpoint has equal distances to the endpoints.

    2. Hint 2

      Average the coordinates for the location and halve the full distance for the half length.

    Answer

    The midpoint coordinate is −4.5-4.5−4.5; each half has length 13.513.513.5 units.

    Full solution

    The midpoint coordinate is

    (−18+9)/2=−4.5.(-18+9)/2=-4.5.(−18+9)/2=−4.5.

    The whole length is 9−(−18)=279-(-18)=279−(−18)=27 units, so each half has length 27/2=13.527/2=13.527/2=13.5 units.

    The distances from −4.5-4.5−4.5 to −18-18−18 and to 999 both equal 13.513.513.5.

    Answer

    The midpoint coordinate is −4.5-4.5−4.5; each half has length 13.513.513.5 units.

    Key idea

    A midpoint's coordinate is a location, while a half length is a distance.

  7. Problem 7 Measure a misplaced ruler

    A paper strip starts at the 272727 mm mark and ends at the 868686 mm mark of a ruler.

    Find its measured length in millimeters and centimeters.

    A second strip is measured as 6.26.26.2 cm with the same precision.

    Which is longer, and by how much?

    1. Hint 1

      The first strip's length is the difference between two readings.

    2. Hint 2

      Convert the second length to millimeters before comparing.

    Answer

    The first strip measures 595959 mm, or 5.95.95.9 cm. The second is longer by 333 mm, or 0.30.30.3 cm.

    Full solution

    Subtracting endpoint readings gives 86−27=5986-27=5986−27=59 millimeters.

    This is 5.95.95.9 centimeters.

    The second strip measures 6.26.26.2 centimeters, or 626262 millimeters.

    The difference is 62−59=362-59=362−59=3 millimeters, so the second strip is longer by that measured amount.

    These are physical measurements at the ruler's precision, rather than exact geometric congruence claims.

    Answer

    The first strip measures 595959 mm, or 5.95.95.9 cm. The second is longer by 333 mm, or 0.30.30.3 cm.

    Key idea

    A ruler's zero position is optional, but subtraction and consistent units are essential.

  8. Problem 8 Two possible locations

    Point AAA has number-line coordinate 333.

    Point BBB on the same number line is 101010 units from AAA.

    Find every possible coordinate of BBB and the midpoint coordinate for each resulting segment.

    1. Hint 1

      Distance gives a gap but does not select a direction.

    2. Hint 2

      Place BBB ten units to the left or right of 333, then average each endpoint pair.

    Answer

    If BBB has coordinate −7-7−7, the midpoint coordinate is −2-2−2. If BBB has coordinate 131313, the midpoint coordinate is 888.

    Full solution

    Moving ten units left from 333 gives coordinate 3−10=−73-10=-73−10=−7 for BBB.

    The midpoint coordinate is

    (3+(−7))/2=−2.(3+(-7))/2=-2.(3+(−7))/2=−2.

    Moving ten units right gives coordinate 3+10=133+10=133+10=13 for BBB.

    The midpoint coordinate is

    (3+13)/2=8.(3+13)/2=8.(3+13)/2=8.

    The two choices exhaust the possible directions on the number line, and each midpoint is five units from AAA.

    Answer

    If BBB has coordinate −7-7−7, the midpoint coordinate is −2-2−2. If BBB has coordinate 131313, the midpoint coordinate is 888.

    Key idea

    A distance condition on a line can leave two possible locations.

  9. Problem 9 What each marking tells you

    Use only these verbal descriptions of marks; no drawing is supplied before the solution.

    Situation A: a line crosses a segment at a marked square corner, with no other marks.

    Situation B: a line crosses a segment whose two resulting parts carry matching congruence ticks, with no angle mark.

    For each situation, state whether perpendicularity, bisection, both, or neither is established. The descriptions are the complete givens.

    1. Hint 1

      Treat each described mark as information. No other crossing information is given.

    2. Hint 2

      A square corner concerns the angle; matching length ticks concern equal halves.

    Answer

    Situation A establishes perpendicularity only. Situation B establishes bisection only.

    Full solution

    The square-corner mark described in Situation A declares perpendicularity.

    It gives no equality of part lengths, so bisection is not established.

    The matching ticks described in Situation B declare equal part lengths at a crossing point on the segment.

    That point is the midpoint, so the line bisects the segment.

    No given angle information establishes perpendicularity.

    Answer

    Situation A establishes perpendicularity only. Situation B establishes bisection only.

    Key idea

    Perpendicularity and bisection require different geometric information.
