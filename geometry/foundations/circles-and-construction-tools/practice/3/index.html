    Points AAA and BBB lie on a circle with center OOO. Their distances from OOO are (3x+1)(3x+1)(3x+1) cm and (5x−11)(5x-11)(5x−11) cm. Find the diameter's length.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Both distances are radii, so

    3x+1=5x−11.3x+1=5x-11.3x+1=5x−11.

    Subtract 3x3x3x and add 11 to obtain

    12=2x.12=2x.12=2x.

    Dividing by 2 gives x=6x=6x=6. The radius in centimeters is

    3(6)+1=19.3(6)+1=19.3(6)+1=19.

    Double the radius to find the diameter's length.

    2×19=382\times19=382×19=38

    The diameter is 383838 cm.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The two radii are equal, so

    2y+4=5y−8.2y+4=5y-8.2y+4=5y−8.

    Subtract 2y2y2y and add 8 to obtain

    12=3y.12=3y.12=3y.

    AB=12+12=24AB=12+12=24AB=12+12=24

    The diameter is 242424 cm long.

  5. 5

    For an equilateral construction on AB=9AB=9AB=9 cm, which candidate has the required two circle distances?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The circle centered at AAA requires AC=9AC=9AC=9 cm.

    The circle centered at BBB separately requires BC=9BC=9BC=9 cm.

    The chosen intersection must satisfy both conditions.

  6. 6

    Two disks in the same plane share a center and have radii 555 cm and 888 cm. Where is every point on the smaller disk's boundary circle?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Every point on the smaller boundary circle lies in the common plane and is exactly 555 cm from the common center.

    That equals the smaller radius and is less than the larger radius, so the point is on the smaller boundary and inside the larger disk.

  10. 10

    A circle centered at PPP is drawn with positive compass opening UVUVUV, meeting ray PXPXPX at QQQ. Without changing the opening, a second circle is drawn centered at QQQ, and RRR is chosen on it. Which reason establishes QR=UVQR=UVQR=UV?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The first circle has center PPP and radius UVUVUV, giving PQ=UVPQ=UVPQ=UV. The unchanged opening is therefore also PQPQPQ.

    The second circle has center QQQ and that same opening as its radius. Since RRR lies on it, QR=PQQR=PQQR=PQ, and hence QR=UVQR=UVQR=UV.
