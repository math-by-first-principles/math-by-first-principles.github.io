  1. Problem 1 Name the opening

    Rays JV→\overrightarrow{JV}JV and JW→\overrightarrow{JW}JW form a selected angle. Name its vertex and write two valid three-letter names for the angle.

    1. Hint 1

      Both rays begin at the same point.

    2. Hint 2

      Put the common starting point between the other two letters.

    Answer

    The vertex is JJJ; the names are ∠VJW\angle VJW∠VJW and ∠WJV\angle WJV∠WJV.

    Full solution

    Both rays start at JJJ, so that point is the vertex.

    The vertex goes in the middle, giving ∠VJW\angle VJW∠VJW or ∠WJV\angle WJV∠WJV for the selected opening.

    Answer

    The vertex is JJJ; the names are ∠VJW\angle VJW∠VJW and ∠WJV\angle WJV∠WJV.

    Key idea

    The middle letter of an angle name identifies its vertex.

  2. Problem 2 Explain the boundaries

    Classify each selected opening:

    Case A: 18∘18^\circ18∘.

    Case B: 90∘90^\circ90∘.

    Case C: 152∘152^\circ152∘.

    Case D: 180∘180^\circ180∘.

    Case E: 271∘271^\circ271∘.

    Case F: 360∘360^\circ360∘.

    Use the definitions to explain why 90∘90^\circ90∘ is neither acute nor obtuse and why 180∘180^\circ180∘ is neither obtuse nor reflex.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare the measures with quarter, half, and full turns.

    2. Hint 2

      Apply the strict less-than and greater-than conditions when checking the boundary values.

    Answer

    In order: acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex, full turn. The strict bounds exclude 90∘90^\circ90∘ from acute and obtuse angles, and exclude 180∘180^\circ180∘ from obtuse and reflex angles.

    Full solution

    Case A: The measure 18∘18^\circ18∘ is between zero and 90∘90^\circ90∘, so it is acute.

    Case B: 90∘90^\circ90∘ is right.

    Case C: The measure 152∘152^\circ152∘ is between 90∘90^\circ90∘ and 180∘180^\circ180∘, so it is obtuse.

    Case D: 180∘180^\circ180∘ is straight.

    Case E: The measure 271∘271^\circ271∘ is between 180∘180^\circ180∘ and 360∘360^\circ360∘, so it is reflex.

    Case F: 360∘360^\circ360∘ is a full turn.

    An acute angle must be less than 90∘90^\circ90∘ and an obtuse angle must be greater than 90∘90^\circ90∘, so neither definition includes 90∘90^\circ90∘.

    An obtuse angle must be less than 180∘180^\circ180∘ and a reflex angle must be greater than 180∘180^\circ180∘, so neither definition includes 180∘180^\circ180∘.

    Answer

    In order: acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex, full turn. The strict bounds exclude 90∘90^\circ90∘ from acute and obtuse angles, and exclude 180∘180^\circ180∘ from obtuse and reflex angles.

    Key idea

    Boundary measures form separate classes because the neighboring definitions use strict inequalities.

  3. Problem 3 Select a scale

    A protractor is centered on vertex KKK, and its baseline is aligned with ray KLKLKL.

    Ray KLKLKL meets the outer zero.

    Ray KMKMKM meets outer 124 and inner 56.

    Give the smaller opening's measure and explain your choice of scale.

    1. Hint 1

      Choose a scale using the starting ray rather than the ending ray.

    2. Hint 2

      Follow the scale from the zero on KLKLKL to the mark at KMKMKM.

    Answer

    m∠LKM=124∘m\angle LKM=124^\circm∠LKM=124∘; use the outer scale because its zero lies on KLKLKL.

    Full solution

    The outer zero lies on the starting ray KLKLKL.

    Following that scale to KMKMKM gives

    m∠LKM=124∘.m\angle LKM=124^\circ.m∠LKM=124∘.

    The inner reading starts from the opposite direction and measures a different opening.

    Answer

    m∠LKM=124∘m\angle LKM=124^\circm∠LKM=124∘; use the outer scale because its zero lies on KLKLKL.

    Key idea

    Read the same protractor scale from its starting zero to the second ray.

  4. Problem 4 Draw and check

    On paper, draw a ray PQPQPQ.

    Use a protractor to draw ray PRPRPR so that m∠QPR=115∘m\angle QPR=115^\circm∠QPR=115∘.

    Describe where you place the tool and how you choose the scale.

    State the angle type.

    1. Hint 1

      The center mark and the zero each have a different alignment job.

    2. Hint 2

      After marking the chosen degree, join that mark to the vertex.

    Answer

    Place the center at PPP, align the baseline with PQPQPQ, mark 115 on the scale starting at PQPQPQ, and draw PRPRPR through the mark. The angle is obtuse.

    Full solution

    Draw PQPQPQ and place the protractor's center on PPP.

    Align the baseline with PQPQPQ, then mark 115 on the scale whose zero lies on PQPQPQ.

    Remove the protractor and draw PRPRPR through that mark.

    The measure is between 90∘90^\circ90∘ and 180∘180^\circ180∘, so the opening is obtuse; the paper measurement is approximate.

    Answer

    Place the center at PPP, align the baseline with PQPQPQ, mark 115 on the scale starting at PQPQPQ, and draw PRPRPR through the mark. The angle is obtuse.

    Key idea

    A measured drawing needs both center alignment and the correct starting zero.

  5. Problem 5 Complete an opening

    Ray OBOBOB lies inside the selected angle AOCAOCAOC. The whole measures 161∘161^\circ161∘, and m∠AOB=73∘m\angle AOB=73^\circm∠AOB=73∘. Find m∠BOCm\angle BOCm∠BOC.

    1. Hint 1

      The interior ray divides the whole opening into two parts.

    2. Hint 2

      Subtract the known part from the whole.

    Answer

    m∠BOC=88∘m\angle BOC=88^\circm∠BOC=88∘.

    Full solution

    Angle addition gives

    73∘+m∠BOC=161∘.73^\circ+m\angle BOC=161^\circ.73∘+m∠BOC=161∘.

    Subtracting the known part gives

    161∘−73∘=88∘.161^\circ-73^\circ=88^\circ.161∘−73∘=88∘.

    Answer

    m∠BOC=88∘m\angle BOC=88^\circm∠BOC=88∘.

    Key idea

    An unknown angular part is the whole opening minus the known part.

  6. Problem 6 The other way around

    Two rays bound a selected reflex opening of 252∘252^\circ252∘. Find their smaller opening and the measure of each part when that smaller opening is bisected.

    1. Hint 1

      The two openings together complete a full turn.

    2. Hint 2

      Find the smaller opening before halving it.

    Answer

    The smaller opening is 108∘108^\circ108∘; each bisected part is 54∘54^\circ54∘.

    Full solution

    The smaller opening measures

    360∘−252∘=108∘.360^\circ-252^\circ=108^\circ.360∘−252∘=108∘.

    The bisector divides that opening equally, so each part measures

    108∘÷2=54∘.108^\circ\div2=54^\circ.108∘÷2=54∘.

    Answer

    The smaller opening is 108∘108^\circ108∘; each bisected part is 54∘54^\circ54∘.

    Key idea

    Select the intended opening before applying an angle bisector.

  7. Problem 7 Three consecutive parts

    Three nonoverlapping consecutive angles fill one selected 198∘198^\circ198∘ opening.

    Their measures, in order, are (x+20)∘(x+20)^\circ(x+20)∘, (2x+10)∘(2x+10)^\circ(2x+10)∘, and (x+8)∘(x+8)^\circ(x+8)∘.

    Find xxx and all three measures.

    1. Hint 1

      The three part measures must add to the specified whole.

    2. Hint 2

      Combine the variable terms and constants, then substitute your value into each part.

    Answer

    x=40x=40x=40; the measures are 60∘60^\circ60∘, 90∘90^\circ90∘, and 48∘48^\circ48∘, in order.

    Full solution

    Angle addition gives 4x+38=1984x+38=1984x+38=198.

    Subtracting 38 and dividing by 4 gives x=40x=40x=40.

    Substitution gives 60∘60^\circ60∘, 90∘90^\circ90∘, and 48∘48^\circ48∘.

    Their sum is 198∘198^\circ198∘, as required by the selected reflex opening.

    Answer

    x=40x=40x=40; the measures are 60∘60^\circ60∘, 90∘90^\circ90∘, and 48∘48^\circ48∘, in order.

    Key idea

    Angle addition applies around a specified reflex opening as well as a smaller opening.

  8. Problem 8 Keep track of overlap

    Ray OBOBOB lies inside the selected angle AOCAOCAOC, with m∠AOB=47∘m\angle AOB=47^\circm∠AOB=47∘ and m∠BOC=62∘m\angle BOC=62^\circm∠BOC=62∘.

    Find m∠AOCm\angle AOCm∠AOC.

    Then calculate m∠AOC+m∠BOCm\angle AOC+m\angle BOCm∠AOC+m∠BOC and explain which region that sum counts twice.

    1. Hint 1

      First add the two parts that fill AOCAOCAOC.

    2. Hint 2

      The whole opening already contains the part BOCBOCBOC.

    Answer

    m∠AOC=109∘m\angle AOC=109^\circm∠AOC=109∘; the second sum is 171∘171^\circ171∘ and counts the 62∘62^\circ62∘ region BOCBOCBOC twice.

    Full solution

    The nonoverlapping parts add to

    47∘+62∘=109∘.47^\circ+62^\circ=109^\circ.47∘+62∘=109∘.

    Adding BOCBOCBOC again gives

    109∘+62∘=171∘.109^\circ+62^\circ=171^\circ.109∘+62∘=171∘.

    The whole AOCAOCAOC already contains BOCBOCBOC, so this second sum counts that part twice rather than measuring the whole once.

    Answer

    m∠AOC=109∘m\angle AOC=109^\circm∠AOC=109∘; the second sum is 171∘171^\circ171∘ and counts the 62∘62^\circ62∘ region BOCBOCBOC twice.

    Key idea

    A shared vertex does not guarantee that a sum of angle measures counts a region once.

  9. Problem 9 Equal parts with expressions

    Ray HTHTHT bisects the selected angle RHSRHSRHS. Its parts measure (4x+3)∘(4x+3)^\circ(4x+3)∘ and (6x−21)∘(6x-21)^\circ(6x−21)∘. Find xxx, each part, and the whole measure.

    1. Hint 1

      A bisector gives an equality between the two part measures.

    2. Hint 2

      Solve that equality before adding the two equal parts.

    Answer

    x=12x=12x=12; each part is 51∘51^\circ51∘ and the whole is 102∘102^\circ102∘.

    Full solution

    Equal parts give

    4x+3=6x−21.4x+3=6x-21.4x+3=6x−21.

    Rearranging gives 24=2x24=2x24=2x, so x=12x=12x=12.

    Each part measures 4(12)+3=514(12)+3=514(12)+3=51 degrees.

    Angle addition gives

    51∘+51∘=102∘.51^\circ+51^\circ=102^\circ.51∘+51∘=102∘.

    Answer

    x=12x=12x=12; each part is 51∘51^\circ51∘ and the whole is 102∘102^\circ102∘.

    Key idea

    The bisector supplies equality; angle addition supplies the whole measure.

  10. Problem 10 Choose the reflex route

    The selected reflex opening from ray OAOAOA to ray OBOBOB measures 244∘244^\circ244∘.

    Ray OCOCOC bisects that opening.

    Ray ODODOD lies inside its selected part AOCAOCAOC, with m∠AOD=49∘m\angle AOD=49^\circm∠AOD=49∘.

    Find m∠DOCm\angle DOCm∠DOC and m∠COBm\angle COBm∠COB, and classify both of those smaller angles.

    1. Hint 1

      Find each half of the selected reflex opening first.

    2. Hint 2

      Subtract AODAODAOD from the half AOCAOCAOC; the other half is unchanged.

    Answer

    m∠DOC=73∘m\angle DOC=73^\circm∠DOC=73∘, acute; m∠COB=122∘m\angle COB=122^\circm∠COB=122∘, obtuse.

    Full solution

    The bisector makes each half measure

    244∘÷2=122∘.244^\circ\div2=122^\circ.244∘÷2=122∘.

    Within the selected half AOCAOCAOC, angle addition gives

    122∘−49∘=73∘.122^\circ-49^\circ=73^\circ.122∘−49∘=73∘.

    Thus DOCDOCDOC is acute, while the other half COBCOBCOB remains 122∘122^\circ122∘ and is obtuse.

    Answer

    m∠DOC=73∘m\angle DOC=73^\circm∠DOC=73∘, acute; m∠COB=122∘m\angle COB=122^\circm∠COB=122∘, obtuse.

    Key idea

    Track which selected opening is being halved and which part is being subtracted.
