  1. 1

    A right cylinder has an interior base-parallel section of area 49π49\pi49π square cm and height 11 cm. Which filled rectangle generates it by turning about one side?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The disk area determines the positive radius.

    πr2=49πr=7 cm.\begin{aligned}\pi r^2&=49\pi\\r&=7\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}πr2r​=49π=7 cm.​

    The fixed 11 cm side provides the height, and the perpendicular 7 cm side sweeps the radius.

  2. 2

    The section through the axis of a right cone is an isosceles triangle with base 18 cm and perpendicular height 12 cm. Which filled region and axis generate the cone?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The axial base spans the whole diameter, so the cone radius is 9 cm. Half the axial triangle is a right triangle with radius-leg 9 cm and axis-leg 12 cm.

  3. 3

    Every interior section of a pyramid parallel to its base is a pentagon. What does this determine about its base?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A base-parallel section has the same number of sides as the base. The pentagonal shape fixes five sides, but does not force equal side lengths or angles.

  4. 4

    A plane cuts exactly six faces of a convex polyhedron, contains no polyhedron edge, passes through no vertex, and meets each of those faces in a boundary segment. What is the section?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The section boundary consists of the six segments supplied by the six crossed flat faces. A six-sided polygon is a hexagon.

  5. 5

    A sphere is tilted on a drawing, and its central plane section is drawn as an oval. Which statement is forced by the geometry?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Every point of the central section lies in the plane and is one sphere radius from the sphere center. That is a circle, whatever the projection looks like.

  6. 6

    Rectangle ABCD has AB of 6 cm and AD of 10 cm. A full turn about a side must produce a cylinder with height less than its radius. Which side works?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    About AB, the height is 6 cm and the radius is 10 cm, satisfying the inequality. About AD, the height is 10 cm and radius 6 cm, which reverses the comparison.

  7. 7

    A right triangle has legs 8 cm and 15 cm. Its filled region must generate a cone with base circumference 16π16\pi16π cm. Which leg must be the axis?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The circumference sets the radius.

    2πr=16πr=8 cm.\begin{aligned}2\pi r&=16\pi\\r&=8\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}2πrr​=16π=8 cm.​

    The 8 cm leg must therefore sweep the radius. The perpendicular 15 cm leg is the axis.

  8. 8

    A sphere has radius 10 cm. A plane lies 6 cm from the center. Find the exact area of the disk cut from the ball.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Use the sphere radius as the hypotenuse, then use the resulting squared section radius directly in the disk area.

    r2=102−62r2=64A=64π cm2.\begin{aligned}r^2&=10^2-6^2\\r^2&=64\\A&=64\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}r2r2A​=102−62=64=64π cm2.​
  9. 9

    Two parallel planes cut the interior of the same sphere. Each is 4 cm from its center, on opposite sides of the center. Compare the circular intersections.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Each section radius belongs to a right triangle with the same sphere-radius hypotenuse and the same perpendicular center distance. Their positive radii are therefore equal.

  10. 10

    A filled right triangle with legs 6 cm and 636\sqrt363​ cm turns about the 636\sqrt363​ cm leg. What is the shape of the whole axial section of the cone?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The cone radius is 6 cm, so the whole axial base is 12 cm. Each sloping side has the length of the generating hypotenuse c.

    c2=62+(63)2c2=144c=12 cm.\begin{aligned}c^2&=6^2+(6\sqrt3)^2\\c^2&=144\\c&=12\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}c2c2c​=62+(63​)2=144=12 cm.​

    All three sides of the axial section are 12 cm.

  11. 11

    A cylinder is generated by turning a filled rectangle about a side. Its axial section is a square of side 14 cm. What rectangle and axis work?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A square axial section means the cylinder height and diameter are both 14 cm. The radius is 7 cm, so the generator reaches 7 cm from its fixed 14 cm side.

  12. 12

    Compare turning only a semicircular arc with turning the filled semicircular region of the same radius about their common diameter. Which description is correct?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The boundary arc supplies only points on the sphere surface. The filled region includes the segments from the axis to that arc, so it fills the ball as it turns.
