Cross-Sections and Solids of Revolution: Practice

12 multiple-choice questions, progressively harder.

Question 1 of 12
  1. 1

    A right box has a 3 cm by 4 cm base and height 6 cm. A plane perpendicular to the base contains a base diagonal. What are the true section dimensions?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The plane rises above the diagonal, making a rectangle. The diagonal length d satisfies

    d2=32+42d=5 cm.\begin{aligned}d^2&=3^2+4^2\\d&=5\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}d2d​=32+42=5 cm.​

    The upright dimension is the full 6 cm height.

  2. 2

    A cube is sliced by a plane through the midpoints of three edges that meet at one vertex. The plane cuts off that corner. What is the section?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The plane meets the three faces adjoining that corner. Each face contributes the segment joining two of the given midpoints. Those three segments bound a triangle.

  3. 3

    A right square pyramid is cut by the plane through its apex and a base diagonal. Which section results?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The base diagonal and the two equal apex-to-opposite-vertex edges bound the section. This plane includes the apex, so it gives a triangle.

  4. 4

    Compare interior base-parallel sections of a prism and a pyramid while moving toward their tops. Which description is correct?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A prism repeats the same base shape by translation. A pyramid narrows to its apex, so its base-parallel sections become smaller.

  5. 5

    An oblique plane cuts a right circular cylinder. Its complete section boundary lies on the curved side, and the plane misses both end disks. What is its true section?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The plane is tilted relative to the bases, so the complete closed side cut stretches in one direction. The true section is a noncircular ellipse, not just a perspective view of a circle.

  6. 6

    Which selected cut makes a noncircular elliptical section of a right circular cone?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A base-parallel cut is circular, while an axial cut is triangular. The specified tilted plane makes a closed oval entirely on the curved side.

  7. 7

    A sphere has radius 13 cm. A cutting plane is 5 cm from its center. What is the radius of the circular intersection?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The sphere radius is the hypotenuse of the right triangle formed with the perpendicular center distance and the section radius r.

    r2=132−52r2=144r=12 cm.\begin{aligned}r^2&=13^2-5^2\\r^2&=144\\r&=12\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}r2r2r​=132−52=144=12 cm.​
  8. 8

    A right cylinder has radius 5 cm. Find the exact area of an interior section parallel to its bases.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The true section is a disk of radius 5 cm. Square the radius for its area.

    A=π(5)2A=25π cm2.\begin{aligned}A&=\pi(5)^2\\A&=25\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}AA​=π(5)2=25π cm2.​
  9. 9

    Rectangle ABCD has AB of 5 cm and perpendicular AD of 9 cm. Compare a turn about AB with a turn about AD. Which description is correct?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The axis side becomes the height. The other side becomes the radius. Thus the two named axes exchange the roles of 5 cm and 9 cm.

  10. 10

    Right triangle ABC has right angle A, AB of 7 cm, and AC of 24 cm. The filled triangle turns about AB. What is the diameter of the cone base?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    AB is the height-axis. AC sweeps the base radius, which is 24 cm, so the diameter is

    d=2(24)d=48 cm.\begin{aligned}d&=2(24)\\d&=48\end{aligned}\text{ cm}.dd​=2(24)=48​ cm.
  11. 11

    A filled semicircular region has diameter 14 cm. It turns about that diameter. What is the radius of the ball formed?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The rotation preserves distance from the semicircle center. Its radius is half its diameter.

    r=14÷2r=7 cm.\begin{aligned}r&=14\div2\\r&=7\end{aligned}\text{ cm}.rr​=14÷2=7​ cm.
  12. 12

    A right cylinder has height 10 cm and an axial section 6 cm wide. Which filled rectangle and axis generate it?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The axial width is a diameter, so the radius is 3 cm. The generator must have that 3 cm reach from a 10 cm axis side.
