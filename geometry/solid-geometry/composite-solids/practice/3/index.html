  1. 1

    A right pipe is 8 cm long, open at both ends, and has wall thickness 2 cm. Its material volume is 192π192\pi192π cubic centimeters. What is the outer radius?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The annular area is 24 pi square centimeters. Factor the difference of squared radii.

    (R−r)(R+r)=24(R-r)(R+r)=24(R−r)(R+r)=24

    With thickness 2 cm, the radius sum is 12 cm. The radii are 7 and 5 cm, so the outer radius is 7 cm.

  2. 2

    A solid capsule has radius 2 cm and volume 200π3\frac{200\pi}{3}3200π​ cubic centimeters. It consists of a right cylinder and two hemispheres of that radius. Find its full tip-to-tip length.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The two hemispheres contribute 32 pi divided by 3 cubic centimeters.

    4πh=200π3−32π34\pi h=\frac{200\pi}{3}-\frac{32\pi}{3}4πh=3200π​−332π​

    The cylinder length is 14 cm. Add two radius-length ends to obtain full length 18 cm.

  3. 3

    A rectangular block has a face 8 cm by 6 cm and unknown perpendicular thickness h. A radius-2 cm cylindrical bore passes through that thickness, strictly inside the face. The retained material volume is (336−28π)(336-28\pi)(336−28π) cubic centimeters. Find h.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    For each centimeter of thickness, the retained cross-sectional area is 48 minus 4 pi square centimeters.

    (48−4π)h=336−28π(48-4\pi)h=336-28\pi(48−4π)h=336−28π

    The right side is seven times the positive cross-sectional area, so h is 7 cm.

  4. 4

    A side-10 cm cube has a radius-3 cm cylindrical blind cavity drilled 4 cm down from its top, strictly inside that top face. Find all exposed area, including the cavity wall and floor as well as the cube's underside.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The opening removes one disk, while the cavity floor adds one disk of the same radius. These cancel in the area count.

    S=600−9π+9π+2π(3)(4)S=600-9\pi+9\pi+2\pi(3)(4)S=600−9π+9π+2π(3)(4)

    The area is (600+24π)(600+24\pi)(600+24π) square centimeters.

    A right circular cylinder of radius 4 cm has one same-radius hemisphere attached to an end. The exposed boundary consists of the cylinder curved side and hemisphere curved side only; the other flat end is covered by a support. That exposed area is 96π96\pi96π square centimeters. Find the cylinder length.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    There is no included flat disk.

    2π(4)h+2π(4)2=96π2\pi(4)h+2\pi(4)^2=96\pi2π(4)h+2π(4)2=96π

    The hemisphere curve uses 32 pi square centimeters, leaving 64 pi for the cylinder side. Its length is 8 cm.

    A rectangular box has perpendicular base edges 6 cm and 10 cm. Its space diagonal is 14 cm. What is its exact height?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Subtract both squared base edges from the squared space diagonal.

    h2=142−62−102h^2=14^2-6^2-10^2h2=142−62−102

    The square is 60 square centimeters. The positive height is 2152\sqrt{15}215​ cm.

    Two solid parts meet along one matching rectangle 5 cm by 3 cm. Their interiors do not overlap and there is no other contact. The assembled exterior area is 216 square centimeters. What was the sum of their separate total surface areas?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Assembly hides two copies of the 15-square-centimeter join. Restore both to recover the separate total.

    S1+S2=216+2(5)(3)S_1+S_2=216+2(5)(3)S1​+S2​=216+2(5)(3)

    The original sum was 246 square centimeters.

  9. 9

    A right stepped prism has constant depth 3 cm. Its lower block is 10 cm wide and 4 cm high. An upper block of width 4 cm adds unknown height x over the left side. Its total volume is 276 cubic centimeters. Find the added height x.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The lower block occupies 120 cubic centimeters. The added block has base area 12 square centimeters.

    12x=276−12012x=276-12012x=276−120

    Its volume is 156 cubic centimeters, so the added height is 13 cm.

    A solid hemisphere of radius 5 cm is joined by its entire flat disk to the base of a right cone of radius 5 cm and perpendicular height 12 cm. Find the exterior area.

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The cone slant height is 13 cm by Pythagoras. Only the two curved surfaces are exposed.

    S=2π(5)2+π(5)(13)S=2\pi(5)^2+\pi(5)(13)S=2π(5)2+π(5)(13)

    The exterior area is 115π115\pi115π square centimeters.
