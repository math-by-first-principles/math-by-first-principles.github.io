    A right cone of radius 4 cm and perpendicular height 3 cm is attached to one end of a right circular cylinder of radius 4 cm and height 6 cm. Find the whole volume.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The pieces meet only at their bases. Add the cylinder volume and one third of the matching cone base-height product.

    V=π(4)2(6)+13π(4)2(3)V=\pi(4)^2(6)+\frac13\pi(4)^2(3)V=π(4)2(6)+31​π(4)2(3)

    The whole volume is 112π112\pi112π cubic centimeters.

    A right pipe has outer radius 7 cm, inner radius 5 cm, and length 4 cm. Both ends are open. Find the area of all exposed material surfaces, including the bore wall and both annuli.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The outer and inner walls contribute separately, and the end rings occur twice.

    S=2π(7)(4)+2π(5)(4)+2π(49−25)\begin{aligned}S&=2\pi(7)(4)+2\pi(5)(4)\\&\quad+2\pi(49-25)\end{aligned}S​=2π(7)(4)+2π(5)(4)+2π(49−25)​

    The exposed area is 144π144\pi144π square centimeters.

  3. 3

    A 12 cm by 9 cm by 5 cm block has a radius-2 cm bore through its 5 cm thickness, perpendicular to and strictly inside the 12 cm by 9 cm faces. Find all exposed area, including the bore wall and underside.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The original box area is 426 square centimeters. Remove two opening disks and add the bore wall.

    S=426−2π(2)2+2π(2)(5)S=426-2\pi(2)^2+2\pi(2)(5)S=426−2π(2)2+2π(2)(5)

    The result is (426+12π)(426+12\pi)(426+12π) square centimeters.

    A solid cylinder of radius 5 cm has one same-radius solid hemispherical end. Its total volume is 700π3\frac{700\pi}{3}3700π​ cubic centimeters. Find the cylinder length.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The hemisphere contributes 250 pi divided by 3 cubic centimeters.

    25πh=700π3−250π325\pi h=\frac{700\pi}{3}-\frac{250\pi}{3}25πh=3700π​−3250π​

    The cylinder volume is 150 pi cubic centimeters, so the length is 6 cm.

    A solid capsule has radius 3 cm and full tip-to-tip length 18 cm. It consists of one right cylinder and two hemispheres. Find its exterior area.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The cylinder length is 12 cm after subtracting both radius-length ends.

    S=2π(3)(12)+4π(3)2S=2\pi(3)(12)+4\pi(3)^2S=2π(3)(12)+4π(3)2

    The exterior area is 108π108\pi108π square centimeters. No joining disk is exposed.

  6. 6

    A rectangular box has a base diagonal of 15 cm and height 8 cm. What is a space diagonal length?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The base diagonal and vertical height are perpendicular. They are the legs of a right triangle whose hypotenuse is the space diagonal.

    d2=152+82d^2=15^2+8^2d2=152+82

    The squared length is 289 square centimeters, so the diagonal is 17 cm.

  7. 7

    A right circular pipe has outer radius 5 cm, inner radius 4 cm, and material volume 108π108\pi108π cubic centimeters. Find its length.

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The annular end area is 9 pi square centimeters.

    9πh=108π9\pi h=108\pi9πh=108π

    Dividing by the positive annular area gives length 12 cm.

  8. 8

    A cube of side 6 cm has a side-2 cm cube cut from one corner, with the three cuts parallel to the cube faces. Find the total area of the remaining boundary, including all new cut faces.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The cut removes three squares of area 4 square centimeters from the original boundary. It creates three new squares of the same area.

    S=6(62)−3(22)+3(22)S=6(6^2)-3(2^2)+3(2^2)S=6(62)−3(22)+3(22)

    The boundary area remains 216 square centimeters.

  9. 9

    A cylinder has total surface area 32π32\pi32π square centimeters. Its radius is 2 cm. It is attached by one full circular base to a flat face of a solid box whose total surface area was 70 square centimeters. Their interiors do not overlap, and the cylinder has no other contact with the box. Find the assembly's exterior area.

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The common disk has area 4 pi square centimeters and occurs in each original surface total.

    S=70+32π−2(4π)S=70+32\pi-2(4\pi)S=70+32π−2(4π)

    The exterior area is (70+24π)(70+24\pi)(70+24π) square centimeters.

  10. 10

    A rectangular box has base edges 9 cm and 12 cm, and space diagonal 25 cm. Find its height.

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    First the base diagonal squared is 225 square centimeters. Then subtract that square from the space-diagonal square.

    h2=252−92−122h^2=25^2-9^2-12^2h2=252−92−122

    The height squared is 400 square centimeters, so the positive height is 20 cm.

  11. 11

    A solid right circular cylinder has outer radius 4 cm and height 9 cm. A coaxial cylindrical cavity of radius 3 cm extends 7 cm down from the top and stops before reaching the bottom. Find the remaining material volume.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The cavity is not a through hole. Its removed height is 7 cm.

    V=π(4)2(9)−π(3)2(7)V=\pi(4)^2(9)-\pi(3)^2(7)V=π(4)2(9)−π(3)2(7)

    The remaining material volume is 81π81\pi81π cubic centimeters.

  12. 12

    A closed solid consists of a right square prism with base side 6 cm and height 4 cm, topped by a regular square pyramid of base side 6 cm and perpendicular height 4 cm. The matching square bases are fully joined. Find all exterior area, including the bottom.

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The pyramid face slant height is 5 cm, from the right triangle with legs 3 cm and 4 cm.

    S=4(6)(4)+62+4(6)(5)/2S=4(6)(4)+6^2+4(6)(5)/2S=4(6)(4)+62+4(6)(5)/2

    The prism sides, bottom, and four triangular roof faces total 192 square centimeters. The joined square is excluded.
