  1. 1

    Which length is the slant height of a regular pyramid?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Slant height lies within a triangular side face and is perpendicular to that face's base edge. The interior perpendicular height and an apex-to-vertex edge are different segments.

    Solution

    Add the triangle areas by using their common altitude and total base length.

    A regular square pyramid has base side 444 cm and slant height 777 cm. Find its closed total surface area.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The four triangles have total area 565656 square centimeters. Add one square base.

    L=12(16)(7)S=56+16S=72 cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=\tfrac12(16)(7)\\S&=56+16\\S&=72\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LSS​=21​(16)(7)=56+16=72 cm2.​
  4. 4

    When a right cone's curved side is unfolded, which cone length becomes the sector radius?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The straight apex-to-rim segments become radii of the sector. Each has length equal to the cone's slant height.

    Solution

    C=2π(6)C=12π cm.\begin{aligned}C&=2\pi(6)\\C&=12\pi\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}CC​=2π(6)=12π cm.​

    A right cone has radius 444 cm and slant height 111111 cm. Find its lateral area.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The curved side is the lateral surface.

    L=π(4)(11)L=44π cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=\pi(4)(11)\\L&=44\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LL​=π(4)(11)=44π cm2.​

  8. 8

    A pyramid's triangular side faces have different altitudes. Which method is justified for its lateral area?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Each face is a triangle and has its own area. There is no common face altitude to factor from the sum, so one selected altitude cannot be used for them all.

  9. 9

    A right cone has radius 999 cm and slant height 151515 cm. Its base disk is removed while the curved side is kept. How much surface area is removed?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Only the base disk is removed. Its area is

    B=π(9)2B=81π cm2.\begin{aligned}B&=\pi(9)^2\\B&=81\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}BB​=π(9)2=81π cm2.​
  10. 10

    Which statement correctly describes a nondegenerate right circular cone?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    In half of a right cone's axial section, the height meets the base radius at a right angle. The apex-to-rim segment is opposite that right angle and is the hypotenuse.

    An ideal right-conical shell has its entire curved side and no base disk. Which expression gives its single-layer material area?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution
