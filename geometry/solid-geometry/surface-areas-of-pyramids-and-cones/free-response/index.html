  1. Problem 1 The five pieces

    A regular square pyramid has base side 888 cm and slant height 666 cm.

    Find its lateral area and its total surface area when closed.

    Text description of this figure

    Complete net of a regular square pyramid. The central square has side 8 cm. Four congruent triangles attach, one to each square edge. A dotted perpendicular within the top triangle labels face altitude 6 cm. Dashed square edges are folds.

    1. Hint 1

      Identify which piece is the base and which pieces are lateral faces.

    2. Hint 2

      Each of the four triangular faces has base 888 cm and altitude 666 cm.

    Answer

    L=96 cm2S=160 cm2\begin{gathered}L=96\text{ cm}^2\\S=160\text{ cm}^2\end{gathered}L=96 cm2S=160 cm2​

    Full solution

    Each triangle has area 242424 square centimeters.

    There are four side triangles, so

    L=4(24)L=96 cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=4(24)\\L&=96\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LL​=4(24)=96 cm2.​

    The square base has area 646464 square centimeters.

    A closed pyramid includes it once.

    S=96+64S=160 cm2.\begin{aligned}S&=96+64\\S&=160\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}SS​=96+64=160 cm2.​

    Answer

    L=96 cm2S=160 cm2\begin{gathered}L=96\text{ cm}^2\\S=160\text{ cm}^2\end{gathered}L=96 cm2S=160 cm2​

    Key idea

    A closed pyramid includes its triangular sides and one base.

  2. Problem 2 Two roles for height

    A regular square pyramid has base side 181818 cm and perpendicular height 121212 cm.

    Find its slant height and lateral area.

    Text description of this figure

    True right triangle VOM in a regular pyramid. VO is perpendicular height 12 cm; OM is base apothem 9 cm; VM is slant height ℓ. The right angle is at O.

    1. Hint 1

      The perpendicular height is not the altitude of a side face.

    2. Hint 2

      The base center is 999 cm from a side midpoint.

    3. Hint 3

      Use the right triangle from apex to center to midpoint.

    Answer

    ℓ=15 cmL=540 cm2\begin{gathered}\ell=15\text{ cm}\\L=540\text{ cm}^2\end{gathered}ℓ=15 cmL=540 cm2​

    Full solution

    The center-to-midpoint distance is half the square side, or 999 cm.

    In triangle VOM, the height and this distance are perpendicular legs.

    The slant height is the positive hypotenuse.

    ℓ2=122+92ℓ=15 cm.\begin{aligned}\ell^2&=12^2+9^2\\\ell&=15\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}ℓ2ℓ​=122+92=15 cm.​

    The base perimeter is 727272 cm.

    Hence

    L=12(72)(15)L=540 cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=\tfrac12(72)(15)\\L&=540\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LL​=21​(72)(15)=540 cm2.​

    Answer

    ℓ=15 cmL=540 cm2\begin{gathered}\ell=15\text{ cm}\\L=540\text{ cm}^2\end{gathered}ℓ=15 cmL=540 cm2​

    Key idea

    The base apothem and perpendicular height determine the face altitude.

  3. Problem 3 The semicircular pattern

    A semicircular sheet has radius 181818 cm. Its two straight radii are joined without overlap to form a right cone, with the whole semicircular arc becoming the base rim.

    Find the cone's radius, perpendicular height, and lateral area exactly.

    Text description of this figure

    A circular sector with radius 18 cm and selected angle 180 degrees. The straight radii are cut edges.

    1. Hint 1

      Match the curved edge of the sheet to the entire cone rim.

    2. Hint 2

      The sheet radius becomes the cone slant height.

    3. Hint 3

      Find the rim circumference first, then use the cone's right triangle.

    Answer

    r=9 cmh=93 cmL=162π cm2\begin{gathered}r=9\text{ cm}\\h=9\sqrt3\text{ cm}\\L=162\pi\text{ cm}^2\end{gathered}r=9 cmh=93​ cmL=162π cm2​

    Full solution

    The semicircle arc is 18π18\pi18π cm long.

    That is the cone base circumference, so

    r=9 cm.r=9\text{ cm}.r=9 cm.

    The slant height is 181818 cm.

    The perpendicular height follows from

    h2=182−92h=93 cm.\begin{aligned}h^2&=18^2-9^2\\h&=9\sqrt3\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}h2h​=182−92=93​ cm.​

    The curved area equals the area of the original semicircle.

    L=12π(18)2L=162π cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=\tfrac12\pi(18)^2\\L&=162\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LL​=21​π(18)2=162π cm2.​

    Answer

    r=9 cmh=93 cmL=162π cm2\begin{gathered}r=9\text{ cm}\\h=9\sqrt3\text{ cm}\\L=162\pi\text{ cm}^2\end{gathered}r=9 cmh=93​ cmL=162π cm2​

  4. Problem 4 A hexagonal boundary

    A regular hexagonal pyramid has base side 101010 cm and slant height 141414 cm. Give exact areas for both cases.

    Case A: find the area of the boundary consisting of all six triangular side faces, excluding the hexagonal base.

    Case B: find the closed total surface area, including the hexagonal base.

    Text description of this figure

    A regular hexagonal base has side 10 cm. One congruent triangular face is attached to each of its six edges. The altitude of the top triangular face is 14 cm. Dashed base edges are folds. The base polygon is excluded in Case A and included in Case B.

    1. Hint 1

      List the surfaces included in each case.

    2. Hint 2

      The six triangle bases total 606060 cm.

    3. Hint 3

      The hexagon separates into six equilateral triangles of side 101010 cm.

    Answer

    A: 420 cm2B: (420+1503) cm2\begin{gathered}\text{A: }420\text{ cm}^2\\\text{B: }(420+150\sqrt3)\text{ cm}^2\end{gathered}A: 420 cm2B: (420+1503​) cm2​

    Full solution

    Case A: all six side triangles share altitude 141414 cm.

    L=12(60)(14)L=420 cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=\tfrac12(60)(14)\\L&=420\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LL​=21​(60)(14)=420 cm2.​

    Case B: each equilateral base triangle has area 25325\sqrt3253​ square centimeters.

    The full hexagonal base has area 1503150\sqrt31503​ square centimeters.

    Case B includes the same sides and this one base, for total area (420+1503)(420+150\sqrt3)(420+1503​) square centimeters.

    Answer

    A: 420 cm2B: (420+1503) cm2\begin{gathered}\text{A: }420\text{ cm}^2\\\text{B: }(420+150\sqrt3)\text{ cm}^2\end{gathered}A: 420 cm2B: (420+1503​) cm2​

    Key idea

    Adding a base changes the included area by precisely that base area.

  5. Problem 5 The shade blank

    An ideal right-conical shade has base radius 777 cm and perpendicular height 95\sqrt{95}95​ cm. It includes its curved side and no base disk.

    Find the sheet area and the exact sector angle of a pattern that covers this shade without overlap. Ignore seams and thickness.

    Text description of this figure

    True right triangle VOM in a right cone. VO is perpendicular height √95 cm; OM is radius 7 cm; VM is slant height ℓ. The right angle is at O.

    1. Hint 1

      The sheet is the curved side, so its radius is the slant height.

    2. Hint 2

      Add the squared radius to the squared perpendicular height.

    3. Hint 3

    Thus

    ℓ2=49+95ℓ=12 cm.\begin{aligned}\ell^2&=49+95\\\ell&=12\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}ℓ2ℓ​=49+95=12 cm.​

    The included area is only the curved side.

    L=π(7)(12)L=84π cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=\pi(7)(12)\\L&=84\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LL​=π(7)(12)=84π cm2.​

    The sector radius is 121212 cm, and its arc must be 14π14\pi14π cm.

    Its angle is

    θ=360∘⋅712θ=210∘.\begin{aligned}\theta&=360^\circ\cdot\frac7{12}\\\theta&=210^\circ.\end{aligned}θθ​=360∘⋅127​=210∘.​

    Answer

    L=84π cm2θ=210∘\begin{gathered}L=84\pi\text{ cm}^2\\\theta=210^\circ\end{gathered}L=84π cm2θ=210∘​

  6. Problem 6 A side-face edge

    A regular square pyramid has apex-to-base-vertex edges 262626 cm long. Each triangular side face has altitude 242424 cm.

    Find the square base side and the pyramid's closed total surface area.

    1. Hint 1

      Work within one isosceles triangular face.

    2. Hint 2

      The face altitude bisects the base edge.

    3. Hint 3

      The half-edge and face altitude are legs with hypotenuse 262626 cm.

    Answer

    s=20 cmS=1360 cm2\begin{gathered}s=20\text{ cm}\\S=1360\text{ cm}^2\end{gathered}s=20 cmS=1360 cm2​

    Full solution

    In half of a triangular face, the base half-edge has square

    (s/2)2=262−242.(s/2)^2=26^2-24^2.(s/2)2=262−242.

    Thus the half-edge is 101010 cm and the full base side is 202020 cm.

    The base perimeter is 808080 cm.

    The four triangles have total area

    L=12(80)(24)L=960 cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=\tfrac12(80)(24)\\L&=960\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LL​=21​(80)(24)=960 cm2.​

    The square base adds 400400400 square centimeters.

    Therefore

    S=1360 cm2.S=1360\text{ cm}^2.S=1360 cm2.

    Answer

    s=20 cmS=1360 cm2\begin{gathered}s=20\text{ cm}\\S=1360\text{ cm}^2\end{gathered}s=20 cmS=1360 cm2​

    Key idea

    A face edge is a hypotenuse in a different triangle from the solid height.

  7. Problem 7 The two altitude pairs

    A pyramid has a square base of side 121212 cm. Two of its side-face altitudes are 52\sqrt{52}52​ cm and the other two are 28\sqrt{28}28​ cm.

    Find its lateral area exactly. Explain why half the base perimeter times one of the listed altitudes is not justified.

    Text description of this figure

    Two triangular face types. Each type occurs twice. The first has base 12 cm and altitude √52 cm. The second has base 12 cm and altitude √28 cm. Dotted altitudes are perpendicular to the face bases.

    1. Hint 1

      Compute each pair of triangular face areas separately.

    2. Hint 2

      Each face has base 121212 cm, but the face altitudes differ.

    Answer

    12(52+28) cm212(\sqrt{52}+\sqrt{28})\text{ cm}^212(52​+28​) cm2; unequal face altitudes prevent using either as a common altitude.

    Full solution

    Two triangles have altitude 52\sqrt{52}52​ cm.

    Their combined area is 125212\sqrt{52}1252​ square centimeters.

    The other two have altitude 28\sqrt{28}28​ cm.

    Their combined area is 122812\sqrt{28}1228​ square centimeters.

    Adding the pairs gives

    L=12(52+28) cm2.L=12(\sqrt{52}+\sqrt{28})\text{ cm}^2.L=12(52​+28​) cm2.

    The regular-pyramid shortcut factors out one shared face altitude.

    These face altitudes are unequal, so that factorization does not apply.

    Answer

    12(52+28) cm212(\sqrt{52}+\sqrt{28})\text{ cm}^212(52​+28​) cm2; unequal face altitudes prevent using either as a common altitude.

    Key idea

    The sum of actual face areas remains valid when a common-altitude shortcut does not.

  8. Problem 8 Every allowed sector

    A circular sector has positive radius RRR cm and angle θ\thetaθ degrees, strictly between 000 and 360360360. Its two straight radii are joined to form a right cone without overlap, using the whole arc as the base rim.

    Let rrr denote the cone base radius and LLL its lateral area. Derive expressions for both in terms of RRR and θ\thetaθ. Explain why the cone has positive perpendicular height.

    1. Hint 1

      The sheet radius becomes the slant height.

    2. Hint 2

      Match the sector arc to the base circumference.

    3. Hint 3

      Use the strict angle bounds to compare the cone radius with its slant height.

    Answer

    Full solution

    It makes a cone of radius 444 cm and slant height 161616 cm, with lateral area 64π64\pi64π square centimeters.

    For general R and theta, match the whole selected arc to the base circumference.

    2πr=θ360⋅2πRr=θR360.\begin{aligned}2\pi r&=\frac{\theta}{360}\cdot2\pi R\\r&=\frac{\theta R}{360}.\end{aligned}2πrr​=360θ​⋅2πR=360θR​.​

    The curved area is exactly the sector area.

    L=θ360πR2.L=\frac{\theta}{360}\pi R^2.L=360θ​πR2.

    The angle fraction is strictly between zero and one, so the base radius is strictly between zero and R.

    The slant height is R, giving positive square difference R2−r2R^2-r^2R2−r2 and therefore positive perpendicular height.

    Answer

    r=θR360L=θ360πR2\begin{gathered}r=\frac{\theta R}{360}\\L=\frac{\theta}{360}\pi R^2\end{gathered}r=360θR​L=360θ​πR2​

  9. Problem 9 Two linked surface records

    A closed right cone has total surface area 252π cm2252\pi\text{ cm}^2252π cm2. Its lateral area is 108π cm2108\pi\text{ cm}^2108π cm2 greater than its base area.

    Find its base radius, slant height, and perpendicular height exactly. Check both surface records.

    1. Hint 1

      Use the total and the difference to separate base area from lateral area.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the lateral area as the base area plus 108π108\pi108π.

    3. Hint 3

      After finding the radius, reverse the lateral-area formula.

    Answer

    r=62 cmℓ=152 cmh=342 cm\begin{gathered}r=6\sqrt2\text{ cm}\\\ell=15\sqrt2\text{ cm}\\h=3\sqrt{42}\text{ cm}\end{gathered}r=62​ cmℓ=152​ cmh=342​ cm​

    Full solution

    Let B be base area and L be lateral area.

    The two records give

    L+B=252πL−B=108π.\begin{aligned}L+B&=252\pi\\L-B&=108\pi.\end{aligned}L+BL−B​=252π=108π.​

    Adding gives lateral area 180π180\pi180π square centimeters, leaving base area 72π72\pi72π square centimeters.

    The base radius is the positive solution of

    r2=72.r^2=72.r2=72.

    Hence the radius is 626\sqrt262​ cm.

    Reverse the lateral-area product.

    ℓ=18062ℓ=152 cm.\begin{aligned}\ell&=\frac{180}{6\sqrt2}\\\ell&=15\sqrt2\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}ℓℓ​=62​180​=152​ cm.​

    The slant height exceeds the radius.

    Subtract their squares.

    h2=450−72h=342 cm.\begin{aligned}h^2&=450-72\\h&=3\sqrt{42}\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}h2h​=450−72=342​ cm.​

    The recovered base area is 72π72\pi72π and the lateral area is 180π180\pi180π, in square centimeters.

    Their sum is 252π252\pi252π and their difference is 108π108\pi108π, as required.

    Answer

    r=62 cmℓ=152 cmh=342 cm\begin{gathered}r=6\sqrt2\text{ cm}\\\ell=15\sqrt2\text{ cm}\\h=3\sqrt{42}\text{ cm}\end{gathered}r=62​ cmℓ=152​ cmh=342​ cm​

    Separate the included areas before recovering lengths from them.

  10. Problem 10 Rebuild the pyramid

    A closed regular square pyramid has total area 800 cm2800\text{ cm}^2800 cm2. Its lateral area is three times its base area.

    Find the base side, slant height, and perpendicular height exactly.

    Check that the recovered side faces have the required lateral area and that the solid has positive height.

    1. Hint 1

      First divide the total into one base share and three lateral shares.

    2. Hint 2

      Once the square side is known, its perimeter determines the slant height.

    3. Hint 3

      The square apothem is half its side.

    Answer

    s=102 cmℓ=152 cmh=20 cm\begin{gathered}s=10\sqrt2\text{ cm}\\\ell=15\sqrt2\text{ cm}\\h=20\text{ cm}\end{gathered}s=102​ cmℓ=152​ cmh=20 cm​

    Lateral area 600 cm2600\text{ cm}^2600 cm2; height is positive.

    Full solution

    The total consists of four equal area shares, one for the base and three for the sides.

    The base area is 200200200 square centimeters and the lateral area is 600600600 square centimeters.

    The positive square side is

    s=102 cm.s=10\sqrt2\text{ cm}.s=102​ cm.

    The perimeter is 40240\sqrt2402​ cm.

    Use the lateral-area relation to recover slant height.

    600=12(402)ℓℓ=152 cm.\begin{aligned}600&=\tfrac12(40\sqrt2)\ell\\\ell&=15\sqrt2\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}600ℓ​=21​(402​)ℓ=152​ cm.​

    The apothem is 525\sqrt252​ cm.

    The perpendicular height satisfies

    h2=(152)2−(52)2h2=400h=20 cm.\begin{aligned}h^2&=(15\sqrt2)^2-(5\sqrt2)^2\\h^2&=400\\h&=20\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}h2h2h​=(152​)2−(52​)2=400=20 cm.​

    Each face has area 150150150 square centimeters, because half of (102)(152)(10\sqrt2)(15\sqrt2)(102​)(152​) is 150150150.

    Four faces give the required 600600600 square centimeters, and adding the 200200200 square-centimeter base returns 800800800.

    The height is positive, so the dimensions describe a nondegenerate regular pyramid.

    Answer

    s=102 cmℓ=152 cmh=20 cm\begin{gathered}s=10\sqrt2\text{ cm}\\\ell=15\sqrt2\text{ cm}\\h=20\text{ cm}\end{gathered}s=102​ cmℓ=152​ cmh=20 cm​

    Lateral area 600 cm2600\text{ cm}^2600 cm2; height is positive.

    Reconstruction needs both the area relations and a compatible perpendicular height.
