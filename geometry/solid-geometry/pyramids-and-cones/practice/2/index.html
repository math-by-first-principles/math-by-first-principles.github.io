  1. 1

    A pyramid has a trapezoidal base with parallel sides 777 cm and 131313 cm, separated perpendicularly by 444 cm within the base. Its perpendicular solid height is 999 cm. Find its volume.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The base area is the average of its parallel lengths times their separation.

    B=7+132(4).B=\frac{7+13}{2}(4).B=27+13​(4).

    This gives 404040 square centimeters. Use the 999 cm solid height.

    V=13(40)(9).V=\frac13(40)(9).V=31​(40)(9).

    The volume is 120120120 cubic centimeters.

    A right circular cone has radius 555 cm and slant height 131313 cm. Find its exact volume.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Radius and perpendicular height are legs; slant height is the hypotenuse.

    h2=132−52h=12 cm.\begin{aligned}h^2&=13^2-5^2\\h&=12\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}h2h​=132−52=12 cm.​

    Use the recovered height.

    V=13π(5)2(12)V=100π cm3.\begin{aligned}V&=\frac13\pi(5)^2(12)\\V&=100\pi\text{ cm}^3.\end{aligned}VV​=31​π(5)2(12)=100π cm3.​

    A circular cone has volume 54π54\pi54π cubic cm and perpendicular height 181818 cm. Find its radius.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Solve first for the squared radius.

    54π=13πr2(18)r2=9.\begin{aligned}54\pi&=\frac13\pi r^2(18)\\r^2&=9.\end{aligned}54πr2​=31​πr2(18)=9.​

    The positive radius is 333 cm.

  6. 6

    A cone and a pyramid each have base area 606060 square units. The cone has perpendicular height 121212 units; the pyramid has height 999 units. What is the ratio of cone volume to pyramid volume?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Both volume expressions contain the same factor, one third of sixty. The ratio is therefore the ratio of the two perpendicular heights.

    VconeVpyramid=129.\frac{V_{\text{cone}}}{V_{\text{pyramid}}}=\frac{12}{9}.Vpyramid​Vcone​​=912​.

    In simplest form this is 4:34:34:3.

    An oblique circular cone has base radius 666 cm. Its apex is 777 cm perpendicularly from the base plane and 999 cm from the base center along a slanted segment. What is its exact volume?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    V=13π(6)2(7).V=\frac13\pi(6)^2(7).V=31​π(6)2(7).

    The volume is 84π84\pi84π cubic centimeters.

  11. 11

    A circular cone has perpendicular height 121212 cm. A base-parallel section 444 cm below its apex has radius 333 cm. What is the whole cone volume?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The section-to-base length fraction is one third. Therefore the base radius is three times the section radius, or nine centimeters.

    V=13π(9)2(12).V=\frac13\pi(9)^2(12).V=31​π(9)2(12).

    The volume is 324π324\pi324π cubic centimeters.

  12. 12

    A cone and a pyramid have equal positive base areas B and equal perpendicular heights h. At distance z above their base planes, which expression gives the section area for both, throughout the height?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The distance down from the apex is h minus z. Divide that distance by h and square for area.

    A(z)=B(1−zh)2.A(z)=B\left(1-\frac zh\right)^2.A(z)=B(1−hz​)2.

    This equality holds at every corresponding height, which supports a Cavalieri comparison.
