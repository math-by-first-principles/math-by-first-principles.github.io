  1. 1

    A pyramid has base area 242424 square cm and perpendicular height 777 cm. Find its volume.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Use one third of base area times perpendicular height.

    V=13(24)(7).V=\frac13(24)(7).V=31​(24)(7).

    The volume is 565656 cubic centimeters.

  2. 2

    A pyramid has a square base of side 555 cm and perpendicular height 999 cm. What is its volume?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Find the square base area first.

    B=25 cm2.B=25\text{ cm}^2.B=25 cm2.

    Then take one third of the matching prism volume.

    V=13(25)(9).V=\frac13(25)(9).V=31​(25)(9).

    The volume is 757575 cubic centimeters.

  3. 3

    A circular cone has radius 444 cm and perpendicular height 666 cm. Give its exact volume.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The base disk area is sixteen times pi square centimeters.

    V=13π(4)2(6).V=\frac13\pi(4)^2(6).V=31​π(4)2(6).

    The exact volume is 32π32\pi32π cubic centimeters.

  4. 4

    A circular cone has base diameter 101010 cm and perpendicular height 333 cm. What is its exact volume?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The radius is half the diameter, or 555 cm.

    V=13π(5)2(3).V=\frac13\pi(5)^2(3).V=31​π(5)2(3).

    The exact volume is 25π25\pi25π cubic centimeters.

  5. 5

    A pyramid has volume 424242 cubic cm and base area 181818 square cm. Find its perpendicular height.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Multiply the volume by three before dividing by base area.

    h=3(42)18.h=\frac{3(42)}{18}.h=183(42)​.

    The height is 777 cm.

  6. 6

    A cone has volume 48π48\pi48π cubic cm and perpendicular height 999 cm. Find its positive radius.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Substitute the volume and height.

    48π=13πr2(9).48\pi=\frac13\pi r^2(9).48π=31​πr2(9).

    Cancel pi and divide by three.

    r2=16.r^2=16.r2=16.

    Take the positive square root. The radius is 444 cm.

  7. 7

    Which measurement is the height in the volume formula for a pyramid?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The height measures separation perpendicular to the base plane. A side edge or a side-face altitude may be slanted relative to that plane.

  8. 8

    An oblique pyramid has base area 353535 square cm, perpendicular height 666 cm, and a slanted edge of 101010 cm. Find its volume.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Use the perpendicular height of 666 cm, not the 101010 cm edge.

    V=13(35)(6).V=\frac13(35)(6).V=31​(35)(6).

    The volume is 707070 cubic centimeters.

  9. 9

    A pyramid has base area 646464 square units. A plane parallel to its base passes halfway from the apex to the base plane. What is its section area?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The section lengths are half the base lengths. The area factor is one quarter.

    A=64(12)2.A=64\left(\frac12\right)^2.A=64(21​)2.

    The section area is 161616 square units.

  10. 10

    A cone and a cylinder have the same circular base and the same perpendicular height. What is the ratio of cone volume to cylinder volume?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The cylinder has volume BhBhBh. The cone occupies one third of that volume.

    VconeVcylinder=13.\frac{V_{\text{cone}}}{V_{\text{cylinder}}}=\frac13.Vcylinder​Vcone​​=31​.

    The requested order is cone to cylinder, so the ratio is 1:31:31:3.

  11. 11

    A pyramid has a right triangular base with legs 888 cm and 333 cm. Its perpendicular height is 101010 cm. Find its volume.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The triangular base area is half of eight times three.

    B=12 cm2.B=12\text{ cm}^2.B=12 cm2.

    Use the separate solid height.

    V=13(12)(10).V=\frac13(12)(10).V=31​(12)(10).

    The volume is 404040 cubic centimeters.

  12. 12

    A cone calculation multiplies a base area in square centimeters by a perpendicular height in centimeters and divides by three. Which unit belongs to its volume?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Square centimeters multiplied by centimeters give cubic centimeters. Dividing by the dimensionless number three does not change that unit.
