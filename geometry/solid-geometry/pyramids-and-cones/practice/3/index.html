    A pyramid has base area 727272 square cm and volume 360360360 cubic cm. Its apex-to-plane perpendicular foot is H. A point Q in the base plane has HQ of 888 cm and apex-to-Q distance 171717 cm. Which perpendicular height agrees with both records?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The volume determines the perpendicular height.

    h=3(360)72.h=\frac{3(360)}{72}.h=723(360)​.

    This gives fifteen centimeters. Its square plus the offset square is 225+64225+64225+64, which equals the square of seventeen. Both records therefore support 151515 cm.

    A right circular cone has perpendicular height 121212 cm and volume 256π256\pi256π cubic cm. What is its slant height?

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Use the volume to find the radius square.

    A pyramid has perpendicular height 121212 units. Its section parallel to the base, 444 units below the apex, has area 202020 square units. Find its whole volume.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The section has one third of the base lengths and one ninth of its area. Thus the base area is 180180180 square units.

    V=13(180)(12).V=\frac13(180)(12).V=31​(180)(12).

    The volume is 720720720 cubic units.

    A cone has radius 444 cm and perpendicular height 10.510.510.5 cm. A pyramid of equal volume has base area 21π21\pi21π square cm. Find the pyramid height.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The cone volume is fifty-six times pi cubic centimeters.

    V=13π(4)2(10.5).V=\frac13\pi(4)^2(10.5).V=31​π(4)2(10.5).

    Set the pyramid volume equal to it and solve.

    56π=13(21π)hh=8 cm.\begin{aligned}56\pi&=\frac13(21\pi)h\\h&=8\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}56πh​=31​(21π)h=8 cm.​
  5. 5

    A right square pyramid has base side 101010 cm, apex S directly above base center O, and M the midpoint of one base side. The side-face altitude SM is 131313 cm. What is the pyramid volume?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The center-to-midpoint distance is five centimeters. In right triangle SOM, the thirteen-centimeter face altitude is the hypotenuse.

    h2=132−52h=12 cm.\begin{aligned}h^2&=13^2-5^2\\h&=12\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}h2h​=132−52=12 cm.​

    The square base area is one hundred square centimeters.

    V=13(100)(12).V=\frac13(100)(12).V=31​(100)(12).

    The volume is 400400400 cubic centimeters.

  6. 6

    A prism side face BCFE is a parallelogram. Its diagonal BF splits equal-area triangles BCF and BEF. Point A lies outside that face plane. Why do tetrahedra ABCF and ABEF have equal volumes without assuming a pyramid-volume formula?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    View BCF and BEF as bases in the same plane, with common apex A. Their perpendicular heights agree. Each parallel section uses the same squared height fraction of equal base areas, so Cavalieri gives equal volumes. Neither base perimeter nor congruence is required, and one sampled section alone is insufficient.

    A pyramid has volume 808080 cubic cm and perpendicular height 101010 cm. Its right triangular base has one leg of unknown length and another of 666 cm. Find that base edge.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Recover the triangular base area from the volume.

    B=3(80)10.B=\frac{3(80)}{10}.B=103(80)​.

    This is twenty-four square centimeters. Let b be the requested edge length.

    A cone has base radius 666 cm and volume 120π120\pi120π cubic cm. A plane parallel to the base cuts a section of radius 333 cm. How far above the base plane is the cut?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The volume gives the whole height.

    h=3(120)36.h=\frac{3(120)}{36}.h=363(120)​.

    The height is ten centimeters. The section radius is half the base radius, so the section is five centimeters below the apex. Subtract from the whole height to get 555 cm above the base plane.
