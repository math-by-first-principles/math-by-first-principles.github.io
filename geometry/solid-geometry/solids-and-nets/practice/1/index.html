  1. 1

    Which description gives a prism?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A prism repeats the same polygon in a parallel plane and joins matching vertices.

    A pyramid has an apex instead of a second congruent base, and circular bases do not make a polyhedron.

  2. 2

    Which part of a polyhedron is an edge?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    An edge is a straight segment shared by faces.

    A corner is a vertex. A circular rim belongs to a curved-solid description, not to the straight-edge counts for polyhedra.

  3. 3

    How many faces, edges, and vertices does a cube have, in that order?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The top and bottom contribute two faces, and four faces join them. The two square boundaries contribute eight edges, and four more edges join corresponding corners.

    F=2+4E=4+4+4V=4+4\begin{aligned}F&=2+4\\E&=4+4+4\\V&=4+4\end{aligned}FEV​=2+4=4+4+4=4+4​

    Thus the counts are 6 faces, 12 edges, and 8 vertices.

  4. 4

    A triangular prism has two triangular bases. How many vertices does it have?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Each triangular base has three vertices. The two bases lie in distinct parallel planes, so none of their vertices coincide.

    V=2(3)V=2(3)V=2(3)

    There are six vertices.

  5. 5

    A square pyramid has one square base and an apex off its plane. How many faces does it have?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The base is one face. Each of its four edges joins the apex to make a triangular face.

    F=1+4F=1+4F=1+4

    There are five faces.

  6. 6

    Which property makes a prism right?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The base-plane relationship defines a right prism.

    Page orientation can change when the solid is turned, and the bases can be polygons other than squares.

  7. 7

    Which description distinguishes skew lines from parallel lines?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Skew lines do not lie in one plane.

    Parallel lines are coplanar. An apparent crossing in a projection does not determine whether the actual lines meet.

  8. 8

    Two distinct planes share two distinct points. What must their intersection be?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Each plane contains the complete line through the two points.

    Distinct intersecting planes have exactly one line in common, so the intersection continues beyond the segment shown.

  9. 9

    Why is a cylinder not a polyhedron?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A polyhedron has a surface made entirely of polygonal faces.

    A cylinder has a curved side and circular bases, so it fails that requirement.

  10. 10

    What must a net of a closed polyhedron do when folded?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A net supplies every face once and folds to the complete boundary.

    Cut edges can meet again on closing, but face interiors must not overlap.

  11. 11

    Which two dimensions does a front orthographic view record?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Looking from front toward back projects away the depth direction.

    The front view retains left-to-right width and vertical height.

  12. 12

    Which statement uses sphere and ball precisely?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A sphere consists of points at one fixed positive distance from its center.

    A ball also includes the points closer to the center, so it contains the enclosed region.
