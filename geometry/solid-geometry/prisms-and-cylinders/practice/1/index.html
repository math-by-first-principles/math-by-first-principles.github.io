  1. 1

    A prism has base area 212121 square cm and perpendicular height 444 cm. What is its volume?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Multiply the repeated base area by the perpendicular height.

    V=21(4)V=84 cm3.\begin{aligned}V&=21(4)\\V&=84\text{ cm}^3.\end{aligned}VV​=21(4)=84 cm3.​
  2. 2

    Which unit measures the volume of a prism whose lengths are measured in meters?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Base area contributes two length factors, and height contributes a third.

    m2⋅m=m3.\text{m}^2\cdot\text{m}=\text{m}^3.m2⋅m=m3.

    The resulting unit is cubic meters.

  3. 3

    An oblique prism has base area 161616 square cm, perpendicular height 999 cm, and joining edges of length 151515 cm. What is its volume?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Every base-parallel section has area 16 square cm. Use the 9 cm distance between base planes.

    V=16(9)V=144 cm3.\begin{aligned}V&=16(9)\\V&=144\text{ cm}^3.\end{aligned}VV​=16(9)=144 cm3.​
  4. 4

    A circular cylinder has radius 333 cm and perpendicular height 555 cm. What is its volume?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The base area is pi times the radius squared.

    B=9π cm2V=9π(5)V=45π cm3.\begin{aligned}B&=9\pi\text{ cm}^2\\V&=9\pi(5)\\V&=45\pi\text{ cm}^3.\end{aligned}BVV​=9π cm2=9π(5)=45π cm3.​
  5. 5

    A circular cylinder has diameter 101010 cm and perpendicular height 444 cm. What is its volume?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Halve the diameter before squaring the radius.

    r=5 cmV=π(5)2(4)V=100π cm3.\begin{aligned}r&=5\text{ cm}\\V&=\pi(5)^2(4)\\V&=100\pi\text{ cm}^3.\end{aligned}rVV​=5 cm=π(5)2(4)=100π cm3.​
  6. 6

    A right prism has base perimeter 262626 cm and height 333 cm. What is its lateral area?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The side rectangles share height 3 cm, and their widths total 26 cm.

    L=26(3)L=78 cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=26(3)\\L&=78\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LL​=26(3)=78 cm2.​
  7. 7

    A closed right prism has base area 181818 square cm, base perimeter 222222 cm, and height 555 cm. What is its total surface area?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Include the lateral rectangles and two bases.

    L=22(5)L=110 cm2S=110+2(18)S=146 cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=22(5)\\L&=110\text{ cm}^2\\S&=110+2(18)\\S&=146\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LLSS​=22(5)=110 cm2=110+2(18)=146 cm2.​
  8. 8

    A right circular cylinder has radius 444 cm and height 333 cm. What is the area of its curved side alone?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Unroll the side into a rectangle of width equal to the circumference.

    L=2π(4)(3)L=24π cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=2\pi(4)(3)\\L&=24\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LL​=2π(4)(3)=24π cm2.​
  9. 9

    A closed right circular cylinder has radius 222 cm and height 777 cm. What is its total surface area?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    L=28π cm22B=8π cm2S=36π cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=28\pi\text{ cm}^2\\2B&=8\pi\text{ cm}^2\\S&=36\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}L2BS​=28π cm2=8π cm2=36π cm2.​
  10. 10

    An ideal right cylindrical boundary has radius 333 cm and height 444 cm. It includes a bottom and the curved side, but no top. What is its area?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The missing top contributes no disk. Include one base.

    L=2π(3)(4)L=24π cm2S=24π+9πS=33π cm2.\begin{aligned}L&=2\pi(3)(4)\\L&=24\pi\text{ cm}^2\\S&=24\pi+9\pi\\S&=33\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}LLSS​=2π(3)(4)=24π cm2=24π+9π=33π cm2.​

  12. 12

    Which information lets Cavalieri's principle establish equal volumes for two ordinary solids between the same parallel planes?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Cavalieri compares section areas across the entire common perpendicular height. A single middle section, base perimeter, or outside area does not supply that condition.
