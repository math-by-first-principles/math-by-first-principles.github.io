    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Surface areas give the square of the length factor, not the volume factor.

    k=980/500,k=7/5,Vi=500(7/5)3,Vi=1372 cm3.\begin{aligned}k&=\sqrt{980/500},\\k&=7/5,\\V_i&=500(7/5)^3,\\V_i&=1372\text{ cm}^3.\end{aligned}kkVi​Vi​​=980/500​,=7/5,=500(7/5)3,=1372 cm3.​
