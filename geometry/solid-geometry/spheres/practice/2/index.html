  9. 9

    A ball is cut through its center into two solid hemispheres. How does the volume of either piece compare with the original volume?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The cut divides the ball into two congruent regions with nonoverlapping interiors. Each has half the original volume. The new flat boundary changes area, not the amount of either half.
