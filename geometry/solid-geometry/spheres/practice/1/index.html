Question 1 of 12
  1. 1

    Which description defines a sphere in this lesson?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A sphere consists of points at one fixed distance from its center. The filled region together with that surface is the ball.

  2. 2

    Which object includes both a sphere and the region it encloses?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A ball contains the boundary sphere and all points inside it. A great circle is only a circle on the surface.

  3. 3

    A sphere has radius 8 cm. What is the radius of a great circle?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A great-circle plane passes through the sphere center, so its circle has the full sphere radius, 8 cm.

  4. 4

    A ball has diameter 10 cm. What is its exact volume?

    Solution

    V=43π(5)3V=500π3 cm3.\begin{aligned}V&=\frac43\pi(5)^3\\V&=\frac{500\pi}{3}\text{ cm}^3.\end{aligned}VV​=34​π(5)3=3500π​ cm3.​
  5. 5

    A sphere has radius 4 cm. What is its surface area?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Square the radius and multiply by four pi.

    S=4π(4)2S=64π cm2.\begin{aligned}S&=4\pi(4)^2\\S&=64\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}SS​=4π(4)2=64π cm2.​
  6. 6

    What volume does a hemisphere of radius 3 cm enclose?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A half-ball has half the corresponding ball volume.

    VH=23π(3)3VH=18π cm3.\begin{aligned}V_H&=\frac23\pi(3)^3\\V_H&=18\pi\text{ cm}^3.\end{aligned}VH​VH​​=32​π(3)3=18π cm3.​
  7. 7

    A hemisphere has radius 6 cm. What is its curved area, excluding its flat base?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The curved cap is half the sphere surface.

    Sc=2π(6)2Sc=72π cm2.\begin{aligned}S_c&=2\pi(6)^2\\S_c&=72\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}Sc​Sc​​=2π(6)2=72π cm2.​
  8. 8

    A solid hemisphere has radius 2 cm. What is its total boundary area, including the flat base?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Include the curved cap and one disk.

    St=3π(2)2St=12π cm2.\begin{aligned}S_t&=3\pi(2)^2\\S_t&=12\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}St​St​​=3π(2)2=12π cm2.​
  9. 9

    A sphere has radius 2.5 cm. What is the circumference of a great circle?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The great circle has radius 2.5 cm. Use a circumference formula.

    C=2π(2.5)C=5π cm.\begin{aligned}C&=2\pi(2.5)\\C&=5\pi\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}CC​=2π(2.5)=5π cm.​
  10. 10

    A sphere radius is measured in centimeters. Which unit belongs to its surface area?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Surface area measures a two-dimensional surface, so its unit is square centimeters. Cubic centimeters measure enclosed volume.

  11. 11

    A solid hemisphere has radius 7 cm. What is the area of its flat base?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The base is one disk of radius 7 cm.

    B=π(7)2B=49π cm2.\begin{aligned}B&=\pi(7)^2\\B&=49\pi\text{ cm}^2.\end{aligned}BB​=π(7)2=49π cm2.​
  12. 12

    A sphere has radius 5 cm. A plane is 4 cm from its center, measured perpendicularly. What is the section circle radius?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Use the right triangle from the center to the plane and then to the section circle.

    ρ2=52−42ρ2=9ρ=3 cm.\begin{aligned}\rho^2&=5^2-4^2\\\rho^2&=9\\\rho&=3\text{ cm}.\end{aligned}ρ2ρ2ρ​=52−42=9=3 cm.​
