  8. 8

    In the informal radial pyramid explanation of sphere area, which step requires refinement rather than being exact for a fixed finite partition?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The curved patches are not flat. Their flat approximations have heights near the radius. Refinement is what motivates replacing their total contribution by one-third radius times sphere area. The other listed operations are exact under their stated conditions.
