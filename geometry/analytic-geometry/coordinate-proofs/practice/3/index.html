    A convex parallelogram is placed A(0,0), B(6,0), D(t,4), C(t+6,4). For which real t are its diagonals congruent?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Equate their squared lengths.

    (t+6)2+16=(t−6)2+1624t=0t=0.\begin{aligned}(t+6)^2+16&=(t-6)^2+16\\24t&=0\\t&=0.\end{aligned}(t+6)2+1624tt​=(t−6)2+16=0=0.​

    At this value the figure is a 6 by 4 rectangle. The height is nonzero for every t, so no degenerate candidate was introduced.

  2. 2

    A convex quadrilateral has A(-4,-2), B(2,1), C(6,6), D(0,3) in boundary order. Both diagonal midpoints are (1,2). Which additional computed fact rules out a rectangle?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Adjacent slopes are 1/2 and 5/4.

    mABmBC=5/8m_{AB}m_{BC}=5/8mAB​mBC​=5/8

    Both are defined and nonzero. Their product is not -1, so the adjacent angle is not right. Opposite-side equalities and diagonal bisection are already parallelogram properties.

  3. 3

    A triangle has A(-5,2), B(7,-4), and C(t,11). Its centroid lies on the vertical line x=3. What is t?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Only the first-coordinate average is constrained.

    (−5+7+t)/3=32+t=9t=7.\begin{aligned}(-5+7+t)/3&=3\\2+t&=9\\t&=7.\end{aligned}(−5+7+t)/32+tt​=3=9=7.​

    B and C then have the same first coordinate but different heights, and A is off their line, so the triangle remains nondegenerate.

  7. 7

    For positive a,b, a quadrilateral has A(-a,0), B(0,b), C(a,0), D(0,-b) in boundary order. Which condition exactly characterizes when it is a square?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    All four side squares are the sum of a squared and b squared, so this is a rhombus. Its diagonals have lengths 2a and 2b.

    For a rectangle and hence a square, these must agree.

    2a=2b2a=2b2a=2b a=b.a=b.a=b.
  8. 8

    A convex quadrilateral has congruent diagonals but their midpoints differ. What follows?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Every parallelogram has a common midpoint for its two diagonals. The failed necessary condition excludes that family.

    Rectangles are parallelograms, so it excludes rectangles as well. Congruent diagonal lengths cannot repair the missing bisection.

    A simple polygon has boundary vertices (0,0), (10,0), (10,6), (u,6), (u,2), (0,2), where 0<u<10. Its area is 44 square units. What is u?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    It contains a 10 by 2 lower rectangle and a right-hand upper rectangle of width 10-u and height 4.

    20+4(10−u)=4460−4u=44u=4.\begin{aligned}20+4(10-u)&=44\\60-4u&=44\\u&=4.\end{aligned}20+4(10−u)60−4uu​=44=44=4.​

    The answer lies strictly between 0 and 10, preserving the stated simple boundary.

  11. 11

    A coordinate proof shows that both pairs of opposite sides of a convex quadrilateral have equal squared lengths. Which conclusion is justified without further calculations?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Each side length is positive, so equality of squared lengths gives equality of the corresponding lengths.

    Both opposite-side pairs are therefore congruent. The earlier opposite-side test proves a parallelogram, but supplies no extra right angle or adjacent-side equality.

    p2=q2p^2=q^2p2=q2

    For positive side lengths p and q this gives

    p=q.p=q.p=q.
  12. 12

    Let a,c be positive and b real. A triangle has A(0,0), B(2a,0), C(2b,2c), and centroid G. When is the median from C perpendicular to AB?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The midpoint of AB is (a,0). Since AB is horizontal, its perpendicular median must be vertical. Thus C must have first coordinate a.

    2b=a2b=a2b=a b=a/2.b=a/2.b=a/2.

    The positive height keeps C distinct from that midpoint.
