    Triangles ABCABCABC and DEFDEFDEF have AB=DE=7AB=DE=7AB=DE=7, BC=EF=9BC=EF=9BC=EF=9, and CA=FD=12CA=FD=12CA=FD=12. Which method proves their congruence most directly from these data?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The three corresponding side equalities are already given, so SSS proves congruence without introducing coordinates. The lengths form nondegenerate triangles because 7+9>127+9>127+9>12.

    A coordinate proof could calculate side lengths if coordinates were given, but here that would add work. No right angle was supplied, and equal perimeters alone do not establish congruence.

  3. 3

    To prove a statement about every scalene triangle, which coordinate choice needs correction?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The axes meet at a right angle. Putting both sides on them requires the triangle to be right.

    A scalene triangle may be right, acute, or obtuse, so that placement does not cover the whole claim.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    The midpoints are M(1,4) and N(6,4).

    MN=6−1MN=6-1MN=6−1 MN=5.MN=5.MN=5.

    Their common height also shows that MN is parallel to AB.

    A convex quadrilateral has A(1,1), B(6,2), C(8,7), D(3,6) in boundary order. Both diagonal midpoints are (9/2,4). What does this calculation alone establish?

    The same point is the midpoint of each diagonal, so the diagonals bisect each other.

    The diagonal-bisection test establishes a parallelogram. It supplies no side equality or right-angle test.

  8. 8

    A triangle has vertices (-2,-1), (6,-1), and (1,4). What is its area?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The horizontal base has length 8. Its line is at height -1, and the opposite vertex has height 4, so the perpendicular height is 5.

    A=12(8)(5)A=\tfrac12(8)(5)A=21​(8)(5) A=20 square units.A=20\text{ square units}.A=20 square units.
  9. 9

    A simple pentagon has vertices (0,0), (6,0), (6,3), (4,5), and (0,3) in boundary order. Which decomposition gives its area?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The horizontal segment from (0,3) to (6,3) lies inside the pentagon.

    Below it is the 6 by 3 rectangle; above it is a triangle with base 6 and perpendicular height 2. The pieces fill the polygon without overlapping interiors.

    A=6(3)+12(6)(2).A=6(3)+\tfrac12(6)(2).A=6(3)+21​(6)(2).
  10. 10

    A coordinate calculation verifies a claim for one rectangle of width 9 and height 2. What is still needed to prove the claim for every rectangle?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A fixed width and height describe one instance.

    Arbitrary positive side lengths keep all nondegenerate rectangles in the argument; another picture of the same instance does not.

  11. 11

    A square is verified by coordinates. Which additional classification must also apply under the inclusive definitions?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A square has four right angles, so it meets the rectangle definition.

    It also has four congruent sides, so it meets the rhombus definition.

  12. 12

    A polygon is divided by an internal diagonal to calculate area. How is this diagonal treated in the outside perimeter?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    An internal diagonal is not part of the outside boundary.

    It helps identify area pieces, while perimeter follows only the exposed edges.
