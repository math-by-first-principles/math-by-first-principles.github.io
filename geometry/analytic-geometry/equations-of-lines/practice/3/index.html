    Vertex AAA lies on y=8y=8y=8. In triangle ABCABCABC,

    B=(−1,1),C=(5,4).\begin{aligned}B&=(-1,1),\\C&=(5,4).\end{aligned}BC​=(−1,1),=(5,4).​

    The altitude line from AAA passes through (2,2)(2,2)(2,2). What is AAA?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The side BCBCBC has slope 1/21/21/2, so the altitude has slope −2-2−2. Through (2,2)(2,2)(2,2) it satisfies

    y−2=−2(x−2),y=−2x+6.\begin{aligned}y-2&=-2(x-2),\\y&=-2x+6.\end{aligned}y−2y​=−2(x−2),=−2x+6.​

    At height 8,

    8=−2x+6,x=−1.\begin{aligned}8&=-2x+6,\\x&=-1.\end{aligned}8x​=−2x+6,=−1.​

    This point is not on the line of BCBCBC, so it gives a nondegenerate triangle.

    Solution
