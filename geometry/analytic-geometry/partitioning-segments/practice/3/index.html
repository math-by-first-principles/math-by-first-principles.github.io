    A is (−7,5)(-7,5)(−7,5) and B is (14,−9)(14,-9)(14,−9). An internal point P has first coordinate kkk and second coordinate 1. Find kkk and AP:PBAP:PBAP:PB.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The vertical fraction is -4/-14, or two sevenths. Use it in the horizontal coordinate.

    k=−7+27(21)=−1.\begin{aligned}k&=-7+\frac27(21)\\&=-1.\end{aligned}k​=−7+72​(21)=−1.​

    The common fraction is internal, and five sevenths remain, so the ratio is 2:5.

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The changes from A to P are 12 and -6, four sevenths of the full changes.

    xB=−9+74(12)=12,yB=4+74(−6)=−132.\begin{aligned}x_B&=-9+\tfrac74(12)\\&=12,\\y_B&=4+\tfrac74(-6)\\&=-\tfrac{13}{2}.\end{aligned}xB​yB​​=−9+47​(12)=12,=4+47​(−6)=−213​.​

    8−217−2=25,0−(−8)12−(−8)=25.\begin{aligned}\frac{8-2}{17-2}&=\frac25,\\\frac{0-(-8)}{12-(-8)}&=\frac25.\end{aligned}17−28−2​12−(−8)0−(−8)​​=52​,=52​.​

    A is (−4,−7)(-4,-7)(−4,−7) and B is (11,8)(11,8)(11,8). P has AP:PB=2:3AP:PB=2:3AP:PB=2:3 and Q has AQ:QB=4:1AQ:QB=4:1AQ:QB=4:1. What is PQ:ABPQ:ABPQ:AB?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    P is two fifths along AB and Q is four fifths along it, in the same direction. Their separation is two fifths of the whole.

    PQAB=45−25=25.\begin{aligned}\frac{PQ}{AB}&=\frac45-\frac25\\&=\frac25.\end{aligned}ABPQ​​=54​−52​=52​.​

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The first coordinate alone would give b=31/2. But the second coordinate required by the ratio is 7, regardless of b.

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Solution

    A is (−15,10)(-15,10)(−15,10), internal P is (−3,4)(-3,4)(−3,4), and B has second coordinate -8. What is B and the ordered ratio AP:PBAP:PBAP:PB?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The vertical fraction is -6/-18, or one third. The horizontal change 12 must also be one third of its whole change.

    xB=−15+3(12)=21.\begin{aligned}x_B&=-15+3(12)\\&=21.\end{aligned}xB​​=−15+3(12)=21.​

    Both coordinate fractions are one third, strictly internal. The leftover fraction is two thirds, giving ratio 1:2.
