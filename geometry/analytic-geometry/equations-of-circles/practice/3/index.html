  1. 1

    A circle is tangent to y=3y=3y=3 at T=(−4,3)T=(-4,3)T=(−4,3). Its center has height negative 2. Which equation describes it?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The horizontal tangent has a vertical contact radius, so the center shares first coordinate negative 4 with T. Its stated height is negative 2, making the radius 5.

    (x+4)2+(y+2)2=25.(x+4)^2+(y+2)^2=25.(x+4)2+(y+2)2=25.

    The alternatives give a different center or use the radius rather than its square. The recovered vertical radius is perpendicular to the given line at T.

  2. 2

    For what positive R do these graphs have exactly one common point?

    (x−1)2+(y−3)2=R,y=x−2.\begin{aligned}(x-1)^2+(y-3)^2&=R,\\y&=x-2.\end{aligned}(x−1)2+(y−3)2y​=R,=x−2.​
    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Substitute and complete the remaining square.

    (x−1)2+(x−5)2=R,2(x−3)2+8=R.\begin{aligned}(x-1)^2+(x-5)^2&=R,\\2(x-3)^2+8&=R.\end{aligned}(x−1)2+(x−5)22(x−3)2+8​=R,=R.​

    Exactly one real first coordinate occurs when R is 8, forcing x to be 3 and y to be 1. Smaller listed values give no real root; 16 gives two. The contact-radius slope is negative 1 and the line slope is 1, confirming tangency.

  3. 3

    Find the midpoint of the chord cut from this circle by the line.

    (x−3)2+(y+1)2=25,y=2.\begin{aligned}(x-3)^2+(y+1)^2&=25,\\y&=2.\end{aligned}(x−3)2+(y+1)2y​=25,=2.​
    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Substituting the height gives

    (x−3)2=16.(x-3)^2=16.(x−3)2=16.

    The endpoints are (-1,2) and (7,2). Averaging the first coordinates gives

    −1+72=3.\frac{-1+7}{2}=3.2−1+7​=3.

    Their common height remains 2. The point (3,-1) is the center, while the other choices are not halfway between the endpoints.

  4. 4

    The circle and line meet at (2,−3)(2,-3)(2,−3) and one other point. Find the other point.

    x2+y2=13,y=−2x+1.\begin{aligned}x^2+y^2&=13,\\y&=-2x+1.\end{aligned}x2+y2y​=13,=−2x+1.​
    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Substitute the line equation and factor.

    x2+(−2x+1)2=13,5x2−4x−12=0,(x−2)(5x+6)=0.\begin{aligned}x^2+(-2x+1)^2&=13,\\5x^2-4x-12&=0,\\(x-2)(5x+6)&=0.\end{aligned}x2+(−2x+1)25x2−4x−12(x−2)(5x+6)​=13,=0,=0.​

    The remaining first coordinate is negative 6/5; the line gives height 17/5. Its squared coordinate sum is

    3625+28925=13.\frac{36}{25}+\frac{289}{25}=13.2536​+25289​=13.

    The positive 6/5 choice satisfies the line but has squared sum 85/25, not 13. The point (-2,5) gives 29, and (2,3) fails the line.

  5. 5

    The vertical line is tangent to this positive-radius circle. Which q and contact point are correct?

    (x−2)2+(y+1)2=q,x=5.\begin{aligned}(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2&=q,\\x&=5.\end{aligned}(x−2)2+(y+1)2x​=q,=5.​
    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Substitute the fixed first coordinate.

    9+(y+1)2=q.9+(y+1)^2=q.9+(y+1)2=q.

    One real height occurs at q equal to 9, forcing y to be negative 1. The contact radius is horizontal, perpendicular to the vertical line. The value 3 gives no intersection; (2,-1) is the center rather than a line point; zero is not a positive squared radius.

  6. 6

    For which K is the x-axis tangent to x2+y2+6x+8y+K=0x^2+y^2+6x+8y+K=0x2+y2+6x+8y+K=0?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Complete the squares to recover the center.

    (x+3)2+(y+4)2=25−K.(x+3)^2+(y+4)^2=25-K.(x+3)2+(y+4)2=25−K.

    The x-axis is 4 units above the center. Tangency therefore requires squared radius 16.

    25−K=16.25-K=16.25−K=16.

    This gives K equal to 9, with contact (-3,0) and a vertical radius. At 25 the graph is one point off the axis; at 16 the radius is 3 and the axis misses; at 41 there are no real points.

  7. 7

    Find all points of the segment from (0,3)(0,3)(0,3) to (3,0)(3,0)(3,0) that lie on x2+y2=5x^2+y^2=5x2+y2=5.

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The segment lies on the line with equation

    x+y=3.x+y=3.x+y=3.

    Substitution into the circle gives

    x2+(3−x)2=5,(x−1)(x−2)=0.\begin{aligned}x^2+(3-x)^2&=5,\\(x-1)(x-2)&=0.\end{aligned}x2+(3−x)2(x−1)(x−2)​=5,=0.​

    The points (1,2) and (2,1) both lie between the stated endpoints and have squared coordinate sum 5. The endpoints have squared sum 9. The negative-height pairs fail the segment line.

  8. 8

    A circle has center C at (1,−1)(1,-1)(1,−1) and squared radius 20. The perpendicular foot from C to the line below is H at (23/5,4/5)(23/5,4/5)(23/5,4/5). How many intersections are there?

    2x+y=10.2x+y=10.2x+y=10.
    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The squared distance from C to the given foot is

    CH2=(185)2+(95)2,CH2=815.\begin{aligned}CH^2&=\left(\frac{18}5\right)^2+\left(\frac95\right)^2,\\CH^2&=\frac{81}5.\end{aligned}CH2CH2​=(518​)2+(59​)2,=581​.​

    This is less than 20. The perpendicular foot lies inside the circle, so the line crosses the circle on both sides of H. The numerical comparison and squared-distance values in the other choices are false.

  9. 9

    For which heights t does a horizontal line at height t have two intersections with this circle?

    x2+y2−12x+6y+29=0.x^2+y^2-12x+6y+29=0.x2+y2−12x+6y+29=0.
    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Complete the squares.

    (x−6)2+(y+3)2=16.(x-6)^2+(y+3)^2=16.(x−6)2+(y+3)2=16.

    After substituting the line height, two distinct first coordinates require a positive right side in

    (x−6)2=16−(t+3)2.(x-6)^2=16-(t+3)^2.(x−6)2=16−(t+3)2.

    The vertical gap must have magnitude less than 4, giving heights strictly between negative 7 and 1. At the endpoints there is one contact; beyond them there is none. The last interval uses heights measured from zero rather than the center height negative 3.

  10. 10

    The circle below is translated 3 units right and 4 units down. How does the fixed line below meet the translated circle?

    (x+2)2+(y−1)2=4,x=0.\begin{aligned}(x+2)^2+(y-1)^2&=4,\\x&=0.\end{aligned}(x+2)2+(y−1)2x​=4,=0.​
    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The new center is (1,-3) and the radius remains 2, giving

    (x−1)2+(y+3)2=4.(x-1)^2+(y+3)^2=4.(x−1)2+(y+3)2=4.

    At first coordinate zero,

    (y+3)2=3.(y+3)^2=3.(y+3)2=3.

    There are two distinct real heights. The fixed line is a secant and does not pass through the new center, whose first coordinate is 1.

  11. 11

    How many common points do these graphs have?

    (x−8)2+(y+2)2=0,y=3.\begin{aligned}(x-8)^2+(y+2)^2&=0,\\y&=3.\end{aligned}(x−8)2+(y+2)2y​=0,=3.​
    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The sum of squares can be zero only at (8,-2). That point has a different height from the line. Substitution confirms this.

    (x−8)2+25=0.(x-8)^2+25=0.(x−8)2+25=0.

    There is no real root. The zero-right-side graph is a point, so positive-radius circle tangency rules do not apply.

  12. 12

    A circle has radius 3. Its center lies on the horizontal line at height 1. It is tangent to the vertical line with first coordinate negative 2 and passes through (1,4)(1,4)(1,4). Which equation satisfies all conditions?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A center of height 1 must have first coordinate 3 units from the vertical tangent x equals negative 2. The possibilities are 1 and negative 5.

    For center (1,1), the squared distance to (1,4) is

    02+32=9.0^2+3^2=9.02+32=9.

    For center (-5,1), it is

    62+32=45.6^2+3^2=45.62+32=45.

    Only (1,1) works. The contact (-2,1) has a horizontal radius, perpendicular to the vertical tangent. The radius-9 option violates the radius condition, and the center-height-4 option violates the center line.
