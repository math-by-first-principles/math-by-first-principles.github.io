    What is the sum of the interior angles of a convex pentagon?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A convex pentagon divides into three triangles from one vertex.

    S=3×180∘S=3\times180^\circS=3×180∘ S=540∘S=540^\circS=540∘
  2. 2

    How many triangles does a convex nonagon form when every diagonal from one vertex is drawn?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    There are two fewer triangles than sides. A nonagon has nine sides.

    9−2=79-2=79−2=7

    An interior angle of a convex polygon measures 112∘112^\circ112∘. What is its exterior turn?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The interior angle and its exterior turn form a linear pair.

    e=180∘−112∘e=180^\circ-112^\circe=180∘−112∘ e=68∘e=68^\circe=68∘

    A convex polygon has eleven sides. What is the sum of exactly one exterior turn at every vertex, following its boundary in one direction?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The number of sides changes the interior sum, but one consistently chosen exterior turn per vertex totals one full turn.

    E=360∘E=360^\circE=360∘

    What is one exterior turn of a convex regular octagon?

    Solution

    Eight equal turns share the full turn.

    e=360∘÷8e=360^\circ\div8e=360∘÷8 e=45∘e=45^\circe=45∘

    What is one interior angle of a convex regular hexagon?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The exterior turn is one sixth of a full turn.

    e=60∘e=60^\circe=60∘

    The interior angle is its supplement.

    i=180∘−60∘i=180^\circ-60^\circi=180∘−60∘ i=120∘i=120^\circi=120∘

    A convex regular polygon has twenty sides. What is one exterior turn?

    Solution

    e=360∘÷20e=360^\circ\div20e=360∘÷20 e=18∘e=18^\circe=18∘

    Solution

    n=360÷30n=360\div30n=360÷30 n=12n=12n=12

  12. 12

    Why can every diagonal from a chosen vertex of a convex polygon be used in an interior fan triangulation?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Convexity ensures a segment between vertices stays in the polygon. Distinct diagonals from the chosen vertex cannot cross away from that vertex, so the triangles partition the interior. Equal lengths are unnecessary.
