    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The labeled sides are opposite.

    In parallelogram ABCDABCDABCD, diagonal ACACAC gives two alternate interior-angle pairs, and ACACAC is the common included side. Which conclusion uses those facts directly?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The angle at AAA in triangle ABCABCABC matches the angle at CCC in triangle CDACDACDA, while CCC matches AAA. The shared side lies between those angles, so the ordered match is ASA.

    △ABC≅△CDA.\triangle ABC\cong\triangle CDA.△ABC≅△CDA.

    Parallelogram ABCDABCDABCD has diagonal intersection OOO. Under the half-turn about OOO, which segment is the image of side ABABAB?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The half-turn sends AAA to CCC and BBB to DDD. A rigid motion sends a segment to the segment joining its endpoint images, so ABABAB maps to CDCDCD.

    A↦C,B↦D.\begin{gathered} A\mapsto C,\\ B\mapsto D. \end{gathered}A↦C,B↦D.​

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution
