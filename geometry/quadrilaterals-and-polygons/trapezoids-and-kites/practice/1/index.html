  1. 1

    Which condition defines a trapezoid in this course?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The inclusive definition requires at least one opposite parallel pair. It includes parallelograms; the exactly-one condition is stronger than the definition.

  2. 2

    Convex trapezoid ABCDABCDABCD has chosen bases ABABAB and CDCDCD. Which sides are its legs?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The bases are the chosen opposite parallel sides. The other two boundary sides, AD and BC, are the legs.

  3. 3

    Why does a rectangle qualify as an isosceles trapezoid here?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A rectangle has parallel opposite sides and congruent right angles at a chosen base. Neither equal adjacent sides nor perpendicular diagonals is needed.

  4. 4

    A convex isosceles trapezoid ABCDABCDABCD has bases ABABAB and CDCDCD. Its angle at AAA is 61∘61^\circ61∘. What is the angle at CCC?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The angle at B equals the angle at A. The angles at B and C along leg BC are supplementary.

    m∠C=180∘−61∘m∠C=119∘.\begin{aligned} m\angle C&=180^\circ-61^\circ\\ m\angle C&=119^\circ. \end{aligned}m∠Cm∠C​=180∘−61∘=119∘.​
  5. 5

    A trapezoid has bases of 17 cm and 29 cm. What is the length of its midsegment?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The midsegment is the average of the base lengths.

    (17+29)/2=23.(17+29)/2=23.(17+29)/2=23.
  6. 6

    Which endpoints define a trapezoid midsegment?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A trapezoid midsegment joins the leg midpoints. Joining base midpoints is a different segment and does not invoke this theorem.

  7. 7

    An isosceles trapezoid has one diagonal of length 212121 cm. What is the length of the other diagonal?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Reflection in the common perpendicular bisector of the bases swaps the diagonals. They therefore have equal lengths.

    BD=21 cm.BD=21\text{ cm}.BD=21 cm.
  8. 8

    In convex kite ABCDABCDABCD, the equal side pairs meet at AAA and CCC. Which diagonal is guaranteed to bisect the other?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    AC connects the equal-side meeting vertices. The congruent-triangle proof makes AC the perpendicular bisector of BD; the reverse bisection needs extra information.

  9. 9

    Convex kite ABCDABCDABCD has equal pairs meeting at AAA and CCC. Its diagonals meet at OOO, and BDBDBD is 181818 cm long. What is BOBOBO?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    AC bisects BD at O, so BO is half of the whole diagonal BD.

    BO=182BO=9 cm.\begin{gathered}BO=\frac{18}{2}\\BO=9\text{ cm}.\end{gathered}BO=218​BO=9 cm.​
  10. 10

    In a convex kite, ACACAC joins the equal-side meeting vertices. The whole angle at AAA is 80∘80^\circ80∘. What is m∠BACm\angle BACm∠BAC?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    AC bisects the vertex angle at A, so angle BAC is half of 80 degrees.

    m∠BAC=40∘.m\angle BAC=40^\circ.m∠BAC=40∘.
  11. 11

    A convex kite ABCDABCDABCD has equal pairs meeting at AAA and CCC. Its angle at BBB is 103∘103^\circ103∘. Which angle must also measure 103∘103^\circ103∘?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    SSS makes triangle ABC congruent to triangle ADC. The whole angles at B and D correspond, while the whole angles at A and C need not be congruent.

  12. 12

    A non-parallelogram isosceles trapezoid has how many rotational symmetries, including the identity?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Only the identity works. A half-turn would interchange unequal bases, and a quarter-turn would require two opposite parallel pairs.
