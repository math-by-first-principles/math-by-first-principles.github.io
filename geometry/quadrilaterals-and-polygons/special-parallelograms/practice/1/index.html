Rectangles, Rhombi, and Squares: Practice

Question 1 of 12
  1. 1

    Rectangle ABCDABCDABCD has a diagonal ACACAC of 262626 cm. What is the length of diagonal BDBDBD?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A rectangle has congruent whole diagonals.

    BD=ACBD=ACBD=AC

    Thus BD also measures 26 cm.

  2. 2

    A rhombus has diagonals meeting at OOO. What is the measure of each angle formed by the diagonals?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Rhombus diagonals are perpendicular.

    Perpendicular lines form four right angles, each measuring 90 degrees.

  3. 3

    In rhombus ABCDABCDABCD, angle DABDABDAB measures 138∘138^\circ138∘. What is the measure of angle BACBACBAC?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The diagonal AC bisects the whole angle at A.

    m∠BAC=138∘÷2m\angle BAC=138^\circ\div2m∠BAC=138∘÷2

    The requested half-angle measures 69 degrees.

  4. 4

    Which statement about every square is true?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A square has four right angles, so it is a rectangle.

    It also has four congruent sides, so it is a rhombus. Rotation preserves both facts.

  5. 5

    You know that a quadrilateral is a parallelogram. Which extra fact guarantees a rectangle?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Congruent diagonals identify a rectangle among parallelograms.

    Bisection is already a parallelogram property. The other two conditions identify rhombi and need not give right angles.

  6. 6

    You know that a quadrilateral is a parallelogram. Which extra fact guarantees a rhombus?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Perpendicular diagonals identify a rhombus among parallelograms.

    Congruent diagonals identify a rectangle. Congruent opposite sides and opposite angles are already parallelogram properties.

  7. 7

    The diagonals of rectangle JKLMJKLMJKLM meet at OOO. If JOJOJO is 171717 cm, what is the length of KMKMKM?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A rectangle is a parallelogram, so O bisects JL.

    JL=2(17)JL=2(17)JL=2(17)

    The whole diagonals are congruent, so KM measures 34 cm.

  8. 8

    In a rhombus, which angles does diagonal ACACAC necessarily bisect?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A diagonal bisects the two vertex angles at its own endpoints.

    For AC those endpoints are A and C. The other diagonal handles B and D.

  9. 9

    The diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other. What extra fact is still needed to use the congruent-diagonal rectangle test?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Bisection says the two halves of each diagonal agree.

    The rectangle test needs equality between the two whole diagonal lengths; that does not follow from bisection.

  10. 10

    A parallelogram has one right angle. Which conclusion is guaranteed?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The angle opposite the given right angle is congruent to it. Each neighboring angle is supplementary to a right angle, so it is also right.

    Four right angles establish a rectangle; equal adjacent sides were not given.

  11. 11

    A square has diagonals meeting at OOO. What is the measure of angle BACBACBAC when its boundary vertices are A,B,C,DA,B,C,DA,B,C,D?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A square has a 90-degree angle at A. As a rhombus, it has a diagonal AC that bisects that angle.

    m∠BAC=90∘÷2m\angle BAC=90^\circ\div2m∠BAC=90∘÷2

    The measure is 45 degrees.

  12. 12

    A convex quadrilateral has perpendicular diagonals. Which further hypothesis lets you conclude that it is a rhombus?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The sufficient theorem applies to a parallelogram with perpendicular diagonals.

    Boundary order, convexity, and intersection alone do not supply the missing parallelogram evidence.
