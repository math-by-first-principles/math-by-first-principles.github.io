  1. Problem 1 A small card

    A card shows ana^nan, with a=38a=\tfrac38a=83​ and n=4n=4n=4. Write the multiplication it represents, without finding the product.

    1. Hint 1

      The base supplies the factor, while the exponent counts its copies.

    2. Hint 2

      Replace aaa by 38\tfrac3883​ and use as many copies as the value of nnn requires.

    Answer

    38×38×38×38\tfrac38\times\tfrac38\times\tfrac38\times\tfrac3883​×83​×83​×83​.

    Full solution

    The base is 38\tfrac3883​, and the exponent is 444, so four copies of 38\tfrac3883​ are multiplied.

    (38)4=38×38×38×38\left(\tfrac38\right)^4=\tfrac38\times\tfrac38\times\tfrac38\times\tfrac38(83​)4=83​×83​×83​×83​

    Answer

    38×38×38×38\tfrac38\times\tfrac38\times\tfrac38\times\tfrac3883​×83​×83​×83​.

    Key idea

    An exponent counts copies of the base in a product.

  2. Problem 2 A calculator record

    A calculator record gives 1.5×1.5=2.251.5\times1.5=2.251.5×1.5=2.25. Find 1.531.5^31.53.

    1. Hint 1

      Three factors are needed, and the record already combines two of them.

    2. Hint 2

      Multiply the recorded product by one more copy of 1.51.51.5.

    Answer

    3.3753.3753.375.

    Full solution

    The cube uses three factors of 1.51.51.5, so the recorded square supplies the first two.

    1.53=2.25×1.51.5^3=2.25\times1.51.53=2.25×1.5

    Multiply to obtain the value.

    2.25×1.5=3.3752.25\times1.5=3.3752.25×1.5=3.375

    Answer

    3.3753.3753.375.

    Key idea

    A running product lets you evaluate a power one factor at a time.

  3. Problem 3 A long numeral

    A power of ten is written as an ordinary whole number with nine digits. Write it as 10n10^n10n, giving the value of nnn.

    1. Hint 1

      Count the leading digit separately from the zeros.

    2. Hint 2

      The exponent counts the zeros after the leading 111.

    Answer

    10810^8108, so n=8n=8n=8.

    Full solution

    A power of ten has a leading 111 followed by zeros.

    Nine digits leave eight places for zeros.

    9−1=89-1=89−1=8

    The exponent is therefore 888.

    108=100,000,00010^8=100{,}000{,}000108=100,000,000

    Answer

    10810^8108, so n=8n=8n=8.

    Key idea

    The exponent in a positive whole-number power of ten is one less than its digit count.

  4. Problem 4 A model cube

    A model cube has edge length 0.40.40.4 meter. Write the area of one square face and the volume of the cube each as a power of 0.40.40.4, and find both. (A cube with edge 111 meter has a volume of 111 cubic meter.)

    1. Hint 1

      A face's area multiplies two of its equal edges, while the cube's volume multiplies three equal edges.

    2. Hint 2

      Multiply two copies of 0.40.40.4 for the face area, then one more copy for the volume.

    Answer

    Face area: 0.42=0.160.4^2=0.160.42=0.16 square meter. Volume: 0.43=0.0640.4^3=0.0640.43=0.064 cubic meter.

    Full solution

    Each face is a square with side 0.40.40.4 meter, so its area uses two factors of 0.40.40.4 and is read as zero point four squared.

    0.42=0.4×0.40.4^2=0.4\times0.40.42=0.4×0.4

    Multiply the two factors.

    0.4×0.4=0.160.4\times0.4=0.160.4×0.4=0.16

    The volume uses three equal edge lengths and is read as zero point four cubed, so multiply the face area by one more edge length.

    0.43=0.16×0.40.4^3=0.16\times0.40.43=0.16×0.4

    Multiply again.

    0.16×0.4=0.0640.16\times0.4=0.0640.16×0.4=0.064

    The face area is 0.160.160.16 square meter, and the volume is 0.0640.0640.064 cubic meter.

    Answer

    Face area: 0.42=0.160.4^2=0.160.42=0.16 square meter. Volume: 0.43=0.0640.4^3=0.0640.43=0.064 cubic meter.

    Key idea

    A cube's face area is its edge squared and its volume is its edge cubed, so the volume is the face area times one more edge.

  5. Problem 5 A mixed expression

    Find the value of (−10)3÷200+2×52(-10)^3\div200+2\times5^2(−10)3÷200+2×52.

    1. Hint 1

      Finish each power before working on the division or multiplication.

    2. Hint 2

      The cube has three negative factors, so its value is negative.

    3. Hint 3

      Add the result of the division to the result of the multiplication.

    Answer

    454545.

    Full solution

    The powers are evaluated first.

    (−10)3=−1000(-10)^3=-1000(−10)3=−1000 52=255^2=2552=25

    Now divide and multiply.

    −1000÷200=−5-1000\div200=-5−1000÷200=−5 2×25=502\times25=502×25=50

    Add these two results.

    −5+50=45-5+50=45−5+50=45

    Answer

    454545.

    Key idea

    Powers are evaluated before multiplication and division, which are evaluated before addition.

  6. Problem 6 A forwarded message

    In the first round, Lina sends a message to 777 people. In each later round, every person who received it in the round before forwards it to 777 new people, and no one receives the message more than once. Write the number of people who receive it in the third round as a power of 777 and find it. Then find how many people receive it over all three rounds.

    1. Hint 1

      The first round reaches 777 people and each later round multiplies the count by 777, so count how many factors of 777 the third round uses.

    2. Hint 2

      Keep a running product: find the second round's count first, then multiply by 777 once more.

    3. Hint 3

      For the total, add the counts of all three rounds, not only the count of the last one.

    Answer

    73=3437^3=34373=343 people in the third round; 399399399 people over all three rounds.

    Full solution

    The first round reaches 777 people, which is one factor of 777.

    71=77^1=771=7

    Each of those 777 people forwards it to 777 new people, and since no one receives it twice, these are all different people, so the second round uses two factors of 777.

    72=7×77^2=7\times772=7×7 7×7=497\times7=497×7=49

    Each of those 494949 people forwards it to 777 new people, all different for the same reason, so the third round uses three factors of 777.

    73=49×77^3=49\times773=49×7 49×7=34349\times7=34349×7=343

    Add the three rounds to find the total.

    7+49+343=3997+49+343=3997+49+343=399

    So 343343343 people receive the message in the third round, and 399399399 receive it over all three rounds.

    Answer

    73=3437^3=34373=343 people in the third round; 399399399 people over all three rounds.

    Key idea

    When the first round reaches a number of people and every later round multiplies the count by that same number, each round's count is a power of that number, and the total over several rounds is a sum of those powers.

  7. Problem 7 A written expression

    Find the value of −(−3+1)4−3×(−2)3+(−7)1-(-3+1)^4-3\times(-2)^3+(-7)^1−(−3+1)4−3×(−2)3+(−7)1.

    1. Hint 1

      Parentheses determine each base, and any minus sign outside a power waits until that power is evaluated.

    2. Hint 2

      The first minus sign sits outside a fourth power, while the cube contains three negative factors.

    3. Hint 3

      A first power leaves its base unchanged; finish the multiplication before adding or subtracting.

    Answer

    111.

    Full solution

    Inside the first parentheses, −3+1=−2-3+1=-2−3+1=−2.

    The leading minus sign remains outside the power.

    (−2)4=16(-2)^4=16(−2)4=16 −(−2)4=−16-(-2)^4=-16−(−2)4=−16

    Evaluate the cube and first power.

    (−2)3=−8(-2)^3=-8(−2)3=−8 (−7)1=−7(-7)^1=-7(−7)1=−7

    Perform the multiplication before the subtraction.

    3×(−8)=−243\times(-8)=-243×(−8)=−24

    Subtracting the negative result adds its opposite.

    −16−(−24)=8-16-(-24)=8−16−(−24)=8 8+(−7)=18+(-7)=18+(−7)=1

    Answer

    111.

    Key idea

    Parentheses determine the base, and the exponent determines how many copies of that base are used.

  8. Problem 8 Mira's size claim

    Mira says 10610^6106 is twice as large as 10310^3103, because its exponent is twice as large. Is she correct? Explain, and find how many times as large 10610^6106 is as 10310^3103.

    1. Hint 1

      The exponent counts factors of ten, so write each power of ten as an ordinary number by counting its zeros.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare 10610^6106 with twice the value of 10310^3103, then divide 10610^6106 by 10310^3103 to see how many times as large it is.

    3. Hint 3

      Dividing a whole number that ends in at least three zeros by 100010001000 removes three of those zeros.

    Answer

    No. 106=1,000,00010^6=1{,}000{,}000106=1,000,000, while twice 10310^3103 is only 200020002000; 10610^6106 is 100010001000 times as large as 10310^3103.

    Full solution

    The exponent on ten counts the zeros after the leading 111, so 10310^3103 is a 111 followed by three zeros.

    103=100010^3=1000103=1000

    In the same way, 10610^6106 is a 111 followed by six zeros.

    106=1,000,00010^6=1{,}000{,}000106=1,000,000

    Twice 10310^3103 is only two thousand.

    2×1000=20002\times1000=20002×1000=2000

    Since 1,000,0001{,}000{,}0001,000,000 is far more than 200020002000, the number 10610^6106 is not twice as large as 10310^3103, and Mira is not correct.

    To find how many times as large it is, divide.

    Dividing by 100010001000 removes three of the six zeros.

    1,000,000÷1000=10001{,}000{,}000\div1000=10001,000,000÷1000=1000

    So 10610^6106 is 100010001000 times as large as 10310^3103.

    Answer

    No. 106=1,000,00010^6=1{,}000{,}000106=1,000,000, while twice 10310^3103 is only 200020002000; 10610^6106 is 100010001000 times as large as 10310^3103.

    Key idea

    Doubling the exponent on ten doubles the count of zeros, and each extra zero multiplies the value by ten, so the value grows far more than twice.

  9. Problem 9 Noor's prediction

    In 22×332^2\times3^322×33, each base is replaced by its opposite, written in parentheses so the negative number is the whole base, and the exponents are kept. Noor says the final product changes sign but keeps the same size. Is Noor correct? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Consider the effect on each power separately before considering their product.

    2. Hint 2

      An even number of negative factors has a different sign effect from an odd number.

    3. Hint 3

      Compare the signs and sizes of the two factors before and after the replacements.

    Answer

    Yes. The product changes from 108108108 to −108-108−108.

    Full solution

    The original factors have values 22=42^2=422=4 and 33=273^3=2733=27, so

    4×27=1084\times27=1084×27=108

    After replacement, the square is unchanged in value, while the cube is negative.

    (−2)2=4(-2)^2=4(−2)2=4 (−3)3=−27(-3)^3=-27(−3)3=−27

    The new product is

    4×(−27)=−1084\times(-27)=-1084×(−27)=−108

    The product changes sign and keeps size 108108108, so Noor is correct.

    Answer

    Yes. The product changes from 108108108 to −108-108−108.

    Key idea

    Changing a base to its opposite preserves an even power and changes the sign of an odd power.

  10. Problem 10 Ari's claim

    Ari says 111 is the only positive number equal to its own fourth power. Test 666 and 0.30.30.3, then decide whether Ari is right and explain why.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare what repeated multiplication does to a base bigger than 111 and to a positive base less than 111.

    2. Hint 2

      Each extra factor bigger than 111 makes a positive product larger, and each extra factor between 000 and 111 makes it smaller.

    Answer

    Yes. 64=12966^4=129664=1296 and 0.34=0.00810.3^4=0.00810.34=0.0081; no positive number other than 111 equals its own fourth power.

    Full solution

    Test 666 with a running product of four factors.

    6×6=366\times6=366×6=36 36×6=21636\times6=21636×6=216 216×6=1296216\times6=1296216×6=1296

    Since 129612961296 is greater than 666, the number 666 does not equal its fourth power.

    Test 0.30.30.3 the same way.

    0.3×0.3=0.090.3\times0.3=0.090.3×0.3=0.09 0.09×0.3=0.0270.09\times0.3=0.0270.09×0.3=0.027 0.027×0.3=0.00810.027\times0.3=0.00810.027×0.3=0.0081

    Since 0.00810.00810.0081 is less than 0.30.30.3, the number 0.30.30.3 does not equal its fourth power either.

    For a base greater than 111, each extra factor multiplies a positive product by a number bigger than 111 and makes it larger, so the fourth power is greater than the base.

    For a positive base less than 111, each extra factor is between 000 and 111 and makes the product smaller, so the fourth power is less than the base.

    The base 111 gives 1×1×1×1=11\times1\times1\times1=11×1×1×1=1, so no positive number other than 111 equals its own fourth power, and Ari is right.

    Answer

    Yes. 64=12966^4=129664=1296 and 0.34=0.00810.3^4=0.00810.34=0.0081; no positive number other than 111 equals its own fourth power.

    Key idea

    Repeated multiplication grows a base above one and shrinks a positive base below one, so no positive number other than one equals its own fourth power.
