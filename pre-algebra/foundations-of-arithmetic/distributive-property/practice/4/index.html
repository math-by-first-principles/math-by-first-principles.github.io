  1. 1

    Which expression equals 9×479 \times 479×47?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Split 474747 as 40+740 + 740+7 and distribute the 999 to both parts.

    9×47=9(40+7)=9×40+9×7=360+63=4239 \times 47 = 9(40 + 7) = 9 \times 40 + 9 \times 7 = 360 + 63 = 4239×47=9(40+7)=9×40+9×7=360+63=423

    The form 9×40+79 \times 40 + 79×40+7 multiplies only the first part, and 9×4+9×79 \times 4 + 9 \times 79×4+9×7 uses 444 instead of 404040, so both are wrong.

  2. 2

    Use the distributive property to evaluate 8×958 \times 958×95 with a difference.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Since 959595 is 555 below the round 100100100, write it as 100−5100 - 5100−5 and distribute the 888.

    8×95=8(100−5)=8×100−8×5=800−40=7608 \times 95 = 8(100 - 5) = 8 \times 100 - 8 \times 5 = 800 - 40 = 7608×95=8(100−5)=8×100−8×5=800−40=760

    The correction 8×5=408 \times 5 = 408×5=40 is small because 959595 is close to 100100100.

  3. 3

    Which line shows the correct distribution of 6(20−3)6(20 - 3)6(20−3)?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The outside 666 multiplies both terms inside, and the minus sign between them is kept.

    6(20−3)=6×20−6×3=120−18=1026(20 - 3) = 6 \times 20 - 6 \times 3 = 120 - 18 = 1026(20−3)=6×20−6×3=120−18=102

    The line 6×20−36 \times 20 - 36×20−3 multiplies only the first term, and 6×20+6×36 \times 20 + 6 \times 36×20+6×3 flips the subtraction to addition, so both are wrong.

  4. 4

    Which expression equals 5×1975 \times 1975×197?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Write 197197197 as 200−3200 - 3200−3 and distribute the 555 to each part.

    5×197=5(200−3)=5×200−5×3=1000−15=9855 \times 197 = 5(200 - 3) = 5 \times 200 - 5 \times 3 = 1000 - 15 = 9855×197=5(200−3)=5×200−5×3=1000−15=985

    Subtracting only 333 leaves 997997997, and adding 5×35 \times 35×3 gives 101510151015, so both of those are wrong.

  5. 5

    Evaluate 9(100−4)9(100 - 4)9(100−4) using the distributive property.

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Distribute the 999 across the difference, keeping the minus sign.

    9(100−4)=9×100−9×4=900−36=8649(100 - 4) = 9 \times 100 - 9 \times 4 = 900 - 36 = 8649(100−4)=9×100−9×4=900−36=864

    Check the inside first: 100−4=96100 - 4 = 96100−4=96, and 9×96=8649 \times 96 = 8649×96=864.

  6. 6

    You want 6×1486 \times 1486×148 in your head. Which split of 148148148 leaves the least work?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    All four splits are true, and all four reach the same answer, so the question is only which leaves the least work.

    6×148=6(150−2)=900−12=8886 \times 148 = 6(150 - 2) = 900 - 12 = 8886×148=6(150−2)=900−12=888

    Both of those products are ones you can do at a glance. The other splits still leave a two-digit product to work out, 6×486 \times 486×48, 6×746 \times 746×74, or 6×286 \times 286×28, so none of them is as light as 150−2150 - 2150−2.

  7. 7

    Factor 45+2745 + 2745+27 completely.

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The greatest common factor of 454545 and 272727 is 999, since 45=9×545 = 9 \times 545=9×5 and 27=9×327 = 9 \times 327=9×3.

    45+27=9×5+9×3=9(5+3)45 + 27 = 9 \times 5 + 9 \times 3 = 9(5 + 3)45+27=9×5+9×3=9(5+3)

    Check: 9(5+3)=9×8=729(5 + 3) = 9 \times 8 = 729(5+3)=9×8=72, and 45+27=7245 + 27 = 7245+27=72. The form 3(15+9)3(15 + 9)3(15+9) pulls out only a 333, so it is not fully factored, and the others give wrong totals.

  8. 8

    Use the distributive property to evaluate 12×4512 \times 4512×45 by writing 45=40+545 = 40 + 545=40+5.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Split 454545 as 40+540 + 540+5 and distribute the 121212 to both parts.

    12×45=12(40+5)=12×40+12×5=480+60=54012 \times 45 = 12(40 + 5) = 12 \times 40 + 12 \times 5 = 480 + 60 = 54012×45=12(40+5)=12×40+12×5=480+60=540

    Forgetting the 12×5=6012 \times 5 = 6012×5=60 would leave 480480480, which is too small.

  9. 9

    A student writes 9×53=9(50+3)=450+3=4539 \times 53 = 9(50 + 3) = 450 + 3 = 4539×53=9(50+3)=450+3=453. What is the correct value?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Both parts of 50+350 + 350+3 must be multiplied by 999, but the student forgot to multiply the 333.

    9×53=9(50+3)=9×50+9×3=450+27=4779 \times 53 = 9(50 + 3) = 9 \times 50 + 9 \times 3 = 450 + 27 = 4779×53=9(50+3)=9×50+9×3=450+27=477

    The student added the unmultiplied 333 instead of 9×3=279 \times 3 = 279×3=27.

  11. 11

    Evaluate 4(12+9+6)4(12 + 9 + 6)4(12+9+6) by distributing the 444 to all three terms.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Multiply each of the three terms by 444, then add the products.

    4(12+9+6)=4×12+4×9+4×6=48+36+24=1084(12 + 9 + 6) = 4 \times 12 + 4 \times 9 + 4 \times 6 = 48 + 36 + 24 = 1084(12+9+6)=4×12+4×9+4×6=48+36+24=108

    Check the inside first: 12+9+6=2712 + 9 + 6 = 2712+9+6=27, and 4×27=1084 \times 27 = 1084×27=108.

  12. 12

    A shop opens 777 boxes of mugs. Each box holds 121212 mugs, and 222 mugs in every box are chipped. Which expression counts the mugs the shop can sell?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    One box yields 12−2=1012 - 2 = 1012−2=10 sellable mugs, and there are 777 identical boxes.

    7(12−2)=7×10=707(12 - 2) = 7 \times 10 = 707(12−2)=7×10=70

    Distributing gives the same count: 7×12−7×2=84−14=707 \times 12 - 7 \times 2 = 84 - 14 = 707×12−7×2=84−14=70. The form 7×12−27 \times 12 - 27×12−2 removes only 222 chipped mugs in total instead of 222 from every box, and 12(7−2)12(7 - 2)12(7−2) subtracts boxes rather than mugs.
