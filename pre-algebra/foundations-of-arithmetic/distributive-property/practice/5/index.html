  1. 1

    A student says 7(10+3)=70+3=737(10 + 3) = 70 + 3 = 737(10+3)=70+3=73. What is the correct value?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The 777 must multiply both terms inside, not just the first.

    7(10+3)=7×10+7×3=70+21=917(10 + 3) = 7 \times 10 + 7 \times 3 = 70 + 21 = 917(10+3)=7×10+7×3=70+21=91

    The student forgot to multiply the 333 by 777.

  2. 2

    Use the distributive property to evaluate 14×10314 \times 10314×103.

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Write 103103103 as 100+3100 + 3100+3 and distribute the 141414 to each part.

    14×103=14(100+3)=14×100+14×3=1400+42=144214 \times 103 = 14(100 + 3) = 14 \times 100 + 14 \times 3 = 1400 + 42 = 144214×103=14(100+3)=14×100+14×3=1400+42=1442

    The second product must be 14×3=4214 \times 3 = 4214×3=42. Every wrong option keeps the 140014001400 and then mishandles that second product: 140314031403 adds the 333 without multiplying it, 141414141414 adds 14×114 \times 114×1, and 143014301430 adds 10×310 \times 310×3.

  3. 3

    Evaluate 5(2+3+4)5(2 + 3 + 4)5(2+3+4) by distributing the 555 to all three terms.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Multiply each of the three terms by 555, then add.

    5(2+3+4)=5×2+5×3+5×4=10+15+20=455(2 + 3 + 4) = 5 \times 2 + 5 \times 3 + 5 \times 4 = 10 + 15 + 20 = 455(2+3+4)=5×2+5×3+5×4=10+15+20=45

    Check: 5×9=455 \times 9 = 455×9=45.

  4. 4

    Factor 42+5642 + 5642+56 completely.

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The greatest common factor of 424242 and 565656 is 141414, since 42=14×342 = 14 \times 342=14×3 and 56=14×456 = 14 \times 456=14×4.

    42+56=14×3+14×4=14(3+4)42 + 56 = 14 \times 3 + 14 \times 4 = 14(3 + 4)42+56=14×3+14×4=14(3+4)

    Check by distributing back: 14(3+4)=14×7=9814(3 + 4) = 14 \times 7 = 9814(3+4)=14×7=98, and 42+56=9842 + 56 = 9842+56=98. Forms like 7(6+8)7(6 + 8)7(6+8) and 2(21+28)2(21 + 28)2(21+28) are true equalities but pull out only part of the common factor, so they are not fully factored.

  5. 5

    Which expression equals 6×996 \times 996×99?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Write 99=100−199 = 100 - 199=100−1 and distribute the 666 to each part.

    6×99=6(100−1)=6×100−6×1=600−6=5946 \times 99 = 6(100 - 1) = 6 \times 100 - 6 \times 1 = 600 - 6 = 5946×99=6(100−1)=6×100−6×1=600−6=594

    So the matching form is 6×100−66 \times 100 - 66×100−6. The others evaluate to 501501501, 606606606, and 588588588.

  6. 6

    Evaluate 4(3×5)4(3 \times 5)4(3×5).

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The parentheses hold a product, not a sum, so there is nothing to distribute. Work out the inside first.

    4(3×5)=4×15=604(3 \times 5) = 4 \times 15 = 604(3×5)=4×15=60

    Distributing the 444 to both factors gives 12×20=24012 \times 20 = 24012×20=240, which multiplies by 444 twice. Treating the inside as a sum gives 323232. Replacing the outside multiplication with an addition gives 191919.

  7. 7

    Which expression equals 484848?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Distribute and add each option, then keep the one equal to 484848.

    4(5+7)=4×12=484(5 + 7) = 4 \times 12 = 484(5+7)=4×12=48

    The others give 8×5=408 \times 5 = 408×5=40, 6×7=426 \times 7 = 426×7=42, and 11×4=4411 \times 4 = 4411×4=44, none of which is 484848.

  8. 8

    Evaluate 6(15−8)6(15 - 8)6(15−8) using the distributive property.

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Distribute the 666 across the difference, keeping the minus sign.

    6(15−8)=6×15−6×8=90−48=426(15 - 8) = 6 \times 15 - 6 \times 8 = 90 - 48 = 426(15−8)=6×15−6×8=90−48=42

    Check the inside first: 15−8=715 - 8 = 715−8=7, and 6×7=426 \times 7 = 426×7=42.

  9. 9

    You want 9×529 \times 529×52 in your head. Which split of 525252 turns it into one easy product plus one small one?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    All four splits are true, and all four give the same answer, so the question is only which leaves the least work.

    9×52=9(50+2)=450+18=4689 \times 52 = 9(50 + 2) = 450 + 18 = 4689×52=9(50+2)=450+18=468

    Both of those products are ones you can do at a glance. The other splits still leave a two-digit product to work out, 9×269 \times 269×26, 9×399 \times 399×39, or 9×229 \times 229×22, so none of them is as light as 50+250 + 250+2.

  10. 10

    Which expression equals 11×1411 \times 1411×14?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Write 141414 as 10+410 + 410+4 and distribute the 111111 to both parts.

    11×14=11(10+4)=11×10+11×4=110+44=15411 \times 14 = 11(10 + 4) = 11 \times 10 + 11 \times 4 = 110 + 44 = 15411×14=11(10+4)=11×10+11×4=110+44=154

    The form 11×10+411 \times 10 + 411×10+4 multiplies only the first part, and 11×1+11×411 \times 1 + 11 \times 411×1+11×4 uses 111 instead of 101010, so both are wrong.

  12. 12

    Factor 48+8048 + 8048+80 completely.

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The greatest common factor of 484848 and 808080 is 161616, since 48=16×348 = 16 \times 348=16×3 and 80=16×580 = 16 \times 580=16×5.

    48+80=16×3+16×5=16(3+5)48 + 80 = 16 \times 3 + 16 \times 5 = 16(3 + 5)48+80=16×3+16×5=16(3+5)

    Check: 16(3+5)=16×8=12816(3 + 5) = 16 \times 8 = 12816(3+5)=16×8=128, and 48+80=12848 + 80 = 12848+80=128. The forms 8(6+10)8(6 + 10)8(6+10) and 4(12+20)4(12 + 20)4(12+20) leave a common factor inside, so they are not fully factored.
