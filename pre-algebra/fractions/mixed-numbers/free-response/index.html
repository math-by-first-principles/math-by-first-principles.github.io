Problem 1 of 10

  1. Problem 1 A form entry

    A mixed number has whole-number part 444. Its fraction part is positive, has denominator 999, and has the largest whole-number numerator that a proper fraction allows. Write the mixed number.

    1. Hint 1

      A proper fraction’s numerator is smaller than its denominator.

    2. Hint 2

      List the numerators that keep a fraction with denominator 999 proper, and write the largest one over 999 beside the 444.

    Answer

    4894\tfrac89498​.

    Full solution

    The largest whole-number numerator smaller than 999 is 888.

    The fraction part is therefore 89\frac8998​.

    Together with the whole part, the number is

    4+89=489.4+\frac89=4\tfrac89.4+98​=498​.

    The space in the mixed number stands for addition.

    Answer

    4894\tfrac89498​.

    Key idea

    A mixed number combines its whole part with a fraction part that is smaller than one.

  2. Problem 2 A tiled border

    A border is made of 616161 tiles laid end to end in one row, and each tile is 18\frac1881​ of a meter long. Write the length of the border as a mixed number.

    1. Hint 1

      The total length is a count of eighths of a meter, and eight eighths make one meter.

    2. Hint 2

      Divide 616161 by 888: the quotient counts whole meters, and the remainder counts the eighths of a meter left over.

    Answer

    7587\tfrac58785​ meters.

    Full solution

    The border is 616161 eighths of a meter long, which is 618\frac{61}8861​ meters.

    Eight goes into 616161 seven times, using 565656, and leaves 555, so

    618=758.\frac{61}8=7\tfrac58.861​=785​.

    The remainder 555 is less than 888, so the fraction part is proper.

    Converting back gives 7×8+5=617\times8+5=617×8+5=61, matching the number of tiles.

    Answer

    7587\tfrac58785​ meters.

    Key idea

    The quotient of the numerator divided by the denominator counts the complete wholes, and the remainder over the same denominator becomes the fraction part.

  3. Problem 3 A specified label

    Write 3563\tfrac56365​ as one fraction with denominator 181818 and a whole-number numerator.

    1. Hint 1

      The mixed number means three wholes plus five sixths.

    2. Hint 2

      Count the sixths in the three wholes, then change the resulting fraction to eighteenths.

    Answer

    6918\frac{69}{18}1869​.

    Full solution

    Three wholes contain eighteen sixths, and five more sixths give

    3×6+5=23.3\times6+5=23.3×6+5=23.

    Therefore

    356=236.3\tfrac56=\frac{23}6.365​=623​.

    Multiply the numerator and denominator by 333 to obtain the requested form:

    236=6918.\frac{23}6=\frac{69}{18}.623​=1869​.

    Dividing both by 333 checks that the value was preserved.

    Answer

    6918\frac{69}{18}1869​.

    Key idea

    A mixed number can be counted as one improper fraction before it is rewritten with a required denominator.

  4. Problem 4 Two walkers

    Ana walks 1581\tfrac58185​ kilometers in the morning and 27102\tfrac7{10}2107​ kilometers in the afternoon. Ben walks 4184\tfrac18481​ kilometers in one walk. Who walks farther, and by how much? Give the difference as a fraction in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      Ana’s two walks must be combined into one distance before it can be compared with Ben’s.

    2. Hint 2

      Add Ana’s whole parts and fraction parts separately, using fortieths, and check whether the fraction parts pass one whole.

    3. Hint 3

      Write Ben’s fraction part in fortieths too, then compare the two distances and subtract the smaller from the larger.

    Answer

    Ana, by 15\frac1551​ of a kilometer.

    Full solution

    Add Ana’s walks by parts.

    The whole parts give 1+2=31+2=31+2=3.

    In fortieths, 58=2540\frac58=\frac{25}{40}85​=4025​ and 710=2840\frac7{10}=\frac{28}{40}107​=4028​, so the fraction parts give

    2540+2840=5340.\frac{25}{40}+\frac{28}{40}=\frac{53}{40}.4025​+4028​=4053​.

    Since 53=1×40+1353=1\times40+1353=1×40+13, that is 113401\tfrac{13}{40}14013​, so carry the 111 into the whole part: Ana walks 413404\tfrac{13}{40}44013​ kilometers.

    In fortieths, 18=540\frac18=\frac5{40}81​=405​, so Ben walks 45404\tfrac5{40}4405​ kilometers.

    Both distances have whole part 444, and 1340\frac{13}{40}4013​ is more than 540\frac5{40}405​, so Ana walks farther.

    The whole parts are equal, so the difference is the difference of the fraction parts:

    1340−540=840.\frac{13}{40}-\frac5{40}=\frac8{40}.4013​−405​=408​.

    Dividing the top and bottom by 888 gives 15\frac1551​ of a kilometer, in lowest terms.

    Check with improper fractions.

    Since 1×8+5=131\times8+5=131×8+5=13 and 2×10+7=272\times10+7=272×10+7=27, Ana walks 138+2710\frac{13}8+\frac{27}{10}813​+1027​ kilometers, which in fortieths is

    6540+10840=17340.\frac{65}{40}+\frac{108}{40}=\frac{173}{40}.4065​+40108​=40173​.

    Since 4×8+1=334\times8+1=334×8+1=33, Ben walks 338\frac{33}8833​ kilometers, which is 16540\frac{165}{40}40165​, so the difference is again 840\frac8{40}408​.

    Answer

    Ana, by 15\frac1551​ of a kilometer.

    Key idea

    When fraction parts add past one whole, carry that whole into the whole-number part before comparing, since the whole parts alone can mislead.

  5. Problem 5 A package adjustment

    A package contains just two things: 2382\tfrac38283​ kilograms of packing material and 3233\tfrac23332​ kilograms of equipment. A worker removes 78\frac7887​ of a kilogram of packing material. What is the total mass of its contents now? Give a mixed number with its fraction part in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      Find the packing material left, then combine it with the unchanged equipment mass.

    2. Hint 2

      The packing subtraction is in eighths, and the final addition can be done in sixths.

    Answer

    5165\tfrac16561​ kilograms.

    Full solution

    The fraction part 38\frac3883​ is less than 78\frac7887​, so borrow one whole: since one whole is 88\frac8888​, 2382\tfrac38283​ becomes 11181\tfrac{11}81811​.

    Subtracting the fraction parts leaves

    1118−78=148.1\tfrac{11}8-\frac78=1\tfrac48.1811​−87​=184​.

    This simplifies to 1121\tfrac12121​ kilograms of packing material.

    Now add 1121\tfrac12121​ and 3233\tfrac23332​ by parts.

    The whole parts give 1+3=41+3=41+3=4.

    In sixths, the fraction parts give

    36+46=76.\frac36+\frac46=\frac76.63​+64​=67​.

    Since 76=116\frac76=1\tfrac1667​=161​, carry the 111: the contents now total 5165\tfrac16561​ kilograms.

    As a check, in twenty-fourths 38=924\frac38=\frac9{24}83​=249​ and 23=1624\frac23=\frac{16}{24}32​=2416​, so the original contents are 555 kilograms plus 2524\frac{25}{24}2425​ of a kilogram.

    Removing 78=2124\frac78=\frac{21}{24}87​=2421​ leaves 555 kilograms plus 424\frac4{24}244​, which is 5165\tfrac16561​ kilograms.

    Answer

    5165\tfrac16561​ kilograms.

    Key idea

    Updating one part of a total and then combining the parts keeps the bookkeeping clear.

  6. Problem 6 A trimmed poster

    A poster is 2252\tfrac25252​ meters long. It is enlarged so that its new length is 1231\tfrac23132​ times its old length, and then 12\frac1221​ of a meter is trimmed from the enlarged poster. Find its final length as a mixed number in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      The enlargement happens before the trimming.

    2. Hint 2

      Write both mixed numbers as improper fractions for the multiplication, then subtract the trimmed length.

    Answer

    3123\tfrac12321​ meters.

    Full solution

    The original length is 125\frac{12}5512​ meters, since 2×5+2=122\times5+2=122×5+2=12, and the multiplier 1231\tfrac23132​ is 53\frac5335​, since

    1×3+2=5.1\times3+2=5.1×3+2=5.

    The 555s cancel, and 121212 divided by 333 is 444, so

    125×53=4.\frac{12}5\times\frac53=4.512​×35​=4.

    The enlarged poster is 444 meters long.

    Trim half a meter from that length:

    4−12=72.4-\frac12=\frac72.4−21​=27​.

    The final length is 3123\tfrac12321​ meters.

    Adding the trimmed half meter returns the enlarged length of 444 meters.

    Answer

    3123\tfrac12321​ meters.

    Key idea

    Convert mixed numbers before multiplying, and apply later changes to the resulting amount.

  7. Problem 7 A filling timer

    A container already holds 16\frac1661​ of a liter. A pump steadily adds 211122\tfrac{11}{12}21211​ liters each minute. How many minutes will it take to reach 4344\tfrac34443​ liters? Give a mixed number in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      The pump needs to supply the target amount minus the amount already present.

    2. Hint 2

      Convert the target and the amount per minute to improper fractions, then divide the amount still needed by the amount supplied each minute.

    Answer

    1471\tfrac47174​ minutes.

    Full solution

    The target is 194\frac{19}4419​ liters, since

    4×4+3=19.4\times4+3=19.4×4+3=19.

    In twelfths, 194=5712\frac{19}4=\frac{57}{12}419​=1257​ and

    16=212.\frac16=\frac2{12}.61​=122​.

    The amount still needed, in liters, is

    194−16=5512.\frac{19}4-\frac16=\frac{55}{12}.419​−61​=1255​.

    The pump adds 3512\frac{35}{12}1235​ liters each minute, since

    2×12+11=35.2\times12+11=35.2×12+11=35.

    The time is the needed amount divided by the amount per minute:

    5512÷3512=5512×1235.\frac{55}{12}\div\frac{35}{12}=\frac{55}{12}\times\frac{12}{35}.1255​÷1235​=1255​×3512​.

    The 121212s cancel, and 5535\frac{55}{35}3555​ simplifies by 555, so

    5512×1235=117.\frac{55}{12}\times\frac{12}{35}=\frac{11}7.1255​×3512​=711​.

    Since 11=1×7+411=1\times7+411=1×7+4, the time is 1471\tfrac47174​ minutes.

    In that time the pump adds 117×3512=5512\frac{11}7\times\frac{35}{12}=\frac{55}{12}711​×1235​=1255​ liters.

    Adding the initial 16=212\frac16=\frac2{12}61​=122​ of a liter gives 5712=434\frac{57}{12}=4\tfrac341257​=443​ liters, the target.

    Answer

    1471\tfrac47174​ minutes.

    Key idea

    The time to reach a target is found from the amount still needed, divided by the amount supplied per unit of time.

  8. Problem 8 A card with two forms

    A card shows 269=1+179\frac{26}9=1+\frac{17}9926​=1+917​. Explain why the right side has the correct value, then write that value as a mixed number with a proper fraction part.

    1. Hint 1

      Check the value of the sum separately from the required form of its fraction part.

    2. Hint 2

      Nine of the seventeen ninths form an additional whole.

    Answer

    2892\tfrac89298​.

    Full solution

    The right side has the correct value, because one whole is 99\frac9999​ and

    99+179=269.\frac99+\frac{17}9=\frac{26}9.99​+917​=926​.

    But 179\frac{17}9917​ is improper, so the right side does not yet have a proper fraction part.

    The seventeen ninths contain one whole and eight more ninths:

    179=1+89.\frac{17}9=1+\frac89.917​=1+98​.

    Combining that extra whole with the existing whole gives 2892\tfrac89298​.

    The fraction part is now proper, so the rewritten form is 2892\tfrac89298​.

    Answer

    2892\tfrac89298​.

    Key idea

    A correct whole-plus-fraction expression may still need a whole moved out of its fraction part.

  9. Problem 9 Rae’s comparison

    Rae says 7467\tfrac46764​ and 7237\tfrac23732​ cannot be equal, because when each is written as an improper fraction the two results have different denominators. Is she correct? Convert both and explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Rae’s reason is about how the two fractions are written. Decide what actually shows whether two fractions name the same amount.

    2. Hint 2

      Convert each with the whole part times the denominator plus the numerator, then check whether each result is in lowest terms.

    Answer

    No; 746=4667\tfrac46=\frac{46}6764​=646​, which is 233\frac{23}3323​, and 723=2337\tfrac23=\frac{23}3732​=323​.

    Full solution

    Converting keeps each mixed number’s denominator.

    For the first, 7×6+4=467\times6+4=467×6+4=46, so

    746=466.7\tfrac46=\frac{46}6.764​=646​.

    For the second, 7×3+2=237\times3+2=237×3+2=23, so

    723=233.7\tfrac23=\frac{23}3.732​=323​.

    Rae is right that these two results have different denominators.

    But 464646 and 666 share the factor 222, and dividing both by 222 gives

    466=233.\frac{46}6=\frac{23}3.646​=323​.

    The two mixed numbers name the same value, so Rae is not correct.

    Different denominators in unsimplified forms do not mean different values.

    Answer

    No; 746=4667\tfrac46=\frac{46}6764​=646​, which is 233\frac{23}3323​, and 723=2337\tfrac23=\frac{23}3732​=323​.

    Key idea

    Converting keeps the denominator, but simplifying afterward can change it, so one value can appear with different denominators.

  10. Problem 10 Theo’s reading

    Theo reads 9459\tfrac45954​ as 9×459\times\frac459×54​ and says its value is 7157\tfrac15751​. Is he correct? Explain, and give the value of 9459\tfrac45954​ as an improper fraction.

    1. Hint 1

      Ask what the space between the whole part and the fraction part of a mixed number stands for.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare where 9459\tfrac45954​ must sit among the whole numbers with the size of 999 times a number less than 111.

    3. Hint 3

      To convert, count the fifths in nine wholes, then add the four more fifths.

    Answer

    No; 945=4959\tfrac45=\frac{49}5954​=549​.

    Full solution

    Theo’s reading gives

    9×45=365.9\times\frac45=\frac{36}5.9×54​=536​.

    Since 36=7×5+136=7\times5+136=7×5+1, that is 7157\tfrac15751​, so his arithmetic is right but his reading is not.

    The space in a mixed number means add, so 9459\tfrac45954​ stands for 9+459+\frac459+54​, a value between 999 and 101010.

    Nine wholes are 9×5=459\times5=459×5=45 fifths, and four more fifths make 494949, so

    945=495.9\tfrac45=\frac{49}5.954​=549​.

    A proper fraction is less than 111, so 999 times it is less than 999.

    The mixed number is more than 999, so no such product can equal it, and Theo is not correct.

    Answer

    No; 945=4959\tfrac45=\frac{49}5954​=549​.

    Key idea

    The space in a mixed number means add, so its value lies between its whole part and the next whole number.
