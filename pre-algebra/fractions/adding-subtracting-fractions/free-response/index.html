  1. Problem 1 Four contributions

    Find 116+516+716+316\frac1{16}+\frac5{16}+\frac7{16}+\frac3{16}161​+165​+167​+163​ in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      All four fractions count the same-sized parts.

    2. Hint 2

      Total the numerators and keep the size of each part fixed.

    Answer

    111.

    Full solution

    Each piece is a sixteenth, so the denominator stays 161616.

    The total number of pieces is

    1+5+7+3=16.1+5+7+3=16.1+5+7+3=16.

    Therefore the total is

    1616=1.\frac{16}{16}=1.1616​=1.

    Sixteen sixteenths make exactly one whole.

    Answer

    111.

    Key idea

    Adding several fractions with the same denominator adds their counts of one shared part size.

  2. Problem 2 Twelfths and twentieths

    Find 712+320\frac7{12}+\frac3{20}127​+203​ in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      Twelfths and twentieths are different-sized parts, so they cannot be counted together yet.

    2. Hint 2

      Rebuild both fractions over the least common multiple of 121212 and 202020, then look for a common factor in the result.

    Answer

    1115\frac{11}{15}1511​.

    Full solution

    The multiples of 202020 are 202020, 404040, 606060, and so on, and 606060 is the first that is also a multiple of 121212.

    The least common denominator is therefore 606060.

    Since 12×5=6012\times5=6012×5=60, multiply the top and bottom of the first fraction by 555:

    712=3560.\frac7{12}=\frac{35}{60}.127​=6035​.

    Since 20×3=6020\times3=6020×3=60, multiply the top and bottom of the second fraction by 333:

    320=960.\frac3{20}=\frac9{60}.203​=609​.

    Both fractions now count sixtieths, so

    3560+960=4460.\frac{35}{60}+\frac9{60}=\frac{44}{60}.6035​+609​=6044​.

    The greatest common factor of 444444 and 606060 is 444, so

    4460=1115.\frac{44}{60}=\frac{11}{15}.6044​=1511​.

    The numbers 111111 and 151515 share no factor above 111, so the result is in lowest terms.

    Answer

    1115\frac{11}{15}1511​.

    Key idea

    Unlike fractions are rebuilt over their least common denominator before their numerators are combined, and the result is then simplified.

  3. Problem 3 Thirtieths and tenths

    Find 2930−110\frac{29}{30}-\frac1{10}3029​−101​ in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      Check whether one denominator is already a multiple of the other.

    2. Hint 2

      Rebuild only the tenths as thirtieths, subtract, then look for a common factor.

    Answer

    1315\frac{13}{15}1513​.

    Full solution

    Since 10×3=3010\times3=3010×3=30, the denominator 303030 is already a multiple of 101010, so the least common denominator is 303030.

    The fraction 2930\frac{29}{30}3029​ stays as it is, and only the tenths need rebuilding: multiply the top and bottom of 110\frac1{10}101​ by 333, so

    110=330.\frac1{10}=\frac3{30}.101​=303​.

    Both fractions now count thirtieths, so

    2930−330=2630.\frac{29}{30}-\frac3{30}=\frac{26}{30}.3029​−303​=3026​.

    The greatest common factor of 262626 and 303030 is 222, so

    2630=1315.\frac{26}{30}=\frac{13}{15}.3026​=1513​.

    The numbers 131313 and 151515 share no factor above 111, so the result is in lowest terms.

    Answer

    1315\frac{13}{15}1513​.

    Key idea

    When one denominator is a multiple of the other, only the fraction with the smaller denominator needs rebuilding.

  4. Problem 4 Paint between cans

    Can A contains 34\frac3443​ of a liter of paint and Can B contains 58\frac5885​ of a liter. A painter transfers 16\frac1661​ of a liter from A to B without spilling. How much paint is in each can afterward? Give each amount in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      The transferred paint leaves one can and enters the other.

    2. Hint 2

      Subtract from A using twelfths, and add to B using twenty-fourths.

    Answer

    A: 712\frac7{12}127​ of a liter; B: 1924\frac{19}{24}2419​ of a liter.

    Full solution

    Can A loses 16\frac1661​ of a liter.

    In twelfths,

    34=912\frac34=\frac9{12}43​=129​ 16=212\frac16=\frac2{12}61​=122​

    so A contains

    912−212=712.\frac9{12}-\frac2{12}=\frac7{12}.129​−122​=127​.

    Can B gains the transferred amount.

    In twenty-fourths,

    58=1524\frac58=\frac{15}{24}85​=2415​ 16=424\frac16=\frac4{24}61​=244​

    so B contains

    1524+424=1924.\frac{15}{24}+\frac4{24}=\frac{19}{24}.2415​+244​=2419​.

    Both answers are in lowest terms.

    As a check, in twenty-fourths 712=1424\frac7{12}=\frac{14}{24}127​=2414​, so the cans now hold 1424+1924=3324\frac{14}{24}+\frac{19}{24}=\frac{33}{24}2414​+2419​=2433​ of a liter.

    At the start, 34=1824\frac34=\frac{18}{24}43​=2418​ and 1824+1524=3324\frac{18}{24}+\frac{15}{24}=\frac{33}{24}2418​+2415​=2433​, so no paint was lost.

    Answer

    A: 712\frac7{12}127​ of a liter; B: 1924\frac{19}{24}2419​ of a liter.

    Key idea

    A transfer is subtracted from its source and added to its destination, preserving the combined amount.

  5. Problem 5 A boat on a canal

    A boat on a straight canal is 1120\frac{11}{20}2011​ of a kilometer past a lock. It travels 710\frac7{10}107​ of a kilometer farther from the lock, then turns and travels 35\frac3553​ of a kilometer back toward it. How far past the lock is the boat now, and how far did it travel on these two trips? Give both as fractions in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      The boat’s distance from the lock depends on the direction of each trip, while the distance traveled counts both trips as lengths.

    2. Hint 2

      Use twentieths to combine the starting distance from the lock with the trip away and the trip back.

    3. Hint 3

      For the distance traveled, rewrite 35\frac3553​ in tenths and add it to 710\frac7{10}107​.

    Answer

    Distance past the lock: 1320\frac{13}{20}2013​ of a kilometer; distance traveled on the two trips: 1310\frac{13}{10}1013​ kilometers.

    Full solution

    Write the trips in twentieths: 710=1420\frac7{10}=\frac{14}{20}107​=2014​ and

    35=1220.\frac35=\frac{12}{20}.53​=2012​.

    The trip away from the lock adds to the boat’s distance from it, and the trip back subtracts, giving

    11+14−1220=1320.\frac{11+14-12}{20}=\frac{13}{20}.2011+14−12​=2013​.

    The distance traveled counts both trip lengths, whatever their direction.

    Write 35=610\frac35=\frac6{10}53​=106​, so

    710+610=1310.\frac7{10}+\frac6{10}=\frac{13}{10}.107​+106​=1013​.

    Each resulting fraction is in lowest terms.

    The trip away was longer than the trip back, which agrees with the boat finishing farther from the lock than where it started.

    Answer

    Distance past the lock: 1320\frac{13}{20}2013​ of a kilometer; distance traveled on the two trips: 1310\frac{13}{10}1013​ kilometers.

    Key idea

    Total distance traveled adds trip lengths, while a final position also depends on their directions.

  6. Problem 6 A revised walking route

    A walking route is 222 kilometers long. A section of length 78\frac7887​ of a kilometer is replaced by a new section of length 1112\frac{11}{12}1211​ of a kilometer. What is the revised route length as one fraction in lowest terms?

    1. Hint 1

      Remove the old section from the total and put the new section in its place.

    2. Hint 2

      Write both section lengths and the whole route in twenty-fourths.

    Answer

    4924\frac{49}{24}2449​ kilometers.

    Full solution

    The new section replaces, rather than joins, the old one.

    The revised length is 2−78+11122-\frac78+\frac{11}{12}2−87​+1211​.

    The least common multiple of 888 and 121212 is 242424, and the whole number 222 can be rebuilt over any denominator, so rewrite the amounts with denominator 242424:

    2=48242=\frac{48}{24}2=2448​ 78=2124\frac78=\frac{21}{24}87​=2421​ 1112=2224\frac{11}{12}=\frac{22}{24}1211​=2422​

    Combine the counts:

    48−21+2224=4924.\frac{48-21+22}{24}=\frac{49}{24}.2448−21+22​=2449​.

    The numbers 494949 and 242424 share no factor above 111.

    The new section is one twenty-fourth of a kilometer longer, so the result being one twenty-fourth above 222 kilometers is consistent.

    Answer

    4924\frac{49}{24}2449​ kilometers.

    Key idea

    Replacing part of a total subtracts the old part and adds its replacement.

  7. Problem 7 Zoe’s shortcut

    Zoe says 56+19=615\frac56+\frac19=\frac6{15}65​+91​=156​, since she added the numerators and added the denominators. Is she correct? Explain, and give the sum in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      A denominator names the size of each part, so ask whether sixths and ninths are the same size.

    2. Hint 2

      Estimate first: how should the sum compare with 56\frac5665​ alone, and how does Zoe’s result compare with it?

    3. Hint 3

      Rebuild both fractions over the least common multiple of 666 and 999, then combine the numerators.

    Answer

    No; the sum is 1718\frac{17}{18}1817​.

    Full solution

    Zoe’s result simplifies to 25\frac2552​, which is less than 12\frac1221​.

    But 56\frac5665​ alone is more than 12\frac1221​, and adding 19\frac1991​ can only make the total larger.

    So 615\frac6{15}156​ cannot be the sum.

    The denominators name part sizes, and sixths and ninths are different-sized parts.

    Their counts cannot be added until both are measured in one part size.

    Adding 666 and 999 instead names fifteenths, a part size that neither fraction uses.

    The least common multiple of 666 and 999 is 181818, so rebuild both fractions in eighteenths:

    56=1518\frac56=\frac{15}{18}65​=1815​ 19=218\frac19=\frac2{18}91​=182​

    Now both count eighteenths, so

    1518+218=1718.\frac{15}{18}+\frac2{18}=\frac{17}{18}.1815​+182​=1817​.

    The number 171717 is prime and is not a factor of 181818, so the sum is in lowest terms, and Zoe is not correct.

    Answer

    No; the sum is 1718\frac{17}{18}1817​.

    Key idea

    Fractions add only as counts of one part size, so their denominators are never added.

  8. Problem 8 Two submitted totals

    For 14+514\frac14+\frac5{14}41​+145​, Lina submits 1728\frac{17}{28}2817​ and Max submits 3456\frac{34}{56}5634​. Lina says both answers have the correct value, although only hers is in lowest terms. Is her statement correct? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Check the sum independently before comparing the two submitted forms.

    2. Hint 2

      Twenty-eighths can describe both starting fractions, and a common factor can relate the two submitted answers.

    Answer

    Yes; both values are 1728\frac{17}{28}2817​, and only Lina’s form is in lowest terms.

    Full solution

    The least common denominator is 282828, the least common multiple of 444 and 141414.

    Rewrite 14=728\frac14=\frac7{28}41​=287​ and

    514=1028.\frac5{14}=\frac{10}{28}.145​=2810​.

    Then

    728+1028=1728.\frac7{28}+\frac{10}{28}=\frac{17}{28}.287​+2810​=2817​.

    Max’s form is what the larger common denominator 565656, the product of 444 and 141414, produces: 14=1456\frac14=\frac{14}{56}41​=5614​ and 514=2056\frac5{14}=\frac{20}{56}145​=5620​, which total 3456\frac{34}{56}5634​.

    Dividing Max’s numerator and denominator by 222 gives

    3456=1728.\frac{34}{56}=\frac{17}{28}.5634​=2817​.

    Both answers therefore have the correct value.

    The numbers 171717 and 282828 share no factor above 111, while 343434 and 565656 share 222, so Lina’s full statement is correct.

    Answer

    Yes; both values are 1728\frac{17}{28}2817​, and only Lina’s form is in lowest terms.

    Key idea

    Any common denominator gives the correct sum, but one larger than the least, as the product of the denominators often is, leaves an extra common factor to divide out.

  9. Problem 9 A grouped calculation

    Tao rewrites 89−512−16\frac89-\frac5{12}-\frac1698​−125​−61​ as 89−(512−16)\frac89-\left(\frac5{12}-\frac16\right)98​−(125​−61​). Does this preserve the value? Find the value of each expression and explain.

    1. Hint 1

      In the original expression, both later fractions are taken away.

    2. Hint 2

      In the new expression, the parentheses form one difference that is taken away.

    3. Hint 3

      Thirty-sixths allow every term to be compared directly.

    Answer

    No; original: 1136\frac{11}{36}3611​; rewritten: 2336\frac{23}{36}3623​.

    Full solution

    Write 89=3236\frac89=\frac{32}{36}98​=3632​, 512=1536\frac5{12}=\frac{15}{36}125​=3615​, and

    16=636.\frac16=\frac6{36}.61​=366​.

    The original expression gives

    32−15−636=1136.\frac{32-15-6}{36}=\frac{11}{36}.3632−15−6​=3611​.

    The parentheses in the rewrite give

    15−636=936.\frac{15-6}{36}=\frac9{36}.3615−6​=369​.

    Taking that difference away gives

    32−936=2336.\frac{32-9}{36}=\frac{23}{36}.3632−9​=3623​.

    The rewrite takes away a smaller amount.

    Its inner subtraction reduces what is removed, whereas the original removes both fractions.

    The unequal results show that the value is not preserved.

    Answer

    No; original: 1136\frac{11}{36}3611​; rewritten: 2336\frac{23}{36}3623​.

    Key idea

    Grouping a string of subtractions can change which amounts are taken away.

  10. Problem 10 Matching box entries

    The same whole number must fill both boxes in □10+□15=1\frac{\square}{10}+\frac{\square}{15}=110□​+15□​=1. Find that number and explain why no other whole number works.

    1. Hint 1

      Think of the two fractions as counts of one shared part size.

    2. Hint 2

      In thirtieths, each copy of the box contributes three parts from the first fraction and two from the second.

    3. Hint 3

      Find how many groups of that combined size make thirty thirtieths.

    Answer

    666.

    Full solution

    In thirtieths, the first numerator becomes three times the box and the second becomes twice the box.

    Together they count five times the box in thirtieths.

    One whole needs thirty thirtieths, so the box is

    30÷5=6.30\div5=6.30÷5=6.

    The check is

    610=1830\frac6{10}=\frac{18}{30}106​=3018​ 615=1230\frac6{15}=\frac{12}{30}156​=3012​

    and their sum is

    3030=1.\frac{30}{30}=1.3030​=1.

    A smaller whole number supplies fewer than thirty parts and a larger one supplies more, so no other whole number works.

    Answer

    666.

    Key idea

    A repeated missing count can be found by counting how many common-sized parts each copy contributes.
