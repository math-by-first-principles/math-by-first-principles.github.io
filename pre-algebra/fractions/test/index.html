    The segment of a number line from 000 to 111 is divided into 777 equal steps. Which fraction sits at the third mark counting from 000?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    You chose A: Writes the number of steps in the whole on top and the number of steps counted underneath, reversing the two jobs.

    You chose B: Puts the number of steps still to come, 7−3=47 - 3 = 47−3=4, under the bar instead of the number of steps in the whole.

    You chose D: Names the mark by its position number as a unit fraction, as though the third mark were one third.

    The denominator records how many equal steps the whole was cut into, and the numerator counts how many of those steps you take. Seven steps make the whole, and you take three of them.

    37=17+17+17\frac{3}{7} = \frac{1}{7} + \frac{1}{7} + \frac{1}{7}73​=71​+71​+71​

    So the third mark is three sevenths, and it lands before 111 because 333 is smaller than 777.

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    You chose A: Treats a zero anywhere in a fraction as fatal, though taking none of the seven parts is a perfectly good amount, namely 000.

    You chose B: Reads a numerator and denominator that match as canceling each other into nothing, rather than as taking every part, which is one whole.

    You chose D: Reads a denominator of 111 as no cutting at all and so no fraction, though it names the whole number 777.

    07=0,77=1,71=7,70 is undefined\frac{0}{7} = 0, \qquad \frac{7}{7} = 1, \qquad \frac{7}{1} = 7, \qquad \frac{7}{0} \text{ is undefined}70​=0,77​=1,17​=7,07​ is undefined

    A numerator of 000 is allowed; a denominator of 000 is not, because you cannot cut a whole into zero parts.

  1. Problem 1 Labels along a line

    Equally spaced marks on a number line include marks labeled 25\frac2552​ and 45\frac4554​, with exactly one mark between them. What fraction with denominator 555 belongs on the first mark to the right of 45\frac4554​? State whether that fraction is proper or improper.

    1. Hint 1

      The two labeled points are two equal steps apart.

    2. Hint 2

      Find the number of fifths in one step, then count one more step past the right label.

    Answer

    55\frac5555​; improper.

    Full solution

    The labeled points are two fifths apart and have two steps between them, so each step is one fifth.

    One step to the right of four fifths lands on five fifths.

    The new label is

    55=1.\frac55=1.55​=1.

    Its numerator equals its denominator, so the fraction is improper.

    Answer

    55\frac5555​; improper.

    Key idea

    Equal spacing fixes the part size of a number-line step, and a nonzero numerator equal to its denominator marks one whole.

  2. Problem 2 A label and a printer

    A label reads 1833\frac{18}{33}3318​. A printer can print a fraction only if its denominator is 222222, 242424 or 555555. Write every fraction the printer can print that has the same value as the label and a whole-number numerator.

    1. Hint 1

      None of 222222, 242424 and 555555 is a multiple of 333333, so the label needs a simpler name before it can be rebuilt.

    2. Hint 2

      Divide the numerator and denominator by the greatest common factor of 181818 and 333333, then ask which allowed denominators are multiples of the new denominator.

    Answer

    1222\frac{12}{22}2212​ and 3055\frac{30}{55}5530​; no fraction with denominator 242424 works.

    Full solution

    The greatest common factor of 181818 and 333333 is 333.

    Dividing the numerator and denominator by 333 gives

    1833=611.\frac{18}{33}=\frac6{11}.3318​=116​.

    Since 22=11×222=11\times222=11×2, multiplying the numerator and denominator by 222 gives

    611=1222.\frac6{11}=\frac{12}{22}.116​=2212​.

    Since 55=11×555=11\times555=11×5, multiplying them by 555 gives

    611=3055.\frac6{11}=\frac{30}{55}.116​=5530​.

    Any fraction equal to 611\frac6{11}116​ simplifies to 611\frac6{11}116​, so its denominator is 111111 times the common factor that was divided out.

    Denominator 242424 is not a multiple of 111111, since 24÷1124\div1124÷11 is 222 remainder 222, so no fraction with denominator 242424 works.

    The cross products confirm it: a fraction n24\frac{n}{24}24n​ equal to 611\frac6{11}116​ would need 11×n11\times n11×n to equal 6×246\times246×24, which is 144144144.

    But 144144144 is not a multiple of 111111, since 11×13=14311\times13=14311×13=143 and 11×14=15411\times14=15411×14=154, so no whole number nnn works.

    As a check, 18×2218\times2218×22 and 33×1233\times1233×12 both equal 396396396, and 18×5518\times5518×55 and 33×3033\times3033×30 both equal 990990990.

    Answer

    1222\frac{12}{22}2212​ and 3055\frac{30}{55}5530​; no fraction with denominator 242424 works.

    Key idea

    Simplify before rebuilding: a required denominator can be reached with a whole-number numerator exactly when it is a multiple of the lowest-terms denominator, even when it is not a multiple of the original denominator.

  3. Problem 3 Improper fractions in elevenths

    Write every improper fraction with denominator 111111 and a whole-number numerator less than 404040 whose mixed-number form has fraction part 411\frac4{11}114​.

    1. Hint 1

      The fraction part of a mixed number holds the elevenths left over after the complete wholes.

    2. Hint 2

      Each numerator is eleven times the number of wholes, plus four.

    Answer

    1511\frac{15}{11}1115​, 2611\frac{26}{11}1126​ and 3711\frac{37}{11}1137​.

    Full solution

    Each complete whole uses eleven elevenths, and the fraction part adds four more.

    So each numerator is eleven times the number of wholes, plus four.

    The fraction must be improper, so it has at least one whole, since 411\frac4{11}114​ on its own is proper.

    One, two and three wholes give these numerators:

    1×11+4=152×11+4=263×11+4=37\begin{aligned} 1\times11+4 &= 15 \\ 2\times11+4 &= 26 \\ 3\times11+4 &= 37 \end{aligned}1×11+42×11+43×11+4​=15=26=37​

    Four wholes would give 484848, which is not less than 404040.

    Dividing checks each one: 15÷1115\div1115÷11 is 111 remainder 444, 26÷1126\div1126÷11 is 222 remainder 444, and 37÷1137\div1137÷11 is 333 remainder 444.

    So their mixed-number forms are 14111\tfrac4{11}1114​, 24112\tfrac4{11}2114​ and 34113\tfrac4{11}3114​, each with fraction part 411\frac4{11}114​.

    Answer

    1511\frac{15}{11}1115​, 2611\frac{26}{11}1126​ and 3711\frac{37}{11}1137​.

    Key idea

    When the fraction part keeps the original denominator, the numerator is that denominator times the whole-number part, plus the numerator of the fraction part.

  4. Problem 4 A report field

    A report records the total 34+110\frac34+\frac1{10}43​+101​ using a fraction whose denominator must be 606060. What whole number belongs in its numerator field?

    1. Hint 1

      Find the value of the total first; the required denominator only changes how that value is written.

    2. Hint 2

      Twentieths combine the two terms; then compare that denominator with 606060.

    Answer

    515151.

    Full solution

    The fractions can both be written in twentieths: 34=1520\frac34=\frac{15}{20}43​=2015​ and

    110=220.\frac1{10}=\frac2{20}.101​=202​.

    Their sum is

    1520+220=1720.\frac{15}{20}+\frac2{20}=\frac{17}{20}.2015​+202​=2017​.

    The report requires denominator 606060, which is three times 202020.

    Multiply the numerator and denominator by 333:

    1720=5160.\frac{17}{20}=\frac{51}{60}.2017​=6051​.

    The numerator field must contain 515151.

    Answer

    515151.

    Key idea

    The value of a sum and the denominator required to display it are separate parts of the task.

  5. Problem 5 A hidden starting card

    A positive number on a card is multiplied by 310\frac3{10}103​. That result is divided by 914\frac9{14}149​, giving 712\frac7{12}127​. What number was on the card?

    1. Hint 1

      Work backward through the two operations in reverse order.

    2. Hint 2

      Undoing a division by 914\frac9{14}149​ is multiplying by 914\frac9{14}149​; undoing a multiplication by 310\frac3{10}103​ is dividing by 310\frac3{10}103​.

    Answer

    54\frac5445​ (or 1141\tfrac14141​).

    Full solution

    Multiplying by 914\frac9{14}149​ undoes the division, so the number just before it was

    712×914=38.\frac7{12}\times\frac9{14}=\frac38.127​×149​=83​.

    Dividing by 310\frac3{10}103​ undoes the first multiplication, and dividing by 310\frac3{10}103​ is multiplying by 103\frac{10}3310​.

    The starting number is

    38×103=54.\frac38\times\frac{10}3=\frac54.83​×310​=45​.

    Check forward: 54\frac5445​ multiplied by 310\frac3{10}103​ gives 38\frac3883​.

    Dividing by 914\frac9{14}149​ gives

    38×149=712,\frac38\times\frac{14}9=\frac7{12},83​×914​=127​,

    which is the stated final result.

    Answer

    54\frac5445​ (or 1141\tfrac14141​).

    Key idea

    A sequence of multiplication and division can be undone in reverse order.

  6. Problem 6 Tape through a machine

    A machine advances tape 1561\tfrac56165​ centimeters for each turn of its handle. An operator turns it 1141\tfrac14141​ turns, pauses, and then turns it another 15121\tfrac5{12}1125​ turns in the same direction. How far does the tape advance? Give a mixed number with its fraction part in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      Combine the turns before finding the distance they produce.

    2. Hint 2

      The fraction parts of the turn counts can be combined in twelfths.

    3. Hint 3

      Convert the total turns and the distance per turn to improper fractions before multiplying.

    Answer

    4894\tfrac89498​ centimeters.

    Full solution

    The whole parts of the turn counts give 1+1=21+1=21+1=2.

    In twelfths, 14=312\frac14=\frac3{12}41​=123​, and

    312+512=812,\frac3{12}+\frac5{12}=\frac8{12},123​+125​=128​,

    which is 23\frac2332​ after dividing the numerator and denominator by 444.

    So the handle turns 2232\tfrac23232​ times in all, and since 2×3+2=82\times3+2=82×3+2=8, that is 83\frac8338​ turns.

    Since 1×6+5=111\times6+5=111×6+5=11, the advance per turn is 156=1161\tfrac56=\frac{11}6165​=611​ centimeters.

    Multiply the total turns by the advance per turn.

    The 888 and the 666 share a factor of 222, which cancels to leave 43×113\frac43\times\frac{11}334​×311​, so

    83×116=449.\frac83\times\frac{11}6=\frac{44}9.38​×611​=944​.

    Since 444444 and 999 share no factor above 111, this is in lowest terms.

    Since 44=4×9+844=4\times9+844=4×9+8, the tape advances 4894\tfrac89498​ centimeters.

    Two turns advance 3233\tfrac23332​ centimeters and three turns advance 5125\tfrac12521​ centimeters.

    The total is between two and three turns, and 4894\tfrac89498​ centimeters lies between those two advances, as expected.

    Answer

    4894\tfrac89498​ centimeters.

    Key idea

    Distance is the advance per turn times the total number of turns, so the turns can be combined before a single multiplication.

  7. Problem 7 Rope left after two cuts

    A rope is 9189\tfrac18981​ meters long. Two pieces are cut from it, one 1231\tfrac23132​ meters long and the other 17121\tfrac7{12}1127​ meters long, and no rope is lost in the cutting. How long is the rope that is left? Give a mixed number in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      The rope that is left is the starting length with both cut pieces taken away.

    2. Hint 2

      Find the total length of the two cut pieces first, then remove it from the starting length.

    3. Hint 3

      Use a common denominator whenever fraction parts are added or subtracted.

    Answer

    5785\tfrac78587​ meters.

    Full solution

    The cut pieces have fraction parts 23=812\frac23=\frac8{12}32​=128​ and 712\frac7{12}127​.

    Those add to 1512\frac{15}{12}1215​, which is 1141\tfrac14141​.

    The whole parts give 1+1=21+1=21+1=2, and the carried 111 makes the two pieces 3143\tfrac14341​ meters long in total.

    Subtract this total from the starting length, working in eighths.

    Since 9×8+1=739\times8+1=739×8+1=73, the starting length is 738\frac{73}8873​ meters.

    Since 3×4+1=133\times4+1=133×4+1=13, the pieces total 134\frac{13}4413​ meters, which is 268\frac{26}8826​ meters.

    The rope left is

    738−268=478.\frac{73}8-\frac{26}8=\frac{47}8.873​−826​=847​.

    Since 47=5×8+747=5\times8+747=5×8+7, that is 5785\tfrac78587​ meters.

    As a check, the cut pieces, 3143\tfrac14341​ meters, and the rope left, 5785\tfrac78587​ meters, together make 9189\tfrac18981​ meters, the starting length.

    Answer

    5785\tfrac78587​ meters.

    Key idea

    The length left after several cuts is the starting length minus the total cut, so the cut pieces can be combined before one subtraction.

  8. Problem 8 A fraction in the gap

    Name a fraction that lies between 79\frac7997​ and 1115\frac{11}{15}1511​ on the number line, and explain how you know it lies between them.

    1. Hint 1

      Two fractions are easiest to compare when they count the same size of part.

    2. Hint 2

      Rewrite both over a common multiple of 999 and 151515, then look for a count in between.

    Answer

    Any fraction greater than 1115\frac{11}{15}1511​ and less than 79\frac7997​, for example 3445\frac{34}{45}4534​ (or 6790\frac{67}{90}9067​).

    Full solution

    The least common multiple of 999 and 151515 is 454545, so rewrite both fractions in forty-fifths.

    Multiply the numerator and denominator of 79\frac7997​ by 555:

    79=3545.\frac79=\frac{35}{45}.97​=4535​.

    Multiply the numerator and denominator of 1115\frac{11}{15}1511​ by 333:

    1115=3345.\frac{11}{15}=\frac{33}{45}.1511​=4533​.

    Both fractions now count forty-fifths, so the numerators give the order, and 1115\frac{11}{15}1511​ is the smaller.

    The count 343434 is more than 333333 and less than 353535, so 3445\frac{34}{45}4534​ lies between the two fractions.

    Other answers work too.

    In ninetieths the two fractions are 6690\frac{66}{90}9066​ and 7090\frac{70}{90}9070​, so 6790\frac{67}{90}9067​, 6890\frac{68}{90}9068​ and 6990\frac{69}{90}9069​ lie between them, and 6890\frac{68}{90}9068​ is 3445\frac{34}{45}4534​ again.

    Answer

    Any fraction greater than 1115\frac{11}{15}1511​ and less than 79\frac7997​, for example 3445\frac{34}{45}4534​ (or 6790\frac{67}{90}9067​).

    Key idea

    Once two fractions share a denominator, any count between their numerators names a fraction between them.

  9. Problem 9 Two ways to resize a photo

    Lena resizes a photo in two steps: she multiplies every length in it by 56\frac5665​, then multiplies every new length by 43\frac4334​. Omar resizes the same photo in one step, dividing every length by 910\frac9{10}109​. Pat says the two resized photos match for every starting photo, whatever its lengths. Is Pat correct? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare the single multiplier that each resizing applies to one length.

    2. Hint 2

      Combine Lena’s two factors into one, and rewrite Omar’s division as a multiplication.

    Answer

    Yes; both resizings multiply every length by 109\frac{10}9910​.

    Full solution

    One photo could show that the two resizings agree for that photo, but agreement for every photo needs the overall multipliers.

    Lena’s two successive multipliers combine to

    56×43=109.\frac56\times\frac43=\frac{10}9.65​×34​=910​.

    Omar divides by 910\frac9{10}109​, so he multiplies by its reciprocal 109\frac{10}9910​.

    Both resizings therefore multiply every length by exactly the same fraction.

    The resized lengths agree for every starting photo, so Pat is correct.

    Answer

    Yes; both resizings multiply every length by 109\frac{10}9910​.

    Key idea

    Two chains of multiplications and divisions by nonzero fractions give the same output for every starting value when their overall multipliers are equal.

  10. Problem 10 Working bulbs in three cartons

    Mia describes a carton of light bulbs by one fraction: the number of working bulbs in the carton over the number of bulbs in the carton. Carton A holds 141414 bulbs, none of them working. Carton B holds 141414 bulbs, all of them working. Carton C is empty. For each carton, does Mia’s fraction name a number? Give its value, or explain why it names none.

    1. Hint 1

      Write each carton’s fraction first, with the two counts as its numerator and denominator.

    2. Hint 2

      Read each fraction as a division, and ask whether that division picks out exactly one number.

    Answer

    Carton A: 014=0\frac0{14}=0140​=0. Carton B: 1414=1\frac{14}{14}=11414​=1. Carton C: 00\frac0000​ names no number.

    Full solution

    Carton A has 000 working bulbs out of 141414, so its fraction is 014\frac0{14}140​.

    The division 0÷140\div140÷14 asks for the number that gives 000 when multiplied by 141414, and only 000 does, so

    014=0.\frac0{14}=0.140​=0.

    Carton B has 141414 working bulbs out of 141414, so its fraction is 1414\frac{14}{14}1414​.

    Only 111 gives 141414 when multiplied by 141414, so

    1414=1.\frac{14}{14}=1.1414​=1.

    Carton C has 000 working bulbs out of 000 bulbs, so its fraction is 00\frac0000​.

    With no bulbs at all, there is no share of the carton’s bulbs to describe.

    As a division, 0÷00\div00÷0 asks for the number that gives 000 when multiplied by 000, and because any number would do, no single value answers it, so 00\frac0000​ names no number.

    Answer

    Carton A: 014=0\frac0{14}=0140​=0. Carton B: 1414=1\frac{14}{14}=11414​=1. Carton C: 00\frac0000​ names no number.

    Key idea

    A zero numerator over a nonzero denominator names 000, a nonzero count over itself names 111, and 00\frac0000​ names no number, because every number times 000 gives 000.
