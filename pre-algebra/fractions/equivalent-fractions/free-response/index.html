  1. Problem 1 A fraction built from products

    Write 3×148×21\frac{3\times14}{8\times21}8×213×14​ in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      The greatest common factor of the top and bottom is built from the primes they share.

    2. Hint 2

      Break each of 333, 141414, 888 and 212121 into prime factors.

    3. Hint 3

      Divide out every prime the top and bottom share, then see what is left of each.

    Answer

    14\frac1441​.

    Full solution

    Break each number into primes: 14=2×714=2\times714=2×7, 8=2×2×28=2\times2\times28=2×2×2 and

    21=3×7.21=3\times7.21=3×7.

    So the numerator is 2×3×72\times3\times72×3×7 and the denominator is 2×2×2×3×72\times2\times2\times3\times72×2×2×3×7.

    The top has one 222 and the bottom has three, and each has one 333 and one 777, so taking each shared prime at the smaller of its two counts gives the greatest common factor

    2×3×7=42.2\times3\times7=42.2×3×7=42.

    Dividing both by 424242 leaves 111 on top, because every prime in the numerator is shared, and 2×2=42\times2=42×2=4 on the bottom:

    3×148×21=14.\frac{3\times14}{8\times21}=\frac14.8×213×14​=41​.

    The numbers 111 and 444 share no factor above 111, so the fraction is in lowest terms.

    As a check, the numerator is 3×14=423\times14=423×14=42 and the denominator is 8×21=1688\times21=1688×21=168, which is 4×424\times424×42.

    Answer

    14\frac1441​.

    Key idea

    To put a fraction built from products in lowest terms, divide its top and bottom by their greatest common factor, the product of the primes they share, each taken at the smaller of its two counts, read from the factors of each product.

  2. Problem 2 Equal fractions in a range

    Which fractions equal to 715\frac7{15}157​ have whole-number terms and a denominator between 404040 and 808080?

    1. Hint 1

      Each fraction must name the same amount using a different part size.

    2. Hint 2

      Since 777 and 151515 share no factor above 111, any fraction equal to 715\frac7{15}157​ is 715\frac7{15}157​ built up by one whole-number multiplier.

    3. Hint 3

      Find the multiples of 151515 between 404040 and 808080, then multiply 777 by the number that turns 151515 into each one.

    Answer

    2145\frac{21}{45}4521​, 2860\frac{28}{60}6028​ and 3575\frac{35}{75}7535​.

    Full solution

    The numbers 777 and 151515 share no factor above 111, so 715\frac7{15}157​ is in lowest terms.

    Any fraction equal to it has the same lowest-terms form.

    So dividing the numerator and denominator of such a fraction by their greatest common factor gives 715\frac7{15}157​, which means the fraction is 715\frac7{15}157​ with its numerator and denominator multiplied by that same whole number.

    Its denominator is therefore a multiple of 151515.

    The multiples of 151515 between 404040 and 808080 are 454545, 606060 and 757575, which are 151515 times 333, 444 and 555.

    Multiply the numerator by the same number each time:

    7×315×3=2145\frac{7\times3}{15\times3}=\frac{21}{45}15×37×3​=4521​ 7×415×4=2860\frac{7\times4}{15\times4}=\frac{28}{60}15×47×4​=6028​ 7×515×5=3575\frac{7\times5}{15\times5}=\frac{35}{75}15×57×5​=7535​

    The next multiple of 151515 is 909090, which is past 808080, so these three are all of them.

    Answer

    2145\frac{21}{45}4521​, 2860\frac{28}{60}6028​ and 3575\frac{35}{75}7535​.

    Key idea

    Listing the multiples of a fraction's lowest-terms denominator that fall in a range finds every equal fraction with whole-number terms whose denominator lies in that range.

  3. Problem 3 A fraction built from sums

    Write 24+1235+19\frac{24+12}{35+19}35+1924+12​ in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      The whole expression above the bar is the numerator, and the whole expression below is the denominator.

    2. Hint 2

      After finding both numbers, look for their greatest common factor.

    Answer

    23\frac2332​.

    Full solution

    Evaluate the grouped parts of the fraction:

    24+12=3624+12=3624+12=36 35+19=5435+19=5435+19=54

    The greatest common factor of 363636 and 545454 is 181818.

    Dividing both by 181818 gives

    3654=23.\frac{36}{54}=\frac23.5436​=32​.

    The remaining numbers 222 and 333 have no common factor above 111, so the fraction is in lowest terms.

    Answer

    23\frac2332​.

    Key idea

    When the top or bottom of a fraction is a sum, evaluate the sum before simplifying the resulting fraction.

  4. Problem 4 A dial setting

    A dial stores a setting as 3342\frac{33}{42}4233​. Its screen must show the same value with denominator 848484, while its printed report must use lowest terms. What should the screen and the report show?

    1. Hint 1

      The screen and the report need different names for one value.

    2. Hint 2

      The screen denominator is twice the stored denominator.

    3. Hint 3

      For the report, find the greatest common factor of 333333 and 424242.

    Answer

    Screen: 6684\frac{66}{84}8466​; report: 1114\frac{11}{14}1411​.

    Full solution

    The denominator doubles from 424242 to 848484, so double the numerator as well:

    3342=6684.\frac{33}{42}=\frac{66}{84}.4233​=8466​.

    This is what the screen shows.

    The greatest common factor of 333333 and 424242 is 333.

    Divide both by 333:

    3342=1114.\frac{33}{42}=\frac{11}{14}.4233​=1411​.

    Since 111111 and 141414 share no factor above 111, this is what the report shows.

    Both describe the same dial setting.

    Answer

    Screen: 6684\frac{66}{84}8466​; report: 1114\frac{11}{14}1411​.

    Key idea

    A required denominator and lowest terms are different forms that can describe the same value.

  5. Problem 5 An editor’s division

    An editor divides the numerator and denominator of a fraction by 666 and gets 49\frac4994​. What was the original fraction, and was 666 the greatest common factor of its numerator and denominator?

    1. Hint 1

      Undoing the editor’s division recovers the original top and bottom, and the greatest common factor is the largest number that divides both.

    2. Hint 2

      Write both original numbers as products of primes, then multiply together the primes they share.

    Answer

    2454\frac{24}{54}5424​; yes, 666 is the greatest common factor of 242424 and 545454.

    Full solution

    Before division by 666, the numerator was 4×6=244\times6=244×6=24 and the denominator was

    9×6=54.9\times6=54.9×6=54.

    Thus the original fraction was

    2454.\frac{24}{54}.5424​.

    In primes, 24=2×2×2×324=2\times2\times2\times324=2×2×2×3 and

    54=2×3×3×3.54=2\times3\times3\times3.54=2×3×3×3.

    The primes they share are one 222 and one 333, so their greatest common factor is

    2×3=6.2\times3=6.2×3=6.

    As a check, 24=6×424=6\times424=6×4 and 54=6×954=6\times954=6×9, and the leftovers 4=2×24=2\times24=2×2 and 9=3×39=3\times39=3×3 share no prime, so 49\frac4994​ is in lowest terms.

    Had 666 not been the greatest common factor, 444 and 999 would still share a factor, so 666 was the greatest.

    Answer

    2454\frac{24}{54}5424​; yes, 666 is the greatest common factor of 242424 and 545454.

    Key idea

    Reversing a common-factor division restores the original numerator and denominator together, and the divisor was their greatest common factor exactly when the result is in lowest terms.

  6. Problem 6 Two sensor records

    Two sensor records show 614\frac6{14}146​ and 1021\frac{10}{21}2110​. Lower exactly one of the two numerators by 111, leaving every other number unchanged, so that the records agree. Which record should change, and what should it become? Justify your decision.

    1. Hint 1

      There are two possible edited pairs, and their values determine which change works.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare the cross products for each possible pair.

    Answer

    Change 1021\frac{10}{21}2110​ to 921\frac9{21}219​; leave 614\frac6{14}146​ unchanged.

    Full solution

    If the first numerator decreases, the records are 514\frac5{14}145​ and 1021\frac{10}{21}2110​.

    Their cross products are

    5×21=1055\times21=1055×21=105 14×10=14014\times10=14014×10=140

    These differ, so that change does not make the records agree.

    If the second numerator decreases, the records are 614\frac6{14}146​ and 921\frac9{21}219​.

    Their cross products are

    6×21=1266\times21=1266×21=126 14×9=12614\times9=12614×9=126

    These match, so the second record should become 921\frac9{21}219​.

    As a check, both final records reduce to 37\frac3773​.

    Both allowed changes have been tested, so this is the one that works.

    Answer

    Change 1021\frac{10}{21}2110​ to 921\frac9{21}219​; leave 614\frac6{14}146​ unchanged.

    Key idea

    Cross products can test each allowed change when two fraction records must agree.

  7. Problem 7 Allowed divisions

    A machine stores the fraction 165195\frac{165}{195}195165​. It may divide both numbers by any common factor, but its new denominator must be less than 404040. List every new fraction it can produce in one such division.

    1. Hint 1

      Every allowed division uses a number that divides both the numerator and the denominator.

    2. Hint 2

      List those common factors, then test which ones bring the denominator below 404040.

    Answer

    3339\frac{33}{39}3933​ and 1113\frac{11}{13}1311​.

    Full solution

    The factors of 165165165 are 111, 333, 555, 111111, 151515, 333333, 555555 and 165165165.

    Of these, the ones that also divide 195195195 are 111, 333, 555 and 151515, so those are the common factors.

    Dividing 195195195 by them gives denominators 195195195, 656565, 393939 and 131313, respectively.

    Only division by 555 or 151515 gives a denominator less than 404040:

    165÷5195÷5=3339\frac{165\div5}{195\div5}=\frac{33}{39}195÷5165÷5​=3933​ 165÷15195÷15=1113.\frac{165\div15}{195\div15}=\frac{11}{13}.195÷15165÷15​=1311​.

    These are all the allowed results because every common factor has been checked.

    Answer

    3339\frac{33}{39}3933​ and 1113\frac{11}{13}1311​.

    Key idea

    A restriction on a new denominator can be checked against the complete list of common factors.

  8. Problem 8 Milo’s claim

    Milo says two fractions can be equal only if multiplying the numerator and denominator of one by the same whole number gives the other. Is he correct? Support your answer with an example.

    1. Hint 1

      Equal fractions share one lowest-terms form.

    2. Hint 2

      Test Milo’s claim on two different fractions that simplify to the same lowest-terms form.

    3. Hint 3

      Check that no whole number turns one of your denominators into the other.

    Answer

    No; for example 2745=3050\frac{27}{45}=\frac{30}{50}4527​=5030​ (any pair of equal fractions in which neither denominator is a whole-number multiple of the other is accepted).

    Full solution

    One pair of equal fractions in which neither is the other with its numerator and denominator multiplied by one whole number is enough to show that Milo is wrong.

    Start from a fraction in lowest terms, such as 35\frac3553​.

    Since 5×9=455\times9=455×9=45, multiply the numerator and denominator of 35\frac3553​ by 999:

    35=2745.\frac35=\frac{27}{45}.53​=4527​.

    Since 5×10=505\times10=505×10=50, multiply them by 101010 instead:

    35=3050.\frac35=\frac{30}{50}.53​=5030​.

    Both results equal 35\frac3553​, so they equal each other.

    As a check, their cross products agree:

    27×50=135027\times50=135027×50=1350 45×30=135045\times30=135045×30=1350

    No whole number times 454545 gives 505050, and no whole number times 505050 gives 454545.

    So neither fraction is the other with its numerator and denominator multiplied by one whole number, and Milo is not correct.

    Both are built from the same lowest-terms form, 35\frac3553​, by different multipliers.

    Answer

    No; for example 2745=3050\frac{27}{45}=\frac{30}{50}4527​=5030​ (any pair of equal fractions in which neither denominator is a whole-number multiple of the other is accepted).

    Key idea

    Two fractions can be equal even when neither is the other with its numerator and denominator multiplied by one whole number.

  9. Problem 9 A finer pattern

    A pattern is divided into nine equal parts, and four of them are colored. Every part is divided into five equal pieces, and then each new piece is divided into two equal pieces. Mara says the colored fraction is now 4090\frac{40}{90}9040​ and the colored area has not changed. Is she correct? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Count what the two stages of cutting do to each original part.

    2. Hint 2

      The same two stages apply to every colored part and every part in the whole.

    Answer

    Yes; 4090=49\frac{40}{90}=\frac499040​=94​.

    Full solution

    Each original ninth becomes 5×2=105\times2=105×2=10 equal pieces.

    The nine parts of the whole therefore become 9×10=909\times10=909×10=90 pieces, and the four colored parts become 4×10=404\times10=404×10=40 colored pieces.

    The new description is

    4×109×10=4090.\frac{4\times10}{9\times10}=\frac{40}{90}.9×104×10​=9040​.

    No area was added or removed by drawing the extra dividing lines, so the amount colored is unchanged.

    Dividing 404040 and 909090 by 101010 returns 49\frac4994​.

    Answer

    Yes; 4090=49\frac{40}{90}=\frac499040​=94​.

    Key idea

    Repeated equal subdivision changes the counts of all parts together without changing the amount represented.

  10. Problem 10 Kai’s replacement

    Kai replaces 5481\frac{54}{81}8154​ with 58\frac5885​ by deleting the last digit of each number. Does the replacement preserve the value? Give the original fraction in lowest terms and justify your decision.

    1. Hint 1

      Two fractions are equal exactly when their lowest-terms forms match.

    2. Hint 2

      Simplify the original fraction, then compare it with the replacement.

    Answer

    No; 5481=23\frac{54}{81}=\frac238154​=32​, and 23≠58\frac23\ne\frac5832​=85​.

    Full solution

    The greatest common factor of 545454 and 818181 is 272727, so

    5481=23.\frac{54}{81}=\frac23.8154​=32​.

    The proposed replacement is 58\frac5885​.

    The cross products for 23\frac2332​ and 58\frac5885​ are

    2×8=162\times8=162×8=16 3×5=15.3\times5=15.3×5=15.

    Since they do not match, the replacement changes the value.

    Here, deleting the last digits turned 545454 into 555 and 818181 into 888, which is not dividing both numbers by one common factor.

    Answer

    No; 5481=23\frac{54}{81}=\frac238154​=32​, and 23≠58\frac23\ne\frac5832​=85​.

    Key idea

    Simplifying removes a common factor from the numbers, not matching positions from their written digits.
