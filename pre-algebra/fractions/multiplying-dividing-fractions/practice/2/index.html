    Find 34×89\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{8}{9}43​×98​, written in lowest terms.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Multiply the numerators together and the denominators together.

    34×89=3×84×9=2436\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{8}{9} = \frac{3 \times 8}{4 \times 9} = \frac{24}{36}43​×98​=4×93×8​=3624​

    Now simplify by the greatest common factor of 242424 and 363636, which is 121212.

    2436=24÷1236÷12=23\frac{24}{36} = \frac{24 \div 12}{36 \div 12} = \frac{2}{3}3624​=36÷1224÷12​=32​

    Solution
