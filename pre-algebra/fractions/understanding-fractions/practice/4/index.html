    What is 47\frac{4}{7}74​ of 636363?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    One seventh of 636363 is 63÷7=963 \div 7 = 963÷7=9, then take 444 of those parts.

    4×9=364 \times 9 = 364×9=36

    So 47\frac{4}{7}74​ of 636363 is 363636.

    If 14\frac{1}{4}41​ of a number is 666, what is 34\frac{3}{4}43​ of that number?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Since one fourth is 666, three fourths is three of those parts.

    3×6=183 \times 6 = 183×6=18

    So 34\frac{3}{4}43​ of the number is 181818. (The whole number is 242424, and 34\frac{3}{4}43​ of 242424 is indeed 181818.)

    Which of these is NOT a way to write the number 111?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    A fraction equals 111 when its numerator and denominator are equal. Check each one.

    1010=1,33=1,2525=1\frac{10}{10} = 1, \quad \frac{3}{3} = 1, \quad \frac{25}{25} = 11010​=1,33​=1,2525​=1

    But 81=8÷1=8\frac{8}{1} = 8 \div 1 = 818​=8÷1=8, not 111, so it is the exception.

  5. 5

    A pie is cut into 121212 equal slices. One family eats 13\frac{1}{3}31​ of the pie and another eats 14\frac{1}{4}41​ of it. How many slices are left?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    One third of 121212 slices is 12÷3=412 \div 3 = 412÷3=4 slices, and one fourth of 121212 is 12÷4=312 \div 4 = 312÷4=3 slices, so 4+3=74 + 3 = 74+3=7 slices are eaten.

    12−7=512 - 7 = 512−7=5

    That leaves 555 slices.

    Which fraction is greater, 45\frac{4}{5}54​ or 910\frac{9}{10}109​? (Hint: compare how far each falls short of 111.)

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Each fraction falls one unit fraction short of 111: 45=1−15\frac{4}{5} = 1 - \frac{1}{5}54​=1−51​ and 910=1−110\frac{9}{10} = 1 - \frac{1}{10}109​=1−101​. The one missing the smaller piece is closer to 111.

    110<15  ⇒  910>45\frac{1}{10} < \frac{1}{5} \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{9}{10} > \frac{4}{5}101​<51​⇒109​>54​

    Since 910\frac{9}{10}109​ is only 110\frac{1}{10}101​ short, it is the greater fraction.

  7. 7

    Half of a pizza is split equally among 444 friends. What fraction of the whole pizza does each friend get?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Cut the half-pizza into 444 equal pieces. If the whole pizza were cut the same way, each half would hold 444 pieces, so the whole holds 888 equal pieces.

    each piece=18 of the whole pizza\text{each piece} = \frac{1}{8} \text{ of the whole pizza}each piece=81​ of the whole pizza

    Each friend gets one of those pieces, which is 18\frac{1}{8}81​ of the whole.

    Which symbol makes a true statement: 66  □  89\frac{6}{6} \;\square\; \frac{8}{9}66​□98​?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    First simplify what you can: 66=1\frac{6}{6} = 166​=1, while 89\frac{8}{9}98​ is a proper fraction, so it is less than 111.

    66=1>89\frac{6}{6} = 1 > \frac{8}{9}66​=1>98​

    Since 89\frac{8}{9}98​ falls one ninth short of a whole, 66\frac{6}{6}66​ is greater.

  9. 9

    For which value of the blank is 8□\frac{8}{\square}□8​ an improper fraction?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A fraction is improper when its denominator is less than or equal to its numerator.

    □≤8\square \le 8□≤8

    Of the choices, only 666 is at most 888, so 86\frac{8}{6}68​ is improper (its value is more than 111). The values 999, 101010, and 121212 all give proper fractions.

  10. 10

    A bottle holds 34\frac{3}{4}43​ of a liter and you pour out 14\frac{1}{4}41​ of a liter. What fraction of a liter is left?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Both amounts are measured in fourths of a liter, so subtract the numerators over the common denominator 444.

    34−14=24\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4}43​−41​=42​

    Since 3−1=23 - 1 = 23−1=2 fourths remain, the leftover is 24\frac{2}{4}42​ of a liter.
