  1. 1

    A number's prime factorization is 22×3×72^2 \times 3 \times 722×3×7. What is the number?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Expand the powers and multiply.

    22×3×7=4×3×7=842^2 \times 3 \times 7 = 4 \times 3 \times 7 = 8422×3×7=4×3×7=84

    So the number is 848484. The value 42=2×3×742 = 2 \times 3 \times 742=2×3×7 has only one 222.

  2. 2

    What is the prime factorization of 286286286?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    It is even: 286÷2=143286 \div 2 = 143286÷2=143. Now 143143143 is odd with digit sum 888, so test 777 (no), then 111111: 143÷11=13143 \div 11 = 13143÷11=13.

    286=2×11×13286 = 2 \times 11 \times 13286=2×11×13

    Both 111111 and 131313 are prime, so the factorization is complete.

  3. 3

    A number's prime factorization is 23×522^3 \times 5^223×52. How many times does the prime 222 appear?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Read the exponent on the prime 222.

    23=2×2×22^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 223=2×2×2

    The exponent 333 counts the copies, so 222 appears three times (the value 23=82^3 = 823=8 is a different question).

  4. 4

    Which number has the prime factorization 2×32×522 \times 3^2 \times 5^22×32×52?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Expand and multiply, smallest primes first.

    2×32×52=2×9×25=4502 \times 3^2 \times 5^2 = 2 \times 9 \times 25 = 4502×32×52=2×9×25=450

    So the number is 450450450. (150=2×3×52150 = 2 \times 3 \times 5^2150=2×3×52 is missing a factor of 333.)

  5. 5

    What is the prime factorization of 1,0001{,}0001,000?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Take three 222s, then three 555s: 1000→500→250→1251000 \to 500 \to 250 \to 1251000→500→250→125, and 125=53125 = 5^3125=53.

    1,000=2×2×2×5×5×5=23×531{,}000 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 5 \times 5 \times 5 = 2^3 \times 5^31,000=2×2×2×5×5×5=23×53

    The forms 10310^3103 and 8×1258 \times 1258×125 are equal in value but not built from primes.

  6. 6

    What is the prime factorization of 675675675?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Its digit sum is 181818, so take the 333s, then the 555s: 675÷3=225675 \div 3 = 225675÷3=225, 225÷3=75225 \div 3 = 75225÷3=75, 75÷3=2575 \div 3 = 2575÷3=25, 25=5225 = 5^225=52.

    675=3×3×3×5×5=33×52675 = 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 5 \times 5 = 3^3 \times 5^2675=3×3×3×5×5=33×52

    The form 32×52=2253^2 \times 5^2 = 22532×52=225 has one 333 too few.

  7. 7

    Jada writes 300=4×75300 = 4 \times 75300=4×75 and Theo writes 300=6×50300 = 6 \times 50300=6×50. After finishing both trees down to primes, what happens?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Finish each: Jada gets 4×75=22×(3×52)4 \times 75 = 2^2 \times (3 \times 5^2)4×75=22×(3×52); Theo gets 6×50=(2×3)×(2×52)6 \times 50 = (2 \times 3) \times (2 \times 5^2)6×50=(2×3)×(2×52).

    300=2×2×3×5×5=22×3×52300 = 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5 \times 5 = 2^2 \times 3 \times 5^2300=2×2×3×5×5=22×3×52

    By the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic the starting split cannot change the answer, so both reach the same factorization.

    What is the prime factorization of 539539539?

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    It is odd, not a multiple of 333 or 555. Test 777: 539÷7=77539 \div 7 = 77539÷7=77, and 77=7×1177 = 7 \times 1177=7×11.

    Solution

  10. 10

    What is the prime factorization of 1,0801{,}0801,080?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Halve three times, then take the 333s: 1080→540→270→1351080 \to 540 \to 270 \to 1351080→540→270→135, and 135=27×5=33×5135 = 27 \times 5 = 3^3 \times 5135=27×5=33×5.

    1,080=23×33×51{,}080 = 2^3 \times 3^3 \times 51,080=23×33×5

    Three 222s and three 333s; 23×32×5=3602^3 \times 3^2 \times 5 = 36023×32×5=360 is missing a factor of 333.

  11. 11

    What is the prime factorization of 784784784?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Halve down to an odd number, then factor it: 784→392→196→98→49784 \to 392 \to 196 \to 98 \to 49784→392→196→98→49, and 49=7×749 = 7 \times 749=7×7.

    784=24×72784 = 2^4 \times 7^2784=24×72

    That is four 222s and two 777s; 16×4916 \times 4916×49 is the same value but not written with primes.

  12. 12

    Which number is a perfect square, recognizable because every exponent in its prime factorization is even?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A perfect square is a number times itself, so in its prime factorization every prime must appear an even number of times (the two equal copies split the primes evenly). Check 22×342^2 \times 3^422×34: two 2s and four 3s, both even, so the primes pair into two identical groups.

    22×34=(2×3×3)×(2×3×3)=18×18=3242^2 \times 3^4 = (2 \times 3 \times 3) \times (2 \times 3 \times 3) = 18 \times 18 = 32422×34=(2×3×3)×(2×3×3)=18×18=324

    Every exponent is even. Each of the others has a prime with an odd exponent (a leftover that cannot be paired), so they are not perfect squares.
