Question 1 of 12
  1. 1

    Which number is divisible by every one of 222, 444, and 888?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Divisibility by 888 is the strictest test here, and any multiple of 888 is automatically a multiple of 444 and 222. Test the last three digits.

    1,016→016=16=8×21{,}016 \to 016 = 16 = 8 \times 21,016→016=16=8×2

    So 1,0161{,}0161,016 is divisible by 888, hence by 444 and 222 as well. The others (012,020,028012, 020, 028012,020,028) are not multiples of 888.

  2. 2

    What is the remainder when 6,8546{,}8546,854 is divided by 999?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    A number leaves the same remainder when divided by 999 as its digit sum does. Add the digits, folding if needed.

    6+8+5+4=23,2+3=56 + 8 + 5 + 4 = 23, \qquad 2 + 3 = 56+8+5+4=23,2+3=5

    The digit sum folds down to 555, so the remainder is 555.

  3. 3

    A number is divisible by both 333 and 444. Which of these must it be divisible by?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The numbers 333 and 444 share no common factor above 111, so passing both tests forces divisibility by their product.

    3×4=123 \times 4 = 123×4=12

    So the number must be divisible by 121212. It need not reach 242424 or 888; for example 121212 is divisible by 333 and 444 but by neither of those, and a number divisible by 333 and 444 need not end in 000, so 101010 is not forced either.

  4. 4

    Which number is divisible by 999 but not by 666?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Divisible by 999 needs a digit sum that is a multiple of 999; not divisible by 666 means it fails the 222 test or the 333 test. Since a multiple of 999 always passes the 333 test, the only way to dodge 666 is to be odd.

    531→5+3+1=9  (by 9),ends in 1  (odd)531 \to 5+3+1 = 9 \;(\text{by } 9), \quad \text{ends in } 1 \;(\text{odd})531→5+3+1=9(by 9),ends in 1(odd)

    So 531531531 is divisible by 999 but odd, hence not by 666. The others (522522522, 540540540, 594594594) are even and divisible by 999, so they are all divisible by 666.

  5. 5

    Which number is divisible by 242424?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Because 24=8×324 = 8 \times 324=8×3 with 888 and 333 sharing no factor above 111, test divisibility by 888 (last three digits) and 333 (digit sum).

    2,160→160=8×20  (by 8),2+1+6+0=9  (by 3)2{,}160 \to 160 = 8 \times 20 \;(\text{by } 8), \quad 2+1+6+0 = 9 \;(\text{by } 3)2,160→160=8×20(by 8),2+1+6+0=9(by 3)

    So 2,1602{,}1602,160 passes both and is divisible by 242424. Of the others, 2,1642{,}1642,164 and 2,1682{,}1682,168 fail the 333 test (digit sums 131313 and 171717), and 2,1722{,}1722,172 passes the 333 test but its last three digits 172172172 are not a multiple of 888.

  6. 6

    Which number is divisible by 181818?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Because 18=2×918 = 2 \times 918=2×9 with 222 and 999 sharing no factor above 111, test divisibility by 222 (even last digit) and 999 (digit sum a multiple of 999).

    4,608→ends in 8  (even),4+6+0+8=18=9×2  (by 9)4{,}608 \to \text{ends in } 8 \;(\text{even}), \quad 4+6+0+8 = 18 = 9 \times 2 \;(\text{by } 9)4,608→ends in 8(even),4+6+0+8=18=9×2(by 9)

    So 4,6084{,}6084,608 passes both and is divisible by 181818, and indeed 4,608=18×2564{,}608 = 18 \times 2564,608=18×256. The others are even but fail the 999 test (digit sums 111111, 131313, 151515).

  7. 7

    What is the remainder when 4,0044{,}0044,004 is divided by 999?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Find the remainder when divided by 999 using the digit sum.

    4+0+0+4=84 + 0 + 0 + 4 = 84+0+0+4=8

    The digit sum is 888, already less than 999, so the remainder is 888.

  8. 8

    A number is divisible by 333 but its digit sum is 242424. Is it divisible by 999?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Divisibility by 999 requires the digit sum to be a multiple of 999. Check whether 242424 qualifies.

    24=9×2+624 = 9 \times 2 + 624=9×2+6

    Since 242424 leaves remainder 666, it is not a multiple of 999, so the number is not divisible by 999. (It is divisible by 333, since 24=3×824 = 3 \times 824=3×8.)

  9. 9

    Which number is divisible by 202020?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Because 20=4×520 = 4 \times 520=4×5 with 444 and 555 sharing no factor above 111, test divisibility by 444 (last two digits) and 555 (last digit 000 or 555).

    5,320→20=4×5  (by 4),ends in 0  (by 5)5{,}320 \to 20 = 4 \times 5 \;(\text{by } 4), \quad \text{ends in } 0 \;(\text{by } 5)5,320→20=4×5(by 4),ends in 0(by 5)

    So 5,3205{,}3205,320 passes both and is divisible by 202020. Of the others, 5,3105{,}3105,310 fails the 444 test (10÷410 \div 410÷4 leaves remainder 222), and 5,3055{,}3055,305 and 5,3155{,}3155,315 are odd.

  10. 10

    A number passes the 444 test and the 999 test. What is the largest of these divisors it is guaranteed to pass?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Divisible by 444 and by 999, with 444 and 999 sharing no factor above 111, forces divisibility by the product.

    4×9=364 \times 9 = 364×9=36

    So it is guaranteed divisible by 363636, which also covers 666. It is not guaranteed to be divisible by 888 (that needs a third factor of 222) or by 242424 (which needs 888). Among the choices, the largest guaranteed divisor is 363636.

  11. 11

    Which number is not divisible by 999, even though its digit sum is a multiple of 333?

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Each candidate has a digit sum that is a multiple of 333; the question is which one is a multiple of 333 but not of 999.

    4+5+1+2=12=3×44 + 5 + 1 + 2 = 12 = 3 \times 44+5+1+2=12=3×4

    The digit sum 121212 is a multiple of 333 but not of 999, so 4,5124{,}5124,512 is divisible by 333 and not by 999. The others have digit sums 999, 181818, and 181818, all multiples of 999.

  12. 12

    Why is 1,000,0001{,}000{,}0001,000,000 divisible by 888?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Divisibility by 888 depends only on the last three digits, and 000000000 stands for 000, which is a multiple of 888 (0=8×00 = 8 \times 00=8×0).

    last three digits=000=8×0\text{last three digits} = 000 = 8 \times 0last three digits=000=8×0

    So 1,000,0001{,}000{,}0001,000,000 is divisible by 888. Ending in a single 000 only guarantees divisibility by 101010, not by 888.
