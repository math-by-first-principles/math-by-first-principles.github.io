  1. Problem 1 The factor total

    A whole number has exactly two distinct positive factors. Their sum is 174174174. What is the number?

    1. Hint 1

      A whole number with exactly two distinct positive factors is prime.

    2. Hint 2

      Its two factors must be 111 and the number itself.

    Answer

    173173173.

    Full solution

    The factors must be 111 and the number itself, so subtract the first factor from their sum.

    174−1=173174-1=173174−1=173

    As a check, 173173173 is odd, its digit sum is 111111, and it does not end in 000 or 555, so 222, 333, and 555 do not divide it.

    Division by 777, 111111, and 131313 leaves a remainder.

    173=7×24+5173=11×15+8173=13×13+4\begin{aligned}173&=7\times24+5\\173&=11\times15+8\\173&=13\times13+4\end{aligned}173173173​=7×24+5=11×15+8=13×13+4​

    The next prime candidate is 171717, and 17×17=28917\times17=28917×17=289 passes 173173173, so no further trial is needed.

    Its factors are exactly 111 and 173173173.

    Answer

    173173173.

    Key idea

    The two factors of a prime are one and the prime itself.

  2. Problem 2 Two even numbers

    Both 222 and 142142142 are even. Classify each as prime or composite.

    1. Hint 1

      Being even means 222 is a factor, so ask what that factor adds to each number's list of factors.

    2. Hint 2

      Compare the factor 222 with 111 and with the number itself: for which of the two numbers is it a new factor?

    3. Hint 3

      A prime has exactly two distinct factors, and a composite has more than two.

    Answer

    222 is prime; 142142142 is composite.

    Full solution

    The only whole numbers that divide 222 are 111 and 222.

    That is exactly two distinct factors, so 222 is prime.

    The number 142142142 is even, so 222 divides it.

    142=2×71142=2\times71142=2×71

    So 222 is a factor of 142142142 besides 111 and 142142142 itself.

    Then 142142142 has at least the three distinct factors 111, 222, and 142142142.

    That is more than two, so 142142142 is composite.

    For 222, the factor 222 is the number itself, so being even adds no new factor.

    For every even number greater than 222, the factor 222 is an extra one, which is why 222 is the only even prime.

    Answer

    222 is prime; 142142142 is composite.

    Key idea

    Every even number greater than 222 has 222 as a third factor, so 222 is the only even prime.

  3. Problem 3 Four numbers sorted

    Classify each of 111, 595959, 959595, and 141141141 as prime, composite, or neither.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare each number's count of distinct factors with the definitions of prime and composite.

    2. Hint 2

      For each number above 111, try the tests for 222, 333, and 555 first, then divide by 777 where the stopping rule still requires it.

    Answer

    111: neither; 595959: prime; 959595: composite; 141141141: composite.

    Full solution

    The number 111 has only one distinct factor, itself, so it is neither prime nor composite.

    The number 595959 is odd, its digit sum is 141414, and it does not end in 000 or 555, so 222, 333, and 555 do not divide it.

    Division by 777 leaves a remainder.

    59=7×8+359=7\times8+359=7×8+3

    The next prime candidate is 111111, and 11×11=12111\times11=12111×11=121 passes 595959, so 595959 is prime.

    The number 959595 ends in 555, so 555 is a factor besides 111 and 959595.

    95=5×1995=5\times1995=5×19

    So 959595 is composite.

    The digit sum of 141141141 is 666, a multiple of 333, so 333 is a factor besides 111 and 141141141.

    141=3×47141=3\times47141=3×47

    So 141141141 is composite.

    Answer

    111: neither; 595959: prime; 959595: composite; 141141141: composite.

    Key idea

    Counting distinct factors sorts a number: one factor means neither, two means prime, and more than two means composite.

  4. Problem 4 The inner factors

    List every positive factor of 165165165 other than 111 and 165165165, and justify that your list is complete.

    1. Hint 1

      Factors come in pairs, so each smaller partner you find also gives you its larger partner.

    2. Hint 2

      Test each whole number from 222 upward, and stop once the number times itself passes 165165165.

    3. Hint 3

      The divisibility rules settle most candidates: 165165165 is odd, ends in 555, and has digit sum 121212.

    Answer

    333, 555, 111111, 151515, 333333, and 555555.

    Full solution

    Factors come in pairs whose product is 165165165, and the smaller partner of a pair, times itself, is at most 165165165.

    Since 13×13=16913\times13=16913×13=169 passes 165165165, every pair has a smaller partner from 111 to 121212.

    The number 165165165 is odd, so none of 222, 444, 666, 888, 101010, and 121212 divides it.

    Its digit sum is 121212, a multiple of 333 but not of 999, so 333 divides it and 999 does not.

    It ends in 555, so 555 divides it.

    The candidates 777 and 111111 need division.

    165=7×23+4165=11×15\begin{aligned}165&=7\times23+4\\165&=11\times15\end{aligned}165165​=7×23+4=11×15​

    So 777 leaves a remainder and 111111 divides exactly.

    Dividing by 333 and by 555 gives the partners 555555 and 333333.

    The factor pairs are 1×1651\times1651×165, 3×553\times553×55, 5×335\times335×33, and 11×1511\times1511×15, so the factors other than 111 and 165165165 are 333, 555, 111111, 151515, 333333, and 555555.

    Answer

    333, 555, 111111, 151515, 333333, and 555555.

    Key idea

    Testing every possible smaller partner up to the stopping point finds every factor pair.

  5. Problem 5 The sorting cards

    A sorter has a pile of cards numbered 222 through 606060. It sets aside the smallest card in the pile, removes every larger multiple of that number from the pile, and repeats until the pile is empty.

    List the cards it sets aside, and list the cards it removes at the step where it sets aside 777.

    1. Hint 1

      This is the Sieve of Eratosthenes, so each card set aside has no smaller card dividing it.

    2. Hint 2

      Work through 222, 333, and 555 first, and keep track of which cards are already gone before the step for 777.

    3. Hint 3

      Once the card being set aside, times itself, passes 606060, every card still in the pile is prime.

    Answer

    Set aside: 222, 333, 555, 777, 111111, 131313, 171717, 191919, 232323, 292929, 313131, 373737, 414141, 434343, 474747, 535353, 595959. Removed at the step for 777: 494949 only.

    Full solution

    Setting aside 222 removes every larger even card.

    Setting aside 333 next removes the odd multiples of 333 still in the pile: 999, 151515, 212121, 272727, 333333, 393939, 454545, 515151, and 575757.

    Setting aside 555 then removes 252525, 353535, and 555555, the multiples of 555 that are still in the pile.

    At the step for 777, its larger multiples up to 606060 are 141414, 212121, 282828, 353535, 424242, 494949, and 565656.

    Every one except 494949 has a factor of 222, 333, or 555 and is already gone.

    49=7×749=7\times749=7×7

    So 494949 is the only card removed at this step.

    The next card in the pile is 111111, and 11×11=12111\times11=12111×11=121 passes 606060.

    By the stopping rule every card still in the pile is prime: a composite card up to 606060 has a factor pair whose smaller partner is from 222 to 777, since 8×8=648\times8=648×8=64 passes 606060.

    If that partner is 222, 333, 555, or 777, it divides the card directly; if it is 444 or 666, the card is even.

    So every composite card up to 606060 is a multiple of 222, 333, 555, or 777, and was removed at one of the first four steps.

    Each remaining card is set aside in turn, and no more cards are removed.

    The cards set aside are 222, 333, 555, 777, 111111, 131313, 171717, 191919, 232323, 292929, 313131, 373737, 414141, 434343, 474747, 535353, and 595959.

    Answer

    Set aside: 222, 333, 555, 777, 111111, 131313, 171717, 191919, 232323, 292929, 313131, 373737, 414141, 434343, 474747, 535353, 595959. Removed at the step for 777: 494949 only.

    Key idea

    A sieve removes each composite card at the step for the smallest prime that divides it.

  6. Problem 6 The display request

    A display needs two prime numbers whose product is 176176176. Can the request be filled? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      If two primes multiply to 176176176, together they form one of its factor pairs.

    2. Hint 2

      Consider separately the case where one prime is even and the case where both primes are odd.

    3. Hint 3

      For two odd primes, the smaller one times itself is at most 176176176, so only a few odd candidates need testing.

    Answer

    No.

    Full solution

    Suppose two primes multiply to 176176176.

    Then they form a factor pair of 176176176.

    If one of them is 222, the other is forced.

    176÷2=88176\div2=88176÷2=88

    But 888888 is even and greater than 222, so it has 222 as an extra factor and is composite.

    Otherwise neither prime is 222, so both are odd, because 222 is the only even prime: every even number greater than 222 has 222 as a factor besides 111 and itself.

    The smaller prime, times itself, is at most 176176176, and 14×14=19614\times14=19614×14=196 passes 176176176, so it is one of 333, 555, 777, 111111, and 131313.

    The digit sum of 176176176 is 141414 and it ends in 666, so 333 and 555 do not divide it.

    Division by 777 and by 131313 leaves a remainder.

    176=7×25+1176=13×13+7\begin{aligned}176&=7\times25+1\\176&=13\times13+7\end{aligned}176176​=7×25+1=13×13+7​

    That leaves 111111.

    176=11×16176=11\times16176=11×16

    Its partner 161616 is even and greater than 222, so it is composite.

    No pair of primes has product 176176176, so the request cannot be filled.

    Answer

    No.

    Key idea

    Checking the factor pairs of a number settles whether it is a product of two primes.

  7. Problem 7 The covered labels

    Two covered labels each show a prime number strictly between 310310310 and 320320320. What is the greatest possible distance between the numbers on the number line? Justify that no other allowed labels give a greater distance.

    1. Hint 1

      The greatest distance comes from the smallest and the largest eligible labels, so find those first.

    2. Hint 2

      After the tests for 222, 333, and 555, the candidates left are 311311311, 313313313, 317317317, and 319319319.

    3. Hint 3

      Since 19×19=36119\times19=36119×19=361 passes every candidate, trial division by 777, 111111, 131313, and 171717 settles each one.

    Answer

    666 units.

    Full solution

    Every even number here is composite, and 315315315 ends in 555.

    The digit sums of 311311311, 313313313, 317317317, and 319319319 are 555, 777, 111111, and 131313, so 333 divides none of them.

    The greatest distance uses the smallest and the largest eligible labels, so test from each end.

    Since 17×17=28917\times17=28917×17=289 and 19×19=36119\times19=36119×19=361, the prime candidates to try are 777, 111111, 131313, and 171717.

    The largest candidate, 319319319, fails at 111111.

    319=11×29319=11\times29319=11×29

    So it is composite, although none of the tests for 222, 333, and 555 caught it.

    Each trial leaves a remainder for 311311311.

    311=7×44+3311=11×28+3311=13×23+12311=17×18+5\begin{aligned}311&=7\times44+3\\311&=11\times28+3\\311&=13\times23+12\\311&=17\times18+5\end{aligned}311311311311​=7×44+3=11×28+3=13×23+12=17×18+5​

    So 311311311 is prime, and it is the smallest eligible label.

    Each trial leaves a remainder for 317317317 as well.

    317=7×45+2317=11×28+9317=13×24+5317=17×18+11\begin{aligned}317&=7\times45+2\\317&=11\times28+9\\317&=13\times24+5\\317&=17\times18+11\end{aligned}317317317317​=7×45+2=11×28+9=13×24+5=17×18+11​

    So 317317317 is prime, and it is the largest eligible label.

    The widest separation is between these two.

    317−311=6317-311=6317−311=6

    Any other pair of eligible labels lies between 311311311 and 317317317, so it is closer together.

    Answer

    666 units.

    Key idea

    The greatest distance between two primes in a range joins the smallest and the largest primes in it.

  8. Problem 8 The factor claim

    A student says that every positive factor of a composite number must also be composite. Is the student right? Use the factors of 626262 to explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Test the claim on the actual factors of 626262, one factor at a time.

    2. Hint 2

      Write 626262 as a product of two factors and classify each factor you find.

    3. Hint 3

      A single factor of 626262 that is not composite is enough to settle the claim.

    Answer

    No; for example, 222 is a factor of 626262 and is prime. The factors 111 and 313131 are also not composite.

    Full solution

    Split 626262 into a factor pair.

    62=2×3162=2\times3162=2×31

    So 626262 has the factor 222 besides 111 and 626262, which makes 626262 composite.

    Yet 222 is prime, not composite.

    One factor that is not composite is enough to disprove a claim about every factor.

    The factor 111 is another counterexample, since 111 is neither prime nor composite, and so is the prime 313131.

    Answer

    No; for example, 222 is a factor of 626262 and is prime. The factors 111 and 313131 are also not composite.

    Key idea

    Being composite describes a number, not each of its factors: 111 divides every composite number and is never composite.

  9. Problem 9 The written list

    Alex writes 111, 139139139, 111, 139139139 as the factor list of 139139139 and calls it composite. Is that classification valid? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Repeated entries do not create new factors.

    2. Hint 2

      Test whether 139139139 has any factor besides the two different numbers Alex wrote.

    Answer

    No; 139139139 is prime.

    Full solution

    The list contains only two distinct numbers, 111 and 139139139.

    Repeating them does not increase the factor count.

    The number 139139139 is odd, its digit sum is 131313, and it does not end in 000 or 555.

    These observations rule out 222, 333, and 555.

    Division by 777 and by 111111 leaves a remainder.

    139=7×19+6139=11×12+7\begin{aligned}139&=7\times19+6\\139&=11\times12+7\end{aligned}139139​=7×19+6=11×12+7​

    The next prime candidate is 131313, and 13×13=16913\times13=16913×13=169 passes 139139139.

    There are no additional factors, so 139139139 is prime.

    Answer

    No; 139139139 is prime.

    Key idea

    Classification depends on different factors, not the number of times a factor is written.

  10. Problem 10 The five-divisor claim

    A student claims that every whole number from 222 to 168168168 that is not divisible by any of 222, 333, 555, 777, and 111111 is prime. Is the claim correct? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Think about what a composite number's factor pairs look like, and how large the smaller partner can be.

    2. Hint 2

      The smaller partner of a factor pair, times itself, is at most the number, so compare 13×1313\times1313×13 with 168168168.

    3. Hint 3

      Check that every whole number from 222 to 121212 is divisible by at least one of 222, 333, 555, 777, and 111111.

    Answer

    Yes, the claim is correct.

    Full solution

    Take a number from 222 to 168168168 that none of 222, 333, 555, 777, and 111111 divides, and suppose it were composite.

    Then it has a factor pair whose smaller partner is greater than 111.

    That smaller partner, times itself, is at most the number, so at most 168168168.

    13×13=16913\times13=16913×13=169

    So the smaller partner is a whole number from 222 to 121212.

    If the partner is 222, 333, 555, 777, or 111111, it divides the number directly.

    If it is 444, 666, 888, 101010, or 121212, it is even, so the number is even and 222 divides it.

    If it is 999, the number is a multiple of 999 and so of 333.

    Every case contradicts the choice of the number, so it cannot be composite, and it is prime.

    The upper limit matters: 169169169 is divisible by none of the five, yet it is composite.

    169=13×13169=13\times13169=13×13

    Answer

    Yes, the claim is correct.

    Key idea

    A composite number below 169169169 has a factor pair whose smaller partner is from 222 to 121212, so one of 222, 333, 555, 777, and 111111 divides it.
