  1. 1

    The box plot shows a class's test scores, and no two students had the same score. Roughly what fraction of the class scored 646464 or more?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The left edge of the box is Q1=64Q_1 = 64Q1​=64, which sits about a quarter of the way along the sorted list. With no repeated scores, roughly a quarter of the class scored below it.

    1−14=341 - \tfrac14 = \tfrac341−41​=43​

    So roughly three quarters of the class scored 646464 or more: the box and the right whisker together.

    Seven prices, in dollars, are 333, 444, 444, 555, 666, 777, 888. The 888 is changed to 808080. Which of the mean, median, range and IQR change?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The 808080 is still the largest price, so every price keeps its place. The median is still the 444th value, 555, and with 777 values Q1Q_1Q1​ and Q3Q_3Q3​ are still the 222nd and 666th values, 444 and 777.

    IQR=7−4=3 before and after\text{IQR} = 7 - 4 = 3 \text{ before and after}IQR=7−4=3 before and after

    The range uses the maximum, so it grows from 555 to 777777. The mean uses every value's size, so it grows too, from 37÷737 \div 737÷7 to 109÷7109 \div 7109÷7.

  6. 6

    A dot plot of 151515 values has a tall stack at 222 and 333, then fewer and fewer dots out to a single dot at 141414. Which word best describes its shape?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The dots crowd together at the low end and trail off toward the high end, so the long tail points to the right.

    long tail on the right⇒skewed right\text{long tail on the right} \Rightarrow \text{skewed right}long tail on the right⇒skewed right

    Skew is named for the side the tail stretches toward, not for where the tall stacks are.

    For the data 111, 333, 555, 888, 888, 888, 888, the median, Q3Q_3Q3​ and the maximum are all 888. What does its box plot look like?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A
