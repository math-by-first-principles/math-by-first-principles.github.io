  1. Problem 1 Three numbers, written with integers

    2. Hint 2

      The minus sign in front of a mixed number belongs to the whole of it, the whole-number part and the fraction part together.

    3. Hint 3

  2. Problem 2 Five numbers in a row

  3. Problem 3 Three fractions, three signs

    Compute −56+38−(−14)-\tfrac{5}{6} + \tfrac{3}{8} - \left(-\tfrac{1}{4}\right)−65​+83​−(−41​), and give the answer as a fraction in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      Subtracting a negative number can be rewritten as adding a positive one, which leaves a sum of three terms.

    2. Hint 2

      All three fractions can be written over one denominator, the least common multiple of 666, 888 and 444.

    3. Hint 3

      Combine the two positive terms first, then set their total against the negative term and ask which side is farther from zero.

    Answer

    −524-\tfrac{5}{24}−245​

    Full solution

    Subtracting −14-\tfrac{1}{4}−41​ is adding its opposite, 14\tfrac{1}{4}41​.

    So the expression is −56+38+14-\tfrac{5}{6} + \tfrac{3}{8} + \tfrac{1}{4}−65​+83​+41​.

    The least common multiple of 666, 888 and 444 is 242424.

    Over 242424ths the three terms are −2024-\tfrac{20}{24}−2420​, 924\tfrac{9}{24}249​ and 624\tfrac{6}{24}246​.

    The two positive terms add to 1524\tfrac{15}{24}2415​.

    Against −2024-\tfrac{20}{24}−2420​ the signs differ, so subtract the sizes, and the negative term is farther from zero.

    −2024+1524=−524-\frac{20}{24} + \frac{15}{24} = -\frac{5}{24}−2420​+2415​=−245​

    The numerator 555 is prime and does not divide 242424, so −524-\tfrac{5}{24}−245​ is in lowest terms.

    Answer

    −524-\tfrac{5}{24}−245​

    Key idea

    Rewrite every subtraction as adding the opposite, then the integer sign rules decide the sign while the fraction methods find the size.

  4. Problem 4 A quotient, then a product

    Compute (−3.6)÷(−0.45)×(−18)(-3.6) \div (-0.45) \times \left(-\tfrac{1}{8}\right)(−3.6)÷(−0.45)×(−81​), working from left to right.

    1. Hint 1

      Each step has a sign and a size, and the sign of each step depends only on the signs of its two numbers.

    2. Hint 2

      For the division, slide both decimal points the same number of places until the divisor is a whole number.

    3. Hint 3

      The quotient turns out to be a whole number, so the last step takes an eighth of it.

    Answer

    −1-1−1

    Full solution

    Start with the division.

    Both numbers are negative, so the signs match and the quotient is positive.

    Sliding both points two places right makes the divisor whole:

    3.6÷0.45=360÷45.3.6 \div 0.45 = 360 \div 45.3.6÷0.45=360÷45.

    Since 45×8=36045 \times 8 = 36045×8=360, that quotient is 888.

    Next multiply by −18-\tfrac{1}{8}−81​.

    The signs of 888 and −18-\tfrac{1}{8}−81​ differ, so the product is negative, and its size is 8×18=18 \times \tfrac{1}{8} = 18×81​=1.

    8×(−18)=−18 \times \left(-\frac{1}{8}\right) = -18×(−81​)=−1

    Counting the negatives gives the same sign.

    No number is zero, and three of them are negative, an odd count, so the result is negative.

    Answer

    −1-1−1

    Key idea

    In a chain of multiplications and divisions with no zero, an odd number of negatives gives a negative result and an even number a positive one.

  5. Problem 5 A cold night

    At midnight a thermometer reads −6.4-6.4−6.4 degrees. By 3 in the morning the temperature has dropped another 1.81.81.8 degrees. By 9 in the morning it has risen 4.254.254.25 degrees from its 3 o'clock reading.

    What does the thermometer read at 9 in the morning? How many degrees below zero was the 3 o'clock reading?

    1. Hint 1

      Write every reading as a signed number, then decide whether each change adds or subtracts.

    2. Hint 2

      The 3 o'clock reading combines two negative amounts, so its size is a sum of sizes.

    3. Hint 3

      How far below zero a reading sits is its distance from zero, which is its absolute value.

    Answer

    At 9 in the morning the thermometer reads −3.95-3.95−3.95 degrees. The 3 o'clock reading was −8.2-8.2−8.2 degrees, which is 8.28.28.2 degrees below zero.

    Full solution

    A drop subtracts, so the 3 o'clock reading is −6.4−1.8-6.4 - 1.8−6.4−1.8, which is −6.4+(−1.8)-6.4 + (-1.8)−6.4+(−1.8).

    Both numbers are negative, so add the sizes and keep the minus sign.

    −6.4−1.8=−8.2-6.4 - 1.8 = -8.2−6.4−1.8=−8.2

    A rise adds, so the 9 o'clock reading is −8.2+4.25-8.2 + 4.25−8.2+4.25.

    The signs differ, so subtract the sizes with the points lined up:

    8.20−4.25=3.95.8.20 - 4.25 = 3.95.8.20−4.25=3.95.

    The number −8.2-8.2−8.2 is farther from zero, so the reading is −3.95-3.95−3.95 degrees.

    The 3 o'clock reading sits ∣−8.2∣=8.2\lvert -8.2 \rvert = 8.2∣−8.2∣=8.2 degrees from zero, on the cold side.

    So it was 8.28.28.2 degrees below zero.

    Answer

    At 9 in the morning the thermometer reads −3.95-3.95−3.95 degrees. The 3 o'clock reading was −8.2-8.2−8.2 degrees, which is 8.28.28.2 degrees below zero.

    Key idea

    A signed number records a reading and its side of zero together, and its absolute value says how far from zero it is.

  6. Problem 6 Paying back part of a loan

    Maya owes her brother 7.507.507.50 dollars. She pays back 35\tfrac{3}{5}53​ of what she owes, and then borrows another 1.251.251.25 dollars from him. Write her balance with her brother as a signed number.

    1. Hint 1

      A debt is a negative balance, so paying some of it back moves the balance toward zero.

    2. Hint 2

      Work out how many dollars three fifths of the debt is before changing the balance.

    3. Hint 3

      Borrowing more money takes the balance further below zero, so it subtracts.

    Answer

    Her balance is −4.25-4.25−4.25 dollars: she owes her brother 4.254.254.25 dollars.

    Full solution

    Her starting balance is −7.5-7.5−7.5 dollars.

    Three fifths of the 7.57.57.5 dollars she owes is 35×7.5\tfrac{3}{5} \times 7.553​×7.5.

    A fifth of 7.57.57.5 is 1.51.51.5, and three fifths is 4.54.54.5, so she pays back 4.54.54.5 dollars.

    Paying back adds to the balance.

    The signs differ and −7.5-7.5−7.5 is farther from zero.

    −7.5+4.5=−3-7.5 + 4.5 = -3−7.5+4.5=−3

    As a check, she still owes two fifths of the debt, and 25×(−7.5)=−3\tfrac{2}{5} \times (-7.5) = -352​×(−7.5)=−3, a positive times a negative.

    Borrowing another 1.251.251.25 dollars subtracts, and two negatives combine.

    −3−1.25=−4.25-3 - 1.25 = -4.25−3−1.25=−4.25

    Answer

    Her balance is −4.25-4.25−4.25 dollars: she owes her brother 4.254.254.25 dollars.

    Key idea

    Model a debt as a negative balance: paying back adds, borrowing subtracts, and the integer sign rules do the rest.
