    The temperature goes from 1.251.251.25 degrees to −3.5-3.5−3.5 degrees. Written as a signed number (new reading minus old), what is the change in temperature?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The change is the new reading minus the old one, and subtracting is adding the opposite.

    −3.5−1.25=−3.5+(−1.25)=−4.75-3.5 - 1.25 = -3.5 + (-1.25) = -4.75−3.5−1.25=−3.5+(−1.25)=−4.75

    The change is negative because the temperature fell: 1.251.251.25 degrees down to zero, and 3.53.53.5 more below it.
