    What is −0.25+23-0.25 + \tfrac{2}{3}−0.25+32​?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The fraction 23\tfrac{2}{3}32​ repeats as a decimal, so write the decimal as a fraction instead: −0.25=−14-0.25 = -\tfrac{1}{4}−0.25=−41​. Over twelfths the sizes are 312\tfrac{3}{12}123​ and 812\tfrac{8}{12}128​.

    812−312=512\frac{8}{12} - \frac{3}{12} = \frac{5}{12}128​−123​=125​

    The signs differ and 23\tfrac{2}{3}32​ is farther from zero, so the answer is positive, 512\tfrac{5}{12}125​.

    Six friends share a debt of 4.54.54.5 dollars equally. Written as a signed number, what is each person's share of the balance?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A debt is a negative balance, so the total is −4.5-4.5−4.5 dollars, split into 666 equal parts. A negative divided by a positive is negative.

    4.5÷6=0.754.5 \div 6 = 0.754.5÷6=0.75

    Each share is −0.75-0.75−0.75 dollars. Check: 6×(−0.75)=−4.56 \times (-0.75) = -4.56×(−0.75)=−4.5.

    Solution

    310=930<1030=13\frac{3}{10} = \frac{9}{30} < \frac{10}{30} = \frac{1}{3}103​=309​<3010​=31​

    The other two sizes are 0.350.350.35 and 0.40.40.4, both more than 13\tfrac{1}{3}31​. So 0.30.30.3 is the smallest size, and −0.3-0.3−0.3 is the greatest.

    Three rational numbers, none of them zero, have a negative product. Which statement must be true?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    With no zero factor, the product is negative exactly when the count of negative factors is odd. Out of three numbers, the odd counts are 111 and 333.

    (−1)×2×3=−6,(−1)×(−2)×(−3)=−6(-1) \times 2 \times 3 = -6, \qquad (-1) \times (-2) \times (-3) = -6(−1)×2×3=−6,(−1)×(−2)×(−3)=−6

    Both cases happen, so neither "exactly one" nor "all three" must be true.

    What is the reciprocal of −0.4-0.4−0.4?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Write the decimal as a fraction: −0.4=−410=−25-0.4 = -\tfrac{4}{10} = -\tfrac{2}{5}−0.4=−104​=−52​. Its reciprocal is −52-\tfrac{5}{2}−25​, which keeps the minus sign.

    (−0.4)×(−2.5)=1(-0.4) \times (-2.5) = 1(−0.4)×(−2.5)=1

    The product is 111, so the reciprocal is −2.5-2.5−2.5. A reciprocal never changes the sign, because the product has to be the positive number 111.
