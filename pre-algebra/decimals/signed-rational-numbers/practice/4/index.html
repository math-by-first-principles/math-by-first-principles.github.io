    What is −38+(−0.125)-\tfrac{3}{8} + (-0.125)−83​+(−0.125)?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The decimal 0.1250.1250.125 is 1251000=18\tfrac{125}{1000} = \tfrac{1}{8}1000125​=81​. Both numbers are negative, so add the sizes and keep the sign.
