  1. Problem 1 The jar label

    A jar label records its capacity of 3.0363.0363.036 liters as a mixed number with whole part 333 and fractional numerator 999. What positive whole-number denominator must the fractional part have?

    1. Hint 1

      The fraction beside the whole part must equal the decimal part of the capacity.

    2. Hint 2

      Write the decimal part over its place-value denominator and reduce until its numerator is nine.

    Answer

    250250250.

    Full solution

    The fractional part must equal 0.0360.0360.036, which is thirty-six thousandths.

    0.036=3610000.036=\frac{36}{1000}0.036=100036​

    To change the numerator from 363636 to 999, divide it by 444 and divide the denominator by the same number.

    361000=9250\frac{36}{1000}=\frac{9}{250}100036​=2509​

    The required denominator is 250250250, giving 392503\frac{9}{250}32509​ liters.

    Multiplying the fractional numerator and denominator by 444 reconstructs the original thousandths.

    Answer

    250250250.

    Key idea

    The decimal part sits over its place-value denominator and then reduces; the whole part stays as it is.

  2. Problem 2 The sixteen copies

    Sixteen copies of one number add up to 111111. What is that number as an exact decimal? Check by multiplying.

    1. Hint 1

      That number is the size of one share when eleven is split into sixteen equal shares.

    2. Hint 2

      Divide eleven by sixteen, continuing into smaller places until the remainder clears.

    Answer

    0.68750.68750.6875.

    Full solution

    Sixteen copies make eleven, so the number is eleven sixteenths, 1116\frac{11}{16}1611​, which means eleven divided by sixteen.

    Sixteen fits six times into 110110110 tenths, leaving 141414 tenths.

    Those become 140140140 hundredths, giving eight with 121212 hundredths left.

    Those become 120120120 thousandths, giving seven with 888 thousandths left.

    Those become 808080 in the next place, which gives five, with no remainder, in the fourth decimal place.

    Thus

    1116=0.6875\frac{11}{16}=0.68751611​=0.6875

    The multiplication check gives

    16×0.6875=1116\times0.6875=1116×0.6875=11

    Answer

    0.68750.68750.6875.

    Key idea

    The decimal representing a fraction is the quotient of its numerator by its denominator.

  3. Problem 3 The accepted entry

    A data form records amounts only as decimals. What does it record for 1350\frac{13}{50}5013​?

    1. Hint 1

      An equivalent fraction must change its numerator and denominator by the same factor.

    2. Hint 2

      Find the factor that takes fifty to one hundred, then apply it to the numerator.

    Answer

    0.260.260.26.

    Full solution

    Multiply the denominator by 222 to reach 100100100.

    Multiplying the numerator by 222 preserves the fraction’s value.

    1350=26100\frac{13}{50}=\frac{26}{100}5013​=10026​

    The new fraction names twenty-six hundredths, which reads directly as the decimal 0.260.260.26.

    So the form records 0.260.260.26.

    Answer

    0.260.260.26.

    Key idea

    Scaling a fraction to a denominator of one hundred makes its value readable in hundredths.

  4. Problem 4 The shared sample

    A 0.720.720.72 liter sample is shared equally among 999 small jars. How much liquid goes in each jar? Give both a decimal and a fraction of a liter in lowest terms.

    1. Hint 1

      First find the size of one equal share.

    2. Hint 2

      After finding the decimal amount, read it as hundredths and reduce the fraction.

    Answer

    0.080.080.08 liters, or 225\frac{2}{25}252​ of a liter.

    Full solution

    Divide the total amount by the number of jars.

    0.72÷9=0.080.72\div9=0.080.72÷9=0.08

    Eight hundredths converts and reduces as follows.

    0.08=81000.08=\frac{8}{100}0.08=1008​

    Dividing numerator and denominator by 444 gives

    8100=225\frac{8}{100}=\frac{2}{25}1008​=252​

    Nine shares of 0.080.080.08 liters total 0.720.720.72 liters, which checks the division.

    Answer

    0.080.080.08 liters, or 225\frac{2}{25}252​ of a liter.

    Key idea

    A decimal found by sharing converts like any other: read its last place, then reduce.

  5. Problem 5 The joined strips

    Two strips have lengths 25\frac{2}{5}52​ of a meter and 112\frac{1}{12}121​ of a meter. They are joined end to end without overlap. What is their total length as an exact decimal?

    1. Hint 1

      Find the total length as a fraction before trying to write its decimal.

    2. Hint 2

      Use sixtieths for both lengths, then track the remainders in the division.

    Answer

    0.483‾0.48\overline{3}0.483 meters.

    Full solution

    Two fifths is twenty-four sixtieths and a twelfth is five sixtieths, so the total length is

    2460+560=2960\frac{24}{60}+\frac{5}{60}=\frac{29}{60}6024​+605​=6029​

    This fraction is already in lowest terms, because 292929 is prime and does not divide 606060.

    Dividing 292929 by 606060, sixty fits four times into 290290290 tenths, leaving 505050.

    Sixty fits eight times into 500500500 hundredths, leaving 202020.

    Sixty fits three times into 200200200 thousandths, leaving 202020 again.

    The remainder 202020 has returned, and the same remainder forces the same next digit forever, so the digit 333 repeats after 0.480.480.48.

    2960=0.483‾\frac{29}{60}=0.48\overline{3}6029​=0.483

    The total length is exactly 0.483‾0.48\overline{3}0.483 meters; stopping at any finite string of threes would not give the exact length.

    Answer

    0.483‾0.48\overline{3}0.483 meters.

    Key idea

    A fraction found by adding lengths can require repeating notation for its exact decimal.

  6. Problem 6 The panel record

    A display contains 320320320 equal panels, and 525252 of them are lit. Give the lit part of the display as a fraction in lowest terms and as an exact decimal.

    1. Hint 1

      The lit share is the number of lit panels over the total number of equal panels.

    2. Hint 2

      Reduce that fraction first, then find the number that multiplies its denominator to ten, a hundred, a thousand or ten thousand.

    Answer

    1380\frac{13}{80}8013​; 0.16250.16250.1625.

    Full solution

    The lit share is 525252 out of 320320320.

    Their greatest common factor is 444.

    52320=1380\frac{52}{320}=\frac{13}{80}32052​=8013​

    The denominator 80=2×2×2×2×580=2\times2\times2\times2\times580=2×2×2×2×5 contains no prime other than 222 and 555, so the decimal ends.

    Multiplying numerator and denominator by 125125125 gives

    1380=162510000\frac{13}{80}=\frac{1625}{10000}8013​=100001625​

    This is 0.16250.16250.1625.

    The fraction is in lowest terms because 131313 and 808080 have no common factor greater than one.

    Reducing first kept the scaling short, since 320320320 itself divides no power of ten smaller than a million.

    Answer

    1380\frac{13}{80}8013​; 0.16250.16250.1625.

    Key idea

    A part counted out of a total is a fraction to reduce; a denominator built from 222s and 555s then scales to a power of ten.

  7. Problem 7 The two weights

    Two samples weigh 17125\frac{17}{125}12517​ g and 1445\frac{14}{45}4514​ g. A scale shows only decimals that end, and must show the exact weight. Which weights, if any, can it show? Give the exact decimal for each.

    1. Hint 1

      A weight can be shown exactly only if its fraction can be rewritten over ten, a hundred, a thousand or a longer string of zeros.

    2. Hint 2

      Check the prime factors of each reduced denominator, then find the terminating or repeating digits.

    Answer

    The scale can show the 17125\frac{17}{125}12517​ g weight, which is 0.1360.1360.136 g. It cannot show the other, which is 0.31‾0.3\overline{1}0.31 g.

    Full solution

    The fraction with denominator 125=5×5×5125=5\times5\times5125=5×5×5 ends.

    Multiplying top and bottom by 888 gives

    17125=1361000\frac{17}{125}=\frac{136}{1000}12517​=1000136​

    It is exactly 0.1360.1360.136 g.

    The fraction 1445\frac{14}{45}4514​ is already reduced, and 45=3×3×545=3\times3\times545=3×3×5 contains a prime other than 222 or 555.

    Its decimal must repeat.

    Dividing 140140140 by 454545 gives the first digit 333 and remainder 555.

    Each following step divides 505050 by 454545, giving digit 111 and remainder 555 again.

    1445=0.31‾\frac{14}{45}=0.3\overline{1}4514​=0.31

    The scale cannot show the second weight exactly, because its decimal never ends.

    Answer

    The scale can show the 17125\frac{17}{125}12517​ g weight, which is 0.1360.1360.136 g. It cannot show the other, which is 0.31‾0.3\overline{1}0.31 g.

    Key idea

    A fraction equals a decimal that ends only when its reduced denominator has no prime other than 222 and 555.

  8. Problem 8 Lena’s two fractions

    Lena says that 18150\frac{18}{150}15018​ and 21175\frac{21}{175}17521​ are equal and that their decimal terminates. Is she correct? Justify your answer.

    1. Hint 1

      The prime-factor test applies after common factors have been removed.

    2. Hint 2

      Reduce each fraction and compare the resulting denominators and numerators.

    Answer

    Yes; both equal 0.120.120.12.

    Full solution

    As written, 150=2×3×5×5150=2\times3\times5\times5150=2×3×5×5 and 175=5×5×7175=5\times5\times7175=5×5×7 contain the primes 333 and 777, so each fraction must be reduced before the prime-factor test applies.

    Divide the first numerator and denominator by 666.

    18150=325\frac{18}{150}=\frac{3}{25}15018​=253​

    Divide the second numerator and denominator by 777.

    21175=325\frac{21}{175}=\frac{3}{25}17521​=253​

    Both reduce to the same fraction.

    Its denominator contains only the prime 555, and multiplying top and bottom by 444 gives

    325=12100\frac{3}{25}=\frac{12}{100}253​=10012​

    Thus both are exactly 0.120.120.12, and Lena is correct.

    Answer

    Yes; both equal 0.120.120.12.

    Key idea

    A prime in the original denominator does not prevent termination if reduction removes it.

  9. Problem 9 The opening digits

    A decimal begins 0.27270.27270.2727. Sam says this proves it equals 311\frac{3}{11}113​. Is Sam right? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Ask what equality with 311\frac{3}{11}113​ would require of every decimal place, and what the statement actually tells you.

    2. Hint 2

      Ask whether a decimal could begin 0.27270.27270.2727 and then continue differently from the fraction’s decimal.

    Answer

    No; a decimal can begin 0.27270.27270.2727 without equaling 311\frac{3}{11}113​, for example 0.27270.27270.2727 itself.

    Full solution

    Dividing 333 by 111111 produces 222 with remainder 888, then 777 with remainder 333.

    The starting remainder returns, so

    311=0.27‾\frac{3}{11}=0.\overline{27}113​=0.27

    The terminating decimal 0.27270.27270.2727 begins with the given digits but has zeros in every later place.

    The fraction has a 222 in the fifth decimal place.

    0.27270<0.272720.27270<0.272720.27270<0.27272

    Therefore the beginning alone does not establish equality; a continuing pattern needs justification, such as a returning remainder.

    Answer

    No; a decimal can begin 0.27270.27270.2727 without equaling 311\frac{3}{11}113​, for example 0.27270.27270.2727 itself.

    Key idea

    A finite list of matching decimal digits does not prove that the complete numbers are equal.

  10. Problem 10 The six digits

    A long division of 555 by 131313 has produced six decimal digits, 0.3846150.3846150.384615, and no remainder has been zero. Without carrying the division past the sixth digit, determine which digits form the repeating block, and explain why the later digits can follow no other pattern.

    1. Hint 1

      Each digit of a long division is decided entirely by the remainder carried into that step.

    2. Hint 2

      Work out the remainder left after each of the six digits, and compare the last one with the number the division started from.

    Answer

    The repeating block is 384615384615384615, so 513=0.384615‾\frac{5}{13}=0.\overline{384615}135​=0.384615.

    Full solution

    Redo the division and record each remainder.

    Thirteen fits into 505050 three times, leaving 111111, and into 110110110 eight times, leaving 666.

    It fits into 606060 four times, leaving 888, and into 808080 six times, leaving 222.

    It fits into 202020 once, leaving 777, and into 707070 five times, leaving 555.

    The remainder after the sixth digit is 555, the amount the division started with.

    Each digit is decided entirely by the remainder before it, so the next six steps repeat the first six, and so on forever.

    513=0.384615‾\frac{5}{13}=0.\overline{384615}135​=0.384615

    Since 555 and 131313 share no factor larger than 111, 513\frac{5}{13}135​ is in lowest terms, so the prime 131313 in its denominator, which is neither 222 nor 555, already shows that the decimal repeats; the returning remainder 555 shows which block, 384615384615384615, and no further dividing is needed.

    Answer

    The repeating block is 384615384615384615, so 513=0.384615‾\frac{5}{13}=0.\overline{384615}135​=0.384615.

    Key idea

    Once a remainder returns to an earlier value, the digits after it repeat the earlier block, so the decimal is known without dividing further.
