    Write 940\tfrac{9}{40}409​ as a decimal.

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The denominator 404040 divides a thousand, since 40×25=100040 \times 25 = 100040×25=1000, so multiply the top and bottom by 252525.
