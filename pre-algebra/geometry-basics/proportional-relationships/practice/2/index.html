    A proportional table pairs x=3,6,9x = 3, 6, 9x=3,6,9 with y=12,24,36y = 12, 24, 36y=12,24,36. What is yyy when x=7x = 7x=7?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Find the constant from any column: k=123=4k = \frac{12}{3} = 4k=312​=4, so y=4xy = 4xy=4x.

    y=4×7=28y = 4 \times 7 = 28y=4×7=28

    The table goes up by 121212 for every 333 across, which is the same rate of 444 for each 111.

  2. 2

    A garden hose fills a tub with 454545 liters in 333 minutes and 757575 liters in 555 minutes. What is the unit rate?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Divide liters by minutes in each case.

    453=755=15\frac{45}{3} = \frac{75}{5} = 15345​=575​=15

    The ratios match, so the hose runs at 151515 liters per minute. The units are liters on top and minutes below, in the order you divided.

  3. 3

    In a proportional relationship, y=18y = 18y=18 when x=6x = 6x=6. What is xxx when y=33y = 33y=33?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    First k=186=3k = \frac{18}{6} = 3k=618​=3, so y=3xy = 3xy=3x. Now solve 3x=333x = 333x=33 by dividing both sides by 333.

    x=33÷3=11x = 33 \div 3 = 11x=33÷3=11

    Check: 3×11=333 \times 11 = 333×11=33. Multiplying 333333 by 333 answers the forward question, not this one.

  5. 5

    The graph of a proportional relationship passes through (3,2)(3, 2)(3,2). What is kkk?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The constant is yx\frac{y}{x}xy​, the second coordinate over the first.

    k=23k = \frac{2}{3}k=32​

    Dividing the other way, 32\frac{3}{2}23​, gives the rate read backward. Check: 23×3=2\frac{2}{3} \times 3 = 232​×3=2.

  6. 6

    A proportional relationship has k=−4k = -4k=−4. Which point is on its graph?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    The equation is y=−4xy = -4xy=−4x. Test (3,−12)(3, -12)(3,−12):

    −4×3=−12-4 \times 3 = -12−4×3=−12

    It works. For (−3,−12)(-3, -12)(−3,−12), −4×(−3)=12-4 \times (-3) = 12−4×(−3)=12, not −12-12−12. The point (3,12)(3, 12)(3,12) has the wrong sign, and (−12,3)(-12, 3)(−12,3) has its coordinates swapped.

    A ride costs a 333-dollar fee plus 222 dollars per mile. Why is the cost NOT proportional to the miles?

    Answer choices for question 7

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    A proportional relationship gives 000 when the input is 000. Here a ride of 000 miles still costs the fee:

    cost at 0 miles=3 dollars\text{cost at } 0 \text{ miles} = 3 \text{ dollars}cost at 0 miles=3 dollars

    The cost does rise by a steady 222 dollars each mile, and the points lie on a straight line, but that line meets the y-axis at (0,3)(0, 3)(0,3).

    Two lines through the origin are y=5xy = 5xy=5x and y=2xy = 2xy=2x. Which statement is true?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    At x=1x = 1x=1 the two lines are at heights 555 and 222.

    5>25 > 25>2

    So y=5xy = 5xy=5x climbs more for each step across and is steeper. Both values of kkk are positive, so both lines rise from left to right.

    A recipe uses 333 cups of flour for every 222 cups of milk. With yyy cups of flour and xxx cups of milk, which equation fits?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The constant is flour over milk, yx\frac{y}{x}xy​.

    k=32=1.5k = \frac{3}{2} = 1.5k=23​=1.5

    So y=1.5xy = 1.5xy=1.5x: 1.51.51.5 cups of flour for each cup of milk. The fraction 23\frac{2}{3}32​ is milk over flour, the rate read the other way.
