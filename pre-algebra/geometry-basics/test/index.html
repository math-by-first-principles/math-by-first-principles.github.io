  1. 1

    Which statement about a circle is true?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    You chose A: Reads the diameter's defining property, passing through the center, as belonging to every segment that joins two points of the circle.

    You chose C: Gives the radius two ends on the circle, though one of its ends is the center.

    You chose D: Attaches the name of the curved piece of the boundary to the straight segment that is really the diameter.

    A chord is any straight segment whose two ends are points of the circle. A diameter has both its ends on the circle and happens to pass through the center as well, so it meets that description and is the longest chord of all.

    every diameter is a chord,not every chord is a diameter\text{every diameter is a chord}, \qquad \text{not every chord is a diameter}every diameter is a chord,not every chord is a diameter

    The other three names each fail on where their ends sit: a radius has one end at the center, and an arc is not straight at all but a piece of the boundary itself.

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    You chose D: Records a downward move with a positive second coordinate.
