    A parallelogram and a rectangle both have a base of 101010 cm and a perpendicular height of 666 cm. How do their areas compare?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Both areas are base times perpendicular height, and the base and height match.

    A=b×h=10×6=60 cm2 for eachA = b \times h = 10 \times 6 = 60 \text{ cm}^2 \text{ for each}A=b×h=10×6=60 cm2 for each

    A parallelogram is a sheared rectangle of the same base and height, so the areas are equal.
