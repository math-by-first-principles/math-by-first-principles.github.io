    A right triangle has two equal legs and a hypotenuse of 101010. How long is each leg, in simplest form?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Call each leg aaa. The theorem gives a2+a2=100a^2 + a^2 = 100a2+a2=100, so two equal squares make 100100100.

    a2=100÷2=50a^2 = 100 \div 2 = 50a2=100÷2=50

    So a=25×2=52a = \sqrt{25 \times 2} = 5\sqrt{2}a=25×2​=52​. Halving the hypotenuse to get 555 would make 52+52=505^2 + 5^2 = 5052+52=50, not 100100100.

    A rectangle has a diagonal of 171717 cm and a width of 888 cm. What is its area?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The diagonal and two sides make a right triangle with the diagonal as hypotenuse. Find the length first.

    l2=289−64=225l^2 = 289 - 64 = 225l2=289−64=225

    So l=15l = 15l=15 cm and the area is 8×15=120 cm28 \times 15 = 120 \text{ cm}^28×15=120 cm2. Multiplying by the diagonal gives 136136136, and 464646 is the perimeter in centimeters.

    A right triangle has legs of 666 and 888. A second right triangle has that hypotenuse as one of its legs, and its other leg is 242424. How long is the second triangle's hypotenuse?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    First find the shared side.

    62+82=1006^2 + 8^2 = 10062+82=100

    So it is 101010. Then use 101010 and 242424 as the legs of the second triangle.

    102+242=67610^2 + 24^2 = 676102+242=676

    Since 26×26=67626 \times 26 = 67626×26=676, the answer is 262626. The choice 343434 adds 101010 and 242424 instead of their squares.

    Someone says a right triangle has a hypotenuse of 555 and a leg of 666. What can you conclude?

    Answer choices for question 9

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The hypotenuse is always the longest side of a right triangle, so a leg of 666 cannot sit with a hypotenuse of 555. The subtraction shows the same thing.

    52−62=25−36=−115^2 - 6^2 = 25 - 36 = -1152−62=25−36=−11

    No length has a negative square, so no right triangle has those sides.

    A carpenter measures 606060 cm and 808080 cm along two edges from one corner, and the marks are 989898 cm apart. What can the carpenter conclude?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    If the corner were a right angle, the gap would be the hypotenuse.

    602+802=10,00060^2 + 80^2 = 10{,}000602+802=10,000

    So a right angle would put the marks exactly 10,000=100\sqrt{10{,}000} = 10010,000​=100 cm apart. The gap is 989898 cm, so the corner is not a right angle.

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    The height cuts the triangle into two right triangles. Each has a hypotenuse of 131313 cm and a leg of half the base, 555 cm.

    h2=169−25=144h^2 = 169 - 25 = 144h2=169−25=144

    So h=12h = 12h=12 cm. The choice 269\sqrt{269}269​ makes two mistakes at once: it uses the whole base of 101010 and adds the squares, 132+102=26913^2 + 10^2 = 269132+102=269. Subtracting the sides, 13−1013 - 1013−10, gives 333.

    The squares drawn on all three sides of a right triangle have a total area of 200 cm2200 \text{ cm}^2200 cm2. How long is the hypotenuse?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The two squares on the legs together match the square on the hypotenuse, so the total is two copies of the hypotenuse's square.

    2c2=200, so c2=1002c^2 = 200, \text{ so } c^2 = 1002c2=200, so c2=100

    So c=10c = 10c=10 cm. Taking 200=102\sqrt{200} = 10\sqrt{2}200​=102​ treats the whole total as one square.
