  1. 1

    A box is 777 cm long, 444 cm wide, and 555 cm high. What is its volume?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Multiply the three dimensions.

    V=7×4×5=140 cm3V = 7 \times 4 \times 5 = 140 \text{ cm}^3V=7×4×5=140 cm3

    Volume is in cubic centimetres.

    Solution

  3. 3

    A triangular prism has a triangular base of area 9 cm29 \text{ cm}^29 cm2 and a length of 888 cm. What is its volume?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    For any prism, volume is base area times height (here the length).

    V=B×h=9×8=72 cm3V = B \times h = 9 \times 8 = 72 \text{ cm}^3V=B×h=9×8=72 cm3
