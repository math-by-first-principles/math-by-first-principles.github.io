    1. One number, three ways to write it . Foundational, 9 points. Question 1 of 5.

    Foundational 9 pts

    A percent is a ratio whose second term is fixed at 100100100, which is what lets any percent be rewritten as a fraction or as a decimal, and read back the other way. This question runs those conversions in both directions, including a fraction whose denominator no whole number carries to 100100100, and then asks what makes that case work at all.

    Part A

    Start from the definition and nothing else: the percent sign says 'divide by 100100100', so drop the sign and write the number over 100100100.

    Every percent question is the same relationship with a different number missing:
