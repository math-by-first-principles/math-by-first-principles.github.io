    A mixture of nuts is almonds, cashews, and walnuts in the ratio 3:2:53 : 2 : 53:2:5. The bag weighs 400400400 grams. How many grams are cashews?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Total parts: 3+2+5=103 + 2 + 5 = 103+2+5=10. Each part is 400÷10=40400 \div 10 = 40400÷10=40 grams. Cashews are 222 parts.

    2×40=802 \times 40 = 802×40=80

    So there are 808080 grams of cashews.

  2. 2

    The ratio of red to blue tiles is 4:74 : 74:7. If there are 777777 blue tiles, how many tiles are there altogether?

    Answer choices for question 2

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    Blue's 777 parts are 777777 tiles, so one part is 77÷7=1177 \div 7 = 1177÷7=11. The total is all 4+7=114 + 7 = 114+7=11 parts.

    11×11=12111 \times 11 = 12111×11=121

    So there are 121121121 tiles altogether.

  3. 3

    A recipe has flour to butter in the ratio 5:25 : 25:2. To use exactly 333 cups of butter, how much flour is needed?

    Answer choices for question 3

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The butter went from 222 to 333, a scale factor of 3÷2=1.53 \div 2 = 1.53÷2=1.5. Multiply the flour part by 1.51.51.5.

    5×1.5=7.55 \times 1.5 = 7.55×1.5=7.5

    So you need 7.57.57.5 cups of flour.

  4. 4

    Money is shared in the ratio 2:3:42 : 3 : 42:3:4. The largest share is $48. What is the total amount shared?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    The largest share is 444 parts, worth $48, so one part is 48÷4=1248 \div 4 = 1248÷4=12 dollars. The total is 2+3+4=92 + 3 + 4 = 92+3+4=9 parts.

    9×12=1089 \times 12 = 1089×12=108

    So the total shared is $108.

  6. 6

    A class of 353535 students has boys to girls in the ratio 3:43 : 43:4. How many more girls than boys are there?

    Answer choices for question 6

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Total parts: 3+4=73 + 4 = 73+4=7. Each part is 35÷7=535 \div 7 = 535÷7=5 students. Girls exceed boys by 4−3=14 - 3 = 14−3=1 part.

    1×5=51 \times 5 = 51×5=5

    So there are 555 more girls than boys.

  8. 8

    A profit of $60 is split in the ratio 1:2:31 : 2 : 31:2:3. How much more does the largest share get than the smallest?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Total parts: 1+2+3=61 + 2 + 3 = 61+2+3=6. Each part is 60÷6=1060 \div 6 = 1060÷6=10 dollars. The largest is 333 parts and the smallest is 111 part, a gap of 222 parts.

    2×10=202 \times 10 = 202×10=20

    So the largest gets $20 more.

  10. 10

    In a box of beads, red to yellow is in the ratio 3:43 : 43:4 and yellow to blue is in the ratio 2:52 : 52:5. What is the ratio of red to blue in simplest form?

    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Make the yellow part match in both ratios. Red to yellow is 3:43 : 43:4, and yellow to blue is 2:52 : 52:5; scaling the second so yellow is 444 turns it into 4:104 : 104:10. Now red to yellow to blue lines up as 3:4:103 : 4 : 103:4:10.

    red:blue=3:10\text{red} : \text{blue} = 3 : 10red:blue=3:10

    So red to blue is 3:103 : 103:10.
