  1. 1

    A car uses 121212 gallons of gas to travel 384384384 miles. At this rate, how far can it travel on 555 gallons?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: C

    First find the unit rate in miles per gallon by dividing miles by gallons.

    384÷12=32 miles per gallon384 \div 12 = 32 \text{ miles per gallon}384÷12=32 miles per gallon

    Then scale up to 555 gallons by multiplying.

    32×5=16032 \times 5 = 16032×5=160

    The car can travel 160160160 miles on 555 gallons.

    A faucet leaks 333 liters every 404040 minutes. How many liters leak in a full day (242424 hours)?

    Answer choices for question 5

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    A day is 24×60=1,44024 \times 60 = 1{,}44024×60=1,440 minutes. The leak is 404040 minutes per 333 liters, so the number of 404040-minute blocks in a day is 1,440÷40=361{,}440 \div 40 = 361,440÷40=36, and each block leaks 333 liters.

    36×3=10836 \times 3 = 10836×3=108

    So 108108108 liters leak in a day.
