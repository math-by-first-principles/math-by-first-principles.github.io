  1. 1

    A train travels at 808080 miles per hour. How many hours does it take to travel 360360360 miles?

    Answer choices for question 1

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The unit rate is 808080 miles per hour, so each hour covers 808080 miles. The number of hours is how many groups of 808080 miles fit in 360360360 miles, which is a division.

    360÷80=4.5360 \div 80 = 4.5360÷80=4.5

    It takes 4.54.54.5 hours.

    Solution

    5 pages4 seconds=5×154×15=75 pages60 seconds\frac{5 \text{ pages}}{4 \text{ seconds}} = \frac{5 \times 15}{4 \times 15} = \frac{75 \text{ pages}}{60 \text{ seconds}}4 seconds5 pages​=4×155×15​=60 seconds75 pages​

    Pump A moves 404040 gallons in 555 minutes. Pump B moves 545454 gallons in 666 minutes. Which pump is faster, and by how much per minute?

    Answer choices for question 4

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Find each pump's unit rate in gallons per minute.

    A: 40÷5=8B: 54÷6=9\text{A: } 40 \div 5 = 8 \qquad \text{B: } 54 \div 6 = 9A: 40÷5=8B: 54÷6=9

    So Pump B is faster by 111 gallon per minute.
