  1. Problem 1 A decimal coefficient

    Find yyy if −2.4y−3.8=3.4-2.4y-3.8=3.4−2.4y−3.8=3.4, and check your value in the original equation.

    1. Hint 1

      The variable is multiplied by a negative decimal first, and a constant is subtracted after that.

    2. Hint 2

      Add 3.83.83.8 to both sides, then divide both sides by the full coefficient, sign included.

    Answer

    y=−3y=-3y=−3; both sides equal 3.43.43.4.

    Full solution

    The subtraction of 3.83.83.8 is the outer operation, so undo it first by adding 3.83.83.8 to both sides:

    −2.4y=7.2-2.4y=7.2−2.4y=7.2

    Divide both sides by the full coefficient −2.4-2.4−2.4.

    Since 2.4(3)=7.22.4(3)=7.22.4(3)=7.2 and a positive number divided by a negative number is negative,

    y=−3y=-3y=−3

    Check in the original: −2.4(−3)=7.2-2.4(-3)=7.2−2.4(−3)=7.2, and

    7.2−3.8=3.47.2-3.8=3.47.2−3.8=3.4

    The sides agree.

    Answer

    y=−3y=-3y=−3; both sides equal 3.43.43.4.

    Key idea

    Clear the constant first, then divide by the coefficient with its sign.

  2. Problem 2 Fractions on a card

    Find xxx if 56−23x=14\frac{5}{6}-\frac{2}{3}x=\frac{1}{4}65​−32​x=41​. Give the answer as a fraction in lowest terms and check it.

    1. Hint 1

      The coefficient of xxx includes the negative sign.

    2. Hint 2

      Subtract 56\frac{5}{6}65​ from both sides, then divide by that signed coefficient.

    Answer

    x=78x=\frac{7}{8}x=87​; both sides equal 14\frac{1}{4}41​.

    Full solution

    Subtract the constant from both sides, using twelfths on the right:

    −23x=−712-\frac{2}{3}x=-\frac{7}{12}−32​x=−127​

    Divide by the nonzero coefficient −23-\frac{2}{3}−32​, or multiply by its reciprocal:

    x=(−712)(−32)x=(-\frac{7}{12})(-\frac{3}{2})x=(−127​)(−23​)

    The resulting fraction 2124\frac{21}{24}2421​ reduces to 78\frac{7}{8}87​.

    For the check, 23⋅78=712\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{7}{8}=\frac{7}{12}32​⋅87​=127​, so

    56−712=14\frac{5}{6}-\frac{7}{12}=\frac{1}{4}65​−127​=41​

    Answer

    x=78x=\frac{7}{8}x=87​; both sides equal 14\frac{1}{4}41​.

    Key idea

    An exact fractional solution is checked with the same substitution used for an integer solution.

  3. Problem 3 A decimal outside parentheses

    Find xxx if 1.5(4x−2)+8=201.5(4x-2)+8=201.5(4x−2)+8=20, and check it in the equation as written.

    1. Hint 1

      Identify the terms on the left after the parentheses have been removed.

    2. Hint 2

      Multiply 1.51.51.5 by both terms in the parentheses, then combine the two constant terms before undoing the operations.

    Answer

    x=2.5x=2.5x=2.5, or equivalently 52\frac{5}{2}25​; both sides equal 202020.

    Full solution

    Distribute 1.51.51.5 to both terms in the parentheses: 1.5(4x)=6x1.5(4x)=6x1.5(4x)=6x and

    1.5(−2)=−3.1.5(-2)=-3.1.5(−2)=−3.

    The left side becomes 6x−3+86x-3+86x−3+8, which simplifies to 6x+56x+56x+5.

    The equation is

    6x+5=206x+5=206x+5=20

    Subtract 555 from both sides and divide both sides by 666:

    6x=15x=2.5\begin{aligned}6x&=15\\x&=2.5\end{aligned}6xx​=15=2.5​

    Answer

    Rewrite a crowded side in a simpler form before undoing its operations.

  4. Problem 4 A fractional line

    Find xxx if 34x−12x+5=3\frac{3}{4}x-\frac{1}{2}x+5=343​x−21​x+5=3, and check the original equation.

    1. Hint 1

      The two variable terms have matching variable parts.

    2. Hint 2

      Combine their signed coefficients before clearing the constant.

    Answer

    x=−8x=-8x=−8; both sides equal 333.

    Full solution

    The coefficient difference is 34−12=14\frac{3}{4}-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{1}{4}43​−21​=41​, so the equation becomes

    14x+5=3\frac{1}{4}x+5=341​x+5=3

    Subtract 555 from both sides, then divide by 14\frac{1}{4}41​:

    14x=−2x=−8\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{4}x&=-2\\x&=-8\end{aligned}41​xx​=−2=−8​

    The check uses both original variable terms:

    34(−8)−12(−8)+5=−6+4+5−6+4+5=3\begin{aligned}\frac{3}{4}(-8)-\frac{1}{2}(-8)+5&=-6+4+5\\-6+4+5&=3\end{aligned}43​(−8)−21​(−8)+5−6+4+5​=−6+4+5=3​

    Answer

    x=−8x=-8x=−8; both sides equal 333.

    Key idea

    Fractional coefficients combine before the usual inverse operations are applied.

  5. Problem 5 Bundles of cardstock

    Two bundles contain the same number of cardstock sheets. Three sheets from each bundle are set aside, leaving 505050 sheets in the two bundles together. How many sheets were in each bundle originally? Check your answer.

    1. Hint 1

      Each bundle loses its own three sheets.

    2. Hint 2

      Let one letter represent the original count in one bundle; double its remaining count to describe the total.

    Answer

    282828 sheets in each bundle.

    Full solution

    Let ccc be the original count in one bundle.

    Each has c−3c-3c−3 sheets left, so

    2(c−3)=502(c-3)=502(c−3)=50

    Distribute and solve:

    2c−6=502c=56c=28\begin{aligned}2c-6&=50\\2c&=56\\c&=28\end{aligned}2c−62cc​=50=56=28​

    The moves are adding 666 to both sides, then dividing both sides by 222.

    Checking the remaining count, each bundle has 28−3=2528-3=2528−3=25 sheets and the two together have 505050.

    Answer

    282828 sheets in each bundle.

    Key idea

    A repeated change within each group belongs inside the repeated quantity.

  6. Problem 6 A shared bill

    Eight friends split a restaurant bill equally. Each friend applies a $3.50 coupon to their own share and then pays the remaining $5. How much was the whole bill before the coupons? Check your answer.

    1. Hint 1

      Each friend's share is the whole bill divided by eight.

    2. Hint 2

      Write what one friend pays after the coupon, set it equal to five dollars, and undo the subtraction before the division.

    Answer

    $68 before the coupons.

    Full solution

    Let bbb be the whole bill in dollars.

    Each share is b8\frac{b}{8}8b​, and after the coupon each friend pays

    b8−3.5=5\frac{b}{8}-3.5=58b​−3.5=5

    Add 3.53.53.5 to both sides, then multiply both sides by 888:

    b8=8.5b=68\begin{aligned}\frac{b}{8}&=8.5\\b&=68\end{aligned}8b​b​=8.5=68​

    The whole bill before the coupons was $68.

    Check: each share is 68÷8=8.568\div8=8.568÷8=8.5 dollars, and 8.5−3.5=58.5-3.5=58.5−3.5=5 dollars, as stated.

    Answer

    $68 before the coupons.

    Key idea

    Recover an original amount by undoing, in reverse order, the operations that produced the known result.

  7. Problem 7 A short record

    A solution record says, "Divide both sides by 444, then add 666 to both sides," and ends at x=−2x=-2x=−2. The original left side was 444 times a difference containing xxx once. Reconstruct the original equation with that difference in parentheses and a single number on the right.

    1. Hint 1

      The recorded moves can be undone to recover the earlier equation.

    2. Hint 2

      Undo the last recorded move before undoing the first.

    Answer

    4(x−6)=−324(x-6)=-324(x−6)=−32.

    Full solution

    Begin with the last line, x=−2x=-2x=−2.

    Undo the addition of 666 by subtracting 666 from both sides:

    x−6=−8x-6=-8x−6=−8

    Undo the earlier division by 444 by multiplying both sides by 444:

    4(x−6)=−324(x-6)=-324(x−6)=−32

    This has the required form.

    Checking the recorded solution in it gives

    4((−2)−6)=−324((-2)-6)=-324((−2)−6)=−32

    Dividing this equation by 444 and then adding 666 recovers x=−2x=-2x=−2, as the record requires.

    Answer

    4(x−6)=−324(x-6)=-324(x−6)=−32.

    Key idea

    Reversing valid balance moves can reconstruct an equation from its solution record.

  10. Problem 10 Two printed cards

    One card reads x−67=1\frac{x-6}{7}=17x−6​=1 and another reads x7−6=1\frac{x}{7}-6=17x​−6=1. For each card, name the operation applied last to xxx, then solve and check both cards.

    1. Hint 1

      Consider which amount the fraction bar divides on each card.

    2. Hint 2

      Undo the operation applied last on each left side.

    Answer

    First card: the division by 777 was applied last, x=13x=13x=13; other card: the subtraction of 666 was applied last, x=49x=49x=49; on each card both sides equal 111.

    Full solution

    On the first card, the whole difference x−6x-6x−6 is divided by 777, so the division is applied last and is undone first.

    Multiply both sides by 777, then add 666:

    x−6=7x=13\begin{aligned}x-6&=7\\x&=13\end{aligned}x−6x​=7=13​

    Its check is

    13−67=1.\frac{13-6}{7}=1.713−6​=1.

    On the other card, xxx is divided by 777 before 666 is subtracted, so the subtraction is applied last and is undone first.

    Add 666 to both sides, then multiply by 777:

    x7=7x=49\begin{aligned}\frac{x}{7}&=7\\x&=49\end{aligned}7x​x​=7=49​

    Its check is

    497−6=1.\frac{49}{7}-6=1.749​−6=1.

    The two cards use the same numbers but have different solutions, because the fraction bars group different amounts.

    Answer

    First card: the division by 777 was applied last, x=13x=13x=13; other card: the subtraction of 666 was applied last, x=49x=49x=49; on each card both sides equal 111.

    Key idea

    Grouping decides which operation is applied last, and so which layer comes off first.
