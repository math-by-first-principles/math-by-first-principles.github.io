  1. Problem 1 A grouped value

    Find the value of −(m+n)24-\frac{(m+n)^2}{4}−4(m+n)2​ when m=−5m=-5m=−5 and n=1n=1n=1.

    1. Hint 1

      The minus sign in front acts on the value of the fraction.

    2. Hint 2

      Add the two substituted inputs inside the parentheses, then square that sum before dividing.

    Answer

    −4-4−4

    Full solution

    Substitute both values in parentheses.

    The grouped sum is

    (−5)+(1)=−4(-5)+(1)=-4(−5)+(1)=−4

    Square the entire sum, then divide by 444 and apply the outside minus:

    (−4)2=16−164=−4\begin{aligned}(-4)^2&=16\\-\frac{16}{4}&=-4\end{aligned}(−4)2−416​​=16=−4​

    Answer

    −4-4−4

    Key idea

    Preserve the original grouping and the position of an outside minus during substitution.

  2. Problem 2 A line with letters

    Write 6ab−ab+2ab6ab-ab+2ab6ab−ab+2ab with no like terms left to combine.

    1. Hint 1

      Compare the complete variable part of each term.

    2. Hint 2

      The middle term has coefficient −1-1−1, so include that coefficient when counting the copies.

    Answer

    7ab7ab7ab

    Full solution

    All three terms have the same variable part ababab.

    Their coefficients are 666, −1-1−1, and 222, so distribution read backward gives

    6ab−ab+2ab=(6−1+2)ab6ab-ab+2ab=(6-1+2)ab6ab−ab+2ab=(6−1+2)ab

    Adding the coefficients gives

    6−1+2=76-1+2=76−1+2=7

    Thus the expression is 7ab7ab7ab; the variable part has not changed.

    Answer

    7ab7ab7ab

    Key idea

    Like terms may contain more than one letter as long as their complete variable parts match.

  3. Problem 3 A penciled expression

    Write −(4p−2q+9)-(4p-2q+9)−(4p−2q+9) without parentheses.

    1. Hint 1

      The outside minus applies to the entire grouped expression.

    2. Hint 2

      Read the outside sign as multiplication by −1-1−1 and apply it to each term.

    Answer

    −4p+2q−9-4p+2q-9−4p+2q−9, in any term order.

    Full solution

    The leading minus means −1-1−1 times the group.

    Its products with 4p4p4p, −2q-2q−2q, and 999 are −4p-4p−4p, 2q2q2q, and −9-9−9.

    Therefore

    −(4p−2q+9)=−4p+2q−9-(4p-2q+9)=-4p+2q-9−(4p−2q+9)=−4p+2q−9

    Each term keeps the sign resulting from that multiplication, even if the terms are reordered.

    Answer

    −4p+2q−9-4p+2q-9−4p+2q−9, in any term order.

    Key idea

    A minus in front of a group changes the sign of every term inside it.

  4. Problem 4 Two envelopes of cards

    Two envelopes hold 3r+23r+23r+2 cards and 2r−52r-52r−5 cards, where rrr is a whole number at least 333. Five loose cards are then put into the first envelope. Write the total number of cards in the two envelopes with no like terms remaining, then find the total when r=4r=4r=4.

    1. Hint 1

      The total includes both envelope counts and the loose cards.

    2. Hint 2

      Collect the multiples of rrr separately from the ordinary numbers.

    Answer

    5r+25r+25r+2 cards; 222222 cards when r=4r=4r=4.

    Full solution

    Add the three contributions: 3r+2+2r−5+53r+2+2r-5+53r+2+2r−5+5.

    The matching variable terms and the constants combine separately:

    3r+2r=5r2−5+5=2\begin{aligned}3r+2r&=5r\\2-5+5&=2\end{aligned}3r+2r2−5+5​=5r=2​

    The simplified total is 5r+25r+25r+2.

    Substituting gives

    5(4)+2=225(4)+2=225(4)+2=22

    The original counts at r=4r=4r=4 are 141414, 333, and 555 cards; their sum is 222222, which checks the total.

    Answer

    5r+25r+25r+2 cards; 222222 cards when r=4r=4r=4.

    Key idea

    Combine contributions with matching variable parts and keep the constants in their own group.

  5. Problem 5 A notebook calculation

    Write −(2x2−7x+4)+3x2−2x-(2x^2-7x+4)+3x^2-2x−(2x2−7x+4)+3x2−2x with no parentheses or like terms remaining, and find its value when x=−2x=-2x=−2.

    1. Hint 1

      The terms containing x2x^2x2 are different from those containing xxx.

    2. Hint 2

      Apply the leading minus to every term inside its parentheses.

    3. Hint 3

      After combining matching terms, substitute (−2)(-2)(−2) wherever xxx appears.

    Answer

    x2+5x−4x^2+5x-4x2+5x−4, in any term order; value −10-10−10.

    Full solution

    Removing the parentheses gives −2x2+7x−4+3x2−2x-2x^2+7x-4+3x^2-2x−2x2+7x−4+3x2−2x.

    Collect the squared terms and the first-power terms separately:

    −2x2+3x2=x27x−2x=5x\begin{aligned}-2x^2+3x^2&=x^2\\7x-2x&=5x\end{aligned}−2x2+3x27x−2x​=x2=5x​

    The constant remains −4-4−4, so the result is x2+5x−4x^2+5x-4x2+5x−4.

    At the given input,

    (−2)2+5(−2)−4=4−10−44−10−4=−10\begin{aligned}(-2)^2+5(-2)-4&=4-10-4\\4-10-4&=-10\end{aligned}(−2)2+5(−2)−44−10−4​=4−10−4=−10​

    In the original, the group 2x2−7x+42x^2-7x+42x2−7x+4 is 8+14+4=268+14+4=268+14+4=26, so with its leading minus it contributes −26-26−26.

    The term 3x23x^23x2 is 121212 and −2x-2x−2x is 444, so the value is

    −26+12+4=−10.-26+12+4=-10.−26+12+4=−10.

    Answer

    x2+5x−4x^2+5x-4x2+5x−4, in any term order; value −10-10−10.

    Key idea

    After removing parentheses, combine terms by both their letters and their powers.

  6. Problem 6 A bracketed line

    Write 4a−[2a−(3−a)]4a-[2a-(3-a)]4a−[2a−(3−a)] with no grouping symbols or like terms remaining.

    1. Hint 1

      Work from the innermost grouped expression outward.

    2. Hint 2

      Subtracting a group changes the sign of every term in that group.

    Answer

    a+3a+3a+3, or 3+a3+a3+a.

    Full solution

    First remove the inner parentheses, changing both signs inside, then combine the aaa terms:

    2a−(3−a)=2a−3+a2a−3+a=3a−3\begin{aligned}2a-(3-a)&=2a-3+a\\2a-3+a&=3a-3\end{aligned}2a−(3−a)2a−3+a​=2a−3+a=3a−3​

    The original expression is now 4a−(3a−3)4a-(3a-3)4a−(3a−3).

    Remove that group and combine:

    4a−(3a−3)=4a−3a+34a−3a+3=a+3\begin{aligned}4a-(3a-3)&=4a-3a+3\\4a-3a+3&=a+3\end{aligned}4a−(3a−3)4a−3a+3​=4a−3a+3=a+3​

    As a check at a=−1a=-1a=−1, the inner group has value 444 and the brackets have value −6-6−6, so the original is −4−(−6)=2-4-(-6)=2−4−(−6)=2, which matches (−1)+3=2(-1)+3=2(−1)+3=2.

    Answer

    a+3a+3a+3, or 3+a3+a3+a.

    Key idea

    With nested groups, remove one group at a time, letting each minus change the sign of every term in the group it stands before.

  7. Problem 7 Two ticket orders

    Adult tickets cost aaa dollars each and child tickets cost ccc dollars each. Order A is for 444 adult and 222 child tickets, every ticket at half price. Order B is for 111 adult and 333 child tickets at full price. Write the cost of order A minus the cost of order B as an expression with no parentheses or like terms remaining, then evaluate it when a=20a=20a=20 and c=6c=6c=6.

    1. Hint 1

      Write each order's cost as its own expression, then subtract order B's whole cost.

    2. Hint 2

      Order A's full-price cost is multiplied by 0.50.50.5, and the minus in front of order B's cost acts as −1-1−1.

    3. Hint 3

      Both terms in each group must receive the factor outside that group before you collect like terms.

    Answer

    a−2ca-2ca−2c dollars, in either term order; 888 dollars when a=20a=20a=20 and c=6c=6c=6.

    Full solution

    At full price order A's tickets would cost 4a+2c4a+2c4a+2c dollars, so at half price order A costs 0.5(4a+2c)0.5(4a+2c)0.5(4a+2c) dollars.

    Order B costs a+3ca+3ca+3c dollars, so the difference is

    0.5(4a+2c)−(a+3c)0.5(4a+2c)-(a+3c)0.5(4a+2c)−(a+3c)

    Half of 4a4a4a is 2a2a2a and half of 2c2c2c is ccc, and the minus before the second group makes it −a−3c-a-3c−a−3c:

    0.5(4a+2c)=2a+c−(a+3c)=−a−3c\begin{aligned}0.5(4a+2c)&=2a+c\\-(a+3c)&=-a-3c\end{aligned}0.5(4a+2c)−(a+3c)​=2a+c=−a−3c​

    Combining matching variable terms in 2a+c−a−3c2a+c-a-3c2a+c−a−3c gives

    2a−a=ac−3c=−2c\begin{aligned}2a-a&=a\\c-3c&=-2c\end{aligned}2a−ac−3c​=a=−2c​

    The difference is a−2ca-2ca−2c dollars.

    Substituting the prices gives

    20−2(6)=820-2(6)=820−2(6)=8

    For a check, order A costs 0.5(80+12)=460.5(80+12)=460.5(80+12)=46 dollars and order B costs 20+18=3820+18=3820+18=38 dollars, and 46−38=846-38=846−38=8.

    Answer

    a−2ca-2ca−2c dollars, in either term order; 888 dollars when a=20a=20a=20 and c=6c=6c=6.

    Key idea

    Build each amount as its own group, then distribute a decimal factor and a leading minus in the same way before collecting.

  8. Problem 8 Rita's conclusion

    Rita rewrites 5r−2r−3r+115r-2r-3r+115r−2r−3r+11 as 111111. Her classmate says that because the result is a plain number, Rita must have evaluated the expression rather than simplified it. Are Rita's result and the classmate's objection correct? Explain.

    1. Hint 1

      Decide whether 111111 equals the expression for every value of rrr or only for one chosen value.

    2. Hint 2

      Add the coefficients of the three rrr terms, keeping the sign in front of each.

    Answer

    Rita's result, 111111, is correct; the classmate's objection is wrong: Rita simplified.

    Full solution

    The variable terms can be written as one product:

    5r−2r−3r=(5−2−3)r5r-2r-3r=(5-2-3)r5r−2r−3r=(5−2−3)r

    The coefficient is zero, so those terms contribute zero for every rrr.

    Adding the constant gives

    0r+11=110r+11=110r+11=11

    The rewrite holds for every value of rrr, and no value was chosen to reach it, so Rita simplified rather than evaluated.

    The classmate's objection fails, because a simplified form can be a plain number when the variable terms cancel.

    Answer

    Rita's result, 111111, is correct; the classmate's objection is wrong: Rita simplified.

    Key idea

    Simplifying can produce a number when the variable terms cancel.

  9. Problem 9 Mark's proposed rewrite

    Mark writes 8z2−3z+z28z^2-3z+z^28z2−3z+z2 as 6z26z^26z2. Is this valid for every value of zzz? Give the correct form with no like terms remaining, and test Mark's form and your corrected form at z=2z=2z=2.

    1. Hint 1

      Look for terms whose entire variable part matches.

    2. Hint 2

      Keep the zzz term separate from the z2z^2z2 terms, then compare numerical values.

    Answer

    No; 9z2−3z9z^2-3z9z2−3z; at z=2z=2z=2 the corrected form gives 303030 and Mark's form gives 242424.

    Full solution

    The squared terms are alike, but the term −3z-3z−3z has a different power.

    Combining the squared terms gives

    8z2+z2=9z28z^2+z^2=9z^28z2+z2=9z2

    The correct simplified form is 9z2−3z9z^2-3z9z2−3z.

    At z=2z=2z=2, it gives

    9(2)2−3(2)=309(2)^2-3(2)=309(2)2−3(2)=30

    Mark's expression gives

    6(2)2=246(2)^2=246(2)2=24

    The original also gives 8(2)2−3(2)+(2)2=308(2)^2-3(2)+(2)^2=308(2)2−3(2)+(2)2=30, so Mark's 242424 differs from the true value and his rewrite is not valid for every input.

    Answer

    No; 9z2−3z9z^2-3z9z2−3z; at z=2z=2z=2 the corrected form gives 303030 and Mark's form gives 242424.

    Key idea

    Terms with different powers stay separate when like terms are combined.

  10. Problem 10 Two letters, two reasons

    Simplify 2r+5s−7r+3s2r+5s-7r+3s2r+5s−7r+3s. For each letter, write an equation that shows why its coefficients may be added, and name the property it uses. Do not choose values for the letters.

    1. Hint 1

      Only terms with the same variable part can combine, so treat the rrr terms and the sss terms as two separate groups.

    2. Hint 2

      When you regroup, the 7r7r7r term takes the minus sign in front of it, so its coefficient is −7-7−7.

    3. Hint 3

      In each group the letter is a common factor of both terms; ask which law lets a common factor be pulled out of a sum.

    Answer

    −5r+8s-5r+8s−5r+8s, or 8s−5r8s-5r8s−5r; 2r−7r=(2−7)r2r-7r=(2-7)r2r−7r=(2−7)r and 5s+3s=(5+3)s5s+3s=(5+3)s5s+3s=(5+3)s, each by the distributive property read backward.

    Full solution

    Group the rrr terms and the sss terms, keeping the minus sign with 7r7r7r, so its coefficient is −7-7−7.

    The expression is now (2r−7r)+(5s+3s)(2r-7r)+(5s+3s)(2r−7r)+(5s+3s).

    In each group the whole variable part is a common factor of both terms, so the distributive property read backward pulls it out of the sum:

    2r−7r=(2−7)r5s+3s=(5+3)s\begin{aligned}2r-7r&=(2-7)r\\5s+3s&=(5+3)s\end{aligned}2r−7r5s+3s​=(2−7)r=(5+3)s​

    The coefficient sums are 2−7=−52-7=-52−7=−5 and 5+3=85+3=85+3=8, so the expression simplifies to −5r+8s-5r+8s−5r+8s.

    Each step used the distributive property, which is true for all numbers, so the rewrite holds for every pair of inputs.

    Answer

    −5r+8s-5r+8s−5r+8s, or 8s−5r8s-5r8s−5r; 2r−7r=(2−7)r2r-7r=(2-7)r2r−7r=(2−7)r and 5s+3s=(5+3)s5s+3s=(5+3)s5s+3s=(5+3)s, each by the distributive property read backward.

    Key idea

    Combining like terms is distribution read backward within each matching group.
