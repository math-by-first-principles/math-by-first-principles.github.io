Why proofs
Because math is easier when the rules make sense. Instead of asking you to memorize formulas, we show where they come from and why they work, so you can understand them and use them in problems you have not seen before.
How lessons work
Each lesson starts with the problem an idea helps solve, then builds the idea step by step from what you already know. Worked examples, quick checkpoints, and common mistakes help you understand it before you move on. If a step needs explaining, we explain it.
Practice
Each lesson has three core sets of 12 practice questions that gradually get harder, plus two more sets for extra practice. Every question includes a full worked solution you can reveal when you need it. Work at your own pace, with no timer or score pressure. No account is required, and your answers are saved in your browser.
