    Answer choices for question 10

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    Either even or odd symmetry requires the domain to contain both members of every opposite-input pair. Here −5-5−5 is allowed but 555 is not.

    −5∈[−5,2],5∉[−5,2]-5\in[-5,2],\qquad 5\notin[-5,2]−5∈[−5,2],5∈/[−5,2]

    The point (−5,F(−5))(-5,F(-5))(−5,F(−5)) therefore has no reflected or half-turned partner at input 555. Although the polynomial expression has odd symmetry on its full real domain, this restricted function has neither symmetry. A zero at the origin alone does not establish either kind.
