    Which evaluation of ln⁡((−45)/(−9))\ln\left((-45)/(-9)\right)ln((−45)/(−9)) is valid in the real numbers?

    Answer choices for question 8

    Solution

    Correct answer: A

    The argument of the logarithm is the whole quotient. The two negative numbers give a positive quotient.

    −45−9=5>0\frac{-45}{-9}=5>0−9−45​=5>0

    Thus the expression equals ln⁡5\ln5ln5. The separate arguments −45-45−45 and −9-9−9 are negative, so their real logarithms do not exist. The quotient law cannot split this expression into those two logarithms.
