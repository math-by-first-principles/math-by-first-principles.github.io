    Solution

    Solution

    Answer choices for question 11

    Solution

    Correct answer: B

    Changing the input within the stated interval does not change the output. Its formula is constant, but its modeling domain is restricted.

    The fixed charge is an output, not a slope or an exponential base.

  12. 12

    A circle's radius rrr may be any real value with 0<r≤120<r\le120<r≤12 centimeters. Which area model and domain are appropriate?

    Answer choices for question 12

    Solution

    Correct answer: D

    Circle area is proportional to the square of the radius, so it is a power model. The stated radius bounds determine the physical domain.

    A(r)=πr2,0<r≤12A(r)=\pi r^2,\qquad0<r\le12A(r)=πr2,0<r≤12

    Circumference, an unrestricted domain, and an exponential formula do not meet both requirements.
